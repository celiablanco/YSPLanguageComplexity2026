Amanzi yi-inorganic compound kunye ne-chemical formula H2O. Yimichiza ecacileyo, engenancasa, ayinavumba, kwaye phantse ayinambala. Yeyona ndawo iphambili kwimisinga yoMhlaba, amachibi kunye neelwandlekazi. Amanzi akwalulwelo lwazo zonke izinto eziphilayo ezaziwayo, apho asebenza njengesinyibilikisi. Amanzi, ekubeni yimolekyuli yepolar, adibana ne-intermolecular hydrogen bonding eyomeleleyo enegalelo elikhulu kwizakhiwo zayo zomzimba kunye neekhemikhali. Ibalulekile kuzo zonke iintlobo zobomi ezaziwayo, nangona inganiki mandla okutya okanye i-organic micronutrient. Ngenxa yobukho bayo kuzo zonke izinto eziphilayo, ukuzinza kwayo kwiikhemikhali, ubuninzi bayo behlabathi jikelele, kunye ne-polarity yayo eyomeleleyo xa ithelekiswa nobungakanani bayo obuncinci bemolekyuli, amanzi adla ngokubizwa ngokuba "sisinyibilikisi sendalo yonke". Ngenxa yokuba iqondo lobushushu bomhlaba kunye noxinzelelo lusondele kwindawo ezintathu zamanzi, amanzi akhoyo eMhlabeni asisiqina, ulwelo kunye negesi. Yenza imvula ngendlela yemvula kunye ne-aerosols ngendlela yenkungu. Amafu aquka amathontsi amanzi ajingayo kunye nomkhenkce, ubume bawo obuqinileyo. Xa ucalulwe kakuhle, umkhenkce oyikristale unokuwa ngokwemo yekhephu. Ubume begesi yamanzi ngumphunga okanye umphunga wamanzi. Amanzi agubungela malunga ne-71% yomphezulu woMhlaba, kunye neelwandle kunye neelwandle ezenza umthamo wamanzi amaninzi (malunga nama-96.5%). Iinxalenye ezincinci zamanzi zenzeka njengamanzi aphantsi komhlaba (1.7%), kwimikhenkce kunye nemikhenkce yase-Antarctica naseGreenland (1.7%), kwaye emoyeni njengomphunga, amafu (aquka umkhenkce kunye namanzi angamanzi axhonywe emoyeni), kunye nemvula (0.001%). Amanzi ahamba rhoqo kumjikelo wamanzi wokuba ngumphunga, ukuphefumla (i-evapotranspiration), ukujiya, imvula, kunye nokubaleka, adla ngokufikelela elwandle. Amanzi adlala indima ebalulekileyo kuqoqosho lwehlabathi. Malunga nama-70% amanzi acocekileyo asetyenziswa ngabantu aya kwezolimo. Ukuloba kwityuwa kunye namanzi amatsha kuye kwaba, kwaye kuyaqhubeka, umthombo omkhulu wokutya kwiindawo ezininzi zehlabathi, ukubonelela nge-6.5% yeprotheni yehlabathi. Ubuninzi borhwebo olukude lwempahla (ezifana ne-oyile, igesi yendalo, kunye neemveliso ezenziweyo) zithuthwa ngamaphenyane kwiilwandle, emilanjeni, kumachibi nakwimisele. Izixa ezikhulu zamanzi, umkhenkce, nomphunga zisetyenziselwa ukupholisa nokufudumeza kwimizi-mveliso nasemakhaya. Amanzi sisinyibilikisi esigqwesileyo kwiintlobo ngeentlobo zezinto, zombini iiminerali kunye ne-organic; njengoko kunjalo, isetyenziswa ngokubanzi kwiinkqubo zoshishino kunye nokupheka kunye nokuhlamba. Amanzi, umkhenkce, kunye nekhephu zikwangumbindi wemidlalo emininzi kunye nezinye iindlela zokuzonwabisa, ezinjengokuqubha, ukukhwela isikhephe ngolonwabo, umdyarho wamaphenyane, ukusefa, ukuloba ezemidlalo, ukuntywila emanzini, ukutyibiliza emkhenkceni, ukutyibilika ekhephini, kunye nokutyibiliza ekhephini. I-Etymology
Igama elithi amanzi livela kwi-Old English wæter, ukusuka kwi-Proto-Germanic *watar (umthombo we-Old Saxon watar, i-Old Frisian wetir, amanzi aseDatshi, i-Old High German wazzar, i-German Wasser, i-vatn, i-Gothic 𐍅𐌰𐍄𐍉 (wato)), ukusuka kwi-Proto-Indo-European *ifom ye-suffid (ingcambu ye-suffiw); Kwakhona dibanisa, ngengcambu ye-Indo-European, kunye nesiGrike ύδωρ (ýdor; ukusuka kwisiGrike saMandulo ὕδωρ (hýdōr), apho isiNgesi sisuka khona 'hydro-'), isiRashiya вода́ (vodá), isi-Irish uisce, kunye ne-Albanian ujë. Iipropati
Amanzi (H2O) yipolar inorganic compound. Kwiqondo lobushushu begumbi lilulwelo olungenancasa nolungenavumba, phantse alunambala noluncinci oluluhlaza. Eyona hydrogen chalcogenide ilula, yeyona khemikhali ifundwe kakhulu kwaye ngamanye amaxesha ichazwa njenge "solvent jikelele" ngokukwazi kwayo ukunyibilikisa izinto ezininzi kunalo naluphi na ulwelo, nangona ihlwempuzekile ekunyibilikiseni izinto ezingapholiyo. Oku kuvumela ukuba ibe "kukunyibilika kobomi": ngokwenene, amanzi njengoko efunyenwe kwindalo phantse ahlala ebandakanya izinto ezahlukeneyo ezinyityilisiweyo, kwaye amanyathelo akhethekileyo ayafuneka ukufumana amanzi acocekileyo ngokwemichiza. Amanzi kuphela kwento eqhelekileyo ukubakho njengesiqina, ulwelo, kunye nerhasi kwiimeko eziqhelekileyo zomhlaba. States
Kunye ne-oxidane, amanzi lelinye lamagama amabini asemthethweni kwikhompawundi yeekhemikhali i-H2O; ikwalinqanaba lolwelo le-H2O. Ezinye iimeko ezimbini eziqhelekileyo zomba wamanzi sisigaba esiqinileyo, esingumkhenkce, kunye nesigaba segesi, umphunga wamanzi okanye umphunga. Ukongezwa okanye ukususwa kobushushu kunokubangela utshintsho lwesigaba: ukukhenkceza (amanzi abe ngumkhenkce), ukunyibilika (umkhenkce ukuya emanzini), ukufunxa (amanzi abe ngumphunga), ukujiya (umphunga ukuya emanzini), ukuthotywa kwe-sublimation (umkhenkce emkhenkceni) kunye nokubekwa (umphunga emkhenkceni). Ukuxinana
Amanzi yenye yezinto ezimbalwa ezixhaphakileyo ezandayo zize zibe nganeno xa ephola phakathi kwamaqondo obushushu athile; kuphela kwento eyaziwayo yendalo eyenza oko ngexa ulwelo. Ukongeza, ayiqhelekanga kuba iyancipha kakhulu njengoko ingumkhenkce, nangona ingaqhelekanga kulo mba. Kwi-1 atm yoxinzelelo, ifikelela kubuninzi bayo be-999.972 kg / m3 (62.4262 lb / cu ft) kwi-3.98 ° C (39.16 ° F). Ngaphantsi kwelo qondo lobushushu, kodwa ngaphezu kwendawo yokukhenkceka eyi-0 °C (32 °F), amanzi ayanda (aye axinene kancinci) ade afikelele kwindawo yokukhenkceza, apho ukuxinana kwawo kwisigaba solwelo buyi-999.8 kg/m3 (62.4155 lb/cu ft). Njengoko amanzi ephola ukuya kwi-3.98 °C (39.16 °F), umthamo wamanzi uyehla. Njengoko iba ngumkhenkce kwaye iba ngumkhenkce, amanzi ayanda malunga ne-9%, afikelele kubuninzi be-917 kg/m3 (57.25 lb/cu ft). Oku kwandiswa kunokuzisa uxinzelelo olukhulu, imibhobho egqabhukileyo kunye namatye aqhekekileyo. Njengoluqilima, lubonisa indlela yokuziphatha eqhelekileyo yekhontrakthi kwaye ixinene ngakumbi njengoko iphola. Ezi zakhiwo ezingaqhelekanga ze-thermal zinemiphumo ebalulekileyo ebomini emhlabeni. Echibini okanye elwandle, amanzi akwi-4 °C (39 °F) atshona ukuya ezantsi, kwaye umkhenkce wenze umphezulu, udada emanzini angawomanzi. Lo mkhenkce ukhusela amanzi angaphantsi, nto leyo eyenza ukuba angaqina abe ngumkhenkce. Ngaphandle kolu khuseleko, inkoliso yezinto eziphila emanzini ezihlala kumachibi beziya kutshabalala ebusika. Ukongeza, oku kuziphatha kungathandekiyo yinxalenye ebalulekileyo yokujikeleza kwe-thermohaline ehambisa ubushushu kulwandlekazi lweplanethi. Imagnethi

Kuxinzelelo lweatmosfera enye (atm), umkhenkce uyanyibilika okanye amanzi abe ngumkhenkce (aqine) kwi-0 °C (32 °F) kwaye amanzi abile okanye umphunga ujike kwi-100 °C (212 °F). Nangona kunjalo, nangaphantsi kwendawo yokubilisa, amanzi anokutshintsha abe ngumphunga kumphezulu wawo ngokuba ngumphunga (ukwenziwa ngumphunga kulo lonke ulwelo kwaziwa njengokubilisa). Ukuthotywa kunye nokubekwa kwakhona kwenzeka kumphezulu. Umzekelo, iqabaka ifakwa kwindawo ebandayo ngelixa amahlwantsi ekhephu enza ngokubekwa kwisuntswana le-aerosol okanye i-ice nucleus. Kwinkqubo yokomisa umkhenkce, ukutya kuye kukhenkcezeke emva koko kugcinwe kuxinzelelo oluphantsi ukuze umkhenkce kumphezulu wawo uthobeke. Amanzi anokuhlala ekwimeko yolwelo kumaqondo obushushu aphezulu kulwandle olunzulu okanye ngaphantsi komhlaba. Umzekelo, amaqondo obushushu angaphezulu kwe-205 °C (401 °F) e-Old Faithful, igiza e-Yellowstone National Park. Kwiindawo ezikhupha amanzi ashushu, iqondo lobushushu lingagqitha kuma-400 °C (752 °F). Kwinqanaba lolwandle, iqondo lokubila lamanzi li-100 °C (212 °F). Njengoko uxinzelelo lwe-atmospheri luhla ngokuphakama, indawo yokubila yehla nge-1 °C rhoqo kwiimitha ezingama-274. Ukupheka kwindawo ephakamileyo kuthatha ixesha elide kunokupheka kwinqanaba lolwandle. Ngokomzekelo, kwiimitha ze-1,524 (5,000 ft), ixesha lokupheka kufuneka linyuswe ngesine ukuze kuphunyezwe umphumo oyifunayo. Ngokuchaseneyo, isipheki soxinzelelo sinokusetyenziselwa ukunciphisa amaxesha okupheka ngokunyusa ubushushu obubilayo. Kwi-vacuum, amanzi aya kubilisa kwiqondo lokushisa. Amanqaku amathathu nabalulekileyo
Kumzobo wesigaba soxinzelelo/ubushushu (jonga umfanekiso), kukho iigophe ezahlula okuqinileyo kumphunga, umphunga kulwelo, kunye nolwelo ukusuka kokuqinileyo. Ezi zihlangana kwindawo enye ebizwa ngokuba yi-triple point, apho zontathu izigaba zinokuhlalisana. Inqaku lesithathu kwiqondo lokushisa kwe-273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F) kunye noxinzelelo lwe-611.657 pascals (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); lolona xinzelelo lusezantsi apho amanzi angamanzi anokubakho. Ukuza kuthi ga ngo-2019, inqaku eliphindwe kathathu lasetyenziswa ukuchaza isikali sobushushu sikaKelvin. Ijika lesigaba samanzi / somphunga siphela kwi-647.096 K (373.946 °C; 705.103 °F) kunye ne-22.064 megapascals (3,200.1 psi; 217.75 atm). Oku kwaziwa njengenqaku elibalulekileyo. Kumaqondo obushushu aphezulu kunye noxinzelelo izigaba zolwelo kunye nomphunga zenza isigaba esiqhubekayo esibizwa ngokuba lulwelo oluphezulu. Inokunyanzeliswa ngokuthe ngcembe okanye yandiswe phakathi koxinzelelo olufana negesi kunye nolwelwelo; iimpawu zayo (ezahluke kakhulu kwezo zamanzi angqongileyo) zinovakalelo kuxinaniso. Umzekelo, kuxinzelelo olufanelekileyo kunye namaqondo obushushu anokuxuba ngokukhululekileyo kunye neekhompawundi ze-nonpolar, kubandakanya uninzi lweekhompawundi eziphilayo. Oku kwenza kube luncedo kwiinkqubo ezahlukeneyo ezibandakanya i-electrochemistry yobushushu obuphezulu kunye ne-ecologically benign solvent okanye i-catalyst kwi-chemical reactions ebandakanya i-organic compounds. Kwingubo yoMhlaba, isebenza njengesinyibilikisi ngexesha lokubunjwa kwezimbiwa, ukuchithwa kunye nokubekwa. Izigaba zomkhenkce namanzi
Uhlobo oluqhelekileyo lomkhenkce kumphezulu woMhlaba ngumkhenkce u-Ih, isigaba esenza iikristale ezine-hexagonal symmetry. Enye ene-cubic crystalline symmetry, i-ice Ic, ingenzeka kwi-atmosfera ephezulu. Njengoko uxinzelelo lukhula, umkhenkce wenza ezinye izakhiwo zekristale. Ukusukela ngo-2024, ezingamashumi amabini ziye zaqinisekiswa ngokuvavanywa kwaye ezinye ezininzi ziqikelelwe ngokwethiyori. Uhlobo lweshumi elinesibhozo lomkhenkce, umkhenkce we-XVIII, i-face-centred-cubic, i-superionic ice phase, yafunyanwa xa ithontsi lamanzi liphantsi koxinzelelo lwamaza olunyusa uxinzelelo lwamanzi kwizigidi ze-atmospheres kunye nobushushu bawo ukuya kwii-degrees ezingamawaka, okubangele isakhiwo se-athomu ye-oksijini eqinile apho iiathom ze-hydrogen zihamba ngokukhululekileyo. Xa ufakwe phakathi kweeleya zegraphene, umkhenkce wenza i-square lattice. Iinkcukacha zobume bekhemikhali zamanzi alulwelo aziqondwa kakuhle; ezinye iithiyori zibonisa ukuba ukuziphatha kwayo okungaqhelekanga kungenxa yobukho bamazwe amabini angamalwelo. Ukungcamla kunye nevumba
Amanzi acocekileyo adla ngokuchazwa njengangenancasa kwaye awanavumba, nangona abantu benezivamvo ezithile ezinokuva ubukho bamanzi emilonyeni yabo, kwaye amasele ayaziwa ukuba ayakwazi ukunuka. Nangona kunjalo, amanzi asuka kwimithombo eqhelekileyo (kuquka namanzi eminerali) akholisa ukuba nezinto ezininzi ezinyibilikisiweyo ezinokunika incasa ezahlukeneyo kunye namavumba. Abantu kunye nezinye izilwanyana baye baphuhlisa izivamvo ezibenza bakwazi ukuhlola ukuselwa kwamanzi ukuze baphephe amanzi anetyuwa kakhulu okanye abulileyo. Umbala kunye nenkangeleko
Amanzi asulungekileyo aluhlaza ngokubonakalayo ngenxa yokufunxa kokukhanya kummandla c. 600–800 nm. Umbala unokubonwa ngokulula kwiglasi yamanzi epompo ebekwe ngokuchasene nemvelaphi emhlophe ecocekileyo, emini. Iibhendi eziphambili zokufunxa ezijongene nombala zi-overtones ye-O-H yokwelula i-vibrations. Ukuqina okubonakalayo kombala kwandisa ngobunzulu boluhlu lwamanzi, ngokulandela umthetho weBeer. Oku kusebenza kwakhona, umzekelo, kunye nedama lokuqubha xa umthombo wokukhanya kukhanya kwelanga elivela kwiithayile ezimhlophe zedama. Ngokwendalo, umbala unokuguqulwa ukusuka eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka ukuya eluhlaza ngenxa yobukho bezinto eziqinileyo okanye i-algae. Kwimizi-mveliso, i-spectroscopy ekufuphi ne-infrared isetyenziswa kunye nezisombululo ezinamanzi njengoko ukuqina okukhulu kwee-overtones ezisezantsi zamanzi kuthetha ukuba ii-cuvettes zeglasi ezinobude obufutshane bendlela zingasetyenzwa. Ukuqwalasela i-spectrum engundoqo yokufunxa amanzi okanye isisombululo esimanzi kummandla ojikeleze i-3,500 cm−1 (2.85 μm) ubude bendlela emalunga ne-25 μm iyafuneka. Kwakhona, i-cuvette kufuneka icace malunga ne-3500 cm−1 kwaye inganyibiliki emanzini; icalcium fluoride yenye imathiriyeli esetyenziswa ngokubanzi kwiifestile zecuvette ezinezisombululo ezinamanzi. Iintshukumo ezisisiseko zeRaman zinokujongwa, umzekelo, isampuli yeseli eyi-1 cm. Izityalo zasemanzini, i-algae, kunye nezinye izinto eziphilayo ze-photosynthetic zihlala emanzini ukuya kumakhulu eemitha ubunzulu, kuba ukukhanya kwelanga kunokufikelela kuzo. Ngokwenyani akukho kukhanya kwelanga kufikelela kwiindawo zolwandle ngaphantsi kwe-1,000 yeemitha (3,300 ft) ubunzulu. Isalathiso se-refractive samanzi angamalwelo (1.333 ku-20 °C (68 °F)) siphezulu kakhulu kunomoya (1.0), sifana nezo ze-alkanes kunye ne-ethanol, kodwa zingaphantsi kunezo ze-glycerol (1.473), i-1.501), i-carbon disulfide (1.627), kunye neentlobo eziqhelekileyo zeglasi (1.6). Isalathiso se-refraction yomkhenkce (1.31) singaphantsi kuneso samanzi angamanzi. I-molecular polarity
Kwimolekyuli yamanzi, iiathom zehydrogen zenza i-engile ye-104.5° ngeathom yeoksijini. Iiathom zehydrogen zikufutshane neekona ezimbini zetetrahedron ezigxile kwioksijini. Kwezinye iikona ezimbini kukho izibini zodwa zee-electron ze-valence ezingathabathi nxaxheba ekudibaneni. Kwi-tetrahedron egqibeleleyo, iiathom zingenza i-engile ye-109.5 °, kodwa ukuchasana phakathi kwezibini ezizimeleyo kukhulu kunokokunyathela phakathi kweeathom ze-hydrogen. Ubude bobhondi be-O-H bumalunga ne-0.096 nm. Ezinye izinto zine-tetrahedral molecular structure, umzekelo i-methane (CH4) kunye ne-hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Nangona kunjalo, ioksijini inamandla ombane kunezinye iielementi ezininzi, ngoko ke iathom yeoksijini inentlawuliso embi engaphelelanga ngelixa iiathom zehydrogen zihlawulwe ngokuyinxenye. Kanye kunye nesakhiwo esigobileyo, oku kunika i-molecule umzuzu we-dipole yombane kwaye ihlelwa njenge-molecule ye-polar. Amanzi sisinyibilikisi esilungileyo se-polar, ukunyibilikisa iityuwa ezininzi kunye ne-hydrophilic organic molecules ezifana neswekile kunye nee-alcohols ezilula ezifana ne-ethanol. Amanzi nawo anyibilikisa iigesi ezininzi, ezifana neoksijini kunye nekharbon dayoksayidi – le yokugqibela inika i-fizz yeziselo ezikharbhon, iiwayini ezihlwahlwazayo kunye neebhiya. Ukongezelela, izinto ezininzi kwizinto eziphilayo, ezifana neeprotheni, i-DNA kunye ne-polysaccharides, zichithwa emanzini. Ukusebenzisana phakathi kwamanzi kunye ne-subunits zezi biomacromolecules kubumba iprotheni, i-DNA base pairing, kunye nezinye izinto ezibalulekileyo ebomini (i-hydrophobic effect). Izinto ezininzi eziphilayo (ezifana namafutha kunye neoli kunye ne-alkanes) zi-hydrophobic, oko kukuthi, azinyibiliki emanzini. Izinto ezininzi ezinganyibilikiyo nazo azinyibiliki, kuquka uninzi lweeoksidi zesinyithi, iisulfides, kunye neesilicates. Ukudibanisa i-hydrogen
Ngenxa ye-polarity yayo, imolekyuli yamanzi kulwelo okanye kwimeko eqinileyo inokwenza ukuya kuthi ga kwiibhondi zehydrogen ezine kunye neemolekyuli ezingabamelwane. Iibhondi zeHydrogen zomelele ngokuphindwe kalishumi njengamandla kaVan der Waals atsalela iimolekyuli enye kwenye kulwelo oluninzi. Esi seso sizathu sokuba iindawo zokunyibilika kunye nokubila kwamanzi ziphezulu kakhulu kunezinye iikhompawundi ezifanayo njengehydrogen sulfide. Bakwachaza nomthamo wayo okhethekileyo wobushushu obukhethekileyo (malunga ne-4.2 J/(g·K)), ubushushu bomxube (malunga ne-333 J/g), ubushushu bomphunga (2257 J/g), kunye ne-thermal conductivity (phakathi kwe-0.561 kunye ne-0.679 W/(m·K)). Ezi mpawu zenza amanzi asebenze ngakumbi ekumodareyitheni imozulu yoMhlaba, ngokugcina ubushushu nokubuhambisa phakathi kweelwandle kunye nomoya. Iibhondi ze-hydrogen zamanzi zijikeleze i-23 kJ / mol (xa kuthelekiswa ne-covalent O-H bond kwi-492 kJ / mol). Koku, kuqikelelwa ukuba i-90% ibangelwa yi-electrostatics, ngelixa i-10% eseleyo ihambelana ngokuyinxenye. Ezi bhondi zingunobangela woxinzelelo oluphezulu lwamanzi kumphezulu kunye namandla e-capillary. Isenzo seCapillary sibhekisa kumkhuba wamanzi ukunyusa umbhobho omxinwa ngokuchasene namandla omxhuzulane. Le propati ixhomekeke kuzo zonke izityalo ezine-vascular, njengemithi. I-self-ionization
Amanzi sisisombululo esibuthathaka se-hydronium hydroxide-kukho ukulingana kwe-2H2O ⇌ H3O+ + OH-, ngokudibanisa ne-solvation ye-hydronium kunye ne-hydroxide ions. I-conductivity yombane kunye ne-electrolysis
Amanzi acocekileyo ane-conductivity ephantsi yombane, eyandisa ngokuchithwa kwezinto ezincinci ze-ionic ezifana netyuwa eqhelekileyo. Amanzi alulwelo anokwahlulwa abe yihydrogen neoksijini ngokudlula umbane ngawo—inkqubo ebizwa ngokuba yi-electrolysis. Ukubola kudinga igalelo lamandla amaninzi kunokushisa okukhutshwe yinkqubo ye-inverse (285.8 kJ / mol, okanye i-15.9 MJ / kg). Iimpawu zoomatshini
Amanzi alulwelo anokuthi acingelwe ukuba angenakunyanzeliswa kwiinjongo ezininzi: ukunyanzeliswa kwawo ukusuka kwi-4.4 ukuya kwi-5.1×10−10 Pa−1 kwiimeko eziqhelekileyo. Nakwiilwandle eziku-4 km ubunzulu, apho uxinzelelo luyi-400 atm, amanzi athwaxwa yi-1.8% kuphela yokuncipha komthamo. I-viscosity yamanzi imalunga ne-10−3 Pa·s okanye i-0.01 yokuma kwi-20 °C (68 °F), kwaye isantya sesandi kumanzi alulwelo siphakathi kwe-1,400 kunye ne-1,540 yeemitha ngesekhondi (4,600 kunye ne-5,100 ft / s) ngokuxhomekeke kwiqondo lokushisa. Isandi sihamba imigama emide emanzini ngokuthomalalisa okuncinci, ngakumbi kumaza aphantsi (malunga ne-0.03 dB/km nge-1 kHz), ipropathi exhatshazwa zii-cetaceans kunye nabantu kunxibelelwano kunye nemvakalo yokusingqongileyo (sonar). Ukuphinda kusebenze
Iziqalelo zesinyithi ezine-electropositive ngakumbi kune-hydrogen, ngakumbi iintsimbi zealkali kwaye, kancinci, iintsimbi zomhlaba zealkaline, zisusa ihydrogen emanzini, zenza iihydroxides kwaye zikhuphe ihydrogen. Kubushushu obuphezulu, icoke, uhlobo lwekhabhoni, idibana nomphunga ukuze yenze icarbon monoxide kunye nehydrogen. Emhlabeni
I-Hydrology sisifundo sokuhamba, ukuhanjiswa, kunye nomgangatho wamanzi kuwo wonke uMhlaba. Uphononongo lokusasazwa kwamanzi yihydrography. Uphononongo lokusasazwa kunye nokuhamba kwamanzi aphantsi komhlaba yi-hydrogeology, i-glaciers yi-glaciology, amanzi angaphakathi kwelizwe yi-limnology kunye nokusabalalisa kweelwandle yi-oceanography. Iinkqubo ze-ikholoji nge-hydrology zigxile kwi-ecohydrology. Ubunzima bentlanganisela bamanzi afunyenwe phezu, ngaphantsi, nangaphezulu komphezulu weplanethi bubizwa ngokuba yihydrosphere. Ubungakanani bomthamo wamanzi omhlaba (amanzi ewonke ehlabathi) yi-1.386 yeebhiliyoni zeekyubhiki zeekhilomitha (333 million cubic miles). Amanzi alulwelo afumaneka kwimizimba yamanzi, efana nolwandle, ulwandle, ichibi, umlambo, umlambo, umjelo, idama, okanye idama. Uninzi lwamanzi eMhlabeni ngamanzi olwandle. Amanzi akwakho kwiatmosfera kwindawo eqinileyo, elulwelo nakumphunga. Ikwakho njengamanzi aphantsi komhlaba kwi-aquifers. Amanzi abalulekile kwiinkqubo ezininzi zejoloji. Amanzi aphantsi komhlaba akhona kumatye amaninzi, kwaye uxinzelelo lwala manzi aphantsi komhlaba luchaphazela iipateni zokufafaza. Amanzi kwingubo yokwaleka anoxanduva lokunyibilika okuvelisa iintaba-mlilo kwiindawo zokuthotywa. Kumphezulu woMhlaba, amanzi abalulekile kuzo zombini iinkqubo zemozulu kunye neekhemikhali. Amanzi, kunye nomlinganiselo ongaphantsi kodwa obalulekileyo, umkhenkce, nawo anoxanduva lokuthutha intlenga eyenzeka phezu komhlaba. Ukubekwa kwentlenga ehanjiswayo kwenza iindidi ezininzi zamatye entlenga, enza irekhodi yejoloji yembali yoMhlaba. Umjikelo wamanzi
Umjikelo wamanzi (owaziwa ngokwesayensi njengomjikelezo we-hydrologic) kutshintshiselwano oluqhubekayo lwamanzi ngaphakathi kwe-hydrosphere, phakathi kweatmosfera, amanzi omhlaba, amanzi angaphezulu, amanzi aphantsi komhlaba, kunye nezityalo. Amanzi ahamba ngokuqhubekayo kwingingqi nganye kwezi kumjikelo wamanzi oquka ezi nkqubo zilandelayo zogqithiselo:

Umphunga ovela elwandle nakweminye imimandla yamanzi ukuya emoyeni kunye nokuphuma kwezityalo ezisemhlabeni kunye nezilwanyana emoyeni. imvula, isuka kumphunga wamanzi ukujiya ukusuka emoyeni nokuwela emhlabeni okanye elwandle. ukuqengqeleka kwamanzi aphuma emhlabeni adla ngokufika elwandle. Uninzi lomphunga wamanzi ofunyenwe kakhulu elwandle ubuyela kulo, kodwa imimoya ithwala umphunga wamanzi phezu komhlaba ngesantya esifanayo sokubaleka ukuya elwandle, malunga ne-47 Tt ngonyaka ngelixa ukuvela komphunga kunye nokuhamba komhlaba okwenzeka kumhlaba kukwanegalelo enye i-72 Tt ngonyaka. Imvula, ngomyinge we-119 Tt ngonyaka phezu komhlaba, ineendlela ezininzi: uninzi lwemvula, ikhephu, nesichotho, kunye negalelo elithile lenkungu nombethe. Umbethe ngamathontsi amancinci ajiyileyo xa umphunga wamanzi udibana nomhlaba opholileyo. Ngokuqhelekileyo umbethe ubakho kusasa xa iqondo lobushushu lilelona lisezantsi, ngaphambi nje kokuphuma kwelanga naxa ubushushu bomhlaba buqalisa ukunyuka. Amanzi ajiyileyo asemoyeni anokuphinda athintele ukukhanya kwelanga ukuze avelise umnyama. Amanzi ahambayo ahlala eqokelelana phezu kweeshedi zamanzi aqukuqela emilanjeni. Ngenxa yokhukuliseko, ukubaleka kwamanzi kubumba imekobume idala iintlambo zemilambo needelta ezibonelela ngomhlaba otyebileyo nomhlaba othe tyaba wokusekwa kwamaziko oluntu. Isikhukula sibakho xa ummandla womhlaba, odla ngokuba kwindawo ephantsi, ugutyungelwe ngamanzi athi enzeka xa umlambo uphuphuma iindonga zawo okanye kubekho isiphango. Kwelinye icala, imbalela lithuba elide leenyanga okanye iminyaka xa ummandla uphawula ukunqongophala kwamanzi. Oku kwenzeka xa ummandla ufumana ngokuthe gqolo ngaphantsi komndilili wemvula ngenxa yobume bendawo okanye ngenxa yendawo ekuyo ngokwemiqathango yesibanzi. Ubutyebi bamanzi
Ubutyebi bamanzi bububutyebi bendalo bamanzi obunokuba luncedo ebantwini, umzekelo njengomthombo wamanzi okusela okanye amanzi okunkcenkceshela. Amanzi avela njengazo zombini "izitokhwe" kunye "nokuhamba". Amanzi anokugcinwa njengamachibi, umphunga wamanzi, amanzi aphantsi komhlaba okanye i-aquifers, kunye nomkhenkce nekhephu. Kuwo wonke umthamo wamanzi acocekileyo ehlabathi, kuqikelelwa ukuba ama-69 ekhulwini agcinwa kwindawo enomkhenkce nakwindawo egqunywe likhephu esisigxina; ama-30 ekhulwini kumanzi aphantsi komhlaba; kunye ne-1 ekhulwini eseleyo kumachibi, emilanjeni, emoyeni nakwizinto eziphilayo. Ubude bexesha lamanzi aseleyo kugcino buyaguquguquka kakhulu: eminye i-akhwifa inamanzi agcinwe kwisithuba samawaka eminyaka, kodwa umthamo wamachibi unokuguquguquka ngamaxesha athile onyaka, uyancipha ngexesha lembalela kwaye unyuke ngexesha lokumanzi. Inxalenye enkulu yonikezelo lwamanzi kweminye imimandla ibandakanya amanzi athathwe kumanzi agcinwe kwisitokhwe, kwaye xa ukurhoxiswa kudlula ukutshajiswa kwakhona, isitokhwe siyehla. Ngolunye uqikelelo, malunga nama-30 epesenti yamanzi ewonke asetyenziselwa ukunkcenkceshela avela ekutsalweni ngokungazinzanga kwamanzi aphantsi komhlaba, okubangela ukuncipha kwamanzi aphantsi komhlaba. Amanzi olwandle kunye namaza
Amanzi olwandle aqulethe malunga ne-3.5% ye-sodium chloride ngokomndilili, kunye nezixa ezincinci zezinye izinto. Iimpawu ezibonakalayo zamanzi olwandle zahlukile kumanzi acocekileyo kwiinkalo ezithile ezibalulekileyo. Iba ngumkhenkce kumaqondo obushushu asezantsi (malunga -1.9 °C (28.6 °F)) kwaye ingxinano yawo iyenyuka ngokuhla kweqondo lobushushu ukuya kwindawo yokukhenkceza, endaweni yokufikelela kubuninzi bobuninzi kwiqondo lobushushu elingaphezu kwengqele. Ubutyuwa bamanzi kwiilwandle ezinkulu buyohluka ukusuka malunga ne-0.7% kuLwandle lweBaltic ukuya kwi-4.0% kuLwandle oluBomvu. (ULwandle Olufileyo, olwaziwa ngamaqondo etyuwa aphezulu kakhulu aphakathi kwama-30 nama-40 ekhulwini, lichibi letyuwa ngokwenene.)
Amaza kukunyuka nokuhla kwamaqondo olwandle asekuhlaleni okubangelwa ngamandla amaza eNyanga kunye neLanga asebenza elwandle. Amaza abangela utshintsho kubunzulu bamanzi aselwandle kunye namachweba kwaye avelise imisinga ejikelezayo eyaziwa ngokuba yimisinga yamanzi. Ukutshintsha kwe-tide eveliswa kwindawo ethile sisiphumo sokutshintsha kweNyanga kunye neLanga malunga noMhlaba kunye nemiphumo yokujikeleza komhlaba kunye ne-bathymetry yendawo. Unxweme lolwandle oluntywiliselwa kumaza aphezulu kwaye luvezwe kumaza asezantsi, ummandla ophakathi kwamaza, yimveliso ebalulekileyo yendalo yamaza olwandle. Iziphumo ebomini
Ukusuka kwimbono yebhayoloji, amanzi aneempawu ezininzi ezahlukileyo ezibaluleke kakhulu ekwandeni kobomi. Iphumeza le ndima ngokuvumela iikhompawundi eziphilayo ukuba zisabele ngeendlela ezivumela uphindaphindo ekugqibeleni. Zonke iintlobo zobomi ezaziwayo zixhomekeke emanzini. Amanzi abalulekile njengesinyibilikisi apho uninzi lwezinyibilikisi zomzimba zinyibilika kwaye njengenxalenye ebalulekileyo yeenkqubo ezininzi zemetabolism ngaphakathi emzimbeni. I-Metabolism sisimbuku se-anabolism kunye ne-catabolism. Kwi-anabolism, amanzi asuswa kwiimolekyuli (ngokusebenzisa amandla afuna ukuphendulwa kweekhemikhali ze-enzymatic) ukuze kukhule iimolekyuli ezinkulu (umzekelo, isitashi, i-triglycerides, kunye neeprotheni zokugcina amafutha kunye nolwazi). Kwi-catabolism, amanzi asetyenziselwa ukuqhawula amabhondi ukuvelisa iimolekyuli ezincinci (umzekelo, i-glucose, i-fatty acids, kunye ne-amino acids eziza kusetyenziselwa amafutha okusetyenziswa kwamandla okanye ezinye iinjongo). Ngaphandle kwamanzi, ezi nkqubo zemetabolism bezingenakubakho. Amanzi asisiseko kwiphotosynthesis kunye nokuphefumla. Iiseli zephotosynthetic zisebenzisa amandla elanga ukwahlula ihydrogen emanzini kwioksijini. Phambi kokukhanya kwelanga, i-hydrogen idityaniswa ne-CO2 (efunxwa emoyeni okanye emanzini) ukuze yenze iswekile kwaye ikhulule ioksijini. Zonke iiseli eziphilayo zisebenzisa amafutha kunye ne-oxidize i-hydrogen kunye nekhabhoni ukubamba amandla elanga kunye nokuguqula amanzi kunye ne-CO2 kwinkqubo (ukuphefumla kweselula). Amanzi nawo aphakathi kokungathathi hlangothi kwe-acid-base kunye nomsebenzi we-enzyme. I-asidi, i-hydrogen ion (H +, oko kukuthi, iproton) umnikezeli, inokungathintelwa ngesiseko, i-proton receiver njenge-ion hydroxide (OH-) ukwenza amanzi. Amanzi athathwa njengokungathathi hlangothi, kunye ne-pH (i-log engalunganga ye-hydrogen ion concentration) ye-7 kwindawo efanelekileyo. I-Acids inexabiso le-pH elingaphantsi kwe-7 ngelixa iziseko zinexabiso elikhulu kune-7. Iifom zobomi basemanzini
Amanzi aphezu komhlaba azaliswe bubomi. Iintlobo zobomi bokuqala zavela emanzini; phantse zonke iintlanzi zihlala emanzini kuphela, kwaye zininzi iintlobo zezilwanyana ezanyisayo zaselwandle, njengamahlengesi neminenga. Ezinye iintlobo zezilwanyana, ezifana nezilwanyana ezihlala emanzini nasemanzini, zichitha inxalenye yobomi bazo emanzini kunye nenxalenye emhlabeni. Izityalo ezifana ne-kelp kunye ne-algae zikhula emanzini kwaye zisisiseko sezinto eziphilayo eziphantsi kwamanzi. IPlankton ngokuqhelekileyo isisiseko sokutya kolwandle. Izilwanyana zasemanzini kufuneka zifumane ioksijini ukuze ziphile, kwaye zikwenza oko ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo. Iintlanzi zinemiphunga endaweni yemiphunga, nangona ezinye iindidi zeentlanzi, ezifana nelungfish, zinazo zombini. Izilwanyana zaselwandle ezincancisayo, ezifana namahlengesi, iminenga, ii-otters, neentini zolwandle kufuneka zinyuke ngamaxesha athile ukuze ziphefumle umoya. Ezinye izilwanyana eziphila emhlabeni nasemanzini ziyakwazi ukufunxa ioksijini ngolusu lwazo. Ama-invertebrates abonisa uluhlu olubanzi lokuguqulwa ukuze aphile emanzini angenayo i-oksijini equka iityhubhu zokuphefumula (jonga inambuzane kunye ne-mollusc siphons) kunye neegill (Carcinus). Nangona kunjalo, njengoko ubomi be-invertebrate bavela kwindawo yokuhlala yasemanzini, uninzi luncinci okanye alunalo ulwazi lokuphefumla emanzini. Iziphumo kwimpucuko yabantu
Impucuko iphumelele ngokwembali malunga nemilambo kunye neendlela ezinkulu zamanzi; IMesopotamiya, enye yeendawo ekufane kubizwe ngokuba zisiqalo sempucuko, yayiphakathi komlambo omkhulu iTigris nomEfrate; uluntu lwamandulo lwamaJiphutha lwaluxhomekeke ngokupheleleyo kumNayile. Impucuko yamandulo yase-Indus Valley (malunga nowama-3300 BCE - malunga nowe-1300 BCE) yavela ngakuMlambo i-Indus kunye nemilambo eyayiphuma kwiiHimalaya. IRoma nayo yasekwa kunxweme lomlambo iTiber eItali. Izixeko ezikhulu ezifana neRotterdam, iLondon, iMontreal, iParis, iNew York City, iBuenos Aires, iShanghai, iTokyo, iChicago, neHong Kong zifumene impumelelo yazo ngokuyinxenye ngenxa yokufikeleleka kwazo ngokulula ngamanzi kunye nokwandiswa korhwebo. Iziqithi ezinamazibuko amanzi akhuselekileyo, njengeSingapore, ziye zahluma ngenxa yesizathu esifanayo. Kwiindawo ezinjengoMntla Afrika nakuMbindi Mpuma, apho amanzi enqabe ngakumbi, ukufikelela kumanzi okusela acocekileyo kwakuyeyona nto iphambili ekukhuleni kwabantu. Impilo kunye nongcoliseko
Amanzi alungele ukuselwa ngabantu abizwa ngokuba ngamanzi okusela okanye amanzi okusela. Amanzi angaselwayo anokwenziwa ukuselwa ngokuhluzwa okanye ngokuhluzwa, okanye ngoluhlu lwezinye iindlela. Bangaphezu kwezigidi ezingama-660 abantu abangenawo amanzi okusela acocekileyo. Amanzi angafanelanga ukuselwa kodwa engenabungozi ebantwini xa equbha okanye ekuhlambeni abizwa ngamagama ahlukeneyo ngaphandle kwawaselwayo okanye amanzi okusela, kwaye ngamanye amaxesha abizwa ngokuba ngamanzi akhuselekileyo, okanye "akhuselekile xa kuhlanjwa". I-Chlorine lulusu kunye nenwebu ye-mucous ecaphukisayo esetyenziselwa ukwenza amanzi akhuseleke xa uhlamba okanye ukusela. Ukusetyenziswa kwayo kubuchwephesha obuphezulu kwaye kudla ngokujongwa yimigaqo karhulumente (ngokuqhelekileyo inxalenye enye kwisigidi (ppm) yamanzi okusela, kunye ne-1–2 ppm yeklorini engekadibani nobumdaka bamanzi okuhlamba). Amanzi okuhlamba anokugcinwa kwimeko eyanelisayo ye-microbiological kusetyenziswa ii-disinfectants zekhemikhali ezifana ne-chlorine okanye i-ozone okanye ngokusetyenziswa kokukhanya kwe-ultraviolet. Ukulungiswa kwakhona kwamanzi yinkqubo yokuguqula amanzi amdaka (adla ngokuba lindle, abizwa ngokuba ngamanzi amdaka kamasipala) abe ngamanzi anokuphinda asetyenziselwe ezinye iinjongo. Kukho abantu abazibhiliyoni ezi-2.3 abahlala kumazwe anqongopheleyo ngamanzi, nto leyo ethetha ukuba umntu ngamnye ufumana ngaphantsi kwe-1,700 cubic metres (60,000 cu ft) zamanzi ngonyaka. Ama-380 eebhiliyoni zee-cubic metres (13×10^12 cu ft) zamanzi amdaka kamasipala aveliswa kwihlabathi jikelele nyaka ngamnye. Amanzi amatsha sisixhobo esinokuhlaziywa kwakhona, sijikeleziswa kwakhona ngumjikelo wendalo we-hydrologic, kodwa uxinzelelo lokufikelela kuwo lubangelwa kukusasazwa kokungalingani ngokwendalo kwindawo nexesha, ukukhula kweemfuno zoqoqosho lwezolimo kunye neshishini, kunye nokunyuka kwamanani abantu. Sithetha nje, baphantse babe yibhiliyoni abantu ehlabathini lonke abakwazi ukufikelela kumanzi akhuselekileyo nafikelelekayo. Ngo-2000, iZizwe eziManyeneyo zaseka iiNjongo zoPhuhliso lweMillennium ukuze amanzi acuthe ngesiqingatha ngo-2015 umyinge wabantu kwihlabathi liphela abangenawo amanzi akhuselekileyo nogutyulo. Inkqubela phambili eya kufikelela kuloo njongo yayingalingani, kwaye ngowama-2015 i-UN yazibophelela kwiiNjongo zoPhuhliso oluZinzileyo zokufikelela kwihlabathi liphela kumanzi akhuselekileyo nafikelelekayo kunye nogutyulo ngowama-2030. Ubulunga bamanzi obuphantsi kunye nogutyulo olubi luyabulala; ukufa kwezigidi ezihlanu ngonyaka kubangelwa zizifo ezinxulumene namanzi. IWorld Health Organization iqikelela ukuba amanzi acocekileyo anokuthintela ukufa kwabantwana abasisi-1,4 sesigidi ngenxa yorhudo nyaka ngamnye. Kumazwe asaphuhlayo, i-90% yawo onke amanzi amdaka kamasipala asahamba engacocwa emilanjeni nakwimisinga. Amazwe amalunga nama-50, anabemi behlabathi abamalunga nesinye kwisithathu, nawo anengxaki yokunqongophala kwamanzi aphakathi okanye aphezulu yaye ali-17 kula akhupha amanzi amaninzi ngonyaka kunalawo atshajiswa ngomjikelo wawo wendalo wamanzi. Olu xinzelelo aluchaphazeli nje kuphela imimandla yamanzi acocekileyo angaphezulu njengemilambo namachibi, kodwa lukwathoba ubutyebi bamanzi aphantsi komhlaba. Ukusetyenziswa kwabantu
Ezolimo
Amanzi asetyenziswa kakhulu ngabantu kulimo, kubandakanywa ulimo olunkcenkceshelwayo, oluthatha ukuya kutsho kuma-80 ukuya kuma-90 epesenti yokusetyenziswa kwamanzi xa ewonke. EUnited States, i-42% yamanzi acocekileyo arhoxisiweyo ukuba asetyenziselwe ukunkcenkceshela, kodwa uninzi lwamanzi "asetyenzisiweyo" (asetyenzisiweyo kwaye angabuyi kwindalo) aya kwezolimo. Ukufikelela kumanzi acocekileyo kudla ngokuthathwa njengento engenamsebenzi, ingakumbi kumazwe ahambele phambili kwezoqoqosho nakhe asebenzisa iindlela ezintsonkothileyo zamanzi zokuqokelela, ukucocwa, nokuhanjiswa kwamanzi, nokususa amanzi amdaka. Kodwa ukukhula kwezoqoqosho, amanani abantu, kunye noxinzelelo lwemozulu kuyanda iinkxalabo malunga nemiba yamanzi, okukhokelela kukhuphiswano olwandisiweyo lwemithombo yamanzi esisigxina, okukhokelela kumbono wamanzi aphezulu. Njengoko abantu kunye noqoqosho luqhubeka lukhula, ukusetyenziswa kwenyama enxaniweyo ngamanzi kuyanda, kunye nokunyuka kweemfuno ezintsha ze-biofuels okanye amashishini amatsha asebenzisa amanzi amaninzi, imingeni emitsha yamanzi inokwenzeka. Uvavanyo lolawulo lwamanzi kwezolimo lwenziwa kwi-2007 yi-International Water Management Institute eSri Lanka ukubona ukuba ihlabathi linamanzi aneleyo okubonelela ngokutya kwabantu abakhulayo. Ivavanye ubukho bangoku bamanzi ezolimo kumhlaba jikelele kwaye yenza imephu yeendawo ezinengxaki yokunqongophala kwamanzi. Ifumanise ukuba isinye kwisihlanu sabantu behlabathi, ngaphezu kwe-1.2 yezigidigidi, bahlala kwiindawo ezinqongopheleyo ngokwasemzimbeni, apho kungekho manzi aneleyo okuhlangabezana nazo zonke iimfuno. Abanye abantu abazibhiliyoni ezisisi-1.6 bahlala kwiindawo ezinengxaki yokunqongophala kwamanzi kwezoqoqosho, apho ukungabikho kotyalo-mali kumanzi okanye ukungonelanga kwabantu kwenza kube nzima ukuba abasemagunyeni banelise imfuno yamanzi. Le ngxelo yafumanisa ukuba kunokwenzeka ukuvelisa ukutya okufunekayo kwixa elizayo, kodwa ukuqhubeka kwemveliso yokutya yanamhlanje nemeko yemekobume kuya kubangela iingxaki kwiindawo ezininzi zehlabathi. Ukuze kuphetshwe ingxaki yamanzi ehlabathini lonke, amafama kuya kufuneka azabalazele ukwandisa imveliso ukuze ahlangabezane neemfuno ezikhulayo zokutya, ngoxa imizi-mveliso nezixeko zifumana iindlela zokuwasebenzisa kakuhle amanzi. Ukunqongophala kwamanzi kukwabangelwa kukuveliswa kweemveliso zamanzi kakhulu. Ngokomzekelo, ikotoni: i-1 kg yekotoni-elingana ne-jeans-idinga i-10.9 cubic metres (380 cu ft) amanzi ukuvelisa. Nangona i-cotton yenza i-2.4% yokusetyenziswa kwamanzi kwihlabathi, amanzi asetyenziswa kwimimandla esele isengozini yokunqongophala kwamanzi. Umonakalo omkhulu wemekobume ubangelwe: umzekelo, ukuphambukiswa kwamanzi yilizwe elalisakuba yiSoviet Union ukusuka kwimilambo iAmu Darya neSyr Darya ukuvelisa umqhaphu ubukhulu becala kwakubangele ukunyamalala koLwandle lweAral. Njengomgangatho wesayensi
Ngomhla we-7 ku-Epreli 1795, igram yachazwa eFransi ukuba ilingane "nobunzima obupheleleyo bomthamo wamanzi acocekileyo alingana netyhubhu yekhulu lemitha, kunye nobushushu bomkhenkce onyibilikayo". Ngeenjongo ezisebenzayo nangona kunjalo, umgangatho wereferensi yesinyithi wawufuneka, ubukhulu obuphindwe kaliwaka ngaphezulu, ikhilogram. Umsebenzi ke ngoko wagunyaziswa ukumisela ngokuchanekileyo ubunzima belitha enye yamanzi. Nangona ingcaciso emiselweyo yegram yachaza amanzi ku-0 °C (32 °F)-ubushushu obunokuphindaphindeka kakhulu-izazinzulu zakhetha ukuphinda zichaze umgangatho kwaye zenze imilinganiselo yazo kwiqondo lobushushu eliphakamileyo lokuxinana kwamanzi, elalinganiswa ngelo xesha njenge-4 °C (39 °F). Isikali sobushushu seKelvin senkqubo ye-SI sasisekwe kwindawo ephindwe kathathu yamanzi, echazwa kanye njenge-273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F), kodwa ukusukela ngoMeyi ka-2019 isekwe kwi-Boltzmann engaguqukiyo endaweni yoko. Isikali sisikali esipheleleyo sobushushu esinokwenyuswa okufanayo nesikali sobushushu beCelsius, esasichazwa ngokwesiqalo ngokwendawo yokubila (imiselwe kwi-100 °C (212 °F)) nendawo yokunyibilika (imiselwe kwi-0 °C (32 °F)) yamanzi. Amanzi endalo aqulathe ubukhulu becala isotopes hydrogen-1 kunye neoksijini-16, kodwa kukho nentwana enzima isotopes oxygen-18, oxygen-17, kunye hydrogen-2 (deuterium). Ipesenti ye-isotopes enzima kakhulu incinci, kodwa isachaphazela iipropati zamanzi. Amanzi asuka emilanjeni nasemachibini akholisa ukuba ne-isotopi enzima kunamanzi olwandle. Ke ngoko, amanzi asemgangathweni achazwe kwi-Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water. Eyokusela
Umzimba womntu uqulethe, ngokomndilili, i-50-60% yamanzi, kuxhomekeke kwiminyaka, isini kunye nobukhulu bomzimba, nangona abantu banokuba naphi na phakathi kwe-45% kunye ne-75%. I-U.S. National Academy of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine icebisa ukuba kuthathwe iilitha ezingama-3.7 (iilitha eziyi-0.98 zaseU.S.) zamanzi kumadoda amadala kunye ne-2.7 L (0.71 U.S. gal) kubafazi. Isixa esichanekileyo sixhomekeke kwinqanaba lomsebenzi, ubushushu, ukufuma, kunye nezinye izinto. Uninzi lwale nto igalelwa ngokutya okanye iziselo ngaphandle kokusela amanzi angqale ngqo. Uncwadi lwezonyango luthanda ukusetyenziswa okuphantsi, ngokuqhelekileyo ilitha e-1 yamanzi kumntu oyindoda, ngaphandle kweemfuno ezongezelelweyo ngenxa yokulahlekelwa kwamanzi ngenxa yokuzivocavoca okanye imozulu eshushu. Izintso eziphilileyo zinokukhupha i-0.8 ukuya kwi-1 ilitha yamanzi ngeyure, kodwa uxinzelelo olunjengokuzivocavoca lunokunciphisa le mali. Abantu banokusela amanzi amaninzi kunokuba kuyimfuneko ngelixa besenza umthambo, nto leyo ebabeka emngciphekweni wokunxila ngamanzi (hyperhydration), enokubabulala. Ibango elidumileyo elithi “umntu ufanele asele iiglasi zamanzi ezisibhozo ngosuku” libonakala lingenasiseko sokwenene kwinzululwazi. Uphononongo lubonise ukuba ukufakwa kwamanzi okongeziweyo, ngakumbi ukuya kwi-500 milliliters (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) ngexesha lokutya, yayinxulumene nokulahlekelwa kwesisindo. Ukutya okwaneleyo kwamanzi kuyanceda ekuthinteleni ukuqhina. Isincomo santlandlolo sokusela amanzi ngowe-1945 yiBhodi Yokutya Nesondlo yeBhunga Lesizwe Lophando LaseU.S. lalifundeka ngolu hlobo: “Umlinganiselo oqhelekileyo wabantu abahlukahlukeneyo sisililitha esi-1 kwikhalori nganye yokutya. Ingxelo yamva nje yereferensi yokutya ye-U.S. National Research Council ngokubanzi ecetyisiweyo, ngokusekwe kwi-median iyonke yokuthathwa kwamanzi asuka kwidata yophando lwase-US (kubandakanywa nemithombo yokutya): 3.7 ilitha (0.81 imp gal; 0.98 US gal) kumadoda kunye ne-2.7 ilitha (0.59 imp gal; 0.71 amanzi aqulathe malunga ne-19 yamanzi e-US equlathe malunga ne-19 yabasetyhini). umthamo wamanzi ewonke kuvavanyo. Ngokukodwa, abafazi abakhulelweyo nabancancisayo badinga ulwelo olongezelelweyo ukuze bahlale be-hydrated. I-US Institute of Medicine icebisa ukuba, ngokomlinganiselo, amadoda asele iilitha ezi-3 (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) kunye nabasetyhini iilitha ezi-2.2 (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); abasetyhini abakhulelweyo bafanele banyuse umthamo wokusela ukuya kutsho kwiilitha ezi-2.4 (0.53 imp gal; 0.63 US gal) yaye amabhinqa ancancisayo afanele afumane iilitha ezi-3 (iikomityi ezili-12), ekubeni kulahleka ulwelo oluninzi ngakumbi ebudeni bokuncancisa. Kwakhona kuphawulwe ukuba ngokuqhelekileyo, malunga ne-20% yamanzi athathwayo avela ekutyeni, ngelixa abanye bevela emanzini okusela kunye neziselo (kubandakanywa ne-caffeinated). Amanzi akhutshwa emzimbeni ngeendlela ezininzi; ngomchamo nangelindle, ngokubila, nangokuphefumla umphunga wamanzi emphefumlweni. Ngokuzibhokoxa ngokomzimba kunye nokuvezwa kobushushu, ilahleko yamanzi iya kwanda kwaye iimfuno zolwelo lwemihla ngemihla zinokunyuka ngokunjalo. Abantu bafuna amanzi anobumdaka obumbalwa. Ukungcola okuqhelekileyo kubandakanya iintsimbi ezifana nobhedu kunye nelothe; iikhompawundi zeekhemikhali ezifana nezibulali zinambuzane, iPFAS, okanye iblitshi; kunye neebhaktheriya ezinobungozi, ezifana neCampylobacter, E. coli O157, kunye neVibrio. Ezinye izinyibilikisi zamkelekile kwaye ziyanqweneleka ekuphuculeni incasa kunye nokubonelela ngee-electrolyte ezifunekayo. Ezothutho
Ukusetyenziswa kwemichiza
Amanzi asetyenziswa ngokubanzi kwiikhemikhali njenge-solvent okanye i-reactant kwaye ayiqhelekanga njenge-solute okanye i-catalyst. Kwiimpendulo ze-inorganic, amanzi asisinyibilikisi esiqhelekileyo, anyibilikisa iikhompawundi ezininzi ze-ionic, kunye nezinye iikhompawundi zepolar ezifana ne-ammonia kunye neekhompawundi ezinxulumene ngokusondeleyo namanzi. Ekuphenduleni kwezinto eziphilayo, ayiqhelekanga ukusetyenziswa njenge-solvent reaction, kuba ayinyibiliki i-reactants kakuhle kwaye i-amphoteric (i-acidic kunye nesiseko) kunye ne-nucleophilic. Nangona kunjalo, ezi zakhiwo ngamanye amaxesha ziyanqweneleka. Kwakhona, ukukhawuleza kwee-Diels-Alder reactions ngamanzi kuye kwabonwa. Amanzi anqabileyo asanda kuba ngumxholo wophando. Amanzi azaliswe yioksijini egqithisileyo atshisa izingcolisi eziphilayo ngokufanelekileyo. Ukutshintshana kobushushu
Amanzi kunye nomphunga lulwelo oluqhelekileyo olusetyenziselwa ukutshintshiselana kobushushu, ngenxa yokufumaneka kwayo kunye nomthamo omkhulu wobushushu, zombini ukupholisa nokufudumeza. Amanzi apholileyo asenokufumaneka ngokwemvelo echibini okanye elwandle. Isebenza ngakumbi ukuthutha ubushushu ngokwenziwa ngumphunga kunye nokujiya kwamanzi ngenxa yobushushu bawo obukhulu obufihlakeleyo bomphunga. Into engalunganga kukuba isinyithi esiqhele ukufunyanwa kumashishini afana nentsimbi kunye nobhedu zifakwa ioksijini ngokukhawuleza ngamanzi angacocwanga kunye nomphunga. Phantse kuzo zonke izikhululo zamandla obushushu, amanzi asetyenziswa njengolwelo olusebenzayo (lusetyenziswa kwindawo evaliweyo phakathi kwebhoyila, i-injini yomphunga, kunye necondenser), kunye nesipholisi (esisetyenziselwa ukutshintshisa ubushushu benkunkuma kumzimba wamanzi okanye buthwalwe bube ngumphunga kwinqaba yokupholisa). EUnited States, amaziko ombane okupholisa yeyona ndlela isetyenziswa kakhulu yamanzi. Kwishishini lamandla enyukliya, amanzi anokusetyenziswa njengemodareyitha ye-neutron. Kuninzi lwezixhobo zenyukliya, amanzi azizipholisi kunye nemodareyitha. Oku kunika into yomlinganiselo wokhuseleko we-passive, njengoko ukususa amanzi kwi-reactor nako kuthoba intshukumo yenyukliya. Nangona kunjalo ezinye iindlela zikhethwa ngokunqanda ukusabela kwaye kukhethwa ukugcina umbindi wenyukliya ugqunywe ngamanzi ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukupholisa okwaneleyo. Iingqwalasela zomlilo
Amanzi anobushushu obuphezulu bomphunga kwaye akanenzi kangako, nto leyo ewenza abe lulwelo olulungileyo lokucima umlilo. Umphunga wamanzi uhambisa ubushushu kude nomlilo. Kuyingozi ukusebenzisa amanzi kwimililo ebandakanya i-oyile kunye nezinyibilikisi eziphilayo kuba izinto ezininzi eziphilayo zidada emanzini kwaye amanzi athanda ukusasazeka ulwelo oluvuthayo. Ukusetyenziswa kwamanzi ekulweni nomlilo kufuneka kwakhona kuthathelwe ingqalelo iingozi zokuqhuma komphunga, onokuthi kwenzeke xa amanzi esetyenziswa kwimililo eshushu kakhulu kwiindawo ezivaliweyo, kunye nogqabhuko-dubulo lwe-hydrogen, xa izinto ezisabela emanzini, njengesinyithi okanye i-carbon eshushu njengamalahle, i-charcoal, okanye i-coke graphite, ibolisa amanzi, ivelisa igesi yamanzi. Amandla okuqhuma okunjalo abonwa kwintlekele yaseChernobyl, nangona amanzi abandakanyekayo kule meko ayengaveli ekulweni nomlilo kodwa kwinkqubo yokupholisa amanzi kwi-reactor. Ugqabhuko-dubulo lomphunga lwenzekile xa ukutshisa okugqithisileyo kombindi kubangele ukuba amanzi adane abe ngumphunga. Ugqabhuko-dubulo lwehydrogen lunokuba lwenzeke ngenxa yokusabela phakathi komphunga kunye ne-zirconium eshushu. Ezinye ii-metallic oxides, ingakumbi ezo zealkali zesinyithi kunye ne-alkaline earth metals, zivelisa ubushushu obuninzi ngokusabela emanzini kangangokuba ingozi yomlilo inokuvela. I-alkaline earth oxide quicklime, ekwabizwa ngokuba yi-calcium oxide, yinto eveliswa ngobuninzi nesoloko ithuthwa ngeengxowa zephepha. Ukuba ezi zifakwe emanzini, zinokuvutha njengoko iziqulatho zazo zisabela ngamanzi. Ukuzonwabisa
Abantu basebenzisa amanzi kwiinjongo ezininzi zokuzonwabisa, kunye nokwenza umthambo kunye nemidlalo. Ezinye zezi ziquka ukuqubha, ukutyibilika emanzini, ukutyibilika amaphenyane, ukusefa kunye nokuntywila. Ukongeza, eminye imidlalo, efana nehoki yomkhenkce kunye ne-ice skating, idlalwa emkhenkceni. ILakesides, iilwandle kunye neepaki zamanzi ziindawo ezithandwayo abantu abangaya kuzo ukuze baphumle kwaye banandiphe ukuzonwabisa. Abaninzi bafumanisa isandi kunye nokubonakala kwamanzi aqukuqelayo ukuzola, kwaye imithombo kunye nezinye izakhiwo zamanzi aqukuqelayo ziyimihombiso eyaziwayo. Abanye bagcina iintlanzi kunye nezinye iintyatyambo kunye nezilwanyana ngaphakathi kwe-aquariums okanye amachibi omboniso, ulonwabo kunye nobuqabane. Abantu bakwasebenzisa amanzi kwimidlalo yekhephu, efana nokutyibiliza ekhephini, ukutyibiliza, ukutyibilika ekhephuni okanye kwi-snowboarding, efuna amanzi abe kwiqondo lobushushu eliphantsi nokuba ngumkhenkce okanye akhazimliswe kwikhephu. Ushishino lwamanzi
Ishishini lamanzi libonelela ngamanzi okusela kunye neenkonzo zamanzi amdaka (kubandakanywa nokucocwa kogutyulo) kumakhaya nakumashishini. Izibonelelo zobonelelo lwamanzi ziquka amaqula amanzi, amaqula okuvunwa kwamanzi emvula, uthungelwano lonikezelo lwamanzi, kunye nezixhobo zokucoca amanzi, iitanki zamanzi, iinqaba zamanzi, imibhobho yamanzi kuquka imisele emidala. Iijenereyitha zamanzi asemkhathini ziyaphuhliswa. Amanzi okusela ahlala eqokelelwa emithonjeni, akhutshwe kwimithombo eyenziweyo (amaqula) asemhlabeni, okanye ampontshwe kumachibi kunye nemilambo. Ukwakha amaqula angakumbi kwiindawo ezifanelekileyo kuyindlela enokwenzeka yokuvelisa amanzi amaninzi, kucingwa ukuba loo maqula anokuhambisa amanzi awaneleyo. Eminye imithombo yamanzi iquka ukuqokelelwa kwamanzi emvula. Amanzi anokufuna ukucocwa ukuze asetyenziswe ngabantu. Oku kunokubandakanya ukususwa kwezinto ezinganyibilikiyo, izinto ezinyibilikayo kunye neentsholongwane eziyingozi. Iindlela ezidumileyo kuhluzo ngesanti esusa kuphela izinto ezinganyibilikiyo, ngelixa i-chlorination kunye nokubilisa zibulala iintsholongwane eziyingozi. I-distillation yenza yonke imisebenzi emithathu. Ubuchule obuphezulu bukhona, obufana ne- reverse osmosis. Ukukhutshwa kwetyuwa kumanzi olwandle amaninzi sisisombululo esixabisa kakhulu esisetyenziswa kwiindawo eziyimiqwebedu elunxwemeni. Ukuhanjiswa kwamanzi okusela kwenziwa ngeendlela zikamasipala zamanzi, ukuhanjiswa kwetanki okanye njengamanzi asebhotileni. Oorhulumente kumazwe amaninzi baneenkqubo zokusasaza amanzi kubantu abasweleyo ngaphandle kwentlawulo. Ukunciphisa ukusetyenziswa kwamanzi okusela (okuselwayo) kuphela ukuba asetyenziswe ngabantu kuyenye indlela. Kwezinye izixeko ezifana neHong Kong, amanzi olwandle asetyenziswa kakhulu ekugungxuleni izindlu zangasese kwisixeko siphela ukuze kugcinwe ubutyebi bamanzi acocekileyo. Amanzi angcolisekileyo asenokuba yeyona ndlela inkulu yokusetyenziswa kakubi kwamanzi; ukusa kwinqanaba lokuba isingcolisi sithintele ezinye iindlela zokusetyenziswa kwamanzi, siba yinkcitho yobutyebi, kungakhathaliseki ukuba luyintoni na uncedo kumngcolisi. Njengezinye iintlobo zongcoliseko, oku akungeni ngxelo esemgangathweni yeendleko zemarike, kucingwa njengezinto zangaphandle apho imarike ayinakuphendula. Ngaloo ndlela abanye abantu bahlawula ixabiso longcoliseko lwamanzi, ngelixa iingeniso zeefemu zabucala zingaphinde zisasazwe kubemi basekuhlaleni, amaxhoba olu ngcoliseko. Amachiza asetyenziswa ngabantu ahlala ephelela emanzini kwaye anokuba neziphumo ezibi kubomi basemanzini ukuba aqokelelana ngokwebhayoloji kwaye ukuba awaboli. Amanzi amdaka kamasipala nawemizi-mveliso aqhele ukucocwa kwiindawo zokucocwa kwamanzi amdaka. Unciphiso lokubaleka komhlaba ongcolisekileyo kulungiswa ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo zokuthintela kunye nonyango. Izicelo zamashishini
Iinkqubo ezininzi zemizi-mveliso zixhomekeke ekuphenduleni kusetyenziswa iikhemikhali ezinyibilikiswe emanzini, ukunqunyanyiswa kwezinto eziqinileyo kwi-slurries yamanzi okanye ukusebenzisa amanzi ukunyibilikisa nokukhupha izinto, okanye ukuhlamba iimveliso okanye ukusetyenzwa kwezixhobo. Iinkqubo ezifana nokumbiwa kwemigodi, ukucolwa kweekhemikhali, ukuxutywa kwe-pulp, ukuveliswa kwephepha, ukuveliswa kwelaphu, ukudaya, ukuprinta, kunye nokupholisa izixhobo zombane zisebenzisa amanzi amaninzi, zifuna umthombo wamanzi ozinikeleyo, kwaye zihlala zibangela ukungcoliseka okukhulu kwamanzi. Amanzi asetyenziswa ekwenzeni umbane. Umbane owenziwe ngamanzi ngombane ofumaneka kumandla ombane. Amandla ombane owenziwe ngamanzi aphuma emanzini aqhuba i-iturbine yamanzi edityaniswe kwijenereyitha. Umbane owenziwe ngamanzi ngumthombo wamandla onexabiso eliphantsi, ongangcolisi, ohlaziyekayo. Amandla anikezelwa ngokushukuma kwamanzi. Ngokuqhelekileyo idama lakhiwa emlanjeni, lidala ichibi elenziweyo emva kwalo. Amanzi aqukuqela ephuma echibini anyanzeliswa ngee turbines ezijika iijenereyitha. Amanzi anoxinzelelo asetyenziselwa ukuqhushumba kwamanzi kunye nabasiki bejethi bamanzi. Imipu yamanzi yoxinzelelo oluphezulu isetyenziselwa ukusika ngokuchanekileyo. Isebenza kakuhle kakhulu, ikhuselekile noko, kwaye ayinabungozi kokusingqongileyo. Ikwasetyenziswa ekupholiseni koomatshini ukunqanda ukufudumeza, okanye ukuthintela iisarha ukuba zingatshisi kakhulu. Amanzi nawo asetyenziswa kwiinkqubo ezininzi zoshishino kunye noomatshini, njenge-injini ye-steam kunye ne-heater exchanger, ngaphezu kokusetyenziswa kwayo njenge-solvent yeekhemikhali. Ukukhutshwa kwamanzi angacocwanga kusetyenziso lwemizi-mveliso lungcoliseko. Ungcoliseko lubandakanya izinyibilikisi ezikhutshiweyo (ungcoliseko lwekhemikhali) kunye namanzi akhutshiweyo apholisayo (ungcoliseko lobushushu). Ishishini lifuna amanzi acocekileyo kwizicelo ezininzi kwaye isebenzisa iindlela ezahlukeneyo zokucoca zombini kunikezelo lwamanzi kunye nokukhupha. Icandelo ledijithali, ngakumbi ubukrelekrele be-Artificial basebenzisa amanzi amaninzi, ngoko ke ukwandiswa kwe-AI "kunokubeka emngciphekweni ukhuseleko lwamanzi lwehlabathi kunye nelizwe". Ukulungiswa kokutya
Ukubilisa, ukutshisa, kunye nokubilisa ziindlela zokupheka ezithandwayo ezihlala zifuna ukuntywiliselwa ukutya emanzini okanye imeko yayo enegesi, umphunga. Amanzi asetyenziselwa ukuhlamba izitya. Amanzi nawo adlala indima ebalulekileyo kwinkalo yenzululwazi yokutya. Izinyibilikisi ezifana neetyuwa kunye neeswekile ezifumaneka emanzini zichaphazela iimpawu ezibonakalayo zamanzi. Amanqaku abilayo kunye nokukhenkceza kwamanzi achaphazelekayo yi-solutes, kunye noxinzelelo lomoya, oluthi luchaphazeleke ngokuphakama. Amanzi abila kumaqondo obushushu asezantsi kunye noxinzelelo lomoya olusezantsi olwenzeka kwiindawo eziphakamileyo. I-mole enye ye-sucrose (iswekile) ngekhilogram nganye yamanzi inyusa iqondo lokubila lamanzi nge-0.51 °C (0.918 °F), kunye ne-mole enye yetyuwa ngekhilogram yokunyusa iqondo lokubila nge-1.02 °C (1.836 °F); ngokufanayo, ukwandisa inani lamasuntswana anyityilisiweyo kwehlisa iqondo lomkhenkce wamanzi. I-Solutes emanzini ichaphazela umsebenzi wamanzi ochaphazela ukuphendulwa kweekhemikhali ezininzi kunye nokukhula kwee-microbes ekutyeni. Umsebenzi wamanzi unokuchazwa njengomlinganiselo woxinzelelo lomphunga wamanzi kwisisombululo kuxinzelelo lomphunga wamanzi acocekileyo. I-Solutes emanzini aphantsi komsebenzi wamanzi-oku kubalulekile ukwazi ngenxa yokuba ukwanda kwebhaktheriya kuyeka kumanqanaba aphantsi omsebenzi wamanzi. Ukukhula kwe-microbial akuchaphazeli kuphela ukhuseleko lokutya, kodwa kunye nokugcinwa kunye nobomi beshelufu yokutya. Ubulukhuni bamanzi bukwangumba obalulekileyo ekusetyenzweni kokutya kwaye kunokutshintshwa okanye kunyangwe ngokusebenzisa inkqubo yokutshintshiselana ngemichiza ye-ion. Inokuchaphazela kakhulu umgangatho wemveliso, kunye nokudlala indima yococeko. Ubunzima bamanzi buhlelwa ngokusekwe kugxininiso lwecalcium carbonate emanzini. Amanzi ahlelwa njengento ethambileyo ukuba iqulethe ngaphantsi kwe-100 mg / L (UK) okanye ngaphantsi kwe-60 mg / L (US). Ngokwengxelo epapashwe ngumbutho weWater Footprint ngo-2010, ikhilogram enye yenkomo ifuna iilitha ezingamawaka ali-15 (3.3×10^3 imp gal; 4.0×10^3 US gal) zamanzi; nangona kunjalo, ababhali baphinda bacacise ukuba lo ngumlinganiselo wehlabathi jikelele kunye nezinto ezithintekayo zinquma ubungakanani bamanzi asetyenziswa kwimveliso yenkomo. Kwindalo iphela
Amanzi amaninzi akwindalo iphela avela ngenxa yokuvela kweenkwenkwezi. Ukubunjwa kweenkwenkwezi kuhamba kunye nomoya wangaphandle onamandla wegesi kunye nothuli. Xa oku kuphuma kwezinto ekugqibeleni kuchaphazela igesi ejikelezileyo, amaza othusayo adalwe acinezele kwaye afudumale igesi. Amanzi ajongwayo aveliswa ngokukhawuleza kule gesi eshinyeneyo eshushu. Ngomhla wama-22 kuJulayi 2011, ingxelo yachaza ukufunyanwa kwelifu elikhulu lomphunga wamanzi eliqulathe "i-140 yezigidi zezigidi zezigidi zamanzi ngaphezu kwazo zonke iilwandle zoMhlaba zidityanisiwe" malunga ne-quasar efumaneka kwiminyaka eyi-12 yezigidigidi zokukhanya ukusuka eMhlabeni. Ngokutsho kwabaphandi, "ukufunyanwa kubonisa ukuba amanzi sele exhaphake kwindalo yonke phantse yonke indawo". Amanzi afunyenwe kumafu aphakathi kweenkwenkwezi ngaphakathi kweMilky Way. Amanzi asenokuba maninzi kweminye iminyele, kuba iinxalenye zawo, ihydrogen, neoksijini, ziphakathi kwezona zinto zininzi kwindalo iphela. I-Dihydrogen monoxide parody
'Idihydrogen monoxide' ligama lekhemikhali elichaneke ngokobuchule kodwa elingafane lisetyenziswe lamanzi. Eli gama liye lasetyenziswa kuthotho lweenkohliso kunye namaqhalo ahlekisa ukungakwazi ukufunda nokubhala kwezenzululwazi.
