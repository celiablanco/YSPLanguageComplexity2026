Amazi ni organic organique hamwe na formulaire ya H2O. Nibintu bisobanutse, bitaryoshye, bidafite impumuro nziza, kandi hafi yimiti idafite ibara. Nibintu nyamukuru bigize imigezi yisi, ibiyaga, ninyanja. Amazi nayo ni amazi y'ibinyabuzima byose bizwi, aho akora nk'umuti. Amazi, kuba molekile ya polar, ihura na hydrogène ikomeye ya intermolecular ihuza uruhare runini mumiterere yumubiri nubumara. Nibyingenzi muburyo bwose buzwi bwubuzima, nubwo budatanga ingufu zibiribwa cyangwa kuba micronutrient organic. Bitewe no kuba mu binyabuzima byose, imiterere y’imiti, ubwinshi bw’isi yose, hamwe n’ubuharike bukomeye ugereranije n’ubunini bwayo buto, amazi bakunze kwitwa "solvent rusange". Kuberako ubushyuhe bwisi nubushyuhe bwisi biri hafi yikubye gatatu amazi, amazi abaho kwisi nkikintu gikomeye, amazi, na gaze. Ikora imvura muburyo bwimvura na aerosole muburyo bwigihu. Ibicu bigizwe nibitonyanga byahagaritswe byamazi nubura, imiterere yacyo. Iyo igabanijwe neza, urubura rwa kirisiti irashobora kugwa muburyo bwa shelegi. Amazi ya gaze ni amazi cyangwa imyuka y'amazi. Amazi afite hafi 71% yubuso bwisi, hamwe ninyanja ninyanja bigize igice kinini cyamazi (hafi 96.5%). Ibice bito by'amazi bibaho nk'amazi yo mu butaka (1.7%), mu bibarafu no mu rubura rwa Antarctica na Greenland (1.7%), no mu kirere nk'umwuka, ibicu (bigizwe n'ibarafu n'amazi y'amazi yahagaritswe mu kirere), n'imvura (0.001%). Amazi agenda akomeza kunyura mumuzenguruko wamazi yo guhumeka, transpiration (evapotranspiration), kwegeranya, kugwa, no gutemba, ubusanzwe bigera ku nyanja. Amazi afite uruhare runini mubukungu bwisi. Hafi 70% y'amazi meza akoreshwa n'abantu bajya mubuhinzi. Kuroba mu munyu n’amazi meza byahoze, kandi bikomeje kuba isoko y’ibiribwa mu bice byinshi by’isi, bitanga 6.5% bya poroteyine ku isi. Byinshi mubucuruzi burebure bwibicuruzwa (nka peteroli, gaze gasanzwe, nibicuruzwa byakozwe) bitwarwa nubwato binyuze mumyanyanja, inzuzi, ibiyaga, n'imigezi. Amazi menshi, urubura, hamwe na parike bikoreshwa mugukonjesha no gushyushya inganda no munzu. Amazi ni umusemburo mwiza kubintu byinshi, minerval na organic; nkibyo, ikoreshwa cyane mubikorwa byinganda no muguteka no gukaraba. Amazi, urubura, na shelegi nabyo ni ingenzi muri siporo nubundi buryo bwo kwidagadura, nko koga, kunezeza ubwato, gusiganwa mu bwato, koga, kuroba siporo, kwibira, gusiganwa ku rubura, gusiganwa ku rubura, no gusiganwa ku maguru. Etymology
Ijambo amazi rikomoka mu cyongereza cya kera wæter, kiva muri Proto-Ikidage * watar (isoko nayo ya kera ya Saxon watar, wetir ya kera ya Frisiyani, amazi yo mu Buholandi, Wazzar ya kera yo mu Budage, Umudage Wasser, vatn, Gothique 𐍅𐌰𐍄𐍉 (wato)), ukomoka kuri Proto-Indo-Burayi * wod-cyangwa, uburyo bwometse ku mizi * wed- (') amazi; Menya kandi, ukoresheje umuzi w'Ubuhinde n'Uburayi, hamwe n'Ikigereki ύδωρ (ýdor; ukomoka mu Kigereki cya kera ὕδωρ (hýdōr), aho icyongereza 'hydro-'), Ikirusiya вода́ (vodá), Irlande uisce, na ujë ya Alubaniya. Ibyiza
Amazi (H2O) ni inkingi ya polar. Ku bushyuhe bwicyumba ni uburyohe butagira uburyohe kandi butagira impumuro nziza, hafi idafite ibara hamwe nubururu. Hydrogen yoroshye cyane ya chalcogenide, kugeza ubu ni uruganda rw’imiti rwizwe cyane kandi rimwe na rimwe ruvugwa ko ari "umuti rusange" kubera ubushobozi bwo gushonga ibintu byinshi kuruta andi mazi yose, nubwo bikennye mu gushonga ibintu bidafite inkingi. Ibi bituma iba "solvent of life": mubyukuri, amazi nkuko aboneka muri kamere hafi ya yose arimo ibintu bitandukanye byashonze, kandi harasabwa intambwe zidasanzwe kugirango tubone amazi meza yimiti. Amazi nicyo kintu cyonyine kibaho nkikintu gikomeye, amazi, na gaze mubihe bisanzwe byisi. Ibihugu
Hamwe na oxyde, amazi ni rimwe mu mazina abiri yemewe y’imiti ya H2O; nicyiciro cyamazi ya H2O. Ibindi bintu bibiri bihuriweho n’ibintu byamazi nicyiciro gikomeye, aricyo rubura, nicyiciro cya gaze, imyuka yamazi cyangwa umwuka. Kwiyongera cyangwa kuvanaho ubushyuhe birashobora gutera impinduka zicyiciro: gukonjesha (amazi kurubura), gushonga (urubura kumazi), guhumeka (amazi kumazi), kondegene (imyuka kumazi), sublimation (ice to vapor) hamwe no gushira (vapor to ice). Ubucucike
Amazi ni kimwe mubintu bisanzwe bisanzwe byaguka kandi bigahinduka bike nkuko bikonje mubushuhe bumwe; nikintu cyonyine kizwi mubisanzwe kibaho kibikora mugihe cyamazi. Byongeye kandi, ntibisanzwe kuko iba nkeya cyane nkuko ikonja, nubwo idasanzwe muri urwo rwego. Ku muvuduko wa atm 1, igera ku bunini bwa 999.972 kg / m3 (62.4262 lb / cu ft) kuri 3.98 ° C (39.16 ° F). Munsi yubushyuhe, ariko hejuru yubukonje bwa 0 ° C (32 ° F), amazi araguka (kuba make) kugeza ageze aho akonje, aho ubwinshi bwayo mugice cyamazi ari 999.8 kg / m3 (62.4155 lb / cu ft). Amazi akonje kugeza kuri 3,98 ° C (39.16 ° F), amazi aragabanuka. Iyo ikonje igahinduka urubura, amazi yaguka hafi 9%, akagera ku bucucike bwa kg 917 / m3 (57,25 lb / cu ft). Uku kwaguka kurashobora kugira igitutu kinini, guturika imiyoboro no gutobora amabuye. Nkikomeye, yerekana imyitwarire isanzwe yo kwandura no kurushaho kuba mwinshi uko ikonje. Iyi miterere idasanzwe yubushyuhe ifite ingaruka zingenzi mubuzima bwisi. Mu kiyaga cyangwa inyanja, amazi kuri 4 ° C (39 ° F) arohama hasi, kandi urubura ruba hejuru, rureremba hejuru y'amazi. Iyi barafu irinda amazi hepfo, ikayirinda gukonja cyane. Hatabayeho ubwo burinzi, ibinyabuzima byinshi byo mu mazi bituye mu biyaga byarimbuka mugihe cyitumba. Byongeye kandi, iyi myitwarire idasanzwe nigice cyingenzi cyizunguruka ya termohaline ikwirakwiza ubushyuhe mu nyanja yisi. Magnetism
Amazi ni ibikoresho bya diamagnetic. Nubwo imikoranire idakomeye, hamwe na magnesi zidasanzwe zirashobora kugera kumikoranire igaragara. Inzibacyuho

Nyamara, no munsi yumwanya utetse, amazi arashobora guhinduka kumyuka hejuru yubushyuhe bwayo (guhumeka mumazi yose azwi nko guteka). Sublimation no kubitsa nabyo bibaho hejuru. Kurugero, ubukonje bushyirwa hejuru yubukonje mugihe urubura rwa shelegi ruba rushyizwe kumurongo wa aerosol cyangwa nucleus. Mugihe cyo gukonjesha-gukama, ibiryo birakonjeshwa hanyuma bikabikwa kumuvuduko muke kuburyo urubura hejuru yacyo rugabanuka. Gushonga no guteka biterwa nigitutu. Guteka murwego rwo hejuru bifata igihe kirekire kuruta guteka kurwego rwinyanja. Kurugero, kuri metero 1.524 (5.000 ft), igihe cyo guteka kigomba kongerwa na kane kugirango ugere kubisubizo byifuzwa. Ibinyuranye, guteka birashobora gukoreshwa kugirango ugabanye igihe cyo guteka uzamura ubushyuhe. Mu cyuho, amazi azateka kubushyuhe bwicyumba. Ingingo eshatu kandi zikomeye
Ku gipimo cyerekana ubushyuhe / ubushyuhe (reba ishusho), hariho imirongo itandukanya bikomeye imyuka, imyuka iva mumazi, namazi nibikomeye. Ibi bihurira kumwanya umwe bita triple point, aho ibyiciro bitatu byose bishobora kubana. Ingingo eshatu ziri ku bushyuhe bwa 273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F) n'umuvuduko wa pasikali 611.657 (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); ni umuvuduko wo hasi amazi ashobora kubaho. Kugeza muri 2019, ingingo eshatu zakoreshejwe mu gusobanura ubushyuhe bwa Kelvin. Icyiciro cy'amazi / imyuka irangirira kuri 647.096 K (373.946 ° C; 705.103 ° F) na megapascal 22.064 (3,200.1 psi; 217,75 atm). Ibi bizwi nkingingo ikomeye. Ku bushyuhe bwinshi no kotsa igitutu ibice byamazi nibyuka bigira icyiciro gikomeza cyitwa fluid supercritical fluid. Irashobora guhindagurika buhoro buhoro cyangwa kwagurwa hagati ya gaze na gaze nubucucike; imiterere yacyo (itandukanye cyane n’amazi y’ibidukikije) yunvikana. Kurugero, kumuvuduko ukwiye nubushyuhe burashobora kuvanga mubwisanzure hamwe nibintu bitarimo inkingi, harimo nibintu byinshi kama. Ibi bituma bigira akamaro mubikorwa bitandukanye birimo amashanyarazi yubushyuhe bwo hejuru kandi nkibidukikije byangiza ibidukikije cyangwa umusemburo mubitekerezo byimiti irimo ibinyabuzima. Mu mwenda w'isi, ikora nk'umuti mugihe cyo gukora amabuye y'agaciro, gushonga no guta. Ibyiciro bya barafu n'amazi
Ubwoko busanzwe bwa barafu hejuru yisi ni ice Ih, icyiciro kigizwe na kristu hamwe na santimetero esheshatu. Undi ufite cubic kristaline, ice Ic, irashobora kugaragara mukirere cyo hejuru. Mugihe umuvuduko wiyongera, urubura rukora izindi nyubako za kirisiti. Kuva 2024, makumyabiri byemejwe mubigeragezo nibindi byinshi byahanuwe mubyukuri. Ubwoko bwa cumi n'umunani bwa barafu, urubura XVIII, icyiciro cya ice-centre-cubic, superionic ice icyiciro, cyavumbuwe mugihe igitonyanga cyamazi cyatewe numuvuduko ukabije wazamuye umuvuduko wamazi kuri miriyoni yikirere hamwe nubushyuhe bwacyo kuri dogere ibihumbi, bikavamo imiterere ya atome ya ogisijeni ikaze aho atome ya hydrogène yatembaga mu bwisanzure. Iyo ushyizwe hagati ya graphene, urubura rugizwe na kare. Ibisobanuro birambuye kumiterere yimiti yamazi yamazi ntabwo byumvikana neza; ibitekerezo bimwe byerekana ko imyitwarire yayo idasanzwe iterwa no kubaho kwa leta ebyiri zamazi. Kuryoherwa no kunuka
Amazi meza asobanurwa nkuburyohe kandi butagira impumuro nziza, nubwo abantu bafite sensor zihariye zishobora kumva ko hariho amazi mumunwa wabo, kandi ibikeri bizwi ko bishobora kunuka. Nyamara, amazi ava mumasoko asanzwe (harimo n'amazi yubutare) mubisanzwe afite ibintu byinshi byashonze bishobora kuguha uburyohe butandukanye numunuko. Abantu nandi matungo byateje imbere ibyumviro bibafasha gusuzuma ubushobozi bwamazi kugirango birinde amazi arimo umunyu cyangwa putrid. Ibara n'imiterere
Amazi meza ni ubururu bugaragara kubera kwinjiza urumuri mukarere c. 600-800 nm. Ibara rishobora kugaragara byoroshye mubirahuri byamazi-ashyizwe inyuma yera yera, kumanywa. Ibice byingenzi byo kwinjiza bishinzwe ibara ni hejuru ya O - H irambuye. Imbaraga zigaragara zamabara ziyongera hamwe nuburebure bwinkingi yamazi, ukurikiza amategeko ya Byeri. Ibi biranakoreshwa, kurugero, hamwe na pisine yo koga mugihe isoko yumucyo ari urumuri rwizuba rugaragarira mumatafari yera ya pisine. Muri kamere, ibara rishobora kandi guhinduka kuva mubururu ugahinduka icyatsi bitewe no kuba hari ibimera byahagaritswe cyangwa algae. Mu nganda, hafi-ya-infragre ya spekitroscopi ikoreshwa hamwe nigisubizo cyamazi kuko ubukana bwinshi bwamazi yo hepfo yamazi bivuze ko cuvettes yikirahure ifite inzira ndende-ndende ishobora gukoreshwa. Kugirango witegereze uburyo bwo kurambura ibintu byingenzi byamazi cyangwa igisubizo cyamazi mukarere kangana na cm 3.500 - 1 (2,85 mm) hakenewe inzira y'uburebure bwa metero 25. Nanone, cuvette igomba kuba ibonerana hafi cm 3500 - 1 kandi idashonga mumazi; calcium fluoride nikintu kimwe gikunze gukoreshwa kuri windows ya cuvette hamwe nibisubizo byamazi. Ihindagurika ryibikorwa bya Raman rishobora kugaragara hamwe, kurugero, ingero ya cm 1. Ibimera byo mu mazi, algae, n’ibindi binyabuzima bifotora bishobora gutura mu mazi agera kuri metero amagana, kubera ko izuba rishobora kubageraho. Mubyukuri ntamucyo wizuba ugera mubice byinyanja munsi ya metero 1.300 zubujyakuzimu. Igipimo cyo kuvunika amazi y’amazi (1.333 kuri 20 ° C (68 ° F)) kiri hejuru cyane y’ikirere (1.0), gisa n'icya alkane na Ethanol, ariko kiri munsi ya glycerol (1.473), benzene (1.501), karubone disulfide (1.627), n'ubwoko busanzwe bw'ikirahure (1.4 kugeza 1.6). Igipimo cyo kugabanya urubura (1.31) kiri munsi y’amazi y’amazi. Ibice bya polarike
Muri molekile y'amazi, atome ya hydrogen ikora inguni ya 104.5 ° hamwe na atome ya ogisijeni. Atome ya hydrogène yegereye impande ebyiri za tetrahedron yibanze kuri ogisijeni. Ku zindi mfuruka ebyiri ni jyenyine ya valence electron zititabira guhuza. Muri tetrahedron itunganijwe neza, atome zaba zifite inguni ya 109.5 °, ariko kwanga hagati yabantu bonyine ni byinshi kuruta kwanga hagati ya atome ya hydrogen. Uburebure bwa O - H bugera kuri 0.096 nm. Ibindi bintu bifite imiterere ya tetrahedral, urugero nka metani (CH4) na hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Nyamara, ogisijeni ni electronegative kurusha ibindi bintu byinshi, bityo atome ya ogisijeni ifite umuriro mubi mugihe atome ya hydrogène yashizwemo igice. Hamwe nuburyo bugoramye, ibi biha molekile umwanya wa dipole yamashanyarazi kandi ishyirwa mubikorwa nka molekile ya polar. Amazi ni umusemburo mwiza wa polar, ushonga imyunyu myinshi na molekile ya hydrophilique nka sukari na alcool yoroshye nka Ethanol. Amazi kandi ashonga imyuka myinshi, nka ogisijeni na karuboni ya dioxyde - iyanyuma itanga fiz y'ibinyobwa bya karubone, vino ituje na byeri. Byongeye kandi, ibintu byinshi mu binyabuzima bizima, nka poroteyine, ADN na polysaccharide, bishonga mu mazi. Imikoranire hagati yamazi na subunits yibi biomacromolecules ikora protein igabanuka, guhuza ADN, nibindi bintu byingenzi mubuzima (ingaruka ya hydrophobique). Ibintu byinshi kama (nkamavuta namavuta na alkane) ni hydrophobique, ni ukuvuga kudashonga mumazi. Ibintu byinshi bidakoreshwa nabwo ntibishobora gukemuka, harimo ibyuma byinshi bya okiside, sulfide, na silicates. Guhuza hydrogen
Kubera polarite yayo, molekile y'amazi mumazi cyangwa ikomeye irashobora gukora imigozi igera kuri ine ya hydrogène hamwe na molekile zituranye. Amazi ya hydrogène akubye inshuro icumi imbaraga za Van der Waals zikurura molekile hagati yazo mumazi menshi. Ninimpamvu ituma amazi yo gushonga no guteka ari hejuru cyane ugereranije nibindi bintu bisa nka hydrogen sulfide. Basobanura kandi imbaraga zidasanzwe zidasanzwe (hafi 4.2 J / (g · K)), ubushyuhe bwa fusion (hafi 333 J / g), ubushyuhe bwuka (2257 J / g), nubushyuhe bwumuriro (hagati ya 0.561 na 0.679 W / (m · K)). Iyi miterere ituma amazi akora neza muguhindura ikirere cyisi, mukubika ubushyuhe no kuwutwara hagati yinyanja nikirere. Amazi ya hydrogène y'amazi agera kuri 23 kJ / mol (ugereranije na covalent O - H kuri 492 kJ / mol). Muri ibyo, byagereranijwe ko 90% biterwa na electrostatike, mugihe 10% isigaye ari covalent igice. Izi nkunga nizo zitera amazi hejuru yubushyuhe bwimbaraga nimbaraga za capillary. Igikorwa cya capillary bivuga imyumvire y'amazi yo kuzamuka umuyoboro muto ugereranije n'imbaraga za rukuruzi. Uyu mutungo ushingiye ku bimera byose byimitsi, nkibiti. Kwiyita
Amazi ni igisubizo kidakomeye cya hydronium hydroxide - hariho uburinganire bwa 2H2O ⇌ H3O + + OH−, bufatanije no gukemura hydronium na hydroxide ion. Amashanyarazi hamwe na electrolysis
Amazi meza afite amashanyarazi make, yiyongera hamwe no gusesagura ibintu bike bya ionic nkumunyu usanzwe. Amazi y’amazi arashobora kugabanywamo ibintu hydrogène na ogisijeni unyuramo amashanyarazi - inzira yitwa electrolysis. Kubora bisaba ingufu nyinshi zirenze ubushyuhe bwarekuwe nuburyo butandukanye (285.8 kJ / mol, cyangwa 15.9 MJ / kg). Ibikoresho bya mashini
Amazi y’amazi arashobora gufatwa nkaho adashobora guhindagurika kubwimpamvu nyinshi: kwikanyiza kwayo kuva kuri 4.4 kugeza 5.1 × 10−10 Pa - 1 mubihe bisanzwe. Ndetse no mu nyanja kuri kilometero 4 zubujyakuzimu, aho umuvuduko wa atm 400, amazi agira igabanuka rya 1.8% gusa. Ubukonje bw’amazi bugera kuri 10−3 Pa · s cyangwa 0.01 bituje kuri 20 ° C (68 ° F), kandi umuvuduko wijwi mumazi yamazi uri hagati ya metero 1400 na 1.540 kumasegonda (4,600 na 5.100 ft / s) bitewe nubushyuhe. Ijwi rikora urugendo rurerure mumazi hamwe nubwitonzi buke, cyane cyane kuri radiyo nkeya (hafi 0.03 dB / km kuri 1 kHz), umutungo ukoreshwa nabanyakanada hamwe nabantu kugirango bavugane nibidukikije (sonar). Ibikorwa
Ibintu byuma bifite amashanyarazi menshi kurusha hydrogène, cyane cyane ibyuma bya alkali kandi, ku rugero ruto, ubutaka bwa alkaline, bwimura hydrogène mu mazi, ikora hydroxide ikarekura hydrogene. Ku bushyuhe bwinshi, kokiya, ubwoko bwa karubone, ikora hamwe na parike ikora monoxyde de carbone na hydrogen. Kwisi
Hydrology ni ubushakashatsi bwimikorere, ikwirakwizwa, nubwiza bwamazi kwisi yose. Ubushakashatsi bwo gukwirakwiza amazi ni hydrography. Ubushakashatsi bwo gukwirakwiza no kugenda kwamazi yubutaka ni hydrogeologiya, ibibarafu ni glaciologiya, amazi yimbere ni limnologiya no gukwirakwiza inyanja ni inyanja. Ibikorwa byibidukikije hamwe na hydrology biri murwego rwibidukikije. Ubwinshi bwamazi aboneka hejuru, munsi, no hejuru yumubumbe witwa hydrose. Umubare w'amazi ugereranije n'isi (amazi yose yatanzwe ku isi) ni kilometero kibe miliyari 1.386 (kilometero kibe miliyoni 333). Amazi y’amazi aboneka mu mazi y’amazi, nk'inyanja, inyanja, ikiyaga, uruzi, imigezi, umuyoboro, icyuzi, cyangwa ikidendezi. Amazi menshi kwisi ni amazi yinyanja. Amazi nayo aboneka mwikirere mubihe bikomeye, byamazi, hamwe numwuka. Irahari kandi nkamazi yubutaka mumazi. Amazi ni ingenzi mubikorwa byinshi bya geologiya. Amazi yo mu butaka aboneka mu bitare byinshi, kandi umuvuduko w'aya mazi yo mu butaka ugira ingaruka ku buryo bwo gukosorwa. Amazi muri mantine ashinzwe gushonga bitanga ibirunga kuri zone subduction. Ku isi, amazi ni ingenzi haba mubihe byimiterere nubumenyi bwikirere. Amazi, kandi ku rugero ruto ariko aracyafite akamaro, urubura, nabwo bushinzwe ubwinshi bwubwikorezi bwimitsi iboneka hejuru yisi. Gushyira imyanda itwarwa ikora ubwoko bwinshi bwibitare byimitsi, bigizwe na geologiya yamateka yisi. Inzira y'amazi
Umuzenguruko w'amazi (uzwi mu buhanga nka hydrologic cycle) ni uguhana guhoraho kw'amazi muri hydrose, hagati yikirere, amazi yubutaka, amazi yo hejuru, amazi yubutaka, nibimera. Amazi agenda atembera muri buri karere muri uruziga rwamazi agizwe nuburyo bukurikira bwo kwimura:

Amazi yuzuye mu kirere arashobora kandi kugabanya urumuri rw'izuba kugira ngo atange umukororombya. Amazi atemba akusanyiriza hejuru y'amazi atemba mu nzuzi. Umutungo wamazi ni umutungo kamere wamazi ashobora kugirira abantu akamaro, urugero nkisoko yo gutanga amazi yo kunywa cyangwa amazi yo kuhira. Amazi abaho nk "ibigega" na "bitemba". Amazi arashobora kubikwa nk'ibiyaga, imyuka y'amazi, amazi yo mu butaka cyangwa imigezi, hamwe na barafu na shelegi. Mubunini bwamazi meza yisi yose, abagera kuri 69 ku ijana babikwa mu bibarafu no mu rubura ruhoraho; 30 ku ijana biri mu mazi yo mu butaka; naho 1 ku ijana asigaye mu biyaga, inzuzi, ikirere, na biota. Uburebure bwigihe amazi aguma mububiko burahinduka cyane: imigezi imwe igizwe namazi yabitswe mumyaka ibihumbi, ariko ingano yikiyaga irashobora guhinduka mugihe cyigihe, igabanuka mugihe cyizuba kandi ikiyongera mugihe cyizuba. Igice kinini cyamazi yo mu turere tumwe na tumwe agizwe n’amazi yakuwe mu mazi abitswe mu bubiko, kandi iyo kubikuramo birenze kwishyurwa, ububiko buragabanuka. Bamwe bagereranya, hafi 30 ku ijana by'amazi yose akoreshwa mu kuhira imyaka aturuka ku gukuramo amazi adasubirwaho amazi yo mu butaka, bigatuma amazi yo mu butaka agabanuka. Amazi yo mu nyanja
Amazi yo mu nyanja arimo sodium ya chloride igera kuri 3,5% ugereranije, hiyongereyeho ibindi bintu bito. Imiterere yumubiri winyanja itandukanye namazi meza mubice bimwe byingenzi. Irakonja ku bushyuhe bwo hasi (hafi −1.9 ° C (28,6 ° F)) kandi ubwinshi bwayo bwiyongera hamwe no kugabanuka kwubushyuhe kugera aho bukonje, aho kugera ku bucucike bwinshi ku bushyuhe buri hejuru yubukonje. Umunyu w'amazi mu nyanja nini uratandukanye kuva 0.7% mu nyanja ya Baltique ukagera kuri 4.0% mu nyanja Itukura. (Inyanja y'Umunyu, izwiho kuba umunyu mwinshi cyane uri hagati ya 30 na 40%, ni ikiyaga cyumunyu.)
Amazi ni ukuzamuka no kugabanuka kurwego rwinyanja rwaho biterwa nimbaraga zamazi yukwezi nizuba bikora kumyanyanja. Amazi atera impinduka mubwimbike bwamazi yinyanja na estuarine kandi akabyara imigezi ihindagurika izwi kwizina ryamazi. Guhindura imiraba ikorerwa ahantu runaka nigisubizo cyimihindagurikire yukwezi nizuba ugereranije nisi hamwe ningaruka zo kuzunguruka kwisi hamwe nubwiherero bwaho. Igice cy'inyanja cyarengewe n'amazi maremare kandi kigaragara ku muhengeri muke, akarere gahuza imipaka, ni ikintu cy'ingenzi cy’ibidukikije cy’amazi yo mu nyanja. Ingaruka ku buzima
Ukurikije ibinyabuzima, amazi afite ibintu byinshi bitandukanye bifite akamaro kanini mubuzima. Irangiza iyi nshingano yemerera ibinyabuzima gukora muburyo butuma byigana. Uburyo bwose buzwi bwubuzima bushingiye kumazi. Amazi ni ingenzi haba nk'umuti ushyiramo imbaraga nyinshi z'umubiri zishonga kandi nkigice cyingenzi mubikorwa byinshi byo guhinduranya umubiri. Metabolism nigiteranyo cyuzuye cya anabolism na catabolism. Muri anabolism, amazi akurwa muri molekile (binyuze mu mbaraga zisaba reaction ya chimique enzymatique) kugirango ikure molekile nini (urugero, ibinyamisogwe, triglyceride, na proteyine zo kubika ibicanwa namakuru). Muri catabolisme, amazi akoreshwa mu guca imigozi kugirango habeho molekile nto (urugero: glucose, aside irike, na aside amine acide ikoreshwa mu bicanwa mu gukoresha ingufu cyangwa izindi ntego). Hatariho amazi, ubwo buryo bwihariye bwo guhinduranya ntibushobora kubaho. Amazi ni ingenzi kuri fotosintezeza no guhumeka. Ingirabuzimafatizo zifotora zikoresha ingufu z'izuba kugirango zigabanye hydrogène y'amazi na ogisijeni. Imbere y'izuba, hydrogène ihujwe na CO2 (yakuwe mu kirere cyangwa amazi) ikora glucose ikarekura ogisijeni. Ingirabuzimafatizo zose zikoresha ibicanwa kandi bigahindura hydrogène na karubone kugirango bigabanye ingufu z'izuba no kuvugurura amazi na CO2 mubikorwa (guhumeka selile). Amazi nayo yibanze muri aside-ishingiro itabogamye hamwe nimikorere ya enzyme. Acide, hydrogene ion (H +, ni ukuvuga umuterankunga wa proton), irashobora guteshwa agaciro na base, iyakira proton nka hydroxide ion (OH−) kugirango ikore amazi. Amazi afatwa nkaho atagira aho abogamiye, hamwe na pH (ibiti bibi bya hydrogène ion yibanze) ya 7 muburyo bwiza. Acide ifite pH agaciro kari munsi ya 7 mugihe shingiro zifite agaciro karenze 7. Imiterere yubuzima bwamazi
Amazi yo ku isi yuzuye ubuzima. Imiterere ya mbere yubuzima yagaragaye mumazi; amafi hafi ya yose aba mu mazi gusa, kandi hariho ubwoko bwinshi bw’inyamabere z’inyamabere, nka dolphine na baleine. Ubwoko bumwebumwe bwinyamaswa, nka amphibian, bumara igice cyubuzima bwabo mumazi nibice kubutaka. Ibimera nka kelp na algae bikura mumazi kandi nibyo shingiro ryibinyabuzima bimwe na bimwe byo mumazi. Ubusanzwe Plankton ni ishingiro ryuruhererekane rwibiryo byo mu nyanja. Inyamaswa zo mu mazi zigomba kubona ogisijeni kugirango zibeho, kandi zibikora muburyo butandukanye. Amafi afite ibibyimba aho kuba ibihaha, nubwo amoko amwe y’amafi, nk'amafi y'ibihaha, byombi. Inyamaswa z’inyamabere, nka dolphine, balale, otter, hamwe na kashe bigomba kugaragara buri gihe kugirango bihumeke umwuka. Amphibian bamwe bashoboye kwinjiza ogisijeni binyuze muruhu rwabo. Inyamaswa zidafite ubuzima zerekana ibintu byinshi byahinduwe kugirango zibeho mumazi ya ogisijeni nkeya harimo imiyoboro ihumeka (reba udukoko na siphon ya mollusc) na gilles (Carcinus). Nyamara, nkuko ubuzima butagira ubuzima bwagiye buhinduka ahantu h’amazi, benshi bafite umwihariko cyangwa udafite umwihariko wo guhumeka mumazi. Ingaruka kumico yabantu
Umuco wateye imbere mumateka yinzuzi ninzira nini zamazi; Mezopotamiya, imwe mu zitwa inzererezi z'umuco, yari hagati y'inzuzi nini Tigiri na Efurate; societe ya kera y'Abanyamisiri yari ishingiye kuri Nili yose. Umuco wo mu kibaya cya mbere cya Indus (nko mu 3300 BGC - nko mu 1300 BGC) wateye imbere ku mugezi wa Indus no mu nzuzi ziva muri Himalaya. Roma kandi yashinzwe ku nkombe z'umugezi wa Tiber. Imigi minini nka Rotterdam, London, Montreal, Paris, Umujyi wa New York, Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Tokiyo, Chicago, na Hong Kong babikesha gutsinda kwabo binyuze mu mazi ndetse no kwagura ubucuruzi. Ibirwa bifite ibyambu by’amazi meza, nka Singapore, byateye imbere kubwimpamvu imwe. Mu bice nka Afurika y'Amajyaruguru n'Uburasirazuba bwo Hagati, aho usanga amazi ari make, kubona amazi meza yo kunywa byari kandi bifite uruhare runini mu iterambere ry'abantu. Ubuzima n’umwanda
Amazi akwiriye kurya abantu yitwa amazi yo kunywa cyangwa amazi meza. Amazi adashobora kunyobwa arashobora gukorwa muburyo bwo kuyungurura cyangwa kuyungurura, cyangwa muburyo butandukanye. Abantu barenga miliyoni 660 ntibafite amazi meza yo kunywa. Amazi adakwiriye kunywa ariko ntabwo yangiza abantu mugihe akoreshwa mu koga cyangwa kwiyuhagira yitwa amazina atandukanye uretse amazi meza cyangwa yo kunywa, kandi rimwe na rimwe yitwa amazi meza, cyangwa "umutekano wo kwiyuhagira". Chlorine ni uruhu na mucous membrane irakara ikoreshwa mugutuma amazi meza yo koga cyangwa kunywa. Imikoreshereze yacyo ni tekiniki cyane kandi mubisanzwe ikurikiranwa namabwiriza ya leta (mubisanzwe igice 1 kuri miriyoni (ppm) kumazi yo kunywa, na 1-2 ppm ya chlorine itarakorwa hamwe numwanda wamazi yo koga). Amazi yo kwiyuhagira arashobora kubungabungwa mumiterere ya mikorobe ishimishije ukoresheje imiti yica udukoko nka chlorine cyangwa ozone cyangwa hakoreshejwe urumuri ultraviolet. Gutunganya amazi ninzira yo guhindura amazi mabi (bikunze kuba umwanda, nanone bita amazi y’amazi ya komine) mumazi ashobora gukoreshwa mubindi bikorwa. Hariho abantu miliyari 2,3 baba mu bihugu bifite amazi make, bivuze ko buri muntu yakira amazi atarenga metero kibe 1.700 (60.000 cu ft) buri mwaka. Metero kibe miliyari 380 (13 × 10 ^ 12 cu ft) y’amazi y’amazi ya komini akorwa ku isi buri mwaka. Amazi meza ni umutungo ushobora kuvugururwa, uzengurutswe na hydrologique naturel, ariko igitutu cyo kuyigeraho gituruka ku gukwirakwiza bisanzwe mu kirere no mu gihe, kwiyongera k'ubukungu bukenerwa n'ubuhinzi n'inganda, ndetse n'ubwiyongere bw'abaturage. Kugeza ubu, abantu bagera kuri miliyari ku isi ntibabura amazi meza, ahendutse. Mu 2000, Umuryango w’abibumbye washyizeho intego z’ikinyagihumbi kugira ngo amazi agabanuke mu mwaka wa 2015 umubare w’abantu ku isi hose batabona amazi meza n’isuku. Iterambere rigana kuri iyo ntego ntiryari rimeze, kandi mu 2015 Umuryango w'abibumbye wiyemeje intego z’iterambere rirambye zo kugera ku isi hose amazi meza ndetse n’isuku mu mwaka wa 2030. Ubwiza bw’amazi n’isuku mbi birica; abantu bagera kuri miliyoni eshanu bapfa ku mwaka biterwa n'indwara ziterwa n'amazi. Ishami ry'Umuryango w'Abibumbye ryita ku Buzima rivuga ko buri mwaka amazi meza ashobora gukumira impfu z'abana miliyoni 1.4. Mu bihugu biri mu nzira y'amajyambere, 90% by'amazi yose y’amakomine aracyatunganijwe mu nzuzi n’inzuzi zaho. Ibihugu bigera kuri 50, hafi kimwe cya gatatu cy’abatuye isi, na byo bifite ikibazo cy’ibura ry’amazi yo hagati cyangwa menshi kandi 17 muri byo bikuramo amazi menshi buri mwaka kuruta uko yishyurwa binyuze mu mazi asanzwe. Umuvuduko ntugira ingaruka gusa ku mazi meza yo hejuru nkinzuzi n’ibiyaga, ahubwo unangiza umutungo wamazi yubutaka. Imikoreshereze y'abantu
Ubuhinzi
Ikoreshwa ryinshi ry’amazi ni iy'ubuhinzi, harimo n’ubuhinzi bwuhira, bingana na 80 kugeza kuri 90 ku ijana by’amazi yose y’abantu. Muri Amerika, 42% by'amazi meza yakuweho kugirango akoreshwe ni kuhira imyaka, ariko igice kinini cy'amazi "yakoreshejwe" (akoreshwa kandi ntagaruke ku bidukikije) ajya mu buhinzi. Kubona amazi meza akenshi bifatwa nkibisanzwe, cyane cyane mubihugu byateye imbere byubatsemo amazi akomeye yo gukusanya, kweza, no gutanga amazi, no gukuraho amazi mabi. Ariko umuvuduko w’ubukungu, ubw’abaturage, n’ikirere uragenda wiyongera ku bibazo by’amazi, bigatuma irushanwa ryiyongera ku mutungo w’amazi meza, bituma habaho igitekerezo cy’amazi meza. Mu gihe abaturage n’ubukungu bikomeje kwiyongera, ikoreshwa ry’inyama zifite inyota y’amazi riragenda ryiyongera, kandi ibyifuzo bishya byiyongera ku bicanwa bikomoka ku bicanwa cyangwa inganda nshya zikoresha amazi, ibibazo by’amazi birashoboka. Isuzuma ry’imicungire y’amazi mu buhinzi ryakozwe mu 2007 n’ikigo mpuzamahanga gishinzwe imicungire y’amazi muri Sri Lanka kugira ngo harebwe niba isi ifite amazi ahagije yo guha ibiribwa abaturage bayo biyongera. Yasuzumye amazi ahari ubuhinzi ku isi hose kandi agaragaza ahantu hafite ikibazo cy’ibura ry’amazi. Yagaragaje ko kimwe cya gatanu cyabatuye isi, barenga miliyari 1,2, batuye mu bice by’amazi make, aho usanga nta mazi ahagije yujuje ibyifuzo byose. Abandi baturage bagera kuri miliyari 1,6 batuye mu turere dufite ikibazo cy’ubukungu bw’ubukungu, aho kubura ishoramari mu mazi cyangwa ubushobozi bw’abantu budahagije bituma abayobozi badashobora guhaza amazi. Raporo yasanze bishoboka ko hashobora kubaho ibiribwa bisabwa mu gihe kiri imbere, ariko ko gukomeza umusaruro w’ibiribwa by’iki gihe ndetse n’ibidukikije bizatera ibibazo mu bice byinshi by’isi. Kugira ngo hirindwe ikibazo cy’amazi ku isi, abahinzi bagomba kwihatira kongera umusaruro kugira ngo babone ibyo kurya bikenerwa, mu gihe inganda n’imijyi bishakisha uburyo bwo gukoresha amazi neza. Ubuke bw'amazi nabwo buterwa no gukora ibicuruzwa biva mu mazi. Kurugero, ipamba: kg 1 yipamba-ihwanye na jans-ikenera metero kibe 10.9 (380 cu ft) kugirango itange umusaruro. Mu gihe ipamba ifite 2,4% yo gukoresha amazi ku isi, amazi akoreshwa mu turere dusanzwe dufite ikibazo cyo kubura amazi. Ibyangiritse ku bidukikije byatewe: urugero, kuyobya amazi n’icyahoze ari Leta Zunze Ubumwe z'Abasoviyeti mu ruzi rwa Amu Darya na Syr Darya kugira ngo bitange ipamba ahanini ni byo byabuze inyanja ya Aral. Nkurwego rwa siyansi
Ku ya 7 Mata 1795, garama yasobanuwe mu Bufaransa ko ingana n "" uburemere bwuzuye bw’amazi meza angana na cube ya ijana na metero imwe, no ku bushyuhe bwa barafu ". Ku ntego zifatika nubwo, ibyuma byifashishwa byasabwaga, inshuro igihumbi nini cyane, ikiro. Imirimo rero yashinzwe kumenya neza ubwinshi bwa litiro imwe y'amazi. N'ubwo ubusobanuro bwagenwe bwa garama bwerekanaga amazi kuri 0 ° C (32 ° F) - ubushyuhe bwororoka cyane - abahanga bahisemo gusobanura ibipimo ngenderwaho no gukora ibipimo byabo ku bushyuhe bw’amazi menshi, yapimwe icyo gihe nka 4 ° C (39 ° F). Ubushyuhe bwa Kelvin bwa sisitemu ya SI bwari bushingiye ku ngingo eshatu z’amazi, busobanurwa neza na 273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F), ariko guhera muri Gicurasi 2019 bushingiye kuri Boltzmann ihoraho aho. Igipimo ni igipimo cyuzuye cy'ubushyuhe hamwe no kwiyongera kangana n'ubushyuhe bwa selisiyusi, byasobanuwe mbere ukurikije aho bitetse (bishyirwa kuri 100 ° C (212 ° F)) hamwe no gushonga (gushirwa kuri 0 ° C (32 ° F)) y'amazi. Amazi karemano agizwe ahanini na isotopi hydrogène-1 na ogisijeni-16, ariko hariho kandi umubare muto wa isotopes iremereye ogisijeni-18, ogisijeni-17, na hydrogen-2 (deuterium). Ijanisha rya isotopi iremereye ni nto cyane, ariko iracyafite ingaruka kumiterere yamazi. Amazi ava mu nzuzi no mu biyaga akunda kubamo isotopi iremereye kuruta amazi yo mu nyanja. Kubwibyo, amazi asanzwe asobanurwa muri Vienne Standard Hagati y'amazi yo mu nyanja. Kunywa
Umubiri w'umuntu urimo, ugereranije, amazi ya 50-60%, bitewe n'imyaka, igitsina ndetse n'ubunini bw'umubiri, nubwo abantu bashobora kugira ahantu hose hagati ya 45% na 75%. Ishuri Rikuru ry’Ubumenyi ry’Ubumenyi, Ubwubatsi n’Ubuvuzi muri Amerika rirasaba gufata buri munsi litiro 3,7 (litiro 0,98 z’amerika) y’amazi ku bagabo bakuze na 2.7 L (ikigali 0,71 muri Amerika) ku bagore. Umubare nyawo uterwa nurwego rwibikorwa, ubushyuhe, ubushuhe, nibindi bintu. Byinshi muribi byinjizwa mubiribwa cyangwa ibinyobwa usibye kunywa amazi meza. Ubuvanganzo bwubuvuzi bushigikira gukoresha bike, mubisanzwe litiro 1 yamazi kumugabo ugereranije, ukuyemo ibisabwa byongeweho kubera gutakaza amazi biturutse kumyitozo ngororangingo cyangwa ikirere gishyushye. Impyiko nzima zirashobora gusohora litiro 0.8 kugeza kuri 1 kumasaha, ariko guhangayika nkimyitozo ngororamubiri birashobora kugabanya aya mafaranga. Abantu barashobora kunywa amazi arenze ayo akenewe mugihe bakora siporo, babashyira mu kaga ko gusinda amazi (hyperhydration), bishobora kwica. Abantu benshi bavuga ko "umuntu agomba kurya ibirahuri umunani by'amazi kumunsi" bisa nkaho bidafite ishingiro mubumenyi. Ubushakashatsi bwerekanye ko gufata amazi yinyongera, cyane cyane mililitiro 500 (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) mugihe cyo kurya, byajyanye no kugabanya ibiro. Gufata amazi ahagije bifasha mukurinda impatwe. Icyifuzo cy’umwimerere cyo gufata amazi mu 1945 n’ikigo cy’ibiribwa n’imirire cy’inama y’ubushakashatsi y’Amerika muri Amerika cyagize kiti: "Igipimo gisanzwe ku bantu batandukanye ni mililitiro 1 kuri buri kalori y’ibiribwa. Ibyinshi muri byo bikubiye mu biribwa byateguwe." Raporo iheruka gufata indyo y’inama y’igihugu y’ubushakashatsi muri Amerika muri rusange yasabwe, hashingiwe ku kigereranyo cyo hagati y’amazi yaturutse mu mibare y’ubushakashatsi bwakozwe muri Amerika (harimo n’amasoko y’ibiribwa): litiro 3,7 (0.81 imp gal; 0,98 gal gal yo muri Amerika) ku bagabo na litiro 2.7 (0,59 imp gal; By'umwihariko, abagore batwite n'abonsa bakeneye andi mazi kugirango bagumane amazi. Ikigo cy’ubuvuzi cyo muri Amerika kirasaba ko, ugereranije, abagabo bakoresha litiro 3 (0,66 imp gal; 0,79 gal gal yo muri Amerika) n’abagore litiro 2,2 (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); abagore batwite bagomba kongera gufata kuri litiro 2,4 (0.53 imp gal; 0,63 US gal) naho abagore bonsa bagomba kubona litiro 3 (ibikombe 12), kubera ko amazi menshi yabuze mugihe cyo konsa. Ikindi cyagaragaye ni uko mubisanzwe, hafi 20% byamazi aturuka mubiribwa, mugihe ibindi biva mumazi yo kunywa n'ibinyobwa (cafeyine irimo). Amazi asohoka mu mubiri muburyo bwinshi; binyuze mu nkari n'umwanda, binyuze mu kubira ibyuya, no guhumeka umwuka wumwuka uhumeka. Hamwe nimbaraga zumubiri nubushyuhe, gutakaza amazi biziyongera kandi ibikenerwa byamazi ya buri munsi birashobora kwiyongera. Abantu bakeneye amazi afite umwanda muke. Umwanda usanzwe urimo ibyuma nkumuringa nisasu; imiti yimiti nka pesticide, PFAS, cyangwa bleach; na bagiteri zangiza, nka Campylobacter, E. coli O157, na Vibrio. Ibisubizo bimwe biremewe ndetse byifuzwa kongera uburyohe no gutanga amashanyarazi akenewe. Ikintu kinini kinini (kubwinshi) umutungo wamazi meza akwiye kunywa ni ikiyaga cya Baikal muri Siberiya. Imiti ikoreshwa
Amazi akoreshwa cyane mubitekerezo bya chimique nkibishishwa cyangwa reaction kandi ntibisanzwe nkibisubizo cyangwa cataliste. Mu myitwarire idahwitse, amazi ni umusemburo usanzwe, ushonga ibice byinshi bya ionic, kimwe nibindi bikoresho bya polar nka ammonia hamwe n’ibintu bifitanye isano rya hafi n’amazi. Mubisubizo kama, ntibisanzwe bikoreshwa nkigisubizo cya reaction, kuko ntigishonga neza kandi ni amphoteric (acide na shingiro) na nucleophilique. Nubwo bimeze bityo, iyi mitungo rimwe na rimwe iba yifuzwa. Na none, kwihuta kwa Diels-Alder reaction kumazi byagaragaye. Amazi adasanzwe aherutse kuba ingingo yubushakashatsi. Amazi ya Oxygene yuzuyemo amazi arenze urugero yaka imyanda ihumanya neza. Guhana ubushyuhe
Amazi na parike ni amazi asanzwe akoreshwa muguhana ubushyuhe, kubera kuboneka kwayo nubushobozi bwinshi bwubushyuhe, haba mu gukonjesha no gushyushya. Amazi akonje arashobora no kuboneka mubisanzwe mu kiyaga cyangwa mu nyanja. Ni byiza cyane gutwara ubushyuhe binyuze mu guhumeka no kwegeranya amazi kubera ubushyuhe bwayo bwihishwa bwo guhumeka. Ikibi ni uko ibyuma bikunze kuboneka mu nganda nk'ibyuma n'umuringa bihinduka okiside byihuse n'amazi atavuwe hamwe na parike. Hafi ya sitasiyo zose zumuriro, amazi akoreshwa nkamazi akora (akoreshwa mumuzinga ufunze hagati ya boiler, turbine, na kondenseri), hamwe na coolant (ikoreshwa muguhana ubushyuhe bwimyanda mumubiri wamazi cyangwa kuyitwara no guhumeka muminara ikonje). Muri Amerika, gukonjesha amashanyarazi nicyo gukoresha amazi menshi. Mu nganda zikoresha ingufu za kirimbuzi, amazi ashobora no gukoreshwa nka moderi ya neutron. Mu byuma byinshi bya kirimbuzi, amazi ni akonje kandi akayobora. Ibi bitanga ikintu cyumutekano udahwitse, kuko kuvana amazi mumashanyarazi nabyo bidindiza reaction ya kirimbuzi. Icyakora ubundi buryo butoneshwa kugirango uhagarike reaction kandi birahitamo ko intwaro za kirimbuzi zitwikirwa namazi kugirango habeho ubukonje buhagije. Ibitekerezo byumuriro
Amazi afite ubushyuhe bwinshi bwo guhumeka kandi usanga ari inert, bigatuma iba umuriro mwiza uzimya amazi. Ihinduka ry'amazi ritwara ubushyuhe kure y'umuriro. Ni akaga gukoresha amazi ku muriro urimo amavuta hamwe n’umusemburo kama kuko ibikoresho byinshi kama bireremba hejuru y'amazi kandi amazi akunda gukwirakwiza amazi yaka. Gukoresha amazi mukurwanya inkongi y'umuriro bigomba kandi kuzirikana ingaruka ziterwa no guturika kwamazi, bishobora kubaho mugihe amazi akoreshejwe mumuriro ushushe cyane ahantu hafunzwe, ndetse no guturika kwa hydrogène, mugihe ibintu bigira ingaruka kumazi, nk'ibyuma bimwe na bimwe cyangwa karubone ishyushye nk'amakara, amakara, cyangwa kokiya grafite, bikabora amazi, bigatanga gaze y'amazi. Imbaraga z’ibisasu byagaragaye mu mpanuka ya Chernobyl, nubwo amazi yagize uruhare muri uru rubanza ataturutse ku kurwanya inkongi y'umuriro ahubwo yaturutse kuri sisitemu yo gukonjesha amazi. Igisasu cyaturikiye cyabaye mugihe ubushyuhe bukabije bwimbere bwatumaga amazi atemba. Igisasu cya hydrogène gishobora kuba cyaratewe nigisubizo hagati ya parike na zirconium ishyushye. Okiside zimwe na zimwe, cyane cyane iz'ibyuma bya alkali hamwe n’ubutaka bwa alkaline, zitanga ubushyuhe bwinshi cyane bitewe n’amazi ku buryo impanuka y’umuriro ishobora gutera. Isi ya alkaline yisi yihuta, izwi kandi nka calcium oxyde, ni ibintu byakozwe cyane bikunze gutwarwa mumifuka yimpapuro. Niba ibi byashizwemo, birashobora gutwikwa nkuko ibirimo bifata amazi. Imyidagaduro
Abantu bakoresha amazi mubikorwa byinshi byo kwidagadura, kimwe no gukora siporo no muri siporo. Bimwe muribi birimo koga, gusiganwa ku mazi, ubwato, koga no kwibira. Byongeye kandi, siporo imwe n'imwe, nk'umukino wa ice hamwe na skate ya ice, ikinirwa ku rubura. Ibiyaga, inyanja na parike y’amazi ni ahantu hazwi cyane abantu bajya kuruhukira no kwidagadura. Benshi basanga amajwi nigaragara ryamazi atemba atuje, kandi amasoko nandi mazi atemba ni imitako ikunzwe. Bamwe babika amafi nibindi bimera n’ibinyabuzima imbere muri aquarium cyangwa ibyuzi kugirango berekane, bishimishe, kandi basabane. Abantu kandi bakoresha amazi muri siporo ya shelegi, nko gusiganwa ku maguru, gusiganwa ku maguru, gutwara urubura cyangwa urubura, bisaba ko amazi aba ku bushyuhe buke haba nk'urubura cyangwa akayungurura mu rubura. Inganda z’amazi
Inganda z’amazi zitanga amazi yo kunywa hamwe n’amazi y’amazi (harimo gutunganya imyanda) ingo n’inganda. Ibikoresho byo gutanga amazi birimo amariba y'amazi, amariba yo gusarura amazi y'imvura, imiyoboro itanga amazi, hamwe n'ibikoresho byoza amazi, ibigega by'amazi, iminara y'amazi, imiyoboro y'amazi harimo n'amazi ashaje. Amashanyarazi aturuka mu kirere ari mu majyambere. Amazi yo kunywa akusanyirizwa hamwe ku masoko, agakurwa mu bubiko bwa artile (amariba) mu butaka, cyangwa akavomerwa mu biyaga no mu nzuzi. Kubaka amariba menshi ahantu hahagije rero nuburyo bushoboka bwo kubyara amazi menshi, ukeka ko amazi ashobora gutanga imigezi ihagije. Andi masoko y'amazi arimo gukusanya amazi y'imvura. Amazi arashobora gusaba kwezwa kubyo kurya byabantu. Ibi birashobora kuvamo gukuraho ibintu bitarashonga, ibintu byashonze na mikorobe zangiza. Uburyo bukunzwe ni ugushungura n'umucanga ukuraho gusa ibintu bidashonga, mugihe chlorine hamwe no guteka byica mikorobe zangiza. Disillation ikora imirimo uko ari itatu. Ubuhanga buhanitse burahari, nka revers osmose. Kurandura amazi menshi yo mu nyanja nigisubizo gihenze gikoreshwa mukirere cyumutse. Ikwirakwizwa ry'amazi yo kunywa rikorwa binyuze muri sisitemu y'amazi ya komini, gutanga tanker cyangwa nk'amazi icupa. Guverinoma mu bihugu byinshi zifite gahunda zo gukwirakwiza amazi ku batishoboye nta kiguzi. Kugabanya imikoreshereze ukoresheje amazi yo kunywa (ibinyobwa) kubyo kurya byabantu gusa nubundi buryo. Mu mijyi imwe n'imwe nka Hong Kong, amazi yo mu nyanja akoreshwa cyane mu koza ubwiherero mu mujyi hose mu kubungabunga umutungo w'amazi meza. Amazi yanduye arashobora kuba ikintu kinini cyo gukoresha nabi amazi; ku buryo umwanda ugabanya ubundi buryo bwo gukoresha amazi, bihinduka guta umutungo, hatitawe ku nyungu zangiza. Kimwe nubundi bwoko bwanduye, ibi ntabwo byinjira mubaruramari risanzwe ryibiciro byisoko, bigatekerezwa nkibintu hanze isoko idashobora kubara. Ni yo mpamvu abandi bantu bishyura igiciro cy’umwanda w’amazi, mu gihe inyungu z’ibigo byigenga zitagabanijwe ku baturage bahohotewe n’umwanda. Imiti ikoreshwa nabantu ikunze kurangirira mumazi yamazi kandi irashobora kugira ingaruka mbi kubuzima bwamazi iyo ibaye bioaccumulate kandi niba idashobora kwangirika. Amazi y’amakomine n’inganda asanzwe atunganyirizwa mu nganda zitunganya amazi. Kugabanya imyanda yanduye ikemurwa hakoreshejwe uburyo butandukanye bwo gukumira no kuvura. Gusaba inganda
Inganda nyinshi zishingiye ku nganda zishingiye ku miti ikoresheje imiti yashonze mu mazi, guhagarika ibishishwa mu mazi cyangwa gukoresha amazi mu gushonga no gukuramo ibintu, cyangwa koza ibicuruzwa cyangwa ibikoresho byo gutunganya. Inzira nko gucukura amabuye y'agaciro, kuvoma imiti, guhumeka, gukora impapuro, gukora imyenda, gusiga irangi, gucapa, no gukonjesha amashanyarazi bikoresha amazi menshi, bisaba isoko yabigenewe, kandi akenshi bitera umwanda mwinshi. Amazi akoreshwa mumashanyarazi. Amashanyarazi ni amashanyarazi aboneka muri hydropower. Amashanyarazi ava mu mazi atwara turbine y'amazi ihujwe na generator. Amashanyarazi n’igiciro gito, kidahumanya, isoko y’ingufu zishobora kubaho. Ingufu zitangwa no kugenda kwamazi. Mubisanzwe urugomero rwubatswe kumugezi, rukarema ikiyaga cyubukorikori inyuma yacyo. Amazi ava mu kiyaga ahatirwa binyuze muri turbine zihindura amashanyarazi. Amazi afite ingufu akoreshwa muguturika amazi no gukata amazi. Imbunda y'amazi yumuvuduko mwinshi ikoreshwa mugukata neza. Ikora neza, ifite umutekano muke, kandi ntabwo yangiza ibidukikije. Irakoreshwa kandi mugukonjesha imashini kugirango wirinde gushyuha, cyangwa kurinda ibyuma byashyuha. Amazi akoreshwa kandi mubikorwa byinshi byimashini ninganda, nka turbine ya parike hamwe noguhindura ubushyuhe, hiyongereyeho no gukoreshwa nkumuti wimiti. Kurekura amazi atunganijwe akoreshwa mu nganda ni umwanda. Umwanda urimo imyanda isohoka (umwanda w’imiti) n’amazi akonje (umwanda w’ubushyuhe). Inganda zisaba amazi meza kubikorwa byinshi kandi ikoresha uburyo butandukanye bwo kweza haba mugutanga amazi no gusohora. Urwego rwa digitale, cyane cyane ubwenge bwubukorikori rukoresha amazi menshi, bityo kwaguka kwa AI birashobora "guhungabanya umutekano w’amazi ku isi ndetse n’igihugu". Gutunganya ibiryo
Guteka, guhumeka, no gucanira ni uburyo bukunzwe bwo guteka busaba kwibiza ibiryo mumazi cyangwa imiterere ya gaze, amavuta. Amazi nayo akoreshwa mugukaraba ibikoresho. Amazi nayo agira uruhare runini mubijyanye na siyanse y'ibiribwa. Umuti nkumunyu nisukari biboneka mumazi bigira ingaruka kumiterere yamazi. Ahantu h'amazi abira no gukonjesha haterwa ibisubizo, hamwe numuvuduko wumwuka, ari nako bigira ingaruka ku butumburuke. Amazi abira ubushyuhe buke hamwe numuvuduko wo hasi wikirere uboneka ahantu hirengeye. Umuyoboro umwe wa sucrose (isukari) kuri kilo y'amazi uzamura amazi yatetse kuri 0.51 ° C (0.918 ° F), naho umunyu umwe wumunyu kuri kg uzamura aho utetse kuri 1.02 ° C (1.836 ° F); kimwe, kongera umubare wibice byashonze bigabanya aho ubukonje bwamazi. Umuti mumazi unagira ingaruka kubikorwa byamazi bigira ingaruka kumiti myinshi no gukura kwa mikorobe mubiribwa. Igikorwa cyamazi gishobora gusobanurwa nkigipimo cyumuvuduko wumwuka wamazi mugisubizo cyumuvuduko wumwuka wamazi meza. Umuti mubikorwa byamazi yo hasi-ibi ni ngombwa kubimenya kuko imikurire ya bagiteri myinshi ihagarara kurwego rwo hasi rwibikorwa byamazi. Ntabwo imikurire ya mikorobe igira ingaruka gusa kumutekano wibiribwa, ahubwo no kubungabunga no kubika ubuzima bwibiryo. Gukomera kw'amazi nabyo ni ikintu gikomeye mu gutunganya ibiribwa kandi birashobora guhinduka cyangwa kuvurwa hakoreshejwe uburyo bwo guhanahana imiti. Irashobora kugira ingaruka zikomeye ku bwiza bwibicuruzwa, ndetse no kugira uruhare mu isuku. Gukomera kwamazi gushyirwa mubikorwa hashingiwe ku kwibumbira hamwe kwa calcium karubone amazi arimo. Amazi ashyirwa mubikorwa byoroshye niba arimo mg / L (UK) munsi ya 100 mg / L (US). Raporo yashyizwe ahagaragara n’umuryango w’amazi y’amazi mu mwaka wa 2010, ikiro kimwe cy’inka gisaba litiro ibihumbi 15 (3.3 × 10 ^ 3 imp gal; 4.0 × 10 ^ 3 ikigali cy’amazi); icyakora, abanditsi banasobanura neza ko iyi ari impuzandengo yisi yose kandi ibintu bigenda byerekana umubare wamazi akoreshwa mukubyara inyama zinka. Mu isanzure
Amazi menshi yo mu isanzure yakozwe nk'umusaruro w'inyenyeri. Imiterere yinyenyeri iherekezwa numuyaga ukomeye wo hanze wa gaze numukungugu. Iyo uku gusohoka kw'ibintu amaherezo bigira ingaruka kuri gaze ikikije, imivumba yo guhungabana ikorwa igabanya kandi igashyushya gaze. Amazi yagaragaye akorwa vuba muri iyi gaze yuzuye. Ku ya 22 Nyakanga 2011, raporo yasobanuye ivumburwa ry’igicu kinini cy’umwuka w’amazi urimo "amazi arenga miriyoni 140 ugereranije n’inyanja zose z’isi zishyize hamwe" hafi ya kwasari iherereye kuri miliyari 12 z'umucyo uvuye ku isi. Abashakashatsi bavuga ko "ivumburwa ryerekana ko amazi yiganje mu isanzure hafi ya yose yabayeho". Amazi yagaragaye mu bicu hagati yinzira y'Amata. Amazi birashoboka ko abaho cyane mubindi galaxy, nayo, kubera ko ibiyigize, hydrogène, na ogisijeni, biri mubintu byinshi cyane mu isanzure. Imiterere idasanzwe
Amazi hamwe n’ibindi bihindagurika birashoboka ko bigizwe ninshi mu miterere yimbere ya Uranus na Neptune kandi amazi yo murwego rwimbitse arashobora kuba muburyo bwamazi ya ionic aho molekile zicamo isupu ya hydrogène na ogisijeni, kandi byimbitse bikiri nkamazi adasanzwe aho ogisijeni ihurira, ariko ion ya hydrogene ireremba hejuru yubusa muri latike ya ogisijeni. Amazi n'imibumbe
Kubaho kw'amazi meza, kandi ku rugero ruto imiterere ya gaze kandi ikomeye, kwisi ningirakamaro mubuzima bwisi nkuko tubizi. Isi iherereye muri zone ituwe na Solar System; iyaba yari yegereye gato cyangwa kure yizuba (hafi 5%, cyangwa kilometero zigera kuri miriyoni 8), ibintu byemerera ubwo buryo butatu icyarimwe icyarimwe ntibishoboka kubaho. Ingano yisi nayo igira uruhare: uburemere bwayo butuma ifata ikirere, harimo nubushuhe bwikirere. Umubumbe muto nka Mars ufite ikirere cyoroshye cyane cyangwa nta kirere. Umwuka wamazi hamwe nizindi myuka ihumanya ikirere itanga ikirere (ubushyuhe bwa parike) ifasha kugumana ubushyuhe bwubuso butajegajega mugihe buhari mubwinshi. Imiterere y’amazi ku mubumbe biterwa nigitutu cyibidukikije, bigenwa nuburemere bwisi. Niba umubumbe ari munini bihagije, amazi kuri yo arashobora gukomera haba no mubushyuhe bwinshi, kubera umuvuduko mwinshi uterwa nuburemere, nkuko byagaragaye kuri exoplanets Gliese 436 b na GJ 1214 b. Amategeko, politiki, n'ibibazo
Politiki y’amazi ni politiki yibasiwe n’amazi n’amazi. Amazi, cyane cyane amazi meza, nisoko yingamba kwisi yose kandi nikintu gikomeye mumirwano myinshi ya politiki. Itera ingaruka ku buzima no kwangiza urusobe rw'ibinyabuzima. Kubona amazi meza yo kunywa byateye imbere mu myaka mirongo ishize hafi ya yose ku isi, ariko abantu bagera kuri miliyari imwe baracyafite amazi meza kandi miliyari zisaga 2,5 ntibafite isuku ihagije. Icyakora, bamwe mu babikurikiranira hafi bavuga ko mu 2025 abarenga kimwe cya kabiri cy'abatuye isi bazahura n'ikibazo cyo kwibasirwa n'amazi. Raporo yasohotse mu Gushyingo 2009, igaragaza ko mu 2030, mu turere tumwe na tumwe two mu nzira y'amajyambere ku isi, amazi akenewe azarenga 50%. Abantu babarirwa muri miliyari 1,6 babonye isoko y’amazi meza guhera mu 1990. Umubare w’abantu bo mu bihugu biri mu nzira y'amajyambere bafite amazi meza ubarwa ko wazamutse uva kuri 30% mu 1970 ugera kuri 71% mu 1990, 79% mu 2000, na 84% mu 2004. Raporo y’umuryango w’abibumbye yo mu 2006 yavuze ko "hari amazi ahagije kuri buri wese", ariko ko kuyageraho bibangamirwa n’imicungire mibi na ruswa. Byongeye kandi, gahunda z’isi yose zigamije kunoza imikorere y’itangwa ry’imfashanyo, nk’itangazo rya Paris ryerekeye ubufasha bw’imfashanyo, ntabwo ryigeze rifatwa n’abaterankunga bo mu nzego z’amazi neza nk’uko bafite mu burezi n’ubuzima, bikaba bishoboka ko hasigara abaterankunga benshi bakora ku mishinga irenga ndetse na guverinoma zihabwa nta bushobozi bwo gukora. Abanditsi b'isuzuma rusange ry’imicungire y’amazi mu 2007 mu buhinzi bavuze ko imiyoborere mibi ari imwe mu mpamvu z’ubuke bw’amazi. Imiyoborere y’amazi ni gahunda yuburyo busanzwe kandi butemewe binyuze mu gufata ibyemezo bijyanye no gucunga amazi. Imiyoborere myiza y’amazi ahanini ni ukumenya inzira ikora neza murwego runaka rwimibereho nubukungu. Rimwe na rimwe amakosa yakozwe mu kugerageza gushyira mu bikorwa 'igishushanyo mbonera' gikora mu bihugu byateye imbere mu bihugu biri mu nzira y'amajyambere. Umugezi wa Mekong ni urugero rumwe; ubushakashatsi bwakozwe n’ikigo mpuzamahanga gishinzwe imicungire y’amazi mu bihugu bitandatu bishingiye ku mugezi wa Mekong ku mazi bwagaragaje ko isesengura rishingiye ku nyungu kandi mu mucyo no gusuzuma ingaruka z’ibidukikije bidakunze gukorwa. Basanze kandi umushinga w’itegeko ry’amazi muri Kamboje byari bigoye cyane kuruta uko byari bikwiye. Mu 2004, umuryango utegamiye kuri Leta witwa WaterAid wo mu Bwongereza wavuze ko umwana apfa buri masegonda 15 azize indwara ziterwa n’amazi byoroshye kwirindwa, akenshi bikaba biterwa no kubura isuku ihagije. Kuva mu 2003, Raporo y’umuryango w’abibumbye ishinzwe iterambere ry’amazi, yakozwe na gahunda yo gusuzuma amazi ku isi UNESCO, yahaye abafata ibyemezo ibikoresho byo guteza imbere politiki irambye y’amazi. Raporo ya 2023 ivuga ko abantu miliyari ebyiri (26% by'abaturage) badafite amazi yo kunywa naho miliyari 3.6 (46%) bakaba badafite isuku icungwa neza. Abantu bo mu mijyi (miliyari 2,4) bazahura n’ibura ry’amazi mu 2050. Ubuke bw’amazi bwasobanuwe nk’icyorezo, kubera kunywa cyane n’umwanda. Raporo ivuga ko 10% by'abatuye isi baba mu bihugu bifite ibibazo byinshi cyangwa bikomeye ku mazi. Nyamara mu myaka 40 ishize, ikoreshwa ry’amazi ryiyongereyeho hafi 1% ku mwaka, kandi biteganijwe ko riziyongera ku kigero kimwe kugeza mu 2050. Kuva mu 2000, umwuzure mu turere dushyuha wikubye kane, mu gihe umwuzure w’amajyaruguru rwagati wiyongereyeho 2.5. Ikiguzi cy'iyi myuzure hagati ya 2000 na 2019 ni impfu 100.000 na miliyoni 650. Amashyirahamwe ashishikajwe no kurengera amazi arimo ishyirahamwe mpuzamahanga ry’amazi (IWA), Amazi y’amazi, Amazi ya 1, n’ishyirahamwe ry’amazi muri Amerika. Ikigo mpuzamahanga gishinzwe imicungire y’amazi gikora imishinga hagamijwe gukoresha imicungire myiza y’amazi mu kugabanya ubukene. Umunsi mpuzamahanga w’amazi uba ku ya 22 Werurwe n’umunsi w’inyanja ku isi ku ya 8 Kamena. Mu 2026, Umuryango w’abibumbye watangaje ko ikiremwamuntu cyinjiye mu gihe cy’ "Amazi y’amazi" kubera ko ibice birenga 50% by’ibiyaga binini byagabanutse kuva mu myaka ya za 90, 35% by’ibishanga byazimiye kuva mu 1970, 75% by’abatuye isi baba mu bihugu bidafite umutekano w’amazi, abantu miliyari 4 bahura n’ibura ry’amazi byibuze ukwezi kumwe ku mwaka, kandi amapfa yatwaye miliyari 307 z'amadolari ku mwaka. Iyobokamana
Amazi afatwa nk'isukura mu madini menshi. Imyizerere ikubiyemo gukaraba imihango (kwiyuhagira) harimo ubukirisitu, idini ry'Abahindu, Ubuyisilamu, idini rya Kiyahudi, umuryango wa Rastafari, Shinto, Taoism, na Wicca. Kwibizwa (cyangwa aspersion cyangwa affusion) byumuntu mumazi ni Isakramentu rikuru ryubukristo (aho ryitwa umubatizo); ni kimwe mu bigize andi madini, harimo Islam (Ghusl), idini rya Kiyahudi (mikva) na Sikhisme (Amrit Sanskar). Byongeye kandi, kwiyuhagira umuhango mumazi meza bikorerwa abapfuye mumadini menshi arimo Islam na kiyahudi. Muri Islamu, amasengesho atanu ya buri munsi arashobora gukorwa mubihe byinshi nyuma yo koza ibice bimwe byumubiri ukoresheje amazi meza (wudu), keretse amazi ataboneka (reba Tayammum). Muri Shinto, amazi akoreshwa mumihango hafi ya yose yoza umuntu cyangwa agace (urugero, mumihango ya misogi). Mubukirisitu, amazi yera namazi yejejwe numupadiri agamije kubatizwa, umugisha wabantu, ahantu, nibintu, cyangwa nkuburyo bwo kwanga ikibi. Muri Zoroastrianism, amazi (āb) yubahwa nkisoko yubuzima. Filozofiya
Umuhanga mu bya filozofiya w'Abagereki ba kera Empedocles yabonaga amazi ari kimwe mu bintu bine bya kera (hamwe n'umuriro, isi, n'umwuka), kandi yabifata nka ylem, cyangwa ibintu by'ibanze bigize isanzure. Thales, Aristote yerekanye ko ari umuhanga mu bumenyi bw'ikirere na injeniyeri, yavuze ko isi, yuzuye amazi, yavuye mu mazi. Thales, moniste, yizeraga kandi ko ibintu byose bikozwe mu mazi. Platon yizeraga ko imiterere y'amazi ari icosahedron - itemba byoroshye ugereranije n'isi imeze nka cube. Igitekerezo cyibisekeje bine byumubiri bifitanye isano namazi na flegm, nkubukonje nubushuhe. Ikintu cyambere cyamazi nacyo cyari kimwe mubintu bitanu muri filozofiya gakondo yubushinwa (hamwe nisi, umuriro, ibiti, nicyuma).
