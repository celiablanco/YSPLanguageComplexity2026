ʻO ka wai he mea hoʻohuihui ʻole me ke ʻano kemika H2O. He mea kemika maopopo, ʻono ʻole, ʻala ʻole, a aneane ʻole waihoʻoluʻu. ʻO ia ka mea nui o nā kahawai, nā loko, a me nā moana o ka Honua. ʻO ka wai hoʻi ka wai o nā mea ola a pau i ʻike ʻia, kahi e hana ai ma ke ʻano he mea hoʻoheheʻe. ʻO ka wai, he mole polar, hoʻopaʻa ikaika i ka hydrogen intermolecular i mea kōkua nui i kona mau waiwai kino a me kemika. He mea nui ia no nā ʻano ola a pau, ʻoiai ʻaʻole hāʻawi i ka ikehu meaʻai a i ʻole he micronutrient organik. Ma muli o kona noho ʻana i loko o nā mea ola a pau, kona kūpaʻa kemika, ka nui o ka honua, a me kona polarity ikaika e pili ana i kona nui molekala liʻiliʻi, ʻike pinepine ʻia ka wai ma ke ʻano he "universal solvent". Ma muli o ka pili kokoke ana o ka wela a me ke kaomi o ka honua i ka helu kolu o ka wai, aia ka wai ma ka Honua ma ke ʻano he paʻa, wai, a me ke kinoea. Hoʻokumu ia i ka ua ma ke ʻano o ka ua a me nā aerosol ma ke ʻano o ka noe. Aia nā ao i nā kulu wai a me ka hau, kona ʻano paʻa. Ke māhele maikaʻi ʻia, hiki i ka hau crystalline ke hoʻoheheʻe i ke ʻano o ka hau. ʻO ke kūlana kinoea o ka wai ka mahu a i ʻole ka mahu wai. Uhi ka wai ma kahi o 71% o ka ili o ka Honua, me ke kai a me ka moana ka nui o ka nui o ka wai (ma kahi o 96.5%). ʻO nā wahi liʻiliʻi o ka wai e like me ka wai honua (1.7%), ma nā glaciers a me nā ʻāpana hau o Antarctica a me Greenland (1.7%), a ma ka lewa e like me ka mahu, nā ao (ʻo ia ka hau a me ka wai wai i hoʻokuʻu ʻia i ka lewa), a me ka ua (0,001%). Ke neʻe mau nei ka wai ma o ka pōʻaiapuni wai o ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana, ka transpiration (evapotranspiration), condensation, precipitation, a me ka holo ʻana, a hiki i ke kai. He kuleana koʻikoʻi ka wai i ka hoʻokele waiwai honua. Ma kahi o 70% o ka wai hou i hoʻohana ʻia e nā kānaka e hele i ka mahiʻai. ʻO ka lawaiʻa ʻana i loko o nā wai paʻakai a me nā wai hou, ʻo ia ke kumu nui o ka meaʻai no nā wahi he nui o ka honua, e hāʻawi ana i 6.5% o ka protein honua. ʻO ka nui o ke kālepa lōʻihi o nā waiwai (e like me ka aila, ke kinoea maoli, a me nā huahana i hana ʻia) e lawe ʻia e nā waʻa ma o ke kai, nā muliwai, nā loko, a me nā kahawai. Hoʻohana ʻia ka nui o ka wai, ka hau, a me ka mahu no ka hoʻoluʻu a me ka hoʻomehana ʻana i ka ʻoihana a me nā hale. ʻO ka wai he mea hoʻoheheʻe maikaʻi loa no nā ʻano mea like ʻole, ʻo ka mineral a me ka organik; e like me ia, hoʻohana nui ʻia ia i nā kaʻina hana ʻoihana a i ka kuke ʻana a me ka holoi ʻana. ʻO ka wai, ka hau, a me ka hau ke kikowaena o nā haʻuki he nui a me nā ʻano leʻaleʻa ʻē aʻe, e like me ka ʻauʻau, ka waʻa leʻaleʻa, ka heihei waʻa, ka heʻenalu, ka lawaiʻa haʻuki, ka luʻu, ka holo hau, ka holo hau, a me ka ski. Etymology
Mai ka Old English wæter, mai Proto-Germanic *watar (kumu no hoi o Old Saxon watar, Old Frisian wetir, Dutch water, Old High German wazzar, German Wasser, vatn, Gothic 𐍅𐌰𐍄𐍉 (wato)), from Proto-Indo-European *wod-wet' or). Hoʻohui pū ʻia, ma o ke kumu Indo-European, me ka Helene ύδωρ (ýdor; mai ka Helene kahiko ʻo ὕδωρ (hýdōr), no laila ka ʻōlelo Pelekane 'hydro-'), Russian вода́ (vodá), Irish uisce, a me Albanian ujë. Waiwai
ʻO ka wai (H2O) he mea hoʻohuihui polar. Ma ka lumi wela, he wai ʻono ʻole a ʻala ʻole, aneane ʻaʻohe waihoʻoluʻu me kahi ʻulaʻula uliuli. ʻO ka hydrogen chalcogenide maʻalahi loa, ʻo ia ka mea i aʻo nui ʻia a i kekahi manawa ua wehewehe ʻia ʻo ia ʻo "ka mea hoʻoheheʻe honua" no kona hiki ke hoʻoheheʻe i nā mea ʻoi aku ma mua o nā wai ʻē aʻe, ʻoiai ʻaʻole maikaʻi i ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana i nā mea nonpolar. ʻO kēia ka mea e hiki ai ke lilo i "solvent o ke ola": ʻoiaʻiʻo, ʻo ka wai e like me ka mea i loaʻa ma ke ʻano ʻaneʻane e komo pū me nā mea hoʻoheheʻe like ʻole, a pono nā hana kūikawā e loaʻa ai ka wai maʻemaʻe kemika. ʻO ka wai wale nō ka mea maʻamau e ola ma ke ʻano he paʻa, wai, a me ke kinoea i nā kūlana terrestrial maʻamau. Mokuʻāina
Me ka oxidane, ʻo ka wai kekahi o nā inoa kūhelu ʻelua no ka hui kemika H2O; ʻo ia hoʻi ka māhele wai o H2O. ʻO nā kūlana maʻamau ʻelua o ka wai, ʻo ia ka pae paʻa, ʻo ia ka hau, a me ka pae kinoea, ka mahu wai a i ʻole ka mahu. Hiki i ka hoʻohui ʻana a i ʻole ka wehe ʻana i ka wela ke hoʻololi i ka pae: ka hoʻoheheʻe (ka wai i ka hau), ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana (ka hau i ka wai), ka vaporization (ka wai i ka mahu), ka condensation (ka mahu i ka wai), ka sublimation (ka hau i ka mahu) a me ka waiho ʻana (ka mahu i ka hau). ʻO ka mānoanoa
ʻO ka wai kekahi o nā mea maʻamau liʻiliʻi e hoʻonui a liʻiliʻi ka paʻakai i ka wā e maʻalili ai i loko o kekahi mau pae wela; ʻo ia wale nō ka mea i ʻike maoli ʻia e hana pēlā i ka wai. Eia kekahi, he mea maʻamau ia no ka mea e liʻiliʻi loa ka liʻiliʻi i ka wā e maloʻo ai, ʻoiai ʻaʻole ia he ʻokoʻa ma ia ʻano. Ma 1 atm kaomi, hiki i kona mānoanoa kiʻekiʻe o 999.972 kg/m3 (62.4262 lb/cu ft) ma 3.98 °C (39.16 °F). Ma lalo o kēlā mahana, akā ma luna o ka palena hau o 0 °C (32 °F), hoʻonui ka wai (e liʻiliʻi ka liʻiliʻi) a hiki i ka pae maloʻo, a ʻo kona mānoanoa i ka pae wai he 999.8 kg/m3 (62.4155 lb/cu ft). Ke hoʻolili ka wai i 3.98 °C (39.16 °F), e emi ana ka nui o ka wai. I kona maloʻo ʻana a lilo i hau, hoʻonui ka wai ma kahi o 9%, a hiki i ka mānoanoa o 917 kg/m3 (57.25 lb/cu ft). Hiki i kēia hoʻonui ke hoʻoikaika nui, nā paipu pohā a me nā pōhaku māwae. Ma ke ʻano paʻa, hōʻike ia i ke ʻano maʻamau o ka ʻaelike a lilo i ʻoi aku ka paʻa i ka wā e maʻalili ai. He hopena koʻikoʻi ko kēia mau mea wela maʻamau i ke ola ma ka honua. I loko o ka loko a i ʻole ka moana, piʻo ka wai ma 4 °C (39 °F) i lalo, a puka ka hau ma ka ʻili, e lana ana ma luna o ka wai wai. Mālama kēia hau i ka wai ma lalo, e pale ana i ka maloʻo maloʻo. Me ka ʻole o kēia pale, e make ana ka hapa nui o nā mea ola i loko o nā loko i ka wā hoʻoilo. Eia kekahi, ʻo kēia ʻano anomali he ʻāpana koʻikoʻi o ke kahe thermohaline e puʻunaue ana i ka wela a puni nā kai o ka honua. Mākēneki

No ka laʻana, waiho ʻia ka hau ma nā ʻili anu i ka wā e hoʻokumu ʻia ai nā hau hau ma ka waiho ʻana ma luna o kahi ʻāpana aerosol a i ʻole nucleus hau. I ka emi ʻana o ke kaomi o ka lewa me ke kiʻekiʻe, e emi ana ka paila e 1 °C i kēlā me kēia 274 mika. ʻOi aku ka lōʻihi o ka kuke ʻana i ke kiʻekiʻe ma mua o ka kuke ʻana i ke kai. No ka laʻana, ma 1,524 mika (5,000 ft), pono e hoʻonui ʻia ka manawa kuke e ka hā no ka loaʻa ʻana o ka hopena i makemake ʻia. ʻO ka mea ʻē aʻe, hiki ke hoʻohana ʻia ka ipu kuke e hoʻemi i ka manawa kuke ma ka hoʻonui ʻana i ka wela o ka paila. I loko o kahi mānoanoa, e hoʻolapalapa ka wai ma ka lumi wela. ʻO nā helu ʻekolu a koʻikoʻi
Ma ke kiʻi paʻi a me ka pae wela (e nānā i ke kiʻi), aia nā pihi e hoʻokaʻawale ana i ka paʻa me ka mahu, ka mahu mai ka wai, a me ka wai mai ka paʻa. Hālāwai kēia mau mea ma kahi kikoʻī i kapa ʻia ʻo ka helu triple, kahi e noho like ai nā ʻāpana ʻekolu. Aia ka helu kolu ma kahi mahana o 273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F) a me ke kaomi o 611.657 pascals (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); ʻo ia ke kaomi haʻahaʻa loa e hiki ai i ka wai wai ke noho. A hiki i ka makahiki 2019, ua hoʻohana ʻia ka helu ʻekolu e wehewehe i ke ana wela Kelvin. Hoʻopau ke kaʻe o ka wai/mahana ma 647.096 K (373.946 °C; 705.103 °F) a me 22.064 megapascals (3,200.1 psi; 217.75 atm). ʻIke ʻia kēia ma ke ʻano koʻikoʻi. Ma nā wela kiʻekiʻe a me nā puʻe, hoʻokumu ka wai a me ka mahu i kahi manawa mau i kapa ʻia he wai supercritical. Hiki ke hoʻoemi a hoʻonui ʻia ma waena o nā ʻano like me ke kinoea a me ka wai; ʻO kona mau waiwai (he ʻokoʻa loa ia mai ka wai ambient) ʻoluʻolu i ka paʻa. No ka laʻana, no nā kaomi kūpono a me nā mahana hiki ke hui wale me nā pūhui nonpolar, me ka hapa nui o nā pūhui organik. Hoʻohana kēia i nā ʻano noi like ʻole me ka electrochemistry wela kiʻekiʻe a ma ke ʻano he solvent ecologically benign a catalyst i nā hopena kemika e pili ana i nā pūhui organik. I loko o ka ʻaʻahu o ka Honua, hana ia ma ke ʻano he mea hoʻoheheʻe i ka wā o ka hoʻokumu ʻana o ka mineral, hoʻoheheʻe a me ka waiho ʻana. Nā māhele o ka hau a me ka wai
ʻO ke ʻano maʻamau o ka hau ma ka ʻili o ka Honua, ʻo ia ka hau Ih, kahi ʻāpana e hana ai i nā kristal me ka symmetry hexagonal. ʻO kekahi me ka cubic crystalline symmetry, ice Ic, hiki ke hana i ka lewa luna. Ke piʻi aʻe ke kaomi, hana ka hau i nā hale aniani ʻē aʻe. I ka makahiki 2024, ua hōʻoia ʻia he iwakālua a ua wānana ʻia nā mea hou aʻe. Ua ʻike ʻia ka ʻumikūmāwalu ʻano o ka hau, ka hau XVIII, he ʻano hau i waena o ka maka, superionic ice phase, i ka wā i hoʻokau ʻia ai kahi kulu wai i ka nalu haʻalulu i hoʻokiʻekiʻe i ke kaomi o ka wai i nā miliona o nā lewa a me kona mahana i nā kaukani mau degere, e hopena i kahi ʻano o nā ʻātoma oxygen paʻa kahi i kahe manuahi ai nā mana hydrogen. Ke hoʻopili ʻia ma waena o nā papa graphene, hana ka hau i ka lattice square. ʻAʻole maopopo i nā kikoʻī o ke ʻano kemika o ka wai wai; ʻO kekahi mau manaʻo manaʻo e pili ana kāna hana maʻamau ma muli o ka noho ʻana o nā mokuʻāina wai ʻelua. ʻO ka ʻono a me ke ʻala
ʻO ka wai maʻemaʻe ka mea maʻamau i wehewehe ʻia he ʻono a ʻala ʻole, ʻoiai he mau mea ʻike kikoʻī ko ke kanaka e hiki ai ke ʻike i ka hele ʻana o ka wai i loko o ko lākou waha, a ʻike ʻia nā rana hiki ke honi. Eia nō naʻe, ʻo ka wai mai nā kumu maʻamau (me ka wai mineral) maʻamau ka nui o nā mea hoʻoheheʻe e hāʻawi i nā ʻono a me nā ʻala like ʻole. Ua hoʻomohala nā kānaka a me nā holoholona ʻē aʻe i nā manaʻo e hiki ai iā lākou ke loiloi i ka hiki ʻana o ka wai e pale aku i ka wai paʻakai loa a i ʻole ʻino. Ka waihoʻoluʻu a me ka helehelena
ʻIke ʻia ka polū ma muli o ke komo ʻana o ka mālamalama i ka ʻāina c. 600–800 nm. Hiki ke ʻike maʻalahi ka waihoʻoluʻu i loko o ke aniani wai-wai i kau ʻia ma kahi ʻaoʻao keʻokeʻo maʻemaʻe, i ke ao. ʻO nā puʻupuʻu absorption nui ke kuleana o ke kala, ʻo ia nā leo o nā haʻalulu kikoo O-H. Piʻi ka ikaika o ke kala me ka hohonu o ke kolamu wai, ma muli o ke kānāwai Beer. Hoʻohana pū ʻia kēia, no ka laʻana, me kahi wai ʻauʻau ke ʻike ʻia ke kumu kukui i ka lā o ka lā mai nā tile keʻokeʻo o ka loko. Ma ke ʻano maoli, hiki ke hoʻololi ʻia ke kala mai ka uliuli a i ka ʻōmaʻomaʻo ma muli o ka loaʻa ʻana o nā mea paʻa a i ʻole nā ​​algae. Ma ka ʻoihana, hoʻohana ʻia ka spectroscopy kokoke-infrared me nā ʻoluʻolu wai e like me ka ʻoi aku o ka ikaika o nā ʻili haʻahaʻa o ka wai, ʻo ia ka mea hiki ke hoʻohana ʻia nā cuvettes aniani me ka lōʻihi ala pōkole. No ka ʻike ʻana i ke kiko o ka absorption kikoʻī o ka wai a i ʻole kahi wai wai ma ka ʻāina ma kahi o 3,500 cm−1 (2.85 μm) pono kahi lōʻihi ma kahi o 25 μm. Eia kekahi, pono ke akaka ka cuvette ma kahi o 3500 knm−1 a hiki ole ke hooheheeia i ka wai; ʻO ka calcium fluoride kekahi mea i hoʻohana maʻamau no nā puka makani cuvette me nā ʻano wai. Hiki ke ʻike ʻia nā haʻalulu kumu Raman-active me, no ka laʻana, he 1 knm sample cell. Hiki i nā mea kanu wai, nā limu, a me nā mea ola photosynthetic ʻē aʻe ke ola i loko o ka wai a hiki i nā haneli mau mika ka hohonu, no ka mea hiki ke hiki i ka lā iā lākou. ʻAʻole hiki ka lā i nā wahi o ka moana ma lalo o 1,000 mika (3,300 ft) o ka hohonu. ʻOi aku ka kiʻekiʻe o ka huahelu refractive o ka wai wai (1.333 ma 20 °C (68 °F)) ma mua o ka ea (1.0), like me nā alkanes a me ka ethanol, akā haʻahaʻa ma mua o ka glycerol (1.473), benzene (1.501), carbon disulfide (1.627), a me nā ʻano aniani maʻamau (1.6). ʻOi aku ka haʻahaʻa o ka huahelu refraction o ka hau (1.31) ma mua o ka wai wai. polarity molekala
I loko o kahi mole wai, hoʻokumu nā ʻātoma hydrogen i kahi kihi 104.5° me ka atom oxygen. Kokoke nā ʻātoma hydrogen i nā kihi ʻelua o kahi tetrahedron i kikowaena ma ka oxygen. Ma nā kihi ʻelua ʻelua, aia nā pālua valence electron i komo ʻole i ka hoʻopaʻa ʻana. I loko o kahi tetrahedron maikaʻi loa, e hana nā ʻātoma i kahi kihi 109.5 °, akā ʻoi aku ka nui o ka hoʻihoʻi ʻana ma waena o nā hui lone ma mua o ka hoʻihoʻi ʻana ma waena o nā mana hydrogen. ʻO ka lōʻihi o ka pilina O–H ma kahi o 0.096 nm. Loaʻa i nā mea ʻē aʻe ke ʻano molekula tetrahedral, no ka laʻana methane (CH4) a me ka hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Eia nō naʻe, ʻoi aku ka electronegative o ka oxygen ma mua o ka hapa nui o nā mea ʻē aʻe, no laila, ʻoi aku ka maikaʻi o ka ʻātoma o ka oxygen i ka wā e hoʻoili ʻia ai nā mana hydrogen. Me ke ʻano o ka piko, hāʻawi kēia i ka mole i kahi manawa dipole uila a ua helu ʻia ʻo ia ma ke ʻano he mole polar. He mea hoʻoheheʻe polar maikaʻi ka wai, e hoʻoheheʻe ana i nā paʻakai he nui a me nā molekala organik hydrophilic e like me ke kō a me nā ʻawaʻawa maʻalahi e like me ka ethanol. Hoʻoheheʻe ka wai i nā kinoea he nui, e like me ka oxygen a me ka carbon dioxide - hāʻawi ka mea hope i ka pizz o nā mea inu kalapona, nā waina ʻālohilohi a me nā pia. Eia kekahi, nui nā mea i loko o nā mea ola, e like me nā protein, DNA a me polysaccharides, i hoʻoheheʻe ʻia i ka wai. ʻO ka launa pū ʻana ma waena o ka wai a me nā ʻāpana o kēia mau biomacromolecules ke hoʻohālikelike nei i ka hoʻopili ʻana i ka protein, ka hoʻohui pū ʻana o DNA, a me nā mea ʻē aʻe e koʻikoʻi i ke ola (ka hopena hydrophobic). Nui nā mea ola (e like me nā momona a me nā aila a me nā alkanes) he hydrophobic, ʻo ia hoʻi, hiki ʻole ke hoʻoheheʻe ʻia i ka wai. ʻAʻole hiki ke hoʻoheheʻe ʻia ka nui o nā mea inorganic, ʻo ia hoʻi ka hapa nui o nā metala oxides, sulfides, a me nā silicates. Hoʻopaʻa hydrogen
Ma muli o kona polarity, hiki i kahi mole o ka wai i loko o ka wai a i ʻole ke kūlana paʻa ke hana i ʻehā mau paʻa hydrogen me nā molekala kokoke. He ʻumi paha ka ikaika o nā paʻa hydrogen e like me ka ikaika o Van der Waals e huki ana i nā molekala i kekahi i kekahi i ka nui o nā wai. ʻO ia ke kumu i ʻoi aku ka kiʻekiʻe o nā helu heheʻe a me ka hoʻolapalapa ʻana o ka wai ma mua o nā pūhui like ʻole e like me ka hydrogen sulfide. Ua wehewehe pū lākou i kona mana wela kikoʻī kiʻekiʻe loa (e pili ana i 4.2 J/(g·K)), wela o ka hui ʻana (e pili ana i 333 J/g), wela o ka vaporization (2257 J/g), a me ka hana wela (ma waena o 0.561 a me 0.679 W/(m·K)). ʻO kēia mau waiwai e ʻoi aku ka maikaʻi o ka wai i ka hoʻololi ʻana i ke aniau o ka Honua, ma ka mālama ʻana i ka wela a lawe ʻia ma waena o ke kai a me ka lewa. Aia ma kahi o 23 kJ/mol ka paʻa hydrogen o ka wai (i ka hoʻohālikelike ʻia me ka paʻa covalent O–H ma 492 kJ/mol). ʻO kēia, ua manaʻo ʻia ʻo 90% ka mea e pili ana i nā electrostatics, aʻo ke koena 10% he ʻāpana covalent. ʻO kēia mau mea paʻa ke kumu o ka haʻalulu kiʻekiʻe o ka ʻili a me nā ikaika capillary. ʻO ka hana capillary e pili ana i ka neʻe ʻana o ka wai i luna o kahi paipu haiki e kū'ē i ka ikaika o ka ʻumekaumaha. Ua hilinaʻi ʻia kēia waiwai e nā mea kanu vascular āpau, e like me nā lāʻau. ʻO ka hoʻonaʻauao pilikino
ʻO ka wai he hopena nāwaliwali o ka hydronium hydroxide – aia kahi equilibrium 2H2O ⇌ H3O+ + OH−, i hui pū ʻia me ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana o ka hydronium a me nā ion hydroxide. Ka hoʻoili uila a me ka electrolysis
ʻO ka wai maʻemaʻe ka haʻahaʻa haʻahaʻa o ka uila, e hoʻonui ʻia me ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana o kahi mea liʻiliʻi o nā mea ionic e like me ka paʻakai maʻamau. Hiki ke hoʻokaʻawale ʻia ka wai wai i loko o nā mea hydrogen a me ka oxygen ma o ka hoʻokuʻu ʻana i kahi au uila i loko o ia mea - kahi hana i kapa ʻia ʻo electrolysis. Pono ka decomposition i ka hoʻokomo ikehu ʻoi aʻe ma mua o ka wela i hoʻokuʻu ʻia e ke kaʻina hana inverse (285.8 kJ/mol, a i ʻole 15.9 MJ/kg). Nā waiwai mīkini
Hiki ke manaʻo ʻia ʻaʻole hiki ke hoʻopili ʻia ka wai wai no ka nui o nā kumu: ʻo kona hiki ke hoʻopaʻa ʻia mai 4.4 a 5.1 × 10−10 Pa−1 i nā kūlana maʻamau. ʻOiai i loko o ka moana i ka hohonu 4 km, kahi o ke kaomi ʻana he 400 atm, loaʻa ka wai i ka 1.8% ka emi ʻana o ka nui. ʻO ka viscosity o ka wai ma kahi o 10−3 Pa·s a i ʻole 0.01 poise ma 20 °C (68 °F), a ʻo ka wikiwiki o ke kani i ka wai wai ma waena o 1,400 a me 1,540 mau mika i kekona (4,600 a me 5,100 ft/s) ma muli o ka mahana. Hele lōʻihi ka leo i loko o ka wai me ka liʻiliʻi o ka attenuation, ʻoi aku ma nā alapine haʻahaʻa (ma kahi o 0.03 dB/km no 1 kHz), kahi waiwai i hoʻohana ʻia e nā cetaceans a me nā kānaka no ke kamaʻilio a me ka ʻike ʻana i ke kaiapuni (sonar). Ma nā wela kiʻekiʻe, hana ka coke, kahi ʻano kalapona, me ka mahu e hana i carbon monoxide a me ka hydrogen. Ma ka Honua
ʻO ka Hydrology ke aʻo ʻana i ka neʻe ʻana, ka hāʻawi ʻana, a me ka maikaʻi o ka wai a puni ka Honua. ʻO ke aʻo ʻana o ka hāʻawi ʻana i ka wai he hydrography. ʻO ke aʻo ʻana i ka hāʻawi ʻana a me ka neʻe ʻana o ka wai honua he hydrogeology, ʻo nā glaciers he glaciology, ʻo ka wai i uka he limnology a ʻo ka hāʻawi ʻana i nā moana he oceanography. ʻO nā kaʻina hana kaiaola me ka hydrology aia i ka manaʻo o ka ecohydrology. ʻO ka nui o ka wai i loaʻa ma luna, ma lalo, a ma luna o ka ʻili o ka honua i kapa ʻia ʻo ka hydrosphere. ʻO ka nui o ka wai o ka honua (ka nui o ka wai o ka honua) he 1.386 billion cubic kilometres (333 miliona cubic mile). Loaʻa ka wai wai i loko o nā kino wai, e like me ka moana, ke kai, ka loko, ka muliwai, ke kahawai, ke kahawai, ka loko, a i ʻole ka puddle. ʻO ka hapa nui o ka wai ma ka Honua he wai kai. Aia ka wai ma ka lewa ma nā ʻano paʻa, wai, a me ka mahu. Aia nō ma ke ʻano he wai lepo i loko o nā aquifers. He mea koʻikoʻi ka wai ma nā kaʻina hana honua. Aia ka wai lepo ma ka hapa nui o nā pōhaku, a ʻo ke kaomi o kēia wai lepo e pili ana i nā ʻano o ka hewa. ʻO ka wai i loko o ka ʻaʻahu ke kuleana o ka heheʻe e hoʻohua ai i nā lua pele ma nā ʻāpana hoʻohaʻahaʻa. Ma ka ili o ka Honua, he mea nui ka wai ma ka hana kemika a me ke kino. ʻO ka wai, a ʻoi aku ka liʻiliʻi akā ʻoi aku ka nui, ka hau, ke kuleana no ka nui o ka lawe ʻana i ka sediment e kū ana ma ka ʻili o ka honua. ʻO ka waiho ʻia ʻana o ka sediment i lawe ʻia he mau ʻano pōhaku sedimentary, ʻo ia ka mea i hoʻopaʻa ʻia i ka moʻolelo honua. Poʻa wai
ʻO ka pōʻai wai (i ʻike ʻia ma ka ʻepekema ʻo ka hydrologic cycle) ʻo ia ka hoʻololi mau ʻana o ka wai i loko o ka hydrosphere, ma waena o ka lewa, ka wai lepo, ka wai ʻili, ka wai honua, a me nā mea kanu. Ke neʻe mau nei ka wai ma kēlā me kēia māhele i loko o ka pōʻaiapuni wai e pili ana i kēia mau kaʻina hoʻoili:

ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻia mai nā moana a me nā kino wai ʻē aʻe i ka lewa a me ka neʻe ʻana mai nā mea kanu a me nā holoholona i ka lewa. ka ua, mai ka mahu wai e hu'ihu'i mai ka lewa a haule i ka honua a i ka moana paha. ka holo ʻana mai ka ʻāina a hiki i ke kai. Hoʻi ka nui o nā mahu wai i loko o ka moana, akā lawe ka makani i ka mahu wai ma luna o ka ʻāina e like me ka holo ʻana i loko o ke kai, ma kahi o 47 Tt i kēlā me kēia makahiki a ʻo ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana a me ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana i ka ʻāina he 72 Tt hou i kēlā me kēia makahiki. ʻO ka ua, he 119 Tt i kēlā me kēia makahiki ma luna o ka ʻāina, he mau ʻano: ʻo ka ua, ka hau, a me ka huahekili, me ka hāʻawi ʻana mai ka noe a me ka hau. ʻO ka hau he mau kulu liʻiliʻi o ka wai i hoʻopaʻa ʻia i ka wā e hui ai ka mānoanoa kiʻekiʻe o ka mahu wai me kahi ʻili anuanu. Hana ʻia ka hau i ke kakahiaka i ka haʻahaʻa loa o ka mahana, ma mua o ka puka ʻana o ka lā a i ka wā e hoʻomaka ai ka piʻi ʻana o ka mahana o ka honua. Hiki i ka wai i hoʻopaʻa ʻia i ka lewa ke hoʻopololei i ka lā e hana i nā anuenue. Hōʻiliʻili pinepine ka wai ma luna o nā kahawai e kahe ana i nā kahawai. Ma o ka hili ʻana, hoʻohālikelike ka wai kahe i ke kaiapuni e hana ana i nā awāwa muliwai a me nā delta e hāʻawi ai i ka lepo momona a me ka ʻāina paʻa no ke kūkulu ʻana i nā kikowaena heluna kanaka. Hiki mai ke kaiakahinalii ke uhiia kekahi wahi o ka aina, ma kahi haahaa, i ka wai e puka mai ana ke kahe ka muliwai i kona kapa a i ole he ino. Ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻē aʻe, ʻo ka maloʻo kahi manawa lōʻihi o nā mahina a i ʻole mau makahiki i ka wā e ʻike ai kahi ʻāina i kahi hemahema o kāna lako wai. Hana ʻia kēia i ka loaʻa ʻana o ka ua ma lalo o ka awelika o ka ua ma muli o kona topography a i ʻole ma muli o kona wahi e pili ana i ka latitu. Waiwai
ʻO nā kumu wai ka wai maoli o ka wai e pono ai ke kanaka, no ka laʻana he kumu wai inu a i ʻole wai hoʻoheheʻe. Hana ʻia ka wai ma ke ʻano he "stocks" a me "kahe". He ʻokoʻa ka lōʻihi o ka manawa e waiho ai ka wai i loko o ka waiho ʻana: ʻo kekahi mau aquifers he wai i mālama ʻia i nā kaukani makahiki, akā hiki ke loli ka nui o nā loko i kēlā me kēia kau, e emi ana i ka wā maloʻo a piʻi aʻe i ka wā pulu. ʻO ka hapa nui o ka lako wai no kekahi mau ʻāina, ʻo ia ka wai i unuhi ʻia mai ka wai i mālama ʻia i loko o nā waihona, a inā ʻoi aku ka hoʻihoʻi ʻana ma mua o ka recharge, e emi ana nā waihona. Ka wai a me ke kai
Aia ka wai kai ma kahi o 3.5% sodium chloride ma ka awelika, me ka liʻiliʻi o nā mea ʻē aʻe. ʻOkoʻa nā waiwai kino o ke kai mai ka wai maʻemaʻe ma kekahi mau mea nui. Paʻu ʻo ia ma kahi mahana haʻahaʻa (ma kahi o -1.9 °C (28.6 °F)) a piʻi kona mānoanoa me ka emi ʻana o ka mahana a hiki i ke kiko maloʻo, ma kahi o ka hiki ʻana i ka mānoanoa kiʻekiʻe ma kahi mahana ma luna o ka hau. ʻO ka paʻakai o ka wai ma nā kai nui mai ka 0.7% ma ke Kai Baltic a i ka 4.0% ma ke Kaiʻula. Nā hopena i ke ola
Mai kahi hiʻohiʻona olaola, he nui nā waiwai ʻokoʻa o ka wai e koʻikoʻi no ka hoʻonui ʻana o ke ola. Lawe ʻo ia i kēia kuleana ma ka ʻae ʻana i nā pūhui organik e hana i nā ala e hiki ai ke hana hou. Aia nā ʻano ola a pau i ʻike ʻia i ka wai. He mea koʻikoʻi ka wai ma ke ʻano he solvent kahi e hoʻoheheʻe ai ka nui o nā solutes o ke kino a ma ke ʻano he ʻāpana koʻikoʻi o nā kaʻina metabolic i loko o ke kino. ʻO ka metabolism ka huina o ka anabolism a me ka catabolism. I loko o ka anabolism, hoʻoneʻe ʻia ka wai mai nā molekala (ma o ka ikehu e koi ana i nā hopena kemika enzymatic) e ulu i nā molekala nui (e laʻa, nā starches, triglycerides, a me nā protein no ka mālama ʻana i nā wahie a me ka ʻike). I ka catabolism, hoʻohana ʻia ka wai e wāwahi i nā mea paʻa e hoʻohua ai i nā molekole liʻiliʻi (e laʻa, glucose, fatty acids, a me amino acids e hoʻohana ʻia no nā wahie no ka hoʻohana ʻana i ka ikehu a i ʻole nā ​​​​mea ʻē aʻe). Me ka ʻole o ka wai, ʻaʻole hiki ke loaʻa kēia mau kaʻina metabolic. He mea nui ka wai no ka photosynthesis a me ka hanu. Hoʻohana ʻo Photosynthetic cell i ka ikehu o ka lā e hoʻokaʻawale i ka hydrogen o ka wai mai ka oxygen. Ma ke alo o ka lā, hoʻohui ʻia ka hydrogen me CO2 (i lawe ʻia mai ka ea a i ʻole ka wai) e hana i ka glucose a hoʻokuʻu i ka oxygen. Hoʻohana nā mea ola a pau i ia mau wahie a hoʻoneʻe i ka hydrogen a me ke kalapona no ka hopu ʻana i ka ikehu o ka lā a hoʻoponopono i ka wai a me CO2 i ke kaʻina hana (cellular respiration). ʻO ka wai ke kikowaena o ka neutrality acid-base a me ka hana enzyme. Hiki ke hoʻokaʻawale ʻia ka ʻakika, ka ion hydrogen (H+, ʻo ia hoʻi, ka proton), e ke kumu, kahi ʻae proton e like me ka ion hydroxide (OH−) e hana i ka wai. He ʻenehana nui kona hoʻohana ʻana a nānā mau ʻia e nā lula aupuni (maʻamau 1 ʻāpana o ka miliona (ppm) no ka wai inu, a me 1-2 ppm o ka chlorine ʻaʻole i hoʻopili ʻia me nā haumia no ka wai ʻauʻau). Hiki ke mālama ʻia ka wai no ka ʻauʻau ʻana ma ke kūlana microbiological maikaʻi me ka hoʻohana ʻana i nā disinfectants kemika e like me ka chlorine a i ʻole ka ozone a i ʻole ka hoʻohana ʻana i ke kukui ultraviolet. ʻO ka hoʻihoʻi ʻana i ka wai ke kaʻina hana o ka hoʻololi ʻana i ka wai ʻino (ʻoi aku ka maʻamau, kapa ʻia ʻo wai ʻōpala kūloko) i wai e hiki ke hoʻohana hou ʻia no nā kumu ʻē aʻe. Aia he 2.3 biliona kanaka e noho ana ma nā ʻāina me ka liʻiliʻi o ka wai, ʻo ia hoʻi ka loaʻa ʻana o kēlā me kēia kanaka ma lalo o 1,700 cubic mika (60,000 cubic ft) o ka wai i kēlā me kēia makahiki. ʻO 380 billion cubic mika (13×10^12 cu ft) o ka wai hoʻoneʻe kūlanakauhale i hana ʻia ma ka honua holoʻokoʻa i kēlā me kēia makahiki. ʻO ka wai maʻemaʻe kahi kumu waiwai hou, i hoʻihoʻi ʻia e ka pōʻai hydrologic kūlohelohe, akā ʻo ke kaomi ʻana i ke komo ʻana ma muli o ka puʻunaue like ʻole i ka lewa a me ka manawa, e ulu ana nā koi waiwai e ka mahiʻai a me ka ʻoihana, a me ka piʻi ʻana o ka heluna kanaka. I kēia manawa, kokoke i hoʻokahi piliona kānaka a puni ka honua i nele i ka loaʻa ʻole o ka wai palekana a hiki ke kūʻai aku. I ka makahiki 2000, ua hoʻokumu ka United Nations i nā Millennium Development Goals no ka hoʻokaʻawale ʻana o ka wai i ka 2015 i ka hapa o ka poʻe ma ka honua holoʻokoʻa me ka loaʻa ʻole o ka wai palekana a me ka hoʻomaʻemaʻe. ʻAʻole kūlike ka holomua i kēlā pahuhopu, a ma 2015 ua hoʻokō ka UN i nā Sustainable Development Goals no ka loaʻa ʻana o ke ao holoʻokoʻa i ka wai palekana a me ka hoʻomaʻemaʻe ma mua o 2030. ʻO ka maikaʻi ʻole o ka wai a me ka hoʻomaʻemaʻe maikaʻi ʻole he mea make; ma kahi o ʻelima miliona mau make i ka makahiki ma muli o nā maʻi pili wai. Manaʻo ka World Health Organization e hiki i ka wai palekana ke pale i ka 1.4 miliona mau keiki i make mai ka maʻi maʻi i kēlā me kēia makahiki. Ma nā ʻāina e ulu ana, 90% o nā wai ʻino a pau o ke kūlanakauhale ʻaʻole i mālama ʻia i nā kahawai a me nā kahawai. Ma kahi o 50 mau ʻāina, me kahi hapakolu o ka heluna kanaka o ka honua, e pilikia pū ana i ka liʻiliʻi o ka wai ma waena a kiʻekiʻe paha a ʻo 17 o kēia mau ʻāpana wai ʻoi aku ka nui o ka wai i kēlā me kēia makahiki ma mua o ka hoʻonui ʻia ʻana ma o kā lākou mau kaʻa wai maoli. ʻAʻole pili wale ke koʻikoʻi i nā kino wai maʻemaʻe e like me nā muliwai a me nā loko, akā hoʻohaʻahaʻa hoʻi ia i nā kumu wai honua. Hoʻohana kanaka
Mahiai
ʻO ka hoʻohana nui ʻana o ke kanaka i ka wai no ka mahiʻai, me ka mahiʻai irrigated, ʻo ia ka nui o ka 80 a 90 ka nui o ka nui o ka hoʻohana wai kanaka. Ma ʻAmelika Hui Pū ʻIa, 42% o ka wai maʻemaʻe i hoʻihoʻi ʻia no ka hoʻohana ʻana no ka irrigation, akā ʻo ka hapa nui o ka wai "hoʻopau" (hoʻohana ʻia ʻaʻole hoʻi i hoʻihoʻi ʻia i ke kaiapuni) hele i ka mahiʻai. Hoʻomaopopo pinepine ʻia ka loaʻa ʻana o ka wai maʻemaʻe, ʻoi aku hoʻi ma nā ʻāina i kūkulu ʻia i kūkulu i nā ʻōnaehana wai maʻalahi no ka ʻohi, hoʻomaʻemaʻe, a me ka lawe ʻana i ka wai, a me ka wehe ʻana i ka wai ʻino. Akā ʻo ka ulu ʻana o ka hoʻokele waiwai, demographic, a me ka ʻili e hoʻonui i nā hopohopo e pili ana i nā pilikia wai, e alakaʻi ana i ka hoʻokūkū hoʻokūkū no nā kumu wai paʻa, e hāpai ana i ka manaʻo o ka wai kiʻekiʻe. Ke hoʻomau nei ka ulu ʻana o ka heluna kanaka a me nā ʻoihana, hoʻonui ʻia ka ʻai ʻana i ka ʻiʻo makewai wai, a piʻi aʻe nā koi hou no nā biofuels a i ʻole nā ​​ʻoihana hoʻoikaika wai hou, aia paha nā pilikia wai hou. Ua mālama ʻia kahi loiloi o ka hoʻokele wai i ka mahiʻai ma 2007 e ka International Water Management Institute ma Sri Lanka e ʻike inā lawa ka wai o ka honua e hāʻawi i ka meaʻai no kona ulu ʻana. Ua loiloi ʻo ia i ka loaʻa ʻana o ka wai i kēia manawa no ka mahiʻai ma ka pae honua a hoʻopaʻa ʻia i nā wahi e loaʻa ana i ka nele o ka wai. Ua ʻike ʻia ʻo ka hapalima o ka poʻe o ka honua, ʻoi aku ma mua o 1.2 biliona, e noho ana ma nā wahi o ka nele o ka wai kino, kahi i lawa ʻole ai ka wai e hoʻokō ai i nā koi āpau. He 1.6 biliona mau kānaka e noho ana ma nā wahi e ʻike nei i ka liʻiliʻi o ka wai, kahi i hiki ʻole ai i nā mana ke hoʻokō i ka koi o ka wai. Ua ʻike ʻia ka hōʻike e hiki ke hana i ka meaʻai i makemake ʻia i ka wā e hiki mai ana, akā ʻo ka hoʻomau ʻana i ka hana ʻai meaʻai o kēia lā a me nā ʻano kaiapuni e alakaʻi i nā pilikia ma nā wahi he nui o ka honua. I mea e pale aku ai i ka pilikia o ka wai o ka honua, e hoʻoikaika nā mea mahiʻai e hoʻonui i ka huahana e hoʻokō i nā koi ulu no ka meaʻai, ʻoiai nā ʻoihana a me nā kūlanakauhale e ʻimi i nā ala e hoʻohana pono ai i ka wai. Hoʻokumu ʻia ka nele o ka wai ma muli o ka hana ʻana i nā huahana koʻikoʻi wai. No ka laʻana, pulupulu: 1 kg o ka pulupulu—e like ana me ka lua jeans—koi i 10.9 cubic mika (380 cubic ft) wai e hana ai. ʻOiai ʻo ka pulupulu he 2.4% o ka hoʻohana ʻana i ka wai o ka honua, hoʻopau ʻia ka wai ma nā wahi i pilikia i ka nele o ka wai. Ua nui ka pōʻino o ke kaiapuni: no ka laʻana, ʻo ka hoʻohuli ʻana i ka wai e ka Soviet Union mua mai nā kahawai ʻo Amu Darya a me Syr Darya e hana i ka pulupulu ke kumu nui o ka nalowale ʻana o ke Kai Aral. Ma ke ʻano he kūlana ʻepekema
Ma ka lā 7 ʻApelila 1795, ua wehewehe ʻia ka gram ma Farani e like me "ke kaumaha piha o ka nui o ka wai maʻemaʻe e like me ka cube o hoʻokahi-haneri o ka mika, a ma ka wela o ka hau hehee". No ka hana pono naʻe, ua koi ʻia kahi maʻamau kuhikuhi metallic, hoʻokahi kaukani ʻoi aku ka nui, ʻo ke kilo. No laila ua kauoha ʻia ka hana e hoʻoholo pololei i ka nui o hoʻokahi lita o ka wai. ʻOiai ʻo ka wehewehe ʻana o ka gram i hoʻohālikelike ʻia i ka wai ma 0 °C (32 °F)—he wela hiki ke hana hou ʻia—ua koho nā ʻepekema e wehewehe hou i ka maʻamau a e hana i kā lākou ana i ka wela o ka wai kiʻekiʻe loa, i ana ʻia i ka manawa he 4 °C (39 °F). Hoʻokumu ʻia ka pālākiō wela Kelvin o ka ʻōnaehana SI ma ka helu ʻekolu o ka wai, i wehewehe ʻia ʻo 273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F), akā ma Mei 2019 ua hoʻokumu ʻia ma ka Boltzmann mau. He unahi wela piha ka unahi me ka hoʻonui like me ka unahi wela Celsius, i wehewehe mua ʻia e like me ka helu paila (hoʻonoho ʻia i 100 °C (212 °F)) a me ka helu heheʻe (hoʻonoho ʻia i 0 °C (32 °F)) o ​​ka wai. ʻO ka wai kūlohelohe ka hapa nui o nā isotopes hydrogen-1 a me oxygen-16, akā aia kekahi wahi liʻiliʻi o nā isotopes kaumaha oxygen-18, oxygen-17, a me hydrogen-2 (deuterium). He liʻiliʻi loa ka pākēneka o nā isotopes kaumaha, akā pili mau i nā waiwai o ka wai. ʻOi aku ka liʻiliʻi o nā isotopes kaumaha i ka wai mai nā muliwai a me nā loko ma mua o ka wai kai. No laila, ua wehewehe ʻia ka wai maʻamau i ka Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water specification. No ka inu ʻana
Aia i loko o ke kino o ke kanaka, ma ka awelika, 50-60% wai, e pili ana i ka makahiki, ke kāne a me ka nui o ke kino, ʻoiai aia nā kānaka ma waena o 45% a me 75%. Paipai ka US National Academies of Science, Engineering, and Medicine i ka inu ʻana i kēlā me kēia lā o 3.7 lita (0.98 US galani) o ka wai no nā kāne makua a me 2.7 L (0.71 US gal) no nā wahine. ʻO ka nui pololei e pili ana i ke kiʻekiʻe o ka hana, ka mahana, ka haʻahaʻa, a me nā mea ʻē aʻe. Hoʻokomo ʻia ka hapa nui o kēia ma o nā meaʻai a i ʻole nā ​​​​mea inu ʻē aʻe ma mua o ka inu ʻana i ka wai pololei. Makemake nā palapala lapaʻau i ka ʻai haʻahaʻa, maʻamau he 1 lita o ka wai no ke kāne maʻamau, me ka ʻole o nā koi ʻē aʻe ma muli o ka pau ʻana o ka wai mai ka hoʻoikaika kino a i ʻole ka mahana wela. Hiki i nā puʻupaʻa olakino ke hoʻokuʻu i ka 0.8 i ka 1 lita o ka wai i kēlā me kēia hola, akā hiki i ke kaumaha e like me ka hoʻoikaika kino ke hoʻemi i kēia nui. Hiki i ka poʻe ke inu i ka wai ʻoi aku ka nui ma mua o ka mea e pono ai i ka wā e hoʻoikaika ai, e waiho ana iā lākou i ka pilikia o ka ʻona wai (hyperhydration), hiki ke make. ʻO ka ʻōlelo kaulana ʻo "pono ​​ke kanaka e ʻai i ʻewalu mau aniani wai i ka lā" ʻaʻohe kumu maoli i ka ʻepekema. Ua hōʻike ʻia nā haʻawina ʻo ka hoʻonui ʻana i ka wai, ʻoi aku ka nui o 500 milliliters (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) i ka wā ʻai, pili pū me ka pohō kaumaha. He mea kōkua ka inu wai kūpono i ka pale ʻana i ka constipation. He ʻōlelo paipai mua no ka lawe ʻana i ka wai i ka makahiki 1945 e ka Food and Nutrition Board o ka US National Research Council penei: "ʻO kahi maʻamau maʻamau no nā kānaka like ʻole he 1 milliliter no kēlā me kēia calorie o ka meaʻai. Aia ka hapa nui o kēia nui i nā meaʻai i hoʻomākaukau ʻia." ʻO ka hōʻike hōʻike meaʻai hou loa e ka US National Research Council i manaʻo nui ʻia, e pili ana i ka median total intake wai mai ka ʻikepili noiʻi US (me nā kumu meaʻai): 3.7 lita (0.81 imp gal; 0.98 US gal) no nā kāne a me 2.7 lita (0.59 imp gal; 0.71 US gal; 0.71 US gal, ʻaʻole i loaʻa i ka wai ka nui o ka wai i hāʻawi ʻia) ka nui o ka nui o ka wai i ka nana ana. ʻO ka mea kikoʻī, pono nā wahine hāpai a me nā wahine hānai i nā wai hou e noho hydrated. Ke paipai nei ka US Institute of Medicine, ma ka awelika, ʻai nā kāne i 3 lita (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) a me nā wahine 2.2 lita (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); Pono nā wāhine hāpai e hoʻonui i ka ʻai i 2.4 lita (0.53 imp gal; 0.63 US gal) a pono e loaʻa i nā wahine hānai i 3 lita (12 kīʻaha), no ka mea, ua nalowale ka nui o ka wai i ka wā hānai. Hoʻomaopopo pū ʻia ʻo ka mea maʻamau, ma kahi o 20% o ka inu wai mai ka meaʻai, aʻo ke koena mai ka wai inu a me nā mea inu (me ka caffeinated). Hoʻokuʻu ʻia ka wai mai ke kino ma nā ʻano he nui; ma o ka mimi a me ka feces, ma ka houhu, a me ka hoopuka ana i ka mahu wai ma ka hanu. Me ka hoʻoikaika kino a me ka ʻike ʻana i ka wela, e piʻi aʻe ka pohō o ka wai a e piʻi aʻe nā pono wai i kēlā me kēia lā. Pono nā kānaka i ka wai me ka liʻiliʻi o nā haumia. ʻO nā mea haumia maʻamau nā metala e like me ke keleawe a me ke kēpau; nā pūhui kemika e like me nā pesticides, PFAS, a i ʻole bleach; a me nā hua bacteria ʻino, e like me Campylobacter, E. coli O157, a me Vibrio. ʻAe ʻia kekahi solutes a makemake ʻia no ka hoʻonui ʻana i ka ʻono a me ka hāʻawi ʻana i nā electrolytes e pono ai. ʻO ka wai maʻemaʻe nui loa (ma ka nui) kūpono no ka inu ʻana ʻo Lake Baikal ma Siberia. Holoi
Kaʻahele
Hoʻohana kemika
Hoʻohana nui ʻia ka wai i nā hopena kemika ma ke ʻano he mea hoʻoheheʻe a i ʻole reactant a emi ʻole ma ke ʻano he solute a i ʻole catalyst. I loko o nā hoʻoheheʻe inorganic, he mea hoʻoheheʻe maʻamau ka wai, e hoʻoheheʻe ana i nā pūhui ionic he nui, a me nā pūhui polar ʻē aʻe e like me ka ammonia a me nā pūhui pili pili i ka wai. I loko o nā mea hoʻoheheʻe organik, ʻaʻole ia e hoʻohana mau ʻia ma ke ʻano he mea hoʻoheheʻe hopena, no ka mea, ʻaʻole ia e hoʻoheheʻe maikaʻi i nā reactants a he amphoteric (acidic a me ke kumu) a me ka nucleophilic. Eia naʻe, makemake ʻia kēia mau waiwai i kekahi manawa. Eia kekahi, ua ʻike ʻia ka wikiwiki o nā hopena Diels-Alder e ka wai. ʻO ka wai supercritical i kēia manawa he kumuhana o ka noiʻi. Hoʻopau maikaʻi ka wai supercritical hoʻopiha o ka oxygen i nā mea haumia. Hoʻololi wela
ʻO ka wai a me ka mahu he wai maʻamau i hoʻohana ʻia no ka hoʻololi ʻana i ka wela, ma muli o kona loaʻa ʻana a me ka nui o ka wela wela, no ka hoʻoluʻu a me ka hoʻomehana. Hiki ke loaʻa maoli ka wai anuanu mai loko mai o ke kai a i ʻole ke kai. ʻOi aku ka maikaʻi o ka lawe ʻana i ka wela ma o ka vaporization a me ka condensation o ka wai ma muli o kāna wela latent nui o ka vaporization. ʻO kahi pōʻino, ʻo nā metala i loaʻa maʻamau i nā ʻoihana e like me ke kila a me ke keleawe, ua hoʻoneʻe wikiwiki ʻia e ka wai i mālama ʻole ʻia a me ka mahu. Ma kahi kokoke i nā hale mana wela, hoʻohana ʻia ka wai ma ke ʻano he wai hana (hoʻohana ʻia i loko o ka puka pani ma waena o ka paila, turbine mahu, a me ka condenser), a me ka mea hoʻomaʻamaʻa (hoʻohana ʻia e hoʻololi i ka wela ʻōpala i kahi kino wai a lawe ʻia paha e ka evaporation i loko o kahi hale hoʻomaha). Ma ʻAmelika Hui Pū ʻIa, ʻo ia ka mea hoʻohana nui loa i nā mea mana hoʻomaha i ka wai. I ka ʻoihana mana nuklea, hiki ke hoʻohana ʻia ka wai ma ke ʻano he neutron moderator. I ka hapa nui o nā reactors nuklea, ʻo ka wai ka mea hoʻoluʻu a me ka moderator. Hāʻawi kēia i kahi mea palekana passive, ʻoiai ʻo ka wehe ʻana i ka wai mai ka reactor e hoʻolohi i ka hopena nuklea. Eia nō naʻe, makemake ʻia nā ʻano hana ʻē aʻe no ka hoʻōki ʻana i kahi pane a ʻoi aku ka maikaʻi o ka mālama ʻana i ka nuklea core i uhi ʻia me ka wai i mea e hōʻoia ai i ka hoʻomaha ʻana. Nā manaʻo ahi
ʻO ka wai ka wela nui o ka hoʻohuhū ʻana a ʻano inert, ʻo ia ka mea e lilo ai i wai kinai ahi maikaʻi. ʻO ka hoʻoheheʻe ʻana o ka wai e lawe i ka wela mai ke ahi. He mea pōʻino ka hoʻohana ʻana i ka wai ma ke ahi e pili ana i nā ʻaila a me nā mea hoʻoheheʻe organik no ka mea he nui nā mea olaola e lana ana ma luna o ka wai a hoʻolaha ka wai i ka wai ʻā. Pono e noʻonoʻo ka hoʻohana ʻana i ka wai i ka pahū ʻana i ka mahu, i hiki ke hana ʻia ke hoʻohana ʻia ka wai ma nā ahi wela loa i nā wahi paʻa, a me ka pahū hydrogen, ke hoʻoheheʻe ʻia ka wai e nā mea i hui pū me ka wai, e like me kekahi mau metala a i ʻole ke kalapona wela e like me ka lanahu, ka lanahu, a i ʻole ka coke graphite, e hoʻoheheʻe i ka wai, e hana i ke kinoea wai. Ua ʻike ʻia ka mana o ia mau pahū i loko o ka pōʻino Chernobyl, ʻoiai ʻo ka wai i komo i kēia hihia ʻaʻole i hele mai mai ke ahi ahi akā mai ka ʻōnaehana hoʻoluʻu wai ponoʻī o ka reactor. Ua pahū ka mahu i ka wā o ka wela loa ʻana o ke kumu i hoʻopuʻi ʻia ka wai i ka mahu. Hiki ke pahū hydrogen ma muli o ka hopena ma waena o ka mahu a me ka zirconium wela. ʻO kekahi mau metala metala, ʻoi aku ka nui o nā metala alkali a me nā metala honua alkaline, e hoʻohua i ka wela i ka hoʻopili ʻana me ka wai e hiki ai i kahi pōʻino ke ahi. ʻO ka alkaline earth oxide quicklime, i ʻike ʻia hoʻi he calcium oxide, he mea i hana nui ʻia i lawe pinepine ʻia i loko o nā ʻeke pepa. Inā hoʻokomo ʻia kēia mau mea, e ʻā paha lākou i ka hana ʻana o nā mea i loko me ka wai. Leʻaleʻa
Hoʻohana nā kānaka i ka wai no nā hana leʻaleʻa he nui, a no ka hoʻoikaika kino a me nā haʻuki. ʻO kekahi o ia mau mea, ʻo ia ka ʻauʻau, ka hoʻokele wai, ka waʻa, ka heʻenalu a me ka luʻu. Eia kekahi, hoʻokani ʻia kekahi mau haʻuki, e like me ka hau hockey a me ka hau hau. ʻO nā kahakai, nā kahakai a me nā paka wai nā wahi kaulana no ka poʻe e hele ai e hoʻomaha a hauʻoli i ka leʻaleʻa. Manaʻo ka poʻe he nui ke kani a me ke ʻano o ka wai kahe, a ʻo nā pūnāwai a me nā hale wai kahe ʻē aʻe he mea hoʻonani kaulana. Mālama kekahi i nā iʻa a me nā mea ulu a me nā holoholona ʻē aʻe i loko o nā aquarium a i ʻole nā ​​loko iʻa no ka hōʻikeʻike, leʻaleʻa, a me ka pilina. Hoʻohana nā kānaka i ka wai no nā haʻuki hau, e like me ka skiing, sledding, snowmobiling a i ʻole snowboarding, e koi ana i ka wai ma kahi haʻahaʻa haʻahaʻa e like me ka hau a i ʻole ka crystallized i ka hau. ʻOihana wai
Hāʻawi ka ʻoihana wai i ka wai inu a me nā lawelawe wai ʻino (me ka hoʻomaʻemaʻe ʻana i ka wai) i nā hale a me nā ʻoihana. Loaʻa nā pūnāwai wai, nā lua wai no ka ʻohi ʻana i ka wai ua, nā ʻupena wai, a me nā keʻena hoʻomaʻemaʻe wai, nā pahu wai, nā hale kiaʻi wai, nā paipu wai me nā aqueducts kahiko. Ke kūkulu ʻia nei nā mea hana wai lewa. ʻOhi pinepine ʻia ka wai inu ma nā pūnāwai, i lawe ʻia mai nā ʻauwaʻa hana (nā pūnāwai) i ka lepo, a i ʻole ka paila ʻia mai nā loko a me nā muliwai. ʻO ke kūkulu ʻana i nā luawai hou aku ma nā wahi kūpono, ʻo ia ke ala e hiki ai ke hoʻonui i ka wai, me ka manaʻo e hiki i nā aquifers ke hāʻawi i kahi kahe kūpono. ʻO nā kumu wai ʻē aʻe he ʻohi wai ua. Pono ka wai e hoʻomaʻemaʻe no ka ʻai kanaka.
