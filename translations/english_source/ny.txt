Madzi ndi mankhwala opangidwa ndi mankhwala opangidwa ndi H2O. Ndi mankhwala oonekera, osakoma, osanunkhiza, ndipo pafupifupi opanda mtundu. Ndilo gawo lalikulu la mitsinje, nyanja, ndi nyanja zapadziko lapansi. Madzi ndi madzi amadzimadzi a zamoyo zonse zodziwika bwino, zomwe zimakhala ngati zosungunulira. Madzi, pokhala molekyulu ya polar, amakumana ndi ma intermolecular hydrogen bonding omwe amathandizira kwambiri kuzinthu zake zakuthupi ndi zamankhwala. Ndiwofunika kwa zamoyo zonse zodziwika, ngakhale kuti sizipereka mphamvu pazakudya kapena kukhala organic micronutrient. Chifukwa cha kukhalapo kwake mu zamoyo zonse, kukhazikika kwake kwa mankhwala, kuchuluka kwake padziko lonse lapansi, ndi polarity yake yolimba yokhudzana ndi kukula kwake kwa maselo, madzi nthawi zambiri amatchedwa "zosungunulira zonse". Chifukwa chakuti kutentha kwapadziko lapansi ndi kupsyinjika kwake kuli pafupi ndi malo atatu a madzi, madzi alipo padziko lapansi monga olimba, madzi, ndi mpweya. Amapanga mpweya ngati mvula ndi ma aerosols mu mawonekedwe a chifunga. Mitambo imakhala ndi madontho oimitsidwa amadzi ndi ayezi, malo ake olimba. Akagawanika bwino, ayezi wa crystalline amatha kugwa ngati chipale chofewa. Mkhalidwe wa mpweya wa madzi ndi nthunzi kapena nthunzi wa madzi. Madzi amaphimba pafupifupi 71% ya padziko lapansi, ndipo nyanja ndi nyanja zimapanga madzi ambiri (pafupifupi 96.5%). Magawo ang'onoang'ono amadzi amapezeka ngati madzi apansi (1.7%), mumadzi oundana ndi madzi oundana a Antarctica ndi Greenland (1.7%), komanso mumlengalenga ngati nthunzi, mitambo (yokhala ndi ayezi ndi madzi amadzimadzi omwe amaimitsidwa mumlengalenga), komanso mvula (0.001%). Madzi amayenda mosalekeza kupyola mumpangidwe wa madzi a nthunzi, transpiration (evapotranspiration), condensation, mvula, ndi kusefukira, nthawi zambiri amafika kunyanja. Madzi amagwira ntchito yofunika kwambiri pazachuma padziko lonse lapansi. Pafupifupi 70 peresenti ya madzi abwino omwe anthu amagwiritsa ntchito amapita ku ulimi. Kusodza m'madzi amchere ndi m'madzi atsopano kwakhala, ndipo kukupitirizabe kukhala gwero lalikulu la chakudya kumadera ambiri a dziko lapansi, kupereka 6.5% ya mapuloteni apadziko lonse. Zambiri zamalonda azinthu zakutali (monga mafuta, gasi, ndi zinthu zopangidwa) zimatengedwa ndi mabwato kudutsa nyanja, mitsinje, nyanja, ndi ngalande. Madzi ochuluka, ayezi, ndi nthunzi amagwiritsidwa ntchito poziziritsa ndi kutenthetsa m’mafakitale ndi m’nyumba. Madzi ndi chosungunulira chabwino kwambiri cha zinthu zosiyanasiyana, mchere ndi organic; motero, amagwiritsidwa ntchito kwambiri m'mafakitale komanso kuphika ndi kuchapa. Madzi, madzi oundana, ndi chipale chofewa zilinso zofunika kwambiri m’maseŵero ambiri ndi mitundu ina ya zosangalatsa, monga kusambira, kukwera bwato kosangalatsa, kuthamanga kwa mabwato, kusefukira, kusodza pamasewera, kudumpha m’madzi, kutsetsereka pa ayezi, kukwera pachipale chofeŵa, ndi kutsetsereka. Etymology
Mawu akuti madzi amachokera ku Old English wæter, kuchokera ku Proto-Germanic *watar (gweronso la Old Saxon watar, Old Frisian wetir, Dutch water, Old High German wazzar, German Wasser, vatn, Gothic 𐍅𐌰𐍄𐍉 (wato)), kuchokera ku Proto-Indo-European *wod-wod-or'madzi 'wod-or,' madzi a Proto-Indo-European * wod-wod-or, ''. Komanso amagwirizana, kupyolera muzu wa Indo-European, ndi Greek ύδωρ (ýdor; kuchokera ku Ancient Greek ὕδωρ (hýdōr), wherece English 'hydro-'), Russian вода́ (vodá), Irish uisce, ndi Albanian ujë. Katundu
Madzi (H2O) ndi polar inorganic compound. Kutentha kwa chipinda ndi madzi osakoma komanso osanunkhiza, pafupifupi opanda mtundu ndi kamphindi kakang'ono ka buluu. Hydrogen chalcogenide yosavuta kwambiri, ndiyomwe imadziwika bwino kwambiri ndipo nthawi zina imatchedwa "chosungunulira padziko lonse lapansi" chifukwa cha kuthekera kwake kusungunula zinthu zambiri kuposa madzi ena aliwonse, ngakhale kuti sichitha kusungunula zinthu zopanda polar. Izi zimapangitsa kuti zikhale "zosungunulira zamoyo": ndithudi, madzi monga momwe amapezeka m'chilengedwe pafupifupi nthawi zonse amaphatikizapo zinthu zosiyanasiyana zosungunuka, ndipo masitepe apadera amafunikira kuti apeze madzi opanda mankhwala. Madzi ndi chinthu chokhacho chomwe chimakhalapo ngati cholimba, chamadzimadzi, komanso mpweya wabwino padziko lapansi. Mayiko
Pamodzi ndi oxidane, madzi ndi amodzi mwa mayina awiri ovomerezeka a mankhwala apawiri H2O; ndi gawo lamadzimadzi la H2O. Zina ziwiri zodziwika bwino za madzi ndi gawo lolimba, lomwe ndi ayezi, ndi gawo la mpweya, mpweya wamadzi kapena nthunzi. Kuwonjezera kapena kuchotsa kutentha kungayambitse kusintha kwa gawo: kuzizira (madzi kupita ku ayezi), kusungunuka ( ayezi kupita kumadzi), vaporization (madzi ku nthunzi), condensation (nthunzi kumadzi), sublimation ( ayezi ku nthunzi) ndi kuika ( nthunzi ku ayezi). Kuchulukana
Madzi ndi chimodzi mwazinthu zochepa zomwe zimachulukirachulukira ndikukhala zochepa kwambiri zikamazizira mkati mwa kutentha kwina; ndi chinthu chokhacho chomwe chimadziwika kuti chimachitika mwachilengedwe chomwe chimachita izi ndi madzi. Kuphatikiza apo, ndi zachilendo chifukwa zimakhala zochepa kwambiri zikamaundana, ngakhale sizili zachilendo pankhaniyi. Pakuthamanga kwa 1 atm, imafika ku 999.972 kg / m3 (62.4262 lb / cu ft) pa 3.98 ° C (39.16 ° F). Pansi pa kutentha kumeneko, koma pamwamba pa malo oundana a 0 °C (32 °F), madzi amakula (kucheperachepera) mpaka kufika pamalo oundana, pomwe kuchuluka kwake mumadzimadzi ndi 999.8 kg/m3 (62.4155 lb/cu ft). Madzi akazizira mpaka 3.98 °C (39.16 °F), kuchuluka kwa madzi kumachepa. Pamene amaundana ndikukhala ayezi, madzi amakula pafupifupi 9%, kufika ku 917 kg/m3 (57.25 lb/cu ft). Kukula kumeneku kungayambitse kupanikizika kwakukulu, mapaipi ophulika ndi miyala yosweka. Monga cholimba, chimasonyeza khalidwe lachizolowezi la mgwirizano ndikukhala wandiweyani pamene ukuzizira. Matenthedwe achilendowa ali ndi zotsatira zofunikira pa moyo padziko lapansi. M'nyanja kapena m'nyanja, madzi a 4 °C (39 °F) amamira pansi, ndipo ayezi amapanga pamwamba, akuyandama pamadzi amadzimadzi. Madzi oundana amenewa amateteza madzi amene ali pansi pa madziwo kuti asaundane. Popanda chitetezo chimenechi, zamoyo zambiri za m’madzi zimene zimakhala m’nyanjazi zikanatha m’nyengo yozizira. Kuonjezera apo, khalidwe lodabwitsali ndi gawo lofunika kwambiri la kayendedwe ka thermohaline komwe kumagawa kutentha kuzungulira nyanja za dziko lapansi. Magnetism
Madzi ndi chida cha diamagnetic. Ngakhale kuyanjana kuli kofooka, ndi maginito a superconducting amatha kupeza kuyanjana kodziwika. Kusintha kwa magawo
Pakupanikizika kwa mpweya umodzi (atm), ayezi amasungunuka kapena madzi amaundana (kukhazikika) pa 0 °C (32 °F) ndi zithupsa zamadzi kapena nthunzi ku 100 °C (212 °F). Komabe, ngakhale pansi pa kuwira, madzi amatha kusintha kukhala nthunzi pamwamba pake ndi evaporation (vaporization mumadzi onse amadziwika kuti kuwira). Sublimation ndi kuyika kumachitikanso pamtunda. Mwachitsanzo, chisanu chimayikidwa pamalo ozizira pomwe zitumbuwa za chipale chofewa zimapangika ndikuyika pa tinthu ta aerosol kapena phata la ayezi. M'kati mwa kuzizira kowuma, chakudya chimawumitsidwa ndikusungidwa pamtunda wochepa kwambiri kotero kuti ayezi pamwamba pake amatsika. Malo osungunuka ndi otentha amadalira kupanikizika. Madzi amatha kukhala amadzimadzi pakatentha kwambiri m'nyanja yakuya kapena pansi pa nthaka. Mwachitsanzo, kutentha kumapitirira 205 °C (401 °F) ku Old Faithful, geyser ku Yellowstone National Park. M'malo olowera mpweya wa hydrothermal, kutentha kumatha kupitirira 400 °C (752 °F). Pa mlingo wa nyanja, madzi akuwira ndi 100 °C (212 °F). Pamene kuthamanga kwa mumlengalenga kumachepa ndi msinkhu, malo otentha amatsika ndi 1 °C pa mamita 274 aliwonse. Kuphika kwapamwamba kumatenga nthawi yayitali kuposa kuphika panyanja. Mwachitsanzo, pa 1,524 metres (5,000 ft), nthawi yophika iyenera kuwonjezeredwa ndi gawo limodzi lachinayi kuti mukwaniritse zomwe mukufuna. Mosiyana ndi zimenezi, chophikira chopondera chingagwiritsidwe ntchito kuchepetsa nthawi yophika pokweza kutentha kwa kutentha. Mu vacuum, madzi amawira kutentha kwa firiji. Mfundo zitatu komanso zovuta
Pachithunzi cha kupanikizika/kutentha (onani chithunzi), pali zokhotakhota zolekanitsa zolimba kuchokera ku nthunzi, nthunzi ku madzi, ndi madzi kuchokera ku cholimba. Izi zimakumana pa malo amodzi otchedwa katatu, pamene magawo atatu onse amatha kukhala pamodzi. Mfundo katatu ndi kutentha kwa 273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F) ndi kupanikizika kwa 611.657 pascals (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); ndi mphamvu yotsika kwambiri yomwe madzi amadzimadzi amatha kukhalapo. Mpaka 2019, mfundo zitatuzi zidagwiritsidwa ntchito kutanthauzira sikelo ya kutentha kwa Kelvin. Madzi / mpweya wopindika umathera pa 647.096 K (373.946 °C; 705.103 °F) ndi 22.064 megapascals (3,200.1 psi; 217.75 atm). Izi zimatchedwa mfundo yofunika kwambiri. Pa kutentha kwambiri ndi kukanikiza madzi ndi nthunzi magawo amapanga mosalekeza gawo lotchedwa supercritical madzimadzi. Ikhoza kupanikizidwa pang'onopang'ono kapena kukulitsidwa pakati pa kachulukidwe ngati gasi ndi madzi; katundu wake (omwe ndi osiyana kwambiri ndi madzi ozungulira) tcheru ndi kachulukidwe. Mwachitsanzo, pazovuta zoyenera ndi kutentha zimatha kusakanikirana momasuka ndi mankhwala osagwirizana ndi polar, kuphatikiza ma organic compounds ambiri. Izi zimapangitsa kuti zikhale zothandiza pazinthu zosiyanasiyana kuphatikizapo kutentha kwapamwamba kwambiri kwa electrochemistry komanso monga zosungunulira zachilengedwe kapena zosungunulira muzochitika zamakina zomwe zimakhudzana ndi organic compounds. Muchovala chapadziko lapansi, chimagwira ntchito ngati chosungunulira pakupanga mchere, kusungunuka ndi kuyika. Magawo a ayezi ndi madzi
Mtundu wabwinobwino wa ayezi padziko lapansi ndi ayezi Ih, gawo lomwe limapanga makhiristo okhala ndi masinthidwe a hexagonal. Wina wokhala ndi cubic crystalline symmetry, ice Ic, ukhoza kuchitika kumtunda. Kupanikizika kumawonjezeka, ayezi amapanga makristasi ena. Pofika mu 2024, makumi awiri adatsimikiziridwa moyesera ndipo ena angapo amanenedweratu mwachibwana. Mawonekedwe khumi ndi asanu ndi atatu a ayezi, ayezi XVIII, gawo lapakati pa cubic, superionic ayezi, adapezeka pamene dontho lamadzi lidakhudzidwa ndi kugwedezeka kwamadzi komwe kudakweza madzi kumlengalenga mamiliyoni ambiri komanso kutentha kwake kufika madigiri masauzande, zomwe zidapangitsa kuti maatomu olimba a oxygen momwe maatomu a haidrojeni amayenda momasuka. Akaikidwa pakati pa zigawo za graphene, ayezi amapanga masikweya apakati. Tsatanetsatane wa mankhwala amadzimadzi amadzimadzi samamveka bwino; ziphunzitso zina zimasonyeza kuti khalidwe lake lachilendo ndi chifukwa cha kukhalapo kwa zigawo ziwiri zamadzimadzi. Kulawa ndi fungo
Madzi oyera nthawi zambiri amanenedwa kuti ndi osakoma komanso osanunkhiza, ngakhale kuti anthu ali ndi masensa enieni omwe amatha kumva kupezeka kwa madzi mkamwa mwawo, ndipo achule amadziwika kuti amatha kununkhiza. Komabe, madzi ochokera kumadera wamba (kuphatikiza madzi amchere) nthawi zambiri amakhala ndi zinthu zambiri zosungunuka zomwe zimatha kupangitsa kuti azikonda komanso kununkhiza mosiyanasiyana. Anthu ndi nyama zina zapanga mphamvu zodziwira mphamvu zomwe zimawathandiza kuwunika momwe madzi amakhalira kuti apewe madzi amchere kwambiri kapena ovunda. Mtundu ndi maonekedwe
Madzi oyera amaoneka abuluu chifukwa cha kuyamwa kwa kuwala m'derali c. 600-800 nm. Mtunduwu ukhoza kuwonedwa mosavuta mu kapu yamadzi apampopi yomwe imayikidwa kumbuyo koyera koyera, masana. Magulu akuluakulu amayamwitsa omwe amachititsa mtunduwo ndi ma overtones a O-H kutambasula kugwedezeka. Kuchuluka kwa mtunduwo kumawonjezeka ndi kuya kwa madzi, kutsatira lamulo la Mowa. Izi zimagwiranso ntchito, mwachitsanzo, ndi dziwe losambira pamene gwero la kuwala ndi kuwala kwa dzuwa kumawonekera kuchokera ku matailosi oyera a dziwe. Mwachilengedwe, mtunduwo ukhoza kusinthidwanso kuchokera ku buluu kupita ku wobiriwira chifukwa cha kukhalapo kwa zolimba zoyimitsidwa kapena algae. M'makampani, mawonedwe apafupi ndi infrared spectroscopy amagwiritsidwa ntchito ndi njira zamadzimadzi chifukwa kuchulukira kwamadzi otsika kumatanthauza kuti ma cuvettes agalasi okhala ndi njira zazifupi atha kugwiritsidwa ntchito. Kuti muwone kuchuluka kwa mayamwidwe ofunikira amadzi kapena njira yamadzimadzi m'dera lozungulira 3,500 cm−1 (2.85 μm) pafunika njira yayitali pafupifupi 25 μm. Komanso, cuvette iyenera kukhala yowonekera mozungulira 3500 cm−1 komanso yosasungunuka m'madzi; calcium fluoride ndi chinthu chimodzi chomwe chimagwiritsidwa ntchito kwambiri pamawindo a cuvette okhala ndi njira zamadzi. Kugwedezeka koyambira kwa Raman kumatha kuwonedwa ndi, mwachitsanzo, cell yachitsanzo cha 1 cm. Zomera zam'madzi, algae, ndi zamoyo zina za photosynthetic zimatha kukhala m'madzi mpaka mazana a mita kuya, chifukwa kuwala kwa dzuwa kumatha kuzifika. Pafupifupi palibe kuwala kwa dzuwa komwe kumafika kumadera a nyanja pansi pa kuya kwa mamita 1,000 (3,300 ft). Mlozera wa refractive wa madzi amadzimadzi (1.333 pa 20 °C (68 °F)) ndiwokwera kwambiri kuposa mpweya (1.0), wofanana ndi wa alkanes ndi ethanol, koma wotsika kuposa wa glycerol (1.473), 1.501), carbon disulfide (1.627), ndi mitundu yodziwika bwino yagalasi 1 (1.6). The refraction index of ice (1.31) ndi yotsika kuposa madzi amadzimadzi. Molecular polarity
Mu molekyulu yamadzi, maatomu a haidrojeni amapanga ngodya ya 104.5 ° ndi atomu ya oxygen. Ma atomu a haidrojeni ali pafupi ndi ngodya ziwiri za tetrahedron zokhazikika pa mpweya. Pa ngodya zina ziwiri pali ma electron okha a valence omwe satenga nawo mbali pa mgwirizano. Mu tetrahedron yabwino kwambiri, maatomu amatha kupanga ngodya ya 109.5 °, koma kukanidwa pakati pa awiriawiri amodzi ndikokulirapo kuposa kuthamangitsidwa pakati pa maatomu a haidrojeni. Kutalika kwa bond ya O-H ndi pafupifupi 0.096 nm. Zinthu zina zimakhala ndi tetrahedral molecular structure, mwachitsanzo methane (CH4) ndi hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Komabe, okosijeni ndi mphamvu yamagetsi kwambiri kuposa maelementi ena ambiri, motero atomu ya okosijeni imakhala ndi gawo loyipa pomwe maatomu a haidrojeni ali ndi magetsi pang'ono. Pamodzi ndi mawonekedwe opindika, izi zimapereka molekyu mphindi ya dipole yamagetsi ndipo imayikidwa ngati molekyulu ya polar. Madzi ndi abwino polar zosungunulira, Kusungunula mchere ambiri ndi hydrophilic organic mamolekyu monga shuga ndi mowa wosavuta monga Mowa. Madzi amasungunulanso mipweya yambiri, monga oxygen ndi carbon dioxide - yotsirizirayi imapatsa mpweya wa carbonated, vinyo wonyezimira ndi moŵa. Kuphatikiza apo, zinthu zambiri zamoyo, monga mapuloteni, DNA ndi ma polysaccharides, zimasungunuka m'madzi. Kuyanjana pakati pa madzi ndi ma subunits a biomacromolecules awa kumapanga kupindika kwa mapuloteni, DNA base pairing, ndi zochitika zina zofunika kwambiri pa moyo (hydrophobic effect). Zinthu zambiri zachilengedwe (monga mafuta ndi mafuta ndi alkanes) ndi hydrophobic, ndiye kuti, zosasungunuka m'madzi. Zinthu zambiri za inorganic sizisungunukanso, kuphatikiza ma oxides ambiri azitsulo, sulfide, ndi silicates. Kugwirizana kwa haidrojeni
Chifukwa cha polarity yake, molekyu yamadzi mumadzimadzi kapena olimba imatha kupanga ma hydrogen 4 ogwirizana ndi mamolekyu oyandikana nawo. Zomangira za haidrojeni zimakhala zolimba kuwirikiza kakhumi kuposa mphamvu ya Van der Waals yomwe imakopa mamolekyu pazamadzimadzi zambiri. Ichi ndi chifukwa chake madzi osungunuka ndi otentha amakhala okwera kwambiri kuposa amitundu ina yofananira ngati hydrogen sulfide. Amafotokozanso mphamvu yake yotentha kwambiri (pafupifupi 4.2 J/(g·K)), kutentha kwa maphatikizidwe (pafupifupi 333 J/g), kutentha kwa vaporization (2257 J/g), ndi kutulutsa kwamafuta (pakati pa 0.561 ndi 0.679 W/(m·K)). Zinthu zimenezi zimathandiza kuti madzi azitha kuwongolera bwino nyengo ya Dziko Lapansi, posunga kutentha n’kukayendetsa pakati pa nyanja ndi mlengalenga. Zomangira za haidrojeni zamadzi zimakhala pafupifupi 23 kJ / mol (poyerekeza ndi mgwirizano wa O-H pa 492 kJ / mol). Mwa izi, akuti 90% imabwera chifukwa cha electrostatics, pomwe 10% yotsalayo ndi yogwirizana pang'ono. Zomangira izi ndizomwe zimachititsa kuti madzi azithamanga kwambiri pamtunda komanso mphamvu za capillary. Capillary action imatanthawuza chizolowezi cha madzi kusuntha chubu chopapatiza motsutsana ndi mphamvu yokoka. Katunduyu amadaliridwa ndi zomera zonse zokhala ndi mitsempha, monga mitengo. Self-ionization
Madzi ndi njira yofooka ya hydronium hydroxide -pali mgwirizano wa 2H2O ⇌ H3O+ + OH−, kuphatikiza ndi kusungunuka kwa ayoni a hydronium ndi hydroxide. Magetsi conductivity ndi electrolysis
Madzi oyera amakhala ndi magetsi otsika, omwe amawonjezeka ndi kusungunuka kwa zinthu zochepa za ayoni monga mchere wamba. Madzi amadzimadzi amatha kugawanika kukhala zinthu za haidrojeni ndi okosijeni podutsa mphamvu yamagetsi kudzera mmenemo—njira yotchedwa electrolysis. Kuwonongeka kumafuna mphamvu yowonjezera mphamvu kuposa kutentha komwe kumatulutsidwa ndi njira yosiyana (285.8 kJ / mol, kapena 15.9 MJ / kg). Zimango katundu
Madzi amadzimadzi amatha kuganiziridwa kukhala osasunthika pazifukwa zambiri: kuphatikizika kwake kumayambira 4.4 mpaka 5.1 × 10−10 Pa−1 mumikhalidwe wamba. Ngakhale m'nyanja zakuya kwa 4 km, komwe kuthamanga ndi 400 atm, madzi amavutika ndi kuchepa kwa 1.8%. Kukhuthala kwa madzi ndi pafupifupi 10−3 Pa·s kapena 0.01 poise pa 20 °C (68 °F), ndipo liwiro la phokoso m'madzi amadzimadzi limakhala pakati pa 1,400 ndi 1,540 mamita pamphindi (4,600 ndi 5,100 ft / s) kutengera kutentha. Phokoso limayenda mtunda wautali m'madzi popanda kuchepetsedwa pang'ono, makamaka pamafuriji otsika (pafupifupi 0.03 dB/km kwa 1 kHz), malo omwe amadyetsedwa ndi ma cetaceans ndi anthu kuti azitha kulumikizana komanso kumva chilengedwe (sonar). Reactivity
Zinthu zachitsulo zomwe zimakhala ndi electropositive kuposa haidrojeni, makamaka zitsulo zamchere ndipo, pang'ono, zitsulo zamchere zamchere, zimachotsa haidrojeni m'madzi, kupanga ma hydroxides ndikutulutsa haidrojeni. Kukatentha kwambiri, coke, mtundu wa carbon, umachita ndi nthunzi kupanga carbon monoxide ndi hydrogen. Padziko Lapansi
Hydrology ndi kafukufuku wa kayendedwe, kagawidwe, ndi mtundu wa madzi padziko lonse lapansi. Phunziro la kugawa madzi ndi hydrography. Kafukufuku wokhudza kugawa ndi kuyenda kwa madzi apansi ndi hydrogeology, glaciers ndi glaciology, madzi amkati ndi limnology ndi kugawa kwa nyanja ndi nyanja. Njira zachilengedwe ndi hydrology ndizoyang'ana pa ecohydrology. Kuchuluka kwa madzi opezeka pansi, pansi, ndi pamwamba pa dziko lapansi kumatchedwa hydrosphere. Pafupifupi madzi a padziko lapansi (madzi onse padziko lapansi) ndi ma kiyubiki kilomita 1.386 biliyoni (333 miliyoni cubic miles). Madzi amadzimadzi amapezeka m'madzi amadzi, monga nyanja, nyanja, nyanja, mtsinje, mtsinje, ngalande, dziwe, kapena dziwe. Madzi ambiri pa Dziko Lapansi ndi a m’nyanja. Madzi amapezekanso mumlengalenga m'malo olimba, amadzimadzi, komanso a nthunzi. Imapezekanso ngati madzi apansi panthaka m'madzi. Madzi ndi ofunikira muzochitika zambiri za geological. Madzi apansi panthaka amapezeka m'miyala yambiri, ndipo kuthamanga kwa madzi apansi panthaka kumakhudza machitidwe a zolakwika. Madzi omwe ali mu chovalacho ndi omwe amachititsa kusungunuka komwe kumatulutsa mapiri m'madera ochepa. Padziko lapansi, madzi ndi ofunikira pazochitika zanyengo komanso zakuthupi. Madzi, komanso pamlingo wocheperako koma wofunikira kwambiri, ayezi, amapangitsanso kuchuluka kwa zinyalala zomwe zimachitika padziko lapansi. Kuyika kwa matope onyamula kumapanga mitundu yambiri ya miyala ya sedimentary, yomwe imapanga mbiri yakale ya dziko lapansi. Kuzungulira kwamadzi
Kuzungulira kwa madzi (kodziwika mwasayansi monga hydrologic cycle) ndiko kusinthana kosalekeza kwa madzi mkati mwa hydrosphere, pakati pa mlengalenga, madzi a nthaka, madzi apamwamba, madzi apansi, ndi zomera. Madzi amayenda mosalekeza kupyola madera onsewa mumayendedwe amadzi okhala ndi njira zotsatirazi:

Kutuluka kwa nthunzi kuchokera kunyanja ndi m'madzi ena kupita mumlengalenga ndi kutuluka kwa zomera ndi zinyama zakumtunda kupita mumlengalenga. mvula, kuchokera ku nthunzi wamadzi kugwa kuchokera mumlengalenga ndikugwera ku dziko lapansi kapena nyanja. madzi osefukira kuchokera kumtunda nthawi zambiri amafika kunyanja. Nthunzi yambiri yamadzi yomwe imapezeka makamaka m'nyanjayi imabwereranso, koma mphepo imanyamula nthunzi yamadzi pamtunda mofanana ndi madzi othamangira m'nyanja, pafupifupi 47 Tt pachaka pamene evaporation ndi kutuluka kwa mpweya kukuchitika kumtunda kumathandizanso 72 Tt wina pachaka. Mvula, pamlingo wa 119 Tt pachaka pamtunda, imakhala ndi mitundu ingapo: nthawi zambiri mvula, matalala, matalala, ndi zopereka kuchokera ku chifunga ndi mame. Mame ndi timadontho tating'ono tamadzi timene timafupikitsidwa pamene mpweya wochuluka wa madzi ukumana ndi malo ozizira. Kaŵirikaŵiri mame amapangidwa m’maŵa pamene kutentha kuli kotsika kwambiri, dzuŵa litangotuluka ndi pamene kutentha kwa dziko lapansi kukayamba kukwera. Madzi okhazikika mumlengalenga amathanso kutulutsa kuwala kwa dzuwa kuti apange utawaleza. Madzi osefukira nthawi zambiri amasonkhana m'mitsinje yamadzi yomwe ikuyenda m'mitsinje. Kupyolera mu kukokoloka kwa nthaka, madzi osefukira amapangitsa chilengedwe kupanga zigwa za mitsinje ndi mathithi omwe amapereka dothi lolemera komanso malo oti anthu azikhalamo. Kusefukira kwa madzi kumachitika pamene malo amtunda, omwe nthawi zambiri amakhala otsika, amakutidwa ndi madzi omwe amapezeka mtsinje ukasefukira m'mphepete mwake kapena mphepo yamkuntho ikachitika. Kumbali ina, chilala ndi nyengo yotalikirapo ya miyezi kapena zaka pamene dera liwona kupereŵera kwa madzi ake. Izi zimachitika pamene chigawo chimalandira mvula yochepera pang'ono mwina chifukwa cha malo ake kapena chifukwa cha malo ake malinga ndi latitude. Zida zamadzi
Madzi ndi zinthu zachilengedwe zamadzi zomwe zimatha kukhala zothandiza kwa anthu, mwachitsanzo ngati gwero la madzi akumwa kapena madzi amthirira. Madzi amapezeka ngati "masheya" ndi "mayendedwe". Madzi amatha kusungidwa ngati nyanja, nthunzi yamadzi, madzi apansi panthaka kapena pansi, ndi ayezi ndi matalala. Pa chiwonkhetso cha madzi abwino a padziko lonse, pafupifupi 69 peresenti amasungidwa m’malo oundana ndi chipale chofeŵa chosatha; 30 peresenti ali m’madzi apansi; ndi 1 peresenti yotsalayo m’nyanja, mitsinje, mumlengalenga, ndi zachilengedwe. Utali wa nthawi imene madzi osungirako amasungidwa amasinthasintha kwambiri: madera ena a pansi pa madzi amakhala ndi madzi osungidwa kwa zaka zikwi zambiri, koma madzi a m’nyanja amatha kusinthasintha malinga ndi nyengo, kucheperachepera m’nyengo yamvula ndi kuwonjezereka m’nyengo yamvula. Gawo lalikulu la madzi a m'madera ena ndi la madzi otengedwa m'madzi osungidwa m'matangadza, ndipo pamene kuchotsa kupyola kuwonjezereka, masheya amachepa. Malinga ndi ziwerengero zina, pafupifupi 30 peresenti ya madzi onse omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito kuthirira amachokera ku madzi apansi osasunthika, zomwe zimapangitsa kuti madzi apansi achepe. Madzi a m'nyanja ndi mafunde
M'madzi a m'nyanja muli pafupifupi 3.5% sodium chloride pafupifupi, kuphatikiza zinthu zina zazing'ono. Maonekedwe a madzi a m’nyanja amasiyana ndi madzi abwino pa zinthu zina zofunika. Imaundana ndi kutentha kocheperako (pafupifupi −1.9 °C (28.6 °F)) ndipo kachulukidwe kake kamawonjezeka ndi kutsika kwa kutentha kufika kumalo oundana, m’malo mofikira kuchulukitsitsa kwakukulu pa kutentha koposa kuzizira. Mchere wamadzi a m'nyanja zazikulu umasiyana kuchokera pa 0.7% mu Nyanja ya Baltic mpaka 4.0% mu Nyanja Yofiira. (Nyanja Yakufa, yomwe imadziwika ndi kuchuluka kwake kwa mchere wambiri pakati pa 30 ndi 40%, ndi nyanja yamchere.)
Mafunde ndi kukwera ndi kutsika kwamadzi am'madzi am'deralo komwe kumachitika chifukwa cha mphamvu ya Mwezi ndi Dzuwa zomwe zimachitika panyanja. Mafunde amayambitsa kusintha kwa kuya kwa madzi a m'nyanja ndi m'mitsinje ndipo amatulutsa mitsinje yozungulira yotchedwa tidal streams. Kusintha kwa mafunde opangidwa pa malo operekedwa ndi zotsatira za kusintha kwa Mwezi ndi Dzuwa poyerekeza ndi Dziko Lapansi pamodzi ndi zotsatira za kuzungulira kwa Earth ndi bathymetry yakomweko. Mzere wa m'mphepete mwa nyanja womwe umamizidwa ndi mafunde okwera kwambiri ndipo umawonekera pamafunde otsika, malo omwe ali pakati pa mafunde, ndi chinthu chofunikira kwambiri pazachilengedwe ndi mafunde am'nyanja. Zotsatira pa moyo
Malinga ndi zamoyo, madzi ali ndi zinthu zambiri zosiyana zomwe ndizofunikira kuti moyo ukhale wochuluka. Imagwira ntchitoyi polola kuti ma organic compounds achite m'njira zomwe zimalola kubwerezabwereza. Zamoyo zonse zodziwika zimadalira madzi. Madzi ndi ofunikira monga zosungunulira zomwe zambiri za m'thupi zimasungunuka komanso monga gawo lofunikira la kagayidwe kachakudya m'thupi. Metabolism ndi chiwerengero chonse cha anabolism ndi catabolism. Mu anabolism, madzi amachotsedwa ku mamolekyu (kupyolera mu mphamvu yomwe imafuna enzymatic chemical reaction) kuti ikule mamolekyu akuluakulu (mwachitsanzo, starch, triglycerides, ndi mapuloteni osungira mafuta ndi chidziwitso). Mu catabolism, madzi amagwiritsidwa ntchito kuthyola zomangira kuti apange mamolekyu ang'onoang'ono (mwachitsanzo, shuga, mafuta acids, ndi ma amino acid omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito ngati mafuta opangira mphamvu kapena ntchito zina). Popanda madzi, kagayidwe kachakudya kameneka sikangakhaleko. Madzi ndi ofunika kwambiri pa photosynthesis ndi kupuma. Maselo a photosynthetic amagwiritsa ntchito mphamvu ya dzuŵa kugaŵa mpweya wa haidrojeni m’madzi ndi mpweya. Pakakhala kuwala kwa dzuwa, haidrojeni imaphatikizidwa ndi CO2 (yomwe imatengedwa mumlengalenga kapena m'madzi) kupanga shuga ndikutulutsa mpweya. Maselo onse amoyo amagwiritsa ntchito mafuta oterowo ndi oxidize haidrojeni ndi kaboni kuti agwire mphamvu za dzuwa ndikusintha madzi ndi CO2 panthawiyi (kupuma kwa ma cell). Madzi amakhalanso pakatikati pa kusalowerera ndale kwa acid-base ndi ntchito ya enzyme. Acid, hydrogen ion (H +, ndiko kuti, proton) wopereka, akhoza kuchepetsedwa ndi maziko, ovomerezeka a proton monga hydroxide ion (OH-) kupanga madzi. Madzi amaonedwa kuti salowerera ndale, okhala ndi pH (chipika choyipa cha hydrogen ion concentration) cha 7 m'malo abwino. Ma Acids ali ndi pH yochepera 7 pomwe maziko ali ndi miyeso yayikulu kuposa 7. Mitundu yamoyo yam'madzi
Madzi a pamwamba pa dziko lapansi ali odzaza ndi zamoyo. Mitundu yoyambirira ya moyo idawonekera m'madzi; pafupifupi nsomba zonse zimakhala m’madzi okha, ndipo pali mitundu yambiri ya nyama za m’nyanja, monga ma dolphin ndi anamgumi. Mitundu ina ya nyama, monga zamoyo zam’madzi, zimathera mbali ya moyo wawo m’madzi ndi zina zili pamtunda. Zomera monga kelp ndi algae zimamera m'madzi ndipo ndizo maziko a zamoyo zina za pansi pa madzi. Plankton nthawi zambiri ndiye maziko a chakudya cham'nyanja. Zamoyo za m’madzi zimafunika kupeza mpweya wabwino kuti zikhale ndi moyo, ndipo zimatero m’njira zosiyanasiyana. Nsomba zili ndi zotupa m'malo mwa mapapu, ngakhale mitundu ina ya nsomba, monga lungfish, ili ndi zonse ziwiri. Nyama zam'madzi, monga ma dolphin, anamgumi, otters, ndi seal zimafunikira kumtunda nthawi ndi nthawi kuti zipume mpweya. Amphibians ena amatha kuyamwa mpweya kudzera pakhungu lawo. Ma Invertebrates amawonetsa kusintha kosiyanasiyana kuti apulumuke m'madzi opanda mpweya wabwino kuphatikiza machubu opumira (onani tizilombo ndi mollusc siphons) ndi magill (Carcinus). Komabe, monga mmene zamoyo zopanda msana zinasinthira m’malo okhala m’madzi, ambiri amakhala ndi luso lochepa kapena alibe luso la kupuma m’madzi. Zotsatira pa chitukuko cha anthu
Chitukuko chakhala chikuyenda bwino m'mitsinje ndi mitsinje yayikulu; Mesopotamiya, umodzi mwa malo otchedwa chiyambi cha chitukuko, unali pakati pa mitsinje ikuluikulu ya Tigris ndi Firate; anthu akale a Aigupto ankadalira mtsinje wa Nile. Chitukuko choyambirira cha Indus Valley (c. 3300 BCE - c. 1300 BCE) chinayambira m'mphepete mwa Mtsinje wa Indus ndi mtsinje womwe umachokera ku Himalayas. Roma inakhazikitsidwanso m’mphepete mwa mtsinje wa Tiber wa ku Italy. Mizinda ikuluikulu monga Rotterdam, London, Montreal, Paris, New York City, Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Tokyo, Chicago, ndi Hong Kong chifukwa cha kupambana kwawoko mwa zina chifukwa cha kupezeka kwawo mosavuta kudzera m'madzi komanso kutukuka kwa malonda. Zilumba zokhala ndi madoko amadzi otetezeka, monga Singapore, zakula pazifukwa zomwezo. M’madera monga Kumpoto kwa Africa ndi ku Middle East, kumene madzi ndi osoŵa, kupeza madzi akumwa aukhondo kunali kothandiza kwambiri pa chitukuko cha anthu. Thanzi ndi kuipitsa
Madzi oyenera kumwa anthu amatchedwa madzi akumwa kapena amchere. Madzi omwe satha kuthyoka amatha kuthyoledwa ndi kusefedwa kapena kusungunula, kapena ndi njira zina zosiyanasiyana. Anthu oposa 660 miliyoni alibe madzi akumwa abwino. Madzi osayenera kumwa koma osavulaza anthu akagwiritsidwa ntchito posambira kapena kusamba amatchulidwa ndi mayina osiyanasiyana osati madzi amchere kapena akumwa, ndipo nthawi zina amatchedwa madzi otetezeka, kapena "otetezeka posamba". Chlorine ndi chinthu chomwe chimakwiyitsa khungu ndi mucous membrane chomwe chimagwiritsidwa ntchito kupanga madzi kukhala abwino posamba kapena kumwa. Kugwiritsiridwa ntchito kwake ndikwaukadaulo kwambiri ndipo nthawi zambiri kumayang'aniridwa ndi malamulo aboma (kawirikawiri gawo limodzi pa miliyoni (ppm) la madzi akumwa, ndi 1-2 ppm wa chlorine womwe sunakhudzidwebe ndi zonyansa zamadzi osamba). Madzi osamba amatha kusungidwa mumkhalidwe wokhutiritsa wa tizilombo toyambitsa matenda pogwiritsa ntchito mankhwala ophera tizilombo toyambitsa matenda monga chlorine kapena ozone kapena kugwiritsa ntchito kuwala kwa ultraviolet. Kubwezeretsanso madzi ndi njira yosinthira madzi otayira (amene nthawi zambiri amataya zimbudzi, omwe amatchedwanso kuti madzi onyansa a municipalities) kukhala madzi omwe angagwiritsidwenso ntchito pazinthu zina. Pali anthu 2.3 biliyoni omwe amakhala m'mayiko omwe ali ndi kusowa kwa madzi, zomwe zikutanthauza kuti munthu aliyense amalandira madzi ochepera 1,700 cubic metres (60,000 cu ft) pachaka. Ma cubic metres 380 biliyoni (13 × 10 ^ 12 cu ft) amadzi oipa amapangidwa padziko lonse chaka chilichonse. Madzi abwino ndi chinthu chongowonjezedwanso, chomwe chimayendetsedwanso ndi kayendedwe kachilengedwe ka hydrologic, koma zovuta zofikira kumadzi zimabwera chifukwa cha kugawika kosiyana kwachilengedwe mumlengalenga ndi nthawi, kukula kwachuma kwaulimi ndi mafakitale, komanso kukwera kwa anthu. Panopa, anthu pafupifupi biliyoni imodzi padziko lonse alibe madzi abwino komanso otsika mtengo. Mu 2000, bungwe la United Nations linakhazikitsa zolinga za Millennium Development Goals kuti madzi achepetse ndi theka pofika chaka cha 2015 chiwerengero cha anthu padziko lonse lapansi opanda madzi abwino ndi ukhondo. Kupita patsogolo kwa cholinga chimenecho kunali kosagwirizana, ndipo mu 2015 bungwe la United Nations linadzipereka ku Zolinga za Sustainable Development kuti apeze mwayi wopezeka padziko lonse wa madzi otetezeka ndi otsika mtengo komanso aukhondo pofika chaka cha 2030. Kusakwanira kwa madzi ndi ukhondo woipa ndizoopsa; anthu pafupifupi mamiliyoni asanu amafa chaka chilichonse chifukwa cha matenda obwera chifukwa cha madzi. Bungwe la World Health Organization linanena kuti madzi abwino angathandize kuti ana 1.4 miliyoni aphedwe ndi matenda otsegula m’mimba chaka chilichonse. M'mayiko omwe akutukuka kumene, 90% ya madzi otayira onse amapitabe ku mitsinje ndi mitsinje. Mayiko pafupifupi 50, okhala ndi pafupifupi munthu mmodzi mwa anthu atatu alionse padziko lapansi, alinso ndi kusowa kwa madzi apakati kapena ochuluka ndipo 17 mwa mayikowa amatulutsa madzi ochuluka pachaka kuposa amene amachajidwa m’kayendetsedwe ka madzi achilengedwe. Vutoli silimangokhudza matupi a madzi opanda mchere monga mitsinje ndi nyanja, komanso limawononganso madzi apansi panthaka. Zogwiritsa ntchito anthu
Ulimi
Madzi omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito kwambiri ndi anthu ndi ulimi, kuphatikizapo ulimi wothirira, womwe umatenga pafupifupi 80 mpaka 90 peresenti ya anthu onse omwe amamwa madzi. Ku United States, 42% ya madzi abwino omwe amachotsedwa kuti agwiritsidwe ntchito ndi ulimi wothirira, koma madzi ambiri "omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito" (omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito komanso osabwerera ku chilengedwe) amapita ku ulimi. Kupeza madzi abwino kaŵirikaŵiri kumatengedwa mopepuka, makamaka m’maiko otukuka kumene apanga njira zapamwamba zamadzi zotungira, kuyeretsa, ndi kupereka madzi, ndi kuchotsa madzi oipa. Koma mavuto azachuma, chiwerengero cha anthu, ndi nyengo akuchulukirachulukira pa nkhani za madzi, zomwe zikuchititsa kuti pakhale mpikisano wofuna kupeza madzi okhazikika, zomwe zimapangitsa kuti pakhale lingaliro la madzi apamwamba. Pamene chiwerengero cha anthu ndi chuma chikuchulukirachulukira, kudya nyama yaludzu ndi madzi kukukulirakulira, ndipo zofuna zatsopano zamafuta amafuta kapena mafakitale atsopano ogwiritsa ntchito madzi, zovuta zamadzi zatsopano zitha kuchitika. Kuwunika kwa kayendetsedwe ka madzi mu ulimi kunachitika mu 2007 ndi International Water Management Institute ku Sri Lanka kuti awone ngati dziko lapansi lili ndi madzi okwanira kuti apereke chakudya kwa anthu omwe akukula. Linaonanso za kupezeka kwa madzi paulimi padziko lonse lapansi komanso kuwunikira malo omwe akuvutika ndi kusowa kwa madzi. Idapeza kuti gawo limodzi mwa magawo asanu mwa anthu padziko lapansi, opitilira 1.2 biliyoni, amakhala m'malo akusowa kwamadzi, komwe kulibe madzi okwanira kukwaniritsa zofunikira zonse. Anthu enanso okwana 1.6 biliyoni akukhala m’madera amene madzi akusoŵa pazachuma, kumene kusowa kwa madzi m’thupi kapena kusakwanira kwa anthu kumapangitsa kuti akuluakulu aboma asamapeze madzi okwanira. Lipotilo linapeza kuti n’zotheka kupanga zakudya zofunika m’tsogolo, koma kupitiriza kwa ulimi wamakono wa chakudya ndiponso mmene chilengedwe chikuyendera kungayambitse mavuto m’madera ambiri padziko lapansi. Pofuna kupewa vuto la madzi padziko lonse, alimi ayenera kuyesetsa kuti awonjezere zokolola kuti akwaniritse zofuna za chakudya, pamene mafakitale ndi mizinda amapeza njira zogwiritsira ntchito madzi bwino. Kusoŵa kwa madzi kumadzanso chifukwa chopanga zinthu zomwe zimagwiritsa ntchito madzi kwambiri. Mwachitsanzo, thonje: 1 kg ya thonje—yofanana ndi jinzi—imafuna ma cubic metres 10.9 (380 cu ft) kuti apange madzi. Ngakhale kuti thonje imagwiritsa ntchito 2.4% ya madzi padziko lonse lapansi, madzi amadyedwa m'madera omwe ali pachiopsezo cha kusowa kwa madzi. Kuwonongeka kwakukulu kwa chilengedwe kwachitika: mwachitsanzo, kupatutsidwa kwamadzi ndi omwe kale anali Soviet Union kuchokera ku mitsinje ya Amu Darya ndi Syr Darya kuti apange thonje ndiko makamaka kunachititsa kuti nyanja ya Aral iwonongeke. Monga muyezo wasayansi
Pa 7 Epulo 1795, gilamu idatanthauzidwa ku France kukhala yofanana ndi "kulemera kwathunthu kwa voliyumu yamadzi oyera ofanana ndi cube ya mita zana limodzi, komanso kutentha kwa ayezi wosungunuka". Komabe, kuti zigwiritsidwe ntchito, mulingo wazitsulo wachitsulo unkafunika, kuwirikiza chikwi chimodzi, kilogalamu. Choncho ntchito inaperekedwa kuti adziwe ndendende kuchuluka kwa lita imodzi ya madzi. Ngakhale kuti kutanthauzira kovomerezeka kwa galamu kunatchula madzi pa 0 ° C (32 ° F) -kutentha kochuluka kwambiri - asayansi anasankha kumasuliranso muyezo ndi kuyesa madzi awo pa kutentha kwa madzi ochuluka kwambiri, omwe ankayezedwa panthawiyo monga 4 ° C (39 ° F). Kutentha kwa Kelvin kwa dongosolo la SI kudakhazikitsidwa pamadzi atatu, omwe amafotokozedwa ndendende 273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F), koma kuyambira Meyi 2019 adatengera Boltzmann nthawi zonse. Sikelo yake ndi sikelo ya kutentha kofananako ndi sikelo ya kutentha kwa Selsiasi, yomwe poyambirira idafotokozedwa molingana ndi kuwira (kuyikidwa ku 100 °C (212 °F)) ndi malo osungunuka (kukhazikitsidwa ku 0 °C (32 °F)) yamadzi. Madzi achilengedwe amakhala makamaka ndi isotopes hydrogen-1 ndi oxygen-16, koma palinso kachulukidwe kakang'ono ka isotopi wolemera oxygen-18, oxygen-17, ndi hydrogen-2 (deuterium). Chiwerengero cha isotopi cholemera kwambiri ndi chochepa kwambiri, koma chimakhudzabe mphamvu za madzi. Madzi a m'mitsinje ndi m'nyanja amakhala ndi ma isotopu olemera kwambiri kuposa am'nyanja. Chifukwa chake, madzi wamba amatanthauzidwa mumayendedwe a Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water. Zakumwa
Thupi la munthu lili ndi, pafupifupi, madzi 50-60%, kutengera zaka, jenda ndi kukula kwa thupi, ngakhale anthu akhoza kukhala pakati pa 45% ndi 75%. Bungwe la U.S. National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine limalimbikitsa kuti tsiku lililonse azimwa madzi okwana malita 3.7 (0.98 U.S. galoni) kwa amuna akuluakulu ndi 2.7 L (0.71 U.S. gal) kwa akazi. Kuchuluka kwake kumadalira mlingo wa ntchito, kutentha, chinyezi, ndi zina. Zambiri mwa izi zimalowetsedwa kudzera muzakudya kapena zakumwa kupatula kumwa madzi owongoka. Zolemba zachipatala zimakonda kumwa pang'ono, nthawi zambiri madzi okwanira 1 litre kwa mwamuna wamba, kupatula zofunika zina chifukwa cha kuchepa kwamadzi chifukwa chochita masewera olimbitsa thupi kapena nyengo yofunda. Impso zathanzi zimatha kutulutsa madzi okwanira 0,8 mpaka 1 litre pa ola limodzi, koma kupsinjika monga kuchita masewera olimbitsa thupi kumatha kuchepetsa izi. Anthu amatha kumwa madzi ochulukirapo kuposa momwe amafunikira pochita masewera olimbitsa thupi, zomwe zimawayika pachiwopsezo cha kuledzera kwamadzi (hyperhydration), yomwe imatha kupha. Zimene anthu ambiri amanena zoti “munthu ayenera kumwa magalasi asanu ndi atatu a madzi patsiku” zikuoneka kuti zilibe maziko enieni a sayansi. Kafukufuku wasonyeza kuti kumwa madzi owonjezera, makamaka mpaka 500 milliliters (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) pa nthawi ya chakudya, kunagwirizanitsidwa ndi kuchepa thupi. Kumwa madzi okwanira okwanira kumathandiza kupewa kudzimbidwa. Lingaliro loyambirira la kumwa madzi mu 1945 loperekedwa ndi Bungwe la Food and Nutrition la U.S. National Research Council linati: “Muyezo wamba wa anthu osiyanasiyana ndi mamililita 1 pa kalori iliyonse ya chakudya. Zambiri mwa kuchuluka kumeneku zimakhala m’zakudya zomwe zakonzedwa.” Lipoti laposachedwa lazakudya la U.S. National Research Council linalimbikitsa, kutengera kuchuluka kwa madzi omwe amamwa kuchokera ku data ya kafukufuku waku US (kuphatikiza chakudya): malita 3.7 (0.81 imp gal; 0.98 US gal) kwa amuna ndi malita 2.7 (0.59 imp gal; 0.71 okwana madzi osakhala ndi madzi okwanira 19% a US). kuchuluka kwa madzi omwe amamwa mu kafukufukuyu. Makamaka, amayi oyembekezera ndi oyamwitsa amafunikira madzi owonjezera kuti akhalebe ndi hydrate. Bungwe la US Institute of Medicine limalimbikitsa kuti, pafupifupi, amuna amadya malita atatu (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) ndi akazi malita 2.2 (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); Amayi apakati akuyenera kumwa malita 2.4 (0.53 imp gal; 0.63 US gal) ndi amayi oyamwitsa ayenera kumwa malita atatu (makapu 12), chifukwa madzi ochuluka kwambiri amatayika panthawi yoyamwitsa. Zomwe zadziwikanso ndikuti nthawi zambiri, pafupifupi 20% yamadzi omwe amamwa amachokera ku chakudya, pomwe ena onse amachokera kumadzi akumwa ndi zakumwa (kuphatikizapo caffeine). Madzi amachotsedwa m'thupi m'njira zosiyanasiyana; kudzera m'mkodzo ndi ndowe, kutuluka thukuta, ndi kupuma kwa nthunzi yamadzi mu mpweya. Ndi mphamvu zolimbitsa thupi komanso kutentha, kutaya madzi kumawonjezeka ndipo zosowa zamadzimadzi tsiku ndi tsiku zikhoza kuwonjezeka. Anthu amafuna madzi opanda zonyansa zochepa. Zonyansa zodziwika bwino zimaphatikizapo zitsulo monga mkuwa ndi mtovu; mankhwala monga mankhwala ophera tizilombo, PFAS, kapena bulichi; ndi mabakiteriya owopsa, monga Campylobacter, E. coli O157, ndi Vibrio. Ma solute ena ndi ovomerezeka komanso ofunikira kuti awonjezere kukoma komanso kupereka ma electrolyte ofunikira. Nyanja ya Baikal yomwe ili ku Siberia ndi yaikulu kwambiri (yofanana ndi kuchuluka kwake) yamadzi opanda mchere. Kusamba
Mayendedwe
Kugwiritsa ntchito mankhwala
Madzi amagwiritsidwa ntchito kwambiri pakupanga mankhwala ngati zosungunulira kapena zosungunulira komanso nthawi zambiri ngati solute kapena chothandizira. M'zinthu zosawerengeka, madzi ndi chosungunulira wamba, kusungunula mankhwala ambiri a ionic, komanso mankhwala ena a polar monga ammonia ndi mankhwala ogwirizana kwambiri ndi madzi. Muzochita za organic, sizimagwiritsidwa ntchito ngati zosungunulira, chifukwa sizimasungunula bwino komanso zimakhala amphoteric (acidic ndi basic) ndi nucleophilic. Komabe, zinthu izi nthawi zina zimakhala zofunika. Komanso, kuthamanga kwa ma Diels-Alder ndi madzi kwawonedwa. Madzi apamwamba kwambiri posachedwapa akhala mutu wa kafukufuku. Madzi odzaza ndi okosijeni amawotcha bwino zowononga zachilengedwe. Kusinthana kutentha
Madzi ndi nthunzi ndi madzi omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito posinthanitsa kutentha, chifukwa cha kupezeka kwake komanso kutentha kwakukulu, pozizira ndi kutentha. Madzi ozizira amatha kupezeka m'nyanja kapena m'nyanja. Ndikothandiza makamaka kunyamula kutentha kudzera mu vaporization ndi condensation ya madzi chifukwa cha kutentha kwake kwakukulu kobisika kwa vaporization. Choyipa ndichakuti zitsulo zomwe zimapezeka m'mafakitale monga zitsulo ndi mkuwa zimakokedwa ndi okosijeni mwachangu ndi madzi osayeretsedwa ndi nthunzi. Pafupifupi m'malo onse opangira magetsi otenthetsera, madzi amagwiritsidwa ntchito ngati madzi (omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito potsekeka pakati pa boiler, turbine ya nthunzi, ndi condenser), ndi choziziritsa kukhosi (chomwe chimagwiritsidwa ntchito kusinthanitsa kutentha kwa zinyalala kumadzi am'madzi kapena kunyamula ndi nthunzi mu nsanja yozizirira). Ku United States, malo oziziritsa magetsi ndi omwe amagwiritsa ntchito madzi ambiri. M'makampani opanga magetsi a nyukiliya, madzi amathanso kugwiritsidwa ntchito ngati nyutroni woyang'anira. M'malo ambiri opangira zida za nyukiliya, madzi amakhala ozizira komanso owongolera. Izi zimapereka china chake chachitetezo chokhazikika, popeza kuchotsa madzi kuchokera ku riyakitala kumachepetsanso mphamvu ya nyukiliya. Komabe njira zina zimayamikiridwa poyimitsa kuchitapo kanthu ndipo ndibwino kuti nyukiliya ikhale yophimbidwa ndi madzi kuti zitsimikizire kuzizirira kokwanira. Malingaliro amoto
Madzi amakhala ndi kutentha kwakukulu kwa vaporization ndipo ndi ochepa kwambiri, zomwe zimapangitsa kuti azikhala bwino kuzimitsa moto madzimadzi. Kutentha kwa madzi kumatulutsa kutentha kutali ndi moto. Ndizowopsa kugwiritsa ntchito madzi pamoto wophatikiza mafuta ndi zosungunulira organic chifukwa zinthu zambiri zimayandama pamadzi ndipo madzi amatha kufalitsa madzi akuyaka. Kugwiritsa ntchito madzi polimbana ndi moto kuyeneranso kuganizira zoopsa za kuphulika kwa nthunzi, zomwe zingachitike pamene madzi akugwiritsidwa ntchito pamoto wotentha kwambiri m'malo otsekedwa, komanso kuphulika kwa hydrogen, pamene zinthu zomwe zimakhudzidwa ndi madzi, monga zitsulo zina kapena mpweya wotentha monga malasha, makala, kapena coke graphite, zimawola madzi, kutulutsa mpweya wamadzi. Mphamvu ya kuphulika koteroko inawoneka mu ngozi ya Chernobyl, ngakhale kuti madzi omwe anali nawo pa nkhaniyi sanachokere kuzimitsa moto koma kuchokera ku makina oziziritsa madzi a riyakitala. Kuphulika kwa nthunzi kunachitika pamene kutentha kwakukulu kwa pachimake kunachititsa kuti madzi alowe mu nthunzi. Kuphulika kwa haidrojeni mwina kudachitika chifukwa cha zomwe zimachitika pakati pa nthunzi ndi zirconium yotentha. Ma metallic oxides ena, makamaka a zitsulo za alkali ndi zitsulo zamchere zamchere, amatulutsa kutentha kochuluka chifukwa cha madzi moti ngozi ya moto imatha kuchitika. Alkaline earth oxide quicklime, yomwe imadziwikanso kuti calcium oxide, ndi chinthu chopangidwa mochuluka chomwe nthawi zambiri chimatengedwa m'matumba a mapepala. Ngati izi zanyowetsedwa, zimatha kuyaka pamene zomwe zili mkati mwake zimachita ndi madzi. Zosangalatsa
Anthu amagwiritsa ntchito madzi pazinthu zambiri zosangalatsa, komanso kuchita masewera olimbitsa thupi komanso masewera. Zina mwa izi ndi kusambira, kutsetsereka pamadzi, kukwera bwato, kusefukira ndi kudumpha pansi. Kuphatikiza apo, masewera ena, monga ice hockey ndi ice skating, amaseweredwa pa ayezi. M'mphepete mwa nyanja, magombe ndi malo osungiramo madzi ndi malo otchuka omwe anthu amapita kukapumula ndi kusangalala. Ambiri amawona kuti phokoso ndi maonekedwe a madzi oyenda ndi odekha, ndipo akasupe ndi madzi ena oyenda ndi zokongoletsera zotchuka. Ena amasunga nsomba ndi zomera ndi zinyama m'madzi a m'madzi kapena m'mayiwewa kuti azichita masewero, zosangalatsa, ndi mabwenzi. Anthu amagwiritsanso ntchito madzi pochita masewera a chipale chofewa, monga skiing, sledding, snowmobiling kapena snowboarding, zomwe zimafuna kuti madzi azikhala otsika kwambiri ngati ayezi kapena crystallized mu chipale chofewa. Makampani amadzi
Makampani amadzi amapereka madzi akumwa ndi madzi opanda zinyalala (kuphatikiza chimbudzi) kwa mabanja ndi mafakitale. Malo operekera madzi akuphatikizapo zitsime zamadzi, zitsime zotungira madzi amvula, maukonde operekera madzi, ndi malo oyeretsera madzi, akasinja amadzi, nsanja zamadzi, mapaipi amadzi kuphatikiza ngalande zakale. Majenereta amadzi am'mlengalenga akukula. Madzi akumwa nthawi zambiri amasonkhanitsidwa pa akasupe, otengedwa ku zitsime zopanga (zitsime) pansi, kapena amapopa kuchokera kunyanja ndi mitsinje. Kumanga zitsime zambiri m’malo abwino ndi njira yothekera yopangira madzi ambiri, poganiza kuti akasupewo atha kutulutsa madzi okwanira. Magwero ena amadzi akuphatikizapo kusonkhanitsa madzi amvula. Madzi angafunikire kuyeretsedwa kuti anthu amwe. Izi zingaphatikizepo kuchotsa zinthu zosasungunuka, zinthu zowonongeka ndi tizilombo toyambitsa matenda. Njira zodziwika bwino ndi kusefa ndi mchenga womwe umangochotsa zinthu zosasungunuka, pomwe kuthira chlorine ndi kuwira kumapha tizilombo toyambitsa matenda. Distillation imagwira ntchito zonse zitatu. Njira zotsogola zilipo, monga reverse osmosis. Kuchotsa mchere m'madzi ambiri a m'nyanja ndi njira yokwera mtengo yomwe imagwiritsidwa ntchito m'madera ouma a m'mphepete mwa nyanja. Kugawa kwa madzi akumwa kumachitika kudzera m'makina amadzi am'matauni, kutumiza matanki kapena madzi am'mabotolo. Maboma m’maiko ambiri ali ndi mapologalamu ogaŵira madzi kwa ovutika popanda malipiro. Kuchepetsa kugwiritsa ntchito madzi akumwa (akumwa) kuti anthu amwe ndi njira ina. M’mizinda ina monga Hong Kong, madzi a m’nyanja amagwiritsidwa ntchito kwambiri pochotsa zimbudzi mumzinda wonse kuti asunge madzi opanda mchere. Madzi oipitsa angakhale kugwiritsiridwa ntchito molakwa kumodzi kwakukulu kwa madzi; mpaka pamene choipitsa chimalepheretsa ntchito zina za madzi, chimasanduka kuwononga gwero, mosasamala kanthu za phindu kwa woipitsa. Monga mitundu ina yakuipitsa, izi sizimayika ndalama zowerengera zamitengo yamsika, zomwe zimaganiziridwa ngati zakunja zomwe msika sungathe kuziwerengera. Motero anthu ena amalipira mtengo wa kuipitsidwa kwa madzi, pamene phindu la makampani abizinesi siligawidwanso kwa anthu akumaloko, omwe akukhudzidwa ndi kuipitsidwa kumeneku. Mankhwala ogwiritsidwa ntchito ndi anthu nthawi zambiri amathera m'madzi ndipo amatha kukhala ndi zotsatira zowononga zamoyo zam'madzi ngati achulukana komanso ngati sawonongeka. Madzi otayira am'matauni ndi m'mafakitale nthawi zambiri amatsuka m'malo opangira madzi oyipa. Kuchepetsa kuthamanga kwa madzi oipitsidwa pamtunda kumayendetsedwa kudzera m'njira zosiyanasiyana zopewera ndi kuchiza. Mapulogalamu a mafakitale
Njira zambiri zamafakitale zimadalira zomwe zimachitika pogwiritsa ntchito mankhwala osungunuka m'madzi, kuyimitsidwa kwa zolimba m'madzi otayira kapena kugwiritsa ntchito madzi kusungunula ndi kutulutsa zinthu, kapena kutsuka zinthu kapena kukonza zida. Njira monga migodi, kupopera mankhwala, kuyeretsa zamkati, kupanga mapepala, kupanga nsalu, utoto, kusindikiza, ndi kuziziritsa magetsi amagwiritsa ntchito madzi ambiri, zomwe zimafuna madzi odzipereka, ndipo nthawi zambiri zimayambitsa kuipitsidwa kwa madzi. Madzi amagwiritsidwa ntchito popanga magetsi. Hydroelectricity ndi magetsi opangidwa kuchokera ku hydropower. Mphamvu yamagetsi yamagetsi imachokera kumadzi oyendetsa makina opangira madzi olumikizidwa ndi jenereta. Hydroelectricity ndi gwero lamphamvu lotsika mtengo, lopanda kuipitsa, longowonjezedwanso. Mphamvuyi imaperekedwa ndi kayendedwe ka madzi. Nthawi zambiri damu imamangidwa pamtsinje, ndikupanga nyanja yopangira kumbuyo kwake. Madzi otuluka m'nyanjayi amakakamizidwa kudzera mu makina opangira magetsi omwe amatembenuza majenereta. Madzi oponderezedwa amagwiritsidwa ntchito pophulitsa madzi ndi odula jeti lamadzi. Mfuti zamadzi zothamanga kwambiri zimagwiritsidwa ntchito podula bwino. Zimagwira ntchito bwino kwambiri, ndizotetezeka, ndipo siziwononga chilengedwe. Amagwiritsidwanso ntchito poziziritsa makina kuti apewe kutenthedwa, kapena kuteteza macheka kuti asatenthedwe. Madzi amagwiritsidwanso ntchito m'mafakitale ndi makina ambiri, monga turbine ya nthunzi ndi chotenthetsera kutentha, kuwonjezera pa ntchito yake monga zosungunulira mankhwala. Kutulutsa madzi osayeretsedwa kuchokera ku mafakitale ndi kuipitsidwa. Kuipitsa kumaphatikizapo zosungunula zotayidwa (kuwonongeka kwa mankhwala) ndi madzi ozizira otayidwa (kuwonongeka kwa kutentha). Makampani amafunikira madzi oyera pazinthu zambiri ndipo amagwiritsa ntchito njira zosiyanasiyana zoyeretsera popereka madzi ndi kutulutsa. Gawo la digito, makamaka Artificial Intelligence limagwiritsa ntchito madzi ochulukirapo, kotero kukulitsa kwa AI "kutha "kuwopseza chitetezo chamadzi padziko lonse lapansi". Kukonza chakudya
Kuphika, kutenthetsa, ndi kuwiritsa ndi njira zodziwika bwino zophikira zomwe nthawi zambiri zimafuna kumiza chakudya m'madzi kapena mpweya wake, nthunzi. Madzi amagwiritsidwanso ntchito potsuka mbale. Madzi amakhalanso ndi ntchito zambiri zofunika pazakudya za sayansi. Zosungunuka monga mchere ndi shuga zomwe zimapezeka m'madzi zimakhudza momwe madzi amakhalira. Kutentha ndi kuzizira kwa madzi kumakhudzidwa ndi solutes, komanso kuthamanga kwa mpweya, komwe kumakhudzidwa ndi kutalika. Madzi amawira pa kutentha kochepa ndi kutsika kwa mpweya komwe kumachitika pamalo okwera. Molu umodzi wa sucrose (shuga) pa kilogalamu imodzi ya madzi umakweza kuwira kwa madzi ndi 0.51 °C (0.918 °F), ndipo mole imodzi ya mchere pa kilogalamu imakweza kuwira ndi 1.02 °C (1.836 °F); mofananamo, kuwonjezera chiwerengero cha particles kusungunuka amachepetsa madzi kuzizira mfundo. Zosungunuka m'madzi zimakhudzanso zochita zamadzi zomwe zimakhudza machitidwe ambiri amankhwala komanso kukula kwa ma virus muzakudya. Ntchito yamadzi imatha kufotokozedwa ngati chiŵerengero cha mphamvu ya nthunzi ya madzi mu njira yothetsera mphamvu ya nthunzi ya madzi oyera. Zosungunuka m'madzi zimachepetsa ntchito yamadzi-izi ndizofunikira kudziwa chifukwa kukula kwa mabakiteriya ambiri kumasiya pamadzi otsika kwambiri. Sikuti kukula kwa tizilombo toyambitsa matenda kumakhudza chitetezo cha chakudya, komanso kusunga ndi alumali moyo wa chakudya. Kuuma kwa madzi ndi chinthu chofunikira kwambiri pakukonza chakudya ndipo chitha kusinthidwa kapena kuthandizidwa pogwiritsa ntchito makina osinthira ma ion. Zingathe kukhudza kwambiri khalidwe la mankhwala, komanso kuchita nawo ntchito zaukhondo. Kuuma kwa madzi kumagawidwa kutengera kuchuluka kwa calcium carbonate m'madzi. Madzi amawerengedwa kuti ndi ofewa ngati ali ndi zosakwana 100 mg/L (UK) kapena zosakwana 60 mg/L (US). Malinga ndi lipoti lofalitsidwa ndi bungwe la Water Footprint mu 2010, kilogalamu imodzi ya ng'ombe imafuna malita 15 zikwi (3.3×10^3 imp gal; 4.0×10^3 US gal) madzi; komabe, olembawo amawonekeranso momveka bwino kuti izi ndizochepa padziko lonse lapansi komanso zochitika zowonongeka zimatsimikizira kuchuluka kwa madzi omwe amagwiritsidwa ntchito popanga ng'ombe. Madzi opangira jakisoni ali pamndandanda wamankhwala ofunikira a World Health Organisation. Kugawa mu chilengedwe
Mu chilengedwe
Madzi ambiri a m’chilengedwechi amapangidwa chifukwa cha kupangidwa kwa nyenyezi. Mapangidwe a nyenyezi amatsagana ndi mphepo yamphamvu yakunja ya gasi ndi fumbi. Pamene kutuluka kwa zinthu izi kumakhudza mpweya wozungulira, mafunde odabwitsa omwe amapangidwa kuti azipondereza ndi kutentha mpweya. Madzi omwe amawonedwa amapangidwa mwachangu mu mpweya wotentha wowundanawu. Pa 22 July 2011, lipoti linafotokoza za kupezeka kwa mtambo waukulu wa nthunzi wamadzi wokhala ndi "madzi ochuluka kuwirikiza 140 thililiyoni kuposa nyanja zonse zapadziko lapansi pamodzi" mozungulira quasar yomwe ili zaka 12 biliyoni kuwala kuchokera pa Dziko Lapansi. Malinga ndi ofufuzawo, "kufufuzaku kukuwonetsa kuti madzi akhala akuchulukira m'chilengedwe chonse pafupifupi kukhalapo kwake konse". Madzi apezeka mumtambo wapakati pa nyenyezi mkati mwa Milky Way. N’kutheka kuti madzi amapezekanso ochuluka m’milalang’amba ina, chifukwa chakuti zigawo zake, haidrojeni, ndi okosijeni, zili m’gulu la maelementi ochuluka kwambiri m’chilengedwe chonse. Potengera zitsanzo za kupangidwa ndi kusinthika kwa Dzuwa la Dzuwa ndi za nyenyezi zina, mapulaneti ena ambiri akhoza kukhala ndi zinthu zofanana. Nthunzi wamadzi

Madzi ndi ma volatiles ena mwina amapanga zambiri za mkati mwa Uranus ndi Neptune ndipo madzi omwe ali m'zigawo zakuya akhoza kukhala ngati madzi a ionic momwe mamolekyu amagwera mu supu ya haidrojeni ndi ma ion okosijeni, ndipo mozama kwambiri ngati madzi apamwamba momwe mpweya umawonekera, koma ma hydrogen ions amayandama momasuka mkati mwa mpweya. Madzi ndi mapulaneti okhalamo
Kukhalapo kwa madzi amadzimadzi, komanso pang'onopang'ono mawonekedwe ake a mpweya ndi olimba, Padziko Lapansi ndizofunikira kuti pakhale zamoyo Padziko Lapansi monga momwe tikudziwira. Dziko Lapansi liri m'malo omwe anthu amatha kukhalamo a Solar System; zikadakhala kuyandikira pang'ono kapena kutali ndi Dzuwa (pafupifupi 5%, kapena pafupifupi makilomita 8 miliyoni), mikhalidwe yomwe imalola mitundu itatu kukhalapo nthawi imodzi ingakhale yocheperako. Kukula kwa dziko lapansi kumagwiranso ntchito: mphamvu yokoka imalola kuti ikhale ndi mpweya, kuphatikizapo chinyezi cha mpweya. Mapulaneti ang'onoang'ono ngati Mars ali ndi mpweya wochepa kwambiri kapena alibe. Nthunzi wamadzi ndi mpweya wina wowonjezera kutentha m'mlengalenga umapereka chitetezo cha kutentha (greenhouse effect) chomwe chimathandizira kuti pakhale kutentha kwapamtunda kokhazikika kukakhala kochulukira. Mkhalidwe wa madzi papulaneti umadalira kutsika kwa mlengalenga, komwe kumatsimikiziridwa ndi mphamvu yokoka ya pulaneti. Ngati pulaneti ndi yaikulu mokwanira, madzi pa izo akhoza kukhala olimba ngakhale pa kutentha, chifukwa cha kuthamanga kwambiri chifukwa cha mphamvu yokoka, monga izo zinawonedwa pa exoplanets Gliese 436 b ndi GJ 1214 b. Malamulo, ndale, ndi mavuto
Ndale zamadzi ndi ndale zomwe zimakhudzidwa ndi madzi ndi madzi. Madzi, makamaka madzi abwino, ndi gwero labwino kwambiri padziko lonse lapansi komanso chinthu chofunikira pamikangano yambiri yandale. Zimakhudza thanzi komanso kuwononga zamoyo zosiyanasiyana. Kupeza madzi abwino akumwa kwakhala bwino m’zaka makumi angapo zapitazi pafupifupi padziko lonse lapansi, koma pafupifupi anthu 1 biliyoni akusowabe madzi abwino ndipo oposa 2.5 biliyoni alibe zimbudzi zokwanira. Komabe, akatswiri ena anena kuti pofika chaka cha 2025 anthu oposa theka la anthu padziko lonse adzakhala akukumana ndi vuto la madzi. Lipoti, lomwe linatulutsidwa mu November 2009, likusonyeza kuti pofika m’chaka cha 2030, m’madera ena amene akutukuka kumene padziko lapansi, madzi azidzaposa 50%. Anthu okwana 1.6 biliyoni apeza madzi abwino kuyambira 1990. Chiwerengero cha anthu omwe ali m’mayiko otukuka kumene omwe ali ndi madzi abwino apeza bwino kuchoka pa 30% mu 1970 kufika pa 71% mu 1990, 79% mu 2000, ndi 84% mu 2004. mwa kusayendetsa bwino komanso katangale. Kuonjezera apo, zoyesayesa zapadziko lonse zopititsa patsogolo kayendetsedwe ka kayendetsedwe ka chithandizo, monga Paris Declaration on Aid Effectiveness, sizinatengedwe bwino ndi omwe amapereka gawo la madzi monga momwe amachitira pa maphunziro ndi thanzi, zomwe zingathe kusiya opereka ndalama zambiri akugwira ntchito zomwe zikugwira ntchito ndi maboma olandira popanda kupatsidwa mphamvu kuti achitepo kanthu. Olemba buku la 2007 Comprehensive Assessment of Water Management in Agriculture ananena kuti ulamuliro wabwino ndi chimodzi mwa zifukwa za kusowa kwa madzi. Ulamuliro wa madzi ndi njira yokhazikika komanso yosakhazikika yomwe zisankho zokhudzana ndi kayendetsedwe ka madzi zimapangidwira. Ulamuliro wabwino wa madzi umafuna kudziwa zomwe zimagwira ntchito bwino pazakuthupi komanso pazachuma. Nthawi zina zolakwa zachitika poyesa kugwiritsa ntchito 'mapulani' omwe amagwira ntchito m'maiko otukuka potukula madera ndi zochitika zapadziko lonse lapansi. Mtsinje wa Mekong ndi chitsanzo chimodzi; Ndemanga ya International Water Management Institute of Policy m'mayiko asanu ndi limodzi omwe amadalira mtsinje wa Mekong kuti apeze madzi anapeza kuti kufufuza bwino komanso kosaoneka bwino pamtengo wamtengo wapatali komanso kuwunika kwa chilengedwe sikunachitike kawirikawiri. Anapezanso kuti lamulo la ku Cambodia lolemba madzi okakamiza linali lovuta kwambiri kuposa mmene linafunikira. Mu 2004, bungwe lachifundo ku UK la WaterAid linanena kuti mwana amamwalira masekondi 15 aliwonse chifukwa cha matenda otetezedwa mosavuta okhudzana ndi madzi, omwe nthawi zambiri amadza chifukwa cha kusowa kwaukhondo. Kuyambira 2003, lipoti la UN World Water Development Report, lopangidwa ndi UNESCO World Water Assessment Programme, lapereka ochita zisankho ndi zida zopangira ndondomeko zamadzi zokhazikika. Lipoti la 2023 likuti anthu mabiliyoni awiri (26% ya anthu) alibe madzi akumwa ndipo 3.6 biliyoni (46%) alibe mwayi wopeza zimbudzi zoyendetsedwa bwino. Anthu okhala m’matauni (2.4 biliyoni) adzakumana ndi kusowa kwa madzi pofika chaka cha 2050. Kusoŵa kwa madzi kwanenedwa kukhala kwafala, chifukwa cha kugwiritsidwa ntchito mopitirira muyeso ndi kuipitsa. Lipotilo linanena kuti 10% ya anthu padziko lapansi amakhala m'mayiko omwe ali ndi vuto lalikulu la madzi. Komabe m'zaka zapitazi za 40, madzi akumwa akuwonjezeka ndi pafupifupi 1% pachaka, ndipo akuyembekezeka kukula mofanana mpaka 2050. Kuyambira 2000, kusefukira kwa madzi m'madera otentha kwawonjezeka kanayi, pamene kusefukira kwa madzi kumpoto kwapakati pakatikati kwawonjezeka ndi chiwerengero cha 2.5. Mtengo wa kusefukira kwa madziwa pakati pa 2000 ndi 2019 unali wakufa 100,000 ndi $ 650 miliyoni. Mabungwe okhudzidwa ndi chitetezo cha madzi akuphatikizapo International Water Association (IWA), WaterAid, Water 1st, ndi American Water Resources Association. Bungwe la International Water Management Institute likuchita ntchito ndi cholinga chogwiritsa ntchito kasamalidwe kabwino ka madzi pofuna kuchepetsa umphawi. Misonkhano yokhudzana ndi madzi ndi United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD), International Convention for Prevention of Pollution from Ships, United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea ndi Ramsar Convention. Tsiku la Madzi Padziko Lonse la Madzi likuchitika pa 22 March ndipo Tsiku la World Oceans pa 8 June. Chipembedzo
Madzi amaonedwa kuti ndi oyeretsa m’zipembedzo zambiri. Zikhulupiriro zomwe zimaphatikizira kutsuka mwamwambo (kutsuka) kumaphatikizapo Chikhristu, Chihindu, Chisilamu, Chiyuda, gulu la Rastafari, Shinto, Taoism, ndi Wicca. Kumizidwa (kapena kumizidwa kapena kunyansidwa) kwa munthu m’madzi ndi Sakramenti lapakati la Chikhristu (kumene limatchedwa ubatizo); ilinso mbali ya machitidwe a zipembedzo zina, kuphatikizapo Chisilamu (Ghusl), Chiyuda (mikvah) ndi Sikhism (Amrit Sanskar). Kuphatikiza apo, kusamba mwamwambo m’madzi oyera kumachitidwa kwa akufa m’zipembedzo zambiri kuphatikizapo Chisilamu ndi Chiyuda. Mu Chisilamu, mapemphero asanu a tsiku ndi tsiku amatha kuchitika nthawi zambiri mutatsuka ziwalo zina za thupi pogwiritsa ntchito madzi oyera (wudhu), pokhapokha ngati madzi sakupezeka (onani Tayammum). Mu Shinto, madzi amagwiritsidwa ntchito pafupifupi miyambo yonse yoyeretsa munthu kapena malo (mwachitsanzo, pamwambo wa misogi). Mu Chikhristu, madzi oyera ndi madzi amene ayeretsedwa ndi wansembe ndi cholinga cha ubatizo, kudalitsa anthu, malo, ndi zinthu, kapena ngati njira yochotsera zoipa.
