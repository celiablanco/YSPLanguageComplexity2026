He ʻelemākule ʻo ia i lawaiʻa hoʻokahi i ka waʻa ma ke kahawai ʻo Gulf Stream a ua hala ʻo ia i kanawalukūmāhā mau lā i kēia manawa me ka lawe ʻole i ka iʻa. I na la mua he kanaha, ua noho pu kekahi keiki me ia. Aka, mahope iho o na la he kanaha me ka i'a ole, ua hai mai na makua o ua keiki nei, ua paa loa ka elemakule i ka salao, oia ke ano poino loa, a ua holo aku ke keiki ma ka lakou kauoha ma kekahi waapa i loaa mai ai ekolu i'a maikai i ka pule mua. Ua kaumaha ke keiki i ka ʻike ʻana i ke komo ʻana o ka ʻelemakule i kēlā me kēia lā me ka ʻole o kona waʻa, a iho mau ʻo ia i lalo e kōkua iā ia i ka lawe ʻana i nā laina wili a i ʻole ka ʻauʻau a me ka peʻa i hoʻomoe ʻia a puni ka kia. Ua paʻi ʻia ka peʻa me nā ʻeke palaoa, a me ka huluhulu ʻia, ua like ia me ka hae o ka pio mau.
