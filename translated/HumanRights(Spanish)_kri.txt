Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt 
Di Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli bin adopt am ɛn prɛd am insay in rizɔlt 217 A (III), ɔf 
Disɛmba 10, 1948  
Fɔs di wɔd dɛn  
We wi tink bɔt se fridɔm, jɔstis ɛn pis na di wɔl de bays pan 
fɔ rɛkɔgnayz di intrinsic dignity ɛn ikwal ɛn nɔ gɛt rayt dɛn we dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ tek 
pan ɔl di wan dɛn we de na mɔtalman famili, .  
We wi tink bɔt dat di ignorance ɛn disgres fɔ di rayt dɛn 
mɔtalman dɔn mek pipul dɛn de du bad bad tin dɛn we de mek di kɔnshɛns vɛks 
mɔtalman we gɛt fɔ du wit mɔtalman; ɛn dat dɔn de prɛd, as di ay aspireshɔn fɔ di 
man, di kam fɔ wan wɔl we mɔtalman, fri frɔm 
fred ɛn sɔfa, ɛnjɔy fridɔm fɔ tɔk ɛn fridɔm fɔ 
tin dɛn we pɔsin biliv, .  
We wi tink se i impɔtant fɔ mek dɛn protɛkt mɔtalman rayt bay a 
rijim fɔ Lɔ, so dat dɛn nɔ go fos man fɔ bi di suprɛm 
risɔs fɔ tɔn agens di we aw pipul dɛn de trit dɛn kɔmpin dɛn ɛn we dɛn de mek pipul dɛn sɔfa, .  
Fɔ tink bak se i impɔtant fɔ mek pipul dɛn gɛt padi biznɛs wit dɛnsɛf 
bitwin di neshɔn dɛn, .  
We wi tink bɔt se di pipul dɛn na di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɔn riaffirm insay di 
Lɛta yu fet pan di men rayt dɛm we mɔtalman gɛt, pan rɛspɛkt ɛn valyu 
fɔ di mɔtalman pɔsin ɛn di ikwal rayt dɛn we man ɛn uman gɛt; ɛn i 
dɔn tɔk se dɛn dɔn mekɔp dɛn maynd fɔ mek pipul dɛn go bifo na di soshal layf ɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn liv bɛtɛ layf 
insay wan brayt kɔnsɛpt fɔ fridɔm, .
We wi tink bɔt se di Mɛmba Stet dɛn dɔn ɔndatak fɔ mek shɔ se, in 
wok togɛda wit di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn Ɔganayzeshɔn, ɔlman rɛspɛkt ɛn 
ifektiv to di fawndeshɔnal rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛm we mɔtalman gɛt, ɛn  
We wi tink bɔt se wan kɔmɔn kɔnsɛpshɔn bɔt dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya na 
di impɔtant tin we pas ɔl fɔ mek dɛn du ɔl wetin dɛn dɔn prɔmis, .  
 

 
Di Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli  
Prɔklaym di prɛsɛn Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt as wan ideal 
kɔmɔn gol we ɔl pipul ɛn neshɔn dɛn fɔ tray fɔ, so dat 
ɔl tu di wan dɛn ɛn di institiushɔn dɛn, we de tek inspɛkshɔn frɔm am ɔltɛm, . 
fɔ mek pipul dɛn gɛt rɛspɛkt fɔ dɛn rayt ya, tru tichin ɛn ɛdyukeshɔn 
ɛn fridɔm, ɛn mek shɔ se, tru prɔgrɛsiv mɛsej dɛn fɔ wan nashɔnal ɛn 
intanashɔnal lɔ, in yunivasal ɛn ifɛktiv rɛkɔgnishɔn ɛn aplikeshɔn, ɔl tu bitwin 
di pipul dɛm na di Mɛmba Stet dɛm ɛn bak bitwin di wan dɛm na di teritɔri dɛm we dɛn put 
ɔnda in jɔrisdikshɔn.  
Atikul 1  
Dɛn bɔn ɔl mɔtalman fri ɛn ikwal pan rɛspɛkt ɛn rayt ɛn, . 
as dɛn gɛt rizin ɛn kɔnshɛns, dɛn fɔ biev lɛk brɔda ɛn sista dɛn 
wit dɛnsɛf.  
Atikul 2  
Ɔlman gɛt di rayt ɛn fridɔm we dɛn dɔn tɔk bɔt na dis Diklɛreshɔn, . 
we nɔ gɛt difrɛns bitwin dɛn kɔlɔ, kɔlɔ, man ɔ uman, langwej, rilijɔn, pɔlitikal opinion ɔ 
ɛni ɔda nature, nashɔnal ɔ soshal ɔrijin, ikɔnomi pozishɔn, bɔn ɔ 
ɛni ɔda kɔndishɔn.
Dɔn bak, dɛn nɔ go mek ɛni difrɛns bikɔs ɔf pɔlitiks, ligal ɔ pɔlitikal stetmɛnt. 
intanashɔnal fɔ di kɔntri ɔ teritɔri we pɔsin de ɔnda in jɔrisdikshɔn, . 
if na indipɛndɛnt kɔntri ɔ teritɔri ɔnda 
fidushiari administreshɔn, nɔ ɔtonamɛnt ɔ ɔnda ɛni ɔda limiteshɔn fɔ 
di wan we gɛt pawa fɔ rul.  
Atikul 3  
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv, fridɔm ɛn sef na in layf. 
pɔsin.  
 

 
Atikul 4  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go bi slev ɔ slev; slev ɛn trafik 
Dɛn nɔ gri fɔ mek slev dɛn ɔl kayn we.  
Atikul 5  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ mek dɛn sɔfa ɔ pɔnish am bad bad wan, we nɔ gɛt wanwɔd ɔ we nɔ gɛt wanwɔd. 
we de mek pɔsin nɔ gɛt wan rɛspɛkt  
Atikul 6  
Ɔl mɔtalman gɛt rayt, ɔlsay, fɔ rɛkɔgnayz dɛn 
pɔsin we gɛt fɔ du wit lɔ.  
Atikul 7  
Ɔlman ikwal bifo di lɔ ɛn dɛn gɛt, witout difrɛns, rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn 
fɔ di lɔ. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn agens ɔl di diskrimineshɔn 
nɔ gri wit dis Diklɛreshɔn ɛn agens ɛnitin we go mek dɛn du dis kayn diskrimineshɔn.  
Atikul 8  
Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ɛfifishin rimɛdi bifo di nashɔnal kɔt. 
kɔmpiten ɔtoriti dɛn, fɔ protɛkt dɛn frɔm akt dɛn we de pwɛl dɛn rayt 
fawndeshɔnal rayt dɛn we di kɔnstityushɔn ɔ lɔ gri wit.  
Atikul 9  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ detɛn am, put am na jel ɔ kɛr am go na ɔda kɔntri bay wilful.  
Atikul 10  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt, insay kɔndishɔn we ɔlman ikwal, fɔ mek dɛn yɛri am
pɔblik ɛn fayn fayn wan bay wan indipɛndɛnt ɛn nɔ tek wan pɔsin bɛtɛ pas ɔda pɔsin, fɔ di 
 

 
ditarmin fɔ dɛn rayt ɛn ɔbligayshɔn ɔ fɔ ɛgzamin ɛni wan 
akɔdin to am pan kriminal tin dɛn.  
Atikul 11  
1. Ɛnibɔdi we dɛn aks fɔ kraym gɛt rayt fɔ mek dɛn tek am se na in udat i bi 
inosɛns te dɛn pruv se i gilti, akɔdin to di lɔ ɛn in 
pɔblik trial we dɛn dɔn mek shɔ se ɔl di garanti dɛn 
nid fɔ de fɔ difend am.  
2. Dɛn nɔ go kɔndɛm ɛnibɔdi fɔ di tin dɛn we i du ɔ we i nɔ du we di tɛm we dɛn de 
we dɛn du nɔto bin kriminal ɔnda di nashɔnal ɔ intanashɔnal lɔ. 
Ɛn dɛn nɔ go gi pɔsin mɔ siriɔs pɔnishmɛnt pas di wan we dɛn bin de du di tɛm we dɛn bin de 
di kɔmishɔn fɔ di kraym.  
Atikul 12  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit in prayvet layf, in famili, dɛn 
os ɔ in kɔrɛspɔndɛns, ɔ atak in ɔnɔ ɔ gudnem. 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt di lɔ frɔm da kayn intafɛreshɔn de ɔ 
atak dɛn.  
Atikul 13  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ muv fri wan ɛn pik usay i fɔ de 
na di teritɔri we wan Stet de.  
2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ kɔmɔt na ɛni kɔntri, ivin in yon kɔntri, ɛn 
fɔ go bak na in kɔntri.  
Atikul 14  
1. If dɛn mek dɛn sɔfa, ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ go fɛn asaylans, ɛn fɔ 
Ɛnjɔy am, na ɛni kɔntri.  
 

 
2. Dɛn nɔ go yuz dis rayt agens tru tru jɔjmɛnt akshɔn 
we kɔmɔt frɔm kɔmɔn kraym ɔ bay akt we nɔ gri wit di rizin ɛn
prinsipul dɛn we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn gɛt.  
Atikul 15  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt in kɔntri.  
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go tek in kɔntri ɔ di rayt fɔ 
chenj di kɔntri we dɛn kɔmɔt.  
Atikul 16  
1. Man ɛn uman, frɔm di ej fɔ mared, gɛt rayt, witout 
ɛni ristrikshɔn pan bikɔs ɔf in kɔlɔ, kɔntri ɔ rilijɔn, fɔ mared 
ɛn fɛn famili; ɛn go ɛnjɔy ikwal rayt bɔt di 
mared, di tɛm we dɛn de mared ɛn if di mared skata.  
2. Na tru di fri ɛn ful kɔnsɛntmɛnt fɔ di fiuja man ɔ uman nɔmɔ kin du am 
gɛt mared.  
3. Di famili na di natura ɛn fondamental ɛlimɛnt na sosayti ɛn i gɛt 
rayt fɔ protɛkt di sosayti ɛn di Stet.  
Atikul 17  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt prɔpati, wan wan ɛn ɔlman.  
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go tek in prɔpati bay wilful.  
Atikul 18  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tink, in kɔnshɛns ɛn 
rilijɔn; Dis rayt inklud di fridɔm fɔ chenj rilijɔn ɔ biliv, ɛn bak 
as di fridɔm fɔ sho in rilijɔn ɔ biliv, wan wan ɛn 
togɛda, ɔl tu na pɔblik ɛn prayvet, bay we dɛn de tich, praktis, . 
wɔship ɛn ɔbshɔbishɔn.  
 

 
Atikul 19  
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tink ɛn tɔk wetin i want; dis 
rayt inklud fɔ nɔ ambɔg pɔsin bikɔs ɔf wetin i tink, di rayt fɔ chɛk ɛn
fɔ gɛt infɔmeshɔn ɛn opinion, ɛn fɔ sheb dɛn, we nɔ gɛt limit fɔ di bɔda dɛn, . 
bay ɛni we fɔ tɔk wetin i want.  
Atikul 20  
1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛda ɛn jɔyn an 
pisful wan.  
2. Dɛn nɔ go fos ɛnibɔdi fɔ de na wan ɔganayzeshɔn.  
Atikul 21  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan di gɔvmɛnt na in kɔntri, . 
dairekt ɔ tru pipul dɛn we dɛn pik fri wan.  
2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt akses, ɔnda kɔndishɔn dɛn we ikwal, fɔ 
di pɔblik wok dɛn we dɛn kɔntri de du.  
3. Di pipul dɛn want na di bɛnifit we pɔblik pawa gɛt; dis 
wil go sho tru tru tru tin dɛn we dɛn go pik we go gɛt fɔ bi 
dɛn fɔ ol am wan wan tɛm, bay we ɔlman ɛn ikwal rayt fɔ vot ɛn bay vot 
sikrit ɔ ɔda ikwal prosidur we de garanti di fridɔm fɔ di 
vot fɔ vot.  
Atikul 22  
Ɛnibɔdi, as mɛmba na sosayti, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt sikyɔriti 
soshal, ɛn fɔ gɛt, tru di nashɔnal ɛfɔt ɛn intanashɔnal kɔpɔreshɔn, . 
we yu tek tɛm tink bɔt di ɔganayzeshɔn ɛn di tin dɛn we ɛni Stet gɛt, di satisfayshɔn 
fɔ ikɔnomi, soshal ɛn kɔlchɔ rayt dɛn, we impɔtant fɔ mek dɛn gɛt rɛspɛkt 
ɛn di fri divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ dɛn pɔsin.  
 

 
Atikul 23  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ wok, fɔ fri fɔ pik wok, fɔ 
ikwal ɛn satisfay kɔndishɔn fɔ wok ɛn protɛkshɔn agens 
pipul dɛn we nɔ gɛt wok. 
2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt, witout ɛni diskrimineshɔn, fɔ ikwal pe fɔ
A de wok di sem we.  
3. Ɛnibɔdi we de wok gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal mɔni ɛn 
satisfay, we de mek shɔ se i, ɛn in famili, de 
in akɔdin to mɔtalman rɛspɛkt ɛn dat go dɔn, if na so i bi 
nid fɔ de, bay ɛni ɔda we fɔ protɛkt pipul dɛn na soshal layf.  
4. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ mek yunion ɛn jɔyn yunion fɔ di 
difens fɔ dɛn intres.  
Atikul 24  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ rɛst, fɔ ɛnjɔy fri tɛm, fɔ a 
rizin fɔ stɔp di tɛm we pɔsin fɔ wok ɛn fɔ go vaykashun wan wan tɛm 
we dɛn pe.  
Atikul 25  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ liv in layf we go mek shɔ se, . 
ɛn bak yu famili, wɛlbɔdi ɛn wɛl-at, ɛn mɔ 
it, klos, os, mɛrɛsin ɛn savis 
necessary social Yu gɛt rayt bak fɔ inshɔrans if 
nɔ gɛt wok, sik, disabiliti, uman we in man dɔn day, ol ej ɛn ɔda kes dɛm we pɔsin lɔs 
di we aw dɛn de liv bikɔs ɔf di tin dɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit dɛn 
go.  
2. Mama ɛn pikin gɛt rayt fɔ kia ɛn ɛp 
spɛshal wan dɛn. Ɔl pikin dɛn, ilɛksɛf dɛn bɔn dɛn we dɛn mared ɔ we dɛn nɔ mared 
mared, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal soshal protɛkshɔn.  
 

 
Atikul 26  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ lan buk. Edukeshon must bi 
fri, at ɔl as i kam pan ɛlimɛntri ɛdyukeshɔn ɛn 
fondamental. Ɛlimɛntri ɛdyukeshɔn go bi mandatory. Di instrɔkshɔn
teknikol ɛn pɔshɔnal go gɛt fɔ bi jenɛral; akses to stɔdi dɛn 
di wan dɛn we de oba dɛn go bi di sem fɔ ɔlman, i go dipen pan dɛn rispektiv mɛrit.  
2. Edukeshon go aim fo ful divelopment of pesonaliti 
mɔtalman rayt ɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt mɔtalman rayt ɛn 
di men fridɔm dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt; go mek pipul dɛn ɔndastand, fɔ bia wit ɔda pipul dɛn ɛn 
padi biznɛs bitwin ɔl di neshɔn dɛn ɛn ɔl di etnik ɔ rilijɔn grup dɛn; ɛn 
promot di divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn aktiviti fɔ 
fɔ kip pis.  
3. Mama ɛn papa dɛn go gɛt di rayt fɔ pik di kayn edyukeshɔn 
we dɛn go gi dɛn pikin dɛn.  
Atikul 27  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan di kɔlchɔ layf fri wan 
di kɔmyuniti, fɔ ɛnjɔy di art ɛn fɔ tek pat pan di prɔgrɛs 
sayɛns ɛn di bɛnifit dɛn we de kɔmɔt frɔm am.  
2. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt di gud abit dɛn ɛn 
matirial dɛn we kɔrɛkt to am bikɔs ɔf sayɛns prodakshɔn dɛn, . 
litrɛri ɔ atis wok dɛn we na in rayt.  
Atikul 28  
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ mek sɔshal ɛn intanashɔnal ɔda 
we dɛn mek di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we dɛn dɔn tɔk bɔt na dis Diklɛreshɔn 
ful-ɔp fɔ wok.  
Atikul 29  
 

 
1. Ɔlman gɛt di wok we i fɔ du wit rɛspɛkt fɔ di kɔmyuniti, bikɔs na insay 
i kin ebul fɔ divɛlɔp in pɔsin fri wan ɛn ful wan.  
2. We dɛn de yuz dɛn rayt ɛn ɛnjɔy dɛn fridɔm, ɔlman
pɔsin go jɔs de ɔnda di limit dɛn we di lɔ dɔn mek 
wit di wangren rizin fɔ mek shɔ se dɛn no ɛn rɛspɛkt di 
rayt ɛn fridɔm we ɔda pipul dɛn gɛt, ɛn fɔ satisfay di rayt tin dɛn we dɛn want 
fɔ gud abit, pɔblik ɔda ɛn jenɛral wɛlbɔdi na wan sosayti 
dimokrasi we dɛn de du.  
3. Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ yuz dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya ɔnda ɛni kɔndishɔn 
di we aw dɛn de agens di tin dɛn we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn want ɛn di prinsipul dɛn. 
Atikul 30  
Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ ɛksplen ɛnitin we de insay dis Diklɛreshɔn fɔ min dat 
gi ɛni rayt to di Stet, grup ɔ pɔsin, fɔ du sɔntin 
ɛn divɛlɔp aktiviti ɔ du akt dɛn we dɛn aim fɔ stɔp ɛni wan 
di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we dɛn dɔn prɛd insay dis Diklɛreshɔn.
