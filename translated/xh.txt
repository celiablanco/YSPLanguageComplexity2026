Yayilixhego elaliloba lodwa kwisikhephe eGulf Stream kwaye wayesele ehambe iintsuku ezingamashumi asibhozo anesine ngoku engayibambi intlanzi. Ngeentsuku ezimashumi mane zokuqala kwakukho inkwenkwe kunye naye. Kodwa emva kweentsuku ezingamashumi amane ngaphandle kwentlanzi abazali benkwenkwe babemxelele ukuba ixhego ngoku ngokuqinisekileyo kwaye ekugqibeleni isalao, eyona ndlela imbi yelishwa, kwaye inkwenkwe yayihambe ngokwemiyalelo yabo kwelinye iphenyane elabamba iintlanzi ezintathu ezilungileyo kwiveki yokuqala. Yayibuhlungu inkwenkwe ukubona ixhego lingena suku ngalunye liphethe isikhephe salo singenanto kwaye lalisoloko lisihla ukuze limncedise ekuthwaleni imigca ebhijeneyo okanye i-gaff nehapuni kunye neseyile eyayijikelwe kwimasti. Iseyile yayigxunyekwe ngeengxowa zomgubo kwaye, ifakwe i-furred, yayikhangeleka njengeflegi yokoyiswa ngokusisigxina.
