Isimemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu 
Isendlalelo 
Nakuba ukuqashelwa kwesithunzi semvelo kanye nokulingana nokungenakuphikwa 
amalungelo awo wonke amalungu omndeni wesintu ayisisekelo senkululeko, ubulungisa 
nokuthula emhlabeni,  
Nakuba ukungahloniphi nokudelelwa kwamalungelo abantu kube nomphumela wobulwane 
izenzo ezicasule unembeza wesintu, kanye nokufika komhlaba 
lapho abantu beyojabulela khona inkululeko yokukhuluma nezinkolelo nenkululeko 
ngenxa yokwesaba nokuswela kumenyezelwe njengesifiso esiphezulu sabantu abavamile 
abantu,  
Nakuba kubalulekile, uma umuntu engaphoqwa ukuba afune usizo, njengowokugcina 
baphendukela ekuvukeleni ubushiqela nengcindezelo, okufanele amalungelo abantu abe njalo 
ivikelwe umthetho,  
Nakuba kubalulekile ukukhuthaza ukuthuthukiswa kobudlelwano obunobungani phakathi 
izizwe,  
Nakuba abantu beNhlangano Yezizwe bephinde bakuqinisekisile kuMqulu wabo 
ukukholelwa emalungelweni abantu ayisisekelo, esithunzini nasekuhloniphekeni komuntu 
kanye namalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabesifazane futhi banqume ukukhuthaza 
inqubekelaphambili yomphakathi kanye namazinga angcono empilo enkululekweni enkulu,  
Nakuba Amazwe Angamalungu ezibophezele ukuzuza, ngokubambisana 
neNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene, ukugqugquzelwa kwenhlonipho nokugcinwa kwe 
amalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo,  
Nakuba ukuqonda okufanayo kwalawa malungelo nenkululeko kungokwakho 
okubaluleke kakhulu ukufeza ngokugcwele lesi sifungo,  
Manje-ke,
I-General Assembly,  
Imemezela lesi Simemezelo Sendawo Yonke Samalungelo Abantu njengezinga elivamile le 
impumelelo yabo bonke abantu nezizwe zonke, kuze kube yilapho wonke umuntu futhi 
zonke izinhlaka zomphakathi, zikhumbula lesi siMemezelo njalo, zizolwela ukuthi 
 

 
ukufundisa nokufundisa ukukhuthaza ukuhlonishwa kwala malungelo nenkululeko futhi 
izinyathelo eziqhubekayo, kuzwelonke nakwamanye amazwe, ukuze kuvikeleke umhlaba wonke kanye 
ukuqashelwa ngempumelelo kanye nokugcinwa, kokubili phakathi kwabantu Bamazwe Angamalungu 
bona kanye naphakathi kwabantu bezindawo ezingaphansi kombuso wabo.  
I-athikili I  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi belingana ngesithunzi nangamalungelo. Banjalo 
uphiwe ukucabanga nonembeza futhi kufanele kusebenzelane a 
umoya wobuzalwane.  
I-athikili 2  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lawo wonke amalungelo nezinkululeko ezibalulwe kulesi Simemezelo, 
ngaphandle kokuhlukana kwanoma yiluphi uhlobo, njengohlanga, ibala, ubulili, ulimi, inkolo, 
umbono wepolitiki noma omunye, imvelaphi yobuzwe noma yenhlalo, impahla, ukuzalwa noma esinye isimo.  
Ngaphezu kwalokho, akukho mehluko oyokwenziwa ngesisekelo sepolitiki, 
isimo somthetho noma samazwe ngamazwe sezwe noma indawo umuntu akuyo 
kungokwakho, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi uzimele, uthembele, ungazibuse noma ungaphansi kwanoma iyiphi enye 
ukulinganiselwa kobukhosi.  
I-athikili yesi-3  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kanye nokuvikeleka komuntu.  
I-athikili yesi-4  
Akekho oyokwenziwa isigqila noma agqilazwe; ubugqila kanye nokuhweba ngezigqila kuyoba
enqatshelwe ngazo zonke izindlela.  
I-athikili yesi-5  
Akekho oyohlukunyezwa noma aphathwe ngonya, ngendlela engenabuntu noma ejivazayo 
noma isijeziso.  
 

 
I-athikili yesi-6  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokwamukelwa yonke indawo njengomuntu phambi komthetho.  
I-athikili yesi-7  
Bonke bayalingana phambi komthetho futhi banelungelo lokulingana ngaphandle kokubandlululwa 
ukuvikelwa komthetho. Bonke banelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo kunoma yikuphi 
ukucwasa ngokwephula lesi Simemezelo kanye nanoma yikuphi ukugqugquzela lokho 
ukucwasa.  
Isigaba 8  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuthola ikhambi elisebenzayo yizinkantolo zikazwelonke ezinekhono 
ngezenzo ezephula amalungelo ayisisekelo awanikwa umthethosisekelo noma umthetho.  
I-athikili yesi-9  
Akekho oyoboshwa ngokungafanele, avalelwe noma adingiswe.  
Isigaba 10  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo ngokulingana okuphelele nokulalelwa komphakathi ngokuzimela 
kanye nenkantolo engachemi, ekunqumeni amalungelo nezibopho zakhe nanoma yimaphi 
icala lobugebengu abekwe lona.  
Isigaba 11  
1. Wonke umuntu othweswe icala lokujeziswa unelungelo lokucatshangwa 
emsulwa waze watholakala enecala ngokomthetho ecaleni lomphakathi lapho yena 
ubenazo zonke iziqinisekiso ezidingekayo zokuzivikela.  
2. Akekho oyotholwa enecala ngenxa yanoma yisiphi isenzo noma 
ukweqiwa okungelona icala lokujeziswa, ngaphansi kuzwelonke noma 
umthetho wamazwe ngamazwe, ngesikhathi lapho usenziwa. Futhi ngeke esindayo
inhlawulo ibekwe kunaleyo eyayisebenza ngesikhathi sokujeziswa 
icala lenziwa.  
Isigaba 12  
Akekho oyophazanyiswa ngokungafanele kubumfihlo bakhe, umndeni, ikhaya lakhe 
noma izincwadi, noma ukuhlasela isithunzi nesithunzi sakhe. Wonke umuntu unalo 
ilungelo lokuvikelwa ngumthetho kulokho kugxambukela noma ukuhlaselwa.  
Isigaba 13  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhamba nokuhlala ngaphakathi kwe 
imingcele yoMbuso ngamunye.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokushiya noma yiliphi izwe, kuhlanganisa nelakhe, kanye nokuhamba 
abuyele ezweni lakubo.  
Isigaba 14  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokufuna nokujabulela ukukhoseliswa kwamanye amazwe 
ukushushiswa.  
2. Leli lungelo angeke lisetshenziswe esimweni sokushushiswa ngokweqiniso 
okuvela ebugebengwini obungahlangene nepolitiki noma ezenzweni eziphambene nezinhloso kanye 
imigomo yeNhlangano Yezizwe.  
Isigaba 15  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lobuzwe.  
2. Akekho oyophucwa ubuzwe bakhe noma ancishwe ilungelo lokuthola lokho 
ukushintsha ubuzwe bakhe.  
Isigaba 16  
 

 
1. Abesilisa nabesifazane asebekhulile, ngaphandle kokulinganiselwa ngenxa yobuhlanga, ubuzwe 
noma inkolo, banelungelo lokushada nokuba nomkhaya. Banelungelo 
emalungelweni alinganayo emshadweni, ngesikhathi somshado kanye nasekuhlakazweni kwawo.  
2. Umshado kuyongenwa kuwo kuphela ngokukhululeka nangemvume egcwele 
abazimisele ngokushada.  
3. Umndeni uyiqembu lemvelo neliyisisekelo lomphakathi futhi uyiqembu
unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngumphakathi kanye noMbuso.  
Isigaba 17  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba nobunikazi bempahla yedwa noma ngokuhlanganyela nayo 
abanye.  
2. Akekho oyophucwa impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
Isigaba 18  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga, unembeza nenkolo; leli lungelo 
kubandakanya inkululeko yokuguqula inkolo noma inkolelo yakhe, kanye nenkululeko, eyedwa noma phakathi 
emphakathini nabanye futhi esidlangalaleni noma ngasese, ukuze abonise inkolo noma inkolelo yakhe kuyo 
ukufundisa, ukwenza, ukukhonza nokugcina.  
Isigaba 19  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokubeka imibono yakhe nokuveza imizwa yakhe; leli lungelo lihlanganisa 
inkululeko yokuba nemibono ngaphandle kokuphazanyiswa kanye nokufuna, ukwamukela nokudlulisela 
ulwazi nemibono nganoma iyiphi imidiya futhi kungakhathalekile imingcele.  
Isigaba 20  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana nokuhlangana ngokuthula.  
2. Akekho ongaphoqwa ukuthi abe yilungu lenhlangano.  
Isigaba 21  
 

 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza kuhulumeni wezwe lakhe, 
ngokuqondile noma ngabameleli abakhethwe ngokukhululekile.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo elilinganayo lokufinyelela emisebenzini yomphakathi ezweni lakubo.  
3. Intando yabantu iyoba yisisekelo segunya likahulumeni; 
le ntando izovezwa okhethweni lwezikhathi ezithile nolweqiniso oluyokwenziwa 
kungaba ngelungelo lokuzikhethela elilinganayo emhlabeni wonke futhi lizobanjwa ngevoti eliyimfihlo noma ngo 
izinqubo zokuvota zamahhala ezilinganayo.  
Isigaba 22
Wonke umuntu, njengelungu lomphakathi, unelungelo lokuvikelwa komphakathi futhi unelungelo 
ukufezeka, ngomzamo kazwelonke kanye nokubambisana kwamazwe ngamazwe kanye 
ngokuhambisana nenhlangano nezinsiza zoMbuso ngamunye, wezomnotho, 
amalungelo enhlalakahle namasiko asemqoka kakhulu esithunzini sakhe kanye nentuthuko yamahhala 
lobuntu bakhe.  
Isigaba 23  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusebenza, lokuzikhethela ngokukhululekile umsebenzi, nokuba nobulungiswa kanye 
izimo zokusebenza ezivumayo kanye nokuvikelwa ekusweleni umsebenzi.  
2. Wonke umuntu, ngaphandle kokubandlululwa, unelungelo lokukhokhelwa ngokulinganayo 
umsebenzi.  
3. Wonke umuntu osebenzayo unelungelo lokuthola iholo elilungile nelikahle 
ukuqinisekisa yena nomndeni wakhe ukuphila okufanele isithunzi sobuntu, 
futhi kwengezwe, uma kunesidingo, ngezinye izindlela zokuvikela umphakathi.  
4. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusungula kanye nokujoyina izinyunyana ukuze avikeleke 
izithakazelo zakhe.  
Isigaba 24  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula nokungcebeleka, okuhlanganisa nomkhawulo ofanele 
amahora okusebenza namaholide ezikhathi ezithile akhokhelwayo.  
 

 
Isigaba 25  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lezinga lokuphila elifanele lempilo kanye 
inhlalakahle yakhe nomndeni wakhe, okuhlanganisa ukudla, izingubo zokugqoka, indawo yokuhlala 
kanye nokunakekelwa kwezempilo kanye nezinkonzo zomphakathi ezidingekayo, kanye nelungelo lokuphepha 
uma kwenzeka ungasebenzi, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, ubufelokazi, ukuguga noma 
okunye ukuntula indlela yokuziphilisa ezimweni ezingaphezu kwamandla akhe.
2. Ubumama nobungane bunelungelo lokunakekelwa nosizo olukhethekile. Konke 
izingane, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi zizalwe emshadweni noma ngaphandle, zizojabulela inhlalakahle efanayo 
isivikelo.  
Isigaba 26  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo izoba mahhala, okungenani ku 
izigaba eziyisisekelo neziyisisekelo. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi iyoba 
impoqo. Imfundo yobuchwepheshe neyomsebenzi iyokwenziwa 
etholakala ngokujwayelekile futhi imfundo ephakeme iyotholwa yibo bonke ngokulinganayo 
ngesisekelo sokufaneleka.  
2. Imfundo iyoqondiswa ekuthuthukisweni okuphelele komuntu 
ubuntu kanye nokuqinisa ukuhlonishwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye 
izinkululeko eziyisisekelo. Kuzothuthukisa ukuqonda, ukubekezelelana kanye 
ubungane phakathi kwazo zonke izizwe, izinhlanga noma amaqembu ezenkolo, futhi kuyoqhubeka 
imisebenzi yeNhlangano Yezizwe ukuze kugcinwe ukuthula.  
3. Abazali banelungelo lokuqala lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo ezoba yilo 
baphiwe abantwana babo.  
Isigaba 27  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo ngokukhululekile ukubamba iqhaza empilweni yamasiko 
umphakathi, ukujabulela ezobuciko kanye nokuhlanganyela entuthukweni yesayensi kanye 
izinzuzo zayo.  
 

 
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa kokuziphatha kanye nezinto ezibonakalayo 
okubangelwa yinoma yikuphi ukukhiqizwa kwesayensi, kwemibhalo noma kwezobuciko anguye 
umbhali.  
Isigaba 28  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhleleka kwezenhlalo nezamazwe ngamazwe lapho amalungelo kanye 
inkululeko ebekwe kulesi Simemezelo ingafezeka ngokugcwele.  
Isigaba 29
1. Wonke umuntu unemisebenzi emphakathini lapho uwodwa ukhululekile futhi ugcwele 
ukuthuthukiswa kobuntu bakhe kungenzeka.  
2. Ekusebenziseni amalungelo nenkululeko yakhe, wonke umuntu uyoba ngaphansi kwakhe kuphela 
kuleyo mikhawulo enqunywa umthetho ngenjongo kuphela 
ukuthola ukuqashelwa okufanele nokuhlonipha amalungelo nenkululeko yabanye 
kanye nokuhlangabezana nezidingo ezifanele zokuziphatha, ukuhleleka komphakathi kanye 
inhlalakahle jikelele emphakathini wentando yeningi.  
3. Lawa malungelo kanye nenkululeko akumele nakancane kusetshenziswe ngokuphambene nalokho 
izinjongo nemigomo yeNhlangano yeziZwe.  
Isigaba 30  
Akukho kulesi simemezelo okungahunyushwa njengokusho noma yimuphi uMbuso, iqembu noma 
umuntu noma yiliphi ilungelo lokuzibandakanya kunoma yimuphi umsebenzi noma ukwenza noma yisiphi isenzo esihloselwe 
ukucekelwa phansi kwanoma yimaphi amalungelo nezinkululeko ezibalulwe lapha.
