Isigaba 11
1. (1) Noma yimuphi umuntu obekwe icala lobugebengu uyonikezwa zonke iziqinisekiso ezidingekayo zokuzivikela.
Umuntu unelungelo lokubhekwa njengomsulwa kuze kube yilapho icala lakhe liqulwa futhi icala lakhe litholakala ngokuhambisana nomthetho. 
2. (ii) Noma yisiphi isenzo noma ukweqiwa kwanoma yimuphi umuntu, ngesikhathi senzeka, kunganqatshelwe umthetho kazwelonke noma wamazwe ngamazwe.
Labo abangenalo icala lobugebengu ngaphansi komthetho ngeke balahlwe yicala lobugebengu. Isijeziso ngeke sibe nzima ukwedlula
Izinhlinzeko zomthetho ezisebenzayo lapho wenza ubugebengu.

Angeke kube khona ukugxambukela empilweni yangasese, umndeni, ikhaya kanye nezincwadi zanoma yimuphi umuntu, isithunzi nesithunzi sakhe.
Isithunzi akumele sihlaselwe. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngumthetho kulokho kugxambukela noma ukuhlaselwa
shaya.

izwe.

Isigaba 18

Isigaba 19
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokubeka imibono yakhe nokuveza imizwa yakhe; leli lungelo lihlanganisa ilungelo lokuba nemibono ngaphandle
Ukukhululeka ekuphazamisekeni, nokufuna, ukwamukela kanye nokudlulisa ulwazi nganoma iyiphi imidiya futhi kungakhathalekile ukuthi ikuphi
kanye nenkululeko yokucabanga.  
Isigaba 20
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana nokuhlangana ngokuthula. 
2. (ii) Akekho ongaphoqwa ukuba abe yilungu leqembu. 
Isigaba 21
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ekubuseni kwezwe lakhe, ngokuqondile noma ngabameleli abakhethwe ngokukhululeka. 
2. (2) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuthola ithuba elilinganayo lokuhlanganyela emsebenzini womphakathi wezwe lakhe. 
3. (3) Intando yabantu iyisisekelo samandla kahulumeni; le ntando kufanele ivezwe njalo nangeqiniso
Lokhu kufanele kubonakale okhethweni olusemthethweni, okufanele lusekelwe emalungelweni okuvota jikelele nalinganayo futhi okufanele lusekelwe
Ivoti eliyimfihlo noma inqubo yokuvota yamahhala efanayo izokwenziwa. 
Isigaba 22
Wonke umuntu, njengelungu lomphakathi, unelungelo lokujabulela ukuvikeleka komphakathi futhi unelungelo lokujabulela isiqu sakhe

Isimo sensiza.  
Isigaba 23
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukukhetha ngokukhululekile umsebenzi ozowenza, nezimo zokusebenza ezifanele nezifanelekile
amafayela futhi ujabulele ukuvikeleka ekusweleni umsebenzi. 
 

 
2. (2) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lomholo olinganayo ngomsebenzi olinganayo ngaphandle kokubandlululwa. 
3. (3) Wonke umuntu osebenzayo unelungelo lokuthola iholo elifanelekile nelifanele, eliqinisekisiwe ukuthi lizomenza akwazi ukwenza umsebenzi wakhe.
Abantu nemindeni yabo banesimo sokuphila esihlangabezana nezidingo zabo, okunezelwa ngezinye izindlela lapho kudingeka
ukuphepha komphakathi. 
4. (4) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhlanganisa futhi ajoyine izinyunyana ukuze avikele izimfuno zakhe. 
Isigaba 24
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula nokuzilibazisa, okuhlanganisa nemingcele enengqondo yamahora okusebenza kanye nokukhokha njalo
Ilungelo lokuthatha ilivu ekhokhelwayo.  
Isigaba 25
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokujabulela ukuphila okudingekayo ukuze abe nempilo enhle kanye nenhlalakahle yakhe nomndeni wakhe.

Isigaba 26
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo, futhi imfundo kufanele ibe mahhala, okungenani ezingeni eliphansi neliyisisekelo.
Isiteji kufanele sibe kanje. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi kufanele ibe yimpoqo. Imfundo yezobuchwepheshe neyemisebenzi yezandla kufanele yenziwe ibe yizwe lonke
Isungulwe yonke indawo. Imfundo ephakeme kufanele ivuleleke kubo bonke ngokulinganayo ngesisekelo sokufaneleka. 
2. (ii) Inhloso yemfundo ukuthuthukisa ubuntu bomuntu ngokugcwele kanye nokuqinisa ukuqwashisa ngamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo.
Ngenhlonipho. Imfundo kufanele ikhuthaze ukuxhumana phakathi kwawo wonke amazwe, izinhlanga noma amaqembu ezenkolo.

3. (3) Abazali banelungelo lokubeka kuqala uhlobo lwemfundo okufanele izingane zabo ziyithole. 
Isigaba 27
 

 
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ngokukhululeka empilweni yamasiko omphakathi, athokozele ubuciko kanye nokwabelana ngesayensi
Inqubekelaphambili kanye nezinzuzo ezikhiqizayo. 
2. (ii) Ilungelo lawo wonke umuntu livela kunoma yimuphi umsebenzi wesayensi, wombhalo noma wobuciko awudalwe nguye
Izithakazelo zabo ezingokomoya nezingokwenyama zinelungelo lokujabulela isivikelo. 
Isigaba 28
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokufuna ukuhleleka kwezenhlalo kanye nezamazwe ngamazwe lapho imigomo ebekwe kulesi Simemezelo
Amalungelo nenkululeko equkethwe lapho kungafezeka ngokugcwele.  
Isigaba 29
1. (1) Wonke umuntu unezibopho emphakathini, ngoba kuphela emphakathini lapho ubuntu bakhe bungenzeka
Thola inkululeko nentuthuko egcwele. 
2. (2) Wonke umuntu, ekusebenziseni amalungelo nenkululeko yakhe, ungaphansi kwemikhawulo ebekwe umthetho.
Okuwukuphela kwenjongo yokumisa imingcele enjalo iwukuqinisekisa inhlonipho efanele ngamalungelo nenkululeko yabanye
Okunye ukuqashelwa nenhlonipho, nokuzivumelanisa nezimilo nokuhleleka komphakathi emphakathini wentando yeningi
kanye nezidingo ezisemthethweni zenhlalakahle jikelele. 
3. (iii) Lawa malungelo kanye nezinkululeko angeke zisetshenziswe ngokuphambene neNyunyana ngaphansi kwanoma yiziphi izimo.

Isigaba 30
Akukho kulesi simemezelo okuzothathwa njengokugunyaza buthule ilungelo lanoma yiliphi izwe, iqembu noma umuntu
Noma yimuphi umsebenzi noma ukuziphatha okuhloselwe ukubukela phansi noma yimaphi amalungelo nenkululeko ebalulwe kulesi Simemezelo.
