Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt

Fɔ no di rɛspɛkt we ɔlman na mɔtalman gɛt ɛn di rayt we ikwal ɛn we dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ tek, na di fawndeshɔn fɔ fridɔm, jɔstis ɛn pis na di wɔl; di we aw pipul dɛn nɔ de tek mɔtalman rayt ɛn we dɛn nɔ de tek tɛm wit dɛn, dɔn mek dɛn de du bad bad tin dɛn we dɔn mek mɔtalman in kɔnshɛns vɛks; ɛn di kam fɔ wan wɔl usay mɔtalman go ebul fɔ ɛnjɔy fridɔm fɔ tɔk ɛn biliv ɛn fridɔm frɔm fred ɛn want na di ay tin we ɔl pipul dɛn want, so dat dɛn nɔ go fos mɔtalman fɔ tɔn in bak pan am as las tin fɔ du agens di we aw pipul dɛn de trit dɛn kɔmpin dɛn bad ɛn we dɛn de mek pipul dɛn sɔfa; I impɔtant fɔ mek di lɔ de protɛkt mɔtalman rayt, ɛn i impɔtant fɔ mek padi biznɛs bitwin neshɔn dɛn go bifo. Insay in Chata, ɔl di mɛmba dɛn na di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn bin tɔk bak se dɛn biliv pan di men rayt dɛn we mɔtalman gɛt, di rɛspɛkt ɛn valyu we mɔtalman gɛt, ɛn di ikwal rayt dɛn we man ɛn uman gɛt, ɛn dɛn bin dɔn mekɔp dɛn maynd fɔ mek di soshal prɔgrɛs ɛn bɛtɛ standad fɔ liv insay bɔku fridɔm. Di Mɛmba Stet dɛn bin prɔmis fɔ wok wit di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn fɔ mek ɔlman rɛspɛkt ɛn fala mɔtalman rayt ɛn di men fridɔm dɛn. Bikɔs wan kɔmɔn ɔndastandin bɔt rayt ɛn fridɔm impɔtant pas ɔl fɔ mek dɛn impruv dis Kɔvinant ful wan, di Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli fɔ di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn, so, de tray fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya tru lanin ɛn ɛdyukeshɔn bitwin ɔlman ɛn institiushɔn dɛn na di sosayti, we de tink ɔltɛm bɔt dis Diklɛreshɔn, ɛn we de tray, tru di prɔgrɛsiv nashɔnal ɛn intanashɔnal mɛsej dɛn, fɔ mek shɔ se ɔlman no ɛn ɔbshɔb am fayn fayn wan fɔ dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya bitwin di pipul dɛn na di Mɛmba Stet dɛnsɛf ɛn di pipul dɛn na di teritɔri dɛn we dɛn de ɔnda dɛn; Wi de prich di Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt. Atikul 1

Dɛn bɔn ɔl mɔtalman fri ɛn ikwal pan rɛspɛkt ɛn rayt. Mɔtalman gɛt rizin ɛn kɔnshɛns bay insɛf ɛn dɛn fɔ du tin wit brɔdaship to dɛnsɛf. Atikul 2 Ɛvribɔdi gɛt ɔl di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de insay dis Diklɛreshɔn, we nɔ gɛt ɛni kayn difrɛns, lɛk in kɔlɔ, man ɔ uman, langwej, rilijɔn, pɔlitiks ɔ ɔda opinion, kɔntri ɔ soshal ɔrijin, prɔpati, bɔn ɔ ɔda stetmɛnt. Dɔn bak, dɛn nɔ fɔ gɛt ɛni diskrimineshɔn bikɔs ɔf di pɔlitikal, ligal ɔ intanashɔnal stetɔs we pɔsin in kɔntri ɔ teritɔri gɛt, ilɛksɛf da kɔntri ɔ teritɔri de na indipɛndɛnt stet, trɔstiship, teritɔri we nɔ de rul insɛf, ɔ ɔda tin dɛn we de ambɔg in sovereignty. Atikul 3 Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv, fridɔm ɛn sef. Atikul 4 Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ de na slev ɔ slev. Dɛn nɔ gri fɔ du ɔl kayn slev ɛn fɔ sɛl slev. Atikul 5 Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ mek dɛn sɔfa ɔ pɔnish am bad bad wan, we nɔ gɛt wanwɔd ɔ we de mek i nɔ gɛt wan rɛspɛkt. Atikul 6 Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ mek pipul dɛn no am as mɔtalman bifo di lɔ ɔlsay. Atikul 7
Ɔl pipul dɛn ikwal bifo di lɔ ɛn dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn frɔm di lɔ we nɔ gɛt ɛni diskrimineshɔn. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn agens ɛni diskrimineshɔn we nɔ gri wit dis Diklɛreshɔn ɛn agens ɛnitin we go mek dɛn du dis kayn diskrimineshɔn. Atikul 8 Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ɛfifishin rilif frɔm kɔmpiten domestik kɔt fɔ akt we de agens di fawndeshɔnal rayt dɛn we di Kɔnstityushɔn ɔ lɔ gi. Atikul 9 Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ arɛst, detɛn ɔ kɛr pɔsin go na ɔda kɔntri bay wilful. Atikul 10 Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ mek dɛn jɔj am fayn ɛn na pɔblik wit ful ikwal bifo indipɛndɛnt traybɔn we nɔ de tek wan pɔsin bɛtɛ pas ɔda pɔsin fɔ disayd in rayt, ɔbligayshɔn ɛn kriminal chaj we i gɛt. Atikul 11 ​​1. Ɛnibɔdi we dɛn aks fɔ kraym gɛt rayt fɔ tek am se i nɔ du natin te dɛn pruv se i gilti akɔdin to di lɔ we dɛn de jɔj am na pɔblik usay dɛn de gi ɔl wetin i nid fɔ difend am. 2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go gilti bikɔs ɔf ɛnitin we i du ɔ we i nɔ du we, di tɛm we dɛn du di akt, nɔ bin bi kraym ɔnda di nashɔnal ɔ intanashɔnal lɔ. Apat frɔm dat, dɛn nɔ go gi ɛni pɔnishmɛnt we ebi pas di pɔnishmɛnt we dɛn bin fɔ dɔn gi di tɛm we dɛn du di kraym. Atikul 12 Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit in prayvet layf, os, usay i de ɔ kɔrɛspɔndɛns, ɔ fɔ kɔndɛm in ɔna ɛn gudnem. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ mek di lɔ protɛkt am frɔm da kayn tin we de ambɔg am ɔ kɔndɛm am. Atikul 13

1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ muv ɛn fɔ de na in kɔntri. 2. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ kɔmɔt na ɛni kɔntri, ivin in yon kɔntri, ɛn i gɛt rayt fɔ go bak na in kɔntri. Atikul 14 1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ go fɛn ɛn gɛt asaylans na ɔda kɔntri frɔm we dɛn de mek dɛn sɔfa. 2. Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ tɔk bɔt dis rayt we dɛn kɛr pɔsin go na kɔt fɔ kraym dɛn we rili nɔ gɛt fɔ du wit pɔlitiks ɔ fɔ du tin dɛn we nɔ gri wit wetin di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn want ɛn prinsipul dɛn. Atikul 15 1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ bi kɔntri. 2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go tek in kɔntri bay wilful ɔ nɔ gi am di rayt fɔ chenj in kɔntri. Atikul 16 1. Big man ɛn uman gɛt rayt fɔ mared ɛn mek famili we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ stɔp dɛn bikɔs ɔf dɛn kɔlɔ, kɔntri ɔ rilijɔn. Dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal rayt bɔt mared, we dɛn de mared ɛn we dɛn dɔn skata. 2. Dɛn kin mek mared jɔs wit di fri ɛn kɔmplit kɔnsɛntmɛnt fɔ di wan dɛn we go mared tumara bambay. 3. Di famili na natura ɛn besik yunit na sosayti ɛn i gɛt rayt fɔ mek di sosayti ɛn di stet protɛkt am. Atikul 17 1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt prɔpati fɔ insɛf ɛn wit ɔda pipul dɛn. 2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go tek in prɔpati bay wilful. Atikul 18
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tink, kɔnshɛns ɛn rilijɔn. Dis rayt inklud fridɔm fɔ chenj in rilijɔn ɔ biliv ɛn fɔ sho in rilijɔn ɔ biliv, in wan ɔ na kɔmyuniti wit ɔda pipul dɛn ɛn na pɔblik ɔ prayvet, bay we i de du rilijɔn, wɔship ɛn sɛlibret. Atikul 19 Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tink ɛn tɔk wetin i want. Dɛn rayt ya inklud fridɔm fɔ ol opinion we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit am ɛn fridɔm fɔ fɛn, gɛt ɛn gi infɔmeshɔn ɛn aydia tru ɛni midia, ilɛksɛf na di frɔnt. Atikul 20 1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛda ɛn jɔyn an wit pis. 2. Dɛn nɔ go fos ɛnibɔdi fɔ tek pat pan ɛni asosieshɔn. Atikul 21 1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan di gɔvmɛnt na in kɔntri, ilɛksɛf na dairekt ɔ tru pipul dɛn we dɛn kin pik fri wan. 2. Ɔlman gɛt ikwal rayt fɔ ol pɔblik ɔfis na in kɔntri. 3. Di pipul dɛn want na di men tin we gɔvmɛnt pawa de yuz. Dis go bi tru rɛgyula ɛn tru ilɛkshɔn bay sikrit ɔ ikwal fri vot prosidur akɔdin to yunivasal ɛn ikwal rayt fɔ vot. Atikul 22 Ɔlman, as mɛmba na di sosayti, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt soshal sikyɔriti ɛn rayt fɔ no, tru di nashɔnal ɛfɔt ɛn intanashɔnal kɔpɔreshɔn, ɛn akɔdin to di ɔganayzeshɔn ɛn risɔs dɛn we ɛni Stet gɛt, di ikɔnomi, soshal ɛn kɔlchɔral rayt dɛn we impɔtant fɔ mek i fri fɔ divɛlɔp in rɛspɛkt ɛn pɔsin. Atikul 23

1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ wok, fɔ pik di wok we i want fɔ du, fɔ gɛt rayt ɛn fayn wok kɔndishɔn ɛn fɔ protɛkt am frɔm pipul dɛn we nɔ gɛt wok. 2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal kɔmpɛnshɔn fɔ ikwal wok we nɔ gɛt ɛni diskrimineshɔn. 3. Ɛnibɔdi we de wok gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt jɔs ɛn bɛnifit, we go mek insɛf ɛn in famili gɛt layf we go gri wit mɔtalman rɛspɛkt ɛn we dɛn go ad, if nid de, ɔda soshal sikyɔriti mɛsej dɛn. 4. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ mek ɛn jɔyn leba yunion fɔ protɛkt in intɛres. Atikul 24 Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ rɛst ɛn ɛnjɔy insɛf, ivin di rayt tɛm fɔ wok ɛn fɔ lɛf fɔ wok ɔltɛm. Atikul 25 1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv in layf we go mek in ɛn in famili gɛt wɛlbɔdi ɛn wɛlbɔdi, ivin it, klos, say fɔ slip, mɛdikal kia ɛn soshal wɛlbɔdi we i nid, ɛn di rayt fɔ gɛt sikyɔriti if i nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ tin fɔ liv bikɔs i nɔ gɛt wok, i sik, i gɛt disabled, in man ɔ in wɛf day, i ol ɔ ɔda tin we i nɔ ebul fɔ kɔntrol. 2. Mama ɛn pikin dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt spɛshal protɛkshɔn ɛn sɔpɔt. Ɔl pikin dɛn kin gɛt di sem soshal protɛkshɔn ilɛk usay dɛn de. Atikul 26 1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ lan buk. Edukeshon fɔ fri, at ɔl na di praymari ɛn besik lɛvul. Praymari ɛdyukeshɔn fɔ bi kɔmpalsɔri. Teknik ɛn vokeshɔnal ɛdyukeshɔn fɔ de ɔlsay na di wɔl, ɛn ɔlman fɔ gɛt ay edyukeshɔn ikwal bikɔs ɔf di gud we aw dɛn sabi du dɛn wok. 2. Edukeshon de aim fɔ ful divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ pɔsin in pasɔnaliti ɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt mɔtalman rayt ɛn di men fridɔm dɛn. Edukeshon de mek pipul ɔndastand, tolɛreshɔn ɛn...
Wi fɔ mek padi biznɛs ɛn fɔ mek di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn wok fɔ mek pis kɔntinyu fɔ de. 3. Mama ɛn papa dɛn gɛt di prɔyoritɛd fɔ pik di kayn edyukeshɔn we dɛn go gi dɛn pikin dɛn. Atikul 27 1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat fri wan pan di kɔlchɔ layf na di kɔmyuniti, ɛnjɔy di arts, ɛn sheb di advansmɛnt fɔ sayɛns ɛn di bɛnifit dɛn we i de gi. 2. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt in spiritual ɛn matirial intɛres we kɔmɔt frɔm sayɛns, litrɛri ɔ atis prɔdak dɛm we i mek. Atikul 28 Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt sɔshal ɛn intanashɔnal ɔda we di rayt ɛn fridɔm we dɛn put insay dis Diklɛreshɔn go ful-ɔp. Atikul 29 1. Ɔlman gɛt ɔbligayshɔn to di kɔmyuniti usay na in pɔsin nɔmɔ go ebul fɔ divɛlɔp fri wan ɛn ful wan. . 3. Dɛn nɔ fɔ yuz dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya we nɔ gri wit wetin di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn want ɛn prinsipul dɛn. Atikul 30 Dɛn nɔ go tek ɛnitin na dis Diklɛreshɔn as we de gi ɛni Stet, grup ɔ pɔsin rayt fɔ du ɛnitin ɔ fɔ tek ɛni akshɔn we gɛt fɔ du wit fɔ pwɛl ɛni wan pan di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de insay dis Diklɛreshɔn.
