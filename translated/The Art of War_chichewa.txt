I. Kuyika Mapulani

1. Sun Tzu anati: Luso lankhondo ndi lofunika kwambiri ku Boma.

2. Ndi nkhani ya moyo ndi imfa, njira yopita ku chitetezo kapena chiwonongeko. Chifukwa chake ndi phunziro lofunsidwa lomwe silingathe kunyalanyazidwa.

3. Choncho, luso la nkhondo limayang'aniridwa ndi zinthu zisanu zokhazikika, zomwe ziyenera kuganiziridwa muzokambirana za munthu, pofufuza zomwe zingapezeke m'munda.

4. Izi ndi: (1) Lamulo la Makhalidwe; (2) Kumwamba; (3) Dziko; (4) Mtsogoleri; (5) Njira ndi chilango.

5, 6. Chilamulo cha Makhalidwe Abwino chimachititsa anthu kukhala ogwirizana kotheratu ndi wolamulira wawo, kotero kuti adzamtsatira mosasamala kanthu za moyo wawo, osachita mantha ndi ngozi iliyonse.

7. Kumwamba kumatanthauza usiku ndi usana, kuzizira ndi kutentha, nthawi ndi nyengo.

8. Dziko lapansi lili ndi mipata, Yaikulu ndi yaing'ono; ngozi ndi chitetezo; malo otseguka ndi njira zopapatiza; mwayi wa moyo ndi imfa.

9. Mtsogoleri akuyimira ubwino wa nzeru, kudzipereka, kukoma mtima, kulimbika mtima ndi kukhwima.

10. Mwa njira ndi mwambo ziyenera kumveka kusuntha kwa gulu lankhondo m'magawo ake oyenera, kumaliza maphunziro a maudindo pakati pa akuluakulu, kukonza misewu yomwe katundu angafikire asilikali, ndi kuyang'anira ndalama zankhondo.

11. Mitu isanu iyi iyenera kukhala yodziwika kwa mkulu wa asilikali aliyense: amene akuidziwa ndiye wopambana; amene sadziwa izo adzalephera.

12 Chifukwa chake, m’kukambitsirana kwanu, pofuna kudziwa za nkhondo, akhale maziko a mafanizo, motere:

13. (1) Kodi pa mafumu awiriwa ndi ati amene ali ndi malamulo a makhalidwe abwino? (2) Kodi ndi ndani mwa akazembe awiriwa amene ali ndi luso kwambiri? (3) Kodi ubwino wochokera Kumwamba ndi Padziko Lapansi uli ndi ndani? (4) Ndi mbali iti imene chilango chimaperekedwa mwamphamvu kwambiri? (5) Ndi asilikali ati amphamvu? (6) Kodi ndi mbali iti imene akuluakulu ndi amuna amaphunzitsidwa kwambiri? (7) Kodi ndi gulu liti limene muli kulimbikira kwakukulu ponse paŵiri pa mphotho ndi chilango?

14. Pogwiritsa ntchito mfundo zisanu ndi ziwirizi nditha kulosera kupambana kapena kugonja.

15. Kazembe amene amvera uphungu wanga ndi kuuchita, adzagonjetsa; Woyang'anira wamkulu amene samvera uphungu wanga kapena kuchitapo kanthu, adzalephera: --woterowo achotsedwe!

16. Pamene ndikutsogolera phindu la uphungu wanga, pindulani ndi mikhalidwe iliyonse yothandiza mopitirira malamulo wamba.

17. Malinga ndi mmene zinthu zilili bwino, munthu ayenera kusintha zolinga zake.

18. Nkhondo zonse zakhazikika pachinyengo.

19. Chifukwa chake, tikatha kuukira, tiyenera kuoneka ngati sitingathe; pogwiritsira ntchito mphamvu zathu, tiyenera kuwoneka osagwira ntchito; tikakhala pafupi, tiyenera kupangitsa adani kukhulupirira kuti tili kutali; tikakhala patali, tiyenera kumuchititsa kukhulupirira kuti tili pafupi.

20. Gwirani nyambo kuti mukope mdani. Onetsani chisokonezo, ndi kumuphwanya.

21. Ngati ali wotetezedwa pachilichonse, mkonzereni. Ngati ali ndi mphamvu zopambana, mupewe.

22. Ngati mdani wanu ali ndi mkwiyo, funani kumukwiyitsa. Adziyese wofooka, kuti adzikuza.

23. Ngati akukhala bwino, musamupumule. Ngati mphamvu zake zigwirizana, zilekanitseni.

24. Mumenyereni pomwe sadakonzekere, kawonekere komwe simukuyembekezeredwa.

25. Zida zankhondo izi, zotsogolera ku chigonjetso, siziyenera kuwululidwa kale.

26. Tsopano kazembe amene wapambana pankhondo amawerengera zambiri m'kachisi wake nkhondoyo isanamenyedwe. Mkulu wa asilikali amene wagonja pankhondoyo amawerengetseratu pasadakhale. Momwemonso mawerengedwe ambiri amatsogolera ku chigonjetso, ndi mawerengedwe ochepa kuti agonjetse: kuli bwanji osawerengera konse! Ndi chidwi pa mfundo imeneyi ndimatha kudziwiratu yemwe angapambane kapena kuluza.

II. Kumenya Nkhondo

1. Sun Tzu anati: M’zochitika zankhondo, kumene m’bwalo muli magaleta aliwiro chikwi, monga magareta olemera ochuluka, ndi asilikali ovala makalata zikwi zana limodzi, okhala ndi zakudya zokwanira kuwanyamulira ma li chikwi, ndalama zogulira kunyumba ndi kutsogolo, kuphatikizapo zosangalatsa za alendo, zinthu zing’onozing’ono monga zomatira ndi utoto, ndi ndalama zogwiritsidwa ntchito pa magareta ndi ma ounces a siliva okwana chikwi chimodzi patsiku. Umu ndi mtengo wokweza gulu lankhondo la amuna 100,000.

2. Mukamenya nkhondo yeniyeni, ngati kupambana kwachedwa, ndiye kuti zida za anthu zidzazimiririka ndipo khama lawo lidzachepa. Ukazinga mudzi, mphamvu zako zidzatha.

3. Apanso, ngati kampeni itenga nthawi yayitali, chuma cha Boma sichingafanane ndi zovuta.
4. Tsopano, zida zanu zikatha, kukhudzika kwanu kutha, mphamvu zanu zatha ndipo chuma chanu chatha, mafumu ena adzaphuka kuti atengerepo mwayi pamalekezero anu. Ndiye palibe munthu, ngakhale wanzeru zotani, amene angapewe zotulukapo zake.

5. Chotero, ngakhale kuti tamva za kufulumira kopusa m’nkhondo, kuchenjera sikunawonedwepo kogwirizanitsidwa ndi kuchedwa kwanthaŵi yaitali.

6. Palibe chitsanzo chosonyeza kuti dziko linapindula ndi nkhondo yaitali.

7. Ndi mmodzi yekha amene akudziŵa bwino za kuipa kwa nkhondo amene angamvetse bwino lomwe njira yaphindu yopitirizira nkhondoyo.

8. Msilikali waluso sakweza msonkho waciwiri, ngakhale ngolo zake zonyamula katundu sanyamulidwa koposa kaŵiri;

9. Bweretsani zida zankhondo zochokera kwanuko, koma dyerani adani; Motero asilikali adzakhala ndi chakudya chokwanira.

10. Umphawi wa Boma umapangitsa gulu lankhondo kusamalidwa ndi zopereka zakutali. Kuthandizira kusunga gulu lankhondo kutali kumapangitsa anthu kukhala osauka.

11 Kumbali ina, kuyandikira kwa gulu lankhondo kumapangitsa mitengo kukwera; ndipo mitengo yokwera imachititsa kuti zinthu za anthu zithe.

12. Zinthu zawo zikadzatsanulidwa, mlimi adzasautsidwa ndi zolemetsa zolemetsa.

13, 14. Ndi kutayika kwa zinthu izi ndi kutha kwa mphamvu, nyumba za anthu zidzavula, ndipo magawo atatu a magawo khumi a ndalama zawo adzaphwanyidwa; pamene ndalama za boma za magaleta osweka, akavalo otha, zotetezera pachifuwa ndi zisoti, mauta ndi mivi, mikondo ndi zishango, zovala zotetezera, ng’ombe zolusa ndi ngolo zolemera, zidzafika pa magawo anayi a magawo khumi a ndalama zake zonse.

15. Choncho, mkulu wankhondo wanzeru amangofuna kudyera mdani. Katundu wa ngolo imodzi ya zakudya za mdani ndi wofanana ndi makumi awiri a iye mwini, ndipo momwemonso chikhomo chimodzi cha zakudya zake chimafanana ndi makumi awiri kuchokera ku sitolo yake.

16. Tsopano kuti tiphe mdani, amuna athu ayenera kudzutsidwa; kuti pakhale mwayi wogonjetsa adaniwo, ayenera kukhala ndi mphotho zawo.

17 Chifukwa chake pomenyana ndi magaleta, atagwidwa magareta khumi kapena kuposerapo, ayenera kulipidwa amene adatenga woyamba. Mbendera zathu ziyenera kulowetsedwa m'malo mwa adani, ndi magaleta osakanikirana ndi kugwiritsidwa ntchito molumikizana ndi athu. Asilikali ogwidwawo ayenera kuchitiridwa chifundo ndi kusungidwa.

18. Uku kumatchedwa, kugwiritsa ntchito mdani wogonjetsedwa kuti awonjezere mphamvu zake.

19. Chifukwa chake, pankhondo chinthu chanu chachikulu chikhale chigonjetso, osati kuchita nkhondo zazitali.

20 Potero zidziwike kuti mtsogoleri wa magulu ankhondo ndiye woweruza za tsoka la anthu, munthu amene zimadalira ngati mtunduwo udzakhala mumtendere kapena pangozi.

III. Kuukira kwa Stratagem

1. Sun Tzu anati: Mu luso lothandiza pankhondo, chinthu chabwino koposa ndicho kutenga dziko la mdani lonse ndi kukhala losatha; kuswa ndi kuwononga sikuli bwino. Momwemonso, ndikwabwino kulanda gulu lonse lankhondo kuposa kuliwononga, kulanda gulu lankhondo, gulu lankhondo kapena kampani yonse kuposa kuwawononga.

2. Chifukwa chake kumenya nkhondo ndikugonjetsa nkhondo zanu zonse si kupambana kwakukulu; Kupambana kwakukulu kumaphatikizapo kuswa kukana kwa mdani popanda kumenyana.

3. Choncho njira yapamwamba kwambiri ya ukazembe ndi kutsekereza ziwembu za adani; Chotsatira chabwino ndikuletsa kuphatikizika kwa magulu ankhondo a adani; chotsatira pokonzekera ndi kuukira gulu la adani m'munda; ndipo ndondomeko yoipitsitsa kuposa yonse ndiyo kuzinga mizinda yokhala ndi mipanda.

4. Lamulo nlakuti, osazinga mizinda yokhala ndi mipanda ngati n’kotheka kuipeŵeka. Kukonza zofunda, zogona zosunthika, ndi zida zosiyanasiyana zankhondo, zidzatenga miyezi itatu yathunthu; ndipo kuunjika kwa zitunda mozungulira mpanda kudzatenga miyezi itatu ina.

5. Kazembeyo, wolephera kuugwira mtima kukwiyitsidwa kwake, adzaukira amuna ake kunkhondo ngati nyerere, ndipo chotsatira chake chinali chakuti gawo limodzi mwa magawo atatu a amuna ake aphedwe, pamene mzindawo udakali wosalandidwa. Izi ndi zotsatira zoipa za kuzingidwa.

6. Choncho, Mtsogoleri waluso amagonjetseratu asilikali a adani popanda nkhondo; alanda midzi yao osaizinga; akugwetsa ufumu wawo popanda kugwira ntchito nthawi yayitali m'munda.

7. Ndi magulu ankhondo ake adzatsutsana ndi ulamuliro wa Ufumu, ndipo motero, popanda kutaya munthu, kupambana kwake kudzakhala kokwanira. Iyi ndi njira yolimbana ndi stratagem.

8. Ndilo lamulo pankhondo, ngati magulu athu ali khumi kwa mdani mmodzi, kumzinga; ngati asanu kwa mmodzi, kumenyana naye; ngati kuwirikiza kawiri, kugawa ankhondo athu pawiri.
9. Ngati mofanana zikugwirizana, tikhoza kupereka nkhondo; ngati ndi otsika pang’ono m’chiŵerengero, tingapeŵe mdani; ngati osalingana m'zonse, tikhoza kumthawa.

10. Chifukwa chake, ngakhale kumenyana kolimba kungapangidwe ndi gulu laling'ono, pamapeto pake kuyenera kugwidwa ndi mphamvu yaikulu.

11. Tsopano Jenerali ndiye linga la Boma; ngati chitetezo chokwanira pazigawo zonse; Boma lidzakhala lamphamvu; ngati khomalo lili ndi vuto, Boma likhala lofooka.

12. Pali njira zitatu zomwe wolamulira angabweretse tsoka pa gulu lake lankhondo.

13. (1) Mwa kulamula asilikali kuti apite patsogolo kapena kubwerera m’mbuyo, pokhala wosadziŵa kuti sangathe kumvera. Izi zimatchedwa hobbling asilikali.

14. (2) Poyesa kulamulira gulu lankhondo monga momwe amayendetsera ufumu, pokhala wosazindikira mikhalidwe imene imapezeka m’gulu lankhondo. Izi zimayambitsa kusakhazikika m'maganizo a msilikali.

15. (3) Polemba ntchito akuluakulu a gulu lake lankhondo popanda tsankho, mwa kusazindikira mfundo yankhondo yogwirizana ndi zochitika. Zimenezi zimasokoneza chidaliro cha asilikaliwo.

16. Koma pamene gulu lankhondo liri losakhazikika ndi losakhulupirira, vuto ndithudi lidzabwera kuchokera kwa akalonga ena ankhanza. Izi zikungobweretsa chipwirikiti mu gulu lankhondo, ndikuchotsa chipambano.

17. Motero tingathe kudziwa kuti pali zinthu zisanu zofunika kuti tipambane: (1) Iye adzapambana amene akudziwa nthawi yoti amenye nkhondo komanso nthawi yoti asamenyane. (2) Adzapambana amene amadziwa kulimbana ndi mphamvu zazikulu ndi zotsika. (3) Adzapambana amene gulu lake lankhondo likuyenda ndi mzimu womwewo m’magulu ake onse. (4) Adzapambana amene, adadzikonzekeretsa, amadikirira kutenga mdani wosakonzekera. (5) Adzapambana yemwe ali ndi mphamvu zankhondo ndipo sasokonezedwa ndi wolamulira.

18. Choncho kunena kuti: “Ngati uwadziwa adani ndikudzidziwa wekha, suyenera kuopa zotsatira za nkhondo zana; Ngati mukudzidziwa nokha koma osati mdani, chifukwa chigonjetso chilichonse chomwe mwapeza mudzagonja. Ngati simudziwa mdani kapena inu nokha, mudzagonja pankhondo iliyonse.

IV. Tactical Dispositions

1. Sun Tzu anati: Omenyana abwino akale poyamba anadziika okha kupitirira zotheka kugonja, ndiyeno anadikira mpata wogonjetsa adaniwo.

2. Kudziteteza tokha ku kugonja uli m'manja mwathu, koma mwayi wogonjetsa mdani umaperekedwa ndi mdani mwiniwake.

3. Choncho womenyana Wabwino amatha kudzitchinjiriza kuti asagonjetsedwe, koma sangatsimikize zowagonjetsa adaniwo.

4. Choncho kunena kuti: Munthu atha kudziwa kugonjetsa popanda kukwanitsa.

5. Chitetezo pakugonjetsedwa kumatanthauza njira zodzitetezera; Kukhoza kugonjetsa mdani kumatanthauza kutenga chokhumudwitsa.

6. Kuyimirira pachitetezo kumasonyeza mphamvu zosakwanira; kuukira, kuchuluka kwa mphamvu.

7. Kazembe amene ali ndi luso lachitetezo amabisala m'malo obisika a dziko lapansi; wodziwa nkhondo awala kuchokera kumwambamwamba. Motero mbali imodzi tili ndi kuthekera kodziteteza tokha; koma chigonjetso chokwanira.

8. Kuwona chipambano kokha pamene chiri mkati mwa ken wa gulu wamba si acme ya kupambana.

9. Ndiponso sikupambana ngati Mumenya nkhondo ndi kugonjetsa, ndipo Ufumu wonse ukunena: "Chabwino!

10. Kukweza tsitsi la m'dzinja si chizindikiro cha mphamvu zazikulu; kuona dzuwa ndi mwezi si chizindikiro cha maso akuthwa; kumva phokoso la bingu si chizindikiro cha khutu lofulumira.

11. Zimene anthu akale ankazitcha kuti wankhondo wochenjera ndi amene samangopambana, koma amachita bwino kwambiri popambana mosavuta.

12. Choncho kupambana kwake sikumamubweretsera mbiri ya nzeru kapena ulemu chifukwa cha kulimba mtima.

13. Amapambana pankhondo zake popanda kulakwitsa. Kusalakwitsa ndi kumene kumatsimikizira kutsimikizirika kwa chipambano, pakuti kumatanthauza kugonjetsa mdani amene wagonjetsedwa kale.

14. Chifukwa chake wankhondo waluso amadziyika yekha pamalo omwe amapangitsa kugonja kukhala kosatheka, ndipo samaphonya mphindi yakugonjetsa mdani.

15. Umo ndi momwe zilili kuti pankhondo wopambana amangofuna kumenya nkhondo pambuyo popambana, pomwe amene wapatsidwa kugonjetsa nkhondo zoyamba ndiyeno pambuyo pake amayang'ana kupambana.

16. Mtsogoleri wangwiro amakulitsa lamulo la makhalidwe abwino, ndipo amatsatira mosamalitsa njira ndi mwambo; motero ali m’mphamvu yake kulamulira chipambano.

17. Ponena za njira yankhondo, tili ndi, choyamba, Muyeso; chachiwiri, Kuyerekezera kuchuluka; chachitatu, Mawerengedwe; chachinayi, Kulinganiza mwaŵi; chachisanu, Victory.
18. Muyeso udachokera kudziko lapansi; Kuyerekeza kuchuluka kwa kuyeza; Kuwerengera kwa Kuyerekeza kuchuluka; Kulinganiza mwayi ku Mawerengedwe; ndi Kupambana ku Kulinganiza mwa mwayi.

19. Gulu lankhondo lopambana, lolimbana ndi wopirikitsidwa, lili ngati muyeso wa paundi yoikidwa pa sikelo pa njere imodzi.

20 Kuthamanga kwa mphamvu yogonjetsa kuli ngati kuphulika kwa madzi otsetsereka m’phompho lakuya mamita 1,000.

V. Mphamvu

1. Sun Tzu anati: Kulamulira mphamvu yaikulu ndi mfundo yofanana ndi kulamulira kwa amuna ochepa: ndi funso chabe la kugawa chiwerengero chawo.

2. Kumenyana ndi gulu lalikulu lankhondo pansi pa ulamuliro wanu sikusiyananso ndi kumenyana ndi ang'onoang'ono: ndi funso lokhazikitsa zizindikiro ndi zizindikiro.

3. Kuwonetsetsa kuti gulu lanu lonse likhoza kulimbana ndi kuukira kwa mdani ndikukhalabe osagwedezeka - izi zimachitika ndi machitidwe olunjika ndi osalunjika.

4. Kuti mphamvu ya ankhondo anu ikhale ngati mwala wophwanyidwa pa dzira - izi zimachitika ndi sayansi ya mfundo zofooka ndi zamphamvu.

5. Pankhondo zonse, njira yachindunji ingagwiritsidwe ntchito polowa nawo nkhondo, koma njira zosalunjika zidzafunika kuti apambane.

6. Njira zosalunjika, zogwiritsidwa ntchito bwino, sizitha ngati Kumwamba ndi Dziko Lapansi, zosatha monga kuyenda kwa mitsinje ndi mitsinje; monga dzuwa ndi mwezi, zimathera koma kuyambanso; monga nyengo zinayi, zipita kuti zibwererenso kamodzinso.

7. Palibe nyimbo zokwana zisanu, komabe kusakanizika kwa nyimbo zisanuzi kumapangitsa kuti pakhale nyimbo zambiri kuposa zomwe zingamveke.

8. Palibe mitundu yoyambirira yosapitirira isanu (yabuluu, yachikasu, yofiira, yoyera, ndi yakuda), komabe ikaphatikizana imatulutsa mitundu yambirimbiri kuposa imene sinaonekere.

9. Palibe zokometsera zosaposa zisanu (zowawasa, zowawasa, zotsekemera, zotsekemera, zowawa), komabe kusakanizikana kumapereka zokometsera zambiri kuposa zomwe zingalawe.

10. Pankhondo palibe njira zoposa ziwiri: yachindunji ndi yosalunjika; komabe ziŵirizi zikaphatikizidwa zimabweretsa kusinthasintha kosalekeza kwa machitidwe.

11. Olunjika ndi osalunjika amatsogolerana motsatana. Zili ngati kusuntha mozungulira - sumafika kumapeto. Ndani angathe kuthetsa mwayi wa kuphatikiza kwawo?

12. Kumayambika kwa magulu ankhondo kuli ngati Kuthamanga kwa mtsinje umene ungagubuduze miyala panjira yake.

13. Kusankha bwino kumafanana ndi kuuluka kwanthawi yake bwino kwa mphako komwe kumamuthandiza kumenya ndi kuononga nyamayo.

14. Choncho, Wankhondo Wabwino adzakhala woipa pa chiyambi chake, Ndi wachangu pa chiweruzo.

15. Mphamvu zingayerekezedwe ndi kupindika kwa uta wopingasa; kusankha, kutulutsa choyambitsa.

16. Pakati pa chipwirikiti ndi chipwirikiti cha nkhondo, pakhoza kukhala chisokonezo koma palibe chisokonezo chenicheni; pakati pa chisokonezo ndi chisokonezo, gulu lanu likhoza kukhala lopanda mutu kapena mchira, komabe lidzakhala umboni wotsutsa kugonjetsedwa.

17. Matenda otsatiridwa amabweretsa chilango changwiro, mantha oyerekeza amabweretsa kulimba mtima; kufooka koyerekeza kumapereka mphamvu.

18. Kubisa dongosolo pansi pa chisokonezo ndi funso la kugawa; kubisa kulimba mtima pansi pa chiwonetsero chamanyazi kumatengera thumba la mphamvu zobisika; kubisa mphamvu ndi kufooka kumachitidwa ndi malingaliro anzeru.

19. Choncho, amene ali wodziwa Kumusunga mdani, ali ndi maonekedwe achinyengo, momwe mdaniyo achitira. Iye amapereka nsembe chinachake, kuti mdani akachikwatule.

20. Pogwira nyambo, amamuyendetsa paulendo; ndiye amlalira iye pamodzi ndi gulu la anthu osankhika.

21. Msilikali wochenjera amayang'ana zotsatira za mphamvu zophatikizana, ndipo safuna zambiri kuchokera kwa anthu. Chifukwa chake kuthekera kwake kusankha amuna oyenera ndikugwiritsa ntchito mphamvu zophatikizana.

22. Pamene aphatikizana mphamvu, asilikali ake ankhondo Amakhala ngati nkhuni zokunkhulira, kapena miyala. Pakuti ndi chikhalidwe cha chipika kapena mwala kukhala wosasunthika pa nthaka yosalala, ndi kusuntha pamene uli pa malo otsetsereka; ngati ngodya zinayi, kuti ifike poyima, koma ngati yozungulira, kuti ipitirire pansi.

23. Chotero mphamvu yopangidwa ndi amuna ankhondo abwino ili ngati mphamvu ya mwala wozungulira wogubuduzika pansi pa phiri lalitali mamita zikwi zambiri. Zambiri pa nkhani ya mphamvu.

VI. Mfundo Zofooka ndi Zamphamvu

1. Sun Tzu adati: Aliyense amene ali woyamba m'munda ndikudikirira kubwera kwa adani, adzakhala watsopano kunkhondo; amene ali wachiwiri pabwalo nafulumira kunkhondo adzafika wotopa.
2. Choncho wankhondo wochenjera amaika chifuniro chake kwa adani, koma salola kuti chifuniro cha mdani chikhazikike pa iye.

3. Pomuchitira zabwino, atha kuchititsa mdani kuyandikira mwakufuna kwake; kapena, mwa kuwononga, angapangitse kukhala kosatheka kwa mdani kuyandikira.

4. Ngati mdani adzipumula, akhoza kumchitira chipongwe; ngati ali ndi chakudya chokwanira, akhoza kumupha ndi njala; ngati atamanga msasa mwakachetechete, akhoza kumukakamiza kusamuka.

5. Kuwonekera pamalo omwe mdani ayenera kufulumira kuteteza; gubani mwachangu kupita kumalo omwe simunayembekezere.

6. Gulu lankhondo likhoza kuguba mtunda wautali popanda mavuto, ngati lidutsa m'dziko limene palibe mdani.

7. Mungakhale otsimikiza kuti mupambana muzowukira zanu ngati mutangomenyana ndi malo omwe sali otetezedwa.Mungathe kutsimikizira chitetezo cha chitetezo chanu ngati mutangokhala ndi maudindo omwe sangathe kuukiridwa.

8. Chifukwa chake kazembeyo ndi waluso pomenya nkhondo yemwe mdani wake sadziwa choti ateteze; ndipo ndi waluso podzitchinjiriza, amene mdani wake sadziwa chomuukira.

9. E, iwe, luso la umulungu, lobisika ndi lobisika! Kudzera mwa inu timaphunzira kukhala osaoneka, kudzera mwa inu osamveka; ndipo chifukwa chake tikhoza kugwira tsogolo la mdani m'manja mwathu.

10. Mungathe kutsogola ndi kukhala Opanda chinji, ngati mutapereka zofooka za Adani; mutha kupuma pantchito ndikukhala otetezeka ngati mayendedwe anu ali othamanga kuposa a mdani.

11. Ngati tifuna kumenya nkhondo, mdaniyo angakakamizidwe kuchita chinkhoswe ngakhale atabisalira kuseri kwa linga lalitali ndi dzenje lakuya. Zomwe tiyenera kuchita ndikuukira malo ena omwe adzakakamizika kumasula.

12. Ngati sitikufuna kumenya nkhondo, tingalepheretse mdani kutisokoneza ngakhale mizere ya msasa wathu yangoonetsedwa pansi. Zomwe tikuyenera kuchita ndikuponya chinthu chosamvetseka komanso chosawerengeka m'njira yake.

.

14. Tikhoza kupanga gulu limodzi logwirizana, pamene mdani ayenera kugawanika kukhala tizigawo ting'onoting'ono. Chifukwa chake padzakhala gulu lonse lolimbana ndi magawo osiyanasiyana athunthu, zomwe zikutanthauza kuti tidzakhala ambiri kwa ochepa a adani.

15. Ndipo ngati tingathe kulimbana ndi gulu lochepa ndi lamphamvu, otsutsa athu adzakhala pamavuto aakulu.

16. Malo amene tikufuna kumenya nkhondo asadziwike; chifukwa ndiye mdani adzayenera kukonzekera kuukira komwe kungachitike pazigawo zingapo zosiyanasiyana; ndipo magulu ake akugawidwa m'njira zambiri, ziwerengero zomwe tidzayenera kukumana nazo panthawi iliyonse zidzakhala zochepa.

17. Ngati mdani alimbitsa galimoto yake, Adzafooketsa kumbuyo kwake; ngati alimbitsa kumbuyo kwake, adzafooketsa galimoto yake; akalimbitsa lamanzere, adzafooketsa dzanja lamanja; akalimbitsa dzanja lake lamanja, adzafooketsa lamanzere. Ngati atumiza zolimbikitsa kulikonse, adzakhala wofooka kulikonse.

18. Kufooka kwa manambala kumabwera chifukwa chokonzekera motsutsana ndi kuukira komwe kungatheke; mphamvu ya chiwerengero, kukakamiza mdani wathu kupanga zokonzekera izi motsutsana nafe.

19. Podziwa malo ndi nthawi ya nkhondo yomwe ikubwera, tikhoza kusumika maganizo kuchokera patali kwambiri kuti timenyane.

20. Koma ngati sizidziwika nthawi kapena malo, phiko lakumanzere lidzakhala lopanda mphamvu kuti lithandizire kudzanja lamanja, lamanja lopanda mphamvu mofananamo kuthandiza kumanzere, galimoto yosatha kupulumutsa lakumbuyo, kapena lakumbuyo kuchirikiza van. Ndiye kuli bwanji ngati zigawo zakutali za gulu lankhondo zili zosachepera 100 za LI, ndipo ngakhale zapafupi zikulekanitsidwa ndi LI angapo!

21. Ngakhale malinga ndi kulingalira kwanga asilikali a Yueh aposa chiwerengero chathu, zimenezo sizidzawapindulira kalikonse m’chipambano. Ndiye ndikunena kuti chigonjetso chikhoza kupezeka.

22. Ngakhale mdani achuluke mwaunyinji, Tingamletse kumenyana; Chitani chiwembu kuti mudziwe zolinga zake komanso mwayi wopambana.

23. Mudzutseni, ndipo phunzirani mfundo ya zochita zake kapena kusachitapo kanthu. Mkakamizeni kuti adziwulule yekha, kuti adziwe malo omwe ali pachiwopsezo.

24. Yerekezerani mosamala gulu lankhondo lotsutsana ndi lanu, kuti mudziwe pamene mphamvu ili yochuluka ndi yomwe ili yochepa.

25. Popanga machenjerero, liwu lapamwamba lomwe mungawapeze ndikubisa; bisani maganizo anu, ndipo mudzakhala otetezeka kwa azondi ochenjera kwambiri, ku machenjerero a anthu anzeru kwambiri.

26. Kupambana kungawapezere M'njira za adani;
27. Anthu onse amatha kuona njira zomwe ndimagonjetsera nazo, koma chimene palibe amene angachiwone ndi njira imene kupambana kumatulukamo.

28. Osabwereza machenjerero omwe adakupezerani chigonjetso chimodzi, koma lolani njira zanu ziziyendetsedwa ndi kusiyanasiyana kosiyanasiyana.

29. Machenjerero ankhondo ali ngati madzi; pakuti madzi m’njira yake yachibadwidwe achoka m’mwamba ndi kutsika mofulumira.

30. Choncho, pankhondo, njira ndiyo kupewa champhamvu ndi kumenya chofooka.

31. Madzi amakonza njira yake molingana ndi momwe nthaka ikuyenda pamwamba pake; msilikali amachitira chipambano chake poyerekezera ndi mdani amene akukumana naye.

32. Choncho, monga momwe madzi samakhalira nthawi zonse, momwemonso pankhondo mulibe mikhalidwe yokhazikika.

33. Iye amene angathe kusintha machenjerero ake pokhudzana ndi mdani wake ndipo potero apambana, akhoza kutchedwa kapitao wobadwa kumwamba.

34. Zinthu zisanu (madzi, moto, nkhuni, chitsulo, nthaka) sizikhala zazikulu nthawi zonse; nyengo zinayi zimapanga njira kwa wina ndi mzake motsatizana. Pali masiku aafupi ndi aatali; mwezi uli ndi nyengo zake zofota ndi kutha.

VII. Kuwongolera

1. Sun Tzu anati: Pankhondo, mkulu wa asilikali amalandira malamulo ake kuchokera kwa wolamulira.

2. Atasonkhanitsa gulu lankhondo ndikuunjikiza gulu lake lankhondo, ayenera kusakaniza ndi kugwirizanitsa zinthu zosiyanasiyana asanakhazikitse msasa wake.

3. Pambuyo pake, kumabwera kuwongolera mwanzeru, kuposa zomwe palibe chovuta. Kuvuta kwa kuwongolera mwanzeru kumaphatikizapo kusandutsa achinyengo kukhala achindunji, ndi tsoka kukhala phindu.

4. Choncho, kutenga njira yayitali komanso yozungulira, pambuyo ponyengerera mdani kuti achoke, ndipo ngakhale kuyambira pambuyo pake, kuganiza kuti akwaniritse cholinga chake, kumasonyeza chidziwitso cha luso la kupatuka.

5. Kuyenda ndi gulu lankhondo n'kopindulitsa; ndi unyinji wopanda mwambo, woopsa kwambiri.

6. Mukakhazikitsa gulu lankhondo lomwe lili ndi zida zokwanira mu Marichi kuti mutengepo mwayi, mwayi ndi wakuti mudzachedwa kwambiri. Kumbali ina, kuchotsa mzati wowuluka ndi cholinga kumaphatikizapo kupereka nsembe ya katundu wake ndi masitolo.

7. Chotero, ngati mulamula amuna anu kuti agubuduze malaya awo, ndi kuguba mokakamiza popanda kuyimitsa usana kapena usiku, kuphimba kaŵiri mtunda wanthawi zonse patali, kuchita ma LI zana kuti apeze phindu, atsogoleri a magulu anu onse atatu adzagwa m’manja mwa adani.

8 Amuna amphamvu adzakhala patsogolo, onjenjemera adzagwera m’mbuyo;

9. Ngati muguba ma LI makumi asanu kuti muthe kugonjetsa adani, mudzataya mtsogoleri wa gulu lanu loyamba, ndipo theka la mphamvu yanu ndi yomwe idzakwaniritse cholingacho.

10. Ngati muguba ma LI makumi atatu ndi chinthu chomwecho, magawo awiri pa atatu a ankhondo anu adzafika.

11. Ndithu, tingawachite kuti aonongeke gulu lankhondo Lopanda katundu wake; popanda chakudya chimatayika; popanda maziko operekera zimatayika.

12. Sitingathe kulowa mu mgwirizano mpaka titadziwa mapangidwe a anansi athu.

13. Sitiyenera kutsogolera gulu lankhondo poguba pokhapokha titadziwa bwino za dziko—mapiri ake ndi nkhalango zake, maenje ake ndi mapiri ake, madambo ake ndi madambo ake.

14. Sitingathe kugwiritsa ntchito maupangiri achilengedwe pokhapokha titagwiritsa ntchito maupangiri akumaloko.

15. Pankhondo, chita zachinyengo, ndipo mupambana.

16. Kaya muyang'ane kapena kugawa magulu ankhondo anu, ziyenera kusankhidwa malinga ndi zochitika.

17 Kufulumira kwanu kukhale kwa mphepo, ndi kulimba kwanu kukhale kwa nkhalango.

18. Kufunkha ndi kufunkha kuli ngati moto;

19. Zolinga zako zikhale zakuda ndi zosapenyeka ngati usiku;

20. Mukafunkha m'midzi, zofunkha zigawidwe mwa amuna anu; mukalanda gawo latsopano, liduleni m’magawo kuti asilikali apindule.

21. Lingalirani ndi dala Musanayende.

22. Adzamgonjetsa amene waphunzira luso lopatuka. Umu ndi luso loyendetsa.

23. Buku la Army Management limati: Pabwalo lankhondo, mawu olankhulidwa samanyamula mokwanira: motero kukhazikitsidwa kwa ng'oma ndi ng'oma. Komanso zinthu wamba sizingawoneke bwino mokwanira: chifukwa chake kukhazikitsidwa kwa mbendera ndi mbendera.

24. Ng'oma ndi ng'oma, mbendera ndi mbendera, ndi njira zomwe makutu ndi maso a wolandira alendo amatha kuyang'ana pa mfundo imodzi.
25. Wochereza motero kupanga gulu limodzi logwirizana, kodi nkosatheka kuti olimba mtima apite patsogolo yekha, kapena wamantha abwerere okha. Uwu ndi luso logwira amuna ambiri.

26. Choncho, pomenya nkhondo usiku, gwiritsani ntchito kwambiri zoyimbira ndi ng’oma, ndi kumenyana masana, mbendera ndi mbendera, monga njira yowakozera m’makutu ndi m’maso ankhondo anu.

27. Gulu lonse lankhondo lingalandidwe mzimu wake; kazembe wamkulu akhoza kulandidwa kukhalapo kwake m'maganizo.

28 Tsopano mzimu wa msilikali uli wokondwa m’mawa; masana wayamba kulira; ndipo madzulo maganizo ake akungobwerera kumsasa.

29. Choncho, kazembe wankhondo wanzeru amapewa gulu lankhondo pamene mzimu wake uli Waukali, koma amauukira pamene Ulesi ndi wofuna kubwerera. Uwu ndiye luso lophunzirira ma moods.

30. Kukhala wodziletsa ndi wodekha, kudikirira kuoneka kwa chipwirikiti ndi chipwirikiti pakati pa adani:--uku ndi luso lodzisunga.

31. Kukhala pafupi ndi cholinga mdani akadali kutali nacho, Kudikirira momasuka pamene mdani akugwira ntchito movutikira ndi kuvutikira, kudyetsedwa bwino pamene mdani ali ndi njala:- uku ndi luso la kuweta mphamvu.

32. Kupewa kulimbana ndi mdani amene mbendera zake zili bwino, kupewa kuukira gulu lankhondo lomwe lili m'gulu labata komanso lodzidalira:--------------------------------------------------------------------------- ndi ndi luso lowerenga zochitika.

33. Ndilingaliro lankhondo kuti usakwere phiri kukamenyana ndi mdani, kapena kumtsutsa akadzatsika.

34. Musatsate mdani amene akudziyerekezera ndi kuthawa; musamenyane ndi asilikali omwe ali ndi mkwiyo.

35. Musameze nyambo yoperekedwa ndi mdani. Musasokoneze gulu lankhondo limene likubwerera kwawo.

36. Pamene mwazungulira gulu lankhondo, siyani malo otulukira ali opanda; Osaumiriza kwambiri mdani wothedwa nzeru.

37. Umo ndimo luso lankhondo.

VIII. Kusiyana kwa Tactics

1. Sun Tzu anati: Pankhondo, mkulu wa asilikali amalandira malamulo ake kuchokera kwa mfumu, amasonkhanitsa gulu lake lankhondo ndikuika magulu ake ankhondo.

2. Mukakhala m'dziko lovuta, musamange misasa. M'dziko lomwe misewu yayikulu imadutsa, gwiranani manja ndi anzanu. Osakhalitsa m'malo odzipatula mowopsa. M'malo ovuta, muyenera kupita ku stratagem. Pamalo osimidwa, muyenera kumenya nkhondo.

3. Pali misewu imene sayenera kutsatiridwa, magulu ankhondo amene sayenera kuukiridwa, mizinda imene iyenera kuzingidwa, malo amene sayenera kuimbidwa mlandu, malamulo a mfumu amene sayenera kutsatiridwa.

4. Kazembeyo amene amamvetsa bwino ubwino umene umatsagana ndi machenjerero osiyanasiyana amadziŵa mmene angachitire ndi asilikali ake.

5. Mkulu amene samvetsa izi, akhoza kudziŵa bwino makonzedwe a dziko, komabe sangathe kutembenuza chidziwitso chake ku akaunti yothandiza.

6. Choncho, wophunzira wankhondo yemwe sadziwa luso lankhondo losintha zolinga zake, ngakhale akudziwa bwino za Ubwino Usanu, adzalephera kugwiritsa ntchito bwino amuna ake.

7. Chifukwa chake muzolingalira za mtsogoleri wanzeru, zolingalira za ubwino ndi kuipa zidzaphatikizidwa pamodzi.

8. Ngati chiyembekezo chathu chopindula chikhala chokhazikika mwanjira imeneyi, tingapambane kukwaniritsa mbali yofunika ya makonzedwe athu.

9. Ngati, kumbali ina, pakati pa zovuta ndife okonzeka nthaŵi zonse kupezerapo mwayi, tingadzitulutse ku tsoka.

10. Achepetseni mafumu odana ndi kuwaononga; ndi kuwavutitsa, ndi kuwasunga nthawi zonse; gwirani zokopa zapadera, ndikuwapangitsa iwo kuthamangira kumalo aliwonse.

11. Luso lankhondo limatiphunzitsa kuti tisadalire kuthekera kwa mdaniyo kuti asabwere, koma pakukonzekera kwathu kumulandira; osati chifukwa choti sanaukire, koma chifukwa chakuti tapanga udindo wathu kukhala wosatsutsika.

12. Pali zolakwa zisanu zowopsa zomwe zingakhudze wamba: (1) Kusasamala, komwe kumatsogolera ku chiwonongeko; (2) mantha, amene amatsogolera kugwira; (3) kupsa mtima mopupuluma, kumene kungapse mtima mwa kutukwana; (4) kukoma kwaulemu komwe kumakhudza manyazi; (5) kupemphela mopambanitsa amuna ake, zimene zimamuonetsa nkhawa ndi mavuto.

13. Awa ndi machimo asanu Osautsa a kazembe, oononga kunkhondo.

14. Gulu lankhondo likagwetsedwa ndi kuphedwa mtsogoleri wawo, ndiye kuti chifukwa chake chidzapezeka pakati pa zolakwa zisanu zowopsazi. Asiyeni akhale phunziro losinkhasinkha.

IX. The Army pa March

1. Sun Tzu adati: Tabwera tsopano ku funso lomanga misasa yankhondo, ndikuwona zizindikiro za adani. Yendani msanga mapiri, ndipo yendani pafupi ndi zigwa;
2. Misasa pamalo okwezeka, moyang'anizana ndi dzuwa. Osakwera pamwamba kuti mumenyane. Zochuluka za nkhondo zamapiri.

3. Mukawoloka mtsinje, muyenera kuutalikira.

4. Gulu lankhondo likawoloka mtsinje, musapite kukakumana nawo pakati pa mtsinjewo. Zingakhale bwino kulola theka la ankhondo awoloke, kenako ndikuwukirani.

5. Ngati mukufunitsitsa kumenya nkhondo, musapite kukakumana ndi woukirayo pafupi ndi mtsinje umene ayenera kuwoloka.

6. Konzani zida zanu pamwamba kuposa mdani, ndikuyang'ana dzuwa. Osasunthira kumtunda kukakumana ndi mdani. Zochuluka za nkhondo ya mtsinje.

7. Powoloka madambo amchere, nkhawa yanu yokhayo iyenera kukhala yowathetsa mwachangu, osazengereza.

8. Mukakakamizidwa kumenyana m’dambo la mchere, muyenera kukhala ndi madzi ndi udzu pafupi ndi inu, ndipo nsana wanu ukhale pamtengo. Zochuluka kwambiri pochita ma mayendedwe amchere.

9. M'dziko louma, lopanda malire, khalani pamalo osavuta kufikako ndi malo okwera kumanja kwanu ndi kumbuyo kwanu, kuti ngozi ikhale patsogolo, ndipo chitetezo chikhale kumbuyo. Zochuluka kwambiri pochita kampeni m'dziko lathyathyathya.

10. Izi ndi nthambi zinayi zothandiza za chidziwitso chankhondo zomwe zinapangitsa Mfumu Yachikawa kugonjetsa mafumu anayi angapo.

11. Ankhondo onse amakonda malo okwera kuposa malo otsika ndi adzuwa kuposa malo amdima.

12. “Mukasamala amuna anu, ndi kumanga msasa pamalo olimba, khamu la nkhondo lidzakhala lopanda nthenda zamtundu uliwonse;

13. Ukafika paphiri, kapena pagombe, khala mbali ya dzuwa, ndi malo otsetsereka Kumbuyo kwako chakumanja. Motero inu nthawi yomweyo kuchita phindu asilikali anu ndi ntchito zachilengedwe ubwino pansi.

14. Mtsinje umene mukufuna kuuwoloka ukapsa ndi kuchita thovu, dikirani mpaka uphwa.

15. Dziko lomwe muli matanthwe aatali okhala ndi mitsinje yoyenda pakati, maenje akuya achilengedwe, malo otsekeka, nkhalango zopiringizika, matanthwe ndi ming'oma, ziyenera kusiyidwa mwachangu komanso osayandikika.

16. Pamene tikukhala kutali ndi malo otere, tiyenera kuwafikitsa adaniwo; Pamene tikuyang'anizana nawo, tiwalole mdani akhale nawo kumbuyo kwake.

17 Ngati m’dera la msasa wanu muli mapiri, maiwe ozungulira udzu wa m’madzi, mabeseni odzaza mabango, kapena nkhalango za msipu, azithamangitsidwa mosamala ndi kufufuzidwa; pakuti awa ndi malo amene anthu obisalira kapena azondi ochenjera amabisalirako.

18. Pamene mdani ali pafupi ndikukhala chete, iye akudalira mphamvu yachibadwa ya malo ake.

19. Akakhala kutali ndi kuyesa kuyambitsa nkhondo, amafunitsitsa kuti mbali inayo ipite patsogolo.

20. Ngati malo ake amisasa ndi osavuta kufikako, ndiye kuti akutchera nyambo.

21. Kuyenda pakati pa mitengo ya m’nkhalango kumasonyeza kuti mdani akupita patsogolo. Kuwoneka kwa zowonetsera zingapo pakati pa udzu wokhuthala kumatanthauza kuti mdani akufuna kutipangitsa kuti tizikayikira.

22. Kumakwera kwa Mbalame m'kuthawa Kwawo ndi chizindikiro cha kubisalira. Zilombo zodzidzimutsa zimasonyeza kuti kuukira mwadzidzidzi kukubwera.

23 Pamene fumbi likukwera pamwamba, ndiye chizindikiro cha magaleta akubwera; fumbi likakhala lochepa, koma limafalikira pamalo ambiri, limawonetsa kuyandikira kwa ana oyenda pansi. Ikatuluka mbali zosiyanasiyana, zimasonyeza kuti maphwando atumizidwa kukatola nkhuni. Mitambo yochepa yafumbi imayenda uku ndi uku ikusonyeza kuti gulu lankhondo lamanga misasa.

24. Mawu akunyozeka ndi kuonjezera kukonzekera ndizizindikiro kuti mdani wayandikira. Mawu achiwawa ndi kuyendetsa patsogolo ngati kuti akuukira ndi zizindikiro zoti abwerera.

25. Pamene magaleta opepuka atuluka kaye ndi kuima pa mapiko ake, ndi chizindikiro chakuti adani akukonzekera nkhondo.

26. Malingaliro amtendere osatsagana ndi pangano lalumbiriro akuwonetsa chiwembu.

27. Pamene pali kuthamanga kochuluka ndipo asilikali akugwera pampando, zikutanthauza kuti nthawi yovuta yafika.

28. Pamene ena adzawaona akutsogola, ena akubwerera m’mbuyo, Ndi nyambo.

29. Pamene asilikali aima atatsamira mikondo yawo, Akomoka chifukwa chakusowa chakudya.

30. Ngati otumidwa kukatunga madzi akayamba kumwa iwo okha, gulu lankhondo likumva ludzu.

31. Ngati mdani aona ubwino wopezeka ndipo sachita khama kuti auteteze, asilikaliwo atopa.

32. Mbalame zikasonkhana pamalo aliwonse, ndiye kuti palibe chilichonse. Kulira usiku kumabweretsa mantha.
33. Ngati pali chipwirikiti mumsasa, ulamuliro wa mkulu wa asilikali ndi wofooka. Ngati zikwangwani ndi mbendera zasunthidwa, ndiye kuti kuukira kumachitika. Ngati akuluakuluwo akwiya, ndiye kuti amunawo atopa.

34. Gulu lankhondo likadyetsa akavalo ake ndi tirigu, ndi kupha ng’ombe zake kuti zidye, ndipo anthu akapanda kupachika miphika yawo pamoto wa msasa, kusonyeza kuti sadzabwerera ku mahema awo, udziwe kuti atsimikiza mtima kumenya nkhondo mpaka kufa.

35. Kuona amuna akunong’onezana m’tifundo ting’onoting’ono kapena kuyankhula motsitsa kumawonetsa kusagwirizana pakati pa maudindo.

36. Malipiro ochuluka amasonyeza kuti mdani ali kumapeto kwa chuma chake; zilango zambiri zimasonyeza mkhalidwe wachisoni.

37. Kuyamba ndi bluster, koma pambuyo pake kuchita mantha ndi manambala a adani, kumasonyeza kusowa kwakukulu kwa luntha.

38. Pamene nthumwi zitumidwa ndi kuyamika M'kamwa mwawo, ndi chisonyezo chakuti mdani akufuna Pangano.

39. Ngati magulu ankhondo a adani aguba mokwiya ndikukhalabe moyang'anizana ndi athu kwa nthawi yayitali popanda kulowa nawo kunkhondo kapena kunyamukanso, ndiye kuti mkhalidwewo ndi womwe umafuna kukhala tcheru ndi kusamala.

40. Ngati magulu athu ankhondo sachuluka (kuchuluka) Kuposa adaniwo, ndiye kuti Mwakwanira; zimangotanthauza kuti palibe kuukira mwachindunji. Zomwe tingachite ndikungoyang'ana mphamvu zathu zonse zomwe tili nazo, kuyang'anitsitsa mdani, ndikupeza zilimbikitso.

41. Ndithu, amene sachita chiwembu, koma Akuwapeputsa adani ake, Ndithu, agwidwa ndi iwo.

42. Ngati asilikali alangidwa asanakukondeni, Sangagonjere; ndipo, pokhapokha ngati agonjera, ndiye kuti adzakhala opanda ntchito. Ngati, pamene asilikali akhala ophatikana kwa inu, zilango sizikutsatiridwa, zidzakhalabe pokhapokha.

43 Cifukwa cace ankhondo ayenera kucitidwa mwacikondi ndi anthu, koma ogwidwa ndi citsulo; Iyi ndi njira ina yakupambana.

44. Ngati pophunzitsa asilikali malamulo amatsatiridwa, asilikaliwo amakhala odziletsa; ngati sichoncho, chilango chake chidzakhala choipa.

45. Ngati kazembeyo asonyeza chidaliro mwa anthu ake koma nthawi zonse amaumirira kuti malamulo ake atsatidwe, phindu lidzakhala lofanana.

X. Malo

1. Sun Tzu anati: Tikhoza kusiyanitsa mitundu isanu ndi umodzi ya mtunda, kunena kuti: (1) Malo ofikirika; (2) nthaka yoloŵerera; (3) malo ochepetsetsa; (4) njira zopapatiza; (5) utali wotalikirapo; (6) malo patali kwambiri ndi mdani.

2. Malo omwe amatha kuyenda momasuka ndi mbali zonse ziwiri amatchedwa ofikika.

3. Pankhani ya chikhalidwe ichi, khalani pamaso pa mdani potenga malo okwera ndi adzuwa, ndipo samalani zisankho zanu. Ndiye mudzatha kumenyana ndi mwayi.

4. Malo omwe angathe kusiyidwa koma ovuta kukhalamo amatchedwa entangling.

5. Kuchokera paudindo wotere, ngati mdaniyo sali wokonzeka, inu mukhoza kumuthamangitsa ndi kumugonjetsa. Koma ngati mdani wakonzekera kubwera kwanu, ndipo mukulephera kumugonjetsa, ndiye kuti, kubwerera kukhala kosatheka, tsoka lidzabwera.

6. Pamene malo ali kotero kuti mbali iliyonse idzapindula popanga kusuntha koyamba, imatchedwa temporizing ground.

7. Mmalo mwa mtundu uwu, ngakhale mdani angatipatse nyambo yokongola, kudzakhala koyenera kuti tisagwedezeke, koma m'malo mwake tibwerere, ndikunyengerera mdaniyo pa nthawi yake; ndiye, pamene mbali ya gulu lake lankhondo latuluka, tingathe kugonjetsa mwanzeru.

8. Pamadutsa opapatiza, ngati mungawagwire kaye, atsekereni ankhondo mwamphamvu ndipo adikire kubwera kwa adani.

9. Ngati gulu lankhondo litakulepheretsani kutenga malowo, musamuthamangire ngati malowo ali otetezedwa, koma ngati ali ndi mpanda wolimba kwambiri.

10. Pankhani ya mtunda wautali, ngati muli ndi mdani wanu kale, muyenera kukhala pamalo okwera ndi adzuwa, ndipo dikirani kuti abwere.

11. Ngati mdani adawasokoneza patsogolo Panu, musamtsate, koma thawani ndikumutsekereza.

12. Ngati muli patali ndi adani, ndipo mphamvu za magulu awiri ankhondo Zili zofanana, sikwapafupi kuyambitsa nkhondo, ndipo kumenya nkhondo kukuonongani.

13. Izi zisanu ndi chimodzi ndi mfundo zolumikizidwa ndi Dziko lapansi. Jenerali yemwe wapeza udindo wodalirika ayenera kusamala kuti awaphunzire.
14. Tsopano gulu lankhondo likukumana ndi masoka asanu ndi limodzi angapo, osabwera chifukwa cha zinthu zachilengedwe, koma chifukwa cha zolakwa zomwe mkulu wa asilikali ali ndi udindo. Izi ndi izi: (1) Kuthawa; (2) kusamvera; (3) kugwa; (4) kuwonongeka; (5) kusokonekera; (6) njira.

15. Mikhalidwe ina kukhala yofanana, ngati mphamvu imodzi iponyedwa pa ina kuwirikiza kakhumi kukula kwake, zotsatira zake zidzakhala kuthawa kwa yoyambayo.

16. Asilikali wamba akakhala amphamvu kwambiri ndipo maofesala awo ali ofooka, zotsatira zake zimakhala kusamvera. Pamene maofesala ali amphamvu kwambiri ndipo asilikali wamba ali ofooka kwambiri, zotsatira zake ndi kugwa.

17. Pamene akuluakulu akuluakulu ali okwiya ndi osamvera, ndipo pokumana ndi adani akumenyana pa zifukwa zawo zokha kuchokera ku malingaliro oipidwa, mkulu wa asilikali asanadziwe ngati ali m'malo omenyana kapena ayi, zotsatira zake zimakhala zowonongeka.

18. Pamene mkulu wa asilikali ali wofooka ndi wopanda ulamuliro; pamene malamulo ake sali omveka bwino ndi osiyana; pamene palibe ntchito yokonza yoperekedwa kwa maofesala ndi amuna, ndipo maudindo amapangidwa mosasamala, zotsatira zake zimakhala zosokoneza.

19. Pamene mkulu wa asilikali, wosakhoza kuyerekezera mphamvu ya mdaniyo, akalola gulu lankhondo lotsika kulimbana ndi gulu lalikulu, kapena kuponya gulu lofooka lolimbana ndi lamphamvu, ndi kunyalanyaza kuika asilikali osankhidwa kutsogolo, zotsatira zake ziyenera kukhala zosawerengeka.

20. Izi ndi njira zisanu ndi imodzi zogonjetsera chibwenzi, zomwe ziyenera kuzindikiridwa mosamala ndi wamkulu wankhondo yemwe wapeza udindo wodalirika.

21. Mapangidwe achilengedwe a dziko ndiye bwenzi labwino kwambiri la msirikali; koma mphamvu ya kulingalira mdani, ya kulamulira mphamvu za chipambano, ndi ya kuŵerengera mochenjera zovuta, zoopsa ndi kutalikirana, zimapanga chiyeso cha kazembe wamkulu.

22. Amene akudziwa zinthu izi, ndipo pomenya nkhondo, akugwiritsa ntchito nzeru zake, adzapambana pankhondo zake. Ndithu, amene sakuzidziwa, kapena kuzichita, Agonjetsedwa.

23. Ngati kumenyana kuli kopambana, muyenera kumenya nkhondo, ngakhale wolamulira akuletsa; ngati kumenyana sikungapambane, musamenyane, ngakhale pakufuna kwa wolamulira.

24. Mkulu wa asilikali amene akupita patsogolo popanda kusirira kutchuka, ndikubwerera kwawo popanda kuopa manyazi, amene maganizo ake ndi kuteteza dziko lake ndi kuchita utumiki wabwino kwa ufumu wake, ndiye mwala wa ufumu.

25. Achiteni asilikali anu ngati ana anu, ndipo Adzakutsatirani kuzigwa zakuya; muwawone ngati ana anu okondedwa, ndipo adzayimilira pafupi nanu kufikira imfa.

26 Koma ngati muli olekerera, koma osakhoza kuonetsa ulamuliro wanu; amtima wabwino, koma osatha kutsata malamulo anu; ndi wosakhoza, kuwonjezera apo, wa chisokonezo: ndiye asilikali anu ayenera kufanizidwa ndi ana owonongedwa; nzopanda ntchito pa cholinga chilichonse chothandiza.

27. Ngati tidziwa kuti amuna athu ali pachiwopsezo, koma osadziwa kuti adani sali otseguka, ndiye kuti tapita theka la chigonjetso.

28. Ngati tidziwa kuti adani ali okonzeka kuukira, koma osadziwa kuti amuna athu sali m'malo oti athe kuwukira, tapita theka la chigonjetso.

29. Ngati tidziwa kuti adani ali okonzeka kuukira, komanso tikudziwa kuti amuna athu ali pachiwopsezo, koma osadziwa kuti chikhalidwe cha pansi chimapangitsa kumenyana kukhala kosatheka, tangopitabe theka la chigonjetso.

30. Chifukwa chake msilikali wodziwa zambiri, akamayenda, sadabwitsidwa; akathyoka msasa, sasowa konse.

31. Choncho mawu akuti: "Ngati uwadziwa mdani, ndikudzidziwa wekha, Kupambana kwako sikukhala m'chikaiko; ngati mukudziwa Kumwamba ndi kudziwa dziko lapansi, mukhoza kukwaniritsa chigonjetso chanu.

XI. Nayine Situation

1. Sun Tzu anati: Luso lankhondo limazindikira mitundu isanu ndi inayi ya nthaka: (1) Malo obalalika; (2) malo osavuta; (3) mikangano; (4) malo otseguka; (5) malo odutsa misewu ikuluikulu; (6) maziko aakulu; (7) malo ovuta; (8) nthaka yotsekereza; (9) malo osimidwa.

2. Kalonga akamamenya nkhondo m'dera lake, ndi malo obalalika.

3. Akalowa m'dera la adani, koma osatalikirana, ndi malo osavuta.

4. Zomwe zili nazo zimadzetsa phindu lalikulu kumbali zonse, ndi chifukwa cha mikangano.

5. Malo omwe mbali iliyonse ili ndi ufulu woyendayenda ndi malo otseguka.

6. Bwalo lomwe limapanga chinsinsi cha zigawo zitatu zolumikizana, kotero kuti iye amene akukhalamo poyamba amakhala ndi Ufumu wambiri paulamuliro wake, ndi malo odutsa misewu yayikulu.
7. Gulu lankhondo likalowa mkati mwa dziko laudani, n’kusiya mizinda ingapo yokhala ndi mipanda yolimba kwambiri kumbuyo kwake, ndiye kuti n’kovuta kwambiri.

8. Nkhalango za m'mapiri, mapiri otsetsereka, madambo ndi zigwa - dziko lonse lovuta kudutsa: malowa ndi ovuta.

9. Pansi yomwe imadutsa m'mitsinje yopapatiza, ndipo kuchokera komwe tingangopuma mwa njira zowawa, kotero kuti chiwerengero chochepa cha adani chikwanira kuphwanya gulu lalikulu la amuna athu: izi zatsekeredwa pansi.

10. Malo omwe tingapulumutsidwepo ku chionongeko pomenya nkhondo mosazengereza, ndi malo othedwa nzeru.

11. Choncho, m'malo opezeka anthu ambiri, musamenyane; Pamalo osavuta, musayime. Pa mikangano, musamenye.

12. Pamalo otseguka, musayese kutsekereza njira ya mdani. Pansi pa misewu yayikulu, gwirizanitsani manja ndi anzanu.

13. Sonkhanitsani zofunkha mwamphamvu. M'malo ovuta, pitirizani kuyenda mosalekeza.

14. Pa nthaka yotsekeredwa, tsatirani njira zanzeru; Pamalo osimidwa, menyani.

15. Omwe adali kutchedwa atsogoleri aluso akale, Adadziwa kupha mphero pakati pa kutsogolo ndi kumbuyo kwa adani; kuletsa mgwirizano pakati pa magulu ake akuluakulu ndi ang'onoang'ono; kuti alepheretse ankhondo abwino kupulumutsa oipa, akapitawo kuti asasonkhanitse anthu awo.

16. Pamene adani adagwirizana, adakwanitsa kuwasokoneza.

17. Kukawapindulira, adapita patsogolo; pamene ayi, anaima.

18. Ndikafunsidwa momwe ndingalimbanirane ndi khamu lalikulu la adani mokonzekera bwino ndi kufika pokafika kunkhondo, ndinene: “Yamba ndi kulanda chinthu chimene mdani wako wachikonda;

19. Kuthamanga ndiye chiyambi chankhondo: gwiritsani ntchito kusakonzekera kwa mdani, pangani njira zanu ndi njira zosayembekezereka, ndikuwukirani malo osatetezedwa.

20. Zotsatirazi ndi mfundo zomwe ziyenera kutsatiridwa ndi gulu lankhondo: Pamene mupitirira kulowa m'dziko, m'pamenenso mgwirizano wa asilikali anu udzakhala waukulu, ndipo potero oteteza sadzakugonjetsani.

21 Fulumira m’dziko lachonde, kuti upatse ankhondo ako chakudya;

22. Phunzirani bwino za ubwino wa Amuna anu, ndipo musawakhometse; Limbikitsani mphamvu zanu ndikusunga mphamvu zanu. Sungani gulu lanu lankhondo mosalekeza, ndipo konzekerani mapulani osamvetsetseka.

23. Aponyeni Asilikali anu pamalo opanda pothawirapo, ndipo Akonda imfa kuposa kuthawa. Ngati adzayang'anizana ndi imfa, palibe chomwe sangakwaniritse. Akuluakulu ndi amuna onse adzapereka mphamvu zawo zonse.

24. Asilikali akakhala m'mavuto amasiya kuchita mantha. Ngati palibe pothaŵira, adzaima nji. Ngati ali m'dziko laudani, adzawonetsa kutsogolo kouma khosi. Ngati palibe chithandizo, adzamenyana kwambiri.

25. Choncho, popanda kudikira kuti atsogoleredwe, asilikali adzakhala ali pankhondo nthawi zonse; popanda kuyembekezera kufunsidwa, adzachita chifuniro chanu; popanda ziletso, adzakhala okhulupirika; popanda kulamula, akhoza kukhala odalirika.

26. Letsani kuombeza, ndipo thetsani chikaiko. Ndiye, mpaka imfa yeniyeniyo idzabwere, tsoka siliyenera kuopedwa.

27. Ngati asilikali athu sali olemedwa ndi ndalama, sichifukwa chakuti amanyansidwa ndi chuma; ngati moyo wawo suli wautali mopambanitsa, sikuli chifukwa chakuti sakonda kukhala ndi moyo wautali.

28. (Akumbutse) tsiku limene adzapite kunkhondo, asilikali ako adzalira, (Adzalira) amene akhala pansi akugonera zovala zawo, ndi amene akugona pansi misozi ikutsika m’masaya mwawo. Koma alole iwo abweretsedwe kamodzi, ndipo adzawonetsa kulimba mtima kwa Chu kapena Kuei.

29. Katswiri Wanzeru angafanane ndi Shuai-jan. Tsopano shuai-jan ndi njoka yomwe imapezeka m'mapiri a ChUng. Ikantheni pamutu pake, ndipo inu mudzakugwerani ndi mchira wake; ukamenye mchira wake, ndipo udzakugwera pamutu; ukamenye pakati pake, ndipo mutu ndi mchira zidzakugwerani.

30. Nditafunsidwa ngati gulu lankhondo lingapangidwe kutsanzira shuai-jan, ndiyankhe, Inde. Pakuti anthu a Wu ndi anthu a Yueh adani; komabe ngati akuwoloka mtsinje m’ngalawa imodzi n’kugwidwa ndi namondwe, adzathandizana wina ndi mnzake monga mmene dzanja lamanzere limathandizira kudzanja lamanja.

31 Chifukwa chake sikokwanira kudalira kumanga akavalo, ndi kuika mawilo a magaleta pansi.

32 Mfundo yoyendetsera gulu lankhondo ndiyo kukhazikitsa mulingo umodzi wa kulimba mtima womwe onse ayenera kufikira.
33. Momwe mungapangire zabwino zonse zamphamvu ndi zofowoka - limenelo ndi funso lokhudza kugwiritsa ntchito nthaka moyenera.

34. Potero kazembe waluso akutsogolera gulu lake lankhondo monga ngati akutsogolera ndi dzanja munthu m'modzi;

35. Ndi ntchito ya mkulu wa asilikali kukhala chete ndipo motero kuonetsetsa chinsinsi; olungama ndi olungama, motero kusunga bata.

36. Ayenera kuwazindikiritsa akapitawo ndi anthu ake ndi nkhani zabodza ndi maonekedwe ake, ndi kuwasunga muumbuli.

37. Posintha ndondomeko yake ndikusintha zolinga zake, Amamusunga mdani popanda kuzindikira Kwachindunji. Mwa kusamutsa msasa wake ndi kuyenda njira zozungulira, amalepheretsa mdani kuyembekezera cholinga chake.

38. Panthawi yovuta kwambiri, mtsogoleri wa gulu lankhondo amachita ngati munthu amene wakwera pamwamba ndi kuthamangira makwerero kumbuyo kwake. Iye amanyamula amuna ake kupita nawo kudera laudani asanasonyeze dzanja lake.

39. Awotcha ngalawa zake, Naswa mbiya zake; monga mbusa akuyendetsa gulu la nkhosa, iye amayendetsa anthu ake uku ndi uko, ndipo palibe chimene chikudziwa kumene iye akupita.

40. Kusonkhanitsa womulandirayo ndi kumuika pachiwopsezo: -- izi zitha kutchedwa ntchito ya wamkulu wankhondo.

41. Miyezo yosiyanasiyana yoyenerera mitundu isanu ndi inayi ya nthaka; kuthekera kwa njira zaukali kapena zodzitchinjiriza; ndi malamulo ofunikira a chikhalidwe cha anthu: izi ndi zinthu zomwe ziyenera kuphunziridwa.

42. Polowa m'dera laudani, mfundo yaikulu ndi yakuti, kulowa mkati kumabweretsa mgwirizano; kulowa koma njira yaifupi kutanthauza kubalalitsidwa.

43. Ukatuluka m'dziko lako ndi kupita ndi gulu lako lankhondo kudutsa m'madera oyandikana nawo, udzapeza kuti uli pa malo ovuta. Pakakhala njira zolankhulirana kumbali zonse zinayi, pansi pamakhala misewu ikuluikulu yodutsana.

44. Ukalowa m'dziko mozama, ndiye kuti ndi nthaka yaikulu. Mukalowa koma pang'ono, ndi malo osavuta.

45. Mukakhala ndi misasa ya adani Kumbuyo kwanu, ndipo kutsogolo kwanu kuli mipata yopapatiza, Ndi nthaka yotsekeredwa. Pamene kulibe malo othaŵirako nkomwe, ndi malo opanda chiyembekezo.

46. ​​Choncho, pa malo obalalika, ndikadawalimbikitsa amuna anga ndi cholinga chimodzi. Pamalo osavuta, ndimawona kuti pali kulumikizana kwapakati pakati pa magulu onse ankhondo anga.

47. Ndikadakhala ndikukangana Ndikadabwera pambuyo panga;

48. Ndikakhala pamalo oonekera, ndimayang'anitsitsa chitetezo changa. Pamalo a misewu ikuluikulu, ndimatha kugwirizanitsa mgwirizano wanga.

49. Pazifukwa zazikulu, ndimayesetsa kuonetsetsa kuti zinthu zikuyenda bwino. Pamalo ovuta, ndinkangokhalira kukankha mumsewu.

50. Pa nthaka yotsekeredwa, ndimatsekereza njira iliyonse yothawira. Nditathedwa nzeru, ndinkauza asilikali anga za kupanda chiyembekezo kuti angapulumutse miyoyo yawo.

51 Pakuti msilikali ali ndi mtima wofuna kukana mouma khosi atamuzungulira, kumenya mwamphamvu pamene sangathe kudzithandiza, ndi kumvera mwamsanga pamene wagwa pangozi.

52. Sitingathe kulowa mumgwirizano ndi akalonga oyandikana nawo mpaka titadziwa ziwembu zawo. Sitiyenera kutsogolera gulu lankhondo poguba pokhapokha titadziwa bwino dzikolo—mapiri ake ndi nkhalango zake, maenje ake ndi mapiri ake, madambo ake ndi madambo ake. Sitingathe kuwerengera zabwino zachilengedwe pokhapokha titagwiritsa ntchito malangizo apafupi.

53. Kunyalanyaza imodzi mwa mfundo zinayi kapena zisanu zotsatirazi sikuyenera kalonga wokonda nkhondo.

54. Pamene kalonga wokonda nkhondo akuukira boma lamphamvu, utsogoleri wake umadziwonetsa poletsa kukumana kwa magulu ankhondo a adani. Amawachititsa mantha kwambiri adani ake, ndipo ogwirizana nawo amaletsedwa kugwirizana naye.

55. Choncho, sadzitchinjiriza ndi aliyense, ndiponso salimbikitsa mphamvu za mayiko ena. Iye amachita zopanga zake zachinsinsi, akumachititsa adani ake kuchita mantha. Chotero akutha kulanda mizinda yawo ndi kugwetsa maufumu awo.

56. Kupereka mphotho mopanda kutsata lamulo, kupereka malamulo mosaganizira zomwe zidakonzedwa kale; ndipo mudzatha kuthana ndi khamu lonse lankhondo monga ngati muli ndi munthu mmodzi;

57. Uzani asilikali anu ndi ntchitoyo; musawadziwitse mapangidwe anu. Pamene kuwala kwawala, ubweretse pamaso pawo; koma musawauze chilichonse zinthu zikavuta.

58. Ikani gulu lanu lankhondo pachiwopsezo cha imfa, ndipo lipulumuka; Ukaugwetse m’masautso, ndipo udzatuluka mwamtendere.

59. Ndithu, mphamvu ikagwa m'njira yoopsa ndiyomwe imatha kumenya nkhondo kuti igonjetse.
60. Chipambano pankhondo chimapezedwa mwa kudzipereka tokha mosamala ku cholinga cha mdani.

61. Tikamakangamira pa mbali ya mdani, tidzapambana mu kupha mkulu wa asilikali.

62. Kumeneku kumatchedwa Kukhoza kuchita chinthu mwachinyengo.

63. (Akumbutse) tsiku loti Ulamulirize (kulamula), atsekereze malirewo, ndipo onongani Zolemba zawo, ndipo Imitsani kupita kwa atumiki onse.

64. Limbani mtima m'khoti kuti muthe kuwongolera zinthu.

65. Ngati mdani asiya chitseko chili chotseguka, Muthamangire kulowamo.

66. Mtengereni mdani wanuyo pomlanda zimene wakonda, ndipo konzekerani mochenjera kuti Adzafike pansi.

67. Yendani m’njira yodziwika ndi lamulo, ndipo pitirizani kwa adani anu kufikira mutamenya nkhondo yotsimikizika.

68. Poyamba, sonyeza kunyozeka kwa namwali, Kufikira mdani akutsegulirani; kenako tengerani kufulumira kwa kalulu wothamanga, ndipo kudzakhala mochedwa kuti mdani akutsutsani.

XII. Kuukira kwa Moto

1. Sun Tzu adati: Pali njira zisanu zowukira ndi moto. Choyamba ndi kutentha asilikali mumsasa wawo; chachiwiri ndi kutentha masitolo; chachitatu ndikuwotcha masitima onyamula katundu; chachinayi ndi kuwotcha zida ndi magazini; chachisanu ndi kuponya moto pakati pa adani.

2. Kuti tichite chiwembu, tiyenera kukhala ndi njira. Zida zoyatsira moto ziyenera kukhala zokonzeka nthawi zonse.

3. Pali nyengo yoyenera kuukira ndi moto, ndi masiku apadera oyambitsira moto.

4. Nyengo yoyenera ndi pamene kuli kouma; masiku apadera ndi aja pamene mwezi uli m’magulu a nyenyezi a Sieve, Khoma, Mapiko kapena Cross-bar; pakuti anayi ndiwo masiku onse a mphepo yokwera.

5. Poukira ndi moto, munthu ayenera kukhala wokonzeka kukumana ndi zochitika zisanu:

6. (1) Pamene moto wabuka mkati mwa msasa wa adani, yankhani nthawi yomweyo ndi kuwukira kuchokera kunja.

7. (2) Ngati pabuka moto, koma asilikali a mdani amakhala chete, bide nthawi yanu ndipo musaukire.

8. (3) Mphamvu ya malawi ikafika pamtunda, itsatireni ndi kuwukira ngati nkotheka; ngati sichoncho, khalani komwe muli.

9. (4) Ngati kuli kotheka kuukira ndi moto kuchokera kunja, musadikire mpaka kuphulika mkati, koma perekani kuukira kwanu panthawi yabwino.

10. (5) Ukayatsa moto, ukhale wouzungulira. Osaukira kuchokera ku leeward.

11. Mphepo imene imatuluka masana imatenga nthawi yaitali, koma mphepo yausiku imayamba kugwa.

12. M’gulu lankhondo lirilonse, zochitika zisanu zogwirizanitsidwa ndi moto ziyenera kudziŵika, mayendedwe a nyenyezi aŵerengedwe, ndi ulonda wosungidwira masiku oyenerera.

13. Choncho, amene agwiritsa ntchito moto pomenyana nawo ali ndi nzeru; amene amagwiritsa ntchito madzi ngati thandizo poukira amapeza mphamvu.

14. Ndi madzi, mdani akhoza kulandidwa, koma osalandidwa zinthu zake zonse.

15. Tsoka ilo limakhala losasangalatsa la munthu amene amayesa kupambana pankhondo zake ndi kupambana m'maukali ake popanda kukulitsa mzimu wamalonda; chifukwa zotsatira zake ndikutaya nthawi ndi kuyimirira kwathunthu.

16. Chifukwa chake mawu akuti: Wolamulira wowunikiridwa akonzeratu zolingalira zake; mkulu wabwino amakulitsa chuma chake.

17. Musasunthe pokhapokha mutawona ubwino; musagwiritse ntchito magulu anu ankhondo, pokhapokha mutapindula; musamenye pokhapokha ngati udindowo ndi wovuta.

18 Wolamulira asaike ankhondo kumunda kuti angokhutiritsa ndulu yake; palibe mkulu wa asilikali amene ayenera kumenya nkhondo mongofuna kuti achite.

19. Ngati kuli kwa inu Pitirizani; ngati sichoncho, khalani komwe muli.

20 Mkwiyo ukhoza kusintha m’kupita kwa nthawi, ukhale chisangalalo; kukhumudwa kungapambane ndi zomwe zili.

21. Koma ufumu umene udaonongeka kale sungathe kukhalaponso; ndiponso akufa sangaukitsidwe.

22. Choncho, Woyang'anira wounikiridwa amakhala wolabadira, ndipo Waukulu wabwino amakhala wosamala. Iyi ndi njira yosungitsira dziko pamtendere ndi gulu lankhondo.

XIII. Kugwiritsa Ntchito Azondi

1. Sun Tzu anati: Kulera khamu la amuna zikwi zana limodzi ndi kuwaguba mitunda ikuluikulu kumadzetsa kutaya kwakukulu kwa anthu ndi kuwononga chuma cha Boma. Mtengo wa tsiku ndi tsiku ukhale masekeli asiliva chikwi chimodzi. Kunyumba ndi kunja kudzakhala chipwirikiti, ndipo amuna adzagwa ali otopa m’misewu. Mabanja okwana 700,000 adzalephereka kugwira ntchito yawo.
2. Magulu ankhondo a adani akhoza kuyang'anizana kwa zaka zambiri, kulimbikira kupambana komwe kumasankhidwa mu tsiku limodzi. Izi zili choncho, kukhalabe wosazindikira mkhalidwe wa mdani chabe chifukwa chakuti munthu amakwiyira kutulutsa ma ounces zana a siliva muulemu ndi malipiro, ndiko kutalika kwa nkhanza.

3. Munthu amene amachita motero sali mtsogoleri wa anthu, palibe thandizo lomwe alipo kwa mfumu yake, palibe mbuye wachipambano.

4. Chotero, chimene chimatheketsa wolamulira wanzeru ndi kazembe wabwino kumenya ndi kugonjetsa, ndi kukwaniritsa zinthu zosafikiridwa ndi anthu wamba, ndiko kudziwiratu.

5. Tsopano kudziwiratu uku sikungakhoze kuchotsedwa kwa mizimu; sichingapezeke mwachidziwitso kuchokera muzochitikira, kapena mwa kuwerengera kulikonse.

6. Kudziwa makhalidwe a mdani kungapezeke kwa anthu ena okha.

7. Chifukwa chake kugwiritsiridwa ntchito kwa akazitape, amene ali magulu asanu: (1) Azondi akumaloko; (2) akazitape amkati; (3) azondi otembenuzidwa; (4) azondi amene adzawonongedwe; (5) akazitape otsala.

8. Pamene mitundu isanu iyi ya akazitape onse ali pa ntchito, palibe angazindikire dongosolo chinsinsi. Izi zimatchedwa "kuwongolera kwaumulungu kwa ulusi." Ndilo luso lapamwamba kwambiri la eni ake.

9. Kukhala ndi akazitape akumaloko kumatanthauza kugwiritsa ntchito ntchito za anthu okhala m'boma.

10. Kukhala ndi akazitape amkati, kugwiritsa ntchito akuluakulu a adani.

11. Kukhala ndi akazitape otembenuka, kugwira azondi a adani ndi kuwagwiritsa ntchito pazolinga zathu.

12. Kukhala ndi akazitape oonongedwa, kuchita zinthu zina moonekera ndi zolinga zachinyengo, ndi kulola azondi athu kuti adziwe za izo ndi kuwawuza adaniwo.

13. Ndithu, akazitape omwe adapulumuka ndi amene akubweza nkhani kuchokera Kumsasa wa adani.

14. Choncho, palibe m'gulu lankhondo aliyense amene ali ndi ubale wapamtima kuposa akazitape. Palibe amene ayenera kulipidwa mowolowa manja. Palibe bizinesi ina yomwe iyenera kusungidwa chinsinsi chachikulu.

15. Azondi sangagwiritsidwe ntchito mothandiza popanda nzeru zinazake zanzeru.

16. Sangathe kuyendetsedwa bwino popanda ubwino ndi kuwongoka.

17. Popanda nzeru zobisika zamaganizo, munthu sangatsimikizire zoona za malipoti awo.

18. Khala wochenjera! kukhala wochenjera! ndipo gwiritsani ntchito azondi anu pamalonda amtundu uliwonse.

19. Ngati nkhani yachinsinsi yaululidwa ndi kazitape nthawi isanakwane, aphedwe pamodzi ndi munthu amene adauzidwa chinsinsicho.

20. Kaya chinthucho chikhale chophwanya gulu lankhondo, kuwononga mzinda, kapena kupha munthu, nthawi zonse nkofunika kuyamba ndi kupeza mayina a atumiki, othandizira-de-camp, alonda a pakhomo ndi alonda a mkulu wa asilikali. Azondi athu ayenera kutumidwa kuti atsimikizire izi.

21. Azondi a adani amene anabwera kudzazonda ife ayenera kufufuzidwa, kuyesedwa ndi ziphuphu, kutsogozedwa ndi kukhala ndi nyumba zabwino. Motero adzakhala azondi otembenuzidwa ndi kupezeka pa ntchito yathu.

22. Ndi kudzera mu chidziwitso chomwe chinabweretsedwa ndi kazitape wotembenuka kuti timatha kupeza ndikugwiritsa ntchito azondi am'deralo ndi amkati.

23. Ndi chifukwa cha chidziwitso chake, kachiwiri, kuti tikhoza kuchititsa kazitape woonongeka kufikitsa nkhani zabodza kwa adani.

24. Pomaliza, ndikudziwa kwake kuti kazitape wotsalayo atha kugwiritsidwa ntchito pa nthawi zoikika.

25. Mapeto ndi cholinga chochita ukazitape m'mitundu yake yonse isanu ndikumudziwa mdani; ndipo chidziwitsochi chikhoza kungotengedwa, poyamba, kuchokera kwa kazitape wotembenuka. Chifukwa chake ndikofunikira kuti kazitape wotembenukayo asamalidwe mwaufulu kwambiri.

26. Kale, kuwuka kwa ufumu wa Yin kunali chifukwa cha I Chih yemwe adatumikira pansi pa Hsia. Momwemonso, kuwuka kwa mafumu a Chou kudachitika chifukwa cha Lu Ya yemwe adatumikira pansi pa Yin.

27. Chifukwa chake ndi wolamulira wowunikiridwa ndi kazembe wankhondo wanzeru yekha amene adzagwiritse ntchito luntha lapamwamba la ankhondo pazifuno zaukazitape ndipo potero amapeza zotulukapo zazikulu. Azondi ndi chinthu chofunika kwambiri m'madzi, chifukwa pa iwo zimadalira mphamvu ya asilikali kuyenda.
