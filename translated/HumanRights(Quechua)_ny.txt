Pa December 10, 1948, bungwe la United Nations 
adasonkhana pamodzi, navomereza, nadziwitsa anthu 
ufulu, zomwe ndi zonse zomwe zanenedwa mu kabukuka.  
Msonkhanowo unakambirana za ufulu wa anthu onse 
kuzindikiritsidwa, kuphunzitsidwa, ndi kuwerenga m'maiko onse, 
ziyenera kudziwitsidwa m'masukulu amitundu yonse 
ndi kusiyana pakati pa maiko awa.  
 
KUDZIWA KWA UNITED NATIONS  
UFULU WA ANTHU ONSE 
Chiyambi cha  
ZINDIKIRANI: Ufulu ndi mtendere padziko lapansi 
moyo umakhazikika mu izi: Aliyense 
ndi bwino kuti asataye popeza mayi athu anatibala 
tiyenera kuchitiridwa zinthu mofanana.  
ONANI: Anthu achita zoipa zamitundumitundu, 
ngakhale sadziwa mokwanira za ufulu wa anthu 
kunyalanyaza iwo, ife tonse tikufuna tsogolo 
masiku abwino; m’mantha ndi m’masautso onse 
pamene umphawi udzatha, aliyense adzakhala ndi ufulu wolankhula 
zomwe zili m'mitu yathu, ndipo tidzalandira molimba mtima chidziwitso chimenecho 
ndi zomwe tikufuna.  
ZINDIKIRANI: Payenera kukhala Woteteza Ufulu Wachibadwidwe 
malamulo opanda tsankho 
thandizani oponderezedwa ndi oponderezedwa amene akufuna kulungamitsa ufulu wawo.
ZINDIKIRANI: Ndi kuti mafuko adziko lapansi apange maubwenzi 
tiyenera kukhumbira mu mitima yathu ndi chikhumbo chimenecho 
tiyenera kubalalika.  
ZINDIKIRANI: Mayiko amene ali m’bungwe la United Nations akutchulidwa m’bukuli 
adatsimikizira chikhulupiriro chawo pa ufulu wa Iliw ndi 
adavomereza kuti anthu ndi ofanana posatengera jenda 
zindikirani kuti ali ndi ufulu womwewo. Zinthu zonsezi bwino 
podziwa kuti chilimbikitso cha ufulu wathu ndi chokongola kwambiri 
tigwire ntchito kuti dziko lathu likhale labwino.  
ZINDIKIRANI: Mayiko amene ali m’bungwe la United Nations agwirizana 
ufulu wa anthu ndi kumasuka zili ponseponse 
kulandiridwa ndi  
ZINDIKIRANI NYA: Ufulu wa anthu ndi kumasuka zimazindikirika bwino 
ngati izo ziri, zidzakhala zoona.  
UFULU WA ANTHU WODZINDIKIRA PAMSONKHANO WAWULIRIDWE 
KUTI ZINALI  
Mitundu imene inadza ku pangano lalikulu ili m’buku ili 
kuzindikira ufulu wa anthu onse, kuti anthu 
kulikonse komwe amakhala, amatha kuwona qeliqa iyi mwaulemu komanso mwaufulu 
kuphunzira kukhala ndi moyo; kwa ena momwe angathere 
adzadziwitsa kuti zinthu zonsezi nzoona; izi zidzachitika 
kukhala m'mayiko a mgwirizano kapena izi 
pobisalira amitundu.  
CHOYAMBA (1) 
Anthu onse amabadwa mwaufulu ndipo ali ndi ufulu wonse 
ndife ofanana. Popeza ndife anzeru, tili ngati banja lamtendere 
tili ndi ngongole ya moyo kwa aliyense.  
CHACHIWIRI (2)
Mfundo zaufulu zomwe zili m'chikalatachi ndi zathu tonse, 
zilibe kanthu kuti munthu ndi mtundu wanji kapena jenda 
komanso zilibe kanthu kuti amalankhula bwanji kapena amakhulupirira mulungu wotani 
kapena kuchokera ku boma lomwe ankaganiza kuti lidzakhala 
ngakhale ali wosauka, kapena wolemera, kapena ali yense.  
Mayiko onse, aulere kapena ayi, ndi osiyanasiyana 
tili ndi boma, ndipo ena amadalira mayiko ena, 
koma mulimonsemo sitiyenera kuchitiridwa mosiyana.  
GAWO LACHITATU (3) 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi moyo komanso ufulu 
ndi kutetezedwa.  
CHACHINAYI (4) 
Palibe amene angakhale kapolo wolandidwa, ndikoletsedwa m'njira zonse 
ukapolo ndi kuzembetsa anthu.  
CHACHISANU (5) 
Palibe amene angakumane ndi masautso amtundu uliwonse 
sitingalangidwe chifukwa chonyozetsa umunthu wathu.  
CHACHISANU NDI CHIMODZI (6) 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wozengedwa mlandu kulikonse komwe ali 
kuti zitsimikizidwe.  
CHACHISANU NDI CHIWIRI (7) 
Anthu onse ali ndi ufulu wofanana pansi pa lamulo 
kukhala, lamulo liyenera kutiteteza mosakondera. 
Tilinso ndi ufulu wotitsutsa chifukwa chonyalanyaza ziphunzitsozi 
 

 
mwa iwo amene akufuna kuyimirira. Ifenso tili ndi ufulu wolandira ziphunzitso zimenezi 
ndi kutetezedwa ndi lamulo pakachitika cholakwika mwadzidzidzi.  
CHACHINAYI (8)
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu womvetsera mwachilungamo 
chitetezo ku ufulu wathu walamulo 
tiyenera kutetezedwa kwa iwo amene amatero.  
MUTU WACHISANU NDI CHINANU (9) 
Palibe amene amamangidwa popanda kudziwa cholakwa chake. 
kumangidwa kapena kuthamangitsidwa.  
CHAKHUMI (10) 
Anthu onse ndi ofanana ndi chilungamo chosakondera 
tili ndi ufulu woti timve ndi kuweruzidwa 
udindo wathu kapena ufulu wodziwa. Zomwe akuimbidwa mlandu 
ziyenera kusonyezedwa bwino kwa ife kuti tiphunzire ngakhale titakhala kutali.  
Khumi ndi chimodzi (11) 
1. Anthu osalakwa ndi osalakwa pamlandu uliwonse womwe angapatsidwe 
tili ndi ufulu wodzinenera tokha, monga mwa lamulo 
mpaka tidzapezeka olakwa pamlandu. Palinso mlandu 
ufulu wathu ndi wotsimikizika komanso wotetezedwa 
kukhala omwe akuyenera kukhala.  
2. Kusapereka chilango kwa munthu pazimene wachita 
chilango ngati anachita m'masiku asanakhale lamulo 
akatuluka ndi kutsimikizira kuti ndi mlandu. Chilango chidzakhala 
kokha ndi zilango za chilamulo m’masiku a kuchimwa kwake.  
 

 
Khumi ndi ziwiri (12) 
Palibe amene angasokoneze moyo wathu popanda chifukwa 
ndipo wa mabanja athu, sakhoza kulowa pachabe 
ku nyumba zathu, ndipo makalata athu asayesedwe pachabe. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutetezedwa ndi lamulo kuti asasokonezedwe ndi izi.  
Khumi ndi zitatu (13)
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu woyenda, 
tilinso ndi ufulu wokhala ndi nyumba m'dziko lathu.  
2. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala dziko kapena fuko 
kusiya dziko lililonse ndi chimodzimodzi 
kubwerera kudziko lathu.  
Khumi ndi zinayi (14) 
1. Ngati munthu akuzunzidwa monga momwe boma la dziko lake silichitira 
chifukwa amaganiza, ndiye kuti ali woyenera kusamuka 
kuthawa ndi kubisala.  
2. Kuimbidwa mlandu wopalamula m'dziko lake 
munthu sangadalire ufulu wa anthu kupatulapo 
kuthawira ku dziko ndi kukhala komweko mwa ufulu.  
Khumi ndi zisanu (15) 
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi dziko 
kukhala athu.  
2. Ufuluwu sungathe kulandidwa ufulu wake mwachisawawa. 
Tilinso ndi ufulu wokana kusintha mtundu wathu 
wina kuti atiletse.  
Khumi ndi zisanu ndi chimodzi (16) 
 

 
1. Mwamuna ndi mkazi akafika msinkhu wokwatira amakhala ali kale 
ufulu wathu wokwatira ndi kukhala ndi banja, ayi 
zilibe kanthu kuti ndife fuko lotani kapena fuko lotani, kaya ndife amuna kapena akazi 
mkazi ali ndi ufulu wofanana muukwati wathu zabwino 
kukhala ndi moyo ndi kugawana.  
2. Mwamuna ndi mkazi sangakwatire popanda chilolezo chawo.  
3. Ubale ndi ufulu wa banja lonse ku chitetezo cha boma 
tili ndi ufulu, chifukwa ndife banja lalikulu lomwe limatukula fuko 
ndife amene.
Khumi ndi zisanu ndi ziwiri (17) 
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kukhala yekha kapena awiriawiri 
kukhala ndi katundu ndi anthu.  
2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe chuma chake mwachisawawa.  
Khumi ndi zisanu ndi zitatu (18) 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu woganiza, 
chikumbumtima ndi mwa mulungu wanji 
ndi kutipangitsa ife kukhulupirira. Monga mwa ufulu umenewu, tili mfulu 
kuvomereza chipembedzo ndi kukhulupirira mtundu uliwonse 
kugwira ntchito. Titha kuchita panokha kapena gulu 
chikhulupiriro chimenecho ndipo chingaphunzitsidwe m’nyumba kapena 
kulikonse.  
Khumi ndi zisanu ndi zinayi (19) 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kufotokoza maganizo ake 
tili ndi ufulu ku zinthu izi popanda choletsa chilichonse 
tingathenso kugawana maganizo athu ndi kuvomereza maganizo a ena.  
Makumi awiri (20) 
 

 
1. Tili ndi ufulu wosonkhana ndi kusonkhana 
ndi kumanga mtendere.  
2. Palibe amene adzakakamizidwe kupita ku msonkhano uliwonse 
kukhala mwaufulu.  
Makumi awiri ndi chimodzi (21) 
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu ku boma la dziko lake 
kudzitumikira tokha, kaya panokha kapena 
ngakhale titasankha zomwe tikufuna.  
2. Aliyense ali ndi danga lofanana ndi nchito za boma m'dziko lake 
kulowa m’nyumba ndi kulandira chisamaliro.  
3. Mphamvu ya boma ndi yakuti anthu onse akufuna
tikangopereka ulamuliro, timavomereza kuti tikufuna 
povota mwaufulu; sitiyenera kupanga voti imeneyo osati wina aliyense 
amadziwa kuti titha kuvotera zomwe tikufuna.  
Makumi awiri ndi awiri (22) 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wamaphunziro, katundu ndi 
kutetezedwa m’njira zathu zonse kuti zonse zikhale zabwino 
kuti tifike kuno. Timapeza chitsimikizochi mwa: 
Boma liyenera kuchita zinthu mwadongosolo komanso kutichitira zomwe lingathe 
ndi anthu a dziko lathu, ayenera kuchita kuti tipindule molingana ndi mphamvu zake kapena 
ndi thandizo lakunja.  
Makumi awiri ndi atatu (23) 
1. Anthu abodza ali ndi ufulu wachipembedzo, 
tili ndi ufulu wogwira ntchito ngakhale titakhala opanda ntchito 
kufunafuna ndi kupeza, ngati palibe munthu afuna 
tikhoza kuchotsedwa ntchito.  
 

 
2. LIEW anthu ali ndi ufulu wolankhula 
kulipidwa molingana ndi ntchito yathu.  
3. Aliyense ali ndi danga loyenda ndikukhala mwaufulu mkati mwa malire a dziko lililonse 
kuti tilandire mphotho yathu monga mwa ntchito zabwino 
ubwino wa banja lonse; Apo ayi ayenera kulandira chithandizo 
za othandizira omwe adakwezedwa kuti achite izi.  
4. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu woyanjana ndi ena 
ufulu wathu kupanga kapena kukhala m'magulu otere 
kuti tichiku.  
Makumi awiri ndi anayi (24) 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wopuma ndi kupumula
tili ndi ufulu wakumwetulira, ndipo tili ndi ufulu wopitilira momwe tingathere 
kugwira ntchito masana ndi kupita kutchuthi. 
Makumi awiri ndi zisanu (25) 
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo wokwanila wa iye ndi banja lake 
wodyetsedwa, wovala bwino komanso wathanzi. Iye ali nawo ufulu, nayenso 
tili ndi ufulu wotetezedwa, ngakhale kuti ndife olumala, ndife oferedwa, kapena ndife oferedwa 
kaya ndinu wamkulu kapena wamng'ono; tilinso ndi ufulu 
ngakhale titalephera kupeza ntchito kapena kukhala ndi zinthu zoti tingazipeze mwadzidzidzi 
kutetezedwa pakatayika.  
2. Amayi oyembekezera ndi ana ali ndi ufulu 
ziyenera kusamalidwa bwino. Wokwatiwa kapena ayi 
ana onse ali ndi ufulu wofanana posatengera kuti anabadwira m’banja 
kutetezedwa.  
makumi awiri mphambu zisanu ndi chimodzi (26) 
 

 
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wophunzira. Ayi ndithu 
sukulu ya pulayimale ndi yaulere, choncho ndizokakamizidwa kwa aliyense amene ali ndi sukulu ya pulaimale 
kuti athe. Malinga ndi ndemanga yathu yabwino, ndi zambiri 
tikhoza kudziphunzitsa tokha kuti tikwaniritse zolinga zathu.  
2. Zotsatira za maphunziro zidzakhala ubwino wa anthu ndi onse 
ufulu wake umadziwika bwino kuti ufulu wake ulemekezedwe 
kukhalapo. Tiyenera kukhala odekha ndi kumvetsetsana 
ndi mitundu, mafuko ena, ndi mwa mulungu aliyense 
ndi okhulupirira. Tiyeneranso kuthandizira bungwe la United Nations 
mtendere padziko lapansi.  
3. Makolo ali ndi ufulu wolandira maphunziro
ndi kulandira banja lathu.  
makumi awiri mphambu zisanu ndi ziwiri (27) 
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga loyenda ndikukhala mwaufulu mkati mwa malire a dziko lililonse 
kuti atithandize pa vuto lililonse. Tikhoza kuthandiza m’njira iliyonse 
dziko lathu kuti likhale lokongola ndipo tili ndi ufulu 
kuvomereza zomwe mzinda uli nazo.  
2. Ngati tachotsa m’maganizo chinthu chamtengo wapatali kapena 
titha kuchita zomwe palibe wina aliyense adachitapo kale 
ndife oyenerera ku mtengo wa zolengedwa zimenezo 
kuteteza zomwe zilipo.  
makumi awiri mphambu zisanu ndi zitatu (28) 
Tili ndi ufulu m'dziko lathu komanso m'maiko onse 
kukhala bungwe la ufulu wa anthu, kotero kuti zonsezi 
kuti tikwaniritse ufulu wathu ndi kumasuka kwathu.  
makumi awiri mphambu zisanu ndi zinayi (29) 
 

 
1. Aliyense ndi mfulu m'dziko lathu, choncho m'dziko lathu 
tili ndi ntchito.  
2. Njira yokhayo yolimbikitsira ufulu kapena ufulu wathu ndi lamulo 
zingatilepheretse, monga ufulu wa ena kapena ufulu 
kukhalapo kwawo sikutengedwa, kotero kuti mikangano isatero 
kukhalapo. Kotero kuti aliyense ali m'dziko lake 
kukhala mwamtendere.  
3. Ufulu ndi kumasuka izi sizingagwiritsidwe ntchito 
akhoza kukhala, ngati mumtundu uliwonse wa mayiko ogwirizana 
ngati kuli kotsutsana ndi chifuniro chake.  
Makumi atatu (30) 
Palibe kapena boma lomwe lingadalire mawu awa
tingachite chilichonse, ufulu wathu ndi kumasuka 
kulanda dziko.
