Anali nkhalamba yopha nsomba yekhayekha mu Gulf Stream ndipo anali atadutsa masiku makumi asanu ndi atatu mphambu anayi osatenga nsomba. M'masiku makumi anayi oyamba anali ndi mnyamata. Koma patatha masiku makumi anayi opanda nsomba makolo a mnyamatayo adamuuza kuti nkhalambayo tsopano yakhaladi ndipo potsiriza salao, yomwe ili yoyipa kwambiri, ndipo mnyamatayo adapita monga momwe adalamulira m'bwato lina lomwe linagwira nsomba zitatu zabwino sabata yoyamba. Zinamumvetsa chisoni mnyamatayo kuona mkuluyo akubwera tsiku lililonse ndi chotengera chake chopanda kanthu ndipo nthawi zonse ankatsika kuti akamuthandize kunyamula mizera yopiringizika kapena nsonga ndi ndodo yomwe inali itazunguliridwa pamtengo. Matanga aja anali ndi zigamba ndi matumba a ufa ndipo ataunjidwa, ankaoneka ngati mbendera ya kugonja kosatha.
