Yari umusaza warobye wenyine muri skiff mu mugezi wa Kigobe kandi yari amaze iminsi mirongo inani n'ine ubu adafashe ifi. Mu minsi mirongo ine yambere umuhungu yari kumwe nawe. Ariko nyuma yiminsi mirongo ine idafite ifi ababyeyi bumuhungu bari bamubwiye ko umusaza rwose byanze bikunze kandi amaherezo salao, ubwo ni bwo buryo bubi bwo kutagira amahirwe, kandi umuhungu yari yabitegetse mu bundi bwato bwafashe amafi atatu meza mu cyumweru cya mbere. Byarababaje umuhungu kubona umusaza yinjira buri munsi afite skif ye irimo ubusa kandi buri gihe yamanukaga kumufasha gutwara imirongo yashizwemo cyangwa gaff na harponi hamwe nubwato bwazengurukaga mast. Ubwato bwarimo imifuka y'ifu kandi, burazunguruka, busa n'ibendera ryo gutsindwa burundu.
