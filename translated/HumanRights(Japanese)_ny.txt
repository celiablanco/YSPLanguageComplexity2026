"Universal Declaration of Human Rights"
  
(Yotengedwa ndi 3rd United Nations General Assembly pa December 10, 1948)
  
<Chiyambi>
  
Kuzindikira ulemu wobadwa nawo ndi ufulu wofanana ndi wosachotsedwa wa mamembala onse amtundu wa anthu;
kuzindikira ndiye maziko a ufulu, chilungamo ndi mtendere padziko lapansi,
 
Kunyalanyaza ndi kunyozetsa ufulu wachibadwidwe kwadzetsa mchitidwe wankhanza womwe wapondereza chikumbumtima cha anthu.
Kufika kwa dziko lopanda mantha ndi kusowa, ndi ufulu wa chipembedzo ndi chipembedzo, ndizofunika kwambiri kwa anthu wamba.
Adalengezedwa ngati chikhumbo cha
 
kuopera kuti anthu angatembenuke ngati njira yomaliza yolimbana ndi nkhanza ndi kuponderezana.
Pofuna kuteteza ufulu wachibadwidwe, ndikofunikira kuteteza ufulu wa anthu pogwiritsa ntchito malamulo.
Popeza ndikofunikira kulimbikitsa kukulitsa ubale wabwino pakati pa mayiko,
Anthu a bungwe la United Nations adzipereka ku ufulu wachibadwidwe waumunthu, ulemu waumunthu ndi kufunika kwake, komanso
kutsimikiziranso chikhulupiriro chathu pa ufulu wofanana wa amuna ndi akazi, ndikukhala ndi ufulu wochuluka wa anthu.
Kufunitsitsa kulimbikitsa kupita patsogolo komanso kukhala ndi moyo wabwino,
 
Mayiko omwe ali mamembala, mogwirizana ndi bungwe la United Nations, adzalimbikitsa kulemekeza ndi kusunga ufulu wachibadwidwe ndi kumasuka kwa anthu.
Atalonjeza kuti adzakwezedwa,
  
Kumvetsetsa kofanana kwa maufulu ndi kumasuka kumeneku ndikofunikira kuti tikwaniritse Panganoli.

CHONCHO, United Nations General Assembly,
 
Munthu aliyense komanso bungwe lililonse mdera la anthu liyenera kukumbukira Chidziwitso Chapadziko Lonse cha Ufulu Wachibadwidwe, ndi
 

 
Onse pakati pa anthu awo komanso pakati pa anthu a madera omwe ali pansi pa ulamuliro wa Member States.
Kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu ndi ufulu kudzera mu chitsogozo ndi maphunziro, ndikuwonetsetsa kuti zonse zikuyenda bwino.
