I. Izicwangciso zoKubeka

1. USun Tzu wathi: Ubugcisa bemfazwe bubaluleke kakhulu kuRhulumente.

2 Ngumbandela wobomi nokufa, indlela eya elukhuselweni okanye entshabalalweni. Yiyo loo nto ingumbandela wophando ongenakuthi nangayiphi na indlela ungahoywa.

3. Ubugcisa bemfazwe, ke, bulawulwa zizinto ezintlanu eziqhubekayo, ezinokuthi zithathelwe ingqalelo kwiingxoxo zomntu, xa zifuna ukufumanisa iimeko ezifunyenwe kwintsimi.

4. Ezi zezi: (1) UMthetho Wokuziphatha; (2) Izulu; (3) Umhlaba; (4) UMphathi; (5) Indlela noqeqesho.

5,6. UMthetho Wokuziphatha ubangela ukuba abantu bavisisane ngokupheleleyo nomlawuli wabo, ukuze bamlandele kungakhathaliseki ukuba ubomi babo bunjani, bangothuswa yiyo nayiphi na ingozi.

7. Izulu lithetha ubusuku nemini, ukubanda nobushushu, amaxesha namathuba.

8. Umhlaba uquka imigama, emikhulu nemincinci; ingozi kunye nokhuseleko; umhlaba ovulekileyo kunye neendlela ezimxinwa; amathuba obomi nokufa.

9. UMphathi umele iimpawu zobulumko, ukunyaniseka, ububele, inkalipho nokuba ngqongqo.

10. Ngokwenkqubo noqeqesho kufanele kuqondwe ukumiselwa komkhosi kumacandelwana awo afanelekileyo, uthweso-zidanga phakathi kwamagosa, ukugcinwa kweendlela enokuthi ngazo iimpahla zifike emkhosini, nokulawulwa kwenkcitho yomkhosi.

11 Ezi ntloko zintlanu zimele ukuba ziqhelekile kwinjengele yonke: ozaziyo uya koyisa; lowo ungazaziyo uya kusilela.

12 Ke ngoko, ekucamangeni kwenu, nifuna ukumisela imiqathango yomkhosi, makube ngumzekeliso, ngolu hlobo:

13. (1) Nguwuphi kwaba balawuli babini ophantsi koMthetho Wokuziphatha? (2) Yiyiphi kwezi njengele zimbini enobuchule obuninzi? (3) Zilele koobani iingenelo ezivela eZulwini naseMhlabeni? (4) Liliphi icala elona qeqesho lunyanzeliswa ngqongqo? (5) Nguwuphi umkhosi onamandla? (6) Liliphi icala amagosa namadoda aqeqeshwe ngakumbi? (7) Kuwuphi umkhosi apho uzingisa kakhulu kokubini emvuzweni nakwisohlwayo?

14. Ngezi ngqwalasela zisixhenxe ndingaqikelela uloyiso okanye ukoyiswa.

15 Umthetheli-mkhosi oliphulaphula icebo lam, enze ngalo, wokweyisa; Injengele engaliphulaphuliyo icebo lam, ingenzi ngalo, iya koyiswa:--makagxothwe lowo unjalo!

16 Xa usenza inzuzo yesiluleko sam, nceda wenze nayiphi na imeko eluncedo ngaphezu nangaphaya kwemimiselo eqhelekileyo.

17 Njengoko iimeko zivuma, ubani ufanele aluhlengahlengise izicwangciso zakhe.

18. Zonke iimfazwe zisekelwe kwinkohliso.

19. Ngenxa yoko, xa sikwazi ukuhlasela, simele sibonakale singenako; xa sisebenzisa amandla ethu, simele sibonakale singasebenzi; xa sikufuphi, kufuneka senze utshaba lukholelwe ukuba sikude; xa ukude, simele simenze akholelwe ukuba sikufuphi.

20. Bamba izithiyelo ukurhwebesha utshaba. Zibonakalise ukuphazamiseka, kwaye umtyumze.

21 Ukuba uthe wahlala ukhuselekile, mlungisele tu. Ukuba womelele, ziphephe;

22. Ukuba umntu ochasayo unomsindo, funa ukumcaphukisa. uzenze oswele amandla, ukuze abe nekratshi.

23 Ukuba uthe cwaka, uze ungamphumzi; Ukuba imikhosi yakhe ihlangene, yahlule.

24. Mhlasele apho angazilungiselelanga khona, vela apho ungalindelanga khona.

25. Ezi zixhobo zomkhosi, ezikhokelela kuloyiso, akufanele zibhengezwe kwangaphambili.

26 Ngoku injengele eyoyisa edabini yenza izibalo ezininzi etempileni yayo ngaphambi kokuba kuliwe idabi. Injengele ephulukene nedabi yenza izibalo ezimbalwa kwangaphambili. Ngaloo ndlela izibalo ezininzi zikhokelela kuloyiso, kunye nezibalo ezimbalwa ukoyisa: kukangakanani na ukubala konke konke! Kungokuqwalaselwa kule ngongoma endikwaziyo ukubona kwangaphambili ukuba ngubani onokuthi aphumelele okanye aphulukane.

II. Ukulwa iMfazwe

1. USun Tzu wathi: “Emfazweni, apho ethafeni kukho iwaka leenqwelo zokulwa, iwaka leenqwelo zokulwa ezinzima, namajoni anxibe iposi alikhulu lamawaka, kunye nomphako owaneleyo ukubathwala iwaka leeli, inkcitho yasekhaya nangaphambili, kuquka ulonwabo lweendwendwe, izinto ezincinane ezifana neglu nepeyinti, nemali echithwa kwiinqwelo zokulwa newaka lesilivere ngosuku, iya kufikelela kwixabiso leegram eziliwaka ngosuku. Injalo iindleko zokunyusa umkhosi wamadoda angama-100,000.

2. Xa ubandakanyeka kumlo wokwenyani, ukuba uloyiso lude lufike, ke izixhobo zabantu ziya kuba buthuntu kwaye inzondelelo yabo iya kudamba. Ukuba uthe wawungqinga umzi, wowagqiba amandla akho;

3. Kwakhona, ukuba iphulo lithatha ixesha elide, izibonelelo zikaRhulumente aziyi kulingana noxinzelelo.
4. Ngoku, xa izixhobo zakho ziphelile, intshiseko yakho iyancipha, amandla akho ephelile kwaye ubutyebi bakho buchithiwe, ezinye izikhulu ziya kuvuka ukuze zithathe ithuba lokuxhamla kwakho. Ngoko akukho mntu, enoba ulumke kangakanani na, uya kukwazi ukuyiphepha imiphumo emele ilandele.

5 Ngaloo ndlela, nangona siye seva ngokungxama kobudenge emfazweni, ubulumko abuzange bubonwe bunxulunyaniswa nokulibaziseka okude.

6. Akukho mzekelo welizwe eliye laxhamla kwiimfazwe ezithatha ixesha elide.

7 Nguye kuphela oqhelene ngokucokisekileyo nobubi bemfazwe onokuyiqonda ngokucokisekileyo indlela enenzuzo yokuqhubeka nayo.

8 Ijoni elinobuchule alinyusi mntu wesibini, neenqwelwana zalo azithwali ngaphezu kwezihlandlo ezibini.

9 Yiza nempahla yemfazwe kunye nawe ukusuka ekhaya, kodwa ufulathele utshaba. Ngaloo ndlela umkhosi uya kuba nokutya okwaneleyo.

10. Intlupheko ye-State exchequer ibangela ukuba umkhosi ugcinwe ngamagalelo asuka kude. Ukuba negalelo ekugcineni umkhosi ukude kubangela ukuba abantu bahlwempuzeke.

11 Kwelinye icala, ukusondela komkhosi kubangela ukuba amaxabiso anyuke; kwaye amaxabiso aphezulu abangela ukuba izinto zabantu ziphele.

12. Xa into yabo ikhutshiwe, abalimi baya kuxhwaleka ngenxa yeendleko ezinzima.

13,14. Ngale lahleko yempahla kunye nokudinwa kwamandla, amakhaya abantu aya kuhluthwa, kwaye isahlulo sesithathu sengeniso yabo siya kuchithwa; ngoxa iindleko zikarhulumente zeenqwelo zokulwa ezaphukileyo, amahashe agugileyo, izigcina-sifuba nezigcina-ntloko, izaphetha neentolo, imikhonto namakhaka, iingubo zokuzikhusela, iinkunzi zeenkomo ezinqwenelekayo nezeenqwelo ezinkulu, ziya kuba kwisahlulo sesine sengeniso yayo iyonke.

15. Yiyo ke loo nto injengele elumkileyo inika inqaku lokukhangela utshaba. Umthwalo omnye weenqwelo zotshaba ulingana namashumi amabini omntu, kwaye ngokunjalo umfanekiso omnye wepronder yakhe ilingana namashumi amabini ukusuka kwivenkile yakhe.

16 Ngoku ukuze silubulale utshaba, amadoda ethu makavuswe ngumsindo; ukuze kubekho inzuzo ekoyiseni utshaba, kufuneka babe nemivuzo yabo.

17 Kungoko ekulweni kweenqwelo zokulwa, kwathatyathwa iinqwelo zokulwa ezilishumi, nokuba nangaphezulu, kwavuzwa abo bathabatha abokuqala. Iiflegi zethu kufuneka zifakwe endaweni yezotshaba, kwaye iinqwelo zokulwa zixutywe kwaye zisetyenziswe kunye nezethu. Amajoni athinjiweyo afanele aphathwe ngobubele aze agcinwe.

18. Oku kubizwa, ukusebenzisa utshaba oloyisiweyo ukwandisa amandla kabani.

19 Ngoko ke emfazweni injongo yenu mayibe luloyiso, ingabi lixesha elide.

20 ukuze kwazeke ukuba umthetheli womkhosi nguye ogwebayo phakathi kwabantu, indoda ekuxhomekeke kuyo ukuba uhlanga luxolile, nokuba lusemngciphekweni.

III. Uhlaselo lwe-Strategem

1. USun Tzu wathi: Kubugcisa obusebenzisekayo bemfazwe, eyona nto ingcono kunazo zonke kukuthabatha ilizwe lotshaba liphelele kwaye linjalo; ukuliqhekeza nokulitshabalalisa akulunganga kangako. Ke, kwakhona, kungcono ukuphinda umkhosi uphelele kunokuba uwutshabalalise, ukubamba ibutho, iqela okanye inkampani yonke kunokuba ubatshabalalise.

2. Yiyo ke loo nto ukulwa kwaye woyise kuwo onke amadabi akho asikokugqwesa okuphezulu; ukugqwesa okuphezulu kubandakanya ukwaphula inkcaso yotshaba ngaphandle kokulwa.

3. Ngaloo ndlela olona hlobo luphezulu loburhalarhume kukubhangisa izicwangciso zotshaba; okulandelayo kukuthintela ukuhlangana kwemikhosi yotshaba; okulandelayo ukuze kuhlaselwe umkhosi wotshaba endle; yaye owona mgaqo umbi kakhulu kukungqinga izixeko ezinqatyisiweyo.

4. Umthetho uthi, musa ukungqinga izixeko ezibiyelweyo ukuba kunokwenzeka ukuba kuphetshwe. Ukulungiswa kweengubo, ikhusi, kunye nezixhobo ezahlukeneyo zemfazwe, kuya kuthatha iinyanga ezintathu zonke; yaye ukufunjwa kweenduli ngaphaya kweendonga kuya kuthabatha iinyanga ezintathu ezingakumbi.

5 Injengele, engakwaziyo ukulawula ukucaphuka kwayo, iya kuhlasela amadoda ayo njengeembovane ezigqubayo, ibe umphumo uya kuba sisinye kwisithathu samadoda ayo abulawe, ngoxa idolophu ihleli ingabanjwanga. Injalo ke imiphumo eyintlekele yokungqingwa.

6 Ngako oko inkokeli enobuchule iyawanyathela utshaba; Uyithimba imizi yabo, angayingqinga; Uya kububhukuqa ubukumkani babo ngaphandle kokusebenza ixesha elide entsimini.

7 Ekunye nemikhosi yakhe iya kuphikisa ubukhosi boBukhosi, kwaye ngaloo ndlela, ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa ngumntu, uloyiso lwakhe luya kuphelela. Le yindlela yokuhlaselwa nge-stratagem.

8 Kungummiselo emfazweni, ukuba imikhosi yethu ilishumi komnye wotshaba, ukuyirhawula; ukuba kahlanu komnye, makamhlasele; ukuba baninzi ngokuphindwe kabini, ukuwahlula kubini umkhosi wethu.
9 Ukuba ifaniswe ngokulinganayo, sinokunikela edabini; ukuba bangaphantsi kancinane ngamanani, sinokuluphepha utshaba; ukuba ngandlela zonke asilingananga, sinokubaleka kuye.

10. Ngenxa yoko, nangona umlo olukhuni unokwenziwa ngumkhosi omncinci, ekugqibeleni kufuneka ubanjwe ngamandla amakhulu.

11. Ngoku injengele likhuselo likaRhulumente; ukuba i-bulwark igqityiwe kuzo zonke iindawo; uRhulumente uya komelela; ukuba i-bulwark inesiphene, i-State iya kuba buthathaka.

12 Kukho iindlela ezintathu umlawuli anokuwuzisela ububi umkhosi wakhe:

13. (1) Ngokuyalela umkhosi ukuba uhambele phambili okanye uhlehle, ungasazi isibakala sokuba awunakuthobela. Oku kubizwa ngokuba yi-hobbling the army.

14. (2) Ngokuzama ukulawula umkhosi ngendlela efanayo naleyo alawula ngayo ubukumkani, engazi ngeemeko ezifumaneka emkhosini. Oku kubangela ukungazinzi ezingqondweni zejoni.

15. (3) Ngokuqesha amagosa omkhosi wakhe ngaphandle kocalucalulo, ngokungazi umgaqo-mkhosi wokuziqhelanisa neemeko. Oku kwenza amajoni azithembe.

16 Kodwa xa umkhosi ungazolanga kwaye ungathembeki, ngokuqinisekileyo inkathazo iya kuvela kwezinye iinkosana ezinoburharha. Oku kuzisa isiphithiphithi nje emkhosini, kunye noloyiso olulahlekisayo.

17 Ngaloo ndlela sisenokwazi ukuba kukho izinto ezintlanu ezifunekayo ukuze soyise: (1) Uya koyisa lowo ulaziyo ixesha lokulwa nelokungaliwa. (2) Uya kuphumelela ngubani owaziyo ukuphatha zombini amandla aphezulu kunye angaphantsi. (3) Uya koyisa umkhosi wakhe uqhutywa ngumoya ofanayo kuzo zonke iindidi zawo. (4) Uya kuphumelela lowo, ezilungisile, alinde ukuthabatha utshaba alulungele. (5) Uya kuphumelela lowo unamandla omkhosi kwaye angaphazanyiswa ngumlawuli.

18 Kungoko kusithiwa: Ukuba uyalwazi utshaba kwaye uyazazi, akuyomfuneko ukuba woyike umphumo wekhulu leemfazwe. Ukuba uyazazi kodwa hayi utshaba, kuba lonke uloyiso oluzuziweyo uya koyiswa. Ukuba awuzazi utshaba okanye wena ngokwakho, uya koyiswa kulo lonke idabi.

IV. Iimpawu zobuchule

1. USun Tzu uthe: Abalweli abalungileyo bamandulo baqala bazibeke ngaphaya kokuba kunokwenzeka ukuba boyisiwe, baze balinde ithuba lokoyisa utshaba.

2. Ukuzikhusela ekoyisweni kusezandleni zethu, kodwa ithuba lokoyisa utshaba libonelelwa lutshaba ngokwalo.

3. Ngaloo ndlela umlwi olungileyo uyakwazi ukuzikhusela ekoyisweni, kodwa akanako ukuqiniseka ngokuloyisa utshaba.

4 Kungoko kusithiwa: Ubani usenokukwazi ukoyisa engenakukwenza oko.

5. Ukhuseleko ngokuchasene nokoyiswa kuthetha amaqhinga okuzikhusela; ukukwazi ukoyisa utshaba kuthetha ukuthatha into ekhubekisayo.

6. Ukuma kwindawo yokuzikhusela kubonisa ukungonelanga kwamandla; ukuhlasela, ubuninzi bamandla.

7 Injengele elichule ekukhuseleni izimela kwezona ndawo zifihlakeleyo zomhlaba; Olichule enyangweni ubengezela enyangweni encotsheni yezulu. Ngaloo ndlela kwelinye icala sinamandla okuzikhusela; kwelinye ke, luloyiso oluzalisekileyo.

8. Ukubona uloyiso kuphela xa lungaphakathi komhlambi wemihlambi eqhelekileyo asiyondlela yokugqwesa.

9. Kananjalo akusiyo i-acme yokugqwesa ukuba uyalwa kwaye uphumelele kwaye uBukhosi bonke buthi, "Wenze kakuhle!"

10 Ukuphakamisa iinwele zasekwindla akusiyo uphawu lwamandla amakhulu; ukubona ilanga nenyanga akusiyo imbonakalo ebukhali; ukuva ingxolo yeendudumo asilophawu lwendlebe ekhawulezayo.

11. Oko abantu bamandulo babekubiza ngokuba ngumlwi okrelekrele ngulowo ongaphumeleliyo kuphela, kodwa ogqwesileyo ekuphumeleleni ngokulula.

12. Ngoko uloyiso lwakhe alumzisi udumo lobulumko okanye uzuko ngenxa yenkalipho.

13 Uloyisa amadabi akhe ngokungenzi iimpazamo. Ukungenzi iimpazamo kuko okumisela ukuqiniseka koloyiso, kuba kuthetha ukoyisa utshaba esele loyisiwe.

14. Yiyo loo nto umlwi onobuchule uzibeka kwindawo eyenza ukuba kungenzeki ukoyiswa, kwaye akaphoswa lixesha lokoyisa utshaba.

15. Ngaloo ndlela emfazweni ingcali yobuchule ifuna kuphela idabi emva kokuba uloyiso luloyisile, kanti lowo umiselwe ukoyisa imilo yokuqala aze emva koko ajonge uloyiso.

16. Inkokeli egqibeleleyo iwuhlakulela umthetho wokuziphatha, kwaye ibambelela ngokungqongqo kwindlela noqeqesho; ngaloo ndlela kusemandleni akhe ukulawula impumelelo.

17. Ngokuphathelele kwindlela yomkhosi, okokuqala, sinoMlinganiselo; okwesibini, Uqikelelo lobungakanani; okwesithathu, Ukubala; okwesine, Ukulungelelaniswa kwamathuba; okwesihlanu, uLoyiso.
18. Umlinganiselo uxhomekeke eMhlabeni; Uqikelelo lomyinge kuMlinganiso; Ukubalwa kuqikelelo lobungakanani; Ukulinganisa amathuba ekubaleni; kunye Noloyiso kuBalanceing of amathuba.

19 Umkhosi owoyisileyo umchasile lowo ungqushiweyo, unjengobunzima beponti ebekwe esikalini ngokuchasene nokhozo olunye.

20 Ukugaleleka komkhosi owoyisayo kufana nokutyhoboza kwamanzi aphumela kumhadi oziifatom eziliwaka.

V. Amandla

1. USun Tzu uthe: Ukulawulwa kwamandla amakhulu ngumgaqo ofanayo nolawulo lwamadoda ambalwa: ngumbuzo nje wokwahlula amanani abo.

2. Ukulwa nomkhosi omkhulu phantsi komyalelo wakho akukho nto yahlukile ekulweni nomncinane: ngumbuzo nje wokumisela imiqondiso nemiqondiso.

3. Ukuqinisekisa ukuba wonke umkhosi wakho unokumelana nokuhlasela kotshaba kwaye uhlale ungashukumi-- oku kwenziwa ngokulawula ngokuthe ngqo nangokungathanga ngqo.

4. Ukuze impembelelo yomkhosi wakho ibe njengelitye lokusila eliphonswe kwiqanda - oku kwenziwa yinzululwazi yamanqaku abuthathaka kunye namandla.

5 Kuwo wonke umlo, indlela ethe ngqo isenokusetyenziswa ekuzibandakanyeni edabini, kodwa iindlela ezingathanga ngqo ziya kufuneka ukuze kuqinisekwe uloyiso.

6. Amaqhinga angathanga ngqo, asetyenziswe ngokufanelekileyo, awapheli njengeZulu noMhlaba, angapheli njengokuhamba kwemilambo nemisinga; njengelanga nenyanga, ziyaphela kodwa ziqale ngokutsha; njengamaxesha amane, bayadlula ukuze babuye kube kanye.

7. Akukho ngaphezu kweenowuthsi zomculo ezintlanu, ukanti indibaniselwano yezi zintlanu ivelisa iingoma ezininzi kunanini na ngaphambili.

8. Ayikho ngaphezu kwemibala emihlanu ephambili (ebhlowu, emthubi, ebomvu, emhlophe, nemnyama), ukanti xa idibene ivelisa imibala emininzi kunanini na ngaphambili.

9. Akukho ngaphezu kwentlanu yencasa yekhadinali (emuncu, emuncu, etyiwayo, eswiti, ekrakra), ukanti indibaniselwano yazo ivelisa incasa ezininzi kunezo zinokungcatshwa.

10 Edabini azikho iindlela ezingaphezu kwezimbini zokuhlasela, eyokuqala nengathanga ngqo; ukanti ezi zimbini zidibene zinika uthotho olungapheliyo lweendlela.

11. Ukuthe ngqo kunye nokungathanga ngqo kukhokelela omnye komnye ngokulandelelana. Kufana nokuhamba kwisangqa-awuze ufike esiphelweni. Ngubani onokugqiba amathuba okudityaniswa kwawo?

12 Ukufika komkhosi kunjengokuntlitha komsinga, othi uqengqele amatye endleleni yawo.

13. Umgangatho wesigqibo ufana nokuphaphazela ngexesha elifanelekileyo kokhetshe okwenza ukuba libethe litshabalalise ixhoba lalo.

14 Ke indoda elungileyo elwayo iya kuba yinkwalambisa kwasekuqalekeni kwayo, engxame ekwenzeni isigqibo;

15. Amandla anokufaniswa nokugotywa komnqamlezo; isigqibo, ekukhutshweni kwesixhobo sokuvusa.

16 Phakathi kwesiphithiphithi nesiphithiphithi sedabi, kusenokubonakala ngathi kukho isidubedube kodwa kungabikho siphithiphithi sokwenene kwaphela; phakathi kwesiphithiphithi kunye nesiphithiphithi, uluhlu lwakho lunokuba lungenantloko okanye umsila, kodwa luya kuba bubungqina obuchasene nokoyiswa.

17. Ukuphazamiseka okufanisiweyo kubangela uqeqesho olugqibeleleyo, uloyiko olulinganisiweyo lubeka isibindi; ubuthathaka obufanisiweyo bubeka amandla.

18. Ukufihla ucwangco phantsi kwengubo yokuphazamiseka ngumbuzo nje wokwahlulahlula; ukufihla inkalipho phantsi komboniso woloyiko kucingela ingxowa-mali yamandla afihlakeleyo; amandla okugquma ngobuthathaka kufuneka enziwe ngeendlela zobuchule.

19 Ke yena olichule ekuqhubeni utshaba lwakhe ugcina imbonakalo yenkohliso; Ubingelela into, ukuze utshaba luyixhwile kuyo.

20 Ngokubambelela kwizithiyelo, umgcina ehambeni; aze amlalele kunye neqela labakhethiweyo.

21. Umlo onobuqili ujonge umphumo wamandla adibeneyo, kwaye akafuni kakhulu kubantu ngabanye. Ke ngoko ukukwazi kwakhe ukukhetha amadoda afanelekileyo kunye nokusebenzisa amandla adibeneyo.

22. Xa esebenzisa amandla adityanisiweyo, amadoda akhe okulwa aye afane nezigodo eziqengqayo okanye amatye. Kuba luhlobo lwesiqobo somthi okanye ilitye ukuhlala ungashukumi kumhlaba othe tyaba, nokushukuma xa ukwithambeka; ukuba zikona ezine, ukuma ngxi, kodwa ukuba zimile okwengqukuva, ukuqengqeleka ezantsi.

23. Ngaloo ndlela amandla aphuhliswa ngamadoda okulwa afanelekileyo anjengesantya selitye elingqukuva eliqengqeleke intaba ngamawaka eemitha ukuphakama. Kuninzi kumxholo wamandla.

VI. Amanqaku Abuthathaka kwaye Anamandla

1. USun Tzu wathi: Nabani na ongowokuqala endle aze alindele ukufika kotshaba, uya kuba emtsha edabini; ongowesibini endle kwaye kufuneka akhawuleze ukuya edabini uya kufika ediniwe.
2 Ngako oko umlwi onobuqili unyanzelisa utshaba lwakhe, kodwa akakuvumeli ukuba utshaba lubekwe ngokwentando yakhe.

3 Ngokuzibekela iingenelo kuye, unokwenza utshaba lusondele ngokwalo; okanye, ngokwenza umonakalo, unokwenza utshaba lungakwazi ukusondela.

4 Ukuba utshaba luthe cwaka, luyixhokonxa; ukuba unikwe ngokufanelekileyo ngokutya, unokumbulala; ukuba uthe cwaka, unokumnyanzela ukuba afuduke.

5. Ukuvela kwiindawo apho utshaba kufuneka lukhawuleze ukuwakhusela; matsha ngokukhawuleza ukuya kwiindawo ongazilindelanga.

6 Umkhosi ungahamba imigama emide ungabandezelekile, ukuba uthe wacanda ilizwe apho utshaba lungekhoyo.

7. Unokuqiniseka ukuba uya kuphumelela ekuhlaselweni kwakho ukuba uhlasela kuphela iindawo ezingakhuselekanga.Unokuqinisekisa ukhuseleko lokukhusela kwakho ukuba ubambe kuphela izikhundla ezingenakuhlaselwa.

8. Yiyo loo nto injengele inobuchule ekuhlaseleni umchasi wakhe ongayazi into emayikhusele; Ulichule ekukhuseleni, ochasayo engayazi into amakayenze.

9 Owu ubugcisa bobuthixo bobuqili nobufihlo! Ngawe sifunda ukuba singabonakali, ngawe singavakali; kwaye kungoko sinokulibamba ikamva lotshaba ezandleni zethu.

10 Ungáqhubela phambili, ungongenakuthintelwa, ukuba uthe wavavanya utshaba; ungarhola umhlala phantsi kwaye ukhuseleke ekusukeleni ukuba iintshukumo zakho zikhawuleza kakhulu kunezo zotshaba.

11. Ukuba sinqwenela ukulwa, utshaba lunokunyanzelwa ukuba luthethane nangona lukhuselwe emva kodonga oluphakamileyo kunye nomsele onzulu. Ekuphela kwento esiyifunayo kukuhlasela kwenye indawo aza kunyanzeleka ukuba ayikhulule.

12. Ukuba asifuni kulwa, sinako ukuluthintela utshaba ukuba lungabi nanxaxheba kuthi nangona imida yenkampu yethu ilandwa nje emhlabeni. Ekuphela kwento ekufuneka siyenze kukuphosa into engaqhelekanga nengenakuphenduleka kwindlela yakhe.

13. Ngokufumanisa iimpawu zotshaba size sihlale singabonakali ngokwethu, sinokugcina imikhosi yethu igxile, ngelixa utshaba kufuneka lwahlulwe.

14. Sinokubumba umzimba omnye omanyeneyo, ngelixa utshaba kufuneka lwahlulwe lube ngamaqhezu. Yiyo ke loo nto kuyakubakho ungquzulwano phakathi kwamacandelo awohlukeneyo, into ethetha ukuba siya kuba baninzi kwabambalwa botshaba.

15 Ukuba ke sithe ngokunjalo sihlasela iqela elingaphantsi koyingangalala, soba sengxakini enkulu abachasi bethu.

16 Indawo esizimisele ukulwa kuyo mayingaziwa; kuba ngoko utshaba kuya kufuneka lulungiselele ngokuchasene nohlaselo olunokwenzeka kumanqaku ahlukeneyo; kwaye imikhosi yakhe isasazwa kumacala amaninzi, amanani esiya kufuneka sijongane nawo nakweliphi na ixesha elinikiweyo aya kuba mbalwa ngokomlinganiselo.

17 Ngokuba utshaba luthe lwawomeleza inqwelo yalo, wówenza umqosheliso walo; Ukuba uthe wayiqinisa imva yakhe, uya kuyenza buthathaka iveni yakhe; Ukuba uthe womeleza ukhohlo lwakhe, uya kuncipha ekunene; Ukuba uthe womeleza isandla sakhe sokunene, wówonakalisa ukhohlo lwakhe. Ukuba uthumela iziqinisekiso kuyo yonke indawo, uya kuba buthathaka kuyo yonke indawo.

18. Ubuthathaka bamanani buvela ekuzilungiseleleni ngokuchasene nohlaselo olunokwenzeka; amandla enani, ekunyanzeliseni umchasi wethu ukuba enze la malungiselelo ngokuchasene nathi.

19 Ukwazi indawo kunye nexesha ledabi elizayo, sisenokunikela ingqalelo sikwiindawo ezikude kakhulu ukuze silwe.

20 Kodwa ukuba akukho xesha okanye indawo eyaziwayo, ngoko iphiko lasekhohlo aliyi kuba namandla okunceda ilungelo, ilungelo elilinganayo elingenakunceda ngasekhohlo, iveni engakwazi ukukhulula umva, okanye umva wokuxhasa iveni. Kukangakanani ke ukuba awona macandelo akude omkhosi ayenayo nantoni na engaphantsi kwekhulu le-LI ngokushiyana, kwaye nezona zikufuphi zahlulwe ngee-LI ezininzi!

21 Nangona ngokoqikelelo lwam, amajoni kaYuh engaphezulu kowethu ngenani, oko akuyi kuwanceda nganto ekoyisweni kwawo. Ndithi ngoko uloyiso lunokufumaneka.

22 Nangona utshaba luthe lwaqina ngamanani, sinokuluthintela ekulweni; Yenza isicwangciso sokufumanisa izicwangciso zakhe kunye nokuba nokwenzeka kwempumelelo yazo.

23. Mvuse, kwaye ufunde umgaqo womsebenzi wakhe okanye ukungasebenzi. Mnyanzele ukuba aziveze, ukuze afumane iindawo zakhe ezisengozini.

24 Khawuthelekise ngokucokisekileyo umkhosi ochasayo nowakho, ukuze wazi apho amandla agqithiseleyo nalapho asilela khona.

25. Ekwenzeni isimilo sobuchule, esona sandi siphezulu onokuthi usifumane kukuzifihla; zifihle iingqondo zakho, kwaye uya kukhuseleka ekuthinjweni kweentlola ezinobuqili, kubuqhophololo bezona ngqondo zilumkileyo.

26 Hayi, ukuba uloyiso kubo amaqhinga otshaba lungenako ukukuqonda oko indimbane.
27. Bonke abantu bayawabona amaqhinga endoyisa ngawo, kodwa into engenakubonwa mntu licebo apho uloyiso luvela khona.

28. Musa ukuphinda amaqhinga athe akufumana uloyiso olunye, kodwa vumela iindlela zakho zilawulwe ziimeko ezahlukeneyo ezingapheliyo.

29 Ngamanzi amaqhinga omkhosi; ngokuba amanzi ekuhambeni kwawo kwemvelo abaleka emke ezindaweni eziphakamileyo akhawuleze ukuya ezantsi.

30 Ngoko ke, emfazweni, indlela kukukuphepha oko kunamandla, nokubetha oko kuthambileyo.

31. Amanzi abumba indlela yawo ngokobume bomhlaba ahamba phezu kwawo; ijoni lisebenzela uloyiso lwalo kutshaba elijongene nalo.

32. Ngoko ke, njengokuba amanzi egcina imo engaguqukiyo, ngoko kwimfazwe akukho zimo eziqhubekayo.

33. Lowo unokuguqula amaqhinga akhe ngokubhekiselele kumchasi wakhe kwaye ngaloo ndlela aphumelele ekuphumeleleni, unokubizwa ngokuba ngumthetheli ozelwe ezulwini.

34. Izinto ezintlanu (amanzi, umlilo, iinkuni, isinyithi, umhlaba) azisoloko ziphambili ngokulinganayo; amaxesha amane onyaka enza omnye komnye ngokulandelelana. Kukho iintsuku ezimfutshane kwaye zinde; inyanga inamaxesha ayo okuncipha kunye nokuncipha.

VII. Ukuqhuba

1. USun Tzu wathi: Emfazweni, injengele ifumana imiyalelo yayo kukumkani.

2. Emva kokuba eqokelele umkhosi kwaye wayigxininisa imikhosi yakhe, kufuneka adibanise kwaye angqinelanise izinto ezahlukeneyo kuwo ngaphambi kokumisa inkampu yakhe.

3. Emva koko, kuza i-tactical maneuvering, ekungekho nto inzima ngakumbi. Ubunzima bobuchule bokulawula bubandakanya ukuguqula ubuqhophololo bube ngqo, kwaye ilishwa libe yinzuzo.

4. Ngaloo ndlela, ukuthatha indlela ende kunye ejikelezayo, emva kokuhenda intshaba endleleni, kwaye nangona iqala emva kwayo, ukucwangcisa ukufikelela kwinjongo phambi kwakhe, kubonisa ulwazi lwe-artifice yokuphambuka.

5. Ukuqhuba nomkhosi kuyingenelo; ngesihlwele esingaqeqeshekanga, esiyingozi kakhulu.

6. Ukuba umisela umkhosi oxhobe ngokupheleleyo ngoMatshi ukuze uthabathe iadvanteji, amathuba okuba uya kube ushiywe lixesha. Ngakolunye uhlangothi, ukukhupha ikholamu ephaphazelayo ngenjongo kubandakanya umbingelelo wempahla kunye neevenkile.

7 Ngaloo ndlela, ukuba uyayalela amadoda enu ukuba asonge iidyasi zawo, enze imingcelele ngenkani ngaphandle kokumisa imini okanye ubusuku, agubungele umgama ophindwe kabini kumgama oqhelekileyo ekuwoluleni, esenza ikhulu le-LI ukuze ajike inzuzo, iinkokeli zawo onke amaqela enu amathathu ziya kuwela ezandleni zotshaba.

8 Ke wona amadoda anamandla aya kuba ngaphambili, awele ngasemva, kuthi kolu cwangciso kube sisahlulo seshumi somkhosi wakho esiya kufika apho siya khona.

9. Ukuba uqhankqalaza amashumi amahlanu e-LI ukuze ukwazi ukoyisa utshaba, uya kuphulukana nenkokeli yecandelo lakho lokuqala, kwaye sisiqingatha somkhosi wakho oya kufikelela usukelo.

10. Ukuba unokumatsha amashumi amathathu e-LI ngento enye, isibini kwisithathu somkhosi wakho siya kufika.

11. Singayithatha ngoko ukuba umkhosi ongenawo uloliwe wempahla ulahlekile; ngaphandle kwezibonelelo iyalahleka; ngaphandle kweziseko zobonelelo ilahlekile.

12. Asinakungena kwizivumelwano de sibe siqhelane noyilo lwabamelwane bethu.

13. Asifanelekanga ukukhokela umkhosi kumngcelele ngaphandle kokuba siqhelene nobuso belizwe—iintaba namahlathi alo, imigibe neenduli zalo, imigxobhozo nemigxobhozo yalo.

14. Asiyi kukwazi ukujika i-akhawunti yenzuzo yendalo ngaphandle kokuba sisebenzisa izikhokelo zasekuhlaleni.

15 Emfazweni ziqhelise ukuhanahanisa, yaye uya kuphumelela.

16. Ukuba uza kugxila okanye uyahlula amajoni akho, makugqitywe ngokweemeko.

17 Ukungxama kwakho makube kokomoya, Ukuxina kwakho oko kwehlathi.

18 Ekuphangweni nasekuphangweni kunjengomlilo;

19 Iingcinga zakho mazibe mnyama, zingabi nakungeneka njengobusuku;

20 Xa nithe naliphanga ilizwe, abiwe amaxhoba phakathi kwenu; xa uthimba intsimi entsha, yinqumle ibe yizabelo ukuze kuxhamle ijoni.

21. Camngca uze uqiqe phambi kokuba wenze inyathelo.

22. Uya koyisa ofunde ubuqhetseba bokutenxa. Bunjalo ke ubugcisa bokuqhuba.

23. INcwadi yoLawulo loMkhosi ithi: Kwithafa ledabi, ilizwi elithethiweyo alithwali kude ngokwaneleyo: kungoko kumiselwa iigawuni namagubu. Kwaye izinto eziqhelekileyo azikwazi ukubonwa ngokucacileyo ngokwaneleyo: ngoko ke iziko leebhanile kunye neeflegi.

24. Iigubu namagubu, iibhena kunye neeflegi, ziindlela apho iindlebe namehlo omamkeli-ndwendwe anokuthi agxininise kwindawo enye.
25. Lo mkhosi ubumba umzimba omnye omanyeneyo, akunakwenzeka na ukuba igorha liqhubele phambili lilodwa, okanye ukuba igwala lirhoxe lilodwa. Obu bubuchule bokuphatha inkitha enkulu yamadoda.

26 Ke ngoko, ekulweni ebusuku, sebenzisani kakhulu imililo yomlilo namagubu, nasekulweni emini, iiflegi neebhanile, njengento yokuphembelela iindlebe namehlo omkhosi wenu.

27 Umkhosi uphela unokuphangwa ngumoya; umthetheli-oyintloko usenokuhluthwa ubukho bakhe engqondweni.

28 Kungokunje umoya wobukroti uyolile; emini enkulu iqalisile ukubhabha; kwaye ngokuhlwa, ingqondo yakhe izimisele ukubuyela enkampini kuphela.

29. Ngoko ke, injengele ekrelekrele iyawuphepha umkhosi xa umoya wayo uzimisele, kodwa iyawuhlasela xa ityhafile yaye izimisele ukubuya. Obu bubugcisa bokufunda iimoods.

30. Uqeqeshekile kwaye uzolile, ukulinda ukubonakala kwesiphithiphithi kunye nesiphithiphithi phakathi kotshaba:--obu bubuchule bokuzibamba.

31 Ukuba kufuphi nomgomo ngoxa utshaba lusekude kuyo, Ukulinda ngokukhululeka ngoxa utshaba lusebenza nzima luzabalaza, Ukuhlutha ngoxa utshaba lubhuqwa yindlala: Bububuchule obu bokuphatha amandla kabani.

32. Ukuyeka ukuthintela utshaba olunobhanile balo lulungelelaniswe ngokugqibeleleyo, ukuyeka ukuhlasela umkhosi omiswe ngokuzolileyo nangokuzithemba:--obu bubuchule bokufunda iimeko.

33 Ngumbono womkhosi ukuba unganyuki unyuke utshaba, ungaluchasi xa lusihlayo.

34 Musa ukulusukela utshaba oluphaphatha; musani ukuwahlasela amajoni anomsindo.

35 Musa ukuginya isithiyelo sotshaba. Musani ukuwuphazamisa umkhosi obuyela ekhaya.

36. Xa urhangqa umkhosi, shiya indawo yokuphuma ikhululekile. Musa ukulucinezela kakhulu utshaba oluphelelwe lithemba.

37 Bunjalo ke ubugcisa bemfazwe.

VIII. Ukwahluka kwamaqhinga

1. USun Tzu wathi: Emfazweni, injengele ifumana imiyalelo kukumkani, iqokelele umkhosi wayo ize igxininise imikhosi yayo.

2. Xa ukwilizwe elinzima, musa ukumisa inkampu. Kwilizwe apho iindlela eziphakamileyo zidibana khona, bamba izandla kunye namahlakani akho. Musa ukuhlala kwiindawo ezibekwe yedwa eziyingozi. Kwiimeko ezixineneyo, kuya kufuneka ubhenele kwi-stratagem. Kwimeko yokuphelelwa lithemba, kufuneka ulwe.

3 Kukho iindlela ekungamele zilandelwe, imikhosi ekungafuneki ihlaselwe, iidolophu ekumele zingqingwe, izikhundla ekungamele kukhuphiswe kuzo, imiyalelo yokumkani ekungafanelanga ithotyelwe.

4. Injengele eliqonda ngokucokisekileyo iingenelo ezihamba kunye nokwahlukahlukana kwamaqhinga iyayazi indlela yokusingatha imikhosi yayo.

5. Umntu oqhelekileyo ongaziqondi ezi zinto, unokuqhelana kakuhle nokucwangciswa kwelizwe, kodwa akayi kukwazi ukuguqula ulwazi lwakhe kwi-akhawunti ephathekayo.

6. Ngoko ke, umfundi wemfazwe ongawaziyo ubugcisa bemfazwe wokutshintsha-tshintsha izicwangciso zakhe, nangona eqhelene neFive Advantages, uya kusilela ukuwasebenzisa kakuhle amadoda akhe.

7. Yiyo ke loo nto kwizicwangciso zenkokeli elumkileyo, ukucingela inzuzo kunye nokusilela kuya kudityaniswa.

8 Ukuba ulindelo lwethu lwenzuzo lulinganiselwe ngolu hlobo, sisenokuphumelela ekuphumezeni inxalenye ebalulekileyo yamacebo ethu.

9 Ukuba, kwelinye icala, phakathi kobunzima sisoloko sikulungele ukuxhakamfula inzuzo ethile, sinokuzikhupha kwilishwa.

10 Zinciphiseni iinkosi ezinobutshaba ngokuzenza umonakalo kuzo; nibathwalise inkathazo, nibagcine rhoqo; bamba imitsalane ekhethekileyo, kwaye ubenze bangxamele kuyo nayiphi na indawo.

11. Ubugcisa bemfazwe busifundisa ukuba singathembeli ekuzeni kotshaba, kodwa kukulungela kwethu ukumamkela; hayi ngethuba lokungahlaseli kwakhe, kodwa kwinto yokuba senze ukuba isikhundla sethu singaphikiswa.

12. Zintlanu iziphene eziyingozi ezinokuchaphazela ngokubanzi: (1) Ukungakhathali, okukhokelela kwintshabalalo; (2) ubugwala, obukhokelela ekuthinjweni; (3) ukugqajukelwa ngumsindo, okunokucatshukiswa kukuthuka; (4) isithende sembeko esilihlazo; (5) ukucela kakhulu amadoda akhe, nto leyo embhenca kwixhala nenkathazo.

13 Ezi zizono ezihlanu ezigqwesayo zenjengele, ezonakalisa ekuqhutyweni kwemfazwe.

14 Xa umkhosi ubhukuqwa kwaye inkokeli yawo ibulewe, isizathu ngokuqinisekileyo siya kufunyanwa phakathi kwezi ziphoso zihlanu eziyingozi. Mababe ngumxholo wokucamngca.

IX. Umkhosi ngoMatshi

1. USun Tzu wathi: Ngoku siza kumbuzo wokumisa umkhosi, nokujonga iimpawu zotshaba. Welani iintaba kamsinya, niphambukele ezihlanjeni;
2. Inkampu kwiindawo eziphakamileyo, ujonge ilanga. Musa ukunyuka phezulu ukuze ulwe. Kangangokuba imfazwe yasezintabeni.

3. Emva kokuwela umlambo, kufuneka ube kude kuwo.

4 Xa umkhosi ohlaselayo uwela umlambo xa usiya phambili, musani ukuhambela phambili ukuya kuhlangana nawo phakathi komsinga. Kuya kuba ngcono ukuvumela isiqingatha somkhosi siwele, kwaye emva koko unikezele ngohlaselo lwakho.

5. Ukuba ungxamele ukulwa, akufunekanga uye kumhlangabeza umhlaseli kufuphi nomlambo ekufuneka ewele.

6. Wuphakamisele phezulu umsebenzi wakho kunotshaba, ujonge elangeni. Sukunyuka umsinga ukuya kuhlangana notshaba. Kangangokuba imfazwe yomlambo.

7. Xa uwela imigxobhozo enetyuwa, eyona nto ikuxhalabeleyo kukuba udlule ngokukhawuleza, ngaphandle kokulibazisa.

8. Ukuba unyanzelekile ukuba ulwe kumgxobhozo wetyuwa, kufuneka ube namanzi nengca kufutshane nawe, kwaye ubuyisele umva wakho kwigaqa lemithi. Ininzi kakhulu imisebenzi kwiimatshi zetyuwa.

9. Kwilizwe elomileyo, elikwinqanaba elisezantsi, thatha indawo efikeleleka ngokulula ngokunyuka komhlaba ngasekunene kwakho nangasemva kwakho, ukuze ingozi ibe ngaphambili, kwaye ukhuseleko lulale ngasemva. Kakhulu ngokukhankasela kwilizwe elithe tyaba.

10. La ngamasebe amane aluncedo olwazi lomkhosi awathi enze ukuba uMlawuli oMthubi oyise abalawuli abane abaliqela.

11. Yonke imikhosi ikhetha indawo ephakamileyo kuneendawo eziphantsi nezinelanga kumnyama.

12 Ukuba uthe wawalusa amadoda akho, wamisa kuloo ndawo inzima, wosuka umkhosi ungabi nasifo kuzo zonke, kube lusindiso.

13. Xa ufika endulini okanye ebhankini, hlala kwicala elinelanga, kunye nethambeka elingasemva kwakho ngasekunene. Ke uya kuthi kwangoko wenzele uncedo lwamajoni akho kwaye usebenzise izibonelelo zendalo zomhlaba.

14. Xa, ngenxa yeemvula ezinkulu elizweni, umlambo onqwenela ukuwuwela udumbile kwaye uzele ugwebu, kufuneka ulinde de wothe.

15. Ilizwe apho kukho amawa amakhulu anemilambo ehamba phakathi, imingxunya yendalo enzulu, iindawo ezixineneyo, iintshinyela eziphitheneyo, iingxondorha neengxondorha, kufuneka zishiywe nazo zonke isantya esinokwenzeka kwaye zingasondelwa.

16 Ngelixa sithe qelele kwiindawo ezinjalo, sifanele sifune utshaba lusondele kuzo; ngelixa sijongene nazo, kufuneka sivumele utshaba lube nazo ngasemva kwalo.

17 Ukuba kukho intuba kummandla wenkampu yenu, amachibi ajikelezwe yingca yasemanzini, nezitya zokuhlambela ezizaliswe ziingcongolo, okanye iintshinyela zemithi eshinyeneyo, kufuneka akhutshelwe phandle, agqogqwe; kuba ezi ziindawo apho abantu abalaleleyo okanye iintlola ezinobuqhophololo zidla ngokuzilalela khona.

18. Xa utshaba lusondele kwaye luhlala luzolile, luthembele kumandla emvelo wesikhundla salo.

19 Xa ithe yazikhwebula ifuna ukuxhokonxa idabi, ingxamele ukuba ihambele phambili;

20. Ukuba indawo yakhe yokumisa inkampu ifikeleleka lula, uthengisa isithiyelo.

21. Ukushukuma phakathi kwemithi yehlathi kubonisa ukuba utshaba luhambela phambili. Ukubonakala kwenani lezikrini phakathi kwengca eshinyeneyo kuthetha ukuba utshaba lufuna ukusenza sikrokre.

22 Ukunyuka kweentaka xa zibhabha luphawu lwe-ambuscade. Izilo ezothukileyo zibonisa ukuba uhlaselo lwequbuliso luyeza.

23 Xa kukho uthuli eluthulini eludongeni, oko kungumqondiso weenqwelo zokulwa; xa uthuli luphantsi, kodwa lusasazeke kwindawo ebanzi, lubonisa indlela yokuzalwa. Xa igalela kumacala ahlukeneyo, ibonisa ukuba amaqela athunyelwe ukuba aye kutheza iinkuni. Amafu ambalwa othuli ahla enyuka abonisa ukuba umkhosi umise iintente.

24. Amazwi athobekileyo kunye namalungiselelo awandisiweyo yimiqondiso yokuba utshaba sele luza kuhambela phambili. Ulwimi olunogonyamelo nokuqhubela phambili ngokungathi usiya kuhlaselo luphawu lokuba uya kurhoxa.

25 Xa iinqwelo zokulwa ezilula ziphuma kuqala zize zime ngamaphiko, oko kungumqondiso wokuba utshaba luqulunqa imfazwe.

26. Izindululo zoxolo ezingakhatshwa ngumnqophiso ofungelweyo zibonisa iyelenqe.

27. Xa kukho ukubaleka okukhulu kwaye amajoni awela kwizikhundla, oko kuthetha ukuba ixesha elinzima lifikile.

28. Xa abanye bebonwa behambela phambili kwaye abanye behlehla, ngumtsalane.

29 Xa amajoni emi ayame ngezikhali, atyhafile kukuswela kukudla.

30 Ukuba abo bathunywe ukuya kukha amanzi baqala ngokusela ngokwabo, umkhosi unxaniwe.

31. Ukuba utshaba lubona inzuzo enokufunyanwa kwaye alwenzi mzamo wokuyikhusela, amajoni aphelile.

32 Ukuba iintaka zabuthana naphi na, ayihlali; Ingxokozelo yasebusuku ibonisa ukuba novalo.
33. Ukuba kukho ukuphazamiseka enkampini, igunya lenjengele libuthathaka. Ukuba iibhanile kunye neeflegi zitshintshiwe, uvukelo luyaqhubeka. Ukuba amagosa anomsindo, oko kuthetha ukuba amadoda adiniwe.

34 Xa impi iwadlisa amahashe awo ngengqolowa, iinkomo zayo zixhelelwa kukudla, bangazixhomi iimbiza zabo phezu kweminquba, ukuba abasayi kubuyela ezintenteni zabo, yazi ukuba bangxamele ukulwa bafe.

35. Ukubona amadoda esebeza kunye ngamaqhina amancinane okanye ethetha ngeethowuni ezithotyiweyo kukhomba ukunganeliseki phakathi kwenqanaba nefayile.

36. Ukuvuzwa rhoqo kubonisa ukuba utshaba lusekupheleni kobutyebi balo; Izohlwayo ezininzi zibonisa imeko yonxunguphalo.

37 Ukuqala ngebhulubhu, kodwa emva koko ukoyikiswa ngamanani otshaba, kubonisa ukunqongophala okukhulu kobukrelekrele.

38. Xa abathunywa bethunyelwe nezincomo emilonyeni yabo, luphawu lokuba utshaba lunqwenela uxolo.

39 Ukuba imikhosi yotshaba inyuke ngomsindo kwaye ihlale ijongene nowethu ixesha elide ngaphandle kokungenela edabini okanye ukuzikhulula kwakhona, imeko leyo ifuna ukuphaphela okukhulu kunye nokuphambuka.

40 Ukuba imikhosi yethu ayisekho ngenani kunotshaba, yanele loo nto; kuthetha kuphela ukuba akukho luhlaselo lungqalileyo lunokwenziwa. Into esinokuyenza kukugxila nje kuwo onke amandla ethu akhoyo, sihlale sijonge utshaba, kwaye sifumane ukomelezwa.

41 Ongazicingeliyo ngenxa engaphambili, ebaqhatha abachasayo, uya kubanjwa ngabo;

42 Ukuba uthe amajoni athe ahamba engekabopheli, akayi kuthobela; kwaye, ngaphandle kokuba kuthotyelwe, kuya kuba yinto engento. Ukuba, xa amajoni sele enamathele kuwe, izohlwayo azinyanzeliswa, ziya kuba njalo ngaphandle kokuba.

43 Ngoko ke amajoni kufuneka aphathwe ngokwabantu kuqala, kodwa agcinwe elugcinweni ngokwengqeqesho yentsimbi. Le yindlela ethile eya empumelelweni.

44 Ukuba ekuqeqesheni amajoni imiyalelo iyanyanzeliswa ngokwesiqhelo, umkhosi uya kuziphatha kakuhle; ukuba akunjalo, uqeqesho lwaso luya kuba lubi.

45 Ukuba injengele ibonakalisa intembelo kumadoda ayo kodwa isoloko inyanzelisa ukuba ithotyelwe imiyalelo yakhe, ingeniso iya kuba macala omabini.

X. Umhlaba

1. USun Tzu wathi: Sisenokwahlula iindidi ezintandathu zomhlaba, oko kukuthi: (1) Umhlaba ofikelelekayo; (2) umhlaba obambekayo; (3) umhlaba wexeshana; (4) iindlela ezimxinwa; (5) iindawo eziphakamileyo; (6) izikhundla kumgama omkhulu ukusuka kutshaba.

2. Umhlaba onokuthi ugqitywe ngokukhululekileyo ngamacala omabini ubizwa ngokuba yi-accessible.

3. Ngokubhekiselele kumhlaba wolu hlobo, yiba phambi kotshaba kwindawo ephakanyisiweyo kunye nelanga, kwaye ugade ngononophelo umgca wakho wokubonelela. Emva koko uya kukwazi ukulwa ngenzuzo.

4. Umhlaba onokuthi ushiywe kodwa kunzima ukuwuhlalisa ubizwa ngokuba kukuthandela.

5. Ukusuka kwisikhundla esilolu hlobo, ukuba utshaba alulungele, unokuhamba kwaye uloyise. Kodwa ukuba utshaba lulungiselelwe ukuza kwakho, kwaye uyasilela ukumoyisa, emva koko, ukubuya akunakwenzeka, kuya kulandela intlekele.

6. Xa imeko injalo ukuba akukho cala liya kuzuza ngokwenza intshukumo yokuqala, kubizwa ngokuba yi- temporizing ground.

7 Kwimeko yolu hlobo, nangona utshaba lufanele lusinike isithiyelo esinomtsalane, kuya kucetyiswa ukuba singashukumi, kodwa kunokuba sihlehle, ngaloo ndlela luhende utshaba ekujikeni kwalo; ngoko, xa inxalenye yomkhosi wakhe iphumile, sinokuyihlangula ngokuyingenelo uhlaselo lwethu.

8. Ngokubhekiselele kwiindlela ezimxinwa, ukuba unokuhlala kuzo kuqala, zivumele zigcinwe ngokuqinileyo kwaye zilindele ukufika kotshaba.

9. Ukuba umkhosi uthe wakunqanda ukuba uthabathe ipasi, ungamlandeli ukuba ipasile ivalwe ngokupheleleyo, kodwa kuphela xa ibanjwe buthathaka.

10. Ngokubhekiselele kwiindawo eziphakamileyo eziphakamileyo, ukuba ungaphambili kunye notshaba lwakho, kufuneka uhlale kwiindawo eziphakamileyo kunye nelanga, kwaye ulinde ukuba eze.

11 Ukuba utshaba luthe lwakuxhobela phambi kwakho, musa ukululandela; rhoxa, uzame ukululukuhla.

12 Ukuba uthe wamisa kude notshaba, namandla ale mikhosi yomibini alingana, akulula ukuxhokonxa idabi, ukudabi kukwenzakalisa.

13. Le mithandathu yimigaqo edityaniswe noMhlaba. Injengele ethe yafumana isikhundla esinoxanduva kufuneka ilumke iwafunde.
14. Ngoku umkhosi ujamelene neentlekele ezintandathu eziliqela, ezingaveliyo ngenxa yezizathu zendalo, kodwa ngenxa yeziphoso ezithwalwa yinjengele. Ezi zezi: (1) Ukubhabha; (2) ukungathobeli; (3) ukuwa; (4) intshabalalo; (5) ukungalungelelani; (6) indlela.

15. Eminye imiqathango ilingana, ukuba enye imikhosi iphoswe kwelinye ngokuphindwe kalishumi ubukhulu bayo, umphumo uya kuba kukubaleka kweyokuqala.

16. Xa amajoni aqhelekileyo omelele kakhulu kwaye amagosa awo abuthathaka kakhulu, umphumo kukungathobeli. Xa amagosa omelele kakhulu kwaye amajoni aqhelekileyo abuthathaka kakhulu, umphumo uba kukuwohloka.

17. Xa amagosa aphezulu enomsindo yaye engathobeki, yaye ekudibaneni notshaba alwe ngenxa yemvakalelo yawo yokucaphuka, ngaphambi kokuba umthetheli-mkhosi achaze enoba ukwimeko yokulwa kusini na, umphumo uba yintshabalalo.

18. Xa injengele ibuthathaka kwaye ingenagunya; xa imiyalelo yakhe ingacacanga yaye ingacacanga; xa kungekho misebenzi yokulungisa eyabelwe amagosa kunye namadoda, kwaye izikhundla zenziwe ngokungenabuqhetseba, umphumo kukungalungelelani ngokupheleleyo.

19. Xa injengele, engakwaziyo ukuqikelela amandla otshaba, ivumela umkhosi ongaphantsi ukuba ubandakanyeke omkhulu, okanye iphose ibutho elibuthathaka nxamnye nonamandla, ize ingakuhoyi ukubeka amajoni anyuliweyo kwindawo engaphambili, isiphumo imele ukuba ibe yeyothusayo.

20. Ezi ziindlela ezintandathu zokoyiswa, ekufuneka ziqatshelwe ngononophelo yinjengele ethe yafumana isikhundla esinoxanduva.

21. Ubume bendalo belizwe lelona qabane libalaseleyo lejoni; kodwa amandla okuqikelela umchasi, okulawula imikhosi yoloyiso, kunye nokubala ngobuqili ubunzima, iingozi kunye nemigama, lenza uvavanyo lwenjengele enkulu.

22 Ozaziyo ezo zinto, abe ekwenza eludabini eludakeni lwakhe, uya kweyisa amadabi akhe; Lowo ungazaziyo, engazenzi, uya koyiswa ngokuqinisekileyo.

23 Ukuba ukulwa kuthe koyise, woyisa, nilwe, nokuba uthe wakuthintela umlawuli; ukuba ukulwa anithanga nibe noloyiso, ize ningalwi nangomyalelo womlawuli.

24 Injengele ehambela phambili inganqweneli udumo ize ihlehle ingoyiki ihlazo, ekukuphela kwayo ingcamango ekukhuseleni ilizwe layo nokwenza inkonzo entle kukumkani wayo, likhazikhazi lobukumkani.

25 Wagqaleni amajoni akho njengoonyana benu, yaye aya kunilandela kweyona ntili inzulu; nibajonge njengoonyana benu abathandekayo, yaye baya kuma ngakuni kude kuse ekufeni.

26 Ke ukuba uyayekelela, wesuka akwaba nako ukulimisa igunya lakho; unobubele, kodwa awukwazi ukuyithobela imiyalelo yakho; kwaye engenakukwazi, ngaphezu koko, yokuphelisa isidubedube: ke amajoni akho kufuneka afaniswe nabantwana abaphangisiweyo; azinamsebenzi kuyo nayiphi na injongo esebenzayo.

27 Ukuba siyazi ukuba amadoda ethu akwimeko yokuhlasela, abe engazi ukuba utshaba aluvuyeli ukusihlasela, sisuke sahamba isiqingatha soloyiso.

28 Ukuba siyazi ukuba utshaba luvuleleke ukuba luhlasele, kodwa asazi ukuba amadoda ethu akakho kwimeko yokuhlasela, siye sahamba nje isiqingatha soloyiso.

29. Ukuba siyazi ukuba utshaba luvuleleke ukuba luhlasele, kwaye siyazi nokuba amadoda ethu akwimeko yokuhlasela, kodwa singazi ukuba ubume bomhlaba benza ukuba ukulwa kungenzeki, sisahamba nje isiqingatha soloyiso.

30. Yiyo ke loo nto ijoni elinamava, xa lisenza intshukumo, alikhe libhideke; xa sele eyiqhekezile inkampu, akabi nakulahlekelwa.

31 Kungoko ilizwi eli lithi, Ukuba uyalwazi utshaba, kwaye uyazazi, uloyiso lwakho aluyi kuba nantandabuzo; ukuba uyalazi iZulu kwaye uyawazi umhlaba, ungenza uloyiso lwakho luphelele.

XI. Iimeko eziliThoba

1. USun Tzu wathi: Ubugcisa bemfazwe baqaphela iindidi ezisithoba zomhlaba: (1) Umhlaba osasazekileyo; (2) umhlaba olula; (3) ingxabano; (4) umhlaba ovulekileyo; (5) umhlaba woohola bendlela abangenelayo; (6) umhlaba onzulu; (7) umhlaba onzima; (8) umhlaba obiyelweyo; (9) umhlaba ophelelwe lithemba.

2. Xa inkosi isilwa kwindawo yaso, ngumhlaba ochithakeleyo.

3 Xa engene kumhlaba ochasayo, kodwa umgama ongephi, kuba lula.

4. Umhlaba ongenisa inzuzo enkulu kuwo omabini amacala, ngumhlaba wengxabano.

5. Umhlaba apho icala ngalinye linenkululeko yokushukuma ngumhlaba ovulekileyo.

6. Umhlaba owenza isitshixo kumazwe amathathu adityanisiweyo, ukuze lowo uhleli kuwo kuqala abe nobuninzi boBukhosi ngokomyalelo wakhe, ngumhlaba weendlela ezingenelayo.
7 Xa umkhosi ungenile embindini welizwe elinobutshaba, ushiya inkitha yezixeko ezinqatyisiweyo ngasemva, kuba ngumhlaba omkhulu.

8. Amahlathi asezintabeni, imixawuka, imigxobhozo neefens--lonke ilizwe ekunzima ukunqumla kulo: lo ngumhlaba onzima.

9. Umhlaba ofikelelwa kwimiwonyo emxinwa, kwaye apho sinokuthi sithathe umhlala-phantsi kuphela ngeendlela ezinobunzima, ukuze inani elincinane lotshaba libe nokwanela ukutyumza iqela elikhulu lamadoda ethu: oku kubhijelwe emhlabeni.

10. Umhlaba esinokusindiswa kuwo kuphela kwintshabalalo ngokulwa ngaphandle kokulibazisa, ngumhlaba ophelelwe lithemba.

11 Ngoko ke, kumhlaba othe saa, musani ukulwa. Kumhlaba olula, musa ukuma. Kwimeko yengxabano, musa ukuhlasela.

12. Kumhlaba ovulekileyo, musa ukuzama ukuvala indlela yotshaba. Kumhlaba weendlela ezingenelayo, bamba izandla kunye namahlakani akho.

13 Qokelelani amaxhoba; Kumhlaba onzima, qhubeka ngokuthe chu kuhambo.

14. Kumhlaba oxinanisiweyo, bhenela kwiqhinga. Kumhlaba ophelelwe lithemba, yilwa.

15 Abo ke babebizwa ngokuba ziinkokeli zamandulo, babekwazi ukucanda phakathi komphambili nomva wotshaba; ukuthintela intsebenziswano phakathi kwezahlulo zakhe ezinkulu nezincinci; ukuze bathintele abalungileyo ekuhlanguleni ababi, abathetheli ekuhlanganiseni amadoda abo.

16 Xa amadoda eentshaba emanyene, akwazi ukuwagcina esiphithiphithi.

17 Ke kaloku, kwakubon' ukuba kukulungele, baqhubela phambili; xa kungenjalo, bema bhuxe.

18. Ukuba ndibuzwa indlela yokumelana nomkhosi omkhulu wotshaba ngokulungeleleneyo kwanakwinqanaba lokumatsha ukuya kuhlaselo, bendiya kuthi: “Qala ngokuthabatha into oyixabisileyo umchasi wakho;

19. Ukukhawuleza ngundoqo wemfazwe: thatha ithuba lokungalungeli kotshaba, yenza indlela yakho ngeendlela ezingalindelekanga, kwaye uhlasele iindawo ezingagadwanga.

20. Oku kulandelayo yimigaqo efanele ukuqwalaselwa ngumkhosi ohlaselayo: Okukhona ungena ngakumbi kwilizwe, kokukhona kuya kuba mkhulu umanyano lwemikhosi yakho, kwaye ngaloo ndlela abakhuseli abayi kukoyisa ngokuchasene nawe.

21 Qokelela ilizwe elityebileyo, ukuba uwuxhase umkhosi wakho ngokutya;

22 Yiqondeni kakuhle impilo yamadoda enu, ningawarhafi. Gxininisa amandla akho kwaye ugcine amandla akho. Gcina umkhosi wakho ngokuqhubekayo, kwaye uyile izicwangciso ezingenakuqondakala.

23. Phoseni amajoni akho kwiindawo apho kungekho kusaba, kwaye baya kukhetha ukufa kunokusaba. Ukuba baya kujongana nokufa, akukho nto bangayi kuyifumana. Amagosa namadoda ngokufanayo baya kukhupha amandla abo.

24 Amajoni xa esengxakini enkulu aphelelwa luloyiko. Ukuba akukho ndawo yokusabela, baya kuma beqinile. Ukuba bakwilizwe elinobutshaba, baya kubonisa inkani. Ukuba akukho ncedo kuyo, baya kulwa nzima.

25. Ngaloo ndlela, ngaphandle kokulinda ukukhokelwa, amajoni aya kuhlala ethe gqolo ekulweni; ngaphandle kokulinda ukucelwa, baya kwenza intando yakho; ngaphandle kwemiqathango, baya kuthembeka; ngaphandle komyalelo, banokuthenjwa.

26 Wathintele ukuhlaba izihlabo, uwaphelise namathandabuzo eenkolelo. Ngoko, de kufike ukufa, akukho ntlekele imele yoyike.

27. Ukuba amajoni ethu akasindwa yimali, akungenxa yokuba ebucaphukela ubutyebi; ukuba ubomi babo abukho bubude ngokungafanelekanga, akubangelwa kukuba bengafuni ukuphila ubomi obude.

28 Ngemini yokuphuma kwemfazwe, amajoni akho aya kulila, abo bahleli phantsi benxibe iingubo zabo, nabaleleyo behle iinyembezi ezidleleni zabo. Kodwa mabaziswe kube kanye, kwaye baya kubonisa isibindi seChu okanye iKuei.

29 Ichule elinobuchule lisenokufaniswa neshuai-jan. Ngoku i-shuai-jan yinyoka efumaneka kwiintaba zeChUng. Yibethe entlokweni yayo, wena ukurhole ngomsila wayo; yibethe emsileni, wena uya kukuhlasela ngentloko; betha embindini wayo, kwaye uya kuhlaselwa ngentloko nomsila zombini.

30 Ukubuzwa ukuba umkhosi unokwenziwa ukuba uxelise i-shuai-jan, ndimele ndiphendule, Ewe. Ngokuba amadoda kaWu namadoda kaYuhe aziintshaba; ukanti ukuba bawela umlambo ngephenyane elinye baze babanjwe sisaqhwithi, baya kuncedana kanye njengokuba isandla sasekhohlo sinceda ngasekunene.

31 Ke akwanelanga ukukholosa ngokubophelela amahashe, nangokumbela emhlabeni amavili eenqwelo.

32. Umgaqo wokulawula umkhosi kukuseka umgangatho omnye wenkalipho ekufuneka wonke umntu efikelele kuyo.
33. Ukwenziwa njani okona kulungileyo kokubini elomeleleyo kwanobuthathaka—ngumbuzo obandakanya ukusetyenziswa komhlaba ngokufanelekileyo.

34 Ngaloo ndlela injengele enobuchule iyawuqhuba umkhosi wayo, ngokungathi ikhokela indoda emnye, ngokungafihlisiyo, ngesandla.

35. Ngumsebenzi wenjengele ukuba azole kwaye ngaloo ndlela aqinisekise imfihlo; ithe tye kwaye inobulungisa, kwaye ngaloo ndlela igcina ucwangco.

36 Woba nako ukubaza amagosa akhe namadoda akhe ngeengxelo ezingento, nangembonakalo, abagcine ekungazi nto.

37 Ngokuwaguqula ke amalungiselelo akhe, ewatshintsha amacebo akhe, ugcina utshaba engenalwazi luqinisekileyo. Ngokushenxisa inkampu yakhe nangokuhamba ngeendlela ezijikelezayo, uthintela utshaba ekubeni lungayilindelanga injongo yakhe.

38 Ngexesha eliyingozi, inkokeli yomkhosi yenza njengalowo unyuke waya kwindawo ephakamileyo aze ayikhabe ileli emva kwayo. Uthwala amadoda akhe nzulu kwindawo enobutshaba ngaphambi kokuba abonise isandla sakhe.

39 Iinqanawa zakhe uzitshisa, aziqhekeze iimbiza zakhe; njengomalusi eqhuba umhlambi weegusha, ubaqhubela ngapha nangapha abantu bakhe, kungazi apho aya khona.

40 Ukuhlanganisa umkhosi wakhe aze awufake emngciphekweni: Oku kunokubizwa ngokuba ngumsebenzi wenjengele.

41. Imilinganiselo eyahlukeneyo ifanele iintlobo ezilithoba zomhlaba; ukuba luncedo kwamaqhinga obundlobongela okanye okuzikhusela; kunye nemithetho esisiseko yendalo yomntu: ezi zizinto ekufuneka ngokuqinisekileyo zihlolisiswe.

42. Xa uhlasela ummandla onobutshaba, umgaqo jikelele kukuba, ukungena nzulu kuzisa umanyano; ukungena kodwa indlela emfutshane ithetha ukusasazwa.

43. Xa ushiya ilizwe lakho ngasemva, kwaye uthabathe umkhosi wakho unqumle ummandla wasebumelwaneni, uzifumana ukumhlaba obalulekileyo. Xa kukho iindlela zokunxibelelana macala omane, umhlaba uba ngoohola beendlela.

44. Xa ungena ngokunzulu elizweni, ngumhlaba onzulu. Xa ungena ngendlela encinci, kuba lula.

45 Xa uthe utshaba lwakho luneenqaba ngasemva, kwaye uthe waxinana phambi kwakho, uya kuba ngumhlaba omxinwa. Xa kungekho ndawo yokuzimela konke konke, ngumhlaba ongenathemba.

46 Ke ngoko, kumhlaba othe saa, ndandiya kuwaphefumlela amadoda am ngobunye benjongo. Kumhlaba olula, ndiya kubona ukuba kukho unxibelelwano olusondeleyo phakathi kwazo zonke iindawo zomkhosi wam.

47 Ngeendawo ezibambeneyo, ndisuke ndakhawuleza, emva kwam.

48 Emhlabeni ovulekileyo, bendiya kuzigcina esweni izikhuselo zam. Kumhlaba ongenela uhola wendlela, ndandiza kudibanisa izivumelwano zam.

49. Kumhlaba onzima, ndingazama ukuqinisekisa uthotho lwezibonelelo eziqhubekayo. Kumhlaba onzima, ndandimana ndityhala endleleni.

50. Kumhlaba oxinanisiweyo, ndingavala nayiphi na indlela yokurhoxa. Kumhlaba ophelelwe lithemba, ndandidla ngokuvakalisa kumajoni am ukungabi nathemba kokusindisa ubomi bawo.

51 Kuba kuyisimo sengqondo sejoni ukuchasa ngenkani xa lirhangqwe, lilwe ngamandla xa lingenakuzinceda, nokuthobela ngokukhawuleza xa liwile engozini.

52. Asinakungena kumanyano neenkosana ezingabamelwane de sibe siqhelane nobuchule bazo. Asifanelekanga ukukhokela umkhosi kumngcelele ngaphandle kokuba siqhelene nobuso belizwe--iintaba namahlathi alo, imigibe namawa alo, imigxobhozo nemigxobhozo. Asiyi kukwazi ukujika izibonelelo zendalo kwiakhawunti ngaphandle kokuba sisebenzisa izikhokelo zasekhaya.

53. Ukungahoywa kwayo nayiphi na imigaqo emine okanye emihlanu elandelayo ayifanelanga inkosana ethanda imfazwe.

54. Xa inkosana ethanda imfazwe ihlasela ilizwe elinamandla, i-generalship yayo izibonakalisa ekuthinteleni ukuxinana kwemikhosi yotshaba. Uyazoyika iintshaba zakhe, yaye amahlakani abo ayathintelwa ukuba azithelele nxamnye naye.

55. Ngenxa yoko akazabalazeli ukuzimanya kunye nabo bonke, kwaye akakhuthazi amandla amanye amazwe. Wenza ezakhe izinto ezifihlakeleyo, egcina abachasi bakhe besoyika. Ngaloo ndlela uyakwazi ukuthimba izixeko zabo aze abhukuqe izikumkani zabo.

56. Ukunika amabhaso kungajongwanga umgaqo, kukhuphe iiodolo ngaphandle kokujonga amalungiselelo angaphambili; yaye uya kukwazi ukulawula umkhosi wonke ngokungathi unendoda enye.

57 Yitshoni kumajoni akho; ungaze ubazise uyilo lwakho. Ekukhanyeni kwembonakalo, wuzise phambi kwamehlo abo; kodwa ungabaxeleli nto xa imeko imbi.

58 Wubeke umkhosi wakho esichengeni sokufa, kwaye uya kusinda; yiphose kwingxingongo enqabileyo, kwaye iya kuphuma ikhuselekile.

59 Kuba wothi amandla athe akuwa endleleni enokwenzakala, onokuthi abe nako ukumbetha uloyiso.
60. Impumelelo emfazweni ifunyanwa ngokuzamkela ngononophelo injongo yotshaba.

61. Ngokuzingisa ukujinga kwiflanki yotshaba, siya kuphumelela ekuhambeni kwexesha ekubulaleni umphathi-mkhosi.

62 Oku kubizwa ngokuba kukukwazi ukwenza into ngobuqhophololo.

63 Ngomhla othe wathabatha umyalelo wakho, vala iindawo ezidlulayo, utshabalalise amanani asemthethweni, kwaye umise ukudlula kwabo bonke abathunywa.

64 Yibani ngqongqo endaweni yentlanganiso yamatyala, ukuze niyilawule imeko;

65 Ukuba utshaba lushiya ucango luvuliwe, kufuneka ungene ngokukhawuleza.

66. Thetha umchasi wakho ngokubamba into ayithandayo, kwaye ngobuchule uyilele ixesha lokufika kwakhe emhlabeni.

67. Hamba kwindlela echazwe ngumgaqo, kwaye uzihlalise kutshaba de ube ukwazi ukulwa idabi eliqinisekileyo.

68 Ngoko bonakalisa ubunyulu bentombi ekuqaleni, lude utshaba lukunike ithuba; emva koko linganisa ukukhawuleza komvundla obalekayo, kwaye kuya kuba semva kwexesha ukuba utshaba likuchase.

XII. Uhlaselo Lomlilo

1. USun Tzu uthe: Zintlanu iindlela zokuhlasela ngomlilo. Eyokuqala kukutshisa amajoni eminqubeni yawo; eyesibini kukutshisa oovimba; eyesithathu kukutshisa oololiwe bemithwalo; eyesine kukutshisa amaziko ezixhobo namaphephancwadi; eyesihlanu kukuwisa umlilo phakathi kotshaba.

2. Ukuze senze uhlaselo, kufuneka sibe neendlela ezikhoyo. Izinto zokuphakamisa umlilo kufuneka zihlale zilungile.

3 Kukho ixesha elifanelekileyo lokuhlasela ngomlilo, kunye neentsuku ezikhethekileyo zokuqalisa umlilo.

4. Ixesha elifanelekileyo lonyaka kuxa imozulu yomile kakhulu; iintsuku ezikhethekileyo zezo xa inyanga ikumakroza eSieve, uDonga, iPhiko okanye iCross-bar; kuba ezo zone ziyimihla yomoya ovuthuzayo.

5. Ekuhlaseleni ngomlilo, umntu kufuneka alungele ukuhlangabezana nezinto ezintlanu ezinokubakho:

6. (1) Xa kuqhambuka umlilo ngaphakathi kwinkampu yotshaba, phendula ngoko nangoko ngohlaselo oluvela ngaphandle.

7. (2) Ukuba kukho ukugqabhuka komlilo, kodwa amajoni otshaba ahlala ethule, bide ixesha lakho kwaye ungahlaseli.

8. (3) Xa amandla omlilo efikelele kumphakamo wawo, wulandele ngohlaselo, ukuba oko kunokwenzeka; ukuba akunjalo, hlala apho ukhona.

9. (4) Ukuba kunokwenzeka ukwenza uhlaselo lomlilo ngaphandle, musa ukulinda ukuba ugqabhuke ngaphakathi, kodwa nikela uhlaselo lwakho ngexesha elifanelekileyo.

10. (5) Xa ubasa umlilo, yiba secaleni kwawo. Musa ukuhlasela kwi-leeward.

11 Umoya ovuka emini uthabatha ixesha elide;

12 Kumkhosi ngamnye, uphuhliso oluhlanu olunxulumene nomlilo lumele lwaziwe, ukushukuma kweenkwenkwezi kubalwe, nomlindo ogcinelwe iintsuku ezifanelekileyo.

13 Kungoko abo basebenzisa umlilo njengoncedo kuhlaselo babonakalisa ubulumko; abo basebenzisa amanzi njengoncedo kuhlaselo bafumana ukomelela.

14 Ngamanzi, utshaba lunokuthintelwa, kodwa aluphangwa yonke impahla yalo.

15. Ayonwabanga ikamva lomntu ozama ukuphumelela amadabi akhe kwaye aphumelele kuhlaselo lwakhe ngaphandle kokuhlakulela umoya woshishino; kuba isiphumo yinkcitho yexesha kunye nokudodobala ngokubanzi.

16 Kungoko eli lizwi lithi, Umlawuli olikhanyo ulicwangcisa kakuhle icebo lakhe; injengele elungileyo iyabuhlakulela ubuncwane bakhe.

17 Musani ukushukuma, ukuba anithanga nibone okulungileyo; musani ukusebenzisa imikhosi yenu, ngaphandle kokuba kukho okuthile okuza kuzuza; ungalwi ngaphandle kokuba isikhundla sibalulekile.

18 Akukho mlawuli uya kugalela umkhosi, ukuba anelise udakada lwakhe; akukho njengele kufuneka ilwe idabi ngaphandle nje kokuphazamiseka.

19 Ukuba kukulungele wena, dlulela phambili; ukuba akunjalo, hlala apho ukhona.

20 Ekuhambeni kwexesha umsindo uya kuba luvuyo; Ukucaphuka kunokuphumelela ngumxholo.

21 Ke ubukumkani, obo butshabaleleyo, abuyi kubuya ubekho; nabafileyo abanakuze babuyiselwe ebomini.

22. Ngenxa yoko umlawuli okhanyiselweyo uyathobela, kwaye injengele elungileyo izele lunonophelo. Le yindlela yokugcina ilizwe linoxolo kunye nomkhosi womelele.

XIII. Ukusetyenziswa kweentlola

1. USun Tzu uthe: Ukukhulisa inkitha yamadoda alikhulu lamawaka nokuwahambela imigama emide kubandakanya ilahleko enzima ebantwini kunye nokuchitha ubutyebi bukaRhulumente. Ixabiso lemihla ngemihla liya kuba liwaka leeshekele zesilivere. Kuya kubakho isiphithiphithi ekhaya naphesheya, yaye amadoda aya kuwa ediniwe ezindleleni. Ziya kuthi xhaxhe ngeentsapho ezingamakhulu asixhenxe amawaka emsebenzini wazo.
2. Imikhosi enobutshaba isenokujongana iminyaka, izabalazela uloyiso olugqitywe ngosuku olunye. Oku kuba njalo, ukuhlala ungayazi imeko yotshaba ngenxa nje yokuba ubani ekruqukile ukukhupha ikhulu leekhilogram zesilivere kwiiwonga nemivuzo, bubudenge bokungabi nabuntu.

3 Lowo wenza ngolo hlobo akayonkokeli yamadoda, akaloncedo olukhoyo kumlawuli wakhe, akakho inkosi yoloyiso.

4. Ngaloo ndlela, into eyenza ukuba umlawuli osisilumko nenjengele elungileyo abethe aze oyise, aze aphumeze izinto ezingaphaya kwamandla abantu abaqhelekileyo, kukwazi kusengaphambili.

5 Ngoku oku ukwazi ngenxa engaphambili akunakuvuswa kwimimoya; ayinakufunyanwa ngokunyanzeliswa ngamava, nangaluphi na ubalo lokuxhuzula.

6. Ulwazi lwezimilo zotshaba lunokufunyanwa kuphela kwabanye abantu.

7. Kungoko kusetyenziswa iintlola, ekuziindidi ezintlanu kuzo: (1) Iintlola zasekuhlaleni; (2) iintlola ezingaphakathi; (3) iintlola eziguqukileyo; (4) iintlola ezigwetyiweyo; (5) iintlola ezisindileyo.

8. Xa ezi ntlobo zintlanu zentlola zonke zisebenza, akukho namnye unokufumanisa inkqubo eyimfihlo. Oku kubizwa ngokuba "kulawulo lobuthixo lwemisonto." Lelona khono lixabisekileyo likaMongami.

9. Ukuba neentlola zasekuhlaleni kuthetha ukuqesha iinkonzo zabahlali besithili.

10 Ukuba neentlola zangaphakathi, ezisebenzisa amagosa otshaba.

11 Ukuba neentlola eziguqukileyo, ukubamba iintlola zotshaba size sizisebenzisele iinjongo zethu.

12 Ukuba neentlola ezigwetyiweyo, sisenza izinto ezithile ekuhleni ngeenjongo zokukhohlisa, nokuvumela iintlola zethu ukuba zazi ngazo kwaye zixele kutshaba.

13 Iintlola ezisindileyo, ekugqibeleni, ngabo bazisa iindaba ezivela kwinkampu yotshaba.

14. Yiyo ke loo nto kumkhosi wonke kungabikho bani unobudlelwane obusenyongweni ngaphezu kweentlola. Akukho namnye ofanele avuzwe ngokukhululekileyo. Akukho nakwelinye ishishini ekufuneka imfihlo enkulu igcinwe.

15. Iintlola azinakusetyenziswa ngokufanelekileyo ngaphandle kobuchule obuthile obucacileyo.

16. Azinakulawulwa ngokufanelekileyo ngaphandle kobubele kunye nokuthe tye.

17. Ngaphandle kobuqili bengqondo, umntu akanako ukuqiniseka ngobunyani beengxelo zabo.

18. Yiba nobuqili! yiba nobuqili! kwaye usebenzise iintlola zakho kulo lonke uhlobo lweshishini.

19 Ukuba udaba oluyimfihlo luxelwe yintlola lingekavuthwa ixesha, makabulawe kunye naloo ndoda ixeliweyo kuye.

20. Enoba injongo kukutyumza umkhosi, ukugqogqa isixeko, okanye ukubulala umntu, kusoloko kuyimfuneko ukuqalisa ngokufumanisa amagama abalindi, abancedisi benkampu, nabagcini-mnyango nabalindi benjengele ephetheyo. Iintlola zethu kufuneka zithunywe ukuba ziqinisekise ezi zinto.

21. Iintlola zotshaba ezize kusihlola mazikhangelwe, zilingwe ngezinyobo, zirholwe, zihlaliswe kakuhle. Ngaloo ndlela baya kuba ziintlola eziguqukileyo kwaye zifumaneke kwinkonzo yethu.

22. Kungenxa yolwazi oluziswe yintlola eguqukileyo esikwaziyo ukuyifumana kwaye siqeshe iintlola zasekhaya kunye nezingaphakathi.

23 Kungenxa yolwazi lwakhe, kwakhona, ukuba sinokubangela intlola egwetyiweyo ukuba ise iindaba zobuxoki kutshaba.

24. Okokugqibela, kungenxa yolwazi lwakhe ukuba intlola esaphilayo ingasetyenziswa ngamaxesha amiselweyo.

25. Isiphelo kunye nenjongo yokuhlola kuzo zonke iindidi zayo ezintlanu kukwazi utshaba; kwaye olu lwazi lunokufunyanwa kuphela, okokuqala, kwintlola eguqukileyo. Kungoko kubalulekile ukuba intlola eguquliweyo iphathwe ngeyona nkululeko ibalaseleyo.

26 Kudala, ukuvela kobukhosi beYin kwakungenxa ka-I Chih owayekhonze phantsi kweHsia. Ngokunjalo, ukunyuka kobukhosi bakwaChou kwakungenxa kaLu Ya owayesebenze phantsi kweYin.

27. Yiyo loo nto kuphela umlawuli okhanyiselweyo kunye nenjengele elumkileyo eya kusebenzisa ubukrelekrele obuphezulu bomkhosi ngeenjongo zokuhlola kwaye ngaloo ndlela bazuze iziphumo ezinkulu. Iintlola zezona zinto zibalulekileyo emanzini, kuba kuzo kuxhomekeke kubuchule bomkhosi bokuhamba.
