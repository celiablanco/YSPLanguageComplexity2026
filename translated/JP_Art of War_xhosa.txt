Isahluko 1: Ukucwangcisa

USun Tzu uthe ijoni libalulekile kwilizwe, indawo yobomi nokufa, indlela yokuphila nokufa, kwaye kufuneka iqondwe.

Ke ngoko, ukuba ndihambe ngale nto, ndinezinto ezintlanu, kwaye ukuvavanya oku, ndinesicwangciso, kwaye ndikhangela imvakalelo. Eyokuqala ngumendo, eyesibini lizulu, eyesithathu ngumhlaba, eyesine yinjengele, eyesihlanu ngumthetho.

Imendo yeyokuba abantu bakwikhasi elinye nabo bangaphezulu kwabo, bafe nabo kwaye bahlale nabo, kwaye bangoyiki ingozi. Izulu yi-yin kunye ne-yang, ingqele kunye nobushushu, kunye namaxesha. Umhlaba ukude kwaye ukufuphi, unobungozi kwaye kulula, ubanzi kwaye umxinwa, kunye nobomi nokufa. Ngokubanzi kuthetha ubulumko, ukholo, ububele, inkalipho, nokuba ngqongqo. Umthetho yinkqubo, indlela esemthethweni, kunye nenjongo ephambili.

Nangona injengele ingazukuva ngaba bantu bahlanu, abayaziyo le nto baya kuphumelela, kwaye abangaziyo abayi kuphumelela. Ke ngoko, ndenze icebo lokungqina oku, kwaye ndakhangela uluvo. Kuthiwa inkosi ilumkile, injengele ifanelekile, izulu nomhlaba lifanelekile, umthetho wenziwa, amajoni anamandla, amajoni aqeqeshiwe, umvuzo nesohlwayo ziyabonakala. Siyawazi umphumo weli dabi.

Xa uphulaphule isicwangciso sam sekamva, ukuba uyasisebenzisa, uya kuphumelela ngokuqinisekileyo, ngoko musa ukunamathela kuso. Ukuba awumameli isicwangciso sam, uya kulahlekelwa ngokuqinisekileyo ukuba usebenzisa esi sicwangciso, musa ukusishiya. Ukuba isetyenziswa njengesicwangciso okanye inzuzo, iya kufumana umfutho kwaye incede abanye. Amandla athetha ukulawula amandla ngokusekelwe kwinzuzo.

Ijoni liqili.

Ngoko ke, ubuchule bubonisa ukungakwazi, ukusetyenziswa kubonisa ukungasebenzisi, kufutshane kubonisa kude, kude kubonisa kufuphi, ukusebenzisa i-advanteji ukuhenda, isiphithiphithi sokuhlutha, isiqhamo sokulungiselela, ukuqina kokuphepha, umsindo wokuphambuka, ubuphantsi ukwenza ibhongo, ngelixa usebenza, umzali ukuba ahlukane. Hlasela ukusilela kwabo kwaye ungabonakali ngokungalindelekanga. Olu luloyiso lwamajoni, yaye akukho mfuneko yokuba uxelelwe kuqala.

Abo basaphumelela ngokubala ngaphandle kokulwa kufuneka babe namanqaku amaninzi abawazuzayo. Abo bangaphumeleliyo ngokubala ngaphandle kokulwa abayi kuzuza kancinane ngokubala. Okukhona i-arithmetic iphumelela, i-arithmetic encinci ayiphumeleli. Ungandithethisi okanye undiyeke. Xa siqwalasela oku engqondweni, sinokubona umphumo.

Isahluko 2: Isicwangciso

Ngokutsho kweSun Tzu, umgaqo oqhelekileyo wokusetyenziswa komkhosi kukuchitha iiyen ezili-1 000 kwinqwelo yokulwa, iiyen ezili-1 000 kwinqwelo yokulwa, iili ezili-100 000 zesikrweqe, neeli ezili-1 000 ukuze kunikelwe ukutya, kuquka iindleko zasekhaya nezangaphandle, iindleko zeendwendwe, izinto zeglu nelacquer, nezikrweqe zeenqwelo zokulwa.

Ukuba usebenzisa elo dabi ixesha elide, liya kuwenza buthuntu amajoni akho kwaye liphazamise izakhono zakho ezibukhali. Ukuba uhlasela inqaba, uya kunikezela, kwaye ukuba uveza inkosi yakho ixesha elide, uya kuba yinto engenamsebenzi kwilizwe. Xa iyancipha amajoni, iphazamise ibukhali, yenza buthathaka amandla, kwaye itshabalalise imali, kwenzeka ukuba iinkosi zithatha inzuzo yemiphumo emibi. Nokuba kuthe kwakho umntu osisilumko, akayi kuyilungisa le nto.

Ngoko ke, nangona amajoni esiva ngokungxama kwawo, akanalo nofifi lokuba anezakhono ixesha elingakanani. Kudala singamajoni, kodwa akukabikho mntu uxhamlileyo kweli lizwe. Ngoko ke, nabani na ongazaziyo zonke iingozi zenkonzo yasemkhosini akanakuzazi nazo zonke iingenelo zenkonzo yasemkhosini.

Abo bawasebenzisa kakuhle amajoni abo abasayi kuphinda bakhonze, yaye abayi kunikwa ukutya okwesithathu.

Bathabatha ishishini labo elizweni kunye nokutya kwabo kutshaba. Ngoko ke, kufuneka kubekho imixhesho eyaneleyo yasemkhosini. Ukuba ilizwe lihlwempuzekile, kufuneka lihanjiswe kude. Ukuba zithunyelwa kude, abalimi baba ngamahlwempu. Abo basondeleyo kutitshala kufuneka bathengise iimpahla zabo. Ukuba uyathengisa, abalimi baya kuphulukana nobutyebi babo. Ukuba wayephulukene nobutyebi bakhe, ngesiquphe kwanyanzeleka ukuba ancame imali yakhe, yaye kumbindi wethafa, wayelishiya ikhaya elambatha, neendleko zomlimi, nesixhenxe kwabalishumi. Ixabiso lezikhulu zenkundla, iinqwelo zokulwa, namahashe, iintolo, neentonga, neenqwelo zokulwa, neenqwelo zokulwa, nezamahashe, nezahlulo ezintandathu kuzo. Ngoko ke, injengele elumkileyo iyayifeza injongo yayo yaye izondla ngotshaba. Ukutya ikhuba lotshaba olunye kufana namashumi amabini koku kum, kunye kokunye kokuphuma emthini kulingana namashumi amabini koku kum.
Ngoko ke, abo babulala iintshaba zabo baba nomsindo. Abo baxhaphaza utshaba yimali. Ke ngoko, ukuba uthe waphumelela emfazweni ishumi leenqwelo zokulwa, wovuza lowo uphumeleleyo kuqala, wandule ke ukuyiphakamisa ibhanile, uziqokelele iinqwelo zakho zokulwa, ukuba zikhwele phezu kwazo, ubadlise ngokwenza okulungileyo. Oku kubizwa ngokuba kukufumana amandla ngokoyisa utshaba.

Ngoko ke, amajoni ayaluxabisa uloyiso. Musa ukuxabisa ixesha elidlulileyo. Ngoko ke, injengele ewaziyo amajoni ayo yinkokeli yabantu nenkokeli yokhuseleko lwesizwe.

Isahluko sesi-3: Uhlaselo

Ngokuka-Sun Tzu, umthetho wenkonzo yasemkhosini ngowona ulungele ukuzalisekisa isizwe, kwaye okwesibini kuphela ukutshabalalisa.

Ukoyisa umkhosi yeyona nto ilungileyo, ukoyisa umkhosi ngowesibini. Ukugqibezela uhambo kokona kulungileyo, kwaye ukwaphula uhambo kuza kwindawo yesibini. Ukugqiba kukabani uthweso-zidanga kugqalwa kokona kulungileyo, yaye ukwaphula kungokwesibini. Ukugqiba ibakala lesi-5 kuthathwa njengeyona nto ibhetele, kwaye ukwaphula ibakala lesi-5 yeyona nto yesibini.

Ngenxa yesi sizathu, indoda ephumelele amadabi alikhulu kwaye iphumelele amadabi alikhulu ayiyonto ngaphandle kwendoda elungileyo. Yindoda elungileyo ezithoba kumajoni abantu ngaphandle kokulwa.

Ngoko ke, amajoni aphezulu ahlasela iyelenqe. Emva koko, sika isiphambuka. Emva koko, bulala amajoni. Ngezantsi koko, hlasela inqaba. Umthetho wokungqinga awunakuthintelwa.

Ukuba nobuchule kwii-turrets, ii-turrets, kunye nokukwazi ukusebenzisa izixhobo, kuya kwenzeka ngoMatshi. Umgama uya kwandiswa kwakhona ngoMatshi. Ukuba injengele ayinakoyisa utshaba kwaye imhlasele, iya kubulala isinye kwisithathu samagorha, kwaye ukuba akanako ukubaleka enqabeni, kuya kuba yintlekele kuhlaselo.

Ngoko ke, abo bawasebenzisa kakuhle amajoni abo abayi kuba nanto yimbi ngaphandle kokulwa nokuba baye banikezela kumajoni angabantu. Nokuba ugqithe kwinqaba yomntu, akuncedi nto ukuyihlasela. Kuya kuthatha ixesha elide ngaphambi kokuba utshabalalise ilizwe lomntu. Ngokuqinisekileyo siya kulwa nehlabathi ngamandla ethu onke. Ke ngoko, kuya kufuneka wenze uninzi lweengeniso zakho ngaphandle kokulumka. Lo ngumthetho wohlaselo.

Ngoko ke umthetho wemfazwe uthi xa uneshumi kufuneka ulirhangqe, ukuba unesihlanu ulihlasele, ukuba uyaliphindaphinda, ulahlule, ukuba ulutshaba ulwe nalo, ukuba limbalwa liyiphephe, kwaye ukuba awunalo liphephe. Ngoko ke, amandla otshaba oluncinane ngamandla otshaba olukhulu.

Injengele ligosa lelizwe. Ukuba kukho inkxaso, ilizwe liya kuhlala lomelele, kwaye ukuba akukho nkxaso, ilizwe liya kuhlala libuthathaka. Ngoko ke, zithathu izizathu zokuba umkhosi wakho ubandezeleke. Baxelela umkhosi ukuba uye phambili, bengazi ukuba umkhosi mawuye ngaphambili, kwaye uthi uhlehle, bengazi ukuba umkhosi awunayo enye indlela ngaphandle kokuhlehla. Oku kubizwa ``ukuyekelela emkhosini.''

Ukuba awazi ngemikhosi emithathu kwaye ulandele ipolitiki efanayo nemikhosi emithathu, amajoni aya kubhideka. Ukuba awuwazi amandla emikhosi emithathu kwaye wabelane ngemisebenzi yemikhosi emithathu, uya kukrokrela amajoni. Xa imikhosi emithathu sele ididekile kwaye ithandabuza, iinkosi zisenkathazweni. Oku kubizwa ngokuba ``ukuphazamisa umkhosi nokufumana uloyiso.''

Ngoko ke, kukho iindlela ezintlanu zokwazi uloyiso.

Abo balaziyo ixesha lokulwa nelokungaliwa baya kuphumelela. Abo baqondayo ishishini lenkitha baya kuphumelela. Abo baneminqweno efanayo phezulu nangaphantsi baya kuphumelela. Abo balinda ngoloyiko baya koyisa. Abo bangenako ukulawulwa yinjengele baya kuphumelela.

Ezi zintlanu ziyindlela yokwazi uloyiso. Ngoko ke, kuthiwa ukuba uyamazi kwaye uzazi ngokwakho, uya kulwa amadabi alikhulu kwaye ulahlekelwe yinto encinci kakhulu. Ukuba awumazi kwaye uzazi ngokwakho, uya kuphumelela okanye ulahlekelwe. Ukuba awumazi kwaye awuzazi, ngokuqinisekileyo uya kubhubha ngalo lonke ixesha usilwa.

Isahluko 4: Katahen
USun Tzu uthe abo bebesilwa kakuhle ngaphambili bebengenayo enye indlela ngaphandle kokuphumelela kuqala, emva koko balinde utshaba ukuba luphumelele. Lityala lakho ukuba ungaphumeleli, kodwa lutshaba olufanele ukuphumelela. Ngoko ke, abo balwa kakuhle abafanele banyanzelwe ukuba baphumelele kakuhle, kodwa abafanele banyanzelwe ukuba boyise iintshaba zabo. Ngoko ke, kuthiwa uloyiso kufuneka lwaziwe, kodwa akukho nto inokwenziwa.

Abo bangaphumelelanga kufuneka bakhusele. Abo bafanele ukuphumelela mabahlasele. Ukukhusela, kufuneka ube nokwaneleyo, kwaye ukuhlasela, kufuneka ube nokunye. Abo bakhusela kakuhle baya kugcinwa phantsi kweNine Earths, kwaye abo bahlasela kakuhle baya kuhamba ngaphezu kwe-Nine Heavens. Ke ngoko, zikhusele kakuhle kwaye ufezekise uloyiso.

Ayikokubalungileyo nabalungileyo ukubona uloyiso, kodwa kubantu abaqhelekileyo kuphela ukubona. Asingomntu olungileyo othi ukuphumelela edabini kuya kuzisa okulungileyo ehlabathini. Ke ngoko, ukukhulisa uAkiyoshi akuninzi kakhulu. Andiliboni ilanga nenyanga ndivule amehlo. Kuthatha indlebe elumkileyo ukuva iindudumo.
Abo balwa kakuhle, ekuthiwa kumaxesha amandulo, ngabo baphumelela xa kulula ukuphumelela. Ngoko ke, ukuba umntu usilwa kakuhle uyaphumelela, akuyi kubakho udumo okanye ubukroti. Ngoko ke, akwenzi mahluko ukuba idabi laloyiswa. Abo bangahlukaniyo baya kuhlala bephumelela naphi na apho bakhetha khona. Kufuneka sibaphumelele abo sele boyisiwe. Ke ngoko, abo balwa kakuhle bema kumhlaba wokungoyiswa, kwaye abaphulukani noloyiso lweentshaba zabo. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, umkhosi owoyisileyo uqala woyise uze emva koko ufune idabi, kwaye umkhosi owoyisiweyo uqala ulwe uze emva koko ufune uloyiso.

Abo basebenzisa amajoni kakuhle bahlakulela iTao kwaye baxhasa iDharma. Ngoko ke, bahlala belawula ngendlela yokuwina-ukulahlekelwa.

Ubugcisa bemfazwe, njengoko u-Ichi esitsho, ixesha. Isixa esikhankanywe kwisibini. Inombolo yesithathu ithi. Igama lowesine. Ngokutsho kweGo, yimpumelelo. Umhlaba uvelisa isidanga, isidanga sivelisa isixa, isixa sivelisa inani, inani livelisa isihloko, kwaye isihloko sivelisa uloyiso. Ngoko ke, kufana nokungathi umkhosi owoyisileyo ukhalisa ibhawuthi ngebholiti, yaye umkhosi owoyisiweyo wawungathi ubiza ibhawuthi ngebholiti.

Ngokukhawuleza nje ukuba abantu aboyisileyo banyanzelwe edabini, kuya kufana nokugqibezela iSekisui kwiintlambo eziliwaka.

Isahluko 5: Seihen

Ngokuka-Sun Tzu, ukulawula inkitha nokulawula abambalwa kufana namaqhezu. Lo ngumboniso wokwenza ukuba inyambalala ilwe nokwenza abambalwa balwe. Abantu baloo mikhosi mithathu bamele balwamkele utshaba baze boyiswe, ngoko nantsi into abafanele bayenze. Ukongezwa kwamajoni kufana nokuphosa isaphetha kwiqanda, kodwa kuyinyaniso kwaye kubuxoki.

Ngokubanzi, amadabi aphunyezwa ngobulungisa nokungaqhelekanga. Ngoko ke, abo bavelisa izinto ezilungileyo nezingaqhelekanga bafana nezulu nomhlaba, ongenasiphelo, njengemilambo nemilambo, engenasiphelo. Iyaphela kwaye iqale kwakhona ngolu hlobo yonke imihla kunye nenyanga nganye. Kuthatha intsimbi yesine ukufa nokuzalwa kwakhona. Nangona kukho amazwi amahlanu kuphela, ukungaqhelekanga kwamazwi amahlanu kukhulu kangangokuba awukwazi ukuzibamba ngaphandle kokumamela. Nangona kukho imibala emihlanu kuphela, ukuguqulwa kwemibala emihlanu kufuneka kubonwe. Nangona incasa ihlanu kuphela, ukungaqhelekanga kwezintlanu zokungcamla kunokoyiswa kwaye akufuneki kungcamlwe.

Nangona umkhosi wokulwa uyinto engaqhelekanga, isiganeko saseKiyoshi sinokuphumelela kwaye akukho mfuneko yokuba sengxakini. Okwenzekayo xa kukho into engahambi kakuhle kufana nomjikelo ongenasiphelo. Ngubani okhathalayo ngale nto?

Yayingamandla okuqukuqela kwamanzi okwabangela ukuba amatye akhukuliswe. Yinto eguqukayo ukuba intaka yokhozi ikhawuleze kwaye iphele ngokusilela. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, abo balwa kakuhle baya kuba nomlo onamandla kunye nomlo omfutshane. Amandla afana nentsimbi enqamlezileyo, kwaye iqhina lifana nethuba.

Kuvela uqhushululu e-Osamu, uloyiko luvela ngenkalipho, kwaye ubuthathaka buvela emandleni. Zininzi iimvukelo. Isibindi ngamandla. Ifom ebuthathaka yifomu.

Ngoko ke, abo bazishenxisayo iintshaba zabo ngendlela elungileyo baya kuqinisekisa ukuba ukuba benza oko bakufunayo, ngokuqinisekileyo utshaba luya kulandela oko lukwenzayo, yaye ukuba luceba ukwenjenjalo, ngokuqinisekileyo utshaba luya kuthabatha oko lukwenzayo. Yishukumisele ngokuyingenelo, uyilindele ngobabalo.

Ngoko ke, abo balwa kakuhle bafuna oku njengamandla, kwaye musa ukugxeka abanye. Ngoko ke, ubakhetha kakuhle abantu bakhe yaye akathembeli kumandla akhe. Ukuba uthe wavumela umntu ukuba akurholele emfazweni, kufana nokujika ilitye libe lilitye. Ubume bomthi kunye nelitye kukuba xa ikhuselekile, ihleli; xa iyingozi, iyashukuma; xa ihamba, iyayeka; xa isazinge, iyashukuma. Ngoko ke, amandla okwenza abantu balwele okulungileyo ngamandla ajika ilitye elingqukuva libe ziintaba eziliwaka.

Isahluko 6: Inyaniso kunye nentsomi

Ngokuka-Sun Tzu, abo baya kuqala ukuya edabini baze balinde utshaba baya kubandezeleka, ngelixa abo baya edabini kamva baze balwe idabi baya kusebenza nzima. Ngoko ke, abo balwa kakuhle musa ukuvumela abanye benze kubo. Ukuba uhlala uzenza utshaba, uya kungenelwa koku. Idla ngokuba lityala lotshaba elibangela umonakalo. Ngoko ke, ukuba unotshaba, uya kusebenza nzima, ukuba udiniwe, uya kukutya, kwaye ukuba unexabiso eliphantsi, uyakususa.

Ukuba uphelela kwindawo apho ngokuqinisekileyo uya kuphelela khona, ukuba uphelele kwindawo ongayifuniyo, kwaye ukuba awusebenzi indlela yakho kwiimayile eziliwaka, kufuneka udlule kumhlaba ongenamntu. Indlela yodwa yokuhlasela nokubamba into kukuhlasela iindawo ezingenako ukukhuselwa. Indlela yodwa yokuqina xa ukhusela kukukhusela iindawo ezingenakuhlaselwa. Ngoko ke, abo bahlasela kakuhle abalwazi utshaba lwabo nalapho lukhusela khona. Abo bakhusela kakuhle abalwazi utshaba okanye apho luya kuhlasela khona. Isenokuba inobuqhophololo okanye echuliweyo, kodwa ifikelela kwinqanaba lokungabambeki. Ngaba nguThixo? Ndiyazibuza ukuba ingaba nguThixo na, ide ithi cwaka. Ngoko ke, usoloko elawula utshaba.
Ekuphela kwendlela yokuqhubeka kukulahla obo buxoki. Ukuba awukwazi ukuhlehla kwaye uyilandele, awukwazi ukufikelela kuyo ngokukhawuleza. Ngoko ke, ukuba ndifuna ukulwa, utshaba lungaziphakamisa iziseko zalo kwaye luwenze nzulu imijelo yalo, kodwa ukuba alunandlela yokulwa nam, kufuneka ndihlasele apho lunokusindisa khona. Ukuba bendingafuni ukulwa, bendiza kuphawula umhlaba ndiwukhusele, kodwa utshaba olungenakwenza nto ngaphandle kokulwa nam, kufuneka luye kuloo ndawo.

Ke ngoko, ukuba andenzi abantu ngokwemo yam, oko kukuthi, ndiya kuba nako ukuluqonda utshaba. Ukuba simanyene sibe banye, kwaye iintshaba zethu zahlulwe zibe lishumi, ngoko siya kumhlasela lowo unabalishumi. Ngamanye amazwi, sibaninzi kwaye iintshaba zethu zimbalwa. Ukuba ndithe ndabetha ingxokolo ngengxokolo, baya kutshabalala abalwa nam. Akufunekanga ulazi ilizwe esiza kulwa sobabini. Ukuba awuyazi, baninzi abantu abalungiselelwe utshaba. Ukuba baninzi abaxhobileyo utshaba, bambalwa abalwa nam; Ke ngoko, ukuba ulungiselela umphambili, oko kukuthi, kuncinci ngasemva; ukuba ulungiselela umva, oko kukuthi, ngaphantsi ngaphambili; ukuba ulungiselela ngasekhohlo, oko kukuthi, ngaphantsi ngasekunene; ukuba ulungiselela ekunene, oko kuthetha ngaphantsi ekhohlo; kwaye ukuba akukho nto uyilungiseleleyo, oko kukuthi, akukho kusilela. Ubuhlolokazi yinto elungiselelwe abantu. Isihlwele silungiselela abanye ngokwabo.

Ngoko ke, ukuba uyayazi indawo yedabi kunye nomhla wokulwa, kufuneka ulwe iwaka leekhilomitha. Ukuba awuyazi indawo yedabi kunye nomhla wedabi, ekhohlo alikwazi ukugcina ilungelo, ilungelo alikwazi ukugcina ekhohlo, umphambili awukwazi ukugcina umva, kwaye umva awukwazi ukugcina umphambili. Nokuba uthini na, ingamashumi eemayile kude, neemayile ezininzi kufutshane. Nangona iimazi zinenani elikhulu lamajoni, akusayi kusinceda nganto ngokoyisa okanye ukoyiswa. Ngoko ke, uthi, uloyiso kufuneka luphunyezwe. Nangona utshaba luninzi, asifanele silwe.

Ngoko ke, ngokucwangcisa oku, uya kukwazi isicwangciso sokuzuza kunye nelahleko, ngokudala oku, uya kukwazi umgaqo wokuhamba, ngokwenza oku, uya kuyazi indawo yobomi nokufa, kwaye ngokulandela oku, uya kukwazi apho kukho i-surplus kunye nokungabikho.

Ngoko ke, eyona ndlela ibalaseleyo yejoni kukungabi nasimo. Ukuba ayibambeki, ayinakubonwa nganzulu, kwaye kwanezilumko aziyi kukwazi ukuceba. Nangona uloyiso lunokubhideka ngenxa yefomu, uluntu alusoze lwazi. Abantu bayalwazi uhlobo lwesizathu sokuba ndiphumelele, kodwa abalwazi uhlobo lwesizathu esilawula uloyiso lwam. Ngoko ke, nokuba imfazwe iphumelele, ayiphindisi, kodwa iphendula kwifomu ngaphandle kombuzo.

Ukumila kwejoni kufana namanzi. Ubume bamanzi buphantsi, buphepha ukuphakama. Imilo yejoni iyayiphepha inyani kwaye idubula isithuba. Amanzi alawula ukuhamba kwawo ngokuxhomekeke emhlabeni, kwaye amajoni alawula uloyiso lwawo ngokuxhomekeke kutshaba. Ngoko ke, amajoni akakho ngokusisigxina, kwaye amanzi akanalo uhlobo olusisigxina. Abo baphumelela ngokutshintsha ngokuxhomekeke kutshaba badla ngokubizwa ngokuba ngoothixo. Ngoko ke, akukho luloyiso olungunaphakade kwizinto ezihlanu, akukho ndawo isigxina kwiiyure ezine, kukho ubude obufutshane kunye nobude emini, kwaye kukho ukufa nokuzalwa enyangeni.

Isahluko 7: Imfazwe yasemkhosini

USun Tzu uthe, akukho nto inzima njengemfazwe yomkhosi ukusebenzisa amajoni, ukuba injengele ifumane imiyalelo kwinkosana, idibanise umkhosi wayo, imamele abantu, ihlale ngoxolo. Ubunzima bemfazwe yasemkhosini bumiselwa ngokuphambuka kunye nobunzima. Ngoko ke, ndiyaphambuka, ndisebenzisa elo thuba ukuze ndilinge abanye, ndiqale emva kwabanye, ndize ndifike ngaphambi kwabanye. Lo ngumntu owaziyo amaqhinga okuphambuka.

Ke ngoko, iimfazwe zomkhosi zinenzuzo kwaye iimfazwe zomkhosi ziyingozi. Ukuba uphakamisa umkhosi kwaye ulwele iminqweno yakho, awuyi kukwazi ukufikelela kuyo, kodwa ukuba uphathisa umkhosi wakho ukuba ulwele iimfuno zakho, uya koyiswa. Ngenxa yoko, ukuba akunikezeli emkhosini, uya kufa, ukuba awunakutya, uyafa, kwaye ukuba awunikezelanga uya kufa. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, uyamwonga aze awele, yaye xa enqumla indlela, uwuphinda kabini umgama, aze alwele inzuzo engaphezu kweeli ezilikhulu, unyanzela iinjengele ezintathu ukuba zimhlasele. Abanamandla baya kuhamba kuqala, abadiniweyo baya kusalela ngasemva, kwaye umthetho ulishumi elinanye. Xa ulwela inzuzo ngaphezu kwe-50 li, uthuka i-general ephezulu. Loo mthetho uphakathi kwendlela. Xa ukhuphisana ngenzuzo kumgama we-30 li, umahluko sisibini kwisithathu. Ngale nto, uya kuqonda ubunzima bemfazwe yomkhosi.
Ngoko ke, abo bangawaziyo amacebo ezizwe ngezizwe abanakukwazi ukunxulumana omnye nomnye kusengaphambili. Abo bangakwaziyo ukumila kweentaba, amahlathi neentaba abakwazi ukuya emfazweni. Abo bangalusebenzisiyo ukhokelo abanakuze balizuze ingenelo yomhlaba.

Ke ngoko, amajoni ema ngobuqhetseba, ashukume ngenzuzo, kwaye enze utshintsho ngokwahlulahlula. Ngoko ke, isantya salo sinjengomoya, ukucotha kwalo kufana nehlathi, ukungena kwalo kufana nomlilo, ukungashukumi kwalo kufana nentaba, ukungaqondakali kwalo kufana nesithunzi, nokushukuma kwalo kufana nokubetha kwendudumo. Ubahlulahlula abantu bakhe ukuze bagqogqe ilali, uyahlulahlula izilangazelelo zakhe ekuzulazuleni emhlabeni, yaye uhamba ngegunya elisengozini. Lowo waziyo kwangethuba i-detour iya kuphumelela. Lo ngumthetho wemfazwe yasemkhosini.

Ngokorhulumente wasemkhosini, ``Naxa ndiyithetha, andiyiva, ngoko ke ndenza igubu legolide. Xa ndilibona, andiliboni, ngoko ndenza iflegi ephaphazelayo.'' Yiyo loo nto igubu legolide nendwe zitsala ingqalelo yomntu wonke. Ukuba umntu sele ecingele enye, oko kukuthi, igorha alikwazi ukuqhubela phambili yedwa, kwaye igwala alikwazi ukuhlehla yedwa. Kungumthetho ukusebenzisa aba bantu. Ke ngoko, isibakala sokuba zininzi iigubu zomlilo kumadabi asebusuku kunye neeflegi ezininzi ezibhabhayo kumadabi asemini zitshintsha iimbono zabantu.

Ke ngoko, kuya kufuneka ubambe ingqalelo yakho kwimikhosi emithathu, kunye nentliziyo yakho jikelele. Ngenxa yoko umoya wakusasa ubukhali, umoya wasemva kwemini uba buthathaka, nomoya wangokuhlwa uba buthathaka. Ke ngoko, abo bakwaziyo ukusebenzisa umkhosi kakuhle baphephe uburhalarhume bayo kwaye bahlasele inertia yayo. Nguye lo uzola. Ukulinda isiphithiphithi ngokuzola, ukulinda i-yakuza ngokuzola. Nguye lo uphilisa intliziyo. Ukulinda umgama ngokusondeleyo, ukulindela umsebenzi ngosizi, nokulinda ukutya ngokukruquka. Nguye lo olawula amandla. Ngaphandle kokuphakamisa iflegi yobulungisa, ngaphandle kokuhlasela i-Chen enekratshi. Oku kuya kuyinyanga ingxaki.

Ngoko ke, umthetho wokusetyenziswa kwamajoni uthi: ungaze uye entabeni ephakamileyo, ungaze ujike endulini, ungalandeli umntu osingise emantla, ungaze uhlasele ijoni elinamandla, ungaze ubanjwe lijoni, ungalandeli ndoda ibuyela ekhaya, soloko woyika umrhangqi, ungaze uleqe isela. Kusemthethweni ukusebenzisa amajoni kule nto.

Isahluko 8: Iinkukhu eziThoba

Ngokutsho kukaSun Tzu, umthetho wokusetyenziswa kwamajoni uthi injengele kufuneka ithathe ubomi bayo njengomlawuli, idibanise umkhosi wayo imamele isihlwele, ingalali etyholweni, ixubene etyholweni, ingahlali entlango, iyelenqe kwindawo erhangqileyo, kwaye ilwe kwindawo efileyo. Kukho ezinye izinto ezingaxhomekekanga kwi-coating. Kukho iindawo apho umkhosi awukwazi ukudubula. Kukho iindawo zenqaba ezingenakuhlaselwa. Kukho iindawo emhlabeni apho kungekho ngquzulwano. Kukho ezinye izinto ongenakuzamkela.

Ngoko ke, injengele, umntu oyiqondayo ingenelo yeenguqu ezisithoba, uyazi ukuba unokusebenzisa amajoni. Ngokubanzi, ukuba awuyazi izibonelelo zotshintsho olusithoba, nokuba uyawazi umhlaba, awuyi kufumana nayiphi na inzuzo yendawo. Ukuba awubazi ubuchule boTshintsho oluThoba lokulawula imikhosi yakho, nokuba uyazazi iZibonelelo eziNhlanu, awuyi kuba luncedo kwabanye.

Ngenxa yesi sizathu, iingcamango zomntu osisilumko zisoloko zixutywe nezinto ezinomdla. Kufuneka ukhonze kwaye ukholwe ngelixa ugxile kwinzuzo. Igcwele ingozi kwaye kufuneka isonjululwe. Ngenxa yoko, abo bacinezela iinkosi benza ububi, abo bakhonza iinkosi benza karma, kwaye abo bacinezela iinkosi benza inzuzo.

Ngoko ke, umthetho wokusetyenziswa kwemikhosi ayikokulinda ukuba ize, kodwa kukulinda. Andinandlela yimbi ngaphandle kokuhlasela, kwaye kufuneka ndamkele nokuba kukho iindawo endingenakuzihlasela.

Ngoko ke, kukho iingozi ezintlanu jikelele. Abaxhwalekileyo bafanele babulawe, abo baphelelwe lithemba bafanele babanjwe, abangxamileyo bafanele bacekiswe, abanyanisekileyo bafanele bahlaziswe, yaye abathanda ilizwe bafanele baphazamiseke. Zonke ezi zinto zintlanu ziyimpazamo yenjengele, kwaye kuyintlekele kuye ukusebenzisa amajoni akhe. Ukubhukuqa umkhosi nokubulala injengele, kuhlala kukho iingozi ezintlanu. Kufuneka ndiqikelele.

Isahluko 9: Matshi

USun Tzu uthe: Xa usilwa nomkhosi okanye usilwa notshaba, dlula intaba okanye uthembele entlanjeni; jonga ubomi kwaye ulwe kwindawo ephakamileyo, kwaye ungakhuphukeli kwindawo ephakamileyo ukulwa. Umkhosi uya kubeka oku entabeni.

Ukuba amanzi anqunyulwa, intlanzi iya kuhlala isuka emanzini, kwaye ukuba amanzi anqunyulwe, kunenzuzo ukuwadubula emva kokuba iphelile, kunokuba ibamkele emanzini. Abo banqwenela ukulwa abafanele baye emanzini ukuya kubulisa iindwendwe. Jonga ubomi kwaye uphile phezulu, kwaye ungajongani nokuhamba kwamanzi. Umkhosi uya kubeka oku emanzini.
Ukuba unqumle uKusezawa, vele uhambe ungahlali. Ukuba uzisa umkhosi kwintlambo, qiniseka ukuba uthembele emanzini kwaye ugcine imithi emva kwakho. Lo ngumkhosi oza kohlwaya uHousawa.

Zibeke emathafeni, iindawo eziphakamileyo zibekwe ngasekunene kwakho, ujongane nokufa, ushiye ubomi ngasemva. Lo ngumkhosi oya kuwohlwaya emhlabeni. Inzuzo yale mikhosi mine yayikukuba yakwazi ukoyisa abalawuli abane boMlawuli oMthubi.

Ngokuqhelekileyo, umkhosi uthanda abaphezulu kwaye uyabadela abaphantsi, ukuxabisa okulungileyo kwaye udelela okubi. Ukondla ubomi nokubulala iziqhamo, kwaye akukho ngozi emkhosini. Oku kubizwa ngokuba luloyiso oluqinisekileyo. Xa ukhusela iinduli, qiniseka ukuba uzibeka elangeni umqolo wakho usekunene. Oku kunceda amajoni kwaye kunceda umhlaba. Ukuba kuyanetha kwaye kukho amanzi, abo banqwenela ukuhamba emanzini kufuneka balinde de izinze.

Ukuba kukho inzonzobila, isilingi, umhadi wasezulwini, izulu lasezulwini, umqolomba wasezulwini, okanye umsantsa wasezulwini emhlabeni, qiniseka ukuba uyawushiya yaye ungasondeli kuwo. Ndiya kuzikhwebula kuyo, zisondele iintshaba zam kuyo; Ndiya kuyikhawulela, zimke kuyo iintshaba zam;

Ukuba udibana nayo nayiphi na imiqobo, imisele, iingcongolo, iintaba, okanye izithunzi kuhambo lwakho lomkhosi, qiniseka ukuba uzigquma ngembeko. Le yindawo yodlwengulo.

Ekuphela kwendlela yokuthula xa ukufutshane notshaba kukusebenzisa ingozi. Ukuba ulwa ukude, ufuna abanye baqhubele phambili. Okukhona kulula ukuba kwindawo okuyo, kokukhona kuba ngcono. Iintshukumo zikaShuju ziza kuza njengoko zisiza. Kuyathandabuzeka ukuba zininzi iziphazamiso kuzo zonke izityalo. Iintaka zivuka ngokukhawuleza. Ukuntyiloza kwerhamncwa kukubuyisela umva. Uthuli luphezulu kwaye lubukhali xa kufika imoto. Into ephantsi nebanzi kulapho abenzi bobubi beza khona. Xa umthetho uthe saa, kuba ngathi umgawuli uyawuchola. Ukubuyela emva naphambili nabantu abambalwa kufana nokuqhuba umkhosi.

Ukuba uyazithoba kwaye uzilungiselele, uya kuqhubeka. Ukurhoxa nokuqhubela phambili kukurhoxa. Iphume nje imoto elayitayo ndisecaleni kwayo kucaca nje. Ukucela uxolo ngaphandle kwesithembiso ngamayelenqe. Bagxalathelana ukuya kuqokelela iinqwelo ngokukhawuleza njengoko babelindele. Isiqingatha-inkqubela phambili, isiqingatha-ukuhlehla sisimemo.

Ukuba ume ngentonga, uya kulamba. Into yokuqala oyiselayo emva kokukha amanzi kukukhawuleza unxaniwe. Ukuba awuqhubeli phambili ngokusekelwe kwinzuzo, kuya kufuneka usebenze nzima. Iintaka zibuthana ngelize. Ndiyoyika ukukufowunela ebusuku. Umzabalazo womkhosi uhleli emagxeni enjengele. Ukuhamba kweflegi kumbhodamo. Eli gosa linomsindo kuba lidiniwe. Ukutya amahashe ade afikelele kwimililitha nokutya inyama kuthetha ukuba akukho kutya komkhosi. Ukuba awusebenzi nzima ubuyele kulaa ndlu, uya kuba sengxakini. Njengomntu oyintlebendwane, ukuba ngokuthe ngcembe ulahlekelwa ngabantu, uya kulahlekelwa ngabantu. Ngokufuthi ukuphumelela kukubandezeleka. Kudla ngokuba nzima ukohlwaya. Ibiya kuba bubuvila ukubhenca abantu kuqala uze uboyike. Ukuba ufike ucela uxolo, ucela ukuphumla. Ukuba amajoni akho adibana ngomsindo, kodwa emva kwexesha elide awadibananga, kwaye akaphinda adibane, qiniseka ukuba uhloniphe kwaye uqwalasele oku.

Ijoni alinakuzinceda kodwa lingenelwa kokungakumbi. Kunokuba uhambe uye phambili, ukuba ungasebenzisa amandla akho ukuhlasela utshaba, thatha nje abantu. Noko ke, abo bazixhamlisa ngokungenankathalo iintshaba zabo baya kusoloko behlaselwa ngabanye. Uxolo, kodwa andinakwenza nto ngaphandle kokumohlwaya, nto leyo ethetha ukuba akayi kuthobela. Ukuba awuyisebenzisi, kuba nzima ukuyisebenzisa. Ukuba sele engumzali yaye akohlwaywa, ngamanye amazwi, akanakusetyenziswa. Ngoko ke, xa sinikela imiyalelo, sisebenzisa iileta, yaye ukuyithobela sisebenzisa umkhosi. Oku kubizwa ngokuba yinto ekufuneka ubenayo. Ukuba ummiselo wenziwa kwasekuqaleni kwaye ufundise abantu, oko kukuthi, baya kuba ngabasebenzi baseburhulumenteni. Ukuba ufundisa abantu ngaphandle kokulandela umthetho, oko kukuthi, abayi kunxiba iimpahla zangasese. Abo basebenza kwizinto ezisisiseko baya kukwazi ukusebenzisana nabantu.

Isahluko 10: Umhlaba

Ngokutsho kuka-Sun Tzu, kule ndawo kukho ezo zixhaphakileyo, ezomelele, ezixhasayo, ezimxinwa, ezimthambeka, nezikude.
Kuthiwa makahambe nam ndihambe naye. Ngendlela elula, ukuba uqala ukuhlala eGaoyang kwaye ulwe ngokusebenzisa indlela yokutya, oko kukuthi, kukho inzuzo. Into onokuthi uhambe nayo kunye nento ongenakukwazi ukuyibuyisela ibizwa ngokuba yi-挂. Kwifom yeNaru, ukuba awulungele utshaba lwakho, awuyi kukwazi ukuphuma kwaye uphumelele; ukuba awuzilungiselelanga utshaba lwakho, awuyi kukwazi ukuphuma kwaye uphumelele. Kuya kuba nzima ukubuyela, okuya kuba yinto engalunganga. Ukuba ndibonakala ndikwi disadvantage kwaye naye ubonakala ekwi disadvantage, kuthiwa yinkxaso. Nangona uhlobo lwenkxaso lutshaba lwam, nokuba lulungele mna, andiyi kushiya. Kuya kuba luncedo ukuhlehla, ukushiya oku, kwaye udubule utshaba ngayo phakathi. Ukuba bendihlala kule fomu emnyama, ngokuqinisekileyo ndiya kuyityhila kwaye ndilinde utshaba. Ukuba utshaba lukhona kuqala kwaye lwanyamalala, ngoko musa ukumlandela; ukuba uthe akatshabalali, mlandeleni. Kwimeko eyingozi, ukuba ndihlala apha, ngokuqinisekileyo ndiya kuhlala eGaoyang kwaye ndilinde intshaba. Ukuba utshaba lukhona kuqala, hamba kwaye ungamlandeli. Kwifom ekude, kunzima ukulwa ukuba amandla alinganayo, kwaye ukuba uyalwa, uya kuba sengozini.

Xa sithetha nje, aba bantu bathandathu baziindlela zomhlaba. Andikwazi kuzibamba ngaphandle kokuzibuza malunga noxanduva oluphezulu lwenjengele.

Ke ngoko, inxenye ibaleka, ityhafe, imbi, iwile, ikhubekile, idlongozela, enye ingasentla. Aba bantu bathandathu abayizo iintlekele zezulu nomhlaba, kodwa ziimpazamo zeenjengele. Xa umfutho ulinganayo, ukudubula ishumi nomnye kubizwa ngokuba yi- run. Umntu owomeleleyo nobuthathaka kuthiwa ulivila. Xa umntu enamandla kwaye ebuthathaka, kuthiwa ubuthathaka. Igosa elikhulu linomsindo kwaye alizithobi, kwaye xa lidibana notshaba, lizilwela ngokuzola, kwaye injengele engawaziyo amandla ayo kuthiwa yoyisiwe. Xa injengele ibuthathaka kwaye ingekho ngqongqo, kwaye imfundiso ingacacanga, namajoni engaqeqeshekanga, kubizwa ngokuba yimvukelo. Injengele engaluhlaseli utshaba, ekhonza abantu kunye nabancinane, ihlasela abomeleleyo nababuthathaka, nengenakuzikhethela phakathi kwamajoni ayo kuthiwa yintla. Aba bantu bathandathu basendleleni eya koyiswa. Njengomphathi-mkhosi, andikwazi kuzibamba.

Umhlaba unceda amajoni. Yindlela yomphathi ophakamileyo wokuzuza uloyiso ngokuhlasela utshaba, nokulinganisa ingozi kunye nomgama. Abo bayaziyo le nto kwaye basebenzise idabi baya kuphumelela ngokuqinisekileyo, kwaye abo bangayaziyo le nto kwaye basebenzise idabi ngokuqinisekileyo baya kulahleka. Ngoko ke, nokuba iNkosi ithe ningalwi, kusoloko kulungile ukulwa ukuba uqinisekile ukuba uya kuphumelela kwithafa ledabi. Nangona iNkosi isoloko isixelela ukuba silwe, akunakwenzeka ngaphandle kokulwa. Ngoko ke, abo bangafuni udumo ngokuthanda, abarholi kwaye basiphephe isono, kodwa bagcina nje abantu kwaye bakhonze iimfuno zabantu bubutyebi besizwe.

Ukujonga ukufa komntwana kufana nosana, ngoko kufuneka uye ekupheleni nzulu ngayo. Ukubona ukufa kufana no-Aiko, ngoko kufuneka ufe naye. Ukuba ityebile kwaye ayinakusetyenziswa, ukuba ayithandwa kwaye iyalelwa, ukuba iphazamisekile kwaye ayikwazi ukulawulwa, ngoko ifana nomntwana we-feral kwaye ayikwazi ukusetyenziswa.

Ukuba uyazi ukuba kufuneka udubule nini, kodwa awazi ukuba utshaba kufuneka ludubule nini, uphakathi kwendlela eya kuloyiso. Ukuba uyazi ukuba utshaba kufuneka ludubule nini, kodwa awazi ukuba awufanele udubule nini, sisiqingatha sedabi eso. Ukwazi ukuba udubule nini utshaba, ukwazi ukuba udubule nini ngamandla akho, kodwa ukungazi ukuba ulwe nini ngokusekwe kumhlaba sisiqingatha sedabi. Ngoko ke, abo bawaziyo amajoni abo baya kushukuma ngaphandle kokuthandabuza, kwaye abayi koyiswa. Ngoko ke, uthi, ukuba uyamazi kwaye uzazi ngokwakho, uKatsuno akayi kuba yingxaki kakhulu. Ukuba uyawazi amazulu nomhlaba, uKatsuno akayi kuba sengxakini.

Isahluko 11: Kuchihen
Ngokutsho kwe-Sun Tzu, kukho iindlela zokusebenzisa amajoni: iindawo ezithe saa, iindawo ezikhanyayo, iindawo ezikhuphisanayo, iindawo zokuwela, iindawo ezixhasayo, iindawo ezinzima, iindawo ezikhuselekileyo, iindawo ezivaliweyo kunye neendawo ezifileyo. Iinkosi zalichithachitha ilizwe ukuba zilwe. Ungena elizweni labantu, enze into engekho nzulu kumhlaba wokukhanya. Oko sinokukwenza kunengeniso, yaye oko anokukwenza kukwayingenelo. Ndiza kuthatha into eza kunye nam njengendawo yoqhagamshelwano. Umhlaba ngoweenkosi ezintathu, kwaye xa ufika ekupheleni, uya kuba nako ukuphumelela abantu behlabathi. Ungena nzulu kwilizwe labantu, kwaye wenza izinto ezihlala zineenqaba emva kwakhe zibe ziindawo ezibalulekileyo. Amahlathi, amathambeka, imigxobhozo, nayo nantoni na ethatha indlela ekunzima ukuyihamba igqalwa njengendawo yokuzingela. Kuya kuba mbalwa iindawo zokungena, ngoko abo baya kubuya baya kudlula, kwaye abo bafanele bahlasele abantu bethu baya kurhangqwa. Ukuba ulwa ngokukhawuleza, uya kusinda, kwaye ukuba awulwi ngokukhawuleza, uya kufa. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, musa ukulwa kwiindawo ezithe saa. Musa ukuma kumhlaba okhanyayo. Ngamanye amazwi, musa ukuhlasela kwindawo ekuphikiswana ngayo. Ngamanye amazwi, ungaze unqunyulwe ekudibaneni kweendlela. Ngamanye amazwi, i-lining yindibaniselwano yobudlelwane. Ngamanye amazwi, idla umhlaba onzima. Ngamanye amazwi, yiya ezilalini. Ngamanye amazwi, cwangcisa kwindawo ebiyelweyo. Ngamanye amazwi, yilwa kwindawo efileyo.

Amajoni awayesithi alungile ngamaxesha amandulo ayedla ngokwenza iintshaba aze angafikeleli kwabo bangaphambili okanye abasemva kwawo, engabambisani nenkitha yabantu, engasindisi izihandiba, engabaxoxisani nabaphathi nabaphathi, engazahluli aze ahlanganisane, kodwa ahlanganisana aze enze uhlaselo oluhlangeneyo. Ihamba ngokwenzuzo, kwaye iyayeka xa ingahambelani nenzuzo.

Ndinesibindi sokubuza lo mbuzo, ngethemba lokuba utshaba luya kufika ngokucwangcisiweyo. Akufuneki ndilinde oku. Uthi ukuba uqale ubathathele into abayithandayo, abayi kumamela. Umdla wejoni kukukhawuleza. Ndithatha ithuba lokuba abantu bangakwazi ukufikelela, ndihlasela iindawo endingazilumkisi ngazo, ndisebenzisa indlela endingoyikiyo.

Okukhona ungena kwindlela yokuba ngumthengi, kokukhona ukhethekile kwaye ungayoyisi inkosi. Unyanzeliswa ukuba ondle imikhosi emithathu ngokuthunjwa nguRaono, kwaye endaweni yokondla nokusebenza kanzima, kufuneka aqokelele amandla akhe, aqokelele amandla, imikhosi yokuthutha, isicwangciso kunye nokulinganisa. Ukuba uphosa le nto kungekho ndawo uya kuyo, uya kufa kwaye awuyi kuya ngasentla. Akukho sizathu sokufa. Yenza okugqibelele.

Ijoni, ukuba lithe laphelelwa lithemba, aliyi koyika. Ukuba akukho ndawo yokuya kuyo, oko kukuthi, qina, ukuba uhamba ngokunzulu, oko kukuthi, ukukhawulela, kwaye ukuba awunakho ukhetho, oko kukuthi, ukulwa. Ngenxa yoko, siyalela ngaphandle kwengqeqesho, samkela singacelanga, siyazi ukuba singenzanga zithembiso, kwaye sikholwa ngaphandle komyalelo. Akukho ndawo yale nto, de kuse ekufeni, ukwalela ukukrokrelana. Nkosi yam, ukuba awunayo imali, musa ukumosha imali. Ukuba awunalo ixesha elininzi eliseleyo lokuphila, akufanele ukhathazeke ngobomi bakho obude. Umyalelo ukhutshiwe: Abo bahlala njengama-samurai babeka iintamo zabo kwindawo emnyama, kwaye abo balala phantsi banqunyulwa iintamo zabo. Ukuba uphosa oku ndawo, uya kuba nesibindi esikhulu.

Ngoko ke, umntu oyisebenzisa kakuhle imikhosi yakhe ufana nokuba neliso elibukhali. Ukuba bukhali yinyoka engatshintshiyo. Ukuba udubula entanyeni, uya kufikelela emsileni; ukuba uthe wadubula emsileni, wofika emqaleni; kwaye ukuba udubula ngaphakathi, uya kufikelela emsileni. Ndinobuganga bokubuza, ngaba amajoni kufuneka aziphathe ngokungathi ayasebenza? Uthi kulungile. Nangona i-Wu kunye ne-Etes yayingavisisani omnye nomnye, xa babebelana ngesikhephe esifanayo kwaye badibana nomoya, babefana nezandla kwicala lomnye. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, nangona amahashe ayengcwatywa, ayenganelanga. Umendo woburhulumente kukumanyanisa inkalipho nokumanyana njengento enye. Ukufumana amandla kunye nokuguquguquka ngumthetho welizwe. Ngoko ke, abo bawasebenzisa kakuhle amajoni bafana nokusebenzisa umntu omnye xa bebambana ngezandla. Andinandlela yimbi ngaphandle kokuyiphepha.
Njengomthetheli-mkhosi, uthule kwaye ufihlakele, unyanisekile kwaye uqeqeshekile, uhlala usenza izidenge zamehlo amajoni, kwaye unoloyiko lokuba abayi kuyazi into oyenzayo. Izinto ezilula, guqula izicwangciso zabo, yenza abantu bangaqondi, yenza ubomi babo bube lula, udlule umendo wabo, wenze abantu bangacingi ngezinto. Ukuba uyakwazi ukwenza oku, kufana nokukhwela phezulu kwaye ushiye ileli. Kufana nokungena nzulu kumhlaba weenkosi zasebukhosini kunye nomphathi-mkhosi, yaye xa ithuba livela, ulayita isikhephe, uqhekeze imbiza, uze uqhube umhlambi. Ndityhutyha-tyhutyha, ndityhaphaka ukuba ndize, kodwa apho ndiya khona andikwazi. Yiva abantu bemikhosi emithathu, kwaye ungabaphosi engozini. Le yinto ebizwa ngokuba ngumphathi-mkhosi. Awunakunceda kodwa uqonde iSiganeko seNine Earths, inzuzo yokugoba kunye nokwelula, kunye nengqiqo yobubele bomntu.

Ngokubanzi, ngokunzulu indlela yokuba lundwendwe, kokukhona uzinikele ngakumbi, kwaye indlela ingekho nzulu, kokukhona uya kuchithakala kancinci. Abo bashiya ilizwe labo baze bawele imida ukuze babe ngabafundisi-ntsapho basengozini. Lowo ufikelela kwinqanaba lesine yi-lining. Okukhona uhamba nzulu, kokukhona ubaluleke ngakumbi. Ukuba awunzulu ukungena, ngumhlaba olula. Into enenqaba ngasemva kunye nendawo emxinwa phambi kwayo yindawo ebiyelweyo. Abo bangenayo nendawo yokuya bafile. Ngesi sizathu, sisasazeke kwihlabathi lonke ukuze simanyane kule njongo. Yile nto kanye endifuna ukuyenza ngomhlaba olula. Xa uphakathi kwengxabano, uya kuba ngasemva. Silapha eKoji, yaye sithandazela ukukhuselwa kwayo. Ifakwe kwi-lining ukuqinisekisa ukuba iqhina liqinile. Ndihlala kwilizwe elinzima yaye ndizama ukulidla ilifa oku kutya. Ndiza kudlulela kwi-lacquering ehlathini. Baza kungena kwindawo ebiyelweyo kwaye bazame ukuvala isango. Endaweni yokufa, akukho nto ngaphandle kobomi obuboniswayo. Ngoko ke, ijoni livakalelwa kukuba xa lingqongwe liya kulwa, ukuba alinakwenza nto, lilwe, yaye ukuba kunyanzelekile lithobele.

Ngenxa yesi sizathu, abo bangawaziyo amayelenqe eenkosi abanako ukuxubana nabo. Abo bangakwaziyo ukumila kweentaba, amahlathi, iintaba nemilambo abakwazi ukuya emfazweni. Abo bangalusebenzisiyo ukhokelo abanakuze balizuze ingenelo yomhlaba. Abo baneminyaka engama-45 ubudala nabangazi ukuba kuthetha ukuthini ukuba ngomnye wabo abakufanelekeli ukuba ngamajoni okumkani. Ukuba amajoni e-overlord ebenokoyisa uhlanga olukhulu, oko kukuthi, ebengayi kukwazi ukutsala ingqalelo yabantu bawo. Ukuba ujoyina utshaba, oko kukuthi, awuyi kukwazi ukujoyina. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, asilweli ubudlelwane behlabathi, asihlakulele amandla ehlabathi, sikholelwa kuthi, sinamandla, kwaye sijoyina utshaba. Ngoko ke, ukuze koyiswe loo nqaba, elo lizwe kufuneka litshatyalaliswe. Kufana nokusebenzisa umntu omnye ukuvuza ukuchas’ umthetho, ukuwisa umthetho, nokwaphula imikhosi emithathu. Musani ukuyenza le nto ngamazwi, kwaye musani ukuyixela ngamazwi. Oku kwenzeleni ingeniso, ningashumayeli ngobubi. Yiphose kumhlaba ofileyo kwaye uya kuphila emva koko, kwaye uyiphose kumhlaba ofileyo kwaye uphile emva koko. Benza ingozi kwaye badla ngokuphumelela okanye baphulukene.

Ngoko ke, injongo yokuseka umkhosi kukuphulaphula ngenyameko kwiinjongo zotshaba, ukoyisa utshaba ngaxeshanye, nokubulala injengele kwiikhilomitha eziliwaka. Lo kubizwa ngokuba ngumntu owenza kakuhle nangobuchule. Ngenxa yesi sizathu, ngomhla wonyulo lwezopolitiko, uyasonga i-talisman, akavumeli isithunywa ukuba sidlule, kwaye sibeke phezu kwe-shrine, ngaloo ndlela esenza inqaku lokulila. Xa utshaba lubandakanyeka, uya kuhlala engena ezingalweni zotshaba, abeke isithandwa sakhe kuqala, abe nombono otyhafileyo wento aza kuyenza, alandele ukhokelo lotshaba, emva koko agqibe ngemfazwe.

Ngenxa yesi sizathu, ekuqaleni wavula umnyango kwiintshaba njengentombi enyulu, kwaye kamva, njengomvundla, wayengenakukwazi ukumlahla.

Isahluko 12: Uhlaselo loMlilo

Ngokutsho kuka-Sun Tzu, kukho iindidi ezintlanu zokuhlaselwa komlilo. I-1 ithi, itshisa abantu ngomlilo, i-2 ithi itshise umlilo kwimveliso, i-3 ithi umlilo kwindawo yokugcina impahla, i-4 ithi umlilo kwindawo yokugcina impahla, i-5 ithi umlilo kwiqela. Kuhlala kukho isizathu sokubasa umlilo. Iifireworks zihlala zibonelelwa. Kukho ixesha lokubasa umlilo, imini yokubasa umlilo, nexesha lokoma kwezulu. Ilanga lisefini, kwiindonga, emaphikweni nasethunzini lenyanga. Le yimini apho umoya uvuthuza kwezi ndawo zine.
Ukuhlaselwa komlilo kuhlala kusabela kule nto ngenxa yeSiganeko soMlilo esiHlanu. Ukuba umlilo ukhutshwa ngaphakathi, uya kusabela ngokukhawuleza ngaphandle. Ukuba kuthe kwatsha umlilo aze amajoni athi cwaka, musa ukulinda uze uhlasele. Ukulawula amandla omlilo, ukuthobela njengoko kufanelekile, kunye nokuyeka xa kungafanelekanga. Umlilo kufuneka ukhanyise ngaphandle; musa ukulinda ngaphakathi; khazimlisa ngexesha. Umlilo, ukuba uyithumela kumoya ophezulu, awuyi kuhlasela umoya ophantsi. Umoya wemini awusekho, nomoya wasebusuku awusekho. Umkhosi uya kuhlala usazi malunga nemililo emihlanu kwaye ubakhusele ngamanani abo.

Ngoko ke, lowo uhlasela ngomlilo ucacile. Ohlasela ngamanzi unamandla; Amanzi kufuneka anqunyulwe, kwaye akufanele athathwe.

Bekuya kuba yintlekele ukuba besinokuphumelela edabini size singaziqheliseli iimfanelo zalo. Kuthiwa umyalelo wawunikelwe kuye. Ke ngoko, kuthiwa iMeishu iqwalasela oku, kwaye iqhele ukwenza oku. Ukuba ayincedi, musa ukushukuma; ukuba ayincedi, musa ukuyisebenzisa; ukuba usengozini, musa ukulwa. Inkosi ayifanele iphakamise inkosi yayo ngomsindo, nenjengele ayifanele ilwe ngoloyiko. Ihamba ngokwenzuzo, kwaye iyayeka xa ingahambelani nenzuzo. Umsindo kufuneka ube nemihlali, kwaye umsindo kufuneka ube nemihlali, kodwa ilizwe akufanele liphinde libe khona, kwaye abafileyo bangaze baphinde baphile. Ke ngoko, iinkosi zakwaMing ziyakuphepha ukwenza oku, yaye iinjengele ezilungileyo ziyakulinda oku. Le yindlela yoxolo lwelizwe kunye nokuzalisekiswa komkhosi.

Isahluko 13: Yoma-hen

U-Sun Tzu uthe, Kuthatha malunga ne-100,000 ukuseka inkosi, i-1,000 yeemayile ukuya emfazweni, iindleko zabalimi, iinkonzo zezikhulu zenkundla, iintsapho ezingama-700,000 ezingenakukhetha ngaphandle kokulawula imicimbi, iziphithiphithi ekhaya nangaphandle, ukutyeshela iindlela, ukulwela uloyiso ngosuku olunye iminyaka eliqela.

Noko ke, abo bathanda ubutyebi nobutyebi nabangaziqondi iimvakalelo zeentshaba zabo abanabubele. Akafanele abe ngumlawuli ongumntu. Akunjalo ekuthandeni kukaYehova, kungekuko uloyiso lweNkosi. Ke ngoko, isizathu sokuba iinjengele ezilumkileyo zenkosi yakwaMing zikwazi ukuphumelela abanye kwaye ziphumelele kufuneka zaziwe kuqala. Lowo waziyo kuqala makangathathwa njengothixo weedemon. Ayifanelanga ixeliswe, okanye ibe namava. Isoloko inguye ozaziyo iimvakalelo zotshaba.

Ngoko ke, kukho iindlela ezintlanu zokusebenzisa ixesha. Kukho i-Inma, kukho i-Uchima, kukho i-Anima, kukho ukufa, kukho uBomi. Oku kubizwa ngokuba luqeqesho lobuthixo, xa onke amaxesha amahlanu esenzeka ngaphandle kokwazi umendo. Bubutyebi bakho. I-Inma inokusetyenziswa ngokuxhomekeke kubantu basekuhlaleni. Igama elithi ``igumbi langaphakathi'' linokusetyenziswa ngokuxhomekeke kwigosa ekuthethwa ngalo. Oku kunokusetyenziswa ngokuxhomekeke kutshaba. Igumbi lokufa yindawo apho inkohliso yenziwa yaziwe, kwaye yenziwa yaziwe phakathi kweentshaba. Kukuchasa ukuphila.

Ke ngoko, xa kufikwa kwimikhosi emithathu, akukho nto isondele ngakumbi kunaphakathi, akukho bhaso linzulu kunaphakathi, kwaye izinto zisondele ngakumbi kunaphakathi. Ukuba akukho kubulumko obungcwele, akukho sizathu sokusebenzisa ixesha. Akukho sizathu sokuchitha ixesha lakho ngaphandle kokuba kungenxa yobuntu. Ukuba ayahlukanga kancinci, awuzukufumana ziphumo. Andazi nokuba yinto encinci okanye hayi, kodwa andinayo enye indlela ngaphandle kokusebenzisa unqumamo. Ukuba inyani ayithethwa kwaye uyive kuqala, bonke abo bathetha inyani baya kufa.

Nanini na umkhosi ufuna ukuhlasela, inqaba kufuneka ihlaselwe, okanye umntu ufuna ukubulawa, into yokuqala ukuyenza kukwazi amagama kunye namagama jikelele, ekhohlo nasekunene, amalungu abaphulaphuli, iimonki, kunye neemonki, kwaye uqinisekise ukuba ufumanisa olu lwazi.

Ngokuqinisekileyo ndiya kubafuna abo bafika phakathi kweentshaba zam ukuba bandiphazamise, yaye ndiya kubanceda ngokwayama ngabo, yaye ngokuqinisekileyo ndiya kubakhokela ndibakhusele. Ke ngoko, kufuneka ufumane kwaye usebenzise i-anti-ma. Ukwazi oku ngale ndlela. Ke ngoko, kuyafuneka ukufumana nokusebenzisa idolophu kunye ne-Uchima. Ukwazi oku ngale ndlela. Ngoko ke, ngaphambi kokuba ufe, khohlisa kwaye uxelele iintshaba zakho. Ukwazi oku ngale ndlela. Ngoko ke, ubomi kufuneka buphathwe njengamaxesha. INkosi ngokuqinisekileyo iya kwazi ngale nto yemizuzu emihlanu. Oyena mntu uya kuyazi le nto nguDanma. Ngoko ke, akukho ndlela yokukhetha ngaphandle kokwenza umsantsa ube mkhulu.

Ngesinye isihlandlo, xa ubukhosi bukaShang bavuka, u-Izhe wayehlala ehlotyeni. Xa ubukhosi bukaZhou buphakama, uLu Fang wayekwiShang Dynasty. Ngoko ke, kuphela inkosana elumkileyo kunye nobulumko jikelele onokuthi benze izinto ngobulumko nobulumko, kwaye ngokuqinisekileyo uya kufezekisa izenzo ezinkulu. Eli lilitye lembombo lamajoni, kwaye imikhosi emithathu iyahamba kunye.
