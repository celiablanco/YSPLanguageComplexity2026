Gulf Stream-a skiff-a amah chauha sangha man thin putar a ni a, tunah chuan ni sawmriat leh pali chhung zet chu sangha la lovin a kal tawh a ni. Ni sawmli hmasa berah chuan naupang pakhat a awm tawh a. Mahse ni sawmli chhung zet sangha a neih loh hnuah naupang nu leh pate chuan putar chu tunah chuan a chiang tawh a, a tawpah chuan salao a ni tawh tih an hrilh a, chu chu vanduaithlakna chi khat ber a ni a, chu naupang chu an thupek angin lawng dangin a kal tawh a, chu chuan kar hmasa berah sangha tha pathum a man ta a ni. Chu tlangval chu nitin a skiff khar nen a rawn lut thin chu a ti lungngai hle a, coiled line emaw gaff leh harpoon leh mast vel a sail furled emaw phur turin a pui turin a chhuk fo thin. Sail chu chhangphut bawm hmanga patch a ni a, furled a nih chuan, chak lohna flag ang mai a ni.
