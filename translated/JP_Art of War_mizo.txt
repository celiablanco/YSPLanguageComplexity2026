Bung 1: Ruahmanna siam

Sun Tzu chuan sipai chu ram tana pawimawh, nunna leh thihna hmun, awmna leh thihna kawng, hriatthiam a ngai a ni, a ti.

Chuvangin, he thil hi paltlang tur hian thil panga ka nei a, hei hi fiah tur chuan ruahmanna ka nei a, a rilru put hmang chu ka zawng chhuak thin. Pakhatna chu kawng a ni a, a pahnihna chu van a ni a, pathumna chu lei a ni a, palina chu general a ni a, pangana chu dan a ni.

Kawng chu mite chu an chunga mite nen phek khata awm a, an thihpui a, an bula awmpui a, hlauhawm hlau lo turin a ni. Vanram chu yin leh yang, khaw vawt leh lum, leh tense a ni. Leilung hi hla leh hnai, hlauhawm leh awlsam, zau leh zim, nunna leh thihna a ni. General tih awmzia chu finna, rinna, thatna, huaisenna leh khauh tihna a ni. Dan chu system, official way, leh a tum ber a ni.

General chuan heng mi panga chanchin hi hre lo mah se, hei hi hretute chuan hnehna an chang ang a, hre lote chuan hnehna an chang dawn lo. Chuvang chuan hei hi finfiah turin ruahmanna ka siam a, a sentiment chu ka zawng ta a ni. Lalpa chu a fing a, General chu a ti thei a, lei leh van hi a phu a, dan siam a ni a, sipaite chu an chak a, sipaite chu an inzirtir a, lawmman leh hremna pawh a lang chiang hle niin an sawi. He indona rah chhuah hi kan hria.

Nakin hun atana ka ruahmanna i ngaihthlak hian i hman chuan i chak ngei ang, chuvangin zawm tlat suh. Ka ruahmanna hi i ngaithla lo a nih chuan he ruahmanna hi i hman chuan i hloh ngei ang, chuvangin kalsan suh. Ruahmanna emaw hlawkna emaw atana hman a nih chuan chakna a nei ang a, midangte a pui ang. Power tih awmzia chu hlawkna atanga thuneihna thunun tihna a ni.

Sipai chu mi bumtu a ni.

Chuvangin, theihna hian thiam lohna a kawk a, hmanna hian hman loh a kawk a, hnaih hian hla tak a kawk a, hla hian hnai a kawk a, advantage hmanga thlemna, buaina man, thei rah inbuatsaih, chak loh pumpelh, thinrimna tihdanglam, baseness to make proud, while to work on, parent to separate from. An tlin lohnate chu bei la, beisei loh takin rawn lang suh. Hei hi sipaite hnehna a ni a, hrilh hmasak a ngai lo.

Insual lova chhut chhuaka la chak zawkte hian point tam tak hlawh chhuah tur an nei tur a ni. Insual lova chhut chhuaha hnehna chang lote chuan chhut a\angin hlawkna an nei tlem hle ang. Arithmetic-in hnehna a chan tam poh leh arithmetic-in hnehna a chang tlem zawk. Zawhna min pe suh la, min kalsan suh. Hei hi ngaihtuah chungin kan thlir chuan a rah chhuah chu kan hmu thei a ni.

Bung 2: Hmanraw hman dan

Sun Tzu-a sawi dan chuan sipai hman dan tlangpui chu tawlailir pakhatah yen 1,000, tawlailir pakhatah yen 1,000, ralthuam li 100,000 leh ralthuam atan li 1,000 hman a ni a, chutah chuan ram chhung leh ram pawn senso te, mikhualte tana senso te, glue leh lacquer siamna hmanrua te, leh tawlailir siamna atana ralthuam te pawh a tel a ni.

Chu indona chu rei tak i hman chuan i sipaite chu a ti dull ang a, i theihna êng tak takte chu a tichhe ang. Castle i beih chuan i inpe ang a, i pu chu rei tak i pholang chuan ram tan i tangkai lo ang. Sipai a tihbuai a, sharp te a ti lungngai a, force a tichak lo va, pawisa a tihchhiat hian lalte chuan a nghawng tha lo chu an hmang tangkai ta a ni. Mi fing awm mah se, a hnuah thil a siam \ha lovang.

Chuvangin, sipaite hian an hmanhmawh thu hi hre mah se, eng chen nge an thiam tawh tih pawh an la hre lo. Sipai kan nih atanga rei tak a ni tawh a, mahse tumahin ram tan hlawkna an la nei lo. Chuvangin sipai rawngbawlna chhiatna zawng zawng hre lo chuan sipai rawngbawlna hlawkna zawng zawng pawh a hre thei lo.

An sipaite hmang \hatute chuan rawng an bawl leh tawh lovang a, a vawi thumna atan chaw pek an ni hek lo.

An sumdawnna chu ram atangin an la a, an ei tur chu hmelma lakah an la bawk. Chuvangin sipai ration a awm tawk tur a ni. Ram a rethei a nih chuan hla takah phurh chhuah a ngai a ni. Hmun hla takah an thawn chhuak a nih chuan loneitute chu an rethei ta a ni. Zirtirtu hnaih deuhte chuan an bungrua an hralh a ngai a ni. I hralh chuan loneitute chuan an hausakna an hloh dawn a ni. A hausakna chu hloh ta se, a sum leh pai chu a bânsan nghal mai ang a, tlangram lai takah chuan kut ruakin in a chhuahsan ang a, loneitu pakhat senso a ni ang a, sawm zinga pasarih a ni ang. Court mi ropui te senso te, tawlailir leh sakawr chhe tawh te, armored arrow, turret leh turret te, hillock ox cart te, leh chung zinga paruk te chu sawma ṭhena hmun khata dah a ni. Chuvangin general fing chuan a thiltum a rawng a bawl a, hmelma a chawm thin. Hmelma plow pakhat ei hi ka tan chuan koku sawmhnih tluk a ni a, thing atanga koku pakhat chu ka tan chuan koku sawmhnih tluk a ni.
Chuvang chuan an hmelma thattute chu an thinrim thin. Hmelma a chhawr tangkai thinte chu sum leh pai an ni. Chuvangin, tawlailir indonaah motor sawm i chak chuan a chak hmasa zawk chu lawmman i pe ang a, chutah chuan flag i chawi ang a, i tawlailirte chu i chuang turin i la khawm ang a, thil tha tiin i chawm ang. Hei hi hmelma hneha chakna neih an ti.

Chuvangin sipaite hian hnehna an ngai pawimawh a ni. Hun kal tawh kha hlut suh. Chuvangin a sipaite hrechiangtu General chu mipui hruaitu leh hnam himna hruaitu a ni.

Bung 3: Beihpui thlakna

Sun Tzu-a sawi dan chuan sipai rawngbawlna dan hi hnam tihlawhtlingna atan a tha ber a, hnam tihchhiatna atan pahnihna a ni.

Sipai hneh hi a tha ber a, sipai hneh hi a tha ber pahnihna a ni. Khualzin zawh hi a tha ber a, zinkawng tihchhiat chu pahnihnaah a awm bawk. Graduation zawh zawh chu a tha ber nia ngaih a ni a, pakhat break chu pahnihna a ni. 5th grade zawh chu a tha ber nia ngaih a ni a, 5th grade break chu pahnihna a ni.

Hemi avang hian mipa indona za a chak a, indona za a hnehtu chu mi tha tih loh chu engmah a ni lo. Mihring sipaite hnuaia intukluh mi tha tak a ni.

Chuvangin sipai chungnung zawkte chuan plot chu an bei ta a ni. A dawtah chuan intersection chu cut leh rawh. A dawtah chuan sipaite chu that rawh. Chumi hnuaiah chuan castle chu bei rawh. Siege dan hi pumpelh theih a ni lo.

Turret, turret leh turret te mastering bakah instrument hman theihna te chu March thla hian neih a ni dawn a ni. March thlaah hian a hlat zawng hi tihpun leh a ni ang. General chuan hmelma a hneh thei lo a, a rawn bei a nih chuan indo mi hmun thuma ṭhena hmun khat a that ang a, kulh a\anga a tlanchhuah theih loh chuan beihpui thlakna atan chuan chhiatna nasa tak a ni ang.

Chuvangin, an sipaite hmang \angkaitute chuan mihring sipai lakah an tlu lut a nih pawhin indo mai loh chu duhthlan tur dang an nei lo ang. Mi pakhat castle i overtake pawhin attack chu a hlawk lo. Tu ram i tihchhiat hma chuan hun rei tak a la ngai dawn a ni. Kan theihna zawng zawngin khawvel hi kan do ngei ang. Chuvangin, fimkhur lovin i hlawkna chu a tha thei ang bera hmang tangkai tur a ni. Hei hi beih dan chu a ni.

Chuvangin indona dan chu: sawm i neih chuan i hual tur a ni a, panga i neih chuan i bei tur a ni a, i tipung chuan insem tur a ni a, hmelma i nih chuan i do tur a ni a, tlem i neih chuan i pumpelh tur a ni a, i neih loh chuan i pumpelh tur a ni. Chuvangin hmelma tenau chakna chu hmelma lian chakna a ni.

General chu ram official a ni. Tanpuina a awm chuan ram a chak reng ang a, \anpuina tlakchhamna a awm chuan ram hi a chak lo reng ang. Chuvangin, i sipaite hian an tuar theih chhan pathum a awm a. Sipaiho chu hmalam pan turin an ti a, hmalam pan turin an ti a, hnungtawlh turin an ti a, sipaite hian hnungtawlh mai loh chu duhthlan tur dang an nei lo tih an hre lo. Hei hi ``sipai inremna siam'' an ti.

Sipai pathumte chanchin hre lova, sipai pathumte politics ang bawka i zawm chuan sipaite chu an buai hle ang. Sipai pathumte thiltihtheihna i hriat loh a, sipai pathumte tih tur i insem chuan sipaite chu i ringhlel ang. Sipai pathumte chu an buai tawh a, an rinhlelh tawh chuan lalte chu an buai hle. Hei hi ``sipai tibuai a, hnehna chan'' an ti.

Chuvangin hnehna kan hriat theih dan kawng panga a awm a.

Engtikah nge indo tur leh indo loh tur hre chiangtute chuan hnehna an chang ang. Mipui sumdawnna hrethiamtute chuan hnehna an chang ang. A chung leh a hnuai lama duhthusam inang nei te chuan hnehna an chang ang. Hlauhna nghaktute chuan hnehna an chang ang. General thunun theih lohte chuan hnehna an chang ang.

Heng panga te hi hnehna hriatna kawng a ni. Chuvang chuan amah i hriat a, i inhriat chian chuan indona za chuang i bei ang a, i chak loh pawh a tlem hle ang an ti. Amah i hriat loh a, nangmah i inhriat loh chuan i chak emaw i chak lo emaw a ni mai thei. Amah i hriat loh a, nangmah i inhriat loh chuan i insual apiangin i thih phah ngei ngei ang.

Bung 4-na: Katahen
Sun Tzu chuan tun hmaa indo \ha te chuan chak hmasak bak chu duhthlan tur dang an nei lo a, chumi hnuah hmelma hnehna chu an nghak ta a ni, a ti. I chak loh tur chu i sualna a ni a, mahse hmelma hneh tur chu a ni. Chuvang chuan indo \ha tak takte chu chak taka chak tura nawr luih ni lovin, an hmelma lakah hnehna chang tura nawr luih loh tur a ni. Chuvang chuan hnehna chu hriat tur a ni an ti a, mahse engmah tih theih a ni lo.

Hneh tur lote chuan an venghim tur a ni. Hnehna chang tur te chuan an beih a ngai a ni. Defend tur chuan i neih tling a ngai a, attack tur chuan a tam zawk neih a ngai bawk. Venghim \hate chu Lei Pakua hnuaiah dahkhawm an ni ang a, \ha taka beitute chu Van Pakua chung lamah an kal ang. Chuvangin, tha takin inveng la, hnehna chang rawh.

Mi \ha leh \ha tan hnehna hmuh tur a ni lo va, mi pangngai hmuh tur chauh a ni. Indonaa hnehna chang chuan khawvel tan thil tha a thlen dawn tia sawi chu mi tha a ni lo. Chuvangin Akiyoshi enkawl hi a tam lutuk lo. Ka mit meng chungin ni leh thla ka hmu thei lo. Thunder hriat tur chuan beng fing tak a ngai a ni.
Hmanlai huna an tih \ha te chu chak a awlsam laia hnehna changtute an ni. Chuvang chuan indo \ha tak chuan hnehna a chang a nih chuan hmingthanna leh huaisenna a awm lovang. Chuvang chuan indona chu hnehna a chang tih pawh hian danglamna a awm lo. Indanglam lote chuan an duhna hmun apiangah hnehna an chang reng ang. Hneh tawhte lakah hnehna kan chan a ngai a ni. Chuvangin, indo \ha tak takte chu hneh theih lohna leiah an ding a, an hmelma hnehna chu an hloh lo. Hemi avang hian sipai hnehtu chuan hnehna chang hmasain indona a zawng a, sipai chak lo chuan indo hmasain hnehna a zawng leh thin.

Sipai hmang \hatute chuan Tao an chin \ha a, Dharma an vawng tlat \hin. Chuvangin chak leh chak loh zawngin rorelna an nei fo thin.

Indona art chu Ichi-a sawi angin hun a ni. Second-a tarlan zat chu. Number pathum chuan a ti. Palina hming. Go sawi dan chuan hnehna a ni. Lei hian degree a siam a, degree hian quantity a siam a, quantity hian number a siam a, number hian title a siam a, title hian hnehna a siam bawk. Chuvangin, hnehtu sipai chuan bolt hmanga bolt an fak ang mai a ni a, sipai chak lo chuan bolt hmanga bolt ko ang mai a ni.

Hnehtu mipuite chu indonaa luh tir an nih veleh Sekisui chu ruam sangkhatah thutlukna siam ang mai a ni ang.

Bung 5-na: Seihen

Sun Tzu-a sawi dan chuan mipui rorel leh mi tlemte rorel chu fraction ang mai a ni. Hei hi mipui insual tir leh tlemte insual tir tihna epitome a ni. Sipai pathum mipuite hian hmelma an pawm a, an hneh a ngai a, chuvangin hei hi an tih tur a ni. Sipai dah belh hi artui chunga thal thlak ang mai a ni a, mahse a dik leh dik lo a ni.

A tlangpuiin indonaah hian felna leh mak tihna hmanga hnehna chang a ni. Chuvangin thil tha leh mak tak tak siamtute chu van leh lei, tawp nei lo ang an ni a, lui leh lui, tawp nei lo ang an ni. Nitin leh thla tin hetiang hian a tawp a, a intan leh thin. Thih leh piangthar tur chuan dar li a ngai a ni. Aw panga chauh awm mah se, aw panga makzia chu a ropui em em a, ngaihthlak loh theih loh khawpin i ngaihthlak theih loh. Rawng panga chauh awm mahse, rawng panga inthlak danglamna hi hmuh ngei ngei tur a ni. A thlum chu panga chauh ni mah se, a thlum panga makzia chu hneh theih a ni a, a thlum loh tur a ni.

Indona chakna chu thil mak mai ni lo mah se, Kiyoshi thil thleng hi hneh theih a ni a, buaina chhuah a ngai lo. Thil engemaw a kalsual huna thil thleng chu tawp nei lo cycle ang mai a ni. He thil hi tuin nge ngaihsak?

Tui luang chhuak chakna vang a ni a, lungte chu a hrual chhuak ta a ni. Eagle bird hian a tlan chak a, a hlawhchhamnaah a tawp thin hi turning point a ni. Hemi avang hian inbei tha tak takte chuan inhnek chak tak leh inhnek rei tak an nei dawn a ni. Force chu crossbow ang a ni a, knot chu hun remchang ang a ni.

Osamu-ah buaina a chhuak a, huaisennaah hlauhna a lo chhuak a, thuneihnaah chak lohna a lo chhuak bawk. Helna hi a tam hle. Huaisenna hi thiltihtheihna a ni. Form chak lo chu form a ni.

Chuvangin, tha taka an hmelma sawn thinte chuan an duhzawng an tih chuan hmelmain an thil tih chu an zui ngei ngei tur a ni tih an tichiang ang a, chutianga tih an tum a nih chuan hmelmain an thil tih chu an la ngei ang. Advantage nen sawn la, khawngaihna nen nghak rawh.

Chuvangin, indo \hate chuan hei hi chakna atan an zawng \hin a, midangte an dem lo. Chuvangin a mite a thlang tha a, ama chakna ah a innghat lo. I thuneihna hnuaia mi pakhatin indonaa hruai che i phal chuan lung chu lungah i chantir ang mai a ni. Thing leh lung awm dan chu a him lai chuan a reh reng a; hlauhawm a nih chuan a che a; a kal chuan a tawp a; circular a nih chuan a che thin. Chuvangin, mi thatna tura beitu chakna chu lung zungbun pakhat chu tlang sangkhatah a chantirtu chakna hi a ni.

Bung 6: Thudik leh thawnthu

Sun Tzu sawi dan chuan indona hmuna kal hmasa a, hmelma nghaktute chuan an tuar ang a, a hnua indona hmuna kal a, indona beitute chuan an thawkrim hle ang. Chuvang chuan, indo \hate chuan midang tihsak an phal lo. Hmelma anga i che fo chuan hei hi i hlawkpui ang. Hmelma sual vangin harsatna a thlen fo thin. Chuvangin hmelma i neih chuan i thawkrim ang a, i chau a nih chuan i ei ang a, a man tlawm chuan i sawn mai ang.

I tawp lohna hmuna i tawp chuan, i duh lohna hmunah i tawp a, mel sangkhat zeta hlaa i hnathawh loh chuan mi tumah awm lohna ram i paltlang a ngai a ni. Thil pakhat beih leh man theihna awmchhun chu venhim theih loh hmunte beih hi a ni. Defending-a chak taka awm theihna awmchhun chu beih theih lohna hmunte venhim hi a ni. Chuvang chuan, tha taka beitute chuan an hmelma leh a invenna hmun an hre lo. Venghim \hatute chuan hmelma leh a beih tur hmun an hre lo. A fiah lo emaw, fiah lo emaw pawh a ni thei a, mahse hmuh theih loh khawpin a thleng a ni. Pathian a ni em? Pathian a ni em tih ka ngaihtuah a, a ngawih hma loh chuan. Chuvangin hmelma thupek a la fo thin.
Hmasawnna awm chhun chu chu thu dik lo chu paih chhuah hi a ni. I hnungtawlh thei lo a, i zui thei lo a nih chuan i thleng thuai thei lo. Chuvangin, ka beih duh chuan hmêlma chuan a base a chawi sang a, a trench a thuk zawk mai thei a, mahse min beih mai loh chu duhthlan tur dang a neih loh chuan min chhanchhuah theihna hmunah ka beih a ngai a ni. Indo ka duh lo a nih chuan ram chu ka chhinchhiah ang a, ka humhim ang a, mahse hmêlma, min beih mai loh chu duhthlan tur dang nei lo chu chu hmunah chuan a kal ngei ngei tur a ni.

Chuvang chuan, Mite chu Ka pianphungah ka siam loh chuan, chu chu hmelma ka hrethiam thei dawn tihna a ni. Pakhat anga kan inpumkhat a, kan hmelma te hi sawmah kan inthen a nih chuan heng sawm hmang hian pakhat chu kan bei dawn a ni. Phên dangin sawi ila, kan tam a, kan hmelma pawh an tlem hle. Mipui nen mi tlemte ka vuak chuan keimah nena indotute chu an boral vek ang. Kan indona ram hi in hre lo tur a ni. I hriat loh chuan hmelma inbuatsaih mi tam tak an awm. Hmelma hian inbuatsaih tam tak a neih chuan ka bula indote hi an tlem hle a ni. Chuvangin hmalam pan tura inbuatsaih chuan, chu chu hnung lamah a tlem zawk tihna a ni; hnunglam i inbuatsaih chuan, chu chu hmalam lamah tlem zawk tihna a ni; vei lamah i inbuatsaih chuan, chu chu ding lamah tlem zawk tihna a ni; dinglamah i inbuatsaih chuan vei lamah tlem zawk tihna a ni; tin, inbuatsaih tur i neih loh chuan tlakchhamna a awm lo tihna a ni. Hmeithai nihna hi miten an inbuatsaih em em a ni. Mipui chuan midangte chu anmahni tan an buatsaih thin.

Chuvangin indona hmun leh indona ni i hriat chuan mel sangkhat vel i bei tur a ni. Indona hmun leh indona ni i hriat loh chuan veilam chuan dinglam a chhanchhuak thei lova, dinglam chuan veilam a chhanchhuak thei lo va, hmalam chuan hnunglam a chhanchhuak thei lo va, hnunglam pawhin hmalam a chhanhim thei lo. Eng pawh sawi ila, mel sawm tam tak a hla a, mel engemaw zat a hnai bawk. Ewe-ho hian sipai tam tak nei mah se, hnehna leh hnehna kawngah chuan kan tan engmah a hlawkpui dawn lo. Chuvangin hnehna chan a ngai a ni, a ti. Hmelma tam hle mah se, kan bei tur a ni lo.

Chuvangin, hei hi i ruahman chuan hlawkna leh hlohna ruahmanna i hre ang a, hei hi siamin, chetna thu bul i hre ang a, hei hi siamin, nunna leh thihna hmun i hre ang a, chu chu i zawm chuan khawiah nge surplus leh tlakchhamna a awm tih i hre thei ang.

Chuvangin sipai nihna hnuhnung ber chu pianphung nei lo (formlessness) hi a ni. Hmuh theih loh a nih chuan thuk takin hmuh theih loh a ni ang a, mi fingte pawhin ruahmanna an siam thei lo ang. Hnehna chu a form avang hian buaithlak mah se, mipuiin an hre ngai lovang. Mite chuan ka chak chhan form hi an hria a, mahse ka hnehna thununtu chhan form chu an hre lo. Chuvangin, indonaah hnehna chang mah se, phuba a la lo va, zawhna awm lovin a form chu a chhang let zawk a ni.

Sipai pianzia chu tui ang deuh a ni. Tui pianzia chu hnuai lam hawiin a awm a, sang lam a pumpelh. Sipai pianzia hian thil tak tak a pumpelh a, void chu a kap ta a ni. Tui hian leilung a zirin a luang dan a thunun a, sipaite pawhin hmelma a zirin an hnehna an thunun bawk. Chuvangin sipai hian awmna nghet an nei lo va, tui pawhin awm reng a nei lo. Hmelma a zira inthlak danglama hnehna changte chu pathian an ti fo thin. Chuvang chuan element panga-ah chatuan hnehna a awm lo va, dar li-ah pawh dinhmun nghet a awm lo va, ni khatah sei tawi leh sei a awm a, thlaah thihna leh pian a awm bawk.

Bung 7: Sipai Indona

Sun Tzu chuan sipai indona tluka harsa zawk a awm lo tih sawiin, sipai hman a, General pakhatin lal fapa hnen atanga thupek a dawn te, a sipaite inpumkhat tir te, mipui thu ngaithla te, thlamuang taka awm te hi a harsa zawk lo a ni, a ti. Sipai indonaa harsatna awmte hi detour leh harsatna hmanga hriat theih a ni. Chuvangin detour ka la a, midang thlemna hun remchang ka hmang tangkai a, midang hnung ka tan a, midang hmaah ka thleng thin. Hei hi detour trick hre chiang tak a ni.

Chuvangin sipai indona hian hlawkna a thlen a, sipai indona pawh a hlauhawm hle. Sipai i din a, i duhzawng i beih chuan i thleng thei lovang, mahse i sipaite chu i hlawkna tur bei tura i innghah chuan i rilru a buai hle ang. Hemi avang hian sipaiah i inpek loh chuan i thi a, ei tur i neih loh chuan i thi a, i inpek loh chuan i thi a ni. Hemi avang hian a kut a kuah a, a pel a, kawngpui a pelh a, a hlat zawng chu a let hnih a tihpun a, li za chuang zeta hlawkna tur a beih chuan general pathumte chu bei turin a nawr lui ta a ni. Mi chak zawkte an kal hmasa ang a, chau zawkte an hnufual ang a, dan pawh sawm leh pakhat a ni. Li 50 aia tam advantage i beih hian superior general i hmusit a ni. Chu dan chu a chanve vel a ni tawh. Bung 12: Kangmei chhuahna

Sun Tzu sawi dan chuan kangmei chhuahna hi chi nga a awm a. 1 chuan mi hal rawh an ti a, 2 chuan product chu mei rawh an ti a, 3 chuan warehouse hal rawh an ti a, 4 chuan warehouse hal rawh an ti a, 5 chuan squad hal rawh an ti. Bung 13-na: Yoma-hen
