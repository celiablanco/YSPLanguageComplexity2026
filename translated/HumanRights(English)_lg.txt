Ekirangiriro ky’ensi yonna ekikwata ku ddembe ly’obuntu 
Ennyanjula 
So nga okukkiriza ekitiibwa ekizaaliranwa n’ekyenkanankana era ekitaggyibwako 
eddembe ly’abantu bonna mu maka g’omuntu gwe musingi gw’eddembe, obwenkanya 
n'emirembe mu nsi, .  
So nga obutafaayo n’okunyooma eddembe ly’obuntu kivuddemu obukambwe 
ebikolwa ebinyiizizza omuntu ow’omunda, n’okujja kw’ensi 
abantu mwe banaanyumirwa eddembe ly’okwogera n’okukkiriza n’eddembe 
okuva mu kutya n’obwetaavu kirangiriddwa ng’ekiruubirirwa ekisinga obukulu eky’abantu aba bulijjo 
abantu, .  
So nga kyetaagisa nnyo, omuntu bw’aba tawalirizibwa kuddukira, ng’ekisembayo 
ddukira, okujeemera obutyobooli n’okunyigirizibwa, nti eddembe ly’obuntu lirina okuba 
ekuumibwa enfuga y’amateeka, .  
So nga kyetaagisa okutumbula enkolagana ey’omukwano wakati wa... 
amawanga, .  
So nga abantu b’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte mu Ndagaano eno bazzeemu okukakasa... 
okukkiriza mu ddembe ly’obuntu ery’omusingi, mu kitiibwa n’omuwendo gw’omuntu 
ne mu ddembe eryenkanankana ery’abasajja n’abakazi era nga bamaliridde okutumbula 
enkulaakulana mu bantu n’omutindo gw’obulamu omulungi mu ddembe erisingawo, .  
So nga amawanga agali mu mukago geeyamye okutuukiriza, mu nkolagana 
n’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte, okutumbula okussa ekitiibwa mu bantu bonna n’okukuuma... 
eddembe ly’obuntu n’eddembe ery’omusingi, .  
So nga okutegeera okwawamu ku ddembe n’eddembe lino kuli ku... 
obukulu obusinga obunene eri okutuukiriza mu bujjuvu obweyamo buno, .  
Kati n’olwekyo, .
Olukiiko Olukulu,  
Alangirira ekiwandiiko kino eky’ensi yonna eky’eddembe ly’obuntu ng’omutindo ogw’awamu ogwa... 
okutuuka ku bantu bonna n’amawanga gonna, okutuuka ku nkomerero nti buli muntu ssekinnoomu era 
buli kitundu ky’ekibiina, nga kikuuma Ekirangiriro kino buli kiseera mu birowoozo, kijja kufuba okuyita 
 

 
okusomesa n’okusomesa okutumbula okussa ekitiibwa mu ddembe n’eddembe lino era nga... 
enkola ezigenda mu maaso, ez’eggwanga n’ez’ensi yonna, okukuuma zazo eza bonna era 
okukkiriza n’okukuuma obulungi, byombi mu bantu b’amawanga agali mu mukago 
bo bennyini ne mu bantu b’ebitundu ebiri wansi w’obuyinza bwabwe.  
Akawaayiro I  
Abantu bonna bazaalibwa ba ddembe era benkanankana mu kitiibwa n’eddembe. Bano bwe bali 
endowed with reason and conscience era balina okukola eri bannaabwe mu a 
omwoyo gw’obwasseruganda.  
Akawaayiro 2  
Buli muntu alina eddembe n’eddembe lyonna ebiragiddwa mu Kirangiriro kino, . 
awatali kwawula kwa ngeri yonna, gamba ng’amawanga, langi, ekikula, olulimi, eddiini, . 
endowooza y’ebyobufuzi oba endala, ensibuko y’eggwanga oba ey’embeera z’abantu, ebintu, obuzaale oba ekifo ekirala.  
Ekirala, tewali njawulo ejja kukolebwa ku musingi gw’ebyobufuzi, . 
embeera y’obuyinza oba ey’ensi yonna ey’eggwanga oba ekitundu omuntu gy’agenda 
kibeera, ka kibeere nga kyetongodde, kyesiga, ekitali kya kwefuga oba wansi w’omulala yenna 
okukomya obwetwaze.  
Akawaayiro 3  
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’obulamu, eddembe n’obukuumi bw’omuntu.  
Akawaayiro 4  
Tewali muntu yenna anaakwatibwa mu buddu oba mu buddu; obuddu n’okusuubula abaddu binaaba
ebikugirwa mu ngeri zaabyo zonna.  
Akawaayiro 5  
Tewali muntu yenna ajja kutulugunyizibwa oba okuyisibwa mu ngeri ey’obukambwe, etali ya buntu oba okutyoboola 
oba okubonereza.  
 

 
Akawaayiro 6  
Buli muntu alina eddembe okusiimibwa buli wamu ng’omuntu mu maaso g’amateeka.  
Akawaayiro 7  
Bonna benkanankana mu maaso g’amateeka era balina eddembe awatali kusosola kwonna okwenkanankana 
okukuuma amateeka. Bonna balina obukuumi obwenkanankana ku muntu yenna 
okusosola mu kutyoboola Ekirangiriro kino n’okuwakanya okukubiriza kwonna ku ekyo 
okusosola.  
Akawaayiro 8  
Buli muntu alina eddembe okugonjoolwa obulungi okuva mu kkooti z’eggwanga ezirina obuyinza 
olw’ebikolwa ebityoboola eddembe ery’omusingi eryamuweebwa ssemateeka oba mu mateeka.  
Akawaayiro 9  
Tewali muntu yenna ajja kukwatibwa mu ngeri ya kimpowooze, okusibirwa oba okuwangangusibwa.  
Akawaayiro 10  
Buli muntu alina eddembe mu bwenkanya obujjuvu okuwulirwa mu bwenkanya era mu lujjudde okuva eri omuntu eyeetongodde 
ne kkooti etaliimu kyekubiira, mu kusalawo eddembe lye n’obuvunaanyizibwa bwe n’obw’omuntu yenna 
omusango gw’okumuggulwako.  
Akawaayiro 11  
1. Buli muntu avunaanibwa omusango gw’ekibonerezo alina eddembe okutwalibwa 
talina musango okutuusa lw’akakasiddwa nti alina omusango okusinziira ku mateeka mu musango ogw’olukale mwe 
abadde n’obukakafu bwonna obwetaagisa mu kwewozaako kwe.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuvunaanibwa musango gwonna ogw’ekibonerezo olw’ekikolwa kyonna oba 
okulekayo ekitaali musango gwa kibonerezo, wansi w’eggwanga oba 
amateeka g’ensi yonna, mu kiseera we kyakolebwa. Era n’oyo asinga obuzito tajja kukikola
ekibonerezo kiwebwe okusinga ekyo ekyali kikolebwa mu kiseera ekibonerezo 
omusango gwakolebwa.  
Akawaayiro 12  
Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuyingirirwa mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira mu by’ekyama bye, amaka ge, amaka ge 
oba okuwandiikiragana, wadde okulumba ekitiibwa kye n’erinnya lye. Buli omu alina... 
eddembe ly’okukuumibwa amateeka okuva ku kuyingirira oba okulumbibwa okwo.  
Akawaayiro 13  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutambula n’okubeera mu... 
ensalo za buli Ggwanga.  
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okuva mu nsi yonna, omuli n’eye, n’okuva 
okuddayo mu nsi ye.  
Akawaayiro 14  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okunoonya n’okunyumirwa mu mawanga amalala obubudamu okuva 
okuyigganyizibwa.  
2. Eddembe lino teriyinza kukozesebwa mu nsonga z’okuvunaanibwa mu butuufu 
ebiva mu bikolobero ebitali bya byabufuzi oba okuva mu bikolwa ebikontana n’ebigendererwa era 
emisingi gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte.  
Akawaayiro 15  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna eggwanga.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako ggwanga lye mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira wadde okugaanibwa eddembe 
okukyusa eggwanga lye.  
Akawaayiro 16  
 

 
1. Abasajja n’abakazi ab’emyaka emijjuvu, awatali kukoma kwonna olw’amawanga, eggwanga 
oba eddiini, balina eddembe okufumbirwa n’okutandikawo amaka. Balina obuyinza 
okutuuka ku ddembe eryenkanankana ku bufumbo, mu kiseera ky’obufumbo ne mu kusatululwa kwabwo.  
2. Obufumbo bujja kukolebwa nga omuntu alina okukkiriza okw’eddembe era mu bujjuvu 
abagenderera okufumbirwa.  
3. Amaka kye kitundu ky’ekibinja eky’obutonde era eky’omusingi mu bantu era kiri
alina eddembe okukuumibwa abantu n’Eggwanga.  
Akawaayiro 17  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera n’ebintu yekka nga kw’otadde n’okukolagana ne 
abalala.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako bintu bye mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira.  
Akawaayiro 18  
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okulowooza, omuntu ow’omunda n’eddiini; kino eddembe 
kizingiramu eddembe okukyusa eddiini ye oba enzikiriza ye, n’eddembe, oba yekka oba mu 
ekitundu n’abalala ne mu lujjudde oba mu kyama, okulaga eddiini ye oba enzikiriza ye 
okusomesa, okwegezaamu, okusinza n’okukuuma.  
Akawaayiro 19  
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okulowooza n’okwogera; eddembe lino lirimu 
eddembe ly’okukwata endowooza awatali kutaataaganyizibwa n’okunoonya, okufuna n’okubunyisa 
amawulire n’ebirowoozo nga biyita mu mikutu gyonna era awatali kulowooza ku nsalo.  
Akawaayiro 20  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okukuŋŋaana n’okwegatta mu mirembe.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ayinza kuwalirizibwa kubeera mu kibiina.  
Akawaayiro 21  
 

 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwetaba mu gavumenti y’eggwanga lye, . 
butereevu oba nga bayita mu bakiise abalondeddwa mu ddembe.  
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwenkanankana mu mirimu gya gavumenti mu nsi ye.  
3. Ekiraamo ky’abantu kye kinaabeera omusingi gw’obuyinza bwa gavumenti; 
kino ekiraamo kijja kulagibwa mu kulonda okubaawo buli luvannyuma lwa kiseera era okwa nnamaddala okujja 
ebeere mu ddembe ly’okulonda erya bonna era eryenkanankana era ejja kukuumibwa mu kalulu ak’ekyama oba 
emitendera egy’okulonda egy’obwereere egy’enkanankana.  
Akawaayiro 22
Buli muntu, ng’omuntu mu kibiina, alina eddembe okufuna obukuumi bw’abantu era alina eddembe 
okutuukiriza, okuyita mu kaweefube w’eggwanga n’enkolagana y’ensi yonna ne mu 
okusinziira ku nteekateeka n’eby’obugagga bya buli Ggwanga, eby’ebyenfuna, . 
eddembe ly’embeera z’abantu n’ebyobuwangwa eryetaagisa nnyo olw’ekitiibwa kye n’enkulaakulana ey’eddembe 
ku buntu bwe.  
Akawaayiro 23  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukola, okusalawo emirimu mu ddembe, okukola obwenkanya era 
embeera ennungi ey’emirimu n’okukuuma ebbula ly’emirimu.  
2. Buli muntu, awatali kusosola kwonna, alina eddembe okusasulwa kyenkanyi olw’okwenkanankana 
okukola.  
3. Buli muntu akola alina eddembe okusasulwa mu bwenkanya era mu ngeri ennungi 
okukakasa ye n’ab’omu maka ge okubeerawo okusaanira ekitiibwa ky’omuntu, . 
era nga byongerwako, bwe kiba kyetaagisa, mu ngeri endala ez’obukuumi bw’abantu.  
4. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutandikawo n’okwegatta ku bibiina by’abakozi olw’obukuumi bwa... 
by’ayagala.  
Akawaayiro 24  
Buli muntu alina eddembe okuwummulamu n’okuwummulamu, nga mw’otwalidde n’okukoma ku 
essaawa z’okukola n’ennaku enkulu buli luvannyuma lwa kiseera nga zisasulwa.  
 

 
Akawaayiro 25  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna omutindo gw’obulamu ogumala obulamu n’... 
obulungi bwe n’ab’omu maka ge, omuli emmere, engoye, ennyumba 
n‟obujjanjabi n‟obuweereza obwetaagisa mu mbeera z‟abantu, n‟eddembe ly‟obukuumi 
mu mbeera y’ebbula ly’emirimu, obulwadde, obulemu, obwannamwandu, obukadde oba 
ebirala obutaba na bye yeeyimirizaawo mu mbeera ezitasobola kufuga.
2. Obumaama n’obuto birina okulabirirwa n’okuyambibwa mu ngeri ey’enjawulo. Byonna 
abaana, oba abazaalibwa mu bufumbo oba ebweru w’obufumbo, bajja kunyumirwa embeera y’emu 
obukuumi.  
Akawaayiro 26  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okusomesebwa. Okusomesa kujja kuba kwa bwereere, waakiri mu... 
emitendera egy’olubereberye n’egy’omusingi. Okusoma kwa pulayimale kujja kuba... 
ekikakatako. Okusomesa eby’ekikugu n’eby’ekikugu kujja kukolebwa 
okutwaliza awamu n’amatendekero aga waggulu gajja kutuukirirwa kyenkanyi eri bonna 
ku musingi gw’obulungi.  
2. Okusomesa kujja kutunuulirwa mu nkulaakulana y’omuntu mu bujjuvu 
omuntu n’okunyweza okussa ekitiibwa mu ddembe ly’obuntu n’ 
eddembe ery’omusingi. Kijja kutumbula okutegeeragana, okugumiikiriza n’... 
omukwano wakati w’amawanga gonna, ebibinja by’amawanga oba eby’eddiini, era kijja kwongera 
emirimu gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte olw’okukuuma emirembe.  
3. Abazadde balina eddembe nga tebannaba kulonda ekika ky’obuyigirize ekinaaba 
eziweebwa abaana baabwe.  
Akawaayiro 27  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwetaba mu bulamu bw’obuwangwa bw’abantu mu ddembe 
ekitundu, okunyumirwa eby’emikono n’okugabana mu kukulaakulana mu bya ssaayansi n’... 
emigaso gyayo.  
 

 
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuuma empisa n’ebintu 
ekiva mu kukola kwonna okwa ssaayansi, ebiwandiiko oba okw’ekikugu kw’ali 
omuwandiisi.  
Akawaayiro 28  
Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera n’enteekateeka y’embeera z’abantu n’ensi yonna omuli eddembe n’... 
eddembe eriragiddwa mu Kirangiriro kino liyinza okutuukirira mu bujjuvu.  
Akawaayiro 29
1. Buli muntu alina emirimu eri ekitundu mu ekyo kyokka eky’eddembe era ekijjudde 
okukulaakulanya omuntu we kisoboka.  
2. Mu kukozesa eddembe lye n’eddembe lye, buli muntu ajja kufugibwa yekka 
ku bukwakkulizo obwo nga bwe bulambikibwa mu mateeka olw’ekigendererwa kyokka 
okulaba ng’eddembe n’eddembe ly’abalala bakkirizibwa n’okussibwamu ekitiibwa ekituufu 
n’okutuukiriza ebisaanyizo eby’obwenkanya eby’empisa, enteekateeka y’olukale n’... 
obulungi obw’awamu mu nsi ya demokulasiya.  
3. Eddembe n’eddembe lino mu ngeri yonna tebiyinza kukozesebwa kikontana na... 
ebigendererwa n’emisingi gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte.  
Akawaayiro 30  
Tewali kintu kyonna mu Kirangiriro kino kiyinza kutaputibwa ng’ekitegeeza Eggwanga lyonna, ekibiina oba 
omuntu eddembe lyonna okwenyigira mu mulimu gwonna oba okukola ekikolwa kyonna ekigendereddwamu 
okusaanyaawo eddembe lyonna n’eddembe eriragiddwa wano.
