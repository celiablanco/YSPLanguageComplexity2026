Kuzivisa Kwekodzero Dzevanhu 
Preamble 
Tichifunga kuti kucherechedzwa kwechiremerera chekuzvarwa kwenhengo dzese dzemhuri yemunhu uye kodzero dzavo 
zvakaenzana uye zvisingabvisiki zvinoumba hwaro hwerusununguko, kururamisira nerunyararo munyika;  
Tichifunga nezvekusaziva uye kusaremekedza kodzero dzevanhu kwakatungamirira kuzviito zvehutsinye kutsamwa ikoko 
hana yeMunhu uye kuti kuuya kwenyika umo vanhu vakasununguka kutaura uye kutenda, 
asunungurwa kubva mukutya nenhamo, akaziviswa sefemero yepamusoro-soro yeMunhu;  
Tichifunga kuti kuchengetedzwa kwekodzero dzevanhu kuburikidza nehutongi hwemutemo kwakakosha, kuti murume 
regai kumanikidzwa, nepamusoro-soro, kumukira udzvinyiriri neudzvinyiriri;  
Nepo zvakakosha kukurudzira kuvandudzwa kwehukama hwehukama pakati penyika;  
Tichifunga kuti, muChata, vanhu veUnited Nations vanozivisa, zvakare, kutenda kwavo mukodzero dzakakosha dze 
Murume, mukuremekedzwa nekukosha kwemunhu, mukodzero dzakaenzana dzevarume nevakadzi uye kana 
vakazivisa kutsunga kwavo kusimudzira kufambira mberi kwevanhu uye kugadza mamiriro ekurarama ari nani mukati merusununguko 
wider;  
Tichifunga kuti nyika dzenhengo dzakazvipira kusimudzira, mukubatana neSangano re 
Mubatanidzwa Wenyika Dzepasi Pose, ruremekedzo rwepasi rose uye runoshanda rwekodzero dzevanhu nerusununguko rwakakosha;
Tichifunga kuti fungidziro yakafanana yekodzero idzi nerusununguko ndiyo inonyanya kukosha kuzere 
kugutsikana kwekuzvipira kwakadaro:  
General Assembly inozivisa iyi Universal Declaration  
yeKodzero dzevanhu  
sechinhu chakafanana chinofanirwa kuwanikwa nevanhu vese nemarudzi ese, kuti vanhu vese nenhengo dzese dze 
nzanga, vachiva nazvo nguva dzose mundangariro, vanoedza, nokudzidzisa nedzidzo, kukudziridza ruremekedzo 
yekodzero idzi nerusununguko uye nekusimudzira, kuburikidza nematanho anofambira mberi enyika nepasirese, avo 
kucherechedzwa kwepasirese uye kunoshanda uye kushandiswa kwese pakati pehuwandu hwenhengo dzenhengo pachadzo uye 
pakati peiyo yenzvimbo dzakaiswa pasi pesimba rayo.  
Chisungo chekutanga  
Vanhu vose vanozvarwa vakasununguka uye vakaenzana pachiremera nekodzero. Vakapiwa pfungwa nehana, vanofanira 
itirana mumwe nomumwe mumweya woushamwari.  
Chisungo chechipiri  

Vanhu vese vanogona kushandisa kodzero nerusununguko zvakaziviswa muChiziviso ichi, pasina musiyano 
chero, kureva rudzi, ruvara, zvepabonde, mutauro, chitendero, zvematongerwo enyika kana mamwe maonero, mavambo enyika 
kana magariro, rombo, kuzvarwa kana chero mamiriro ezvinhu. 
Uyezve, hapana mutsauko uchaitwa uchienderana nemamiriro ezvematongerwo enyika, emutemo kana epasirese enyika kana 
nharaunda yekuzvarwa kwemunhu, ingave nyika iyoyo kana nharaunda yakazvimirira, iri pasi pekuchengetwa, kuzvitonga kana kuzviisa pasi peimwe
kuganhurirwa kwouchangamire.  
Chisungo chetatu 
Munhu wese anekodzero yehupenyu, rusununguko nekuchengetedzeka kwake.  
Chisungo chechina  
Hapana achabatwa muuranda kana muuranda; uranda uye kubatwa kwevaranda, mumhando dzose, ndizvo 
kurambidzwa.  
Chisungo chechishanu  
Hapana munhu achatambudzwa kana kuitirwa hutsinye, kusiririsa kana kubatwa zvisina kunaka.  
Chisungo chechitanhatu 
Vanhu vese vane kodzero yekucherechedzwa, kwese kwese, kwehunhu hwavo hwepamutemo.  
Chisungo chechinomwe  
Vese vakaenzana pamberi pemutemo uye, pasina mutsauko, vanekodzero yekuchengetedzwa zvakaenzana nemutemo. Munhu wese anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa zvakaenzana 
pamusoro perusarura rupi zvarwo runotyora Chirevo ichi uye nekukurudzira kusarura kwakadai.  
Chisungo chechisere 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuwana masimba emunyika akakodzera achipikisana nezviito zvinotyora mutemo 
kodzero dzakakoshesesa dzinocherechedzwa neBumbiro reMitemo kana mutemo.  
Chisungo chechipfumbamwe 
Hapana munhu anogona kusungwa, kuvharirwa kana kudzingwa pasina.  
Chisungo chegumi  

Munhu wese anekodzero, mukuenzana kuzere, kuti nyaya yake itongwe zvakanaka uye zviri pachena nedare 
sarudzo yakazvimirira uye isina kwayakarerekera pakodzero dzako nezvisungo zvako kana zvikonzero zvekupomerwa kupi zvako munyaya 
chirango chaanopomerwa.  
Chisungo 11 
1. 
Munhu wese anopomerwa mhosva yekupara mhosva anofungidzirwa kuti haana mhosva kusvika azowanikwa aine mhosva.
zvinopupurirwa zviri pamutemo mukati mehurongwa hweveruzhinji umo vimbiso dzese dzinodiwa dzekudzivirira dzinopihwa 
vanovimbiswa.  
2. 
Hapana anozopihwa mhosva yezviito kana kusiya izvo, panguva yekutumwa kwavo, hazvina kuita chiito. 
mhosva pasi pemutemo wemunyika kana wepasi rose. Saizvozvo, hapana chirango chakaomarara chichapihwa kupfuura 
izvo zvaishanda panguva yakaitwa chiito chemhosva.  
Chisungo 12  
Hapana achatambura kupindira zvisina tsarukano muhupenyu hwavo hwepachivande, mhuri yavo, imba yavo kana yavo 
tsamba, kana kurwisa kukudzwa kwake nemukurumbira. Kurwisa kupindira kana kurwiswa kwakadaro munhu wese ane kodzero yeku 
kuchengetedzwa kwemutemo.  
Chisungo 13  
1. 
Munhu wese anekodzero yekufamba akasununguka kusarudza pekugara mukati meNyika.  
2. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokubuda munyika yaari, kusanganisira yake, uye anekodzero yokudzokera 
kunyika yako.  
Chisungo 14  
1. 
Munhu wese ari pasi pekutambudzwa anekodzero yekutsvaga nekubatsirwa nehupoteri kune dzimwe nyika.  
2. 
Kodzero iyi haikwanise, zvisinei, kushandiswa kana iri nyaya iripo yemhosva pasi pemutemo. 
kana mabasa anopesana nezvinangwa nemisimboti yeUnited Nations.  
Chisungo 15  
1. 
Munhu wese anekodzero yekuve nenyika.  
2. 
Hapana anogona kubvisirwa rudzi rwake kana kodzero yekuchinja nyika yake.  
Chisungo 16  
1.
Kubva pazera rekuroora, varume nevakadzi vane kodzero yekuroora uye kuwana mhuri, pasina zvirambidzo. 
yerudzi, nyika kana chitendero. Munguva yewanano uye panguva yekuparara kwayo, mapato ese ane kodzero 
kuenzana.  
2. 
Muchato haugone kupembererwa pasina kusununguka uye kuzere kubvumirana kwevachazoroora kana kuroorwa.  
3. 
Mhuri chinhu chakasikwa uye chakakosha chenzanga uye ine kodzero yekudzivirirwa kubva munzanga neHurumende.  

Chisungo 17  
1. 
Munhu wese, ega kana seboka, ane kodzero yekuwana midziyo.  
2. 
Hapana anogona kubvisirwa pfuma yake nemadiro.  
Chisungo 18 
Munhu wese anekodzero yerusununguko rwekufunga, rwehana uye rwechitendero; Kodzero iyi inoreva rusununguko rwe 
shandura chitendero chako kana kutenda kwako, pamwe nerusununguko rwekuratidza chitendero chako kana kutenda, wega kana mukati 
zvinozivikanwa, zvose pachena uye zvakavanzika, kuburikidza nekudzidzisa, tsika, kunamata uye tsika.  
Chisungo 19 
Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva nerusununguko rwekutaura zvaanofunga uye rekutaura izvo zvinoreva kodzero yekusakanganiswa 
maonero avo uye kutsvaga, kugamuchira nekuparadzira, pasina hanya nemiganhu, ruzivo uye pfungwa chero ipi zvayo 
nzira yekutaura.

Chisungo 22  
Munhu wese, senhengo yenharaunda, anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa kwemagariro evanhu; uye angada zviri pamutemo kugutsikana kwe 
Kodzero dzinokosha dzehupfumi, dzemagariro netsika, nekuda kwekuedza kwenyika pamwe nekudyidzana nedzimwe nyika, ye 
kuwirirana nesangano nezviwanikwa zvenyika imwe neimwe.  
Chisungo 23 
1. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekushanda, kusarudza basa rakasununguka, kuva nemamiriro ekushanda akaenzana uye anogutsa 
basa nekudzivirirwa pakurovha.  
2. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero, pasina rusaruro rupi zvarwo, yekuwana muhoro wakaenzana nebasa rakaenzana.  

3. 
Ani naani anoshanda anekodzero yekuwana mubairo wakaenzana uye unogutsa, uyo unovabvumira ivo nemhuri yavo 
kuvapo maererano nechiremera chemunhu, uye zvichiwedzerwa, kana zvichibvira, nedzimwe nzira dzose 
kuchengetedzwa kwevanhu.  
4. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuwana mibatanidzwa nevamwe uye kuva nhengo yemasangano ekudzivirira 
zvezvaunofarira.  
Chisungo 24
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuzorora nekutandara, kunyanya, nemiganhu ine mufungo panguva yebasa uye 
periodic yakabhadharwa zororo.  
Chisungo 25 
1. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekurarama hupenyu hwakakwana kuti ave neutano hwakanaka nekugara zvakanaka kwake nemhuri yake
kuve, kunyanya maererano nezvekudya, zvipfeko, pekugara, rubatsiro rwekurapa uye zvakare maererano 
anodiwa masevhisi emagariro, uye ane kodzero yekuchengetedzwa muchiitiko chekushaikwa kwemabasa, kurwara, kuremara, 
kufirwa, kuchembera kana dzimwe nyaya dzekurasikirwa nechokurarama nacho nekuda kwemamiriro ezvinhu akazvimirira 
kuda kwako.  
2.

Kunze kweroorano, vanofarikanya dziviriro yakafanana yenzanga.  
Chisungo 26  
1. 
Munhu wese anekodzero yekuwana dzidzo. Dzidzo inofanira kuva yemahara, inowirirana nedzidziso 
fundamental primary. Dzidzo yekutanga inosungirwa. Dzidzo yehunyanzvi uye yehunyanzvi inofanirwa kuve 
widespread; Kuwana kuzvidzidzo zvepamusoro kunofanirwa kuvhurika kune vese mukuenzana kuzere, zvichienderana ne 
kukodzera kwayo.  
2. 
Dzidzo inofanira kunangana nekuwedzera kuzere kwehunhu hwevanhu uye kusimbiswa kwekodzero dzevanhu uye 
rusununguko runokosha uye runofanira kukurudzira kunzwisisa, kushivirira uye ushamwari pakati pemarudzi ose uye 
mapoka ose emarudzi kana ezvitendero, pamwe chete nekuvandudzwa kwemabasa eUnited Nations nokuda kwe 
kuchengetedza runyararo.  
3.
Vabereki vane kodzero yekutanga kusarudza rudzi rwedzidzo ichapihwa kuvana vavo.  
Chisungo 27  
1. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekutora chikamu akasununguka chetsika nemagariro emunharaunda, kunakidzwa nemitambo uye 
kutora rutivi mukufambira mberi kwesayenzi nebetsero dzinobva mazviri.  
2. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa kwetsika neupfumi zvine chekuita nenganonyorwa ipi zvayo yesainzi 
kana basa reunyanzvi rako pachako.  
Chisungo 28  
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva nemutemo unobata magariro evanhu kana pasi rose, unokwanisa kuita zvizere 
kodzero nerusununguko zvakataurwa muChiziviso chino zvinoshanda.
