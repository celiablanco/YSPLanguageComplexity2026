13. Hmelma rilru put hmang kan hmuhchhuah a, keimahni ngei pawh hmuh theih loha kan awm hian kan sipaite chu kan inzawm khawm thei a, hmêlma erawh chu kan inthen darh a ngai thung.

14. Taksa inpumkhatna pakhat kan siam thei a, chutih laiin hmelma chu fraction-ah a inthen a ngai thung. Chuvangin, pum pui pakhat chu a pum pui hmun hrang hrang nen a inhmachhawn ang a, chu chu hmêlma tlemte tan chuan kan tam ang tihna a ni.

15. Tin, chutianga chakna hnuaihnung zawk chu a chungnung zawk hmanga kan beih theih chuan kan khingpuite chu harsatna nasa tak an tawk dawn a ni.

16. Kan indo tumna hmun hi hriattir tur a ni lo; chutih hunah chuan hmêlma chuan hmun hrang hranga beih theih a nih loh nân a inbuatsaih a ngai dâwn si a; tin, chutianga a sipaite chu kawng tam takah an insem a, hmun eng pawha kan hmachhawn tur zat chu a zatvein a tlem ang.

17. Hmelmain a van a tihchak chuan a hnunglam a tichak lo ang; a hnunglam a tihchak chuan a van a tichak lo ang; a veilam a tihchak chuan a dinglam chu a tichak lo ang; a dinglam a tihchak chuan a veilam a tichak lo ang. Khawi hmunah pawh reinforcement a tirh chuan khawi hmunah pawh a chak lo ang.

18. Numerical weakness hi attack awm thei laka inbuatsaih a ngai atanga lo chhuak a ni a; numerical strength, kan hmelma chu kan laka heng inbuatsaihna siam tura nawr luih a\angin.

19. Indona lo thleng tur hmun leh hun hre rengin, indona tur atan hmun hla ber a\angin kan rilru kan pe thei a ni.

20. Mahse hun leh hmun hriat a nih loh chuan veilam wing chuan dinglam chu a pui thei lo ang a, dinglam pawhin veilam chu a pui thei lo ang a, van chuan hnunglam a ti hahdam thei lo ang a, hnung lam pawhin van chu a thlawp thei lo ang. Sipai hmun hla ber berte chu LI za hnuai lam eng pawh ni se, a hnai ber pawh LI engemaw zatin an inthen a nih chuan a va ni zawk ang!

21. Ka chhut dan chuan Yueh sipaite hi keimahni sipaite aia tam mah sela, chu chuan hnehna chungchangah engmah a hlawkpui lo vang. Chutah chuan hnehna a chang thei tih ka sawi a ni.

22. Hmelma chu a tam zawkah chak zawk mah se, a indona chu kan dang thei a ni. A ruahmanna leh a hlawhtlin theih dan tur hriatchhuah nan scheme siam rawh.

23. Amah chu kaitho la, a thiltih emaw, a thiltih loh emaw thu bul chu zir rawh. Amah leh amah inpuang turin nawr luih la, chutiang chuan a hmun hlauhawm tak takte chu a hriat theih nan.

24. Sipai dodaltu chu i sipai nen uluk takin khaikhin la, chutiang chuan chakna a tamna hmun leh a tlakchhamna hmun i hre thei ang.

25. Tactical disposition siamnaah hian i pitch sang ber chu thup hi a ni a; i rilru put hmang chu thup la, enthlatu fiah lo ber berte chhui chhuah lakah, thluak fing ber berte ruahmanna lakah i him ang.

26. Hmelma ngeiin an tan hnehna a lo chhuah theih dan tur--chu chu mipuiin an hriatthiam theih loh chu a ni.
27. Mi zawng zawng hian ka hneh theihna tur tactics hi an hmu thei a, mahse tumahin an hmuh theih loh chu hnehna lo chhuahna strategy hi a ni.

.

29. Sipai hmanrua chu tui ang a ni a; tui chu a pianphungah chuan hmun sang tak aṭangin a tlan bo a, a hnuai lam a pan nghal vat si a.

30. Chuvangin indonaah chuan kawng chu thil chak tak pumpelh a, thil chak lo beih hi a ni.

31. Tui hian a luangna leilung awm dan azirin a kalna tur a siam a; sipai chuan a hmachhawn tur hmelma nen inzawmin a hnehna chu a thawk chhuak thin.

32. Chuvangin tuiin a pianzia a vawng reng lo ang bawkin indonaah pawh dinhmun awm reng a awm lo.

33. A khingpui nena inzawm a a tactics siam danglam thei a, chuta tang chuan hnehna chang thei chu vanrama piang captain tia sawi theih a ni.

34. Element panga (tui, mei, thing, thir, lei) te hi a inang tlang vek lo va; season li hian a inthlak danglamin hmun a siamsak a ni. Ni tawi leh rei a awm a; thla hian a hun lai a nei a, a tlahniam leh a lo hring chhuak thin.

VII. Maneuvering tih a ni

1. Sun Tzu chuan: Indonaah chuan general chuan a thupek chu lalber hnen atangin a dawng thin.

2. Sipai a khawn khawm a, a sipaite a khawm a, a camp a siam hmain a element hrang hrangte chu a pawlh leh a inmil tir tur a ni.

3. Chumi hnuah chuan tactical maneuvering a lo thleng a, chu aia harsa zawk chu a awm lo. Tactical maneuvering harsatna chu devious chu direct-ah a chantir a, vanduai chu gain-ah a chantir bawk.

4. Chutiang chuan, hmêlma chu kawng aṭanga hruai chhuah hnua, a hnung lama ṭan mah se, a hmaa thil tum thleng tûra ruahmanna siam chu, deviation artifice hriatna a ni.

5. Sipai nena inrem (maneuvring) hi a hlawkthlak a; mipui thununna nei lo, hlauhawm ber nen.

6. Advantage lak tumin sipai tha tak tak march-ah i set a nih chuan i tlai lutuk theihna chance a awm. Kawng lehlamah chuan, chu mi atâna flying column chu detach tûr chuan a bungrua leh a dahna hmunte chu hlan a ngai a ni.

7. Chutiang chuan i sipaite chu an buff-coat hrual turin thu i pe a, ni leh zan pawh ding lovin, a tlangpui aia a let hnih zeta hlat zawng chu a hrual vek a, advantage lak tumin LI za zet i tih chuan, i division pathum hruaitute chu hmêlma kutah an tlu vek ang.

8. Mi chak zawkte chu hmaah an awm ang a, jaded zawkte chu an tla thla ang a, he ruahmannaah hian i sipai hmun sawma ṭhena hmun khat chauhvin an tumna hmun an thleng ang.

9. Hmelma hneh tumin LI sawmnga i kal chuan i division hmasa ber hruaitu i hloh ang a, i chakna chanve chauhvin tum an thleng ang.

10. Thil pakhat nen LI sawmthum i kal chuan i sipai hmun thuma ṭhena hmun hnih an lo thleng ang.

11. Chuti a nih chuan sipai baggage-train nei lo chu a bo tih kan la thei ang; ei tur awm lovin a bo a; bases of supply awm lovin a bo thin.

12. Kan ṭhenawm ramte ruahmanna kan hriat chian hma chuan inzawmna kan siam thei lo.

13. Ram hmel--a tlang leh ramngaw, a khur leh hnim hring, a marsh leh swamp te kan hriat chian loh chuan sipai kaihruai turin kan tling lo.

14. Local guide kan hman loh chuan natural advantage chu account-ah kan chantir thei dawn lo.

15. Indonaah chuan inbumna (dissimulation) hmang la, i hlawhtling ang.

16. I sipaite inzawmkhawm tur nge inthen dawn tih chu thil awmdanin a rel tur a ni.

17. I chakna chu thlipui ang ni rawh se, i compactness chu ramngaw ang ni rawh se.

18. Beih leh rawknaah mei ang ni la, che thei lo chu tlang ang a ni.

19. I ruahmannate chu zan angin thim leh luh theih loh ni rawh se, i che velin zing khawvar ang maiin tla rawh se.

20. Ramhnuai in rawk dawnin rawk chu in mite hnenah sem sela; ram thar i lak hunah chuan sipaite hlawkna tur atan allotment-ah then rawh.

21. I thil tih hmain ngaihtuah la, ngaihtuah hmasa phawt ang che.

22. Deviation artifice zir tawh chu a hneh ang. Chutiang chu maneuvering art chu a ni.

23. Book of Army Management chuan heti hian a sawi: Indona hmunah chuan thu sawi chuan hla tak a phur lo: chuvang chuan gong leh drums din a ni ta a ni. Thil pangngai pawh chiang taka hmuh theih a ni lo: chuvang chuan banner leh flag din a ni ta a ni.

24. Gong leh drum, banner leh flag te hi host beng leh mit chu hmun bik pakhata dah theihna hmanrua a ni.
. Hei hi mipa tam tak enkawl dan thiamna a ni.

26. Chuti a nih chuan, zan lama indonaah chuan signal-fire leh drum te, nitin indonaah te, flag leh banner te hmang nasa la, in sipaite beng leh mit nghawng nan hmang rawh.

27. Sipai pum pui chu an thlarau laksak an ni thei a; commander-in-chief pakhat chu a rilru awmna chu rukbo a ni thei.

28. Tunah chuan sipai thlarau chu zîngah a fiah ber a; chawhnu lamah chuan a flag tan tawh a; tin, tlai lamah chuan a rilru chu camp-a kir leh chauh a ni.

29. Chuvangin general fing tak chuan sipai thlarau a fiah lai chuan a pumpelh a, mahse a rilru a hah a, kir leh duhin a bei thung. Hei hi moods zir chianna art a ni.

30. Disciplined leh calm, hmelma zinga buaina leh buaina lo lang tur nghak tur:--hei hi mahni inrintawkna vawn nun thiamna a ni.

31. Hmelmain a la hlat laiin tum hnaih taka awm te, hmêlma thawhrim leh beih laia thlamuang taka nghah te, hmêlma riltam laiin chaw ṭha tak neih te:--hei hi mahni chakna pasal neih thiamna a ni.

.

33. Hmelma laka tlang sang taka hmasawn loh, tlang atanga a lo chhuk hunah pawh dodal loh chu sipai axiom a ni.

34. Tlanna ang chi hmelma chu zui suh; sipai thinur nasa tak takte chu bei suh.

35. Hmelma thilpek bait chu ei suh. Sipai in lama haw tur chu tibuai suh.

36. Sipai i hual hian outlet free-in dah la. Hmelma beidawng tak chu nawr na lutuk suh.

37. Chutiang chu indona thiamna chu a ni.

VIII. Tactics hrang hrang

1. Sun Tzu chuan heti hian a sawi: Indonaah chuan General chuan a thupekte chu lalber hnen atangin a dawng a, a sipaite a khawn khawm a, a sipaite a dahkhawm thin

2. Ram harsaah chuan riahbuai suh. Kawngpui sang tak tak inzawmna ramah chuan i ṭangrual ramte nen kut inthlak rawh. Hlauhawm taka inhlat hmunah awm reng suh. Hemmed-in situation-ah chuan stratagem i hmang tur a ni. Beidawng dinhmunah chuan i beih a ngai a ni.

3. Kawngpui zawh loh tur, sipai beih loh tur, khawpui hual tur, inel loh tur dinhmun, lal thupek zawm loh tur a awm.

4. General chuan variation of tactics a kaihhnawih thatna chiang taka hrethiam chuan a sipaite enkawl dan a hre chiang hle.

5. Chungte hrethiam lo general chuan ram insiam dan a hre chiang hle mai thei a, chutih rualin a hriatna chu practical account-ah a chantir thei dawn lo.

6. Chuvangin, indona zirlai, a ruahmanna hrang hrangte indona lama thiamna nei lo chuan, A thatna panga chu hre chiang mah se, a mite chu a hmang tangkai thei lo vang.

7. Chuvangin hruaitu fing ruahmannaah chuan hlawkna leh that lohna ngaihtuah te chu a inzawm khawm ang.

8. Chutianga hlawkna kan beisei chu tihziaawm a nih chuan kan scheme-a thil pawimawh tak chu kan hlen chhuak thei a ni.

9. Kawng lehlamah chuan harsatna karah pawh hlawkna pakhat lak tumin kan inpeih reng a nih chuan vanduaina ata kan inhnukdawk thei a ni.

10. Hmelma lalte chu chhiatna thlenin tihtlem; an tan buaina siam la, inhmang reng rawh; hold out specious allurements, eng point pawh thlenga hmanhmawh tir rawh.

11. Indona thiamna hian hmêlma lo kal loh theihnaah innghat lovin, amah dawng tura mahni inpeihnaah innghat turin min zirtir a; a beih loh theihna chance vang ni lovin, kan dinhmun chu beih theih loha kan siam vang zawk a ni.

12. General pakhat nghawng thei tur dik lohna hlauhawm panga a awm a: (1) Recklessness, chhiatna thlentu; (2) hlauhawmna, chu chuan man theihna a thlen a; (3) hmanhmawh taka thinrimna, sawichhiatna avanga tihthinrim theih; (4) chawimawina chaw tui tak, mualphona laka sensitive; (5) a mite tana thil dil lutuk, chu chuan lungkhamna leh buaina a thlen thin.

13. Hengte hi general pakhat sual panga, indona kalpui dan tichhe thei tak takte chu an ni.

14. Sipai pakhat chu paihthlak a nih a, a hruaitu thah a nih chuan, a chhan chu heng dik lohna hlauhawm panga zingah hian hmuh tur a awm ngei ang. Ngaihtuahna thupui ni rawh se.

IX. Army te chuan March thla khan

1. Sun Tzu chuan heti hian a sawi: Tunah chuan sipai riahhmun, leh hmêlma chhinchhiahna enfiah chungchang hi kan lo thleng ta a ni. Tlâng chungah rang takin kal la, ruam bulah awm rawh.
2. Hmun sang takah, ni lam hawiin camp siam rawh. Insual tur chuan sang takah chho suh. Tlâng indona atan chuan chutiang chu a ni.

3. Lui kan pelh hnu chuan hla takah i kal tur a ni.

4. Invading force pakhatin a hmalam panna kawngah lui a pelh chuan mid-stream-a hmu turin hma la suh. Sipai chanve vel chu an pelh theih nan phalsak la, chutah chuan i beihna chu i rawn pe chhuak ang.

5. Indo tura i lungkham a nih chuan, a paltlang tur lui bulah chuan rawn beitu hmu turin i kal tur a ni lo.

6. I craft chu hmêlma aia sang zawkah, ni lam hawiin moor rawh. Hmelma hmachhawn turin up-stream-ah kal suh. Lui indona atan chuan chutiang chu a ni.

7. Salt-marshes paltlang kawngah hian i ngaihtuah ber tur chu rang taka, tihkhawtlai loha i paltlang mai tur a ni.

8. Salt-marsh-a indo tura nawr i nih chuan i bulah tui leh thlai i nei tur a ni a, i hnungzang chu thingpui huanah i hruai tur a ni. Chutiang chuan salt-marches-a operation neihna atan chuan a tam hle.

9. Ram vawt leh level-ah chuan i dinglamah leh i hnung lamah lei sang tak awmna hmun awlsam takah la la, chutiang chuan hlauhawm chu hma lamah a awm thei ang a, himna chu hnung lamah a awm thei ang. Chutiang chuan flat country-a campaign-na atan chuan a tam hle.

10. Hengte hi sipai hriatna peng tangkai pali, Yellow Emperor-in lal pali engemaw zat a hneh theihna tura puitu te chu an ni.

11. Sipai zawng zawng hian hmun sang aiin hmun hniam leh ni chhuahna hmun thim aiin an duh zawk.

12. In mite lakah in fimkhur a, ram khauh takah in riahh chuan sipaite chu natna chi hrang hrang lakah an fihlim ang a, hei hian hnehna a thlen ang.

13. Tlâng emaw, bank emaw i thlen chuan ni chhuahna lam chu luah la, i hnunglam dinglamah chuan tlang chhip chu luah rawh. Chutiang chuan vawi khatah i sipaite hlawkna tur atan i che ang a, leia natural advantages chu i hmang tangkai ang.

14. Up-country-a ruah nasa tak sur avanga lui i luang duh chu a lo hring a, foam-in a lo hring a nih chuan a reh hma chu i nghak tur a ni.

.

16. Chutiang hmun a\anga kan inthiarfihlim lai hian hmêlma chu an hnaih tir tur a ni; kan hmachhawn lai hian hmêlma chu a hnung lamah kan dah mai tur a ni.

17. I camp chhehvelah hian tlangram, tui chhunga thlai hual vel tuikhuah, hnim hring hring hring hring, emaw, thinghnah hnuai lam thuk tak tak awmna emaw a awm a nih chuan, uluk takin an chhuahsan tur a ni a, an zawng chhuak tur a ni chûngte chu mipa inkhuar emaw, enthlatu râpthlâk tak emaw awmna hmun a nih avângin.

18. Hmelma chu a hnaih a, a ngawi reng chuan a dinhmun pianpui chaknaah a innghat tihna a ni.

19. A inthiarfihlim a, indona tiharh a tum hian a lehlamah hma a sawn theih nan a lungkham hle.

20. A campna hmun chu a awlsam a nih chuan bait a tender a ni.

21. Ramngaw thing zinga inher hian hmêlma chu hma a sawn tih a tilang. Thingpui huan hring kara screen engemaw zat a lo lang hian hmêlma hian rinhlelhna min siam duh tihna a ni.

22. Savate an thlawh laia an tho chhuah hi ambuscade chhinchhiahna a ni. Sakawlh mangang tak takte chuan beihpui thlak nghal a lo thleng dawn tih a tilang a ni.

23. Kawngkhar sang takah leivut a lo chhuak chuan tawlailir hmasawn chhinchhiahna a ni a; leivut a hniam a, mahse hmun zau takah a darh chuan kea kal sipaite hnaih chu a tilang a ni. Kawng hrang hranga a branch chhuah chuan party-te chu thingpui lum la turin tirh an nih thu a tarlang a ni. Leivut chhum tlemte a kal kual vel hian sipaite chu an awm tih a tilang a ni.

24. Inngaitlawm taka tawngkam leh inbuatsaihna nasa zawk hi hmelmain hma an sawn dawn tih chhinchhiahna a ni. Tharum thawhna tawngkam leh beihpui thlakna tur ang maia hmalam pan chu a hnungtawlh dawn tih hriattirna a ni.

.

26. Thutiam thutiam nena inzawm lo remna rawtna chuan ruahmanna siam a nih thu a tarlang.

27. Tlan kual tam tak a awm a, sipaite chu rank-a an tlak chuan hun pawimawh tak a lo thleng ta tihna a ni.

28. Ṭhenkhat chu hma an sawn a, ṭhenkhat chu hnungtawlh lai hmuh a nih chuan, hipna a ni.

29. Sipaiho chu an fei nghatin an din chuan ei tur tlakchhamna avangin an chau thin.

30. Tui chawi tura tirhte'n mahni in in an tan chuan sipaite hian tuihalna an tuar tihna a ni.

31. Hmelma chuan hlawkna hmuh tur a hmuh a, chu chu humhim tumin theihtawp a chhuah loh chuan sipaite chu an chau hle.

32. Khawi hmunah pawh sava an inkhawm chuan mi awm lohna a ni. Zan lama thawm ri hian hlauhna a tilang.
33. Camp-a buaina a awm chuan General thuneihna a chak lo. Banner leh flag te chu an inthlak kual a nih chuan sedition a kal zel a ni. Officer-te an thinrim chuan mipa an chau tihna a ni.

36. Lawmman pek tam lutuk hian hmelma chu a thil neih tawp lamah a awm tih a tilang a; hremna tam lutuk chuan lungngaihna nasa tak dinhmun a phatsan a ni.

37. Bluster hmanga tan a, mahse a hnuah hmêlma number-a hlauhna neih chuan finna tlakchhamna sang ber a lantir a ni.

38. Palaite chu an kâa fakna thu kenga tirh an nih hian hmêlmain inkahhai an duh tih chhinchhiahna a ni.

40. Kan sipaite hi hmelma aia tam an tam lo a nih chuan chu chu a tawk tawk a ni; direct attack engmah tih theih a ni lo tihna chauh a ni. Kan tih theih chu kan chakna neih zawng zawng kan dah khawm a, hmêlma chu ngun taka kan en reng a, chakna kan neih mai hi a ni.

41. Ngaihtuahna hmasa nei lo va, a khingpuite chu a ti eng a nih chuan an man ngei ang.

42. Sipaite chu nangmah an lo innghah hmaa hrem an nih chuan an intulût lo vang; tin, thuhnuairawlh a nih loh chuan, chuti a nih chuan a tangkai lo tak zet ang. Sipaiten an inzawm tawh che hunah hremnate chu tihpuitlin a nih loh chuan, a nih loh chuan a la ni reng ang.

43. Chuvangin sipaite chu a hmasa berah chuan mihringte nena enkawl tur a ni a, mahse thir discipline hmanga thununna hnuaia awm tur a ni. Hei hi hnehna kawng chiang tak a ni.

44. Sipai training-naah thupekte tihpuitlin a nih chuan sipaite chu an inthunun tha ang; a nih loh chuan a thununna chu a chhe hle ang.

45. General pakhatin a mite chungah rinna a lantir a, mahse a thupek zawm tura a nawr reng chuan hlawkna chu inzawmkhawm a ni ang.

X. Ramhnuai (terrain) a ni

1. Sun Tzu chuan heti hian a sawi: Terrain chi ruk kan thliar hrang thei a, chungte chu: (1) Accessible ground; (2) lei inzawmkhawm; (3) hun eng emaw chen awmna hmun; (4) kalna kawng tawi tak; (5) hmun sang tak tak; (6) hmêlma atanga hla takah position hrang hrang a awm.

2. Lei chu a sir lehlamah zalen taka kan paltlang theih chu accessible an ti.

3. Hetiang ground chungchangah hian hmun sang leh ni chhuahna hmun luah kawngah hmêlma hmaah awm la, i line of supplies chu uluk takin veng rawh. Tichuan advantage nen i bei thei tawh ang.

4. Lei chu chhuahsan theih mahse luah leh harsa tak chu entangling an ti.

5. Hetiang dinhmun atang hian hmelma chu a inpeih lo a nih chuan i sally chhuak thei a, i hneh thei bawk. Mahse, hmêlma chu i lo kal lehna atân a inbuatsaih a, i hneh loh chuan, kir leh chu thil theih lohvin, chhiatna a lo thleng ang.

6. Position chu a hmasa ber tih avanga a lam leh lam pawhin an hlawkpui loh tur khawpa dinhmun a nih chuan temporizing ground an ti a.

. tichuan, a sipai ṭhenkhat an lo chhuah hunah chuan kan beihna chu hlawkna nen kan pe thei ang.

8. Narrow pass chungchangah chuan i luah hmasa thei a nih chuan chak takin sipai la, hmêlma lo kal hun chu nghak rawh.

9. Sipaiten pass luah turin an tikhawtlai che a nih chuan, pass chu garrisoned kim a nih chuan a hnungah kal suh, garrisoned chak lo a nih chauhvin.

10. Hmun sang tak tak chungchangah chuan i hmelma nen in awm hmasa a nih chuan hmun sang leh ni chhuahna hmunte chu i luah tur a ni a, chutah chuan a lo chhoh hun chu nghak tur a ni.

11. I hmaa hmelmain a lo luah tawh a nih chuan zui suh la, hnungtawlh la, thlem tum rawh.
