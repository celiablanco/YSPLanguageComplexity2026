IsiBhengezo seHlabathi samaLungelo oLuntu 
Intshayelelo 
Ukuthathela ingqalelo ukuba ukuqatshelwa kwesidima sendalo sawo onke amalungu osapho loluntu kunye namalungelo awo 
ukulingana nokungenakohluthwa kwenza isiseko senkululeko, sobulungisa noxolo ehlabathini;  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba ukungazi nokungakhathalelwa kwamalungelo abantu kuye kwakhokelela kwizenzo zobukrwada ngaloo msindo 
isazela soBuntu kunye nokuba ukufika kwehlabathi apho abantu bakhululekile ukuthetha kwaye bakholelwe, 
ekhululwe kuloyiko nakwinkxwaleko, wabhengezwa njengeyona mpembelelo iphakamileyo yoMntu;  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba ukukhuselwa kwamalungelo oluntu ngolawulo lomthetho kubalulekile, ukuze umntu 
musani ukunyanzelwa ukuba nivukele ubuzwilakhe nengcinezelo;  
Nangona kubalulekile ukukhuthaza uphuhliso lobudlelwane bobuhlobo phakathi kwezizwe;  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba, kuMqulu, abantu beZizwe eziManyeneyo bavakalisa kwakhona, ukholo lwabo kumalungelo asisiseko oluntu. 
Umntu, kwisidima kunye nexabiso lomntu womntu, kumalungelo alinganayo amadoda kunye nabasetyhini kwaye ukuba 
babhengeze isigqibo sabo sokukhuthaza inkqubela-phambili yoluntu kunye nokuseka iimeko ezingcono zokuhlala ngaphakathi kwenkululeko 
banzi;  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba amazwe angamalungu azibophelele ekukhuthazeni, ngentsebenziswano noMbutho we 
IZizwe eziManyeneyo, intlonipho yehlabathi jikelele nesebenzayo kumalungelo oluntu kunye nesiseko senkululeko;
Ukuqwalasela ukuba ingcamango efanayo yala malungelo kunye nenkululeko ibaluleke kakhulu ngokupheleleyo 
ulwaneliseko lokuzinikela okunjalo:

La malungelo neenkululeko kunye nokukhuthaza, ngokwenkqubela phambili yesizwe nemilinganiselo yezizwe ngezizwe, yawo 
ukuqondwa kunye nokusetyenziswa jikelele kunye nokusebenzayo phakathi kwabemi bamazwe angamalungu ngokwabo kunye 
phakathi kwaleyo yemimandla ebekwe phantsi kolawulo lwayo.  
Inqaku loku-1  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile kwaye belingana ngesidima nangamalungelo. Ekubeni benikwe ingqiqo nesazela, bamele 
sebenzani omnye komnye ngomoya wobuzalwana.  
Inqaku lesi-2  

Bonke abantu bangasebenzisa amalungelo kunye nenkululeko ebhengezwe kwesi siBhengezo, ngaphandle kokwahlulo 
naluphi na uhlanga, ibala, isini, ulwimi, inkolo, uluvo lwezopolitiko okanye olunye uhlobo, imvelaphi yobuzwe 
okanye ezentlalo, ithamsanqa, ukuzalwa okanye nayiphi na enye imeko. 
Ngaphaya koko, akukho mahluko uya kwenziwa ngokusekelwe kwimo yezopolitiko, esemthethweni okanye yamazwe ngamazwe yelizwe okanye 
Ummandla wokuzalwa komntu, nokuba lelo lizwe okanye ngummandla ozimeleyo, phantsi kogcino, ukuzimela okanye kuxhomekeke kwinto ethile.
ukusikelwa umda kolongamo.  
Inqaku lesi-3 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kunye nokhuseleko lomntu.  
Inqaku lesi-4  
Akukho mntu uya kubanjwa ebukhobokeni okanye ebukhobokeni; ubukhoboka kunye nokuphathwa kwamakhoboka, ngazo zonke iindlela, zinjalo 
kuthintelwe.  
Inqaku lesi-5  
Akukho mntu uya kungcungcuthekiswa okanye ngenkohlakalo, ngendlela engenabuntu okanye ethotywa isidima.  
Inqaku lesi-6 
Bonke abantu banelungelo lokwamkelwa, kuyo yonke indawo, ubuntu babo obusemthethweni.  
Inqaku lesi-7  
Bonke bayalingana phambi komthetho kwaye, ngaphandle kokwahlula, banelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo lomthetho. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo 
ngokuchasene nalo naluphi na ucalucalulo olwaphula esi siBhengezo kunye nakuko nakuphi na ukuphembelela olocalucalulo.  
Inqaku lesi-8 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufikelela kumagunya esizwe afanelekileyo ngokuchasene nezenzo ezaphula umthetho 
amalungelo asisiseko amkelwa nguMgaqo-siseko okanye ngumthetho.  
Inqaku lesi-9 
Akukho mntu uvumelekileyo ukuba abanjwe, avalelwe okanye agxothwe elubhacweni.  
Inqaku le-10  

Wonke umntu unelungelo, ngokulingana ngokupheleleyo, ukuba ityala lakhe ligwetywe ngokungenamkhethe nangokusekuhleni yinkundla 
isigqibo esizimeleyo nesingenamkhethe kumalungelo nezibophelelo zakho okanye izizathu zaso nasiphi na isityholo kumcimbi 
isohlwayo abekwa sona.  
Inqaku le-11 
1. 
Wonke umntu otyholwa ngesenzo solwaphulo-mthetho uthathwa njengomntu ongenatyala ade afunyanwe enetyala.
ingqinwe ngokusemthethweni ngexesha lenkqubo yoluntu apho zonke iziqinisekiso zokhuseleko eziyimfuneko zibonelelwa 
ziqinisekisiwe.  
2. 
Akukho mntu uya kugwetywa ngezenzo okanye izinto ezingenziwanga, ngexesha lokuthunywa kwabo, akuzange kube senzo. 
ulwaphulo-mthetho phantsi komthetho wasekhaya okanye wamazwe ngamazwe. Ngokunjalo, akukho sohlwayo siqatha siya kwenziwa 
oko kwakusebenzayo ngexesha lokwenziwa kolwaphulo-mthetho.  
Inqaku le-12  
Akukho mntu uya kuphazanyiswa kubomi bakhe babucala, kusapho lwakhe, kwikhaya labo okanye kwabo 
ngembalelwano, okanye ukuhlasela isidima nesidima sakhe. Ngokuchasene nokuphazanyiswa okanye ukuhlaselwa okunjalo wonke umntu unelungelo loku 
ukhuseleko lomthetho.  
Inqaku le-13  
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululekileyo kwaye azikhethele indawo yokuhlala kuRhulumente.  
2. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokushiya ilizwe akulo, kuquka nelakhe, kunye nelungelo lokubuyela 
kwilizwe lakho.  
Inqaku le-14  
1. 
Wonke umntu ophantsi kwentshutshiso unelungelo lokufuna kwaye axhamle kwindawo yokhuseleko kwamanye amazwe.  
2. 
Nangona kunjalo, eli lungelo alinakusetyenziswa kwimeko yeenkqubo ezikhoyo zolwaphulo-mthetho phantsi komthetho. 
okanye kwimisebenzi echasene neenjongo nemithetho-siseko yeZizwe eziManyeneyo.  
Inqaku le-15  
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuba nobuzwe.  
2. 
Akukho mntu unokuhluthwa ubuzwe bakhe okanye ilungelo lokutshintsha ubuzwe bakhe.  
Inqaku le-16  
1.
Ukususela kwiminyaka yomtshato, amadoda nabafazi banelungelo lokutshata baze babe nosapho, ngaphandle kwemiqathango. 
yobuhlanga, ubuzwe okanye inkolo. Ngexesha lomtshato nangexesha lokuchithwa kwawo, omabini amaqela anamalungelo 
ngokulinganayo.  
2. 
Umtshato awunakubhiyozelwa ngaphandle kwemvume ekhululekileyo nepheleleyo yabatshati bexesha elizayo.  
3. 
Usapho yinxalenye yendalo nesisiseko soluntu kwaye inelungelo lokukhuselwa kuluntu nakuRhulumente.  

Inqaku le-17  
1. 
Wonke umntu, eyedwa okanye ngokudibeneyo, unelungelo kwipropati.  
2. 
Akukho mntu unokuhluthwa impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
Inqaku le-18 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucinga, isazela nenkolo; Eli lungelo lithetha inkululeko ye 
tshintsha inkolo okanye inkolelo yakho, kunye nenkululeko yokubonakalisa inkolo okanye inkolelo yakho, uwedwa okanye ngaphakathi 
eqhelekileyo, kokubini esidlangalaleni nangasese, ngokufundisa, ngokwenza, unqulo namasiko.  
Inqaku le-19 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuvakalisa izimvo zakhe, nto leyo ethetha ukuba unelungelo lokungaphazanyiswa 
izimvo zabo kunye nokufuna, ukufumana nokusasaza, ngaphandle kokujonga imida, ulwazi kunye neengcinga ngayo nayiphi na 
indlela yokubonakalisa.  
Inqaku le-20 ° 
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yeendibano zoxolo kunye neendibano zoxolo.  
2. 
Akukho mntu unokunyanzelwa ukuba abe yinxalenye yombutho.  
Inqaku lama-21  
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthatha inxaxheba kulawulo lwemicimbi yoluntu yelizwe lakhe, nokuba uthe ngqo,
okanye ngabameli abanyulwe ngokukhululekileyo.  
2. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufikelela, phantsi kweemeko ezilinganayo, kwimisebenzi yoluntu yelizwe lakhe.  
3. 
Intando yabantu sisiseko segunya lamagunya oluntu: kwaye kufuneka ibonakaliswe ngonyulo 
unyulo olunyanisekileyo kufuneka lwenziwe ngamaxesha athile ngokuvunyelwa jikelele nangokulinganayo, ngevoti eyimfihlo okanye inkqubo yesibini. 
elilinganayo elikhusela inkululeko yokuvota.  
Inqaku lama-22  
Wonke umntu, njengelungu loluntu, unelungelo lokhuseleko loluntu; kwaye inokufuna ngokusemthethweni ukwaneliseka kwe 
amalungelo ayimfuneko ezoqoqosho, ezentlalo nenkcubeko, ngenxa yemizamo yesizwe kunye nentsebenziswano yamazwe ngamazwe, ye 
imvisiswano nombutho kunye nemithombo yelizwe ngalinye.  
Inqaku lama-23 
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukuzikhethela umsebenzi ngokukhululekileyo, kwiimeko zokusebenza ezinobulungisa kunye nezanelisayo 
umsebenzi kunye nokhuseleko ngokuchasene nentswela-ngqesho.  
2. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo, ngaphandle komkhethe, lokufumana umvuzo olinganayo ngomsebenzi olinganayo.  

3. 
Nabani na osebenzayo unelungelo lomvuzo olinganayo nowanelisayo, ovumela yena nosapho lwakhe ukuba bafumane umvuzo olinganayo nowanelisayo. 
ubukho ngokuhambelana nesidima somntu, kwaye kongezwe, ukuba kunokwenzeka, ngazo zonke ezinye iindlela 
ukhuseleko loluntu.  
4. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufumana iimanyano nabanye kunye nokujoyina imibutho yombutho wokhuselo 
zomdla wakho.  
Inqaku lama-24
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphumla kunye nokuzonwabisa, ngokukodwa, ukulinganiselwa okufanelekileyo ngexesha lomsebenzi kunye 
ngamaxesha athile iholide ehlawulweyo.  
Inqaku lama-25 
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lomgangatho wokuphila owaneleyo ukuze aqinisekise impilo kunye nokuphila kakuhle kwakhe nosapho lwakhe
ibe, ubukhulu becala malunga nokutya, impahla, indawo yokuhlala, uncedo lwezonyango kananjalo ngokwemiqathango 
iinkonzo zentlalo eziyimfuneko, kwaye unelungelo lokhuseleko xa kukho ukungasebenzi, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, 
ubuhlolokazi, ukwaluphala okanye ezinye iimeko zokuphulukana nendlela yokuphila ngenxa yeemeko ezizimeleyo 
intando yakho.  
2. 
Ukubeleka kunye nobuntwana banelungelo loncedo olukhethekileyo kunye noncedo. Bonke abantwana, abazalelwe okanye 
Ngaphandle komtshato, banandipha ukhuseleko olufanayo loluntu.  
Inqaku lama-26  
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo mayibe yesimahla, ubuncinane engqinelana nokufundisa 
esisiseko. Imfundo yamabanga aphantsi inyanzelekile. Imfundo yobugcisa kunye nobungcali kufuneka 
ngokubanzi; Ukufikelela kwizifundo eziphezulu kufuneka kuvuleke kubo bonke ngokulingana okupheleleyo, ngokuxhomekeke kwi 
Ukufaneleka kwayo.  
2. 
Imfundo kufuneka ijolise ekwandiseni ngokupheleleyo ubuntu bomntu kunye nokuqiniswa kwamalungelo oluntu kunye 
iinkululeko ezisisiseko kwaye kufuneka ikhuthaze ukuqonda, ukunyamezelana kunye nobuhlobo phakathi kwezizwe zonke kunye 
onke amaqela eentlanga okanye ezenkolo, kunye nophuhliso lwemisebenzi yeZizwe eziManyeneyo 
ukugcina uxolo.  
3.
Abazali banelungelo eliphambili lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo abaza kuyinikwa abantwana babo.  
Inqaku lama-27  
1. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba ngokukhululekileyo kubomi benkcubeko yoluntu, ukonwabela ubugcisa kunye 
inxaxheba kwinkqubela yenzululwazi neengenelo ezibangelwa yiyo.  
2. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa ngokweemfuno zokuziphatha kunye nezinto eziphathekayo ezinxulumene nayo nayiphi na imveliso yenzululwazi, yoncwadi 
okanye umsebenzi wakho wobugcisa.  
Inqaku lama-28  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuba nomyalelo olawulayo, kwinqwelo-moya yentlalontle neyezizwe ngezizwe, ekwaziyo ukuwenza ngokupheleleyo 
amalungelo neenkululeko ezichazwe kwesi siBhengezo ayasebenza.

1. 
Umntu unemisebenzi eluntwini, ngaphandle kwalo uphuhliso lwasimahla nolupheleleyo alunakwenzeka.

2. 
Ekusetyenzisweni kwala malungelo kunye nokonwabela ezi nkululeko, akukho mntu uphantsi kwemiqathango 
esekwe ngumthetho ngenjongo eyodwa yokukhuthaza ukuqondwa nokuhlonitshwa kwamalungelo kunye 
inkululeko yabanye kunye nokwanelisa iimfuno ezisesikweni zokuziphatha, ucwangco loluntu kunye nentlalo-ntle
