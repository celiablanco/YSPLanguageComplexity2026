Isimemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu 
Yamukelwa futhi yamenyezelwa yi-General Assembly esinqumweni sayo sama-217 A (III), sokuthi 
Disemba 10, 1948  
Isendlalelo  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi inkululeko, ubulungisa nokuthula emhlabeni kusekelwe kukho 
ukuqashelwa kwesithunzi sangaphakathi namalungelo alinganayo nangenakugwenywa 
yawo wonke amalungu omndeni womuntu,  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi ukungazi nokudelela amalungelo 
abantu baye babangela izenzo zobuqaba ezicasulayo kunembeza womuntu 
ubuntu; futhi lokho kuye kwamenyezelwa, njengesifiso esiphezulu se- 
umuntu, ukufika komhlaba lapho abantu, bakhululeka khona 
ukwesaba nosizi, ujabulele inkululeko yokukhuluma nenkululeko 
izinkolelo,  
Ngokubheka kubalulekile ukuthi amalungelo abantu avikelwe yi-a 
ukubusa koMthetho, ukuze umuntu angaphoqwa ukuba abe phezulu 
umthombo wokuvukela ubushiqela nengcindezelo,  
Ukucatshangelwa futhi kubalulekile ukukhuthaza ukuthuthukiswa kobudlelwano obunobungani 
phakathi kwezizwe,  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi abantu beNhlangano Yezizwe baphinde baqinisekisa ku 
Bhala ukholo lwakho emalungelweni ayisisekelo omuntu, ngesithunzi nangokubaluleka 
womuntu kanye namalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabesifazane; futhi 
baye bamemezela ukuzimisela kwabo ukukhuthaza inqubekelaphambili yomphakathi kanye nokukhuphula izinga lokuphila 
ngaphakathi komqondo obanzi wenkululeko,
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi Amazwe Angamalungu azibophezele ekuqinisekiseni, ngo 
ukusebenzisana neNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene, ukuhlonipha umhlaba wonke kanye 
ngempumelelo emalungelweni ayisisekelo kanye nenkululeko yomuntu, futhi  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi umbono ofanayo ngalawa malungelo nenkululeko 
ukubaluleka okukhulu kakhulu ekugcwalisekeni okuphelele kwalokhu kuzibophezela,  
 

 
I-General Assembly  
Imemezela i-Universal Declaration of Human Rights yamanje njengento efanelekile 
umgomo ofanayo bonke abantu nezizwe okufanele bawuphokophele, ukuze 
kokubili abantu nezikhungo, njalo bethola ugqozi kukho, 
ukuthuthukisa, ngokufundisa nemfundo, ukuhlonishwa kwala malungelo 
kanye nenkululeko, kanye nokuqinisekisa, ngokusebenzisa izinyathelo eziqhubekayo kazwelonke kanye 
umthetho wamazwe ngamazwe, ukuqashelwa kwawo jikelele kanye nokusebenza ngempumelelo, kokubili phakathi 
abantu baMazwe Angamalungu kanye naphakathi kwalezo zindawo ezibekiwe 
ngaphansi kwamandla ayo.  
I-athikili 1  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi belingana ngesithunzi namalungelo futhi, 
njengoba bephiwe ukucabanga nonembeza, kufanele baziphathe ngokuhlanganyela 
nomunye nomunye.

noma yisiphi esinye isimo.

I-athikili yesi-6  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo, yonke indawo, lokuqashelwa kwabo

I-athikili yesi-7  
Bonke bayalingana phambi komthetho futhi, ngaphandle kokuhlukaniswa, banelungelo lokuvikelwa okulinganayo 
womthetho. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo kukho konke ukucwaswa 
wephula lesi Simemezelo futhi ngokumelene nanoma yikuphi ukucasulwa kwalolo bandlululo.  
Isigaba 8  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuthola ikhambi elisebenzayo phambi kwezinkantolo zikazwelonke. 
iziphathimandla ezinekhono, ukuze zibavikele ezenzweni ezephula amalungelo abo 
amalungelo ayisisekelo aqashelwa umthethosisekelo noma umthetho.  
I-athikili yesi-9  
Akekho ovunyelwe ukuboshwa ngokungafanele, aboshwe noma adingiswe.  
Isigaba 10  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo, ngaphansi kwezimo zokulingana okuphelele, lokulalelwa
esidlangalaleni nangokungenzeleli yinkantolo ezimele futhi engachemile, ye 
 

 
ukunqunywa kwamalungelo nezibopho zabo noma ukuhlolwa kwanoma iyiphi 
icala abekwe lona ezindabeni zobugebengu.  
Isigaba 11  
1. Wonke umuntu osolwa ngobugebengu unelungelo lokuthi kuthathwe ngokuthi ungubani 
ubumsulwa kuze kutholakale ukuthi unecala, ngokomthetho nangomthetho 
icala lomphakathi lapho zonke iziqinisekiso ziqinisekisiwe 
okudingekayo ekuzivikeleni kwayo.  
2. Akekho oyolahlwa ngezenzo noma ukweqiwa okwenzeka ngesikhathi 
ababezenzile kwakungebona ubugebengu ngaphansi komthetho kazwelonke noma wamazwe ngamazwe. 
Futhi ngeke kukhishwe isijeziso esinzima kunaleso esasisebenza ngesikhathi sokwenza kanjalo 
ukwenziwa kobugebengu.  
Isigaba 12  
Akekho oyoba ngaphansi kokuphazanyiswa ngokungafanele empilweni yakhe yangasese, umndeni wakhe, wabo 
ikhaya noma izincwadi zakhe, noma ukuhlaselwa kwesithunzi noma isithunzi sakhe. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngumthetho kulokho kugxambukela noma 
ukuhlasela.  
Isigaba 13  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululeka futhi azikhethele indawo yokuhlala 
endaweni yoMbuso.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokushiya noma yiliphi izwe, kuhlanganise nelakhe, kanye 
ukuze abuyele ezweni lakubo.  
Isigaba 14  
1. Esimeni sokushushiswa, wonke umuntu unelungelo lokucela ukukhoseliswa, kanye noku 
Kujabulele, kunoma yiliphi izwe.  
 

 
2. Leli lungelo angeke lisetshenziswe ngokumelene nesenzo sokwahlulela ngempela 
abangelwa ubugebengu obuvamile noma ngezenzo eziphambene nezinhloso kanye
imigomo yeNhlangano Yezizwe.  
Isigaba 15  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lobuzwe.  
2. Akekho oyophucwa ubuzwe bakhe noma ilungelo lakhe 
shintsha ubuzwe.  
Isigaba 16  
1. Amadoda nabesifazane, kusukela eminyakeni yokushada, banelungelo, ngaphandle 
noma yikuphi ukuvinjelwa ngezizathu zohlanga, ubuzwe noma inkolo, ukushada 
futhi wathola umndeni; futhi sizojabulela amalungelo alinganayo maqondana 
umshado, ngesikhathi somshado kanye nalapho kuchithwa umshado.  
2. Kuphela ngemvume yamahhala negcwele yabaganene bakusasa abangakwazi 
shada.  
3. Umndeni uyingxenye yemvelo neyisisekelo emphakathini futhi uye 
ilungelo lokuvikelwa komphakathi kanye noMbuso.  
Isigaba 17  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lempahla, ngabanye nangamaqoqo.  
2. Akekho oyophucwa impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
Isigaba 18  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga, unembeza kanye 
inkolo; Leli lungelo lihlanganisa inkululeko yokuguqula inkolo noma izinkolelo, kanye 
njengenkululeko yokuveza inkolo noma izinkolelo zomuntu, ngamunye kanye 
ngokuhlanganyela, kokubili emphakathini nangasese, ngokufundisa, ngokwenza, 
ukukhonza nokugcina.  
 

 
Isigaba 19  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokubeka imibono neyokuveza imibono yakhe; lokhu 
ilungelo lihlanganisa ukungaphazanyiswa ngenxa yemibono yomuntu, ilungelo lokuphenya kanye
ukuthola ulwazi nemibono, nokuyisabalalisa, ngaphandle komkhawulo wemingcele, 
nganoma iyiphi indlela yokukhuluma.  
Isigaba 20  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana nokuzihlanganisa 
enokuthula.  
2. Akekho ongaphoqwa ukuthi abe yilungu lenhlangano.  
Isigaba 21  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza kuhulumeni wezwe lakhe, 
ngokuqondile noma ngabameleli abakhethwe ngokukhululekile.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokufinyelela, ngaphansi kwemibandela yokulingana, ukuze 
imisebenzi yomphakathi yezwe labo.  
3. Intando yabantu iyisisekelo segunya lamandla omphakathi; lokhu 
izovezwa ngezinqumo eziyiqiniso okufanele zibe khona 
zibanjwa ngezikhathi ezithile, ngelungelo lokugunyaza elilinganayo nelilinganayo kanye nevoti 
imfihlo noma enye inqubo efanayo eqinisekisa inkululeko ye 
ivoti.  
Isigaba 22  
Wonke umuntu, njengelungu lomphakathi, unelungelo lokuphepha 
kwezenhlalo, kanye nokuthola, ngomzamo kazwelonke kanye nokubambisana kwamazwe ngamazwe, 
kucatshangelwa inhlangano nezinsiza zoMbuso ngamunye, ukwaneliseka 
amalungelo ezomnotho, ezenhlalakahle kanye namasiko, abalulekile esithunzini sabo 
kanye nokuthuthukiswa okukhululekile kobuntu babo.  
 

 
Isigaba 23  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusebenza, lokuzikhethela ngokukhululekile umsebenzi 
izimo ezilinganayo nezanelisayo zomsebenzi kanye nokuvikelwa kuzo 
ukungasebenzi. 
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo, ngaphandle kokubandlululwa, lokukhokhelwa ngokulinganayo
Ngisebenza ngokufanayo.  
3. Wonke umuntu osebenzayo unelungelo lokuthola iholo elilinganayo kanye 
okwanelisayo, okuqinisekisa ukuthi yena, kanye nomndeni wakhe, ukuphila 
ngokuhambisana nesithunzi somuntu futhi lokho kuzoqedwa, uma kwenzeka 
okudingekayo, nganoma iyiphi enye indlela yokuvikela umphakathi.  
4. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusungula izinyunyana kanye nokujoyina izinyunyana 
ukuvikela izithakazelo zabo.  
Isigaba 24  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula, lokujabulela isikhathi esikhululekile, a 
umkhawulo ozwakalayo wesikhathi somsebenzi namaholide ezikhathi ezithile 
ikhokhiwe.  
Isigaba 25  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lezinga lempilo elanele eliqinisekisayo, 
kanye nomndeni wakho, impilo kanye nenhlalakahle, futhi ikakhulukazi 
ukudla, okokugqoka, izindlu, ukunakekelwa kwezempilo kanye nezinsiza 
kudingekile emphakathini Futhi unelungelo lomshwalense uma kwenzeka 
ukungasebenzi, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, ubufelokazi, ukuguga nezinye izimo zokulahlekelwa 
indlela yabo yokuziphilisa ngenxa yezimo ezingahlangene nazo 
intando.  
2. Ubumama nobungane bunelungelo lokunakekelwa nokusiza 
okukhethekile. Zonke izingane, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi zizalwe emshadweni noma ngaphandle komshado 
umshado, unelungelo lokuvikelwa komphakathi ngokulinganayo.  
 

 
Isigaba 26  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo kufanele ibe 
mahhala, okungenani maqondana nemfundo yamabanga aphansi kanye 
okuyisisekelo. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi iyoba yimpoqo. Isiyalezo
ezobuchwephesha nezobungcweti kuyodingeka zenziwe zibe jikelele; ukufinyelela ezifundweni 
abaphathi bazofana kubo bonke, kuye ngokufaneleka kwabo.  
2. Imfundo izogxila ekuthuthukisweni okuphelele kobuntu 
amalungelo abantu kanye nokuqiniswa kokuhlonishwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye 
izinkululeko eziyisisekelo; izothuthukisa ukuqonda, ukubekezelelana kanye 
ubungane phakathi kwazo zonke izizwe nawo wonke amaqembu ezizwe noma ezenkolo; futhi 
ukukhuthaza ukuthuthukiswa kwemisebenzi yeNhlangano Yezizwe 
ukugcina ukuthula.  
3. Abazali bayoba nelungelo elikhethekile lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo 
lokho kuyakunikwa abantwana babo.  
Isigaba 27  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ngokukhululeka empilweni yamasiko 
womphakathi, ukujabulela ezobuciko nokubamba iqhaza enqubeni 
ezesayensi kanye nezinzuzo eziwumphumela.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa kwezinhloso zokuziphatha kanye 
izinto ezihambisana nayo ngenxa yemikhiqizo yesayensi, 
imisebenzi yemibhalo noma yobuciko engumbhali wayo.  
Isigaba 28  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusungula uhlelo lokuhlalisana kwabantu kanye nolwamazwe ngamazwe 
lapho kwenziwa khona amalungelo nenkululeko amenyezelwe kulesi siMemezelo 
ngempumelelo ngokugcwele.  
Isigaba 29  
 

 
1. Wonke umuntu unemisebenzi maqondana nomphakathi, kusukela ku- 
angakwazi ukuthuthukisa ubuntu bakhe ngokukhululeka nangokugcwele.  
2. Ekusebenziseni amalungelo abo nasekujabuleleni inkululeko yabo, bonke
umuntu uzoba ngaphansi kwemikhawulo ebekwe umthetho 
ngenhloso eyodwa yokuqinisekisa ukuhlonishwa nokuhlonipha 
amalungelo nenkululeko yabanye, kanye nokwanelisa izimfuno ezinobulungiswa 
zokuziphatha, ukuhleleka komphakathi kanye nokuphila kahle emphakathini 
yentando yeningi.  
3. Lawa malungelo kanye nenkululeko akumele kusetshenziswe ngaphansi kwanoma yiziphi izimo 
ukuphikisa izinhloso nemigomo yeNhlangano Yezizwe. 
Isigaba 30  
Akukho kulesi simemezelo okungahunyushwa njengokusho lokho 
linikeza noma yiliphi ilungelo kuHulumeni, iqembu noma kumuntu, lokuthatha 
futhi athuthukise imisebenzi noma enze izenzo ezihloselwe ukucindezelwa kwanoma yikuphi 
wamalungelo nenkululeko emenyezelwe kulesi Simemezelo.
