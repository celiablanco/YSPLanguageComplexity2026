Vaive harahwa yaibata hove yega muruzevha muGulf Stream uye vakange vaita mazuva makumi masere nemana vasina kubata hove. Pamazuva makumi mana okutanga mumwe mukomana akanga anaye. Asi mushure memazuva makumi mana asina hove vabereki vomukomana vakanga vamuudza kuti harahwa yakanga zvino zvirokwazvo uye pakupedzisira salao, iyo ndiyo nzira yakaipisisa yerombo, uye mukomana akanga aenda sezvavakarayira mune chimwe chikepe chakabata hove nhatu dzakanaka vhiki yokutanga. Zvakamurwadza mukomana kuona harahwa ichiuya zuva rega rega nechikepe chake chisina chinhu uye nguva dzose aidzika kunomubatsira kutakura mitsara yakamonereka kana kuti mhungu nehaponi neseiri rakanga rakasungirirwa padanda rechikepe. Seiri racho rakanga rakanamirwa masaga eupfu uye, rainge rakaputirwa, raiita semureza wokukundwa zvachose.
