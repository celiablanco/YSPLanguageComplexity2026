Essuula 1: Okuteekateeka

Sun Tzu yagamba nti omujaasi kikulu eri eggwanga, ekifo ky’obulamu n’okufa, ekkubo ly’okubeerawo n’okufa, era alina okutegeerwa.

N’olwekyo, okuyita mu kino, nnina ebintu bitaano, era okugezesa kino, nnina enteekateeka, era nnoonya enneewulira. Esooka lye kkubo, eryokubiri lye ggulu, ery’okusatu lye nsi, ery’okuna lye lya bulijjo, ate ery’okutaano lye tteeka.

Ekkubo abantu babeere ku lupapula lumu n’abo abali waggulu waabwe, bafiire nabo n’okubeera nabo, n’obutatya kabi. Eggulu yin ne yang, nnyogovu n’ebbugumu, era lirimu okusika omuguwa. Ensi eri wala era eri kumpi, ya bulabe era nnyangu, ngazi era nfunda, n’obulamu n’okufa. General kitegeeza amagezi, okukkiriza, obulungi, obuvumu, n’obukakanyavu. Etteeka y’enkola, engeri entongole, era ekigendererwa ekikulu.

Wadde Genero tajja kuwulira ku bantu bano abataano, abamanyi kino bajja kuwangula, ate abatamanyi tebajja kuwangula. N’olwekyo, nakola enteekateeka okukakasa kino, era ne nnoonya endowooza. Kigambibwa nti mukama mugezi, Genero alina obusobozi, eggulu n’ensi bisaanira, amateeka gakolebwa, abaserikale ba maanyi, abaserikale batendekebwa, era empeera n’ekibonerezo byeyoleka bulungi. Tumanyi ebinaava mu lutalo luno.

Bw’owuliriza enteekateeka yange ey’ebiseera eby’omu maaso, bw’ogikozesa, mazima ddala ojja kuwangula, kale toginywererako. Bw’otowuliriza nteekateeka yange, mazima ddala ojja kufiirwa singa okozesa enteekateeka eno, kale togireka. Singa ekozesebwa ng’enteekateeka oba omugaso, ejja kufuna amaanyi era eyambe abalala. Amaanyi kitegeeza okufuga obuyinza nga tusinziira ku magoba.

Omujaasi mufere.

N’olwekyo, obusobozi bulaga obutasobola, okukozesa kulaga obutakozesa, okumpi kulaga ewala, ewala kulaga okumpi, okukozesa enkizo okukema, akavuyo okuwamba, ebibala okwetegekera, amaanyi okwewala, obusungu okuwugula, obuseere okwenyumiriza, ate nga okukola ku, omuzadde okwawukana ku. Lumba obutamala bwabwe era tolabika nga tosuubira. Buno buwanguzi eri abaserikale, era tekyetaagisa kusooka kubabuulira.

Abakyawangula nga babalirira nga tebalwana balina okuba n’obubonero bungi bwe balina okufuna. Abo abatawangula nga babala nga tebalwana bajja kuba na kitono kye bafuna mu kubala. Okubala gye kukoma okuwangula, okubala okutono gye kukoma obutawangula. Tompa kuboggolera oba okundeka. Kino bwe tukitunuulira nga kino tukitunuulidde, tusobola okulaba ebinaavaamu.

Essuula 2: Enkola

Okusinziira ku Sun Tzu, etteeka erya bulijjo erikwata ku kukozesa eggye kwe kusaasaanya yen 1,000 ku ggaali, yen 1,000 ku ggaali, li 100,000 mu by’okulwanyisa, ne li 1,000 okusobola okufuna emmere, omuli ssente z’omunda n’ebweru, ensaasaanya eri abagenyi, ebikozesebwa mu kukola sigiri ne laaka, n’ebyokulwanyisa eby’amagaali.

Bw’okozesa olutalo olwo okumala ebbanga eddene, lujja kuzirika abaserikale bo era lujja kukumalamu obusobozi bwo obw’amaanyi. Bw’olumba olubiri, ojja kwewaayo, era bw’obikkula mukama wo okumala ebbanga eddene, ojja kuba tolina mugaso eri eggwanga. Bwe kizibuwalira abaserikale, ne kinyiiza abasongovu, kinafuya amaanyi, n’okusaanyaawo ssente, kituuka nti bakama beeyambisa omukisa gw’ebizibu ebivaamu. Ne bwe wabaawo omugezi, tajja kulongoosa bintu oluvannyuma.

N’olwekyo, wadde ng’abaserikale bawulira ku bwangu bwabwe, nakati tebamanyi bbanga lye bamaze nga bakuguse. Ludde nga tetuli bajaasi, naye tewali muntu yenna aganyudde ggwanga n'okutuusa kati. N’olwekyo, omuntu yenna atamanyi bulabe bwonna obuli mu kuweereza mu magye tayinza kumanya n’emigaso gyonna egy’okuweereza mu magye.

Abo abakozesa obulungi abaserikale baabwe tebajja kuddamu kuweereza, era tebajja kuweebwa mmere omulundi ogw’okusatu.

Bizinensi yaabwe bagiggya mu ggwanga ate emmere yaabwe bagiggya ku mulabe. N’olwekyo, wandibaddewo emmere y’amagye emala. Eggwanga bwe liba lyavu, lirina okutambuzibwa wala. Singa zitwalibwa ewala, abalimi baavu. Abo abali ku lusegere n’omusomesa balina okutunda ebyamaguzi byabwe. Bw’otunda, abalimi bajja kufiirwa obugagga bwabwe. Singa yali afiiriddwa obugagga bwe, yandiwaliriziddwa mu bwangu okuva ku by’ensimbi bye, era wakati mu biwonvu, n’ava awaka ngalo nsa, ng’alina ssente z’omulimi, ate musanvu ku buli kkumi. Ensaasaanya y’abakulu b’omu lubiri, amagaali n’embalaasi ezimenyese, obusaale obw’ebyokulwanyisa, ebigo n’ebigo, ebigaali by’ente eby’oku nsozi, n’omukaaga ku byo bisigadde mu bitundu kkumi. N’olwekyo, Genero ow’amagezi aweereza ekigendererwa kye era aliisa omulabe. Okulya enkumbi y’omulabe emu yenkana koku amakumi abiri gyendi, ate koku emu okuva ku muti yenkana koku amakumi abiri gyendi.
N’olwekyo, abo abatta abalabe baabwe banyiiga. Abo abeeyambisa omulabe ssente. N’olwekyo, bw’owangula emmotoka kkumi mu lutalo lw’amagaali, ojja kusooka kusasula oyo awangudde, olwo n’owanirira bendera, n’okuŋŋaanya amagaali go okugavugako, n’ogaliisa ng’okola bulungi. Kino kiyitibwa okufuna amaanyi ng’owangula omulabe.

N’olwekyo, abaserikale batwala obuwanguzi nga bwa muwendo. Totwala ebyo ebyayita nga bya muwendo. N’olwekyo, Genero amanyi abaserikale be ye mukulembeze w’abantu era ye mukulembeze w’ebyokwerinda by’eggwanga.

Essuula 3: Okulumba

Okusinziira ku Sun Tzu, etteeka ly’okuweereza mu magye lye lisinga okutuukiriza eggwanga, ate lyakubiri mu kulisaanyawo.

Okuwangula eggye kye kisinga, okuwangula eggye kye kisinga ekyokubiri. Okumaliriza olugendo kye kisinga obulungi, ate okumenya olugendo kikwata kyakubiri. Omuntu okumaliriza emisomo gye kitwalibwa ng’ekisinga obulungi, ate okumenyawo ekimu kyakubiri. Okumaliriza ekibiina eky’okutaano kitwalibwa ng’ekisinga obulungi, ate okumenya ekibiina eky’okutaano kye kisinga obulungi.

Olw’ensonga eno, omusajja awangudde entalo kikumi n’awangula entalo kikumi si kirala wabula musajja mulungi. Musajja mulungi agondera abaserikale b’abantu nga talina lutalo.

N’olwekyo, abajaasi abasukkulumye ku balala balumba olukwe. Ekiddako, ssala nkulungo. Ekiddako, mutte abaserikale. Wansi w’ekyo, lumba olubiri. Etteeka ly’okuzingiza terikwewalika.

Okukuguka mu bifo ebiyitibwa turrets, turrets, ne turrets, wamu n’okusobola okukozesa ebivuga, kigenda kubaawo mu March. Olugendo luno lugenda kuddamu okwongerwako mu March. Genero bw’aba tasobola kuwangula mulabe n’amulumba, ajja kutta kimu kya kusatu eky’abalwanyi, era bw’aba tasobola kudduka mu lubiri, kijja kuba kizibu eri olulumba.

N’olwekyo, abo abakozesa obulungi abaserikale baabwe tebajja kuba na kya kukola okuggyako okulwana ne bwe banaagwa wansi w’abaserikale b’abantu. Ne bw’oyita ku lubiri lw’omuntu, tekirina mugaso okululumba. Kijja kutwala ebbanga ddene nga tonnasaanyaawo nsi ya muntu. Mazima ddala tujja kulwanyisa ensi n’amaanyi gaffe gonna. N’olwekyo, olina okukozesa obulungi amagoba go nga tolina kwegendereza. Lino lye tteeka ly’okulumba.

N’olwekyo, etteeka ly’olutalo liri nti: bw’oba ​​olina kkumi, olina okugyetooloola, bw’oba ​​olina ttaano, olina okululumba, bw’ogikubisaamu, olina okugigabanyaamu, bw’oba ​​omulabe, olina okukirwanako, bw’oba ​​n’abatono, olina okugyewala, era bw’oba ​​tolina, olina okugyewala. N’olwekyo, amaanyi g’omulabe omutono ge maanyi g’omulabe omunene.

Genero ye mukungu w’eggwanga. Bwe wabaawo obuwagizi, eggwanga lijja kuba lya maanyi bulijjo, era bwe wabaawo obutaba na buwagizi, eggwanga lijja kuba nnafu bulijjo. N’olwekyo, waliwo ensonga ssatu lwaki eggye lyo liyinza okubonaabona. Bagamba amagye gagende mu maaso, nga tebamanyi nti amagye galina okugenda mu maaso, ne babagamba okudda emabega, nga tebamanyi nti eggye teririna kyakukola okuggyako okudda emabega. Kino kiyitibwa ``okukosa amagye.''

Bw’oba tomanyi ku magye asatu n’ogoberera ebyobufuzi bye bimu n’amagye asatu, abajaasi bajja kusoberwa. Bw’oba ​​tomanyi maanyi ga magye asatu n’ogabana emirimu gy’amagye asatu, ojja kwekengera abaserikale. Amagye asatu bwe gatabulwa dda era nga gabuusabuusa, bakama bafuna obuzibu. Kino kiyitibwa ``okutaataaganya eggye n'okufuna obuwanguzi.''

N’olwekyo, waliwo engeri ttaano ez’okumanya obuwanguzi.

Abamanyi ddi lwe balina okulwana ne ddi lwe batalwana bajja kuwangula. Abo abategeera bizinensi y’abantu abangi be bagenda okuwangula. Abo abalina obwagazi bwe bumu waggulu ne wansi bajja kuwangula. Abo abalindirira okutya n’okutya bajja kuwangula. Abo abatasobola kufugibwa Genero bajja kuwangula.

Bino ebitaano y’engeri y’okumanya obuwanguzi. N’olwekyo, kigambibwa nti bw’omumanyi ne weemanyi, ojja kulwana entalo kikumi n’ofiirwa kitono nnyo. Bw’oba ​​tomumanyi era nga weemanyi, ojja kuwangula oba okuwangulwa. Bw’oba ​​tomumanyi ate nga tomanyi, tekyewalika nti buli lw’olwana ojja kumaliriza ng’ofudde.

Essuula 4: Katahen
Sun Tzu yategeezezza nti abaalwana obulungi mu biseera eby’emabega tebaalina kya kukola okuggyako okusooka okuwangula, olwo ne balinda omulabe okuwangula. Omusango gwo tolina kuwangula, naye omulabe y’alina okuwangula. N’olwekyo, abo abalwana obulungi tebalina kukakibwa kuwangula bulungi, wabula tebalina kukakibwa kuwangula abalabe baabwe. N’olwekyo, kigambibwa nti obuwanguzi bulina okumanyibwa, naye tewali kiyinza kukolebwa.

Abo abatalina kuwangula balina okukuuma. Abalina okuwangula balina okulumba. Okwekuuma, olina okuba n’ekimala, ate okulumba, olina okuba n’ebirala. Abo abakuuma obulungi bajja kuterekebwa wansi w’Ensi Omwenda, era abo abalumba obulungi bajja kutambula waggulu w’Eggulu Omwenda. N’olwekyo weekuume bulungi otuuke ku buwanguzi.

Si kya balungi na balungi kulaba buwanguzi, wabula abantu ba bulijjo bokka balabe. Si muntu mulungi agamba nti okuwangula olutalo kijja kuleeta ebirungi mu nsi. N’olwekyo, si kisusse okukuza Akiyoshi. Sisobola kulaba njuba na mwezi nga amaaso gange gazibye. Kyetaagisa okutu okugezi okuwulira okubwatuka.
Abo abalwana obulungi, kye bayita mu biseera eby’edda, be bawangula nga kyangu okuwangula. N’olwekyo, omuntu alwana obulungi bw’awangula, tewajja kubaawo ttutumu wadde obuzira. N’olwekyo, tekirina njawulo nti olutalo lwawangulwa. Abatayawukana bulijjo bajja kuwangula wonna we banaaba baagala. Tulina okuwangula ku abo abaawanguddwa edda. N’olwekyo, abo abalwana obulungi bayimirira ku ttaka ly’obutawangulwa, era tebafiirwa kuwangulwa kwa balabe baabwe. Olw’ensonga eno, eggye eriwangudde lisooka kuwangula n’oluvannyuma ne linoonya olutalo, ate eggye eriwanguddwa ne lisooka kulwana oluvannyuma ne linoonya obuwanguzi.

Abo abakozesa abaserikale balima bulungi Tao era banywerera ku Dharma. N’olwekyo, batera okufuga mu ngeri ey’obuwanguzi n’okufiirwa.

Obukugu bw’olutalo, nga Ichi bw’agamba, kiseera. Ssente ezoogeddwako mu kyokubiri. Nnamba ssatu bw’egamba. Erinnya ly’ow’okuna. Okusinziira ku Go, buwanguzi. Ensi efulumya diguli, diguli efulumya obungi, obungi buvaamu omuwendo, ennamba efulumya engule, ate engule evaamu obuwanguzi. N’olwekyo, kiringa eggye eryawangudde nga litendereza ekisumuluzo n’ekisumuluzo, ate eggye eryawanguddwa ne liringa okuyita ekisumuluzo n’ekisumuluzo.

Amangu ddala ng’abantu abawangudde bawalirizibwa okugenda mu lutalo, kijja kuba ng’okusalawo Sekisui mu biwonvu lukumi.

Essuula 5: Seihen

Okusinziira ku Sun Tzu, okufuga abantu abangi n’okufuga abatono kiringa obutundutundu. Kino kye kifaananyi ky’okuleetera abantu abangi okulwana n’okuleetera abatono okulwana. Abantu b’amagye asatu balina okukkiriza omulabe ne bawangulwa, kale kino kye balina okukola. Okwongerako abaserikale kiringa okusuula obutaasa ku ggi, naye nga kituufu era kya bulimba.

Okutwaliza awamu, entalo ziwangulwa okuyita mu butuukirivu n’obutamanya. N’olwekyo, abo abavaamu ebintu ebirungi n’eby’ekitalo balinga eggulu n’ensi, ebitakoma, era balinga enzizi n’emigga egitakoma. Kiggwa ne kiddamu okutandika bwe kiti buli lunaku na buli mwezi. Kitwala essaawa nnya okufa n’okuzaalibwa omulundi ogw’okubiri. Newankubadde amaloboozi ataano gokka, ekyewuunyo ky’amaloboozi ataano kinene nnyo nga tosobola butawuliriza. Wadde langi ttaano zokka, okukyusa langi ettaano kikulu nnyo. Wadde obuwoomi buba butaano bwokka, ekyewuunyo ky’obuwoomi obutaano busobola okuvvuunuka era tebulina kuwooma.

Wadde eggye ery’okulwana terisingako kirala okuggyako eryewuunyisa, ebyabaddewo e Kiyoshi bisobola okuwangulwa era tekyetaagisa kubeera mu buzibu. Ekibaawo nga waliwo ekikyamu kiringa enzirukanya etakoma. Ani afaayo ku kino?

Amaanyi g’amazzi agaali gakulukuta ge gaaleetera amayinja okuwuguka. Kye nkyukakyuka nti ekinyonyi ky’empungu kigenda ku sipiidi era kiggwa mu kulemererwa. Olw’ensonga eno, abalwana obulungi bajja kuba n’olutalo olw’amaanyi n’olutalo olumpi. Amaanyi galinga omusaalaba, ate ekikonde kiringa omukisa.

Obujagalalo buba mu Osamu, okutya kuva mu buvumu, n’obunafu mu buyinza. Obujeemu bungi. Obuvumu ge maanyi. Ffoomu enafu ye ffoomu.

N’olwekyo, abo abatambuza abalabe baabwe mu ngeri ennungi bajja kulaba nga bwe banaakola kye baagala, omulabe ddala ajja kugoberera bye bakola, era bwe banaateekateeka okukikola, omulabe mazima ddala ajja kutwala bye bakola. Kitambuze n’enkizo era olinde n’ekisa.

N’olwekyo, abo abalwana obulungi kino bakinoonya ng’amaanyi, era tebanenya balala. N’olwekyo, alondawo bulungi abantu be era teyeesigama ku maanyi ge. Bw’oleka omuntu ali mu buyinza bwo okukutwala mu lutalo, kiba ng’okufuula ejjinja ejjinja. Obutonde bw’enku n’amayinja bwe buba nga tebulina bulabe, buba bukyali; bwe kiba kya bulabe, kitambula; bwe kigenda, kiyimirira; bwe kiba ekyekulungirivu, kitambula. N’olwekyo, amaanyi agaleetera abantu okulwanirira ebirungi ge maanyi agafuula ejjinja eryetooloovu okufuuka ensozi lukumi.

Essuula 6: Amazima n’ebitontome

Okusinziira ku Sun Tzu, abasooka okugenda mu lutalo ne balinda omulabe bajja kubonaabona, ate abagenda mu lutalo oluvannyuma ne balwana olutalo bajja kukola nnyo. N’olwekyo, abo abalwana obulungi tebaleka balala kubakolako. Bw’otera okweyisa ng’omulabe, kino ojja kuganyulwa. Emirundi mingi omusango gw’omulabe gwe guvaako obulabe. N’olwekyo, bw’oba ​​n’omulabe, ojja kumukolera nnyo, bw’oba ​​okooye ojja kumulya, era bw’aba ku buseere, ojja kumutambuza.

Bw’omaliriza mu kifo w’ogenda okukoma awatali kwewalika, bw’omaliriza mu kifo w’otayagala, era bw’otokola kkubo lyo okuyita mu mayiro lukumi, olina okuyita mu ttaka eritali lya muntu. Engeri yokka ey’okulumba n’okuwamba ekintu kwe kulumba ebitundu ebitasobola kukuumibwa. Engeri yokka gy’oyinza okuba ow’amaanyi ng’ozibiriza kwe kukuuma ebitundu ebitasobola kulumbibwa. N’olwekyo, abo abalumba obulungi tebamanyi mulabe waabwe wadde gy’aba yeekuuma. Abo abakuuma obulungi tebamanyi mulabe wadde gy’anaalumba. Kiyinza okuba nga tekitegeerekeka oba nga tekitegeerekeka, naye kituuka ku ssa ly’obutakwatibwako. Ye Katonda? Nneebuuza oba Katonda, okutuusa lwe kisirika. N’olwekyo, atera okutwala obuduumizi bw’omulabe.
Engeri yokka ey’okugenda mu maaso kwe kugoba obulimba obwo. Bw’oba ​​tosobola kudda mabega n’ogigoberera, tosobola kugituukako mangu. N’olwekyo, bwe mba njagala okulwana, omulabe ayinza okusitula enkambi ze n’okunyweza emikutu gye, naye bw’aba talina kya kukola okuggyako okunlwana, nnina okulumba w’asobola okuntaasa. Singa saagala kulwana, nnandibadde nssaako akabonero ku ttaka ne ngikuuma, naye omulabe, atalina kyakukola okuggyako okunlwana, alina okugenda mu kifo ekyo.

N’olwekyo, singa sikola bantu mu kifaananyi kyange, kwe kugamba, nja kusobola okutegeera omulabe. Bwe tuba nga tuli bumu nga tuli kimu, n’abalabe baffe ne baawulwamu kkumi, olwo tujja kulumba oyo n’ekkumi bano. Mu ngeri endala, tuli bangi ate abalabe baffe batono. Singa nkuba abatono n’abantu abangi, abalwana nange bajja kuzikirizibwa. Temulina kumanya nsi mwe tunaalwanira ffenna. Bwoba tomanyi, abantu bangi omulabe beyetegekera. Omulabe bw’aba alina bangi abategese, olwo abo abalwana ku mabbali gange batono. N’olwekyo, bw’oba ​​weetegekera eby’omu maaso, kwe kugamba, emabega biba bitono; bw’oba ​​weetegekera emabega, kwe kugamba, mu maaso ekitono; bw’oteekateeka ku kkono, kwe kugamba, ekitono ku ddyo; bw’oteekateeka ku ddyo, ekyo kitegeeza nti kitono ku kkono; era bw’oba ​​tolina ky’oteekateeka, kwe kugamba, tewali kubula. Obwannamwandu kintu abantu kye beetegekera. Abadigize beetegekera abalala.

N’olwekyo, bw’oba ​​omanyi ekifo w’olutalo n’olunaku lw’olutalo, olina okulwana mayiro lukumi. Bw’oba ​​tomanyi kifo kya lutalo n’olunaku lw’olutalo, owa kkono tasobola kutaasa wa ddyo, owa ddyo tegusobola kutaasa wa kkono, owa mu maaso tegusobola kutaasa mugongo, n’ogw’emabega tegusobola kutaasa mu maaso. Ne bw’oba ​​oyogera otya, kiri mayiro makumi, ate mayiro eziwerako okumpi. Newankubadde aba Endiga balina abaserikale bangi, tekijja kutuyamba mu nsonga z’obuwanguzi oba okuwangulwa. N’olwekyo agamba nti obuwanguzi bulina okutuukirizibwa. Newankubadde omulabe mungi, tetusaanidde kulwana.

N’olwekyo, bw’oteekateeka kino, ojja kumanya enteekateeka y’okuganyulwa n’okufiirwa, bw’otonda kino, ojja kumanya omusingi gw’entambula, bw’okola kino, ojja kumanya ekifo ky’obulamu n’okufa, era bw’ogoberera kino, ojja kumanya awali ebisukkiridde n’ebbula.

N’olwekyo, engeri enkomerero ey’omujaasi kwe butabeera na kifaananyi. Bwe kiba nga tekikwatibwako, tekijja kusoboka eri abaziba kulaba, era n’abagezigezi tebajja kusobola kukola lukwe. Wadde ng’obuwanguzi buyinza okutabulwa olw’engeri gye bukolamu, abantu bonna tebajja kumanya. Abantu bamanyi ffoomu y’ensonga lwaki nwangula, naye tebamanyi ngeri ya nsonga efuga obuwanguzi bwange. N’olwekyo, olutalo ne bwe luwangulwa, terwesasuza, wabula luddamu ffoomu awatali kubuusabuusa.

Enkula y’omujaasi eringa amazzi. Enkula y’amazzi eri wansi, okwewala obuwanvu. Enkula y’omujaasi yeewala ebituufu n’ekuba amasasi mu bwereere. Amazzi gafuga okutambula kwago okusinziira ku nsi, ate abaserikale be bafuga obuwanguzi bwabwe okusinziira ku mulabe. N’olwekyo, abaserikale tebalina kubeerawo kwa lubeerera, era n’amazzi tegalina ngeri ya lubeerera. Abo abawangula nga bakyuka okusinziira ku mulabe batera okuyitibwa bakatonda. Essuula 10: Ensi

Okusinziira ku Sun Tzu, mu ttaka, waliwo ezo eza bulijjo, ezinywevu, eziwanirira, ezifunda, eziwanvuwa, n’ezo eziri ewala.

Mu ngeri ey’obukambwe, abantu bano omukaaga ge makubo g’ensi. Siyinza butabuuza buvunaanyizibwa bwa genero obw’oku ntikko.

N’olwekyo, abaserikale abamu badduka, abamu bazirika, abamu bagwa, abamu bamenyeka, abamu tebateredde, ate abamu ne bagenda mu bukiikakkono. Ng’omuduumizi w’amagye, siyinza butakyetegereza.
