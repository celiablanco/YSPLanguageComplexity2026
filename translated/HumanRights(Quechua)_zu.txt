Ngo-December 10, 1948, iZizwe Ezihlangene 
babuthana ndawonye bakugunyaza futhi bakwazisa abantu 
amalungelo, okuyikona konke okushiwo kule ncwajana.  
Kulo mhlangano bekudingidwa ngamalungelo abo bonke abantu 
ukwethulwa, ukufundiswa, nokufunda kuwo wonke amazwe, 
kufanele kwaziswe kuzo zonke izinhlobo zezikhungo zemfundo 
kanye nomehluko phakathi kwalezi zizwe.  
 
UKUHLONISHWA KWEZIZWE EZIZWE  
AMALUNGELO ABANTU BONKE 
Ukuqala kwe-  
QAPHELA: Inkululeko nokuthula emhlabeni 
impilo igxile kulokhu okulandelayo: Wonke umuntu 
kuhle ukuthi kungamoshi muntu kwazise umama wasizala 
kufanele siphathwe futhi siphathwe ngokulinganayo.  
BHEKA: Abantu sebefinyelele kuzo zonke izinhlobo zezinto ezimbi, 
ngisho noma bengawazi ngokugcwele noma bengawazi ngokugcwele amalungelo abantu 
singazinaki, sonke sifuna ikusasa 
izinsuku zokuphila kahle; kukho konke ukwesaba nokuhlupheka 
lapho ubumpofu buqedwa, wonke umuntu uyoba nenkululeko yokukhuluma 
lokho okusemakhanda ethu, futhi siyokwamukela ngesibindi yiluphi ulwazi 
nalokho esikufunayo.  
QAPHELA: Kumele kube noMvikeli Wamalungelo Abantu 
imithetho engachemi 
ukusiza abacindezelwe nabaxhashazwayo abafuna ukuthethelela amalungelo abo.
QAPHELA: Okwezizwe zomhlaba ukwakha ubungane 
kufanele sikufise ezinhliziyweni zethu kanye naleso sifiso 
kumele sihlakazeke.  
QAPHELA: Izizwe ezingaphansi kweNhlangano Yezizwe ziyakhulunywa kule ncwadi 
baqinisekisa ukukholelwa kwabo emalungelweni e-Iliw kanye 
bavumelana ngokuthi abantu bayalingana kungakhathaliseki ubulili 
qaphela ukuthi unamalungelo afanayo. Zonke lezi zinto kahle 
sazi ukuthi ugqozi lwenkululeko yethu muhle nakakhulu 
asisebenzele ukwenza izwe lethu libe likhulu.  
QAPHELA: Izizwe ezingaphansi kweZizwe Ezihlangene zivumile 
amalungelo kanye nenkululeko yabantu kumhlaba wonke 
ezokwamukelwa yi-  
QAPHELA NYA: Amalungelo abantu nenkululeko aqashelwa kahle 
uma kunjalo, kuyoba yiqiniso.  
AMALUNGELO OMUNTU ABONWA EMHLANGANWENI OJWAYELEKILE 
UKUTHI KWABA  
Izizwe ezafika esivumelwaneni esikhulu zikule ncwadi 
ukuqaphela amalungelo abantu jikelele, ukuze abantu 
noma kuphi lapho behlala khona, bayayibona le qeliqa ngenhlonipho nangenkululeko 
ukufunda ukuphila; kwabanye ngokusemandleni abo 
bayokwenza kwaziwe ukuthi zonke lezi zinto ziyiqiniso; lokhu kuzokwenziwa 
ukuhlala emazweni angaphansi kwenyunyana noma lokhu 
isiphephelo sezizwe.  
OKOKUQALA (1) 
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi banawo wonke amalungelo 
siyalingana. Ngenxa yokuthi sihlakaniphile, sifana nomkhaya onokuthula 
sikweleta wonke umuntu ukuphila.  
LESIBILI (2)
Izimiso zamalungelo eziqukethwe kulo mbhalo ezethu sonke, 
akukhathalekile ukuthi uluphi uhlanga noma ubulili bomuntu 
futhi akunandaba ukuthi ukhuluma kanjani noma ukholelwa kumuphi unkulunkulu 
nokuthi wayecabanga ukuthi uzovela kuluphi uhlobo lukahulumeni 
kungakhathaliseki ukuthi umpofu noma ucebile noma ngabe uyini.  
Zonke izizwe, zikhululekile noma cha, zihlukahlukene 
sinohulumeni, kanti abanye bathembele kwezinye izizwe, 
kodwa noma kunjalo akufanele siphathwe ngendlela ehlukile.  
INGXENYE YESITHATHU (3) 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphila nenkululeko 
kanye nokuvikelwa.  
ESINE (4) 
Akekho ongaba yisigqila esiqhwagwayo, kwenqatshelwe ngazo zonke izinhlobo 
ukugqilazwa nokushushumbiswa kwabantu.  
Eyesihlanu (5) 
Akekho ongabhekana nanoma yiluphi uhlobo lokuhlupheka, futhi 
ngeke sijeziswe ngokuhlazisa ubuntu bethu.  
YESTHUPHA (6) 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuqulwa kwecala noma ngabe ukuphi 
ukuze kuqinisekiswe.  
ESESIKHOMBISA (7) 
Bonke abantu banamalungelo alinganayo ngaphansi komthetho 
ukuze kube njalo, umthetho kufanele usivikele ngokungakhethi. 
Sinelungelo futhi lokuphikisana nathi ngokushaya indiva lezi zimfundiso 
 

 
yalabo abafuna ukusukuma. Sinelungelo futhi lezi zimfundiso 
kanye nokuvikelwa ngumthetho uma kwenzeka iphutha kungazelelwe.  
ESINE (8)
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokulalelwa ngendlela efanele 
ukuvikelwa emalungelweni ethu angokomthetho 
kumele sivikeleke kulabo abasenzayo.  
ISAHLUKO SESISHIYAGALOMBILI (9) 
Akekho oboshiwe engazi iphutha lakhe, 
ukuboshwa noma ukudingiswa.  
YESSHUMI (10) 
Bonke abantu bayalingana ngobulungisa obungakhethi 
sinelungelo lokulalelwa kanye nelungelo lokuqulwa kwecala 
umsebenzi wethu noma ilungelo lethu lokwazi. Yini asolwa ngayo 
kufanele siboniswe ngokucacile kithi ukuze sifunde noma sikude.  
ISHUMI NANYE (11) 
1. Abantu abangenacala bamsulwa kunoma yibuphi ubugebengu ababekwa bona 
sinelungelo lokuziqinisa, ngokomthetho 
size sitholwe sinecala ecaleni. Kukhona nokuqulwa kwecala 
amalungelo ethu aqinisekisiwe futhi avikelwe ngempela 
ukuba yibo.  
2. Ukungagwebeli umuntu ngento ayenzileyo 
isijeziso uma isenzo ezinsukwini ezandulela umthetho 
uma ephuma ekhombisa ukuthi kuyicala. Isijeziso kuyoba yi 
kuphela ngezijeziso zomthetho ezinsukwini zesono sakhe.  
 

 
ISHUMI NAmbili (12) 
Akekho noyedwa ongamosha izimpilo zethu ngaphandle kwesizathu 
futhi emindenini yethu, akakwazi ukungena ngeze 
ezindlini zethu, nezincwadi zethu aziyikuthunyelelwa ize. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngumthetho kulokho kugxambukela.  
ISHUMI NATHATHU (13)
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululeka, 
sinelungelo futhi lezindlu ezweni lethu.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lobuzwe noma 
ukushiya noma yiliphi izwe futhi okufanayo 
ukuze sibuyele ezweni lethu.  
ISHUMI NANE (14) 
1. Uma umuntu eshushiswa njengohulumeni wezwe lakhe 
ngoba ecabanga, ke unelungelo lokuthuthela kwelinye izwe 
ukubaleka aphephele.  
2. Ushushiswe ngecala langempela ezweni lakhe 
umuntu akakwazi ukuthembela emalungelweni abantu ngaphandle 
ukubalekela ezweni futhi uhlale khona ngokukhululeka.  
ISHUMI nesihlanu (15) 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lobuzwe 
ukuba ngowethu.  
2. Leli lungelo angeke liphucwe amalungelo alo ngokungafanele. 
Sinelungelo futhi lokwenqaba ukushintsha ubuzwe bethu 
umuntu ozosimisa.  
ISHUMI nesithupha (16) 
 

 
1. Uma owesilisa nowesifazane befinyelela iminyaka yokushada, basuke sebekhona 
ilungelo lethu lokushada sibe nomndeni, cha 
akukhathaleki ukuthi siyiluphi uhlanga noma isizwe, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi singamadoda noma abesifazane 
owesifazane unamalungelo alinganayo emshadweni wethu okuhle 
ukuphila kanye nokwabelana.  
2. Indoda nowesifazane abakwazi ukushada ngaphandle kwemvume yabo.  
3. Ukuba umzali kuyilungelo lomndeni wonke ekuvikelweni kombuso 
sinelungelo, ngoba siwumndeni omkhulu othuthukisa isizwe 
yithina.
ISHUMI nesikhombisa (17) 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba yedwa noma ngababili 
ukuba nempahla nabantu.  
2. Akekho oyophucwa impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
ISHUMI nesishiyagalombili (18) 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga, 
unembeza futhi kumuphi unkulunkulu 
nokusenza sikholwe. Ngokwaleli lungelo, sikhululekile 
ukwamukela inkolo nokukholelwa kunoma yiluphi uhlobo 
ukwenza umsebenzi. Singakwenza sodwa noma siyiqembu 
leyo nkolelo futhi ingafundiswa ekhaya noma 
noma kuphi.  
ISHUMI nesishiyagalolunye (19) 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuveza imibono yakhe 
sinelungelo lalezi zinto ngaphandle kokuvinjelwa 
singakwazi futhi ukwabelana ngemicabango yethu futhi samukele imibono yabanye.  
AMASHUMI AMABILI (20) 
 

 
1. Sinelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana nokuhlangana 
kanye lokwakha ukuthula.  
2. Akekho oyophoqwa ukuthi ahambele noma yimuphi umhlangano 
ukuba ngowakho ngokuzithandela.  
AMASHUMI AMABILI NANYE (21) 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo kuhulumeni wezwe lakhe 
ukuze sizikhonze thina uqobo noma 
noma sikhetha esikufunayo.  
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo elilinganayo lokufinyelela emisebenzini yomphakathi ezweni lakubo 
ukungena ezindlini nokuthola ukunakekelwa.  
3. Amandla kahulumeni ukuthi bonke abantu bayawafuna
ngokushesha nje lapho sinikeza igunya, siyasivuma leso sifiso 
ngokuvota ngokukhululeka; akumele senze lelo voti akekho omunye 
siyazi ukuze sivotele esikufunayo.  
AMASHUMI AMABILI NAMbili (22) 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo, impahla kanye 
sivikelwe kuzo zonke izindlela zethu ukuze konke kube kuhle 
ukuze sifike lapha. Lesi siqinisekiso sithola ngokuthi: 
Uhulumeni kufanele ahleleke futhi asenzele okusemandleni akhe 
nabantu bezwe lakithi, kumele asebenzele ukusizuzisa ngokwamandla akhe noma 
kanye nosizo lwangaphandle.  
AMASHUMI amabili nantathu (23) 
1. I-LIew abantu banelungelo lenkululeko yenkolo, 
sinelungelo lokusebenza noma singasebenzi 
ukufuna futhi uthole, uma kungekho ofuna 
singaxoshwa yini emisebenzini yethu.  
 

 
2. I-LIew abantu banelungelo lenkululeko yokukhuluma 
ukuze sikhokhelwe ngokomsebenzi wethu.  
3. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhamba nokuhlala ngaphakathi kwemingcele yoMbuso ngamunye 
ukuze samukele umvuzo wethu njengokwemisebenzi emihle 
inhlalakahle yomndeni wonke; kungenjalo kufanele athole usizo 
labasekeli abaphakanyiselwe inhloso.  
4. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuzihlanganisa nabanye 
ilungelo lethu lokusungula noma ukuba semaqenjini anjalo 
ukuze siziku.  
AMASHUMI AMABILI NANE (24) 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula nokuphumula
sinelungelo lokumamatheka, futhi sinelungelo lokuqhubeka ngokusemandleni ethu 
ukusebenza emini futhi sithathe iholidi lethu. 
AMASHUMI AMABILI NANHLANU (25) 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lezinga lokuphila elanele yena nomndeni wakhe 
udlisiwe, ugqoke kahle futhi unempilo. Unelungelo, futhi 
sinelungelo lokuvikelwa, ngisho noma sikhubazekile, singabafelokazi noma singabafelwa 
kungakhathaliseki ukuthi umdala noma usemncane; nathi sinelungelo 
ngisho noma singenakuwuthola umsebenzi noma ngokungazelele sibe nokuthile esingazondla ngakho 
ukuvikelwa uma kwenzeka ukulahlekelwa.  
2. Abesifazane abakhulelwe nezingane banamalungelo 
kufanele zigcinwe ngendlela efanele. Ushadile noma cha 
zonke izingane zinamalungelo alinganayo kungakhathaliseki ukuthi zizalelwa emshadweni 
ukuze bavikelwe.  
AMASHUMI amabili nesithupha (26) 
 

 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Lutho neze 
isikole samabanga aphansi simahhala, ngakho-ke kuyimpoqo kuwo wonke umuntu onesikole samabanga aphansi 
ukukwazi. Ngokusho kwenothi lethu elihle, kungaphezulu 
singazifundisa ukuze sikwazi ukufeza izinhloso zethu.  
2. Umphumela wemfundo kuyoba inhlalakahle yabantu nabo bonke 
amalungelo akhe aqashelwa kahle ukuze inkululeko yakhe ihlonishwe kahle 
ukuze atholakale. Kudingeka sizole futhi siqondane 
nezizwe, nezinye izizwe, nakunoma yimuphi unkulunkulu 
kanye namakholwa. Kufanele futhi sisekele iNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene 
ukuthula emhlabeni.  
3. Abazali banelungelo lokuqala lemfundo
kanye nokwamukela umndeni wethu.  
AMASHUMI amabili nesikhombisa (27) 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhamba nokuhlala ngaphakathi kwemingcele yoMbuso ngamunye 
ukuze asisize kunoma yisiphi isimo. Singasiza ngazo zonke izindlela 
izwe lethu ukuthi lithuthuke kahle futhi sinelungelo 
ukwamukela lokho idolobha elingakunikeza.  
2. Uma sisuse ngokwengqondo okuthile okuyigugu noma 
singenza into engakaze yenziwe muntu ngaphambili 
sinelungelo lokubaluleka kwaleyo ndalo 
ukuvikela okukhona.  
AMASHUMI amabili nesishiyagalombili (28) 
Sinamalungelo ezweni lethu nakuwo wonke amazwe 
ukuba yinhlangano elwela amalungelo abantu, ukuze konke lokhu 
ukuzuza amalungelo nenkululeko esiyibonayo.  
AMASHUMI amabili nesishiyagalolunye (29) 
 

 
1. Wonke umuntu ukhululekile ezweni lakithi, ngakho-ke ezweni lakithi 
sinomsebenzi.  
2. Okuwukuphela kwendlela yokuphoqelela amalungelo noma inkululeko yethu kuwumthetho 
ingase isivimbele, njengamalungelo abanye noma inkululeko 
ubukhona babo abuthathwa, ukuze izingxabano zingenzeki 
ukuze atholakale. Ukuze wonke umuntu esezweni lakhe 
ukuhlala ngokuthula.  
3. Lawa malungelo kanye nenkululeko akumele nakancane kusetshenziswe 
kungaba, uma kunoma yiluphi uhlobo lwezizwe ezihlangene 
uma kuphambene nentando yakhe.  
AMASHUMI AMATHATHU (30) 
Akekho noma uhulumeni ongathembela kulesi sisho
angenza noma yini, amalungelo ethu kanye nenkululeko 
ukuqhwaga izwe.
