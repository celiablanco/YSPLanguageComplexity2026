I. Lay Plɛn dɛn

1. Sun Tzu bin se: Di art fɔ wɔ rili impɔtant to di Stet.

2. Na tin fɔ layf ɛn day, na rod fɔ go sef ɔ fɔ pwɛl. So na sɔntin we dɛn kin aks kwɛstyɔn bɔt we dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ lɛf biɛn.

3. Di art fɔ wɔ, so, na fayv tin dɛn we kin apin ɔltɛm, we pɔsin fɔ tek tɛm tink bɔt we i de tink bɔt, we i de tray fɔ no di kɔndishɔn dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt na di fil.

4. Dɛn tin ya na: (1) Di Lɔ we de sho aw pɔsin fɔ biev; (2) Ɛvin; (3) Di wɔl; (4) Di Kɔmanda; (5) Di we aw dɛn de du am ɛn di we aw dɛn de kɔrɛkt pɔsin.

5,6. Di Mɔral Lɔ de mek di pipul dɛn gri wit dɛn rula, so dat dɛn go fala am ilɛksɛf dɛn de liv dɛn layf, ɛn dɛn nɔ go fred ɛni denja.

7. Ɛvin min nɛt ɛn de, kol ɛn ɔt, tɛm ɛn sizin.

8. Di wɔl gɛt distans dɛn, big ɛn smɔl; denja ɛn sikyɔriti; opin grɔn ɛn smɔl pas dɛn; di chans fɔ liv ɛn day.

9. Di Kɔmanda tinap fɔ di gud kwaliti dɛn we gɛt sɛns, wit ɔl in at, fɔ du gud, fɔ gɛt maynd ɛn fɔ strikt.

10. Bay we ɛn disiplin fɔ ɔndastand di marshaling fɔ di ami na in rayt sabdivishɔn, di gradyueshɔn fɔ rank bitwin di ɔfisa dɛn, di mentenɛns fɔ rod dɛn we di sapɔt dɛn kin yuz fɔ rich to di ami, ɛn di kɔntrol fɔ di soja spɛnd.

11. Dɛn fayv ed ya fɔ bi tin we ɔlman na jenɛral sabi: di wan we sabi dɛn go win; di wan we nɔ no dɛn go fel.

12. So, we yu de tink, we yu de tray fɔ no di soja kɔndishɔn, lɛ dɛn mek dɛn bi di bɛnifit fɔ kɔmpia, insay dis we:--

13. (1) Uswan pan di tu sovayb dɛn we gɛt di lɔ we de sho aw fɔ biev? (2) Uswan pan di tu jenɛral dɛn gɛt mɔ abiliti? (3) Udat dɛn de wit di bɛnifit dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt frɔm ɛvin ɛn di wɔl? (4) Us say dɛn kin mek pipul dɛn kɔrɛkt pɔsin pas ɔlman? (5) Us ami strɔng pas am? (6) Us say ɔfisa dɛn ɛn man dɛn de tren mɔ? (7) Insay us ami dɛn kin kɔntinyu fɔ gɛt blɛsin ɛn pɔnishmɛnt?

14. Bay dis sɛvin tin dɛn we a fɔ tink bɔt a kin tɔk se a go win ɔ win.

15. Di jenɛral we de lisin to mi advays ɛn du am, go win: lɛ dɛn kip da kayn pɔsin de fɔ kɔmand! Di jenɛral we nɔ de lisin to mi advays ɔ du sɔntin pan am, go sɔfa fɔ win:--lɛ dɛn dismis da kayn pɔsin de!

16. Wail yu de hed di profit of mai advais, avail yusef tu of eni helpful situeshɔn ova and biyond di odinari rules.

17. Akɔdin to as tin fayn, pɔsin fɔ chenj in plan.

18. Ɔl wɔ, na fɔ ful pipul dɛn.

19. So, we wi ebul fɔ atak, i fɔ tan lɛk se wi nɔ ebul; we wi de yuz wi pawa, i fɔ tan lɛk se wi nɔ de du natin; we wi de nia, wi fɔ mek di ɛnimi biliv se wi de fa; we wi de fa, wi fɔ mek i biliv se wi de nia.

20. Hol out bet fo entice di enemi. Feign disorder, ɛn krɔs am.

21. If im sef fo ol taim, redi fo am. If i gɛt trɛnk we pas am, rɔnawe pan am.

22. If yu oponunent get choleric temper, tray to irita am. Una mek lɛk se yu wik, so dat i go mek prawd.

23. If im de tek im ease, no giv am rest. If in sojaman dɛn gɛt wanwɔd, una fɔ sheb dɛn.

24. Atak am wia im no pripia, apia wia yu no de expekt.

25. Dɛn soja divays ya, we de mek pɔsin win, nɔ fɔ tɔk bɔt am bifo tɛm.

26. Naw di jenɛral we win fɛt de mek bɔku kɔlkyulɛshɔn na in tɛmpul bifo dɛn fɛt di fɛt. Di jenɛral we lɔs wan fɛt kin mek bɔt smɔl kɔlkyulɛshɔn bifo tɛm. Na so bɔku kɔlkyulɛshɔn kin mek pɔsin win, ɛn smɔl kɔlkyulɛshɔn kin mek pɔsin win: aw mɔ nɔ kɔlkyulɛshɔn atɔl! Na bay we a pe atɛnshɔn to dis pɔynt, a kin si bifo tɛm udat go win ɔ lɔs.

II. Fɔ fɛt wɔ

1. Sun Tzu se: Insay di opareshɔn dɛn we wɔ de du, usay na di fil de na di fil wan tawzin chariɔt dɛn we de rɔn kwik kwik wan, as bɔku ebi ebi chariɔt dɛn, ɛn wan ɔndrɛd tawzin sojaman dɛn we wɛr mail, wit tin dɛn we go du fɔ kɛr dɛn wan tawzin li, di spɛnd we dɛn spɛn na os ɛn na di frɔnt, inklud ɛnjɔymɛnt fɔ di gɔst dɛn, smɔl tin dɛn lɛk glu ɛn pent, ɛn sɔm mɔni we dɛn spɛn pan chariɔt ɛn wɛpɔn dɛn, go rich di totɛl fɔ wan tawzin ɔns silva fɔ wan de. Na so di kɔst fɔ rayz wan ami we gɛt 100,000 man dɛn.

2. Wen yu de enta akchual fait, if viktri de long fo kam, den man dem wepan go grow dull and di ardor go damp. If yu kam rawnd wan tɔŋ, yu go taya yu trɛnk.

3. Agen, if di kampen tek lɔng tɛm, di tin dɛn we di Stet gɛt nɔ go ikwal to di strɛs.
4. Naw, wen yu wepan dem don dull, yu ardor damped, yu strength don taya and yu treasure don spend, oda chifta dem go spring op to tek advantej of yu extremity. Dɔn nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ avɔyd di bad tin dɛn we go apin to am, ilɛksɛf i gɛt sɛns.

5. So, pan ɔl we wi dɔn yɛri bɔt stupid rɔsh fɔ fɛt wɔ, dɛn nɔ ɛva si kleva gɛt sɔntin fɔ du wit fɔ delay fɔ lɔng tɛm.

6. No instans nɔ de fɔ se wan kɔntri dɔn bɛnifit frɔm wɔ we dɛn dɔn fɛt fɔ lɔng tɛm.

7. Na wan we sabi di bad tin dɛn we wɔ de du gud gud wan, na in go ebul fɔ ɔndastand gud gud wan di we we go mek dɛn kɔntinyu fɔ du am we go mek i gɛt prɔfit.

8. Di sojaman we sabi du di wok nɔ de rayz sɛkɔn levi, ɛn dɛn nɔ de lod in sapɔt-wagon dɛn pas tu tɛm.

9. Briŋ wɔ matirial wit yu frɔm os, bɔt fɔ fɛn it pan di ɛnimi. Na so di ami go gɛt tin fɔ it fɔ wetin dɛn nid.

10. Po we di Stet de kip mɔni de mek dɛn de kip wan ami bay we dɛn de gi kɔntribyushɔn frɔm fa. We dɛn kɔntribyut fɔ mek ami kɔntinyu fɔ de fa, dat kin mek di pipul dɛn po.

11. Na di oda han, di klos we ami de nia de mek praiz de go op; ɛn ay prayz kin mek di pipul dɛn sɔbstans drayn.

12. We dɛn dɔn drayn dɛn tin, di pipul dɛn we de wok na fam go sɔfa wit ebi ebi ɛksakshɔn.

13,14. We dɛn lɔs tin ɛn trɛnk we dɛn dɔn taya, dɛn go pul di pipul dɛn os, ɛn tri-tɛn pan di mɔni we dɛn de gɛt go dɔn; we di gɔvmɛnt spɛns fɔ brok chariɔt dɛn, ɔs dɛn we dɔn pwɛl, brɔst-plet ɛn ɛlmɛt, bɔ ɛn aro, spia ɛn shild, mantle fɔ protɛkt, drɔt-ɔk ɛn ebi wagɔn, go rich 4-tɛn pan di ɔl mɔni we i go gɛt.

15. Na dat mek wan jenɛral we gɛt sɛns kin mek pɔynt fɔ fɛn it pan di ɛnimi. Wan kat we ful-ɔp wit di ɛnimi in it dɛn ikwal to twɛnti pan pɔsin in yon, ɛn semweso, wan pikul pan in provayd ikwal to twɛnti frɔm pɔsin in yon stoa.

16. Naw fɔ mek wi go kil di ɛnimi, wi man dɛn fɔ wek fɔ vɛks; so dat advantej go de we dɛn win di ɛnimi, dɛn fɔ gɛt dɛn blɛsin.

17. So insay chariɔt fɛt, we dɛn dɔn tek tɛn ɔ mɔ chariɔt, dɛn fɔ blɛs di wan dɛn we tek di fɔs wan. Wi fɔ tek wi yon flag dɛn fɔ di ɛnimi dɛn, ɛn di chariɔt dɛn fɔ miks ɛn yuz am togɛda wit wi yon. Dɛn fɔ trit di sojaman dɛn we dɛn kech fayn ɛn kip dɛn.

18. Dɛn kɔl dis, yuz di ɛnimi we dɛn dɔn win fɔ mek pɔsin gɛt mɔ trɛnk.

19. So, fo wo, mek yu big tin bi viktri, no bi long taim kampen.

20. So wi go no se di lida of ami na im de arbit di pipul fate, di man we i dipen pan if di neshon go de fo pis o in denja.

III. Atak bay Stratagem

1. Sun Tzu se: Insay di prɛktikal art fɔ wɔ, di bɛst tin pan ɔl na fɔ tek di ɛnimi in kɔntri ɔl ɛn intakt; fɔ brok ɛn pwɛl am nɔ so gud. So, tu, i bɛtɛ fɔ tek wan ami bak ɔl pas fɔ pwɛl am, fɔ kech wan rejimɛnt, wan ditachmɛnt ɔ wan kɔmni ɔl pas fɔ pwɛl dɛn.

2. So fɔ fɛt ɛn win pan ɔl yu fɛt dɛn nɔto suprɛm ɛksɛlɛns; suprɛm ɛksɛlɛns na fɔ brok di ɛnimi in resistans we yu nɔ fɛt.

3. So di ays we fɔ bi jenɛral na fɔ balk di ɛnimi in plan dɛn; di nɛks bɛst na fɔ mek di ɛnimi dɛn sojaman dɛn nɔ jɔyn; di nɛks wan we go mek dɛn atak di ɛnimi dɛn ami na di fil; ɛn di polisi we bad pas ɔl na fɔ kam nia siti dɛn we gɛt wɔl.

4. Di rul na, nɔ fɔ kam rawnd siti dɛn we gɛt wɔl if i pɔsibul fɔ avɔyd am. Di pripia fɔ mantlet, shelta dɛn we pɔsin kin muv, ɛn difrɛn tin dɛn we dɛn kin yuz fɔ fɛt wɔ, go tek tri ol mɔnt; ɛn di bɔku bɔku mawnten dɛn we de nia di wɔl dɛn go tek tri mɔnt mɔ.

5. Di jenɛral, we nɔ ebul fɔ kɔntrol in iritashɔn, go lans in man dɛn to di asɔlt lɛk ant we de swɛla, wit di rizɔlt se dɛn kil wan-tɛd pan in man dɛn, we di tɔŋ stil de we dɛn nɔ tek. Na so bad bad tin dɛn kin apin we dɛn kam nia dɛn.

6. So di lida we sabi du di wok de put di ɛnimi in sojaman dɛn ɔnda dɛn ɛn nɔ fɛt ɛnibɔdi; i de kech dɛn siti dɛn we i nɔ de siŋ dɛn; i de pul dɛn kiŋdɔm we i nɔ de du lɔng ɔpreshɔn na di fil.

7. Wit in fos intakt i go disput di masta we di Empaya get, and so, if i no los man, im triumph go komplit. Dis na di we aw dɛn kin atak pɔsin bay we dɛn de yuz strateji.

8. Na di rul fo wo, if wi fos na ten to di enemi wan, to roun am; if fayv to wan, fɔ atak am; if dɛn bɔku tu tɛm pas dat, fɔ sheb wi ami to tu.
9. If ikwal mach, wi kin ofa fɛt; if wi nɔ bɔku smɔl, wi kin avɔyd di ɛnimi; if wi nɔ ikwal pan ɔltin, wi kin rɔnawe pan am.

10. So, pan ɔl we smɔl fɔs kin fɛt wit traŋa, na di ɛnd, di big pawa fɔ kech am.

11. Naw di jenɛral na di bulwark fɔ di Stet; if di bulwark dɔn kɔmplit na ɔl di say dɛn; di Stet go strɔng; if di bulwark nɔ fayn, di Stet go wik.

12. Tri we de we rula kin bring bad bad tin pan in ami:--

13. (1) Na bay we i de kɔmand di ami fɔ go bifo ɔ fɔ go bak, we i nɔ no se dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ obe. Dɛn kɔl dis fɔ hobbling di ami.

14. (2) Na bay we i de tray fɔ rul wan ami di sem we aw i de administret wan kiŋdɔm, ɛn i nɔ no di kɔndishɔn dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt na ami. Dis kin mek di sojaman in maynd nɔ de rɛst.

15. (3) Na bay we i de wok fɔ di ɔfisa dɛn na in ami witout diskrimineshɔn, bikɔs i nɔ no di soja prinsipul fɔ adap to di tin dɛn we de apin. Dis kin mek di sojaman dɛn gɛt kɔnfidɛns.

16. Bɔt we di ami nɔ de rɛst ɛn nɔ trɔst, trɔbul go mɔs kɔmɔt frɔm di ɔda fiudal prins dɛn. Dis na jɔs fɔ briŋ anarchy insay di ami, ɛn fling di win away.

17. So wi go no se fayv tin dɛn de we impɔtant fɔ mek pɔsin win: (1) I go win udat no ustɛm fɔ fɛt ɛn ustɛm nɔ fɔ fɛt. (2) I go win udat sabi aw fɔ handle ɔl tu di pawa dɛn we pas am ɛn we nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ pawa. (3) I go win we in ami gɛt layf wit di sem spirit ɔlsay na in rank. (4) I go win we, rɛdi insɛf, wet fɔ tek di ɛnimi we i nɔ rɛdi. (5) I go win udat gɛt soja kapasiti ɛn di sovayb nɔ ambɔg am.

18. Na im mek dem se: If yu sabi di enemi and yu sabi yu sef, yu no nid fo fred di rizolt of wan handred fait. If yu no yusɛf bɔt yu nɔ no di ɛnimi, fɔ ɛni win we yu win yu go sɔfa bak fɔ win. If yu nɔ no di ɛnimi ɛn yusɛf, yu go gri pan ɛni fɛt.

IV. Taktik Dispɔzishɔn dɛn

1. Sun Tzu se: Di gud fɛtman dɛn we bin de trade bin fɔs put dɛnsɛf bifo di pɔsibul fɔ win, dɔn dɛn wet fɔ chans fɔ win di ɛnimi.

2. Fɔ mek wi nɔ win, de na wi yon an, bɔt na di ɛnimi insɛf de gi wi di chans fɔ win di ɛnimi.

3. Na so di gud fɛtman kin ebul fɔ mek shɔ se i nɔ win, bɔt i nɔ kin ebul fɔ mek shɔ se i win di ɛnimi.

4. Na dat mek dɛn se: Pɔsin kin no aw fɔ win we i nɔ ebul fɔ du am.

5. Sekyuriti agens defet min difensiv taktik; ebul fɔ win di ɛnimi min fɔ tek di ɔfens.

6. We yu tinap na di difensiv, dat de sho se yu nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ trɛnk; atak, we na wan trɛnk we pasmak.

7. Di jenɛral we sabi fɔ difend, de ayd na di say dɛn we sikrit pas ɔl na di wɔl; di wan we sabi fɔ atak de flash frɔm di ay ay ples dɛn na ɛvin. So na wan say wi gɛt di pawa fɔ protɛkt wisɛf; na di ɔda say, na wan win we kɔmplit.

8. Fɔ si win nɔmɔ we i de insay di ken fɔ di kɔmɔn ship nɔto di acme fɔ ɛksɛlɛns.

9. Na so i bi di acme of excellence if yu fait and win and di ol Empire se, "Wel done!"

10. Fɔ es ɔtom ia nɔto sayn fɔ big trɛnk; fɔ si di san ɛn di mun nɔto sayn fɔ si shap shap; fɔ yɛri di nɔys we tɛnda de mek nɔto sayn fɔ se pɔsin de yes kwik kwik wan.

11. Wetin di pipul we bin de trade trade bin de kɔl kleva fɛtman na pɔsin we nɔ jɔs de win, bɔt i sabi win wit izi.

12. Na dat mek di win dɛn we i win nɔ de mek i gɛt gudnem fɔ se i gɛt sɛns ɛn i nɔ de gi am di prez fɔ we i gɛt maynd.

13. I de win in fɛt dɛn bay we i nɔ de mek mistek. If yu nɔ mek mistek, na in de mek yu biliv se yu go win, bikɔs i min fɔ win ɛnimi we dɛn dɔn ɔlrɛdi win.

14. Na dat mek di pɔsin we sabi fɛt de put insɛf na pozishɔn we de mek i nɔ pɔsibul fɔ win, ɛn i nɔ de mis di tɛm fɔ win di ɛnimi.

15. Na so i bi se insay wɔ di wan we win di strateji kin jɔs luk fɔ fɛt afta dɛn dɔn win di win, bɔt di wan we dɛn dɔn disayd fɔ win kin fɛt fɔs ɛn afta dat i kin luk fɔ win.

16. Di kɔnsumat lida de kɔltiv di moral lɔ, ɛn strikt wan de fala di we aw dɛn de du tin ɛn di disiplin; so na in pawa fɔ kɔntrol sakrifays.

17. We i kam pan soja we, wi gɛt, fɔs, Mɛzhɔmɛnt; sɛkɔn, Ɛstimat ɔf kwantiti; di tɔd wan, Kɔlkyulɛshɔn; di nɔmba 4, Balancing of chances; di nɔmba fayv wan, na Viktri.
18. Mɛzhɔmɛnt gɛt fɔ pe in ɛgzistens to di Wɔl; Ɛstimat ɔf kwantiti to Mɛzhɔmɛnt; Kalkyuleshɔn to Ɛstimat ɔf kwantiti; Balɛns fɔ chans fɔ Kɔlkyul; ɛn Viktri to Balansin fɔ chans.

19. Ami we win we de agens wan we dɛn dɔn win, tan lɛk paund in wet we dɛn put na di skel agens wan gren.

20. Di onrush of wan konkorin fos tan laik se wata we de pent-up de brok insaid wan chasm we dip wan tausin fathom.

V. Ɛnaji

1. Sun Tzu se: Di kɔntrol fɔ big pawa na di sem prinsipul we di kɔntrol fɔ sɔm man dɛn: na jɔs kwɛstyɔn fɔ sheb dɛn nɔmba.

2. Fɔ fɛt wit big ami we yu de kɔmand nɔ difrɛn frɔm fɔ fɛt wit smɔl ami: na jɔs kwɛstyɔn fɔ institut sayn ɛn signal.

3. Fɔ mek shɔ se yu ɔl ɔs kin tinap tranga wan fɔ di ɛnimi in atak ɛn nɔ shek-- dis kin apin bay we yu de manej dairekt ɛn indayrɛkt.

4. Dat di impak we yu ami gɛt go tan lɛk grindston we dɛn dɔn dash agens eg--dis na di sayɛns we de tɔk bɔt wik pɔynt ɛn strɔng de afɛkt am.

5. Insay ɔl di fɛt-fɛt, dɛn kin yuz di dairekt we fɔ jɔyn fɛt, bɔt dɛn go nid indaykt we fɔ mek dɛn win.

6. Indaykt taktik, we dɛn yuz fayn fayn wan, nɔ de dɔn lɛk Ɛvin ɛn di Wɔl, nɔ de dɔn lɛk aw riva ɛn strim dɛn de flɔ; lɛk di san ɛn di mun, dɛn kin dɔn bɔt fɔ bigin nyu wan; lɛk di 4 sizin dɛn, dɛn kin pas fɔ kam bak wan tɛm mɔ.

7. I nɔ pas fayv myuzik not dɛn, bɔt stil di kɔmbaynshɔn fɔ dɛn fayv ya de mek dɛn gɛt mɔ myuzik pas aw pɔsin kin yɛri.

8. Nɔ pas fayv praymari kɔlɔ dɛn (blu, yɔlɔ, rɛd, wayt, ɛn blak), bɔt stil we dɛn jɔyn dɛn togɛda, dɛn kin mek mɔ kɔlɔ pas aw dɛn kin si.

9. Nɔ pas fayv kadinal teys (sɔ, akrid, sɔl, swit, bita), yet we dɛn jɔyn dɛn, dɛn kin gi mɔ flawa pas aw pɔsin kin ɛva test.

10. Insay fɛt, nɔto pas tu we fɔ atak--di dairekt ɛn di indayrɛkt; yet dɛn tu tin ya we dɛn jɔyn togɛda, dɛn kin mek dɛn ebul fɔ du bɔku tin dɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛnd.

11. Di dairekt and di indaykt de lid on to each oda in turn. I tan lɛk se yu de muv insay sɛklɔ--yu nɔ de ɛva kam dɔn. Udat go ebul fɔ taya di pɔsibul dɛn we dɛn jɔyn?

12. Di biginin of troops tan laik di rush of torrent we go ivin rol ston along fo im kos.

13. Di kwaliti fɔ disayd tan lɛk di wɛl-taym swoop we falkɔn de mek we de mek i ebul fɔ slap ɛn pwɛl di pɔsin we i want fɔ kil.

14. So di gud fɛtman go rili bad we i bigin, ɛn i go kwik fɔ disayd.

15. Wi kin kɔmpia ɛnaji to we pɔsin de bɛn krɔsbɔ; disizhɔn, to di rilis fɔ wan trig.

16. Amid di trɔbul ɛn trɔbul we de apin na fɛt, i kin tan lɛk se i nɔ gɛt wanwɔd ɛn yet nɔ rial dizayd nɔ de atɔl; amid kɔnfyushɔn ɛn chaos, yu arenjmɛnt kin nɔ gɛt ed ɔ tel, yet i go bi pruf fɔ mek yu nɔ win.

17. Simulayt dizayd de postulat pafɛkt disiplin, simul fred de postulat kɔrej; simulated wikɛdnɛs de postulat trɛnk.

18. Fɔ ayd ɔda ɔnda di klos fɔ dizayd na jɔs kwɛshɔn bɔt sɔbdivishɔn; fɔ ayd kɔrej ɔnda sho fɔ shem, i presuppose wan fund of latent energy; masking trɛnk wit wiknɛs na fɔ bi ɛfɛkt bay taktik dispɔzishɔn.

19. So pɔsin we sabi fɔ mek di ɛnimi kɔntinyu fɔ muv, de mek i luk lɛk se i de ful, ɛn di ɛnimi go du wetin i se. I sakrifays sɔntin, so dat di ɛnimi go tek am.

20. We i de ol bet, i de kip am na di mach; dɔn wit wan bɔdi we gɛt pipul dɛn we dɛn dɔn pik, i de ledɔm wet fɔ am.

21. Di kleva fɛtman de luk to di ifɛkt we kɔmbayn ɛnaji gɛt, ɛn i nɔ nid tumɔs frɔm wan wan pipul dɛn. Na dat mek i ebul fɔ pik di rayt man dɛn ɛn yuz ɔl in trɛnk.

22. We i de yuz kɔmbayn ɛnaji, in fɛtman dɛn kin tan lɛk se dɛn de rɔl tik ɔ ston. Bikɔs na di kayn we aw tik ɔ ston tan lɛk fɔ de we nɔ de muv na grɔn we lɛf smɔl, ɛn fɔ muf we i de na slop; if i gɛt 4 kɔna, fɔ kam tinap, bɔt if i shep rawnd, fɔ go rɔl dɔŋ.

23. So di enaji we gud fɛtman de divɛlɔp na lɛk di mɔmɛnt we wan rawnd ston de rol dɔŋ wan mawnten we ay tawzin fit. So bɔku pan di tɔpik bɔt ɛnaji.

VI. Wik Points ɛn Strɔng

1. Sun Tzu se: Ɛnibɔdi we fɔs na di fil ɛn wet fɔ di ɛnimi in kam, go fresh fɔ di fɛt; ɛnibɔdi we de sɛkɔn na di fil ɛn we gɛt fɔ go fɛt kwik kwik wan, go kam wit taya.
2. So di kleva fɛtman de put in wil pan di ɛnimi, bɔt i nɔ de alaw di ɛnimi in wil fɔ fos am.

3. We i ol di bɛnifit dɛn to am, i kin mek di ɛnimi kam nia am bay insɛf; ɔ, if i du bad tin, i kin mek i nɔ pɔsibul fɔ mek di ɛnimi kam nia am.

4. If di ɛnimi de tek in izi, i kin mɔna am; if dɛn gi am tin fɔ it fayn, i kin mek i angri; if i kamp kwayɛt wan, i kin fos am fɔ muf.

5. Apia na say dɛn we di ɛnimi fɔ rɔsh fɔ difend; mach kwik kwik wan go na say dɛn we dɛn nɔ de ɛkspɛkt yu.

6. Ami kin mach fa fawe ɛn nɔ gɛt prɔblɛm, if i mach tru kɔntri usay di ɛnimi nɔ de.

7. Yu kin shɔ se yu go ebul fɔ atak if yu jɔs atak ples dɛn we nɔ de difend.Yu kin mek shɔ se yu difens sef if yu jɔs ol pozishɔn dɛn we yu nɔ go ebul fɔ atak.

8. Na dat mek da jenɛral de sabi fɔ atak we in ɛnimi nɔ no wetin fɔ difend; ɛn i sabi fɔ fɛt we in ɛnimi nɔ no wetin fɔ atak.

9. O divayn art fɔ subtil ɛn sikrit! Tru yu wi lan fɔ bi pɔsin we wi nɔ de si, tru yu we wi nɔ de yɛri; ɛn na dat mek wi kin ol di ɛnimi in fate na wi an.

10. Yu kin go bifo ɛn yu nɔ go ebul fɔ stɔp yu atɔl, if yu mek fɔ di ɛnimi in wik pɔynt dɛn; yu kin ritaia ɛn sef fɔ rɔnata yu if yu muvmɛnt dɛn kwik pas di wan dɛn we di ɛnimi de muv.

11. If wi want fɔ fɛt, dɛn kin fos di ɛnimi fɔ fɛt pan ɔl we dɛn dɔn shel am biɛn ay wɔl ɛn dip watawɛl. Na wetin wi nid fɔ du na fɔ atak ɔda ples we i go gɛt fɔ fri.

12. If wi nɔ want fɔ fɛt, wi kin mek di ɛnimi nɔ fɛt wi pan ɔl we dɛn jɔs de tray fɔ no di layn dɛn we wi kamp na grɔn. Ɔl wetin wi nid fɔ du na fɔ trowe sɔntin we nɔ kɔmɔn ɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛni akɔntabl na in we.

13. We wi diskɔba di ɛnimi in dispɔzishɔn ɛn nɔ de si wisɛf, wi kin kip wi fɔs dɛn kɔnsɛntret, we di ɛnimi in yon fɔ sheb.

14. Wi kin mek wan bɔdi we gɛt wanwɔd, we di ɛnimi fɔ split to smɔl smɔl pat dɛn. So wan ol go de we dɛn go pit agens difrɛn pat dɛn pan wan ol, we min se wi go bɔku to di ɛnimi dɛn smɔl.

15. Ɛn if wi ebul so fɔ atak wan pawa we nɔ impɔtant wit wan we pas am, wi ɛnimi dɛn go de pan bad bad tin.

16. Wi nɔ fɔ mek pipul no di ples usay wi want fɔ fɛt; bikɔs da tɛm de di ɛnimi go gɛt fɔ rɛdi fɔ atak we pɔsin go ebul fɔ atak am na bɔku difrɛn say dɛn; ɛn bikɔs dɛn de sheb in fɔs dɛn so na bɔku say dɛn, di nɔmba dɛn we wi go gɛt fɔ fes na ɛni say go smɔl.

17. Bikɔs if di ɛnimi strɔng in vɛn, i go wik in biɛn; if i mek in riya strɔng, i go mek in vɛn wik; if i mek in lɛft strɔng, i go mek in raytan wik; if i mek in raytan strɔng, i go mek in lɛft wik. If i sɛn riinfɔsmɛnt ɔlsay, i go wik ɔlsay.

18. Wikɛdnɛs pan nɔmba kin kɔmɔt frɔm we yu gɛt fɔ rɛdi agens di atak dɛn we pɔsin kin atak; nɔmba trɛnk, frɔm fɔ fos wi ɛnimi fɔ mek dɛn pripia ya agens wi.

19. Bikɔs wi no di ples ɛn di tɛm we di fɛt we gɛt fɔ kam, wi kin pe atɛnshɔn frɔm di fa fa say dɛn so dat wi go ebul fɔ fɛt.

20. Bɔt if dɛn nɔ no di tɛm ɔ ples, dat min se di lɛft wing nɔ go ebul fɔ sɔpɔt di rayt, di rayt nɔ go ebul fɔ sɔpɔt di lɛft, di vɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ pul di riya, ɔ di riya fɔ sɔpɔt di vɛn. I go mɔs bi se di pat dɛn we de fa pas ɔl na di ami na ɛnitin we nɔ de ɔnda wan ɔndrɛd LI apat, ɛn ivin di wan dɛn we de nia dɛn, sɔm LI dɛn de separet!

21. Pan ɔl we akɔdin to mi ɛstimat di sojaman dɛn na Yueh pas wi yon pan nɔmba, dat nɔ go bɛnifit dɛn natin pan di mata fɔ win. A se den se win kin bi.

22. Pan ɔl we di ɛnimi strɔng pasmak pan nɔmba, wi kin mek i nɔ fɛt. Skim so dat yu go no in plan dɛm ɛn di chans fɔ mek dɛn go bifo.

23. Wek am, ɛn lan di prinsipul fɔ in aktiviti ɔ inaktiviti. Fos am fɔ sho insɛf, so dat i go no di say dɛn we i nɔ go ebul fɔ du am.

24. Tek tɛm kɔmpia di ami we de agens yu wit yu yon, so dat yu go no usay trɛnk bɔku ɛn usay i nɔ de.

25. We yu de mek taktik dispɔzishɔn, di ay ay pitch we yu go ebul fɔ gɛt na fɔ ayd dɛn; ayd di we aw yu de biev, ɛn yu go sef frɔm di swɛ we di spay dɛn we nɔ de si klia wan, frɔm di mashin dɛn we di bren dɛn we gɛt sɛns pas ɔl de yuz.

26. Aw dɛn go prodyuz win fɔ dɛn frɔm di ɛnimi in yon taktik--na dat di bɔku bɔku pipul dɛn nɔ ebul fɔ ɔndastand.
27. Ol man fit si di taktik we a de win, bot wetin nobodi no fit si na di strateji we viktri de evolv.

28. Nɔ ripit di taktik dɛn we dɔn mek yu win wan win, bɔt mek yu we dɛn de du tin, de rigul bay di difrɛn difrɛn tin dɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛnd.

29. Soja taktik tan laik wata; bikɔs wata we de na in natura kɔs de rɔn kɔmɔt na ay ples dɛn ɛn go dɔŋ kwik kwik wan.

30. So fo wo, di we na to avoid wetin strong and to strik wetin wik.

31. Wata de shep in kɔs akɔdin to di kayn grɔn we i de flɔ oba; di sojaman de wok aut in win in rilayshɔn to di ɛnimi we i de fes.

32. So, jos laik se wata no de kip no konstant shep, na so fo wo, no kondishon de konstant.

33. Dɛn kin kɔl di pɔsin we ebul fɔ chenj in taktik dɛn we i kam pan in ɛnimi ɛn dat kin mek i win, dɛn kin kɔl am kapten we bɔn na ɛvin.

34. Di fayv ɛlimɛnt dɛn (wata, faya, wud, mɛtal, grɔn) nɔ kin de ɔltɛm ikwal; di 4 sizin dɛn kin mek we fɔ dɛnsɛf in turn. Smɔl de dɛn de ɛn lɔng; di mun gɛt in tɛm dɛn we i de stɔp ɛn we i de waks.

VII. We yu de manej

1. Sun Tzu se: Insay wɔ, di jenɛral kin gɛt in kɔmand frɔm di sovayb.

2. Afta i dɔn gɛda wan ami ɛn kɔnsɛntret in ami, i fɔ miks ɛn mek di difrɛn tin dɛn we de de bifo i mek in kamp.

3. Afta dat, taktik manejment de kam, pas we notin de we at pas am. Di difikulti fɔ taktik manejmɛnt na fɔ tɔn di devious to di dairekt, ɛn misfɔstans to bɛnifit.

4. So, fɔ tek wan lɔng ɛn rawnd rod, afta yu dɔn ɛnta di ɛnimi fɔ kɔmɔt na di rod, ɛn pan ɔl we yu bigin afta am, fɔ plan fɔ rich di gol bifo am, de sho se yu no di artifice of deviation.

5. Fɔ manej wit ami na bɛnifit; wit bɔku bɔku pipul dɛn we nɔ gɛt disiplin, we denja pas ɔl.

6. If yu set ful ep ami fo mach fo mek yu snatch advantej, chans de se yu go tu let. Bɔt fɔ pul wan kɔlɔn we de flay fɔ di rizin, dɛn fɔ sakrifays in bag ɛn di tin dɛn we dɛn de kip.

7. So, if yu oda yu man dem to rol op dia buff-kot, and mek fos mach witout stop de o nait, kova double di usual distans fo wan stretch, du wan handred LI so dat dem go wrest advantej, di lida dem fo ol yu tri divishon go fol fo di enemi han.

8. Di man we strɔng go de bifo, di wan dɛn we dɔn taya go fɔdɔm biɛn, ɛn pan dis plan na wan pat pan tɛn pan yu ami nɔmɔ go rich usay dɛn de go.

9. If yu mach fifti LI so dat yu go outmaneuver di enemi, yu go los di lida fo yu fes divishon, and na onli haf yu fos go rich di gol.

10. If yu mach tati LI wit di sem objek, tu-tad of yu ami go kam.

11. Wi kin tek am den se ami we no get im bagage-tren de los; if dɛn nɔ gɛt tin fɔ it, i kin lɔs; if yu nɔ gɛt bays fɔ saplae i kin lɔs.

12. Wi nɔ go ebul fɔ mek alayns te wi no di we aw wi neba dɛn de disayd.

13. Wi nɔ fit fɔ lid ami fɔ mach pas wi sabi di kɔntri in fes--in mawnten ɛn fɔrɛst dɛn, in trap ɛn prɛsipis dɛn, in mash ɛn swamp dɛn.

14. Wi nɔ go ebul fɔ tɔn natura advantej to akɔn pas wi yuz lokal gayd dɛn.

15. Fo wo, praktis dissimulashon, and yu go sakses.

16. If yu go kɔnsɛntret ɔ yu fɔ sheb yu sojaman dɛn, na di tin dɛn we de apin to yu fɔ disayd.

17. Mek yu rapiditi bi di wan we di briz de blo, yu kompaktnes bi di wan of di forest.

18. We yu de reyd ɛn tif, bi lɛk faya, yu nɔ de muv lɛk mawnten.

19. Mek yu plan dak and nobodi no fit pas laik nait, and wen yu de muv, fol laik tanda.

20. Wen yu de tif na kontri, mek dem sheb di tif fo yu man; we yu kapchɔ nyu teritɔri, kɔt am to alɔtmɛnt fɔ di bɛnifit fɔ di sojaman dɛn.

21. Tink gud gud wan and deliberate bifo yu mek muv.

22. I go win di wan we dɔn lan di artifice of deviation. Na so di art fɔ manej.

23. Di Buk fɔ Ami Manejmɛnt se: Na di fil fɔ fɛt, di wɔd we dɛn de tɔk nɔ de kɛr fa inof: na dat mek dɛn institiushɔn fɔ gong ɛn drɔm. Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ si ɔdinari tin dɛn bak klia wan: na dat mek dɛn mek ban ɛn flag dɛn.

24. Gong ɛn drɔm, ban ɛn flag, na we we di pɔsin we de ɔs di pɔsin in yes ɛn yay kin pe atɛnshɔn pan wan patikyula pɔynt.
25. Di ɔs we de mek wan bɔdi we gɛt wanwɔd so, i nɔ pɔsibul fɔ mek di wan dɛn we gɛt maynd go bifo dɛn wan, ɔ di wan dɛn we de fred fɔ tɔn bak dɛn wan. Dis na di art fɔ ol bɔku bɔku man dɛn.

26. So, fo nait fait, yus plenti signal-faya and drum, and fo fait fo de, flag and baner, as we fo influens yu ami yes and ai.

27. Dɛn kin tif wan ol ami in spirit; dɛn kin tif wan kɔmanda-in-chif in maynd.

28. Naw, sojaman in spirit kin shayn pas ɔl na mɔnin; bay noonde i dɔn bigin fɔ flag; ɛn ivintɛm, in maynd kin jɔs de fɔ go bak na kamp.

29. So, jenɛral we gɛt sɛns, de avɔyd ami we in spirit gɛt maynd, bɔt i de atak am we i slo ɛn want fɔ kam bak. Dis na di art fɔ stɔdi bɔt aw pɔsin kin fil.

30. Disiplin ɛn kol, fɔ wet fɔ di apinɛns fɔ disɔda ɛn hubub bitwin di ɛnimi:--dis na di art fɔ kip yusɛf.

31. Fɔ de nia di gol we di ɛnimi stil de fa frɔm am, fɔ wet at izi we di ɛnimi de wok tranga wan ɛn strɛch, fɔ gɛt tin fɔ it fayn we di ɛnimi de angri:--dis na di art fɔ mared pɔsin in trɛnk.

32. Fɔ avɔyd fɔ intasept ɛnimi we in ban dɛn de insay pafɛkt ɔda, fɔ avɔyd fɔ atak ami we dɛn dɔn drɔ insay kol ɛn kɔnfidɛns arenjmɛnt:--dis na di art fɔ stɔdi sikɔstɛms.

33. Na soja aksiom fɔ nɔ advans ɔp-hil agens di ɛnimi, ɔ fɔ agens am we i kam dɔŋ.

34. Nɔ rɔnata ɛnimi we de mek lɛk se yu de flay; nɔ atak sojaman dɛn we dɛn kin vɛks bad bad wan.

35. Nɔ swɛla bet we di ɛnimi de ɔfrɛd. Nɔ ambɔg ami we de kam bak na dɛn os.

36. Wen yu de roun ami, lef outlet fri. Nɔ pres yu ɛnimi we nɔ gɛt op tumɔs.

37. Na so di art of warfa bi bi.

VIII. Difrɛns insay di Taktik dɛn

1. Sun Tzu se: Insay wɔ, di jenɛral kin gɛt in kɔmand frɔm di sovayb, i kin gɛda in ami ɛn kɔnsɛntret in sojaman dɛn

2. We yu de na kɔntri we nɔ izi, nɔ kam kamp. Insay kɔntri usay ay rod dɛn de miks, jɔyn an wit yu padi dɛn. Nɔ de te na say dɛn we denja fɔ mek yu nɔ de nia ɔda pipul dɛn. Insay hemmed-in situeshɔn, yu fɔ yuz strateji. We yu de na pozishɔn we yu nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ op, yu fɔ fɛt.

3. Rod dɛn de we dɛn nɔ fɔ fala, ami dɛn de we dɛn nɔ fɔ atak, tɔŋ dɛn we dɛn fɔ sidɔm rawnd, pozishɔn dɛn we dɛn nɔ fɔ kɔntes, kɔmand dɛn we di sovayb de gi we dɛn nɔ fɔ obe.

4. Di jenɛral we ɔndastand gud gud wan di bɛnifit dɛn we de wit difrɛn taktik dɛn, no aw fɔ handle in sojaman dɛn.

5. Di jenɛral we nɔ ɔndastand dɛn tin ya, kin no gud gud wan bɔt di kɔnfigyushɔn fɔ di kɔntri, yet i nɔ go ebul fɔ tɔn in no to prɛktikal akɔn.

6. So, di studɛnt fɔ wɔ we nɔ sabi di art fɔ wɔ fɔ chenj in plan dɛn, pan ɔl we i sabi di Fayv Advantej dɛn, nɔ go ebul fɔ yuz in man dɛn di bɛst we.

7. So insay di lida we gɛt sɛns in plan dɛn, dɛn go miks di tin dɛn we dɛn fɔ tink bɔt fɔ bɛnifit ɛn di prɔblɛm.

8. If dɛn stɔp di we aw wi de op fɔ gɛt bɛnifit dis we, wi go ebul fɔ du di impɔtant pat pan wi plan dɛn.

9. If, na di ɔda say, insay di midst fɔ di prɔblɛm dɛn we wi de rɛdi ɔltɛm fɔ tek advantej, wi kin pul wisɛf pan misfɔstans.

10. Ridyus di chif dɛm we gɛt ɛnimi bay we yu de mek dɛn damej; ɛn mek trɔbul fɔ dɛn, ɛn kip dɛn de ɛnjɔy ɔltɛm; hold out specious allurements, ɛn mek dɛn rɔsh to ɛni givɛn pɔynt.

11. Di art of war de tich wi se wi nɔ fɔ abop pan di chans we di ɛnimi nɔ go kam, bɔt wi fɔ abop pan wi yon rɛdi fɔ tek am; nɔto pan di chans we i gɛt fɔ mek i nɔ atak, bɔt na pan di tru tin se wi dɔn mek wi pozishɔn nɔ atak.

12. Fayv denja fɔlt dɛn de we kin afɛkt wan jenɛral: (1) Fɔ nɔ tek tɛm, we kin mek pɔsin pwɛl; (2) fɔ fred, we kin mek pɔsin kech; (3) we pɔsin vɛks kwik kwik wan, we pɔsin kin vɛks we i de provok am; (4) wan fayn it we dɛn kin gi ɔnɔ we kin mek pɔsin shem; (5) fɔ aks in man dɛn pasmak, we kin mek i wɔri ɛn trɔbul.

13. Dis na di fayv sin dɛn we de ambɔg wan jenɛral, we de pwɛl di kɔndɔkt wɔ.

14. We dɛn pul wan ami ɛn kil in lida, dɛn go mɔs fɛn di kɔz bitwin dɛn fayv denja fɔlt ya. Mek dɛn bi sɔntin we wi fɔ tink gud wan bɔt.

IX. Di Ami na di Mach

1. Sun Tzu se: Wi kam naw to di kwɛshɔn fɔ kamp di ami, ɛn wach sayn dɛn fɔ di ɛnimi. Pas kwik kwik wan oba mawnten dɛn, ɛn kip am nia di vali dɛn.
2. Kamp na ay ples, we yu de fes di san. Nɔ klaym ayt fɔ mek yu ebul fɔ fɛt. So bɔku fɔ fɛt wɔ na mawnten.

3. Afta yu kros riva, yu shud go fa frɔm am.

4. We invayd fɔs krɔs riva we i de mach fɔ go bifo, nɔ go bifo fɔ mit am na midul strim. I go bɛtɛ fɔ lɛ af pan di ami krɔs, dɔn yu go gi yu atak.

5. If yu de wori fo fait, yu no shud go mit di invader nia riva we im get fo kros.

6. Moor yu kraft ay pas di enemi, en fes di san. Nɔ muf ɔp-strim fɔ mit di ɛnimi. So bɔku fɔ riva wɔ.

7. We yu de krɔs sɔl-mash, di wangren tin we yu fɔ bisin bɔt na fɔ pas dɛn kwik kwik wan, ɛn nɔ delay.

8. If dem fos yu fo fait fo salt-mash, yu shud get wata and gras nia yu, and get yu bak to wan grup of tik. So bɔku fɔ ɔpreshɔn dɛn na sɔl-mach.

9. Insay dray, lɛvul kɔntri, tek wan pozishɔn we izi fɔ go wit wit grɔn we de go ɔp na yu rayt ɛn biɛn, so dat di denja go de bifo, ɛn sef go de biɛn. So much fo kampen fo flat kontri.

10. Dis na di 4 yusful branch dɛm fɔ soja no we mek di Yɔlɔ Emparɔ ebul fɔ win 4 sɔm sovayb dɛm.

11. Ɔl ami dɛn lɛk ay grɔn pas lɔw ɛn ples we gɛt san pas dak.

12. If una tek tɛm wit una man dɛn, ɛn kamp na tranga grɔn, di ami nɔ go gɛt ɛni kayn sik, ɛn dis go mek dɛn win.

13. Wen yu kom fo hil o bank, occupy di san sait, wit di slope fo yu rait riya. So yu go du sɔntin wantɛm wantɛm fɔ bɛnifit yu sojaman dɛn ɛn yuz di bɛnifit dɛn we di grɔn gɛt.

14. We, bikɔs ɔf ebi ebi ren ɔp-kɔntri, riva we yu want fɔ pas, swel ɛn flek wit fom, yu fɔ wet te i go dɔŋ.

15. Kɔntri we gɛt prɛsipitɔs klif dɛn we gɛt wata we de rɔn bitwin, dip natura hol, ples dɛn we dɛn dɔn kɔnfyus, tik tik dɛn we tandul, kwagmaya ɛn krevas, dɛn fɔ lɛf am wit ɔl di spid we pɔsin kin gɛt ɛn nɔ fɔ kam nia am.

16. Pan ɔl we wi de kip fa frɔm dɛn kayn ples ya, wi fɔ mek di ɛnimi kam nia dɛn; we wi de fes dɛn, wi fɔ mek di ɛnimi gɛt dɛn na in biɛn.

17. If na di neba we yu kamp de, ɛni kɔntri we gɛt il dɛn, pɔnd dɛn we gɛt wata gras rawnd am, ɔlo bɛsin dɛn we ful-ɔp wit lid, ɔ wud dɛn we gɛt tik tik ɔnda grɔn, dɛn fɔ tek tɛm pul dɛn kɔmɔt ɛn luk fɔ dɛn; bikɔs dɛn ples ya na ples dɛn usay man dɛn we de atak ɔ spay dɛn we de ful pipul dɛn kin de slip.

18. We di ɛnimi de nia am ɛn i de kwayɛt, i de abop pan di natura trɛnk we in pozishɔn gɛt.

19. Wen im de kip fain and trai to provoke fait, im de wori se di oda said go go bifo.

20. If di ples we i de kamp izi fɔ go, i de tɛnd bet.

21. We yu muv bitwin di tik dɛm na fɔrɛst, i de sho se di ɛnimi de go bifo. We bɔku skrin dɛn de midul tik tik gras, dat min se di ɛnimi want fɔ mek wi sɔprayz.

22. Di rayz we bɔd dɛn de rayz we dɛn de flay na di sayn fɔ ambuskɛd. Di animal dɛn we de fred de sho se wan atak de kam wantɛm wantɛm.

23. We dɔst de rayz na ay kɔlɔn, na sayn fɔ chariɔt dɛn we de go bifo; we di dɔst smɔl, bɔt i spred oba wan big eria, i de sho se di sojaman dɛn we de waka na fut de kam nia. We i branch aut na difrɛn say dɛn, i de sho se dɛn dɔn sɛn pati dɛn fɔ go gɛda faya wud. Sɔm klawd dɛn we gɛt dɔst we de muf go ɛn kam, de sho se di ami de kamp.

24. Ɔmbul wɔd ɛn fɔ pripia mɔ ɛn mɔ na sayn dɛn we de sho se di ɛnimi de kam bifo. Vaylɛnt langwej ɛn drayv fɔ go bifo lɛk se i de atak am na sayn fɔ se i go tɔn bak.

25. Wen di lait chariot kom out fos and tek posishun fo di wing, na sain se di enemi de fom fo fait.

26. Pis prɔpɔzal dɛn we nɔ gɛt swɛ agrimɛnt de sho se dɛn dɔn plan fɔ du sɔntin.

27. Wen plenti de ron go and di soja dem de fol fo rank, i min se di kritikal taim don kom.

28. Wen dem si som de go bifo and som de retret, na lure.

29. We di sojaman dɛn tinap de ledɔm pan dɛn spia, dɛn kin taya bikɔs dɛn nɔ gɛt tin fɔ it.

30. If di wan dem we dem sen fo dro wata bigin fo drink dem sef, di ami de sofa wit tosti.

31. If di ɛnimi si advantej we dɛn go gɛt ɛn nɔ tray fɔ mek am sef, di sojaman dɛn dɔn taya.

32. If bɔd gɛda na ɛni ples, nɔbɔdi nɔ de de. Klamɔ na nɛt de sho se pɔsin de fred.
33. If disturbans de fo di kamp, ​​di jenerol in otoriti wik. If dɛn shift di ban ɛn flag dɛn, sedishɔn de apin. If di ɔfisa dɛn vɛks, dat min se di man dɛn dɔn taya.

34. We ami de fid in ɔs dɛn wit gren ɛn kil in kaw fɔ it, ɛn we di man dɛn nɔ de hang dɛn kuk-pɔt oba di kamp-faya, fɔ sho se dɛn nɔ go go bak na dɛn tɛnt, yu go no se dɛn dɔn mekɔp dɛn maynd fɔ fɛt te dɛn day.

35. Di si we man de wispa togeda wit smol knot o de tok wit subdued tones de sho se di rank and fail no de safe.

36. Tumɔs riwɔd de sho se di ɛnimi de dɔn in risɔs; tumɔs pɔnishmɛnt de betray wan kɔndishɔn we de mek pɔsin fil bad bad wan.

37. Fɔ bigin wit bluster, bɔt afta dat fɔ tek fred pan di ɛnimi in nɔmba, de sho se yu nɔ gɛt sɛns pas ɔl.

38. Wen dem send envoy wit kompliment fo dia maut, na sain se di enemi want mek truce.

39. If di ɛnimi in sojaman dɛn mach ɔp wit wamat ɛn de fes wi yon fɔ lɔng tɛm we dɛn nɔ jɔyn fɛt ɔ tek dɛnsɛf ɔf bak, di tin we de apin na wan we nid fɔ tek tɛm ɛn tek tɛm.

40. If wi sojaman dɛn nɔ bɔku pas di ɛnimi, dat na amply sufficient; i jɔs min se dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ atak dɛn dairekt wan. Wetin wi kin du na fɔ jɔs put ɔl wi trɛnk we wi gɛt, wach di ɛnimi dɛn gud gud wan, ɛn gɛt pipul dɛn we go ɛp wi.

41. Di wan we nɔ de tink bifo tɛm bɔt we de mek layt pan in ɛnimi dɛn, na in go mɔs bi se na dɛn go kech am.

42. If dɛn pɔnish sojaman dɛn bifo dɛn bigin fɔ lɛk yu, dɛn nɔ go pruv se dɛn de ɔnda yu; ɛn, if i nɔ put insɛf dɔŋ, dat min se i nɔ go gɛt wan yus. If, we di sojaman dɛn dɔn gɛt tayt padi biznɛs wit yu, dɛn nɔ de du pɔnishmɛnt, dɛn go stil bi pas.

43. So dɛn fɔ trit sojaman dɛn fɔs wit mɔtalman, bɔt dɛn fɔ kip dɛn ɔnda kɔntrol bay we dɛn de yuz ayɛn disiplin. Dis na wan patikyula rod fɔ win.

44. If we dɛn de tren sojaman dɛn, dɛn kin abit fɔ mek dɛn kɔmand, di ami go gɛt gud disiplin; if nɔto so, di we aw i de kɔrɛkt am go bad.

45. If jenɛral sho se i gɛt kɔnfidɛns pan in man dɛn bɔt i de insist ɔltɛm fɔ mek dɛn obe in ɔda, di bɛnifit go bi dɛnsɛf.

X. Tɛrin

1. Sun Tzu bin se: Wi kin difrɛns siks kayn tɛritri, we na: (1) Grɔn we pɔsin kin ebul fɔ go; (2) grɔn we de miks; (3) fɔ mek di grɔn fɔ sɔm tɛm; (4) smɔl smɔl pas dɛn; (5) ayt dɛn we de rɔn kwik kwik wan; (6) pozishɔn dɛn we de fa frɔm di ɛnimi.

2. Grɔn we ɔl tu di say dɛn kin pas fri wan, dɛn kɔl am aksesbul.

3. We i kam pan grɔn we tan lɛk dis, bi bifo di ɛnimi fɔ tek di say dɛn we rayz ɛn di say dɛn we di san de shayn, ɛn tek tɛm gayd yu layn fɔ sapɔt. Dɔn yu go ebul fɔ fɛt wit bɛnifit.

4. Grɔn we pɔsin kin lɛf bɔt i at fɔ ri-ɔkup, dɛn kɔl am ɛntangling.

5. Frɔm dis kayn pozishɔn, if di ɛnimi nɔ rɛdi, yu kin sally go bifo ɛn win am. Bɔt if di ɛnimi rɛdi fɔ yu kam, ɛn yu nɔ win am, den, fɔ kam bak bikɔs i nɔ pɔsibul, bad bad tin go apin.

6. We di pozishɔn na so dat nɔbɔdi nɔ go gɛt bay we dɛn mek di fɔs muv, dɛn kin kɔl am temporizing ground.

7. Insay dis kayn pozishɔn, pan ɔl we di ɛnimi fɔ gi wi wan atraktiv bet, i go fayn fɔ mek wi nɔ stir, bɔt wi fɔ tɔn bak, so dat wi go ɛnta di ɛnimi insay in yon tɛm; dɔn, we pat pan in ami dɔn kɔmɔt, wi kin gi wi atak wit bɛnifit.

8. Wit di narrow pas, if yu fit occupy dem fos, mek dem get strong garrisoned and wet di kam of di enemi.

9. If di ami fɔ stɔp yu fɔ tek pas, nɔ go afta am if di pas ful-ɔp wit garison, bɔt na if i wik garison.

10. Wit di precipitous heights, if yu de bifo wit yu enemi, yu shud occupy di raised and san spot spots, and dea wet fo am kom op.

11. If di enemi don occupy dem bifo yu, no folo am, bot retret and trai to entice am go.

12. If yu de fa fawe frɔm di ɛnimi, ɛn di trɛnk we di tu ami gɛt ikwal, i nɔ izi fɔ mek yu fɛt, ɛn fɛt-fɛt go ambɔg yu.

13. Dɛn siks ya na di prinsipul dɛn we gɛt fɔ du wit di wɔl. Di jenɛral we dɔn rich wan rispɔnsibul post fɔ tek tɛm stɔdi dɛn.
14. Naw, wan ami de fo siks plenti bad bad tins, no bi komot fo natura koz, bot from folt we di jenerol de rispansabl. Dɛn tin ya na: (1) Flay; (2) nɔ fɔ obe di lɔ; (3) fɔdɔm; (4) pwɛl pwɛl; (5) nɔ ɔganayz; (6) fɔ mek pɔsin win.

15. Ɔda kɔndishɔn dɛn we ikwal, if dɛn trowe wan pawa agens ɔda wan we pas in saiz tɛn tɛm, di tin we go kɔmɔt frɔm di fɔs wan go flay.

16. We di kɔmɔn sojaman dɛn tu strɔng ɛn dɛn ɔfisa dɛn tu wik, di rizɔlt na we dɛn nɔ de obe. We di ɔfisa dɛn tu strɔng ɛn di kɔmɔn sojaman dɛn tu wik, di rizɔlt na fɔ fɔdɔm.

17. We di ɔfisa dɛn we de ɔp de vɛks ɛn nɔ de obe, ɛn we dɛn mit di ɛnimi dɛn de gi fɛt pan dɛnsɛf bikɔs dɛn de fil bad, bifo di kɔmanda-in-chif ebul fɔ no if i de na pozishɔn fɔ fɛt ɔ nɔ de, di rizɔlt na fɔ pwɛl.

18. We di jenɛral wik ɛn nɔ gɛt pawa; we in ɔda tin dɛn nɔ klia ɛn difrɛn; we no fiks duti nɔ de we dɛn gi ɔfisa ɛn man dɛn, ɛn di rank dɛn de fɔm insay wan slovenly haphazard we, di rizɔlt na utter disorganization.

19. We jenɛral, we nɔ ebul fɔ ɛstimat di ɛnimi in trɛnk, alaw wan fɔs we nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ pawa fɔ fɛt wan big wan, ɔ trowe wik detachment agens wan pawaful wan, ɛn nɔ tek tɛm put pik sojaman dɛn na di fɔs rank, di rizɔlt fɔ bi rout.

20. Dis na siks we fɔ kɔt dif, we di jenɛral we dɔn rich wan rispɔnsibul post fɔ tek tɛm notis.

21. Di natura fomshon fo di kontri na di sojaman bes ally; bɔt pawa fɔ ɛstimat di ɛnimi, fɔ kɔntrol di pawa dɛn we de win, ɛn fɔ kɔl di prɔblɛm dɛn, denja dɛn ɛn distans dɛn wit sɛns, na di tɛst fɔ wan big jenɛral.

22. Ɛnibɔdi we no dɛn tin ya, ɛn we i de fɛt, we de put in no insay prɔsis, go win in fɛt. Di wan we nɔ no dɛn, ɛn we nɔ de du dɛn, go mɔs win am.

23. If fɛt-fɛt go mɔs mek yu win, dat min se yu fɔ fɛt, pan ɔl we di rula nɔ gri fɔ du am; if fɛt nɔ go mek yu win, dat min se yu nɔ fɔ fɛt ivin we di rula tɛl yu.

24. Di jenɛral we de go bifo we i nɔ want fɔ gɛt nem ɛn we nɔ de fred fɔ shem, we in wangren tink na fɔ protɛkt in kɔntri ɛn du gud savis fɔ in sovayb, na di jwɛl fɔ di kiŋdɔm.

25. Una tek una sojaman dɛn lɛk una pikin dɛn, ɛn dɛn go fala una go na di vali dɛn we dip pas ɔl; luk dɛn lɛk yu yon bɔy pikin dɛn we yu lɛk, ɛn dɛn go tinap nia yu te dɛn day.

26. Bɔt if yu de du wetin rayt, bɔt yu nɔ ebul fɔ mek pipul dɛn fil se yu gɛt pawa; dɛn gɛt gud at, bɔt dɛn nɔ ebul fɔ du wetin yu tɛl dɛn fɔ du; ɛn nɔ ebul, pantap dat, fɔ stɔp di prɔblɛm: den dɛn fɔ kɔmpia yu sojaman dɛn to pikin dɛn we dɔn pwɛl; dɛn nɔ gɛt yus fɔ ɛnitin we pɔsin kin du.

27. If wi no se wi oun man de fo kondishon to atak, bot wi no no se di enemi no opin fo atak, wi don go onli haf we to win.

28. If wi no se di enemi opin to atak, bot wi no no se wi oun man no de fo kondishon to atak, wi don go onli haf we to win.

29. If wi no se di ɛnimi opin fɔ atak, ɛn no bak se wi man dɛn de insay kɔndishɔn fɔ atak, bɔt wi nɔ no se di kayn we aw di grɔn tan de mek fɛt nɔ pɔsibul, wi stil dɔn go jɔs af-af fɔ win.

30. Na dat mek di sojaman we gɛt ɛkspiriɛns, we i dɔn muf, nɔ de ɛva kɔnfyus; wans i dɔn brok kamp, ​​i nɔ de ɛva lɔs.

31. Na dat mek dɛn se: If yu sabi di ɛnimi ɛn no yusɛf, yu win nɔ go tinap fɔ dawt; if yu sabi Ɛvin ɛn yu no di Wɔl, yu kin mek yu win kɔmplit.

XI. Di Nayn Situeshɔn dɛn

1. Sun Tzu bin se: Di art fɔ wɔ de no nayn difrɛn kayn grɔn: (1) Grɔn we de skata; (2) izi grɔn; (3) grɔn we pipul dɛn kin agyu bɔt; (4) grɔn we opin; (5) grɔn fɔ di aywe dɛn we de kɔba; (6) siriɔs grɔn; (7) grɔn we at fɔ du; (8) grɔn we dɛn mek wit hem; (9) grɔn we nɔ gɛt op.

2. We chif de fɛt na in yon teritɔri, na dispersive ground.

3. We i dɔn go insay teritɔri we gɛt ɛnimi, bɔt i nɔ de fa, na izi grɔn.

4. Grɔn di posɛshɔn we de import big advantej to ɛni sayd, na kɔntinyuɔs grɔn.

5. Grɔn we ɛni say gɛt fridɔm fɔ muv na opin grɔn.

6. Grɔn we de fɔm di ki fɔ tri kɔntigyuɔs stet dɛn, so dat di wan we de ɔkup am fɔs gɛt bɔku pan di Ɛmpaya we i de kɔmand, na grɔn we di aywe dɛn de kɔba.
7. We ami dɔn go insay di at fɔ kɔntri we gɛt ɛnimi, ɛn lɛf bɔku siti dɛn we gɛt wɔl dɛn biɛn am, na siriɔs grɔn.

8. Mawnt fɔrɛst, stip rɔf, mawnten ɛn fɛn--ɔl di kɔntri we at fɔ travul: dis na grɔn we at.

9. Grɔn we wi kin rich tru smɔl smɔl mawnten dɛn, ɛn we wi kin jɔs ritaia frɔm bay we wi kin yuz rod dɛn we kin rɔn, so dat smɔl nɔmba pan di ɛnimi dɛn go du fɔ krɔs wan big bɔdi pan wi man dɛn: dis na grɔn.

10. Grɔn we wi go jɔs sev frɔm pwɛl pwɛl bay we wi de fɛt witout delay, na desperate ground.

11. Na dispersive ground, so, nɔ fɛt. Na fasili grɔn, nɔ stɔp. Na grɔn we gɛt agyumɛnt, nɔ atak.

12. Na opin grɔn, nɔ tray fɔ blok di ɛnimi in rod. Na di grɔn we di aywe dɛn de kɔba, jɔyn an wit yu padi dɛn.

13. Na siriɔs grɔn, gɛda fɔ tif. Na grɔn we at, kɔntinyu fɔ mach ɔltɛm.

14. Na grɔn we gɛt hem, yuz strateji. Na grɔn we nɔ gɛt op, fɛt.

15. Di wan dem we dem de kol skilful lida fo ol, sabi draiv wed bitwin di enemi fo front and riya; fɔ mek in big ɛn smɔl divishɔn dɛn nɔ wok togɛda; fɔ mek di gud sojaman dɛn nɔ ebul fɔ sev di bad wan dɛn, di ɔfisa dɛn nɔ fɔ gɛda dɛn man dɛn.

16. We di ɛnimi in man dɛn bin gɛt wanwɔd, dɛn bin ebul fɔ mek dɛn nɔ gɛt wanwɔd.

17. Wen e bi to dia advantej, dem mek fowod; we ɔda we, dɛn kin stɔp stil.

18. If dɛn aks mi aw fɔ bia wit bɔku bɔku ɛnimi dɛn we de ɔganayz ɛn we dɛn de mach fɔ di atak, a fɔ se: "Bigin bay we yu tek sɔntin we yu ɛnimi de tek am dia; den i go gri wit wetin yu want."

19. Rɛpiditi na di men tin fɔ wɔ: tek advantej pan di ɛnimi dɛn we nɔ rɛdi, mek yu we bay rod dɛn we yu nɔ bin de ɛkspɛkt, ɛn atak ples dɛn we nɔ gɛt gad.

20. Dis na di prinsipul dɛm we wan invayd fɔs fɔ fala: Di mɔ yu go insay wan kɔntri, na di mɔ yu sojaman dɛn go gɛt wanwɔd, ɛn so di wan dɛn we de fɛt fɔ yu nɔ go win yu.

21. Mek forays fo fertile kontri so dat yu go saplae yu ami wit fud.

22. Tek tɛm stɔdi di wɛlbɔdi we yu man dɛn de du, ɛn nɔ tek dɛn pasmak. Kɔnsɛnt yu ɛnaji ɛn gɛda yu trɛnk. Kip yu ami kɔntinyu fɔ muv, ɛn mek plan dɛn we yu nɔ go ebul fɔ ɔndastand.

23. Trowe yu sojaman dem fo posishun we no eskap no de, and dem go laik day pas flay. If dɛn go fes day, natin nɔ de we dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ du. Ɔfisa ɛn man dɛn ɔl go put dɛn trɛnk we pas ɔl.

24. Sojaman wen dem de fo desperate straits de los di sense of frayd. If no ples nɔ de fɔ rɔn go, dɛn go tinap tranga wan. If dɛn de na kɔntri we gɛt ɛnimi, dɛn go sho se dɛn gɛt traŋa. If no ɛp nɔ de fɔ am, dɛn go fɛt tranga wan.

25. So, witout wet fo marshal, di sojaman dem go de ol taim fo di qui vive; we dɛn nɔ wet fɔ mek dɛn aks dɛn, dɛn go du wetin yu want; if dɛn nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ stɔp dɛn, dɛn go fetful; if dɛn nɔ gi ɔda lɔ, dɛn kin abop pan dɛn.

26. Prohibit fɔ tek ɔmen, ɛn pul di dawt we pɔsin biliv pan lay lay tin dɛn. Dɔn, te day insɛf kam, wi nɔ nid fɔ fred ɛni bad tin.

27. If wi sojaman no de ova lod wit moni, no bi bikos dem no laik rich; if dɛn layf nɔ lɔng pasmak, nɔto bikɔs dɛn nɔ want fɔ liv lɔng.

28. Di de we dɛn tɛl dɛn fɔ go fɛt, yu sojaman dɛn kin kray, di wan dɛn we sidɔm de kɔt dɛn klos, ɛn di wan dɛn we de ledɔm de lɛf di kray wata na dɛn chɛst. Bɔt lɛ dɛn briŋ dɛn wan tɛm na bay, ɛn dɛn go sho di maynd we Chu ɔ Kuei gɛt.

29. Dɛn kin kɔmpia di pɔsin we sabi fɔ du tin wit di shuai-jan. Naw di shuai-jan na snek we dɛn kin si na di ChUng mawnten dɛn. Nak in ed, ɛn in tel go atak yu; nak in tel, ɛn in ed go atak yu; strik na in midul, ɛn yu go atak wit ed ɛn tel ɔl tu.

30. Wen dem aks am if dem fit mek ami falamakata di shuai-jan, a shud ansa se, Yes. Bikɔs di man dɛn na Wu ɛn di man dɛn na Yueh na ɛnimi; yet if dɛn de krɔs riva insay di sem bot ɛn big big briz kech dɛn, dɛn go kam ɛp dɛnsɛf jɔs lɛk aw di lɛft an de ɛp di raytan.

31. So i nɔ go du fɔ put pɔsin in trɔst pan fɔ tay ɔs, ɛn fɔ bɛr chariɔt wil na grɔn

32. Di prinsipul we fɔ manej ami pan na fɔ sɛt wan standad fɔ kɔrej we ɔlman fɔ rich.
33. Aw fɔ mek di bɛst pan ɔl tu strɔng ɛn wik--dat na kwɛshɔn we involv fɔ yuz grɔn di rayt we.

34. Na so di jenɛral we sabi du in wok de kɔndɔkt in ami jɔs lɛk se i de lid wan man, we i nɔ want, wit in an.

35. Na jenɛral biznɛs fɔ kwayɛt ɛn so mek shɔ se sikrit; pɔsin we tinap stret ɛn we de du tin tret, ɛn dis go mek i kɔntinyu fɔ de ɔganayz.

36. I fɔ ebul fɔ mek in ɔfisa dɛn ɛn man dɛn nɔ no bɔt bay lay lay ripɔt ɛn apia, ɛn so i fɔ mek dɛn nɔ no natin.

37. We i de chenj in arenjmɛnt ɛn chenj in plan, i de kip di ɛnimi we i nɔ no bɛtɛ. We i de chenj in kamp ɛn tek rod dɛn we de go rawnd, i de mek di ɛnimi nɔ no wetin i want fɔ du bifo tɛm.

38. Fo di kritikal taim, di lida fo ami de akt laik wan we don klaim op hait and den kik di lada bihain am. I kin kɛr in man dɛn go dip dip wan na say we pipul dɛn nɔ lɛk am bifo i sho in an.

39. I de bɔn in bot dɛn ɛn brok in kuk-pɔt dɛn; lɛk shɛpad we de drɛb bɔku bɔku ship dɛn, i de drɛb in man dɛn dis wan, ɛn natin nɔ no usay i de go.

40. Fɔ gɛda in ɔs ɛn briŋ am to denja:--dɛn kin kɔl dis di jenɛral in biznɛs.

41. Di difrɛn mɛzhɔ dɛn we fit di nayn kayn grɔn; di fayn we aw dɛn de yuz agresiv ɔ difensiv taktik; ɛn di men lɔ dɛn we mɔtalman gɛt: dɛn tin ya na tin dɛn we dɛn fɔ rili stɔdi.

42. We dɛn de invayd ɛnimi teritɔri, di jenɛral prinsipul na, se fɔ go insay dip dip wan de briŋ wanwɔd; we de go insay bɔt shɔt we min fɔ skata.

43. Wen yu komot yu oun kontri bihain, and tek yu ami kross neba teritri, yu de fain se yu de fo krikit graun. We we dɛn de fɔ tɔk to pipul dɛn na ɔl di 4 say dɛn, di grɔn na wan pan di aywe dɛn we de kɔba dɛnsɛf.

44. Wen yu penetrate dip insaid kontri, na sirios graun. We yu penetrate bɔt smɔl we, na facile grɔn.

45. Wen yu get di enemi stronghold fo yu bak, and narrow pas fo front, na hemmed-in graun. We no ples nɔ de fɔ rɔnawe atɔl, na grɔn we nɔ gɛt op.

46. ​​So, fo dispersiv graun, a go inspaya mai man dem wit yuniti of purpose. Na fasili grɔn, a go si se klos kɔnekshɔn de bitwin ɔl di pat dɛn na mi ami.

47. Na graun we pipul de agyu, a go rɔsh go ɔp mi biɛn.

48. Fo opin graun, a go kip wach ai fo mai difens. Na grɔn we aywe dɛn de kɔba, a go mek mi alyans dɛn kɔnsolidɛt.

49. Fo sirios graun, a go trai to mek sho se kontiniu strim of saplae. Na grɔn we nɔ izi, a kin kɔntinyu fɔ push go bifo na di rod.

50. Fo hemmed-in graun, a go blok eni we fo retreat. Na grɔn we nɔ gɛt op, a go tɛl mi sojaman dɛn se a nɔ gɛt op fɔ sev dɛn layf.

51. Bikɔs na di sojaman in dispɔzishɔn fɔ gi wan traŋa resistans we dɛn dɔn rawnd am, fɔ fɛt tranga wan we i nɔ ebul fɔ ɛp insɛf, ɛn fɔ obe kwik kwik wan we i dɔn fɔdɔm pan denja.

52. Wi no fit enta alyans wit neba prins dem sote wi sabi dia dizain. Wi nɔ fit fɔ lid ami fɔ mach pas wi no di fes fɔ di kɔntri--in mawnten ɛn fɔrɛst dɛn, in trap ɛn prɛsipis dɛn, in mawnten ɛn swamp dɛn. Wi nɔ go ebul fɔ tɔn di natura advantej dɛn to akɔn pas wi yuz lokal gayd dɛn.

53. Fɔ mek dɛn nɔ pe atɛnshɔn to ɛni wan pan dɛn 4 ɔ 5 prinsipul ya we de kam biɛn ya nɔ fit prins we lɛk wɔ.

54. We prins we lɛk wɔ atak pawaful stet, in jenɛralship de sho insɛf fɔ mek di ɛnimi in sojaman dɛn nɔ kɔnsɛntret. I kin mek in ɛnimi dɛn fred pasmak, ɛn dɛn kin mek dɛn padi dɛn nɔ jɔyn agens am.

55. Na dat mek i nɔ de tray fɔ mek padi biznɛs wit ɔlman, ɛn i nɔ de fos ɔda stet dɛn pawa. I de du in yon sikrit dizayn dɛn, ɛn mek di wan dɛn we de agens am nɔ fred. Na so i ebul fɔ tek dɛn siti dɛn ɛn pul dɛn kiŋdɔm dɛn.

56. Gi riwɔd dɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit di rul, gi ɔda lɔ dɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit di arenjmɛnt dɛn we dɛn bin dɔn mek bifo; ɛn yu go ebul fɔ ol wan ol ami lɛk se yu gɛt fɔ du wit wan man nɔmɔ.

57. Konfront yu sojaman dem wit di deed sef; nɔ ɛva mek dɛn no yu dizayn. We di lukin-grɔn brayt, briŋ am bifo dɛn yay; bɔt nɔ tɛl dɛn natin we di tin nɔ fayn.

58. Put yu ami pan dedli denja, en i go sev; plɔng am na say dɛn we nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ op, ɛn i go kɔmɔt insay sef wan.

59. Bikɔs na jɔs we fɔs dɔn fɔdɔm na harm’s way we ebul fɔ bit blo fɔ win.
60. Wi kin gɛt sakrifays pan wɔ bay we wi tek tɛm akɔmod wisɛf to wetin di ɛnimi want.

61. We wi de kɔntinyu fɔ hang pan di ɛnimi in flank, wi go ebul fɔ kil di kɔmanda-in-chif fɔ lɔng tɛm.

62. Dɛn kɔl dis abiliti fɔ akɔmplit sɔntin bay sheer kɔni.

63. Di de we yu tek yu komand, blok di frontier pas, destroy di ofishal tallies, and stop di pasj of ol emissaries.

64. Bi stern fo di kaunsil-chamber, so dat yu go kontrol di situeshɔn.

65. If di enemi lef doa opin, yu mos rosh insaid.

66. Forestall yu oponunent by seiz wetin im de hol dia, and subtly contrive to taim im araval fo graun.

67. Waka fo di rod we rul de difain, and akomoda yu sef to di enemi sote yu fit fait disisiv fait.

68. So, fos, sho di coyness of maiden, sote di enemi giv yu opin; afta dat, falamakata di kwik we aw hεri we de rɔn kin rɔn, ɛn i go tu let fɔ mek di ɛnimi agens yu.

XII. Di Atak we Faya De Atak

1. Sun Tzu se: Fayv we de fɔ atak wit faya. Di fɔs wan na fɔ bɔn sojaman dɛn na dɛn kamp; di sɛkɔn wan na fɔ bɔn stɔ dɛn; di tɔd wan na fɔ bɔn bag dɛn we dɛn kin yuz fɔ kɛr pipul dɛn go; di nɔmba 4 wan na fɔ bɔn arsenal ɛn magazin dɛn; di fayv wan na fɔ trowe faya we de drɔp bitwin di ɛnimi dɛn.

2. Fɔ mek wi go ebul fɔ atak wi, wi fɔ gɛt tin dɛn we wi go ebul fɔ du. Di tin fɔ rayz faya fɔ de rɛdi ɔltɛm.

3. Di rayt sizin de fɔ mek atak wit faya, ɛn spɛshal de fɔ bigin faya.

4. Di rayt sizin na we di wɛda kin rili dray; di spɛshal de dɛn na di wan dɛn we di mun de na di kɔnstɛlɛshɔn dɛn we de na di Siev, di Wɔl, di Wing ɔ di Krɔs-ba; bikɔs dɛn 4 de ya na ɔl di de dɛn we briz de blo.

5. We pɔsin de atak wit faya, i fɔ rɛdi fɔ mit fayv tin dɛn we pɔsin kin du:

6. (1) We faya bɔn insay ɛnimi dɛn kamp, ansa wantɛm wantɛm wit atak frɔm do.

7. (2) If faya bigin, bɔt di ɛnimi in sojaman dɛn kwayɛt, wet yu tɛm ɛn nɔ atak.

8. (3) We di pawa we di faya gɛt dɔn rich in ayt, fala am wit atak, if dat pɔsibul; if nɔto so, na fɔ de usay yu de.

9. (4) If i pɔsibul fɔ mek asɔlt wit faya frɔm do, nɔ wet fɔ mek i brok insay, bɔt gi yu atak di fayn tɛm.

10. (5) We yu stat faya, bi to windward of am. Nɔ atak frɔm di say we di briz de blo.

11. Briz we de blo na de, i kin las fɔ lɔng tɛm, bɔt i nɔ kin te, briz kin blo na nɛt.

12. Insay ɛvri ami, dɛn fɔ no di fayv tin dɛn we gɛt fɔ du wit faya, dɛn fɔ kɔl di sta dɛn we de muv, ɛn dɛn fɔ kip wach fɔ di rayt de dɛn.

13. Na dat mek di wan dɛn we de yuz faya as ɛp fɔ atak de sho se dɛn gɛt sɛns; di wan dεm we de yuz wata as εp fכ di atak kin gεt akses fכ trεnk.

14. If dɛn yuz wata, dɛn kin ambɔg ɛnimi, bɔt dɛn nɔ kin tif ɔl in prɔpati dɛn.

15. Nɔ gladi fɔ di fate fɔ pɔsin we de tray fɔ win in fɛt ɛn sakrifays pan in atak dɛn we i nɔ de kɔltiv di spirit fɔ ɛntapraiz; bikɔs di rizɔlt na fɔ west tɛm ɛn jenɛral stagnɛshɔn.

16. Na dat mek dɛn se: Di rula we gɛt sɛns de put in plan dɛn bifo tɛm; di gud jenɛral de kɔltiv in risɔs.

17. Nɔ muf pas yu si advantej; nɔ yuz yu sojaman dɛn pas sɔntin de fɔ gɛt; fɛt nɔto pas di pozishɔn rili impɔtant.

18. No rula nɔ fɔ put sojaman dɛn na fil jɔs fɔ mek in yon splin satisfay; no jenɛral nɔ fɔ fɛt fɛt jɔs bikɔs i vɛks.

19. If na to yu advantej, mek fowod muv; if nɔto so, na fɔ de usay yu de.

20. Wamat kin chenj as tɛm de go to gladi at; vexation kin bi saksesed bay wetin de insay.

21. Bɔt kiŋdɔm we dɛn dɔn pwɛl wan tɛm, i nɔ go ɛva ebul fɔ kam bak igen; ɛn di wan dɛn we dɔn day nɔ go ɛva gɛt layf bak.

22. Na dat mek di rula we gɛt sɛns de pe atɛnshɔn, ɛn di gud jenɛral ful-ɔp wit kɔt. Dis na di we fɔ mek wan kɔntri gɛt pis ɛn ami nɔ go pwɛl.

XIII. Di Yuz we dɛn De Yuz Spay dɛn

1. Sun Tzu se: Fɔ rayz bɔku bɔku man dɛn we gɛt wan ɔndrɛd tawzin man dɛn ɛn mach dɛn fɔ fa fawe, dat kin mek di pipul dɛn lɔs bɔku tin ɛn fɔ mek di Stet gɛt bɔku tin dɛn fɔ drɛn. Di mɔni we dɛn go spɛn ɛvride go rich wan tawzin ɔns silva. Kɔmɔshɔn go de na os ɛn ɔda kɔntri dɛn, ɛn man dɛn go drɔp dɔŋ taya na di aywe dɛn. Na lɛk sɛvin ɔndrɛd tawzin famili dɛn go ambɔg dɛn wok.
2. Di ami dɛn we nɔ lɛk dɛnsɛf kin fɛt dɛnsɛf fɔ lɔng lɔng tɛm, ɛn dɛn kin tray fɔ win di win we dɛn kin disayd insay wan de. Dis bi so, fɔ kɔntinyu fɔ nɔ no bɔt di ɛnimi in kɔndishɔn jɔs bikɔs pɔsin grudge di outlay fɔ wan ɔndrɛd ɔns silva fɔ ɔna ɛn emolument, na di ayt fɔ inhumanity.

3. Pɔsin we de du dis nɔto lida fɔ mɔtalman, nɔto prɛsɛn ɛp to in sovayb, nɔto masta fɔ win.

4. So, wetin de mek di waes sovayb ɛn di gud jenɛral ebul fɔ atak ɛn win, ɛn ajɔst tin dɛn we ɔdinari pipul dɛn nɔ ebul fɔ rich, na fɔ no bifo tɛm.

5. Naw dis fɔ no bifo tɛm nɔ go ebul fɔ pul am frɔm spirit dɛn; i nɔ kin ebul fɔ gɛt induktiv wan frɔm ɛkspiriɛns, ɔ bay ɛni diduktiv kɔlkyulɛshɔn.

6. Na ɔda man dɛn nɔmɔ pɔsin kin no bɔt di ɛnimi in dispɔzishɔn.

7. Na dat mek dɛn yuz spay dɛn, we fayv klas dɛn de: (1) Lokal spay dɛn; (2) spay dɛn we de insay; (3) spay dɛn we dɔn chenj; (4) spay dɛn we dɔn day; (5) spay dɛn we stil de alayv.

8. Wen dis faiv kain spay dem ol de wok, nobodi fit diskova di sikrit sistem. Dɛn kɔl dis "divayn manipuleshɔn fɔ di trɛd dɛm." Na di sovayb in pawa we valyu pas ɔl.

9. Fɔ gɛt lokal spay min fɔ yuz di savis fɔ di pipul dɛn we de na wan distrikt.

10. Fɔ gɛt spay dɛn we de insay, fɔ yuz di ɔfisa dɛn fɔ di ɛnimi.

11. Afta wi don konvet spay, get hol di enemi spay and yuz dem fo wi oun purpose.

12. Fɔ gɛt spay dɛn we dɔn day, fɔ du sɔm tin dɛn opin wan fɔ ful pipul dɛn, ɛn fɔ alaw wi spay dɛn fɔ no bɔt dɛn ɛn ripɔt dɛn to di ɛnimi.

13. Spay dem we de sev, las las, na di wan dem we de bring bak nyus from di enemi kamp.

14. Na dat mek na dat we nɔbɔdi nɔ de na di wan ol ami we gɛt tayt padi biznɛs pas wit spay dɛn. Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ gɛt blɛsin we fri pas dis. Insay no ɔda biznɛs, dɛn nɔ fɔ kip sikrit we pas dis.

15. Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ yuz spay dɛn fayn fayn wan if dɛn nɔ gɛt wan patikyula intuitiv sagacity.

16. Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ manej dɛn fayn if dɛn nɔ gɛt gudnɛs ɛn tɔk stret.

17. If pɔsin nɔ gɛt sɛns we nɔ de si klia wan, i nɔ go ebul fɔ mek shɔ se dɛn ripɔt dɛn na tru.

18. Bi pɔsin we nɔ de si klia wan! bi pɔsin we nɔ de si klia wan! ɛn yuz yu spay dɛn fɔ ɔl kayn biznɛs.

19. If spay mek sikrit nyus bifo di taim rayp, dem mos kil am togeda wit di man we dem tel di sikrit to.

20. Ilɛksɛf di tin we dɛn want fɔ du na fɔ krɔs wan ami, fɔ storm wan siti, ɔ fɔ kil pɔsin, i nid fɔ bigin ɔltɛm bay we wi de fɛn di nem dɛn fɔ di atɛndant dɛn, di ɛdman dɛn we de ɛp di kamp, ​​ɛn di wan dɛn we de kia fɔ di domɔt ɛn di wan dɛn we de kia fɔ di jenɛral we de oba di kɔmand. Dɛn fɔ gi wi spay dɛn di wok fɔ no dɛn tin ya.

21. Dɛn fɔ fɛn di ɛnimi in spay dɛn we dɔn kam fɔ spay wi, tɛmpt dɛn wit brayb, kɛr dɛn go ɛn put dɛn fayn fayn wan. So dɛn go bi spay dɛn we dɔn chenj ɛn we de fɔ wi savis.

22. Na tru di infɔmeshɔn we di spay we dɔn chenj dɔn briŋ wi ebul fɔ gɛt ɛn wok fɔ di lokal ɛn inwɔd spay dɛn.

23. Na bikɔs ɔf in infɔmeshɔn, bak, wi kin mek di spay we dɔn day, kɛr lay lay nyuz to di ɛnimi.

24. Las wan, na bay in infɔmeshɔn, dɛn kin yuz di spay we stil de alayv pan tɛm dɛn we dɛn dɔn pik.

25. Di ɛnd ɛn di aim fɔ spay insay ɔl in fayv difrɛn kayn dɛn na fɔ no di ɛnimi; ɛn dis no kin jɔs kɔmɔt, insay di fɔs tɛm, frɔm di spay we dɔn chenj. So i impɔtant fɔ mek dɛn trit di spay we dɔn chenj wit di bɛst fridɔm.

26. Fɔs, di rayz fɔ di Yin dinasti na bikɔs ɔf I Chih we bin dɔn sav ɔnda di Hsia. Semweso, di rayz fɔ di Chou dinasti bin bi bikɔs ɔf Lu Ya we bin dɔn sav ɔnda di Yin.

27. So na di rula we gɛt sɛns ɛn di jenɛral we gɛt sɛns nɔmɔ go yuz di ami in sɛns fɔ spay ɛn dat go mek dɛn gɛt big big rizɔlt. Spay na di tin we impɔtant pas ɔl na wata, bikɔs na dɛn ami ebul fɔ muv.
