Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt 
Dɛn adopt am ɛn prɛd am bay Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli rizɔlt 217 A (III) we dɛn mek Disɛmba 10 
1948. Di ia 1948. 
DI PRƐAMBLƐM 
We dɛn de no di rɛspɛkt we ɔlman gɛt 
mɛmba dɛn na di mɔtalman famili, ɛn dɛn ikwal rayt ɛn we dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ tek na 
di bɛnifit fɔ fridɔm, jɔstis ɛn pis na di wɔl; ɛn  
We dɛn nɔ de tek tɛm wit pipul dɛn rayt ɛn disgres 
dɔn mek pipul dɛn de du bad bad tin dɛn we de mek mɔtalman kɔnshɛns vɛks, ɛn 
dat di krieshɔn fɔ wan wɔl usay pipul dɛn go gɛt fridɔm fɔ tɔk ɛn 
biliv ɛn go fri frɔm fred ɛn want, we dɛn prɛd as 
di ay aspireshɔn fɔ pipul dɛn; ɛn 
We i impɔtant fɔ mek dɛn protɛkt mɔtalman rayt 
ɔtoriti fɔ lɔ fɔ mek shɔ se dɛn nɔ fos pɔsin 
risɔt, as las tin, fɔ tɔn agens di bad we aw pipul dɛn de rul ɛn 
we dɛn de mek pipul dɛn sɔfa; ɛn  
We wi tink bɔt se i nid fɔ mek di divɛlɔpmɛnt go bifo 
padi biznɛs bitwin pipul dɛn; ɛn  
We di pipul dɛn na di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɔn kɔnfyus in 
Chata in biliv pan fawndeshɔnal mɔtalman rayt, rɛspɛkt ɛn valyu 
mɔtalman pɔsin ɛn di ikwal we man ɛn uman gɛt ɛn disayd 
protɛkt sɔshal prɔgrɛs ɛn impɔtant layf kɔndishɔn ɔnda 
fridɔm we pas ɔl; ɛn 
We di Mɛmba Stet dɛn dɔn kɔmit dɛnsɛf fɔ ɛp, insay 
kɔpɔreshɔn wit di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn, ɔlsay 
rɛspɛkt ɛn obe mɔtalman rayt ɛn di men fridɔm dɛn; Ɛn
We ɔlman ɔndastand di kayn we aw dɛn rayt ya de ɛn 
fridɔm impɔtant pas ɔl fɔ mek dɛn ebul fɔ du dis ful wan 
ɔbligayshɔn dɛn, . 
 

 
Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli, .  
de prɛd dis Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt as 
wan wok we ɔl pipul dɛn ɛn 
stet so dat ɔlman ɛn ɛvri ɔgan na sosayti, . 
Yu fɔ mɛmba dis Diklɛreshɔn ɔltɛm, tray fɔ du am 
ɛdyukeshɔn ɛn trenin fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya ɛn 
mek shɔ se, tru nashɔnal ɛn intanashɔnal prɔgrɛsiv 
aktiviti dɛm, yunivasal ɛn ifektiv rɛkɔgnishɔn ɛn implimɛnt 
ɔl tu bitwin di pipul dɛn na di mɛmba stet dɛn na di Ɔganayzeshɔn ɛn bitwin di pipul dɛn 
teritɔri dɛn we de ɔnda dɛn jɔrisdikshɔn.  
Atikul 1  
Dɛn bɔn ɔl pipul dɛn fri ɛn ikwal pan rɛspɛkt ɛn rayt. 
Dɛn gɛt rizin ɛn kɔnshɛns ɛn dɛn fɔ du sɔntin we gɛt fɔ du wit dɛnsɛf. 
padi insay di spirit fɔ bi brɔda ɛn sista.  
Atikul 2 
Ɔlman fɔ gɛt ɔl di rayt ɛn ɔl fridɔm, . 
we dis Diklɛreshɔn dɔn prɛd, we nɔ gɛt ɛni wan 
difrɛns lɛk kɔlɔ, man ɔ uman, langwej, rilijɔn, . 
pɔlitikal ɔ ɔda biliv, nashɔnal ɔ soshal 
ɔrijin, prɔpati, klas ɔ ɔda stetɔs.  
Pantap dat, dɛn nɔ fɔ mek ɛni difrɛns pan di bies 
pɔlitikal, ligal ɔ intanashɔnal stetmɛnt fɔ wan kɔntri ɔ 
teritɔri usay pɔsin de, ilɛksɛf i de
if di teritɔri indipɛndɛnt, trɔst, nɔ de rul insɛf ɔ 
if nɔto dat, i nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ pawa fɔ rul. 
Atikul 3 
 

 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv, fridɔm ɛn fɔ kip in prayvet pat 
inviolability.  
Atikul 4 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ bi slev ɔ slev; 
Dɛn nɔ gri fɔ mek pipul dɛn bi slev ɛn fɔ sɛl slev dɛn ɔl kayn we.  
Atikul 5 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ mek dɛn sɔfa ɔ kruk, we nɔ gɛt mɔtalman ɔ 
tritmɛnt ɛn pɔnishmɛnt we de mek pɔsin nɔ gɛt wan rɛspɛkt. 
Atikul 6 
Ɛnibɔdi, ɛnisay we i de, gɛt rayt fɔ mek dɛn no am 
pɔsin we gɛt fɔ du wit lɔ. 
Atikul 7 
Ɔl pipul dɛn ikwal bifo di lɔ ɛn dɛn gɛt rayt, we nɔ gɛt ɛni difrɛns, fɔ 
ikwal protɛkshɔn fɔ di lɔ. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn frɔm ɛnibɔdi 
ɛni diskrimineshɔn we nɔ gri wit dis Diklɛreshɔn, ɛn frɔm 
ɛnitin we go mek dɛn du dis kayn diskrimineshɔn.  
Atikul 8 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gi in rayt bak fayn fayn wan 
kɔmpiten nashɔnal kɔt dɛn we dɛn nɔ fala in fawndeshɔnal 
rayt dɛn we di kɔnstityushɔn ɔ lɔ gi am.  
Atikul 9 
 

 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ arɛst, detɛnshɔn ɔ 
we dɛn go kɛr dɛn go na ɔda kɔntri.  
Atikul 10 
Ɛnibɔdi, fɔ disayd in rayt ɛn wok ɛn fɔ 
we de sho se di kriminal chaj we dɛn chaj am, . 
gɛt rayt, pan di bies fɔ ful ikwal, fɔ gɛt in kes 
rivyu na pɔblik ɛn fɔ fala ɔl di tin dɛn we dɛn nid fɔ du fɔ mek dɛn du tin tret 
wan indipɛndɛnt kɔt we nɔ de tek wan pɔsin bɛtɛ pas ɔda pɔsin.  
Atikul 11
1. Ɛnibɔdi we dɛn se i du kraym gɛt 
di rayt fɔ tek am se i nɔ du ɛnitin te dɛn pruv se i gilti 
go mek am ligal wan tru pɔblik yɛri 
prosiding we dɛn gi am ɔl di chans dɛn 
fɔ mek dɛn protɛkt am. 
2. Dɛn nɔ go kɔndɛm ɛnibɔdi fɔ kraym pan di bies 
kɔmishɔn fɔ ɛni akt ɔ fɔ nɔ du ɛnitin, we in 
di tɛm we dɛn kɔmishɔn dɛn nɔ bin bi kraym akɔdin to di nashɔnal 
lɔ dɛn ɔ intanashɔnal lɔ dɛn. Nɔ kin ebul fɔ impɔz am bak 
pɔnishmɛnt we tranga pas di wan we dɛn bin fɔ dɔn aplay insay 
di tɛm we dɛn bin du di kraym. 
Atikul 12 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit in yon ɛn 
famili layf, arbitri atak pan di integriti fɔ 
in os, di sikrit we i de rayt to am ɔ pan in ɔnɔ ɛn gudnem. 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ mek di lɔ protɛkt am frɔm da kayn intafɛreshɔn de 
ɔ dɛn kayn atak dɛn de.  
 

 
Atikul 13 
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ muv fri wan ɛn disayd fɔ du sɔntin 
yu yon ples fɔ de insay ɛni stet. 
2. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ kɔmɔt na ɛni kɔntri, ivin in yon kɔntri 
dɛn yon, ɛn go bak na dɛn kɔntri. 
Atikul 14 
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ aks fɔ asaylans frɔm we dɛn de mek dɛn sɔfa in 
ɔda kɔntri dɛn ɛn tek advantej pan dis say fɔ rɔnawe. 
2. Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ yuz dis rayt if dɛn mek dɛn sɔfa, insay 
rialiti bays pan di kɔmishɔn fɔ nɔ-pɔlitikal
kraym ɔ du tin we nɔ gri wit di gol ɛn prinsipul dɛn 
Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn. 
Atikul 15 
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ bi sitizin. 
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ pul in kɔntri bay wilful ɔ 
di rayt fɔ chenj yu sitizinship. 
Atikul 16 
1. Man ɛn uman we dɔn rich di ej fɔ masta sabi bukman gɛt rayt fɔ nɔ gɛt 
ɛni lɔ we de agens pɔsin bikɔs ɔf in kɔlɔ, in kɔntri ɔ rilijɔn 
mared ɛn bigin famili. Dɛn kin ɛnjɔy 
ikwal rayt we i kam pan mared, di tɛm we 
di tin dɛn we kin apin we dɛn mared ɛn we dɛn de skata. 
2. Na fri ɛn ful kɔnsɛntmɛnt nɔmɔ dɛn kin dɔn mared 
ɔl tu di wan dɛn we de na di mared. 
3. Di famili na di natura ɛn besik yunit na sosayti ɛn i gɛt 
di rayt fɔ gɛt protɛkshɔn frɔm di sosayti ɛn di stet. 
 

 
Atikul 17 
1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt prɔpati as in yon, . 
ɛn togɛda wit ɔda pipul dɛn. 
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ tek in prɔpati bay wilful. 
Atikul 18  
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tink, kɔnshɛns ɛn rilijɔn; dis na 
rayt inklud fridɔm fɔ chenj in rilijɔn ɔ biliv ɛn fridɔm 
fɔ tɔk se pɔsin in rilijɔn ɔ wetin i biliv, ilɛksɛf na wan wan ɔ na di kɔmyuniti wit 
ɔda wan dɛn, pɔblik ɔ prayvet, insay tichin, wɔship ɛn 
fɔ du rilijɔn ɛn ritual sɛrimɔni dɛn.  
Atikul 19 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt fridɔm fɔ tink ɛn fɔ fri 
fɔ tɔk bɔt dɛn; dis rayt inklud fridɔm we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit am 
fala wetin pɔsin biliv ɛn di fridɔm fɔ fɛn, gɛt ɛn
fɔ sheb infɔmeshɔn ɛn aydia dɛn bay ɛni we ɛn ilɛksɛf 
stet bɔda dɛn. 
Atikul 20 
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛda pis ɛn 
asosieshɔn dɛn. 
2. Dɛn nɔ go fos ɛnibɔdi fɔ jɔyn ɛni ɔganayzeshɔn. 
Atikul 21  
1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan di manejmɛnt fɔ in 
kɔntri dairekt ɔ tru fri wan ilɛkt 
ripɔtmɛnt dɛn. 
 

 
2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal akses to pɔblik 
savis na yu kɔntri. 
3. Di pipul dɛn want fɔ bi di bɛnifit we di gɔvmɛnt gɛt; dis go 
must fɛn ɛksprɛshɔn insay periodik ɛn 
ilɛkshɔn we gɛt fɔ du wit lay lay ilɛkshɔn we dɛn fɔ gɛt ɔnda 
yunivasal ɛn ikwal rayt fɔ vot bay sikrit balot 
ɔ tru ɔda ikwal fɔm dɛn we de gi 
fridɔm fɔ vot. 
Atikul 22 
Ɔlman, as mɛmba na sosayti, gɛt rayt fɔ sɔshal 
provayd ɛn implimɛnt we nid fɔ mek i kɔntinyu fɔ de 
digniti ɛn fɔ di fri divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ in pɔsin rayt dɛn na di ikɔnomi, . 
soshal ɛn kɔlchɔral fil dɛm tru di nashɔnal ɛfɔt dɛm 
ɛn intanashɔnal kɔpɔreshɔn ɛn akɔdin to di strɔkchɔ ɛn 
risɔs dɛn we ɛni stet gɛt.  
Atikul 23 
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ wok, fɔ fri fɔ pik wok, . 
to jɔs ɛn fayn kɔndishɔn fɔ wok ɛn fɔ protɛkshɔn frɔm 
pipul dɛn we nɔ gɛt wok. 
2. Ɛnibɔdi, we nɔ gɛt ɛni diskrimineshɔn, gɛt rayt fɔ 
ikwal pe fɔ ikwal wok.
3. Ɛni wokman gɛt rayt fɔ du tin tret ɛn 
satisfay remuneration we de gi disɛnt 
pɔsin in ɛgzistens fɔ insɛf ɛn in famili, ɛn 
supliment, if nid de, bay ɔda we dɛn fɔ sɔshal 
provayd. 
4. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ mek tred union ɛn 
jɔyn tred union dɛn fɔ protɛkt dɛn intɛres. 
 

 
Atikul 24 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ rɛst ɛn ɛnjɔy insɛf, ivin di rayt fɔ gɛt rizin 
limiteshɔn fɔ wok awa ɛn fɔ pe fɔ lɛf fɔ wok wan wan tɛm.  
Atikul 25 
1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv fayn layf, ivin 
it, klos, os, mɛrɛsin ɛn nid sɔshal 
savis dɛn we nid fɔ mek pɔsin gɛt wɛlbɔdi ɛn 
wɛlbɔdi fɔ insɛf ɛn in famili, ɛn di rayt fɔ gɛt sikyɔriti fɔ 
kes we pɔsin nɔ gɛt wok, sik, disabled, uman we in man dɔn day, . 
ol ej ɔ ɔda kes we pɔsin kin lɔs in layf bikɔs ɔf 
di tin dɛn we de apin dipen pan am. 
2. Materniti ɛn infancy gi rayt fɔ spɛshal kia ɛn 
ɛp. Ɔl pikin dɛn we dɛn bɔn we dɛn mared ɔ we dɛn nɔ mared fɔ 
ɛnjɔy di sem soshal protɛkshɔn. 
Atikul 26 
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ lan buk. Edukeshon fɔ 
bi fri at least as fa as initial ɛn 
jenɛral ɛdyukeshɔn. Praymari ɛdyukeshɔn fɔ bi 
mandatory we dɛn fɔ du. Teknik ɛn Vokeshɔnal Ɛdyukeshɔn 
ɔlman fɔ gɛt am, ɛn ay edyukeshɔn fɔ bi
ikwal akses to ɔlman bays pan ɛni wan pan in abiliti. 
2. Edukeshon fɔ aim fɔ ful divɛlɔpmɛnt 
mɔtalman pɔsin ɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt mɔtalman rayt mɔ ɛn mɔ 
ɛn di men fridɔm dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt. Edukeshon fɔ promot 
fɔ ɔndastand dɛnsɛf, fɔ gri wit dɛnsɛf ɛn fɔ bi padi bitwin ɔl pipul dɛn, . 
grup dɛn we gɛt difrɛn kɔlɔ ɛn rilijɔn, ɛn dɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn no bɔt dɛn 
 

 
di wok we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn de du fɔ mek dɛn kɔntinyu fɔ gɛt 
pis. 
3. Mama ɛn papa dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ pik di kayn edyukeshɔn fɔ 
dɛn smɔl pikin dɛn. 
Atikul 27  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan di kɔlchɔ fri wan 
layf na sosayti, ɛnjɔy art, tek pat pan sayɛns 
prɔgrɛs ɛn ɛnjɔy di bɛnifit dɛn we i de gɛt. 
2. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt in gud abit ɛn 
matirial intres we kɔmɔt frɔm sayɛns, . 
litrɛri ɔ atis wok dɛn we na in rayt 
na. 
Atikul 28 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt sɔshal ɛn intanashɔnal ɔda, . 
we di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de insay dis Diklɛreshɔn kin 
fɔ bi ful implimɛnt.  
Atikul 29 
1. Ɔlman gɛt wok fɔ du to sosayti we 
Na di fri ɛn kɔmplit divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ in pɔsin nɔmɔ pɔsibul. 
2. We ɛnibɔdi de yuz in rayt ɛn fridɔm, i fɔ 
fɔ de ɔnda dɛn kayn lɔ dɛn nɔmɔ we dɛn dɔn mek 
lɔ jɔs fɔ mek shɔ se dɛn no di rayt we ɛn 
rɛspɛkt fɔ ɔda pipul dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ɛn satisfay wit fayn 
rikwaymɛnt dɛn fɔ gud abit, pɔblik ɔda ɛn jenɛral
wɛlbɔdi na dimokrasi sosayti. 
 

 
3. Di we aw dɛn de yuz dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya nɔ fɔ bi so 
nɔ gri wit wetin di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn want ɛn prinsipul dɛn 
Neshɔn dɛn. 
Atikul 30  
Nɔtin nɔ de na dis Diklɛreshɔn we dɛn go tek as 
fɔ gi to ɛni stet, grup fɔ pipul ɔ pɔsin 
di rayt fɔ du ɛnitin ɔ du ɛnitin, . 
we dɛn aim fɔ pwɛl di rayt ɛn fridɔm we dɛn put insay dis 
Diklɛreshɔn dɛn.
