Igice cya 1: Igenamigambi

Sun Tzu yavuze ko umusirikare ari ingenzi ku gihugu, aho ubuzima n'urupfu, inzira yo kubaho n'urupfu, kandi bigomba kumvikana.

Kubwibyo, kugirango unyure muri ibi, mfite ibintu bitanu, kandi kugirango ngerageze ibi, mfite gahunda, kandi ndashaka ibyiyumvo. Iya mbere ni inzira, iyakabiri ni ijuru, iya gatatu ni isi, iya kane ni rusange, naho iya gatanu ni amategeko.

Inzira ni iy'abantu kuba kurupapuro rumwe nabari hejuru yabo, bapfa nabo kandi babana nabo, kandi ntibatinye akaga. Ijuru ni yin na yang, ubukonje n'ubushyuhe, kandi birakaze. Isi ni kure kandi iri hafi, iteje akaga kandi yoroshye, yagutse kandi iragufi, n'ubuzima n'urupfu. Rusange bisobanura ubwenge, kwizera, kugirira neza, ubutwari, no gukomera. Amategeko ni sisitemu, inzira yemewe, n'intego nyamukuru.

Nubwo jenerali atazumva aba bantu batanu, ababizi bazatsinda, nabatabizi ntibazatsinda. Kubwibyo, nakoze gahunda yo kubyemeza, nshakisha amarangamutima. Bavuga ko nyagasani afite ubwenge, jenerali arabishoboye, ijuru n'isi birakwiye, amategeko arakozwe, abasirikari bakomeye, abasirikare baratojwe, kandi ibihembo nibihano biragaragara. Twese tuzi ibizava muri iyi ntambara.

Iyo wunvise gahunda yanjye yigihe kizaza, uramutse uyikoresheje, uzatsinda byanze bikunze, ntukayizirikeho. Niba utumvise gahunda yanjye, uzahomba rwose niba ukoresheje iyi gahunda, ntukayireke. Niba ikoreshwa nka gahunda cyangwa inyungu, izagira imbaraga kandi ifashe abandi. Imbaraga bisobanura kugenzura imbaraga zishingiye ku nyungu.

Umusirikare ni umuhemu.

Kubwibyo, ubushobozi bwerekana ubushobozi buke, gukoresha byerekana gukoresha nabi, hafi yerekana kure, kure yerekana hafi, ukoresheje inyungu mugushuka, imvururu zo gufata, imbuto zo kwitegura, zikomeye zo kwirinda, umujinya wo gutandukana, ishingiro ryo kwishima, mugihe ukora, umubyeyi gutandukana. Wibasire ibyo badahagije kandi ntugaragare muburyo butunguranye. Iyi ntsinzi kubasirikare, kandi nta mpamvu yo kubanza kubabwira.

Abakomeje gutsinda kubara batarwanye bagomba kugira amanota menshi yo kunguka. Abadatsinze kubara batarwanye ntacyo bazunguka kubara. Kurenza imibare yatsindiye, imibare mike ntabwo itsinze. Ntumpe gutukwa cyangwa kundeka jyenyine. Iyo turebye ibi mubitekerezo, dushobora kubona ibizavamo.

Igice cya 2: Ingamba

Nk’uko Sun Tzu abitangaza ngo itegeko rusange ryo gukoresha ingabo ni ugukoresha yen 1.000 ku igare, 1.000 yen ku igare, 100.000 li mu ntwaro, na li 1.000 mu rwego rwo gutanga ibiryo, birimo amafaranga yo mu gihugu no mu mahanga, amafaranga yakoreshejwe ku bashyitsi, ibikoresho bya kole na lacquer, n'intwaro ku magare.

Niba ukoresheje iyo ntambara igihe kirekire, bizagabanya abasirikari bawe kandi bitesha agaciro ubushobozi bwawe bukaze. Niba uteye mu gihome, uzemera, kandi nugaragaza shebuja igihe kirekire, ntacyo uzaba umaze igihugu. Iyo itesha umutwe abasirikari, igaca intege ikarishye, igabanya imbaraga, ikanasenya ifaranga, bibaho ko ba shebuja bifashisha ingaruka mbi. Nubwo hariho umunyabwenge, ntabwo azakora ibintu neza nyuma.

Kubwibyo, nubwo abasirikare bumvise kwihuta kwabo, ntibaramenya igihe bamaze bafite ubuhanga. Haraheze igihe kinini tutari abasirikare, ariko ntamuntu numwe wagiriye akamaro igihugu. Kubwibyo, umuntu wese utazi ingaruka zose zumurimo wa gisirikare ntashobora kumenya ninyungu zose zumurimo wa gisirikare.

Abakoresha neza abasirikare babo ntibazongera gukorera, kandi ntibazahabwa ibiryo kunshuro ya gatatu.

Bakura ubucuruzi bwabo mu gihugu n'ibiryo byabo ku mwanzi. Kubwibyo, hagomba kubaho ibisirikare bihagije. Niba igihugu gikennye, kigomba kujyanwa kure. Niba byoherezwa kure, abahinzi baba abakene. Abegereye mwarimu bagomba kugurisha ibicuruzwa byabo. Niba ugurisha, abahinzi bazabura amahirwe. Niba yaratakaje umutungo, yahise ahatirwa kureka umutungo we, kandi hagati mu kibaya, yavaga mu rugo ubusa, akoresheje umuhinzi, na barindwi kuri icumi. Amafaranga yakoreshejwe nabanyacyubahiro b'urukiko, amagare n'amafarashi yamenetse, imyambi yintwaro, ingofero na taret, amagare yimisozi yimisozi, na batandatu muribo basigaye muri cumi. Kubwibyo, umujenerali wubwenge akorera umugambi we kandi agaburira umwanzi. Kurya umuhoro wumwanzi umwe bihwanye na koku makumyabiri kuri njye, kandi koku imwe ku giti ihwanye na koku makumyabiri kuri njye.
Kubwibyo, abica abanzi babo bararakara. Abakoresha umwanzi ni amafaranga. Kubwibyo, uramutse utsinze imodoka icumi kurugamba rwamagare, uzagororera uwatsinze mbere, hanyuma uzamure ibendera, ukoranya amagare yawe kugirango uyagendereho, kandi uyagaburire ukora neza. Ibi byitwa kubona imbaraga mugutsinda umwanzi.

Kubwibyo, abasirikari baha agaciro intsinzi. Ntukibagirwe ibyahise. Kubwibyo, umujenerali uzi abasirikare be ni umuyobozi wabaturage akaba numuyobozi wumutekano wigihugu.

Igice cya 3: Igitero

Nk’uko Sun Tzu abitangaza ngo itegeko ry'imirimo ya gisirikare ni ryiza mu kuzuza igihugu, kandi rikaba irya kabiri nyuma yo kurisenya.

Gutsinda ingabo nibyiza, gutsinda ingabo nibyiza bya kabiri. Kurangiza urugendo nibyiza, kandi guhagarika urugendo biza kumwanya wa kabiri. Kurangiza impamyabumenyi bifatwa nkibyiza, naho kumena ni kabiri. Kurangiza icyiciro cya 5 bifatwa nkibyiza, kandi guca icyiciro cya 5 nicyiza cya kabiri.

Kubera iyo mpamvu, umuntu watsinze intambara ijana agatsinda intambara ijana ntakindi uretse umuntu mwiza. Numuntu mwiza wubaha abasirikari babantu nta kurwana.

Kubwibyo, abasirikari bakuru bateye umugambi. Ubukurikira, gabanya umuhanda. Ubukurikira, wice abo basirikare. Munsi yibyo, tera ikigo. Amategeko yo kugota ntashobora kwirindwa.

Kumenya imitambiko, imitoma, hamwe na taret, kimwe no kuba ushobora gukoresha ibikoresho, bizaba muri Werurwe. Intera izongera kwiyongera muri Werurwe. Niba jenerali adashobora gutsinda umwanzi akamutera, azica kimwe cya gatatu cy’abarwanyi, kandi niba adashobora guhunga ikigo, bizaba ari ibyago kuri icyo gitero.

Kubwibyo, abakoresha neza abasirikari babo nta kundi byagenda uretse kurwana kabone niyo bagwa mubasirikare babantu. Nubwo waba urenze igihome cy'umuntu, ntacyo bimaze kugutera. Bizaba igihe kirekire mbere yo gusenya igihugu cyumuntu. Ntabwo rwose tuzarwanya isi n'imbaraga zacu zose. Kubwibyo, ugomba gukoresha neza inyungu zawe utitonze. Iri ni ryo tegeko ryo gutera.

Kubwibyo, amategeko yintambara ni: niba ufite icumi, ugomba kuyikikiza, niba ufite atanu, ugomba kuyatera, niba uyagwije, ugomba kuyagabana, niba uri umwanzi, ugomba kuyirwanya, niba ufite bake, ugomba kuyirinda, kandi niba udafite, ugomba kwirinda. Kubwibyo, imbaraga zumwanzi muto nimbaraga zumwanzi ukomeye.

Jenerali ni umuyobozi wigihugu. Niba hari inkunga, igihugu kizahora gikomeye, kandi niba habuze inkunga, igihugu kizahora gifite intege nke. Kubwibyo, hari impamvu eshatu zishobora gutuma ingabo zawe zishobora kubabara. Babwira ingabo kujya imbere, batazi ko ingabo zigomba kujya imbere, bakababwira ngo basubire inyuma, batazi ko ingabo nta kundi byagenda uretse gusubira inyuma. Ibi byitwa `` guhungabanya igisirikare. ''

Niba utazi ibijyanye ningabo eshatu ugakurikiza politiki imwe ningabo eshatu, abasirikari bazayoberwa. Niba utazi imbaraga zingabo eshatu kandi ugasangira imirimo yingabo eshatu, uzashidikanya kubasirikare. Iyo ingabo eshatu zimaze kwitiranya no gushidikanya, abatware bafite ibibazo. Ibi byitwa `` guhungabanya ingabo no gutsinda. ''

Kubwibyo, hari inzira eshanu zo kumenya intsinzi.

Abazi igihe cyo kurwana nigihe batagomba kurwana bazatsinda. Abumva ubucuruzi bwa rubanda bazatsinda. Abafite ibyifuzo bimwe hejuru no hepfo bazatsinda. Abategereje ubwoba bafite ubwoba bazatsinda. Abadashobora kugenzurwa na general bazatsinda.

Izi eshanu ninzira yo kumenya intsinzi. Kubwibyo, biravugwa ko niba umuzi kandi ukamenya wowe ubwawe, uzarwana intambara ijana ugatsindwa bike cyane. Niba utamuzi kandi wimenye, uzatsinda cyangwa utsinde. Niba utamuzi kandi utiyizi, byanze bikunze uzarangira upfuye igihe cyose urwanye.

Igice cya 4: Katahen
Sun Tzu yavuze ko abarwanye neza mu bihe byashize nta kundi bari kubigenza uretse gutsinda mbere, hanyuma bagategereza ko umwanzi atsinda. Ni amakosa yawe ko udakwiye gutsinda, ariko umwanzi niwe ugomba gutsinda. Kubwibyo, abarwana neza ntibagomba guhatirwa gutsinda neza, ariko ntibagomba guhatirwa gutsinda abanzi babo. Kubwibyo, bivugwa ko intsinzi igomba kumenyekana, ariko ntakintu gishobora gukorwa.

Abadakwiye gutsinda bagomba kurinda. Abagomba gutsinda bagomba gutera. Kurengera, ugomba kugira bihagije, no gutera, ugomba kugira byinshi. Abarinda neza bazabikwa munsi yisi icyenda, naho abateye neza bazimuka hejuru yijuru icyenda. Noneho, irinde neza kandi ugere ku ntsinzi.

Ntabwo ari byiza nibyiza kubona intsinzi, ahubwo ni rubanda rusanzwe rubona. Ntabwo umuntu mwiza uvuga ko gutsinda urugamba bizazanira isi ibyiza. Kubwibyo, ntabwo ari byinshi cyane kuzamura Akiyoshi. Sinshobora kubona izuba n'ukwezi mpumuye amaso. Bisaba ugutwi kwubwenge kumva inkuba.
Abarwana neza, ibyo bita mubihe bya kera, ni abatsinze mugihe byoroshye gutsinda. Kubwibyo, niba umuntu urwana neza atsinze, ntihazaba icyamamare cyangwa ubutwari. Kubwibyo, ntaho bitandukaniye ko urugamba rwatsinze. Abadatandukanye bazahora batsinze aho bahisemo. Tugomba gutsinda abamaze gutsindwa. Kubwibyo, abarwana neza bahagarara kubutaka budatsindwa, kandi ntibatakaza gutsindwa kwabanzi babo. Kubera iyo mpamvu, ingabo zatsinze zabanje gutsinda hanyuma zigashaka urugamba, kandi ingabo zatsinzwe zabanje kurwana hanyuma zigashaka intsinzi.

Abakoresha abasirikari bahinga neza Tao kandi bagashyigikira Dharma. Kubwibyo, akenshi bayobora muburyo bwo gutsindwa.

Ubuhanga bwintambara, nkuko Ichi abivuga, igihe. Amafaranga yavuzwe mu isegonda. Umubare wa gatatu uravuga. Izina rya kane. Ukurikije Go, ni intsinzi. Isi itanga impamyabumenyi, impamyabumenyi itanga ubwinshi, ubwinshi butanga umubare, umubare utanga umutwe, naho umutwe utanga intsinzi. Kubwibyo, ni nkaho ingabo zatsinze zasakuzaga bolt, kandi ingabo zatsinzwe zasa nkaho zita bolt hamwe na bolt.

Abantu batsinze bakimara guhatirwa kurugamba, bizaba nko gufata Sekisui mubibaya igihumbi.

Igice cya 5: Seihen

Nk’uko Sun Tzu abivuga, kuyobora rubanda no gutegeka bake ni nk'uduce. Nicyo cyerekana gutuma rubanda irwana no gukora bake. Abantu b'ingabo eshatu bagomba kwemera umwanzi bagatsindwa, ibi rero nibyo bagomba gukora. Kwiyongera kw'abasirikare ni nko gutera umuheto ku magi, ariko ni ukuri kandi ni ibinyoma.

Muri rusange, intambara zitsindwa no gukiranuka no kudasanzwe. Kubwibyo, ababyara ibintu byiza kandi bidasanzwe bameze nkijuru nisi, bitagira iherezo, kandi bimeze nkinzuzi ninzuzi, bitagira iherezo. Irangira kandi itangira nanone gutya buri munsi na buri kwezi. Bifata amasaha ane kugirango upfe kandi tuvuke ubwa kabiri. Nubwo hari amajwi atanu gusa, igitangaje cyamajwi atanu ni menshi kuburyo udashobora kureka kuyumva. Nubwo hariho amabara atanu gusa, guhindura amabara atanu ni ngombwa-kureba. Nubwo uburyohe ari butanu gusa, igitangaje cyibiryo bitanu kirashobora kuneshwa kandi ntigomba kuryoha.

Nubwo imbaraga zo kurwana ntakindi kirenze izidasanzwe, ibyabaye kuri Kiyoshi birashobora gutsinda kandi nta mpamvu yo kuba mubibazo. Ibibaho mugihe hari ibitagenda neza ni nkumuzingo utagira iherezo. Ninde ubyitayeho?

Imbaraga zamazi yihuta niyo yatumye amabuye atemba. Nibihinduka ko inyoni ya kagoma yihuta ikarangira itsinzwe. Kubera iyo mpamvu, abarwana neza bazagira urugamba rukomeye nintambara ngufi. Imbaraga ni nkumusaraba, kandi ipfundo ni nkamahirwe.

Imvururu zivuka muri Osamu, ubwoba buturuka mubutwari, n'intege nke zikavamo imbaraga. Hariho imyigaragambyo myinshi. Ubutwari ni imbaraga. Imiterere idakomeye nuburyo.

Kubwibyo, abimura abanzi babo muburyo bwiza bazemeza ko nibakora ibyo bashaka, umwanzi azakurikiza byanze bikunze ibyo bakora, kandi nibateganya kubikora, umwanzi azafata ibyo akora rwose. Himura ufite inyungu hanyuma utegereze kubuntu.

Kubwibyo, abarwana neza bashaka ibi nkimbaraga, kandi ntibagaya abandi. Kubwibyo, ahitamo ubwoko bwe neza kandi ntabwo yishingikiriza ku mbaraga ze. Iyo uretse umuntu mububasha bwawe akakuyobora kurugamba, ni nko guhindura ibuye ibuye. Imiterere yinkwi namabuye nuko iyo ifite umutekano, iba ikiriho; iyo ari akaga, irimuka; iyo igiye, irahagarara; iyo ari umuzenguruko, iragenda. Kubwibyo, imbaraga zituma abantu barwanira ibyiza nimbaraga zihindura ibuye rizengurutse imisozi igihumbi.

Igice cya 6: Ukuri nimpimbano

Nk’uko Sun Tzu abitangaza ngo abajya ku rugamba mbere bagategereza umwanzi bazababara, mu gihe abajya ku rugamba nyuma bakarwana urugamba bazakora cyane. Kubwibyo, abarwana neza ntibareka ngo abandi babakorere. Niba ukunze gukora nk'umwanzi, uzabyungukiramo. Akenshi amakosa yumwanzi niyo ateza ibyago. Kubwibyo, niba ufite umwanzi, uzakora cyane, niba urambiwe, uzarya, kandi niba bihendutse, uzimura.

Niba urangirira ahantu byanze bikunze uzarangirira, niba urangirira ahantu udashaka, kandi niba udakoze inzira yawe mu bilometero igihumbi, ugomba kunyura mubutaka bwumuntu. Inzira yonyine yo gutera no gufata ikintu ni ugutera uduce tudashobora kurindwa. Inzira yonyine yo gukomera mugihe cyo kwirwanaho ni ukurinda uduce tudashobora kwibasirwa. Kubwibyo, abateye neza ntibazi umwanzi wabo cyangwa aho arengera. Abarinda neza ntibazi umwanzi cyangwa aho azatera. Irashobora kuba yoroheje cyangwa yoroheje, ariko igera aho idafatika. Ni Imana? Ndabaza niba ari Imana, kugeza igihe icecekeye. Kubwibyo, akenshi ategeka umwanzi.
Inzira yonyine yo gukomeza ni ugukuraho icyo kinyoma. Niba udashobora gusubira inyuma no kubikurikirana, ntushobora kubigeraho vuba. Kubwibyo, niba nshaka kurwana, umwanzi ashobora kuzamura ibirindiro bye no kongera imyobo ye, ariko niba nta kundi byagenda uretse kundwanya, ngomba gutera aho ashobora kunkiza. Niba ntifuzaga kurwana, nashyira akamenyetso ku butaka nkakirinda, ariko umwanzi, nta kundi byagenda uretse kundwanya, agomba kujya aho hantu.

Kubwibyo, niba ntashizeho abantu muburyo bwanjye, ni ukuvuga ko nzashobora kumva umwanzi. Niba twunze ubumwe nkumwe, kandi abanzi bacu bagabanijwemo icumi, noneho tuzatera umwe hamwe naba icumi. Muyandi magambo, turi benshi kandi abanzi bacu ni bake. Ninkubita bake hamwe na rubanda, abarwana nanjye bazarimbuka. Ntugomba kumenya igihugu tuzarwanira hamwe. Niba utabizi, hari abantu benshi umwanzi yiteguye. Niba umwanzi yateguye benshi, noneho abarwana nanjye ni bake. Kubwibyo, niba witegura imbere, ni ukuvuga, hari bike inyuma; niba witegura inyuma, ni ukuvuga, imbere imbere; niba witegura ibumoso, ni ukuvuga, munsi iburyo; niba witegura iburyo, bivuze bike ibumoso; kandi niba ntacyo ufite cyo gutegura, ni ukuvuga, ntihabuze. Ubupfakazi ni ikintu abantu biteguye. Rubanda rutegura abandi ubwabo.

Kubwibyo, niba uzi aho urugamba n'umunsi w'intambara, ugomba kurwana ibirometero igihumbi. Niba utazi aho urugamba rwabereye n'umunsi w'intambara, ibumoso ntibushobora gukiza iburyo, iburyo ntibushobora gukiza ibumoso, imbere ntishobora gukiza inyuma, kandi inyuma ntishobora gukiza imbere. Ntukagire icyo uvuga, ni kilometero mirongo, na kilometero nyinshi hafi. Nubwo Intama zifite umubare munini wabasirikare, ntacyo bizatumarira mubijyanye no gutsinda cyangwa gutsindwa. Avuga rero ko intsinzi igomba kugerwaho. Nubwo umwanzi ari benshi, ntitugomba kurwana.

Kubwibyo, mugutegura ibi, uzamenya gahunda yinyungu nigihombo, nukurema ibi, uzamenya ihame ryimikorere, nukubikora, uzamenya aho ubuzima nurupfu, kandi nukurikiza ibi, uzamenya aho hari ibisagutse nibibura.

Kubwibyo, uburyo bwanyuma bwumusirikare nubusa. Niba bidafatika, ntibizashoboka ko abimbitse babibona, ndetse nabanyabwenge ntibazashobora gutegura umugambi. Nubwo intsinzi ishobora kwitiranywa kubera imiterere, abaturage ntibazigera babimenya. Abantu bazi imiterere yimpamvu natsinze, ariko ntibazi imiterere yimpamvu igenga intsinzi yanjye. Kubwibyo, niyo urugamba rwatsinze, ntabwo rwihorera, ahubwo rusubiza ifishi ntakibazo.

Imiterere yumusirikare isa namazi. Imiterere y'amazi iramanuka, irinda uburebure. Imiterere yumusirikare irinda ukuri kandi irasa icyuho. Amazi agenzura imigezi yayo bitewe nisi, kandi abasirikari bagenzura intsinzi yabo bitewe numwanzi. Kubwibyo, abasirikari ntibahoraho, kandi amazi ntagahinduka gahoraho. Abatsinze bahinduka bitewe numwanzi bakunze kwitwa imana. Kubwibyo, nta ntsinzi ihoraho mubintu bitanu, nta mwanya uhoraho mumasaha ane, hariho uburebure buke kandi burebure kumunsi, kandi hariho urupfu no kuvuka mukwezi.

Igice cya 7: Intambara ya Gisirikare

Sun Tzu yavuze ko nta kindi kigoye uretse intambara ya gisirikare yo gukoresha abasirikare, kugira ngo umujenerali ahabwe amabwiriza n'umutware, guhuza ingabo ze, kumva abaturage, no kubaho mu mahoro. Ingorane zintambara za gisirikare zigenwa no kuzenguruka no kugorana. Kubwibyo, mfata inzira, nkoresha amahirwe yo kugerageza abandi, gutangira nyuma yabandi, nkagera imbere yabandi. Uyu numuntu uzi amayeri yo kuzenguruka.

Kubwibyo, intambara za gisirikare zunguka kandi intambara za gisirikare ni mbi. Niba uzamuye ingabo ukarwanira inyungu zawe, ntuzashobora kuwugeraho, ariko nushira ingabo zawe kurwanira inyungu zawe, uzarengerwa. Kubera iyo mpamvu, niba udatanze ingabo, urapfa, niba udafite ibyo kurya, urapfa, kandi niba utanze, urapfa. Kubera iyo mpamvu, amuzunguza amaboko arambuka, kandi iyo yambutse umuhanda, akubye kabiri intera, kandi arwanira inyungu zirenga li ijana, ahatira abajenerali batatu kumutera. Abakomeye bazagenda mbere, abarushye bazasigara inyuma, kandi amategeko ni icumi na rimwe. Iyo urwanira inyungu zirenga 50 li, utuka general mukuru. Iri tegeko ni kimwe cya kabiri cyakozwe. Iyo uhatanira inyungu kurenza intera ya 30 li, itandukaniro ni bibiri bya gatatu. Hamwe nibi, uzumva ingorane zintambara ya gisirikare.
Kubwibyo, abatazi imigambi yibihugu bitandukanye ntibazashobora kwiteranya mbere. Abatazi imiterere yimisozi, amashyamba, n imisozi ntibashobora kujya kurugamba. Abadakoresha ubuyobozi ntibazigera babona inyungu zisi.

Kubwibyo, abasirikari bahagarara muburiganya, bagenda bafite inyungu, kandi bahindura amacakubiri. Kubwibyo, umuvuduko wacyo umeze nkumuyaga, gutinda kwayo ni nkishyamba, kwiyegereza kwayo ni nkumuriro, kudahungabana kwayo ni nkumusozi, kutumvikana kwayo ni nkigicucu, kandi kugenda kwayo ni nkinkuba. Yagabanyijemo ubwoko bwe kugira ngo atere umudugudu, agabana inyungu ze mu kuzerera mu butaka, kandi yimuka afite ububasha. Uzi mbere yigihe azenguruka azatsinda. Iri ni ryo tegeko ry'intambara ya gisirikare.

Nk’uko guverinoma ya gisirikare ibivuga, `` Ndetse iyo mbivuze, sinshobora kubyumva, bityo nkora ingoma ya zahabu. Iyo mbibonye, ​​sinshobora kuyibona, bityo nkora ibendera riguruka. Nk’uko Sun Tzu abitangaza ngo hari ubwoko butanu bwo kwibasira umuriro. 1 ivuga, gutwika abantu, 2 ivuga gutwika ibicuruzwa, 3 ivuga ko watwitse ububiko, 4 ivuga ko watwitse ububiko, 5 ivuga ko watwitse iyi kipe. Hama hariho imvo yo gukora umuriro. Fireworks zitangwa buri gihe. Hariho igihe cyo gutwika umuriro, umunsi wo gutwika umuriro, kandi igihe nikuma cyijuru. Izuba riri muri wino, urukuta, amababa, nigicucu cyukwezi. Uyu ni umunsi umuyaga uhuha muri izi ndiri enye.
Ibitero byumuriro burigihe bisubiza ibi kubera Impanuka Zitanu. Niba umuriro usohotse imbere, uzahita usubiza hanze. Niba umuriro waka kandi abasirikare bakicecekera, ntutegereze gutera. Kumenya imbaraga zumuriro, kuyumvira uko bikwiye, no guhagarara mugihe bitagomba. Umuriro ugomba gukwirakwira hanze; ntutegereze imbere; kumurika mugihe. Umuriro, iyo wohereje kumuyaga wo hejuru, ntabwo uzatera umuyaga wo hasi. Umuyaga wo ku manywa urashize, kandi umuyaga nijoro urahagarara. Igisirikare kizahora kimenya ibijyanye numuriro utanu kandi ubarinde numero zabo.

Kubwibyo, uwibasiye umuriro arasobanutse. Utera amazi arakomeye. Amazi agomba gucibwa, kandi ntagomba kujyanwa.

Byaba ari ibyago turamutse dutsinze urugamba kandi ntitwitoze ibyiza byarwo. Bavuga ko iryo tegeko yahawe. Kubwibyo, biravugwa ko Meishu ibitekereza, kandi nibikorwa rusange muri rusange. Niba bidafasha, ntukimuke; niba bidafasha, ntukoreshe; niba uri mu kaga, ntukarwanye. Shebuja ntagomba kurera shebuja arakaye, kandi jenerali ntagomba kurwana afite ubwoba. Igenda ikurikije inyungu, igahagarara iyo idahuye ninyungu. Uburakari bugomba kwishima, kandi uburakari bugomba kwishima, ariko igihugu ntigikwiye kubaho ukundi, kandi abapfuye ntibagomba kubaho ukundi. Kubwibyo, abatware ba Ming birinda gukora ibi, kandi abajenerali beza birinda ibi. Ninzira y'amahoro kubwigihugu no kuzuza igisirikare.

Igice cya 13: Yoma-inkoko

Sun Tzu yagize ati, Bisaba abagera ku 100.000 kugira ngo bashireho shobuja, ibirometero 1.000 kugira ngo bajye ku rugamba, amafaranga y’abahinzi, serivisi z’abanyacyubahiro bo mu nkiko, imiryango 700.000 nta kundi byagenda uretse kugenzura ibibazo, imidugararo mu gihugu ndetse no mu mahanga, birengagiza imihanda, barwanira intsinzi mu munsi umwe mu myaka myinshi.

Ariko, abakunda ubutunzi nubutunzi kandi batazi ibyiyumvo byabanzi babo ntibagereranywa. Ntagomba kuba umuyobozi wabantu. Ntabwo ari muburyo bwa Nyagasani, ntabwo ari intsinzi y'Uwiteka. Kubwibyo, impamvu abajenerali b'abanyabwenge ba nyagasani Ming bashoboye gutsinda abandi kandi bagatsinda neza bigomba kumenyekana mbere. Uzi mbere ntagomba gufatwa nkimana yabadayimoni. Ntigomba kwiganwa, cyangwa ntigomba kuba inararibonye. Buri gihe niwe uzi ibyiyumvo byumwanzi.

Kubwibyo, hari inzira eshanu zo gukoresha igihe. Hariho Inma, hariho Uchima, hariho Anima, hariho Urupfu, kandi hariho kubaho. Ibi byitwa indero yimana, mugihe ibihe bitanu byose bibaho utazi inzira. Ni ubutunzi bwawe. Inma irashobora gukoreshwa bitewe nabantu baho. Ijambo "icyumba cyimbere" rishobora gukoreshwa bitewe numuyobozi uvugwa. Ibi birashobora gukoreshwa bitewe numwanzi. Icyumba cyapfuye niho hamenyekana uburiganya, kandi bikamenyekana mu banzi. Bitandukanye no kubaho.

Kubwibyo, iyo bigeze ku ngabo eshatu, ntakintu cyegereye kuruta hagati, nta gihembo cyimbitse kuruta hagati, kandi ibintu biri hafi kuruta hagati. Niba atari mubwenge bwera, ntampamvu yo gukoresha igihe. Ntampamvu yo guta igihe cyawe keretse biterwa nubumuntu. Niba bidatandukanye gato, ntuzabona ibisubizo. Sinzi niba ari bike cyangwa atari byo, ariko nta kundi nabigenza uretse gukoresha ikiruhuko. Niba ukuri kutavuzwe ukabyumva mbere, abavuga ukuri bose bazapfa.

Igihe cyose ingabo zishaka gutera, ikigo gikeneye kwibasirwa, cyangwa umuntu ushaka kwicwa, ikintu cya mbere cyo gukora nukumenya amazina nizina rya jenerali, ibumoso n iburyo, abateranye, abihayimana, nabihayimana, kandi ukareba neza ayo makuru.

Nzashakisha rwose abaza mu banzi banjye ngo bambuze, kandi nzabafasha mbishingikirije, kandi nzabayobora kandi ndabarinde. Kubwibyo, ugomba kubona no gukoresha anti-ma. Uku nuburyo ubizi. Kubwibyo, birakenewe kubona no gukoresha umujyi na Uchima. Uku nuburyo ubizi. Kubwibyo, mbere yuko upfa, shuka kandi ubwire abanzi bawe. Uku nuburyo ubizi. Kubwibyo, ubuzima bugomba gufatwa nkibihe. Uwiteka rwose azamenya iby'iki kintu cy'iminota itanu. Umuntu wenyine uzabimenya ni Danma. Kubwibyo, nta kundi byagenda uretse gukora icyuho.

Kera, igihe ingoma ya Shang yazamutse, Izhe yabayeho mu cyi. Igihe Ingoma ya Zhou yazamutse, Lu Fang yari mu ngoma ya Shang. Kubwibyo, igikomangoma cyubwenge gusa numujenerali wubwenge arashobora gukora ibintu nubwenge nubwenge, kandi rwose azagera kubikorwa bikomeye. Iri ni ryo buye ry'ifatizo ry'abasirikare, kandi ingabo eshatu zigenda hamwe.
