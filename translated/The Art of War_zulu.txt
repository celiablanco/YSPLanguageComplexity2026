I. Izinhlelo Zokubeka

1. USun Tzu uthe: Ubuciko bempi bubaluleke kakhulu kuHulumeni.

2. Kuyindaba yokuphila nokufa, indlela eya ekuphepheni noma encithakalweni. Ngakho-ke kuyisihloko sophenyo esingenakunakwa ngaso.

3. Ngakho-ke, ubuciko bempi bulawulwa yizici ezinhlanu ezingaguquki, okufanele zicatshangelwe ezingxoxweni zomuntu, lapho efuna ukucacisa izimo ezitholakala ensimini.

4. Yilezi: (1) Umthetho Wokuziphatha; (2) Izulu; (3) Umhlaba; (4) UMkhuzi; (5) Indlela nesiyalo.

5,6. UMthetho Wokuziphatha ubangela ukuba abantu bavumelane ngokuphelele nombusi wabo, ukuze bamlandele kungakhathaliseki ukuthi ukuphila kwabo kukuphi, bangethuswa iyiphi ingozi.

7. Izulu lisho ubusuku nemini, amakhaza nokushisa, izikhathi nezinkathi.

8. Umhlaba wakhiwe amabanga, amakhulu namancane; ingozi nokuvikeleka; indawo evulekile nezindawo eziwumngcingo; amathuba okuphila nokufa.

9. UMkhuzi umele izimfanelo ezinhle zokuhlakanipha, ubuqotho, umusa, isibindi kanye nokuqina.

10. Ngokwenqubo kanye nokuziphatha kufanele kuqondwe ukuhlelwa kwebutho ezigabeni ezifanele, ukuthweswa iziqu kwezikhundla phakathi kwezikhulu, ukugcinwa kwemigwaqo izimpahla ezingafinyelela ngayo ebuthweni, nokulawulwa kwezindleko zezempi.

11. Lawa makhanda amahlanu kufanele ajwayele wonke ujenene: owaziyo uyoba ngumnqobi; ongazaziyo uyohluleka.

12 Ngakho-ke, ezingxoxweni zenu, lapho nifuna ukunquma izimo zezempi, makube yisisekelo sokuqhathanisa, ngale ndlela:-

13. (1) Imuphi kulaba ababili ababusi ogcwele uMthetho Wokuziphatha? (2) Imuphi kujenene ababili onekhono elikhulu? (3) Zilele kobani izinzuzo ezitholakala eZulwini naseMhlabeni? (4) Kukuluphi uhlangothi isiyalo esiphoqelelwa ngokuqinile kakhulu? (5) Iliphi ibutho elinamandla? (6) Kukuluphi uhlangothi izikhulu namadoda aqeqeshwe kakhulu? (7) Kukuliphi ibutho okukhona ukungaguquguquki okukhulu kokubili emvuzweni nasekujeziseni?

14. Ngalezi zinto eziyisikhombisa ngingabikezela ukunqoba noma ukwehlulwa.

15. Ujenene olalela iseluleko sami futhi enze ngokuvumelana naso, uyonqoba: onjalo makagcinwe emyalweni! Ujenene ongasilaleli iseluleko sami futhi angasisebenzisi, uyokwehlulwa:--makachithwe onjalo!

16. Ngenkathi ngihola inzuzo yeseluleko sami, zitholele futhi kunoma yiziphi izimo eziwusizo ngaphezu nangaphezulu kwemithetho evamile.

17 Njengoba izimo zivuma, umuntu kufanele aguqule izinhlelo zakhe.

18. Zonke izimpi zisekelwe ekukhohliseni.

19. Ngakho, lapho sikwazi ukuhlasela, kumelwe sibonakale singakwazi; lapho sisebenzisa amandla ethu, kumelwe sibonakale singasebenzi; uma siseduze, kufanele senze isitha sikholelwe ukuthi sikude; lapho ukude, kumelwe simenze akholwe ukuthi siseduze.

20. Bamba izithiyo ukuze uhehe isitha. Feign disorder, futhi umchoboze.

21. Uma elondekile kukho konke, mlungiselele; Uma enamandla amakhulu, mxwaye.

22. Uma omelene nawe ethukuthele, funa ukumcasula. Azenze obuthakathaka, ukuze azikhukhumeze.

23. Uma ekhululeka, ungamphumuli; Uma amabutho akhe ehlangene, ahlukanise.

24. Mhlasele lapho engakakulungeli khona, vela lapho ungalindelwe khona.

25. Lezi zikhali zempi, eziholela ekunqobeni, akufanele zidalulwe ngaphambili.

26 Manje ujenene onqoba impi wenza izibalo eziningi ethempelini lakhe impi ingakaliwa. Ujenene ohluliwe empini wenza izibalo ezimbalwa ngaphambili. Ngaleyo ndlela izibalo eziningi ziholela ekunqobeni, futhi izibalo ezimbalwa ezizonqotshwa: kangakanani-ke akukho zibalo nhlobo! Kungenxa yokunaka leli phuzu lapho ngikwazi ukubona kusengaphambili ukuthi ubani ongase anqobe noma ahlulwe.

II. Ukulwa Impi

1. USun Tzu wathi: Emikhankasweni yempi, lapho enkundleni kukhona izinqola ezisheshayo eziyinkulungwane, nezinqola eziningi ezinzima, namasosha agqoke iposi ayizinkulungwane eziyikhulu, nokudla okwanele ukubathwala ama-li ayinkulungwane, izindleko zasekhaya nangaphambili, kuhlanganise nokuzijabulisa kwezivakashi, izinto ezincane ezifana neglu nopende, nezibalo ezichithwa ezinqoleni nesamba sezikhali zesiliva ngosuku zizofinyelela ingqikithi yezikhali eziyinkulungwane ngosuku. Zinjalo izindleko zokukhulisa ibutho lamadoda ayi-100,000.

2. Uma ubamba iqhaza ekulweni kwangempela, uma ukunqoba kusazofika, khona-ke izikhali zamadoda zizoba buthuntu futhi ukushisekela kwazo kuzoncipha. Uma uvimbezela umuzi, uyakuqeda amandla akho.

3. Futhi, uma umkhankaso uthatha isikhathi eside, izinsiza zoMbuso ngeke zilingane nobunzima.
4. Manje, lapho izikhali zakho seziphelile, intshiseko yakho isinciphile, amandla akho ephelile futhi umcebo wakho usuchithiwe, ezinye izikhulu zizovela ukuze zisebenzise umkhawulo wakho. Khona-ke akukho muntu, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi uhlakaniphe kangakanani, oyokwazi ukugwema imiphumela okumelwe ilandele.

5. Ngakho, nakuba siye sezwa ngokuxhamazela kobuwula empini, ukuhlakanipha akukaze kubonakale kuhlotshaniswa nokubambezeleka isikhathi eside.

6. Asikho isibonelo sezwe elihlomule ezimpini ezithatha isikhathi eside.

7. Nguye kuphela owazi kahle ububi bempi ongayiqonda kahle indlela enenzuzo yokuqhubeka nayo.

8. Isosha elinekhono alinyusi intela yesibili, nezinqola zalo zokuthwala azilayishwa ngaphezu kokuphindwe kabili.

9. Woza nempahla yempi evela ekhaya, kodwa udle isitha. Ngaleyondlela ibutho liyoba nokudla okwanele izidingo zalo.

10. Ubumpofu besikhwama soMbuso budala ukuthi ibutho ligcinwe ngeminikelo esuka kude. Ukuba nesandla ekulondolozeni ibutho kude kudala ukuthi abantu bahlupheke.

11. Ngakolunye uhlangothi, ukusondela kwebutho kubangela ukuba amanani enyuke; futhi amanani entengo aphezulu adala ukuthi impahla yabantu iphele.

12. Uma izinto zabo seziphelile, abalimi bayohlushwa yizindleko ezinzima.

13,14. Ngalokhu kulahlekelwa yizinto kanye nokuphelelwa amandla, amakhaya abantu azokwehlulwa, futhi ingxenye yesithathu yeshumi yemali abayiholayo iyoqedwa; kuyilapho izindleko zikahulumeni zezinqola zempi eziphukile, amahhashi agugile, izihlangu zesifuba nezigqoko zokuzivikela, iminsalo nemicibisholo, imikhonto nezihlangu, iziphuku zokuzivikela, izinkabi ezigugile nezinqola ezisindayo, ziyoba ingxenye yesine yenzuzo yayo yonke.

15. Ngakho-ke ujenene ohlakaniphile wenza iphuzu lokuphakela isitha. Umthwalo wenqola owodwa wokuphakelwa kwesitha ulingana namashumi amabili omuntu siqu, futhi ngokufanayo nesithombe esisodwa somphakeli waso silingana namashumi amabili esitolo somuntu.

16. Manje ukuze sibulale isitha, amadoda akithi kufanele avukwe ulaka; ukuze kube khona inzuzo ekunqobeni isitha, kufanele babe nemivuzo yabo.

17 Ngakho-ke ekulweni kwezinqola, lapho sekuthathwe izinqola eziyishumi noma ngaphezulu, kufanele banikezwe umvuzo ohamba phambili. Amafulegi ethu kufanele afakwe esikhundleni salawo esitha, nezinqola zempi zixutshwe futhi zisetshenziswe ngokuhlanganyela nawethu. Amasosha athunjiwe kufanele aphathwe ngomusa futhi agcinwe.

18. Lokhu kubizwa, ukusebenzisa isitha esinqotshiwe ukukhulisa amandla omuntu.

19 Ngakho-ke, empini, into yenu enkulu mayibe ukunqoba, kungabi imikhankaso emide.

20. Ngakho-ke kuzokwaziwa ukuthi umholi wamabutho nguyena ongumxazululi wekusasa labantu, umuntu okuncike kuye ukuthi isizwe siyohlala ngokuthula noma sisengozini.

III. Ukuhlaselwa yi-Stratagem

1. U-Sun Tzu uthe: Kubuciko bempi obungokoqobo, into engcono kunazo zonke ukuthatha izwe lesitha liphelele futhi liphelele; ukuyibhidliza nokuyichitha akukuhle kangako. Ngakho-ke, futhi, kungcono ukuphinda uthumbe ibutho liphelele kunokulibhubhisa, ukubamba ibutho, ithimba noma inkampani yonke kunokulibhubhisa.

2. Ngakho-ke ukulwa nokunqoba kuzo zonke izimpi zakho akuwona umsebenzi ophakeme kakhulu; ubuhle obuphakeme buqukethe ukwephula ukumelana nesitha ngaphandle kokulwa.

3. Ngakho-ke uhlobo oluphakeme kakhulu lobujenene wukugoba amacebo esitha; okulandelayo okungcono kakhulu ukuvimbela ukuhlangana kwamabutho esitha; okulandelayo ukuze uhlasele ibutho lesitha ensimini; futhi umgomo omubi kunayo yonke owokuvimbezela amadolobha abiyelwe ngezindonga.

4. Umthetho uwukuthi, hhayi ukuvimbezela amadolobha abiyelwe uma kungenzeka kugwenywe. Ukulungiswa kwezigqoko, izindawo zokukhosela ezihambayo, kanye nezikhali ezihlukahlukene zempi, kuzothatha izinyanga ezintathu eziphelele; futhi ukunqwabelanisa izindunduma phezu kwezindonga kuzothatha izinyanga ezintathu ngaphezulu.

5. Ujenene, engakwazi ukulawula ukucasuka kwakhe, uzohlasela amadoda akhe njengezintuthwane eziswelayo, kube nomphumela wokuthi ingxenye eyodwa kwezintathu yamadoda akhe ibulawe, kuyilapho idolobha lisalokhu lingathathwa. Injalo imiphumela eyinhlekelele yokuvinjezelwa.

6 Ngakho-ke umholi ohlakaniphile uthobisa amabutho esitha ngaphandle kokulwa; uthumba imizi yabo ngaphandle kokuyivimbezela; uchitha umbuso wabo ngaphandle kokusebenza isikhathi eside ensimini.

7. Ngamabutho akhe eqinile uzophikisana nokuphatha koMbuso, futhi ngaleyo ndlela, ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa umuntu, ukunqoba kwakhe kuyobe kuphelele. Lena indlela yokuhlasela nge-stratagem.

8. Kungumthetho empini, uma amabutho ethu eyishumi kwelinye lesitha, asihaqe; uma eyisihlanu koyedwa, mhlasele; uma kuphindwe kabili, ukuhlukanisa impi yethu kabili.
9. Uma kufaniswe ngokulinganayo, singanikela ngempi; uma sincane ngezinombolo, singasigwema isitha; uma singalingani ngazo zonke izindlela, singambalekela.

10. Ngakho-ke, nakuba ukulwa okunenkani kungase kwenziwe yibutho elincane, ekugcineni kufanele kuthunjwe amandla amakhulu.

11. Manje ujenene useyisivikelo soMbuso; uma i-bulwark iphelele kuzo zonke izindawo; uMbuso uyoba namandla; uma udonga lunephutha, uMbuso uzoba buthakathaka.

12 Kunezindlela ezintathu umbusi angaletha ngazo amashwa phezu kwebutho lakhe:--

13. (1) Ngokuyala ibutho ukuba liqhubeke noma lihlehle, lingenalwazi ngeqiniso lokuthi alikwazi ukulalela. Lokhu kubizwa nge-hobbling the army.

14. (2) Ngokuzama ukubusa ibutho ngendlela efanayo naleyo ephethe ngayo umbuso, engazi lutho ngezimo ezitholakala ebuthweni. Lokhu kubangela ukungahlaliseki emiqondweni yesosha.

15. (3) Ngokuqasha izikhulu zebutho lakhe ngaphandle kokubandlulula, ngokungazi umthetho wezempi wokuzivumelanisa nezimo. Lokhu kuzamazamisa ukuzethemba kwamasosha.

16. Kodwa lapho ibutho lingahlaliseki futhi lingathembeki, ngokuqinisekile inkathazo izovela kwamanye amakhosana angama-feudal. Lokhu kumane kuletha isiphithiphithi ebuthweni, futhi kulahlekise ukunqoba.

17. Ngakho singase sazi ukuthi kunezimfuneko ezinhlanu ezibalulekile zokunqoba: (1) Uyonqoba owaziyo ukuthi kufanele alwe nini nokuthi angalwi nini. (2) Uzowina owazi ukuthi angabhekana kanjani namandla aphakeme naphansi. (3) Uyonqoba ibutho lakhe eliqhutshwa umoya ofanayo kuzo zonke izigaba zawo. (4) Uyonqoba owathi, wazilungiselela, walinda ukuthatha isitha engakulungele. (5) Uzowina lowo onamandla ezempi futhi ongaphazanyiswa yinkosi.

18. Ngakho-ke isisho esithi: Uma uzazi isitha futhi uzazi wena, akufanele wesabe umphumela wezimpi eziyikhulu. Uma uzazi wena kodwa ungazi isitha, ngakho konke ukunqoba okuzuziwe nawe uzokwehlulwa. Uma ungazazi isitha noma wena ngokwakho, uzonqotshwa kuzo zonke izimpi.

IV. Izindlela Zokuziphatha

1. USun Tzu uthe: Abalwi abahle bakudala baqale bazibeke phezu kwamathuba okuhlulwa, base belinda ithuba lokunqoba isitha.

2. Ukuzivikela ekunqotshweni kusezandleni zethu, kodwa ithuba lokunqoba isitha linikezwa yisitha uqobo.

3. Kanjalo umlwi omuhle uyakwazi ukuzivikela ekunqotshweni, kodwa akakwazi ukuqiniseka ngokunqoba isitha.

4. Ngakho-ke isisho esithi: Umuntu angase azi ukuthi anganqoba kanjani engakwazi ukukwenza.

5. Ukuvikeleka ekunqotshweni kusho amaqhinga okuzivikela; ikhono lokunqoba isitha lisho ukuthatha okucasulayo.

6. Ukuma endaweni yokuzivikela kukhombisa ukuntuleka kwamandla; ukuhlasela, ukuchichima kwamandla.

7. Ujenene onekhono kwezokuzivikela ucasha endaweni eyimfihlo kakhulu yomhlaba; ohlakaniphileyo ekuhlaseleni uyanyazima esezulwini phezulu. Kanjalo ngakolunye uhlangothi sinamandla okuzivikela; ngakolunye, ukunqoba okuphelele.

8. Ukubona ukunqoba kuphela lapho kungaphakathi kwe-ken yomhlambi ovamile akuyona i-acme yobuhle.

9. Akuyona i-acme yobuhle uma ulwa futhi unqoba futhi uMbuso wonke uthi, "Wenze kahle!"

10. Ukuphakamisa izinwele zasekwindla akulona uphawu lwamandla amakhulu; ukubona ilanga nenyanga akulona uphawu lokubona okubukhali; ukuzwa umsindo wokuduma akuwona uphawu lwendlebe esheshayo.

11. Okwakudala ababekubiza nge-clever fighter yilowo onganqobi kuphela, kodwa ogqama ngokunqoba kalula.

12. Ngakho ukunqoba kwakhe akumlethisi idumela lokuhlakanipha noma udumo ngesibindi.

13. Unqoba izimpi zakhe ngokungalenzi iphutha. Ukungenzi amaphutha yikho okwakha isiqiniseko sokunqoba, ngoba kusho ukunqoba isitha esesinqotshiwe.

14. Ngakho-ke umlwi onekhono uzibeka endaweni eyenza ukuthi kungenzeki ukwehlulwa, futhi angaphuthelwa isikhathi sokunqoba isitha.

15. Kanjalo-ke ukuthi empini usomaqhinga onqobile ufuna impi kuphela uma esenqobile, kanti lowo omiselwe ukunqoba izimpi kuqala bese kuthi kamuva abheke ukunqoba.

16. Umholi ophelele uhlakulela umthetho wokuziphatha, futhi ubambelela ngokuqinile endleleni nesiyalo; kanjalo kusemandleni akhe ukulawula impumelelo.

17. Maqondana nendlela yezempi, okokuqala, sineMeasurement; okwesibili, Isilinganiso senani; okwesithathu, Ukubala; okwesine, Ukulinganisa amathuba; okwesihlanu, uMnqobi.
18. Ukulinganisa kuba khona ngenxa yoMhlaba; Isilinganiso senani lokukala; Ukubalwa kuya Ekulinganisweni kobuningi; Ukulinganisa amathuba ekubalweni; kanye Nokunqoba ukuze Balanceing of amathuba.

19. Ibutho elinqobayo elimelene nohluliwe, linjengephawundi elibekwe esikalini ngokumelene nohlamvu olulodwa.

20. Ukutheleka kwebutho elinqobayo kufana nokuqhunyiswa kwamanzi acijile abe umgodi ojulile wamafathome ayinkulungwane.

V. Amandla

1. U-Sun Tzu uthe: Ukulawulwa kwamandla amakhulu kuyisimiso esifanayo sokulawula amadoda ambalwa: kumane kuwumbuzo wokuhlukanisa izinombolo zabo.

2. Ukulwa nebutho elikhulu ngaphansi komyalo wakho akusafani neze nokulwa nencane: kumane kuwumbuzo wokufaka izimpawu nezimpawu.

3. Ukuqinisekisa ukuthi wonke umninikhaya wakho angase amelane nobunzima bokuhlasela kwesitha futhi ahlale enganyakaziswa-- lokhu kwenziwa ukuqondisa okuqondile nokungaqondile.

4. Ukuze umthelela webutho lakho ube njengetshe lokugaya eliphonswe eqandeni-lokhu kwenziwa isayensi yamaphuzu abuthakathaka futhi aqinile.

5. Kukho konke ukulwa, indlela eqondile ingase isetshenziselwe ukujoyina impi, kodwa izindlela ezingaqondile zizodingeka ukuze uthole ukunqoba.

6. Amaqhinga angaqondile, asetshenziswa kahle, awapheli njengeZulu noMhlaba, awapheli njengokugeleza kwemifula nemifudlana; njengelanga nenyanga, ziyaphela kodwa ziqale kabusha; njengezinkathi ezine zonyaka, ziyadlula ukuze zibuye futhi.

7. Awekho amanothi omculo angaphezu kwamahlanu, nokho inhlanganisela yalezi ezinhlanu zenza kube nezingoma eziningi kunanoma yini engake izwakale.

8. Ayikho imibala eyinhloko engaphezu kwemihlanu (oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, ophuzi, obomvu, omhlophe, nomnyama), kodwa uma ihlangene ikhiqiza imibala eminingi kunaleyo engake yabonwa.

9. Akukho ukunambitheka kwekhadinali engaphezu kwezinhlanu (okumuncu, okumuncu, usawoti, okumnandi, okubabayo), nokho inhlanganisela yakho ikhiqiza ukunambitheka okungaphezu kwalokho okungake kunambitha.

10. Empini, azikho izindlela ezingaphezu kwezimbili zokuhlasela—eqondile nengaqondile; nokho lezi ezimbili zihlangene zenza kube nochungechunge olungapheli lokuqondisa.

11. Okuqondile nokungaqondile kuholela komunye nomunye ngokushintshana. Kufana nokuhamba endingilizini--awufiki esiphethweni. Ubani ongaqeda amathuba enhlanganisela yabo?

12. Ukuqala kwamabutho kufana nokugijima kwesifufula esiginqa namatshe endleleni yawo.

13. Izinga lesinqumo lifana nokuzulazula okunesikhathi kahle kokheshe okwenza ukuba lishaye libulale isisulu salo.

14 Ngakho-ke isilwi esihle siyoba sibi ekuqaleni kwayo, futhi masinyane esinqumweni saso.

15. Amandla angafaniswa nokugoba umnsalo; isinqumo, ekukhululweni kwe-trigger.

16. Phakathi kwezinxushunxushu nesiyaluyalu sempi, kungase kube khona ukuphazamiseka kodwa nokho kungabi bikho nhlobo isiphithiphithi sangempela; phakathi nokudideka nezinxushunxushu, uhlu lwakho lungase lungabi nakhanda noma umsila, nokho luyoba ubufakazi bokunqotshwa.

17. Ukuphazamiseka okulingiswayo kuveza isiyalo esiphelele, ukwesaba okulingiswayo kubangela isibindi; ubuthakathaka obulingisiwe bubeka amandla.

18. Ukufihla ukuhleleka ngaphansi kwesigubuzelo sesiyaluyalu kumane kuwumbuzo wokuhlukanisa iziqephu; ukufihla isibindi ngaphansi kombukiso wokwesaba kuphakamisa isikhwama samandla acashile; ukufihla amandla ngobuthakathaka kufanele kwenziwe ngezindlela zamaqhinga.

19. Kanjalo lowo onekhono lokugcina isitha sihamba ugcina ukubonakala okukhohlisayo, okuyokwenza isitha ngokwaso. Unikela ngokuthile, ukuze isitha sikuhlwithe.

20. Ngokubamba izithiyo, uyamgcina ohambeni; bese emqamekela enesixuku sabakhethiweyo.

21. Umlwi ohlakaniphile ubheka umphumela wamandla ahlangene, futhi akadingi okuningi kakhulu kubantu ngabanye. Ngakho ikhono lakhe lokukhetha amadoda afanele nokusebenzisa amandla ahlangene.

22. Uma esebenzisa amandla ahlangene, izimpi zakhe zifana nezingodo eziginqikayo noma amatshe. Ngoba kuyimvelo yogodo noma itshe ukuthi lihlale linganyakazi endaweni eqondile, futhi linyakaze lapho lisendaweni eyehlelayo; uma inamakona amane, ukuma, kodwa uma kuyindilinga, ukugingqika phansi.

23. Kanjalo amandla athuthukiswa amadoda amahle okulwa anjengomfutho wetshe eliyindilinga elehliselwa entabeni izinkulungwane zamamitha ukuphakama. Kakhulu ngendaba yamandla.

VI. Amaphuzu Abuthakathaka Futhi Aqinile

1. USun Tzu uthe: Noma ubani ozoba ngowokuqala ensimini futhi alindele ukufika kwesitha, uzobe esanda kulwa; owesibili ensimini futhi okufanele aphuthume empini uyofika ekhathele.
2. Ngakho-ke umlwi ohlakaniphile ubeka intando yakhe esitheni, kodwa akavumeli intando yesitha ukuba ibekwe kuye.

3. Ngokuyibekela izinzuzo, ingenza isitha sisondele ngokuthanda kwayo; noma, ngokudala umonakalo, angenza kungenzeki ngesitha ukuba sisondele.

4. Uma isitha sikhululeka, singamhlupha; uma enikezwa kahle ngokudla, angambulala; uma ekanise buthule, angamphoqa ukuba athuthe.

5. Zivele ezindaweni okumele isitha sisheshe sizivikele; imasha ngokushesha uye ezindaweni ongalindelekile kuzo.

6. Ibutho lingase lihambe amabanga amade ngaphandle kokucindezeleka, uma lidabula izwe lapho isitha singekho.

7. Ungaqiniseka ukuthi uzophumelela ekuhlaselweni kwakho uma uhlasela kuphela izindawo ezingavikelekile.Ungaqinisekisa ukuphepha kokuzivikela kwakho uma ubambe izikhundla ezingeke zihlaselwe.

8. Ngakho-ke lowo jenene unekhono ekuhlaseleni ophikisana naye engazi ukuthi kumele avikele ini; futhi unekhono ekuvikeleni isitha sakhe esingazi ukuthi sizohlasela ini.

9. O ubuciko bobunkulunkulu bobuqili kanye nemfihlo! Ngawe sifunda ukuba singabonakali, ngawe singezwakali; futhi ngalokho singabamba isiphetho sesitha ezandleni zethu.

10. Ungase uthuthuke futhi ungavimbeki nhlobo, uma wenza amaphuzu abuthakathaka esitha; ungase uthathe umhlalaphansi futhi uphephe ekuphishekeleni uma ukunyakaza kwakho kushesha ukwedlula okwesitha.

11. Uma sifisa ukulwa, isitha singaphoqwa ukuba sithembisene umshado nakuba sivikelwe ngemva kodonga oluphakeme nomgodi ojulile. Esikudingayo nje ukuhlasela kwenye indawo azophoqeleka ukuthi ayikhulule.

12. Uma singathandi ukulwa, singavimbela isitha ukuba singasihileli nakuba imigqa yekamu lethu ilandelwa phansi. Esikudingayo nje ukuphonsa into eyinqaba futhi engaphenduleki endleleni yakhe.

13. Ngokuthola izimo zesitha futhi sihlale singabonakali thina, singagcina amandla ethu egxile, kuyilapho izitha kufanele zihlukaniswe.

14. Singakha umzimba owodwa obumbene, kuyilapho isitha kufanele sihlukane sibe izingxenyana. Ngakho-ke kuzoba nenani elixabene nezingxenye ezihlukene zayo yonke, okusho ukuthi sizoba baningi kwabambalwa besitha.

15 Futhi uma sikwazi ukuhlasela kanjalo ibutho eliphansi nelinamandla, abamelene nathi bayoba sebunzimeni obukhulu.

16. Indawo lapho esihlose ukulwa khona akumele yaziwe; ngoba ngaleso sikhathi isitha kuyodingeka silungiselele ngokumelene nokuhlasela okungenzeka ngamaphuzu amaningana ahlukene; futhi amabutho akhe njengoba esakazwa ngezindlela eziningi, izinombolo okuzodingeka sibhekane nazo nganoma isiphi isikhathi ziyoba zimbalwa ngokwesilinganiso.

17. Ngokuba uma isitha siqinisa iveni laso, siyakwenza buthakathaka ingemuva laso; uma eqinisa ingemuva lakhe, uyokwenza buthaka iveni lakhe; uma eqinisa isandla sakhe sokhohlo, uyakwenza buthakathaka esokunene sakhe; uma eqinisa esokunene sakhe, uyakwenza buthakathaka esokhohlo sakhe. Uma ethumela ama-reinforcements yonke indawo, uyoba buthakathaka yonke indawo.

18. Ubuthakathaka bezinombolo buvela ekubeni uzilungiselele ngokumelene nokuhlaselwa okungenzeka; amandla ezinombolo, ekuphoqeni isitha sethu ukuthi senze la malungiselelo ngokumelene nathi.

19 Ukwazi indawo nesikhathi sempi ezayo, singase sigxilise ingqondo sisekude kakhulu ukuze silwe.

20. Kodwa uma isikhathi noma indawo ingaziwa, khona-ke uphiko lwesokunxele ngeke lukwazi ukusiza kwesokudla, ilungelo elingenawo amandla alinganayo okusiza kwesokunxele, iveni elingakwazi ukukhulula ingemuva, noma ingemuva ukusekela iveni. Kangakanani-ke uma izingxenye ezikude kakhulu zebutho zingangaphansi kwekhulu lama-LI ngokuhlukana, futhi ngisho neziseduze zihlukaniswa ngama-LI amaningana!

21 Nakuba ngokulinganisa kwami ​​amasosha kaYueh edlula awethu ngenani, lokho ngeke kuwasize ngalutho endabeni yokunqoba. Ngithi-ke ukunqoba kungazuzwa.

22. Noma isitha sinamandla ngamanani, singase sisivimbele ekulweni. Isu ukuze uthole izinhlelo zakhe kanye namathuba empumelelo yazo.

23. Mvuse, futhi ufunde isimiso somsebenzi wakhe noma ukungasebenzi. Mphoqe ukuthi aziveze, ukuze athole izindawo zakhe ezisengozini.

24. Qhathanisa kahle ibutho eliphikisayo nelakho, ukuze wazi lapho amandla eningi khona nalapho entula khona.

25. Ekwenzeni izimo zamaqhinga, iphimbo eliphakeme kakhulu ongalifinyelela ukulifihla; zifihle izimo zakho, futhi uzophepha ekuthunjweni yizinhloli ezicashile, emaqhingeni ongqondongqondo abahlakaniphe kakhulu.

26. Ukunqoba kungase kukhiqizwe kanjani kubo ngamasu ezitha—lokho isixuku esingenakukuqonda.
27. Bonke abantu bayawabona amaqhinga enginqoba ngawo, kepha okungekho ongakubona yisu okuphuma kulo ukunqoba.

28. Ungawaphindi amaqhinga akuzuzele ukunqoba okukodwa, kodwa vumela izindlela zakho zilawulwe yizinhlobonhlobo ezingapheli zezimo.

29. Amaqhinga ezempi anjengamanzi; ngoba amanzi ekuhambeni kwawo kwemvelo agijima esuka ezindaweni eziphakeme futhi aphuthume ehle.

30. Ngakho-ke empini, indlela iwukugwema okunamandla nokugadla kokubuthakathaka.

31. Amanzi akha indlela yawo ngokwesimo somhlabathi ageleza phezu kwawo; isosha lisebenzela ukunqoba kwalo ngokuphathelene nesitha elibhekene nalo.

32. Ngakho-ke, njengoba nje amanzi engagcini ukuma njalo, kanjalo nasempini azikho izimo ezihlala njalo.

33. Ongakwazi ukuguqula amaqhinga akhe maqondana nembangi yakhe futhi ngaleyo ndlela aphumelele ekunqobeni, angabizwa ngokuthi ukapteni ozelwe ezulwini.

34. Izakhi ezinhlanu (amanzi, umlilo, izinkuni, insimbi, umhlaba) azihlali zihamba phambili ngokulinganayo; izinkathi ezine zonyaka zenza omunye nomunye ngokulandelana. Kukhona izinsuku ezimfushane nezinde; inyanga inezikhathi zayo zokufiphala nokukhula.

VII. Ukuqondisa

1. USun Tzu wathi: Empini, ujenene uthola imiyalo yakhe enkosini.

2. Ngemva kokuqoqa ibutho futhi agxilise amabutho akhe, kufanele ahlanganise futhi avumelanise izici ezihlukene ngaphambi kokumisa ikamu lakhe.

3. Ngemuva kwalokho, kuza ukuqondisa kwamasu, okungekho lutho olunzima kakhulu. Ubunzima bokuqondisa ngesu buhlanganisa ukuguqula okhohlisayo abe oqondile, kanye neshwa libe yinzuzo.

4. Ngakho-ke, ukuthatha umzila omude futhi ojikelezayo, ngemva kokuyenga isitha endleleni, futhi nakuba eqala ngemva kwakhe, ukuklama ukufinyelela umgomo ophambi kwakhe, kubonisa ulwazi lwe-artifice yokuchezuka.

5. Ukuzulazula nebutho kunenzuzo; nesixuku esingaqondile, esiyingozi kakhulu.

6. Uma usetha ibutho elihlome ngokugcwele ngoMashi ukuze uhlwithe inzuzo, maningi amathuba okuthi uzobe sekwephuze kakhulu. Ngakolunye uhlangothi, ukukhipha ikholomu endizayo ngenjongo kuhilela ukunikela ngemithwalo yayo nezitolo.

7. Ngakho, uma niyala amadoda enu ukuba asonge amajazi awo, futhi enze imashi ephoqelelwe ngaphandle kokumisa imini nobusuku, ehlanganisa ibanga eliphindwe kabili elivamile endaweni enwetshiwe, enze ama-LI ayikhulu ukuze aphuce inzuzo, abaholi bazo zonke izigaba zenu zontathu bayowela ezandleni zesitha.

8. Amadoda anamandla ayakuba ngaphambili, ama-jaded asale ngemuva, futhi kulolu hlelo ingxenye eyodwa kweshumi kuphela yebutho lakho iyofinyelela lapho iya khona.

9. Uma umasha ama-LI angamashumi amahlanu ukuze unqobe isitha, uzolahlekelwa umholi wesigaba sakho sokuqala, futhi ingxenye yamandla akho kuphela ezofinyelela umgomo.

10. Uma umasha ama-LI angamashumi amathathu ngento efanayo, izingxenye ezimbili kwezintathu zebutho lakho zizofika.

11. Singakuthatha-ke ukuthi ibutho elingenalo isitimela sempahla lilahlekile; ngaphandle kwamalungiselelo liyalahleka; ngaphandle kwezisekelo zokuhlinzeka liyalahleka.

12. Asikwazi ukungena emifelandawonye kuze kube yilapho sesijwayelene nemiklamo yomakhelwane bethu.

13. Asifanele ukuhola ibutho uma sihamba ngaphandle uma sijwayelene nobuso bezwe—izintaba namahlathi alo, izihibe nezinguzunga zalo, amaxhaphozi namaxhaphozi alo.

14. Ngeke sikwazi ukuguqulela okuhle kwemvelo ku-akhawunti ngaphandle kokuthi sisebenzisa iziqondiso zendawo.

15. Empini zijwayeze ukuzenzisa, uzophumelela.

16. Ukuthi uzogxila noma uhlukanise amasosha akho, kumele kunqunywe ngokwezimo.

17. Ukushesha kwenu makube okomoya, ukubumbana kwenu okwehlathi.

18. Ekuhlaseleni nasekuphangeni makube njengomlilo, akunakunyakaziswa njengentaba.

19. Amacebo akho mawabe mnyama futhi angangeneki njengobusuku, futhi lapho uhamba, awe njengonyazi lwezulu.

20 Lapho niphanga izwe, impango mayihlukaniselwe abantu benu; lapho uthatha indawo entsha, yihlukanise ibe izabelo ukuze kuzuze amasosha.

21. Zindla futhi wenze ngamabomu ngaphambi kokwenza umnyakazo.

22. Uzonqoba ofunde ubuciko bokuchezuka. Bunjalo ubuciko bokuqondisa.

23. Incwadi Yokuphatha Amasosha ithi: Enkundleni yempi, izwi elikhulunywayo alihambi ngokwanele: ngakho-ke ukumiswa kwezingubhu nezigubhu. Futhi izinto ezijwayelekile azikwazi ukubonakala ngokucacile ngokwanele: yingakho isikhungo samabhanela namafulegi.

24. Izinsimbi nezigubhu, amabhanela kanye namafulege, izindlela lapho izindlebe namehlo omethuleli angase agxile ephuzwini elithile.
25. Ngaleyo ndlela umninikhaya akha umgwamanda owodwa obumbene, akunakwenzeka yini ukuthi isibindi siqhubeke sisodwa, noma amagwala ahlehle yedwa. Lona ubuciko bokuphatha izixuku ezinkulu zamadoda.

26. Ekulweni ebusuku, sebenzisani kakhulu imililo nezigubhu, nasekulweni emini, amafulege namabhanela, njengendlela yokuthonya izindlebe namehlo ebutho lenu.

27. Ibutho lonke lingase liphucwe umoya walo; umkhuzi wamabutho angase aphucwe ukuba khona kwakhe kwengqondo.

28 Manje umoya wesosha uyavutha ekuseni; emini isiqalile ukuhlaba umkhosi; futhi kusihlwa, ingqondo yakhe igxile kuphela ekubuyeleni ekamu.

29. Ngakho-ke ujenene ohlakaniphile uyalivika ibutho lapho umoya walo ubukhali, kodwa uyalihlasela uma livilapha futhi selizimisele ukubuya. Lobu ubuciko bokufunda imizwa.

30. Ukuzikhuza nokuzola, ukulinda ukubonakala kwesiphithiphithi nokuxokozela phakathi kwezitha:--lobu ubuciko bokugcina ubuwena.

31. Ukuba seduze negoli isitha sisekude nalo, ukulinda ngokukhululeka lapho isitha sikhandleka sishikashikeka, ukusuthiswa kuyilapho isitha silambile:--lobu ubuciko bokuphatha amandla omuntu.

32. Ukugwema ukuvimba isitha esinamabhanela aso ahleleke kahle, ukugwema ukuhlasela ibutho elibunjwe ngohlelo oluzolile nokuzethembayo:--lobu ubuciko bokufunda izimo.

33. Kuwumgomo wezempi ukuthi ungakhuphukeli phezulu ngokumelene nesitha, noma ukuphikisana naso lapho sehla.

34. Ungajahi isitha esilingisa ukundiza; ungawahlaseli amasosha athukuthele.

35. Ungagwinyi udobo olunikezwa yisitha. Ungaphazamisi ibutho elibuyela ekhaya.

36. Uma uzungeza ibutho, shiya indawo yokuphuma ikhululekile. Ungacindezeli kakhulu isitha esiphelelwe yithemba.

37 Bunjalo ubuciko bempi.

VIII. Ukwehluka Kwamaqhinga

1. USun Tzu uthe: Empini, ujenene uthola imiyalo yakhe evela enkosini, aqoqe ibutho lakhe futhi agxilise amabutho akhe.

2. Uma usezweni elinzima, ungakanisi. Ezweni lapho imigwaqo ephakeme ihlangana khona, hlanganisa izandla nabalingani bakho. Ungahlali ezindaweni ezingazodwa eziyingozi. Ezimweni ezimbi kakhulu, kufanele usebenzise i-stratagem. Esimeni esiphelelwe ithemba, kufanele ulwe.

3. Kukhona imigwaqo okungamele ilandelwe, amabutho okungamele ahlaselwe, amadolobha okumele avinjezelwe, izikhundla okungamele zibangwe, imiyalelo yenkosi okungafanele ilandelwe.

4. Ujenene oziqonda kahle izinzuzo ezihambisana nokuhlukahluka kwamaqhinga uyazi ukuthi angawaphatha kanjani amabutho akhe.

5. Ujenene ongaziqondi lezi, angase akwazi kahle ukumiswa kwezwe, nokho ngeke akwazi ukuguqula ulwazi lwakhe ku-akhawunti engokoqobo.

6. Ngakho-ke, umfundi wezempi ongawazi kahle ubuciko bempi bokuhlukahluka kwezinhlelo zakhe, nakuba ejwayele Izinzuzo Eziyisihlanu, uzohluleka ukusebenzisa kahle amadoda akhe.

7. Ngakho ezinhlelweni zomholi ohlakaniphile, ukucatshangelwa kwenzuzo nokubi kuyohlanganiswa ndawonye.

8 Uma ukulindela kwethu inzuzo kulinganiselwe ngale ndlela, singase siphumelele ekufezeni ingxenye ebalulekile yamacebo ethu.

9. Uma, ngakolunye uhlangothi, phakathi kobunzima sihlala sikulungele ukuthatha inzuzo, singase sizikhiphe ebubini.

10. Nciphisa amakhosi anobutha ngokuwalimaza; futhi ubakhathaze, futhi ubagcine bethembisene njalo; ubambe izinto eziyengayo, futhi uzenze zigijimele kunoma iyiphi indawo.

11. Ubuciko bempi busifundisa ukuba singanciki emathubeni okuthi isitha singafiki, kodwa ekulungiseleleni kwethu ukusemukela; hhayi ngenxa yokuthi akazange ahlasele, kodwa ngenxa yokuthi senze isikhundla sethu singenakuphikiswa.

12 Kunamaphutha amahlanu ayingozi angase athinte ujenene: (1) Ubudedengu, obuholela ekubhujisweni; (2) ubugwala, obuholela ekuthunjweni; (3) ukuthukuthela ngamawala, okungase kucasulwe ukuthuka; (4) ubumnandi bokuhlonipha obuzwela amahloni; (5) ukucela ngokweqile amadoda akhe, okumdalula ekukhathazekeni nasezinkingeni.

13. Lezi yizono ezinhlanu ezikhungathekisayo zikajenene, ezilimaza ukuziphatha kwempi.

14. Uma ibutho liketulwa nomholi walo ebulawa, imbangela iyotholakala ngempela kulawa maphutha amahlanu ayingozi. Makube yindaba yokuzindla.

IX. Ibutho ngoMashi

1. USun Tzu uthe: Sesiza manje embuzweni wokukanisa amabutho, nokubona izimpawu zesitha. Dlulani masinyane izintaba, nihlale ezindaweni ezingomakhelwane.
2. Kanisa ezindaweni eziphakemeyo, ubheke elangeni. Ungakhuphukeli phezulu ukuze ulwe. Kakhulu ngempi yezintaba.

3. Ngemva kokuwela umfula, kufanele uqhelelane nawo.

4. Lapho ibutho elihlaselayo liwela umfula ekuhambeni kwawo ukuya phambili, ungaqhubeki uhlangane nawo phakathi nomfudlana. Kuyoba kuhle kakhulu ukuvumela ingxenye yebutho iwele, bese uletha ukuhlasela kwakho.

5. Uma ulangazelele ukulwa, akumele uyohlangana nomhlaseli eduze nomfula okufanele awuwele.

6. Qondisa umsebenzi wakho wezandla phezulu kunesitha, futhi ubheke elangeni. Ungasuki phezulu ukuze uhlangane nesitha. Kakhulu ngempi yasemfuleni.

7. Lapho uwela amaxhaphozi anosawoti, okuwukuphela kwento okukhathazayo kufanele kube ukudlula kuwo ngokushesha, ngaphandle kokulibala.

8. Uma uphoqeleka ukuba ulwe endaweni enosawoti, kufanele ube namanzi notshani eduze nawe, futhi ubuyisele umhlane wakho esigangeni sezihlahla. Kakhulu ekusebenzeni kumamashi kasawoti.

9. Ezweni elomile nelisezingeni, thatha indawo efinyeleleka kalula enendawo ekhuphukayo kwesokudla sakho nangemuva kwakho, ukuze ingozi ibe ngaphambili, futhi ukuphepha kulele ngemuva. Kakhulu ngokukhankasa ezweni eliyisicaba.

10. Lawa amagatsha amane awusizo olwazi lwezempi avumela uMbusi Ophuzi ukuba anqobe amakhosi amane amaningana.

11. Wonke amabutho akhetha indawo ephakeme kunezindawo eziphansi nezinelanga kumnyama.

12. Uma nibaqaphela abantu benu, nikanise emhlabathini olukhuni, ibutho liyakuba lingenasifo kuzo zonke izinhlobo, futhi lokhu kuyakuba ngukunqoba.

13. Uma ufika egqumeni noma ebhange, thatha uhlangothi olunelanga, nomthambeka ongemuva kwesokudla. Ngakho-ke uzothatha isinyathelo ngokushesha ukuze kuzuze amasosha akho futhi usebenzise izinzuzo zemvelo zomhlabathi.

14. Uma, ngenxa yezimvula ezinkulu ezweni, umfula ofisa ukuwuwela ukhukhumala ukhihlize amagwebu, kufanele ulinde uze uyeke.

15. Izwe lapho kunamawa aphakeme anezifufula ezigeleza phakathi, imigodi yemvelo ejulile, izindawo ezivalekile, amahlozi aphithene, amadwala namachweba, kufanele ashiywe nayo yonke isivinini esingaba khona futhi angasondeli kuso.

16. Nakuba sisuka kude nezindawo ezinjalo, kufanele senze isitha sisondele kuzo; ngenkathi sibhekene nabo, kufanele sivumele isitha sibe nabo ngemuva.

17. Uma endaweni yekamu lakho kukhona noma yiliphi izwe elinamagquma, amachibi azungezwe utshani basemanzini, nezindebe eziyimigodi ezigcwele umhlanga, noma izinkuni ezinotshani obuminyene, kumelwe zikhishwe ngokucophelela futhi ziseshwe; ngoba lezi yizindawo lapho abantu abaqamekeleyo noma izinhloli ezicashile kungenzeka zicashe khona.

18. Uma isitha siseduze futhi sithule, sithembele emandleni emvelo esikhundla saso.

19. Uma engazibandakanyi futhi ezama ukuvusa impi, usuke elangazelele ukuthi olunye uhlangothi luqhubekele phambili.

20. Uma indawo yakhe yokukanisa ifinyeleleka kalula, usuke ethenda isiyengo.

21. Ukunyakaza phakathi kwezihlahla zehlathi kubonisa ukuthi isitha siya phambili. Ukubonakala kwenani lezikrini phakathi kotshani obuminyene kusho ukuthi isitha sifuna ukusenza sisolise.

22. Ukukhuphuka kwezinyoni ekundizeni kwazo kuwuphawu lwe-ambuscade. Izilwane ezithukile zibonisa ukuthi ukuhlasela kungazelelwe kuyeza.

23. Lapho kukhuphuka uthuli ensikeni ephakeme, kuwuphawu lwezinqola zempi; lapho uthuli luphansi, kodwa lusakazeke endaweni ebanzi, lubonisa indlela yamasosha ahamba ngezinyawo. Uma ihluma ezinhlangothini ezahlukene, ikhombisa ukuthi izinhlangothi zithunywe ukuyotheza izinkuni. Amafu ambalwa othuli aya le nale akhombisa ukuthi ibutho likanisa.

24. Amazwi athobekile namalungiselelo andayo ayizimpawu zokuthi isitha sesizodlulela phambili. Ulimi olunobudlova nokushayela phambili sengathi uya kuhlasela kuyizimpawu zokuthi uzohlehla.

25. Uma izinqola ezilula ziphuma kuqala futhi zime phezu kwezimpiko, kuwuphawu lokuthi isitha senza impi.

26. Iziphakamiso zokuthula ezingahambisani nesivumelwano esifungelwe zikhomba itulo.

27. Uma kugijima kakhulu futhi amasosha awela ezikhundleni, kusho ukuthi isikhathi esibucayi sesifikile.

28. Uma abanye bebonakala bethuthuka abanye behlehla, kusuke kuwukuyenga.

29. Lapho amabutho emi encike ngemikhonto, aphelelwa amandla ngenxa yokuswela ukudla.

30. Uma abathunywe ukuyokha amanzi beqala ngokuphuza bona, ibutho lihlushwa ukoma.

31. Uma isitha sibona inzuzo esizoyithola futhi singenzi mzamo wokuyivikela, amasosha aphelile.

32. Uma izinyoni zibuthana endaweni ethile, akuhlali muntu; Ukukhala ebusuku kubangela ukwethuka.
33. Uma kukhona ukuphazamiseka ekamu, igunya likajenene libuthakathaka. Uma izibhengezo namafulegi kuhanjiswa, ukuvukela umbuso kuyaqhubeka. Uma amaphoyisa ethukuthele, kusho ukuthi amadoda akhathele.

34. Uma ibutho liphakela amahhashi alo ngamabele, lihlaba izinkomo zalo ukuba lidle, nalapho amadoda engawalengisanga amabhodwe awo emlilweni, ekhombisa ukuthi awayikubuyela ematendeni awo, niyazi ukuthi azimisele ukulwa kuze kube sekufeni.

35. Ukubona amadoda ehlebeza ndawonye ngamafindo amancane noma ekhuluma ngezwi eliphansi kukhomba ukungezwani phakathi kwezikhundla.

36. Imivuzo evame kakhulu ikhombisa ukuthi isitha sisekupheleni kwezinsiza zaso; izijeziso eziningi kakhulu zibonisa isimo sokucindezeleka okukhulu.

37. Ukuqala nge-bluster, kodwa kamuva ukwethuka izinombolo zesitha, kubonisa ukuntuleka okukhulu kokuhlakanipha.

38. Uma izithunywa zithunyelwa izincomo emilonyeni yazo, kuwuphawu lokuthi isitha sifisa isivumelwano.

39. Uma amabutho ezitha ekhuphuka ngolaka ahlale ebhekene nawethu isikhathi eside engazange ajoyine impi noma azikhiphe futhi, isimo yisona esidinga ukuqapha nokuqapha okukhulu.

40. Uma amabutho ethu engengaphezu kwesitha ngenani, lokho kwanele; kusho kuphela ukuthi akukho kuhlasela okuqondile okungenziwa. Esingakwenza nje ukugxilisa wonke amandla ethu atholakalayo, sibhekisise isitha, futhi sithole ukuqiniswa.

41. Ongacabangi kusengaphambili kodwa edelela abamelene naye nakanjani uzobanjwa yibo.

42. Uma amasosha ejeziswa ngaphambi kokuthi anamathele kuwe, awayikuzithoba; futhi, ngaphandle uma kuzithoba, khona-ke kuyoba yize. Uma, lapho amasosha esenamathele kuwe, izijeziso aziphoqelelwa, zizoqhubeka ngaphandle uma.

43 Ngakho-ke amasosha kufanele aphathwe ngokwesintu kuqala, kodwa agcinwe ngaphansi kokuqondisa kwensimbi. Lena indlela ethile eya ekunqobeni.

44. Uma ekuqeqesheni amasosha imiyalo ijwayele ukugcinwa, ibutho lizobe lizikhuze kahle; uma kungenjalo, ukulaya kwayo kuyoba kubi.

45. Uma ujenene ekhombisa ukuwethemba amadoda akhe kodwa elokhu ephikelela ukuthi imiyalo yakhe ilalelwe, inzuzo iyoba ngokuhlanganyela.

X. Indawo

1. USun Tzu wathi: Singase sihlukanise izinhlobo eziyisithupha zendawo, okungukuthi: (1) Indawo okufinyeleleka kuyo; (2) umhlabathi othandelayo; (3) umhlabathi wesikhashana; (4) amaphasi amancane; (5) izindawo eziphakeme eziphakeme; (6) izikhundla ezikude kakhulu nesitha.

2. Umhlaba ongadalulwa ngokukhululeka yizinhlangothi zombili ubizwa ngokuthi uyafinyeleleka.

3. Maqondana nomhlaba walolu hlobo, yiba phambi kwesitha lapho uhlala ezindaweni eziphakeme nezinelanga, futhi uqaphe ngokucophelela ulayini wakho wezimpahla. Khona-ke uzokwazi ukulwa ngokunenzuzo.

4. Indawo engashiywa kodwa okunzima ukuphinde ihlale kuyo ibizwa ngokuthi ukuthandela.

5. Kusukela esikhundleni salolu hlobo, uma isitha singazilungiselele, ungase ugijime futhi usinqobe. Kodwa uma isitha silungiselelwe ukuza kwakho, futhi wehluleka ukusinqoba, khona-ke, ukubuya kungenakwenzeka, kuzolandela inhlekelele.

6. Uma isikhundla siwukuthi akukho uhlangothi oluzozuza ngokwenza umnyakazo wokuqala, kubizwa ngokuthi i-temporizing ground.

7. Esimeni salolu hlobo, nakuba isitha kufanele sisinike isiyengo esikhangayo, kuyoba kuhle ukuthi singanyakazi, kodwa kunalokho sihlehle, ngaleyo ndlela sihehe isitha ngesikhathi saso; khona-ke, lapho ingxenye yebutho lakhe isiphumile, singase sikhulule ukuhlasela kwethu ngokunenzuzo.

8. Mayelana namaphasi amancane, uma ukwazi ukuwathatha kuqala, mawavinjwe kakhulu futhi alinde ukufika kwesitha.

9. Uma ibutho likuvimbele ekuthatheni ipasi, ungamlandeli uma iphasi liboshwe ngokugcwele, kodwa kuphela uma livalelwe kancane.

10. Mayelana nezindawo eziphakeme eziphakeme, uma uphambi kwesitha sakho, kufanele uhlale ezindaweni eziphakeme nezinelanga, futhi ulinde lapho ukuthi akhuphuke.

11. Uma isitha sizixabanisile ngaphambi kwakho, ungasilandeli, kodwa hlehla uzame ukusihunga.

12. Uma uhlezi kude nesitha, namandla amabutho womabili elingana, akulula ukuvusa impi, futhi ukulwa kuyokuphuca wena.

13. Lezi eziyisithupha ziyizimiso ezixhumene noMhlaba. Ujenene othole isikhundla esinomthwalo wemfanelo kufanele aqikelele ukuthi uyawafunda.
14. Manje ibutho libhekene nezinhlekelele eziyisithupha eziningana, ezingaveli ngenxa yezimbangela zemvelo, kodwa ngenxa yamaphutha ujenene onecala lawo. Yilezi: (1) Indiza; (2) ukungathobeki; (3) ukuwa; (4) incithakalo; (5) ukungahleleki; (6) umzila.

15. Eminye imibandela ilingana, uma ibutho elilodwa lijikijelwa kwelinye ngokuphindwe kashumi ubukhulu balo, umphumela uyoba ukundiza kweyokuqala.

16. Lapho amasosha avamile enamandla kakhulu futhi izikhulu zawo zibuthakathaka kakhulu, umphumela uba ukungalaleli. Lapho izikhulu zinamandla kakhulu futhi amasosha avamile ebuthaka kakhulu, umphumela uba ukuwa.

17. Lapho izikhulu eziphakeme zithukuthele futhi zingazithobi, futhi lapho zihlangana nesitha zilwa ngokwazo ngenxa yomuzwa wokucasuka, ngaphambi kokuba umkhuzi wamabutho asho ukuthi usesimweni sokulwa noma cha, umphumela uba incithakalo.

18. Lapho ujenene ebuthaka futhi engenagunya; lapho imiyalo yakhe ingacacile futhi ingacacile; lapho ingekho imisebenzi yokulungisa eyabelwe izikhulu namadoda, futhi izikhundla zakheka ngendlela engahlelekile, umphumela uba ukungahleleki ngokuphelele.

19. Lapho ujenene, engakwazi ukulinganisa amandla esitha, evumela amandla aphansi ukuba ahilele elikhulu, noma aphonse ibutho elibuthaka ngokumelene nonamandla, futhi edebesela ukubeka amasosha aqokiwe endaweni engaphambili, umphumela kumelwe ube umkhuba.

20. Lezi izindlela eziyisithupha zokwehlulwa, okumele kuqashelwe ngokucophelela ngujenene othole isikhundla esinomthwalo wemfanelo.

21. Ukwakheka kwezwe ngokwemvelo kungumngane ongcono wesosha; kodwa amandla okulinganisa isitha, okulawula amandla okunqoba, nokubala ngobuhlakani ubunzima, izingozi namabanga, ahlanganisa uvivinyo lukajenene omkhulu.

22. Ozaziyo lezi zinto, futhi ekulweni asebenzise ulwazi lwakhe, uzonqoba izimpi zakhe. Lowo ongazazi, noma azenze, nakanjani uzonqotshwa.

23. Uma ukulwa kuphumela ekunqobeni, niyakulwa, noma umbusi ekwalela lokho; uma ukulwa kungeke kuphumele ekunqobeni, akumelwe nilwe ngisho nangomyalelo wombusi.

24. Ujenene othuthukela phambili ngaphandle kokuhahela udumo ahlehle engesabi ihlazo, okuwukuphela komcabango wakhe owukuvikela izwe lakhe nokwenzela inkosi yakhe inkonzo enhle, uyitshe eliyigugu lombuso.

25. Bhekani amasosha enu njengabantwana benu, futhi ayakulandela ezigodini ezijulile; nibabheke njengamadodana enu abathandekayo, futhi bayokuma kini kuze kube sekufeni.

26 Nokho, uma uyenza, kodwa ningakwazi ukwenza igunya lakho lizwakale; abanenhliziyo enhle, kodwa abakwazi ukuphoqelela imiyalo yakho; futhi engakwazi, ngaphezu kwalokho, yokuqeda isiphithiphithi: khona-ke amasosha akho kufanele afaniswe nezingane ezoniwe; azinamsebenzi wanoma iyiphi injongo engokoqobo.

27. Uma sazi ukuthi amadoda ethu asesimweni sokuhlasela, kodwa engazi ukuthi isitha asivulekele ukuhlasela, sesihambe ibanga elide ekunqobeni.

28. Uma sazi ukuthi isitha sivulekele ukuhlasela, kodwa singanakile ukuthi amadoda ethu awekho esimweni sokuhlasela, sesihambe ibanga elide ekunqobeni.

29. Uma sazi ukuthi isitha sivulekele ukuhlasela, futhi sazi ukuthi amadoda ethu asesimweni sokuhlasela, kodwa singazi ukuthi isimo senhlabathi senza ukulwa kungenzeki, sisahamba ibanga elide ekunqobeni.

30. Ngakho-ke isosha elinolwazi, uma lihamba, alidideki; uma esephule ikamu, akalahlekelwa lutho.

31. Ngakho-ke isisho esithi: Uma uzazi isitha futhi uzazi wena, ukunqoba kwakho ngeke kube nokungabaza; uma ulazi iZulu futhi uwazi umhlaba, ungenza ukunqoba kwakho kuphelele.

XI. Izimo Eziyisishiyagalolunye

1. U-Sun Tzu wathi: Ubuciko bempi buqaphela izinhlobo eziyisishiyagalolunye zomhlaba: (1) Umhlabathi ohlakazekile; (2) indawo elula; (3) izingxabano; (4) indawo evulekile; (5) indawo yemigwaqo emikhulu ephambanayo; (6) isisekelo esingathi sína; (7) indawo enzima; (8) inhlabathi evalekile; (9) umhlaba ongenathemba.

2. Uma isikhulu silwa endaweni yaso, kusuke kuhlakazeka.

3. Lapho esengene endaweni enobutha, kodwa engekude kangako, kuba indawo elula.

4. Isisekelo esikhona esingenisa inzuzo enkulu kunoma yiluphi uhlangothi, kuwumbangela yombango.

5. Indawo lapho uhlangothi ngalunye lunenkululeko yokunyakaza iyindawo evulekile.

6. Inkundla eyenza ukhiye ezifundazweni ezintathu ezihlangene, ukuze lowo ohlezi kuyo kuqala abe nombuso omningi ngaphansi komyalelo wakhe, iyindawo yemigwaqo emikhulu ephambanayo.
7. Lapho ibutho selingene enhliziyweni yezwe elinobutha, lishiya inqwaba yemizi enezivikelo eziqinile ngemuva, liyisizinda esibucayi.

8. Amahlathi asezintabeni, imiqansa, amaxhaphozi namathafa—wonke amazwe okunzima ukuwadabula: le ndawo inzima.

9. Umhlabathi ofinyelelwa ngezihosha eziwumngcingo, futhi lapho esingahle siphume khona kuphela ngezindlela eziwuhlupho, ukuze inani elincane lesitha lanele ukuchoboza inqwaba yamadoda ethu: lokhu kubiyelwe emhlabathini.

10. Umhlabathi esingasindiswa kuwo kuphela ekubhujisweni ngokulwa ngaphandle kokulibala, wumhlaba ongenathemba.

11. Ngakho-ke, endaweni ehlakazekile, ningalwi. Ezweni elilula, ungami. Ngokuphikisana, ungahlaseli.

12. Endaweni evulekile, ungazami ukuvimba indlela yesitha. Emhlabathini wemigwaqo ephambanayo, hlanganisa izandla nabalingani bakho.

13 Buthani impango endaweni enzima. Emhlabathini onzima, qhubeka uhamba kancane.

14. Emhlabathini ovalekile, sebenzisa iqhinga. Emhlabathini ongenathemba, yilwa.

15. Labo ababebizwa ngokuthi abaholi basendulo abanekhono babekwazi ukuhlukanisa umphambili wesitha nengemuva; ukuvimbela ukubambisana phakathi kwezigaba zakhe ezinkulu nezincane; ukuvimbela amabutho amahle ekusindiseni ababi, izinduna ekubutheni amadoda azo.

16. Lapho amadoda esitha esehlangene, akwazi ukuwagcina esiphithiphithi.

17. Lapho kubasiza, baqhubekela phambili; lapho kungenjalo, bama banganyakazi.

18. Uma ngibuzwa ukuthi ungabhekana kanjani nebutho elikhulu lesitha ngokuhleleka okuhlelekile futhi ngasephuzwini lokumasha ukuya ekuhlaselweni, kufanele ngithi: "Qala ngokubamba into oyibamba kakhulu; khona-ke uzobe ekhululekile entandweni yakho."

19. Ukushesha kuwumgogodla wempi: sebenzisa ukungalungi kwesitha, yenza indlela yakho ngemizila engalindelekile, futhi uhlasele izindawo ezingagadiwe.

20. Okulandelayo yizimiso okufanele zigcinwe yibutho elihlaselayo: Lapho uqhubeka ungena ezweni, kuyoba mkhulu ubumbano lwamasosha akho, futhi ngaleyo ndlela abavikeli ngeke bakunqobe.

21. Dlulisa amehlo ezweni elivundile ukuze unikeze ibutho lakho ukudla.

22. Fundisisani kahle inhlalakahle yamadoda enu, futhi ningabathelisi ngokweqile. Gxilisa amandla akho futhi uqoqe amandla akho. Gcina ibutho lakho lihamba njalo, futhi uhlele amacebo angenakuqondakala.

23. Phonsa amasosha akho ezindaweni lapho kungekho khona ukubaleka, futhi bayokhetha ukufa kunokubaleka. Uma bezobhekana nokufa, akukho abangase bangakuzuzi. Izikhulu namadoda ngokufanayo bazokhipha amandla abo amakhulu.

24. Amasosha lapho ecindezelekile alahlekelwa umuzwa wokwesaba. Uma ingekho indawo yokuphephela, bayoma baqine. Uma besemazweni anobutha, bazokhombisa inkani. Uma lungekho usizo kuwo, bazolwa kanzima.

25. Ngakho-ke, ngaphandle kokulinda ukuqoqwa, amasosha azohlala esemgwaqweni; ngaphandle kokulinda ukucelwa, bayokwenza intando yakho; ngaphandle kwemingcele, bayothembeka; ngaphandle kokunikeza imiyalo, bangathenjwa.

26. Vimbela ukubhula, futhi uqede ukungabaza okuyizinkolelo-ze. Khona-ke, kuze kube yilapho kufika ukufa ngokwakho, akukho nhlekelele okumelwe yesatshwe.

27. Uma amasosha ethu engasindwa yimali, akungoba enokunengeka ingcebo; uma ukuphila kwabo kungekude ngokungafanele, akubangelwa ukuthi abakuthandi ukuphila isikhathi eside.

28. Ngosuku abayalwa ngalo impi, amasosha akho angakhala, abahlezi befeke izingubo zabo, nabalele phansi bededela izinyembezi ezihlathini zabo. Kodwa ake zilethwe kanye, futhi bazobonisa isibindi se-Chu noma i-Kuei.

29. Usomaqhinga onekhono angafaniswa neshuai-jan. Manje i-shuai-jan iyinyoka etholakala ezintabeni zaseChUng. Shaya ekhanda layo, futhi uyohlaselwa ngumsila wayo; shaya emsileni wayo, futhi uyoshaywa ngekhanda layo; shaya phakathi, futhi uzohlaselwa ikhanda nomsila kokubili.

30. Uma ngibuzwa ukuthi ibutho lingenziwa yini ukulingisa i-shuai-jan, kufanele ngiphendule, Yebo. Ngokuba amadoda kaWu namadoda kaYueh ayizitha; kodwa uma bewela umfula ngesikebhe esisodwa futhi babanjwe isiphepho, bazosizana njengoba nje isandla sokunxele sisiza kwesokunene.

31. Ngakho-ke akwanele ukuthembela ekubophweni kwamahhashi, nasekumbebeni amasondo ezinqola emhlabathini.

32. Umgomo wokuphatha ibutho uwukumisa izinga elilodwa lesibindi okufanele bonke bafinyelele kulo.
33. Indlela yokwenza okungcono kakhulu kokubili okunamandla nokubuthakathaka - lowo ngumbuzo ohilela ukusetshenziswa kahle komhlabathi.

34. Kanjalo ujenene ohlakaniphileyo uphatha ibutho lakhe njengokungathi uhola indoda eyodwa, ngokungazi, ngesandla.

35. Kuwumsebenzi kajenene ukuthi athule ngaleyo ndlela aqinisekise imfihlo; uqotho futhi ulungile, ngaleyo ndlela ugcine ukuhleleka.

36 Kumelwe akwazi ukufihla izikhulu zakhe namadoda ngemibiko engamanga nokuvela, kanjalo abagcine ekungazini okuphelele.

37. Ngokushintsha izinhlelo zakhe nokuguqula izinhlelo zakhe, ugcina isitha ngaphandle kolwazi oluqondile. Ngokususa ikamu lakhe nangokuthatha imizila ejikelezayo, uvimbela isitha ukuba singalindele injongo yakhe.

38. Ngesikhathi esibucayi, umholi webutho wenza njengomuntu okhuphukele phezulu bese ekhahlela iladi ngemuva kwakhe. Uyisa amadoda akhe endaweni enobutha ngaphambi kokuba abonise isandla sakhe.

39. Ushisa izikebhe zakhe, ahlephule izimbiza zakhe; njengomalusi eqhuba umhlambi wezimvu, uqhuba abantu bakhe ngapha nangapha, akazi lapho eya khona.

40. Ukuhlanganisa umninikhaya wakhe nokumfaka engozini:--lokhu kungase kubizwe ngokuthi ibhizinisi likajenene.

41. Izilinganiso ezihlukene ezifanele izinhlobo eziyisishiyagalolunye zomhlabathi; ukufaneleka kwamaqhinga anolaka noma okuzivikela; kanye nemithetho eyisisekelo yemvelo yomuntu: lezi yizinto okufanele nakanjani zifundwe.

42. Lapho uhlasela indawo enobutha, isimiso esivamile siwukuthi, ukungena ngokujulile kuletha ukubumbana; ukungena kodwa indlela emfushane kusho ukuhlakazeka.

43. Uma ushiya izwe lakini, futhi uthatha ibutho lakho unqamule indawo yangakubo, uzithola usendaweni ebucayi. Lapho kunezindlela zokuxhumana kuzo zozine izinhlangothi, umhlabathi ungomunye wemigwaqo emikhulu ephambanayo.

44. Uma ungena ujule ezweni, kuwumhlaba obucayi. Uma ungena ngendlela encane, ilula.

45. Lapho unezinqaba zesitha ngemuva kwakho, nezidlule eziwumngcingo phambi kwakho, kubiyelwe phansi. Lapho ingekho nhlobo indawo yokukhosela, kuba yindawo ephelelwe ithemba.

46 Ngakho-ke, endaweni ehlakazekile, ngizogqugquzela amadoda ami ngobunye benhloso. Ezweni elilula, ngingabona ukuthi kukhona ukuxhumana okuseduze phakathi kwazo zonke izingxenye zebutho lami.

47. Endabeni yokuxabana ngangiphuthuma ngemuva.

48. Endaweni evulekile, ngangihlala ngiqaphile izivikelo zami. Emigwaqweni emikhulu ephambanayo, ngangihlanganisa imifelandawonye yami.

49. Ngokujulile, ngizozama ukuqinisekisa ukusakazwa okuqhubekayo kwempahla. Ezweni elinzima, ngangilokhu ngiphusha emgwaqweni.

50. Emhlabathini ovalekile, ngangivimba noma iyiphi indlela yokuhlehla. Lapho ngiphelelwa ithemba, ngangimemezela emasosheni ami ukungabi nathemba kokusindisa ukuphila kwawo.

51. Ngoba kuyisimo sesosha ukumelana nenkani lapho lizungezwe, lilwe kanzima lapho lingakwazi ukuzisiza, nokulalela ngokushesha lapho liwele engozini.

52. Asikwazi ukungena ebudlelwaneni nezikhulu ezingomakhelwane size sijwayelane nemiklamo yazo. Asifanele ukuhola amabutho omashi ngaphandle uma sijwayelene nobuso bezwe—izintaba namahlathi alo, izihibe nezinguzunga zalo, amaxhaphozi namaxhaphozi alo. Ngeke sikwazi ukuphendula izinzuzo zemvelo ku-akhawunti ngaphandle kokuthi sisebenzisa imihlahlandlela yendawo.

53. Ukushaywa indiva noma yimuphi kule migomo emine noma emihlanu elandelayo akufanelekile inkosana ethanda impi.

54. Uma inkosana ethanda impi ihlasela umbuso onamandla, ubujenene bayo buzibonakalisa ekuvimbeleni ukuhlangana kwamabutho esitha. Ubesaba kakhulu abamelene naye, futhi abasekeli babo bavinjelwa ukuba bahlanganyele ngokumelene naye.

55. Ngakho-ke akazami ukuzihlanganisa nakho konke, futhi akakhuthazi amandla ezinye izifundazwe. Wenza eyakhe imiklamo eyimfihlo, egcina abamelene naye bemangele. Kanjalo uyakwazi ukuthumba imizi yabo futhi agumbuqele imibuso yabo.

56. Nikeza imiklomelo ngaphandle kokubheka umthetho, khipha ama-oda ngaphandle kokubheka izinhlelo zangaphambilini; futhi uzokwazi ukuphatha ibutho lonke njengokungathi unendoda eyodwa kuphela.

57. Bhekana namasosha akho ngesenzo uqobo; ungalokothi ubazise umklamo wakho. Lapho umbono ukhanya, uwulethe phambi kwamehlo abo; kodwa ungabatsheli lutho uma isimo simuncu.

58. Beka ibutho lakho engozini enkulu, futhi liyosinda; liphonse osizini olukhulu, futhi liyophuma ngokuphepha.

59. Ngoba impela lapho amandla ewele endleleni elimazayo akwazi ukushaya igalelo ukuze anqobe.
60. Impumelelo ezimpini itholakala ngokuzamukela ngokucophelela inhloso yesitha.

61. Ngokuqhubeka silenga ohlangothini lwesitha, sizophumelela ekugcineni ekubulaleni umkhuzi wamabutho.

62. Lokhu kubizwa ngokuthi ikhono lokufeza into ngobuqili nje.

63. Ngosuku othatha ngalo umyalo wakho, vala imingcele, uchithe izibalo ezisemthethweni, futhi umise ukudlula kwazo zonke izithunywa.

64. Yibani lukhuni endlini yomkhandlu, ukuze nilawule isimo.

65. Uma isitha sishiya umnyango sivuliwe kumele ningene niphuthume.

66. Gcina umphikisi wakho ngokubamba lokho akuthanda kakhulu, futhi ngobuqili uhlele isikhathi sokufika kwakhe phansi.

67. Hamba endleleni echazwe ngumthetho, futhi uzihlalise esitheni uze ukwazi ukulwa impi ewujuqu.

68. Ngakho-ke, ekuqaleni, bonisa ukukhohlakala kwentombi, kuze kube yilapho isitha sikuvulela ithuba; emva kwalokho ulingise ukushesha kukanogwaja ogijimayo, futhi kuzobe sekwephuze kakhulu ukuthi isitha simelane nawe.

XII. Ukuhlasela Ngomlilo

1. USun Tzu uthe: Zihlanu izindlela zokuhlasela ngomlilo. Okokuqala ukushisa amasosha ekamu labo; okwesibili ukushisa izitolo; eyesithathu eyokushisa izitimela zemithwalo; okwesine ukushisa izikhali kanye nomagazini; okwesihlanu ukujikijela umlilo phakathi kwezitha.

2. Ukuze senze ukuhlasela, kufanele sibe nezindlela ezitholakalayo. Impahla yokuvusa umlilo kufanele ihlale ilungile.

3. Kunesikhathi esifanele sokuhlasela ngomlilo, nezinsuku ezikhethekile zokuqala umlilo.

4. Isikhathi esifanele lapho isimo sezulu somile kakhulu; izinsuku ezikhethekile yilezo lapho inyanga isemilayezweni yeSieve, iWall, iPhiko noma iCross-bar; ngokuba lezi ezine ziyizinsuku zonke zomoya okhuphukayo.

5. Lapho ehlasela ngomlilo, umuntu kufanele alungele ukuhlangabezana nentuthuko emihlanu engenzeka:

6. (1) Lapho kuqubuka umlilo ngaphakathi ekamu lesitha, phendula ngokushesha ngokuhlasela okuvela ngaphandle.

7. (2) Uma kuqubuka umlilo, kodwa amasosha esitha athule, bide isikhathi sakho futhi ungahlaseli.

8. (3) Lapho amandla amalangabi esefinyelele ukuphakama kwawo, alandele ngokuhlasela, uma lokho kungenzeka; uma kungenjalo, hlala lapho ukhona.

9. (4) Uma kungenzeka ukuhlasela ngomlilo ovela ngaphandle, ungalindi ukuthi uqhume ngaphakathi, kodwa ulethe ukuhlasela kwakho ngesikhathi esifanele.

10. (5) Uma ubasa umlilo, yiba nokuwuvalela. Ungahlaseli kusuka ku-leeward.

11. Umoya ovuka emini uhlala isikhathi eside, kepha umoya wasebusuku uyashesha ukuvunguza.

12 Kuwo wonke amabutho, intuthuko emihlanu ehlobene nomlilo kufanele yaziwe, ukunyakaza kwezinkanyezi kubalwe, futhi iwashi ligcinelwe izinsuku ezifanele.

13. Ngakho-ke labo abasebenzisa umlilo njengosizo ekuhlaseleni babonisa ukuhlakanipha; labo abasebenzisa amanzi njengosizo ekuhlaseleni bathola amandla.

14 Ngamanzi, isitha singavinjwa, kodwa singaphucwa yonke impahla yaso.

15. Ayijabulisi isiphetho salowo ozama ukunqoba izimpi zakhe futhi aphumelele ekuhlaseleni kwakhe ngaphandle kokuhlakulela umoya webhizinisi; ngoba umphumela uwukumosha isikhathi nokuma okuvamile.

16 Ngakho-ke isisho esithi: Umbusi okhanyiselwe ubeka amacebo akhe phambili; ujenene omuhle uhlakulela izinsiza zakhe.

17. Ninganyakazi ngaphandle kokuthi nibone inzuzo; ningawasebenzisi amabutho enu ngaphandle kokuthi kukhona okungazuzwa; ungalwi ngaphandle uma isikhundla sibucayi.

18. Akekho umbusi okufanele abeke amabutho ensimini ukuze anelise ubende lwakhe; akekho ujenene okufanele alwe impi nje ngaphandle kwesiphithiphithi.

19. Uma kukuzuzisa, qhubekela phambili; uma kungenjalo, hlala lapho ukhona.

20. Ngokuhamba kwesikhathi intukuthelo ingaphenduka intokozo; ukucasuka kungase kuphumelele ngokuqukethwe.

21 Kodwa umbuso owake wachithwa ungephinde ube khona; futhi abafileyo abanakuphinde babuyiselwe ekuphileni.

22. Ngakho-ke umbusi okhanyiselwe uyalalela, futhi ujenene omuhle ugcwele ukuqapha. Lena indlela yokugcina izwe linokuthula nebutho liqinile.

XIII. Ukusetshenziswa Kwezinhloli

1. USun Tzu uthe: Ukukhulisa inqwaba yamadoda ayizinkulungwane eziyikhulu nokuwahambela amabanga amade kuhlanganisa ukulahlekelwa okukhulu kubantu kanye nokudonsa izinsiza zoMbuso. Izindleko zansuku zonke ziyoba amashekeli ayinkulungwane esiliva. Kuyoba nesiphithiphithi ekhaya naphesheya, amadoda adinwe ashone emigwaqweni emikhulu. Imindeni efinyelela ezinkulungwaneni ezingamakhulu ayisikhombisa izovinjelwa emsebenzini wayo.
2. Amabutho anobutha angase abhekane iminyaka, elwela ukunqoba okunqunywa ngosuku olulodwa. Lokhu njengoba kunjalo, ukuhlala ungasazi isimo sesitha ngenxa nje yokuthi umuntu uyakuzonda ukukhishwa kwekhulu lesiliva emalini yokuhlonishwa kanye nemivuzo, kuwubude obungenabuntu.

3. Umuntu owenza kanje akayena umholi wabantu, akalutholi usizo olukhona enkosini yakhe, akayena inkosi yokunqoba.

4. Ngakho-ke, okwenza inkosi ehlakaniphile nojenene olungile bakwazi ukushaya futhi banqobe, futhi bazuze izinto ezingaphezu kwamandla abantu abavamile, wukwazi kusengaphambili.

5. Manje lokhu ukwazi kusengaphambili akunakukhishwa emimoyeni; ayikwazi ukutholwa ngokuziphendulela kokuhlangenwe nakho, noma nganoma yisiphi isibalo esidonswayo.

6. Ulwazi ngezimo zesitha lungatholakala kuphela kwabanye abantu.

7. Ngakho-ke ukusetshenziswa kwezinhloli, okunezigaba ezinhlanu zazo: (1) Izinhloli zendawo; (2) izinhloli zangaphakathi; (3) izinhloli eziguquliwe; (4) izinhloli ezizobhujiswa; (5) izinhloli ezisaphila.

8. Lapho lezi zinhlobo ezinhlanu zezinhloli zonke zisebenza, akekho ongathola uhlelo oluyimfihlo. Lokhu kubizwa ngokuthi "ukuxhaphaza kwaphezulu imicu." Kuwubuhlakani obuyigugu kakhulu bobukhosi.

9. Ukuba nezinhloli zendawo kusho ukuqasha izakhamizi zesifunda.

10. Ukuba nezinhloli zangaphakathi, ukusebenzisa izikhulu zesitha.

11. Ukuba nezinhloli eziphendukile, ukubamba izinhloli zesitha futhi sizisebenzisele izinhloso zethu.

12. Ukuba nezinhloli ezilahliwe, ukwenza izinto ezithile obala ngenjongo yokukhohlisa, nokuvumela izinhloli zethu ukuba zazi ngazo futhi zibike ezitheni.

13. Izinhloli ezisaphila, ekugcineni, yilabo ababuya nezindaba ezivela ekamu lesitha.

14. Ngakho-ke yilokho okungekho embuthweni wonke ubuhlobo obuseduze okufanele bugcinwe kunezinhloli. Akekho okufanele aklonyeliswe ngokukhululekile. Alikho elinye ibhizinisi okufanele kugcinwe imfihlo enkulu.

15. Izinhloli azikwazi ukuqashwa ngokunenzuzo ngaphandle kobuhlakani obuthile obunembile.

16. Angeke ziphathwe ngendlela efanele ngaphandle kobubele nokungagwegwesi.

17. Ngaphandle kobuhlakani bengqondo obucashile, umuntu akakwazi ukuqiniseka ngeqiniso lemibiko yabo.

18. Yiba nobuqili! hlakanipha! futhi usebenzise izinhloli zakho kulo lonke uhlobo lwebhizinisi.

.

20. Noma ngabe into iwukubhidliza ibutho, ukugasela umuzi, noma ukubulala umuntu, kubalulekile ukuthi kuqalwe ngokuthola amagama ezikhonzi, ama-aid-de-camp, nabagcini beminyango kanye nabalindi bojenene ophethe. Izinhloli zethu kumele zijutshwe ukuthi ziqinisekise lokhu.

21. Izinhloli zezitha ezizele ukuzosihlola kumele zifunwe, zilingwe ngamalobolo, ziholwe, zihlaliswe ngokunethezeka. Kanjalo bayoba izinhloli eziguquliwe futhi batholakalele inkonzo yethu.

22. Kungenxa yolwazi olulethwe yinhloli eguquliwe ukuthi sikwazi ukuthola futhi siqashe izinhloli zendawo kanye nezingaphakathi.

23. Kungenxa yolwazi lwakhe futhi, ukuthi singenza inhloli ebhujisiwe ihambise izindaba ezingamanga ezitheni.

24. Okokugcina, kungenxa yolwazi lwakhe ukuthi inhloli esaphila ingasetshenziswa ngezikhathi ezimisiwe.

25. Isiphetho nenhloso yokuhlola kuzo zonke izinhlobo zayo ezinhlanu ulwazi lwesitha; futhi lolu lwazi lungatholakala kuphela, ekuqaleni, kuyinhloli eguquliwe. Ngakho-ke kubalulekile ukuthi inhloli eguquliwe iphathwe ngokukhululeka okukhulu.

26. Endulo, ukuvela kobukhosi bakwaYin kwakungenxa ka-I Chih owayekhonze ngaphansi kweHsia. Ngokunjalo, ukuphakama kobukhosi bakwaChou kwakungenxa kaLu Ya owayesebenze ngaphansi kweYin.

27. Ngakho-ke kuphela umbusi okhanyiselwe kanye nojenene ohlakaniphile ozosebenzisa ubuhlakani obuphezulu bebutho ngezinjongo zokuhlola futhi ngalokho bazuze imiphumela emihle. Izinhloli ziyisici esibaluleke kakhulu emanzini, ngoba kuzo kuncike ikhono lebutho lokuhamba.
