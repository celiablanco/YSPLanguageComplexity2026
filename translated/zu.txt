Kwakuyikhehla elalidoba lodwa esikebheni eGulf Stream futhi kwase kuphele izinsuku ezingamashumi ayisishiyagalombili nane lingayithathi inhlanzi. Ezinsukwini ezingamashumi amane zokuqala umfana wayenaye. Kodwa ngemva kwezinsuku ezingamashumi amane ngaphandle kwenhlanzi abazali bomfana babemtshele ukuthi ikhehla manje laliqinisekile futhi ekugcineni isalao, okuwuhlobo olubi kakhulu lwebhadi, futhi umfana wayehambe ngokuyala kwabo ngesinye isikebhe esabamba izinhlanzi ezintathu ezinhle ngesonto lokuqala. Kwamphatha kabi umfana ukubona ikhehla lingena mihla namalanga liphethe isikejana salo singenalutho futhi njalo laliyehla ukuze limsize ukuthwala imigqa eboshiwe noma i-gaff nehabhu kanye noseyili owawuzungezwe insika. Useyili wawufakwe amasaka empuphu, futhi, umboziwe, wawubukeka njengefulege lokunqotshwa unomphela.
