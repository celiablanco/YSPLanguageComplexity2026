Mihring dikna chanvo chungchanga Universal Declaration

Mihring hnam zawng zawngte pianpui zahawmna leh dikna chanvo intluktlanna leh laksak theih loh hriatpuina hi khawvela zalenna, dikna leh remna dinna lungphum a ni a; mihring dikna chanvo ngaihsak lohna leh hmuhsitna chuan mihringte chhia leh ṭha hriatna tithinur thei khawpa tharum thawhna a thlen a; tin, mihringin zalenna thusawi leh rinna leh hlauhna leh tlakchhamna laka zalenna a neih theihna khawvel lo thleng chu mi zawng zawng duhthusam sang ber a nih thu puan a ni tawh bawk a, chuti lo chuan mihring chu thuneihna leh tihduhdahna dodalna hnuhnung ber atan hel tura nawr a ni ang Dan hmanga rorelna hmanga mihring dikna chanvo humhalh a pawimawh hle a, hnam hrang hrangte inkara inlaichinna tha tihhmasawn hi a pawimawh hle. United Nations member zawng zawngte chuan a Charter-ah chuan mihring dikna chanvo bulpui, mihring nihna zahawmna leh hlutna, leh hmeichhia leh mipa dikna chanvo inang tlanga an rinna chu an nemnghet leh a, khawtlang hmasawnna leh zalenna zau zawka nunphung tha zawk tihhmasawn tumin an inpe a ni. Member State-te chuan United Nations nen thawhhona tha tak neiin, mihring dikna leh zalenna bulpui te chu mi zawng zawng zah leh zawm a nih theih nan hmalak an tiam a ni. He Thuthlung hi a taka kalpui a nih theih nan dikna leh zalenna chungchanga hriatthiamna inang tlang chu a pawimawh ber a nih avangin, chuvangin, United Nations General Assembly chuan mimal leh khawtlang pawl zawng zawngte zingah zirlai leh zirtirna hmanga heng dikna leh zalenna zah hi a tipung a, he Thuthlung hi ngaihtuah rengin, ram pum leh ram pawn lama hmalakna hmasawn tak tak hmangin, khawvel pum huapa hriatpuina leh zawm a nih theih nan hma a la a ni Member State-a mipuite ngei leh an thuneihna hnuaia awmte zinga heng dikna leh zalenna te; Universal Declaration of Human Rights kan puang chhuak thin. Article 1-na a ni

Mihring zawng zawng hi zalen taka piang, zahawmna leh dikna chanvo inang vek kan ni. Mihringte hi pianphungah chuan rilru leh chhia leh tha hriatna nei kan ni a, unau nihna rilru pu chungin kan che tur a ni. Article 2 Mi tinin he Thupuan-a tarlan dikna leh zalenna zawng zawng, chi, vun rawng, mipa leh hmeichhia, tawng, sakhua, politics emaw ngaihdan dang emaw, hnam emaw, khawtlang emaw, thil neih, pianpui emaw, dinhmun dang emaw thliar hranna awm lovin, a dikna leh zalenna zawng zawng chu a nei thei a ni. Chu bâkah, chu ram emaw, ram emaw, chu ram emaw, ram emaw chu ram zalên a nih emaw, rintlakna emaw, mahni inrelbawlna nei lo emaw, a thuneihna chungchânga khapna dang hnuaia awm emaw pawh ni se, a ram emaw, a bial emaw chu a politics, dân emaw, international emaw dinhmun a\angin thliar hranna a awm lo vang. Article 3 Mi tin hian nunna, zalenna leh himna chanvo kan nei a. Article 4 Tumah sal leh bawih dinhmunah an awm tur a ni lo. Saltanna chi hrang hrang leh sal zawrh hi khap a ni. Article 5 Mi tumah tihduhdah emaw, nunrawng tak, mihring zah lohna emaw, zahmawh taka tihduhdah emaw, hremna emaw tuar tur a ni lo. Article 6 Mi tin hian khawi hmunah pawh dan hmaah mihring anga pawm theihna kan nei a. Article 7-na a ni
Mi zawng zawng hi dan hmaah intluktlang kan ni a, thliar hranna awm lovin danin humhalhna inang tlang kan nei a ni. Mi tinin he Thupuan bawhchhiaa inthliarna eng pawh lakah leh chutiang inthliarna tichhe thei tura fuihna laka humhimna inang tlang kan nei vek a ni. Article 8 Mi tinin Constitution emaw dan emawin dikna bulpui a pekte bawhchhiatna a neih chuan ram chhunga court thunei tak takte hnen atangin chhanchhuahna tha tak a dawng thei a ni. Article 9 Mi tumah duhthusam anga man, hren leh hnawhchhuah theih a ni lo. Article 10 Mi tinte chu a dikna, mawhphurhna leh a chunga thubuai siamsakte relna atan independent leh mi dangte laka inthliarna nei lo tribunal hmaah intluktlanna famkim nen dik tak leh vantlang hmaah rorelna neih theihna a nei a ni. Article 11 1. Thil sual tia puh mi tinte chuan a invenna atana thil tul zawng zawng tihfelna hmuna vantlang rorelnaah dan anga thiam loh chantir a nih hma chuan thiam loh chantir theihna a nei. 2. Tumah thil tih emaw tih loh emaw avanga thiam loh chantir tur a ni lo va, chu thil tih lai khan ram pum emaw, ram pawn emaw dan hnuaiah chuan thil sual tihna a ni lo. Tin, thil sual tih laia hremna hman theih tur aia hremna rit zawk pek tur a ni lo. Article 12 Mi tumah mimal nun, in, chenna leh lehkha thawnah duhthusamin a inrawlh tur a ni lo va, a chawimawina leh a hmingthatna sawiselna a tawk tur a ni lo. Chutiang inrawlhna emaw, sawiselna emaw laka danin a humhimna chu mi tinin kan nei a ni. Article 13-na a ni

1. Mi tinin mahni ram chhungah zalenna leh chenna tur dikna kan nei. 2. Mi tinin mahni ram tiamin ram dang chhuahsan theihna leh mahni ram lama kir leh theihna a nei. Article 14 1. Mi tinin tihduhdahna laka ram danga inhumhimna zawng leh dawn theihna kan nei a. 2. Politics ni lo thil sual tak tak emaw, United Nations thil tum leh thu bul kalh zawnga thiltih emaw avanga thubuai siamsak a nih chuan he dikna hi tihngheh theih a ni lo. Article 15 1. Mi tin hian hnam nihna kan nei. 2. Mi tumah duhthusamin a hnam nihna laksak emaw, a hnam thlak danglam theihna chanvo hnial emaw a ni lo vang. Article 16 1. Hmeichhia leh mipa puitlingte chuan hnam, hnam leh sakhua a\anga khapna awm lovin nupui pasal neih leh chhungkaw din theihna an nei. Inneihna chungchangah te, nupui pasal neih chhung leh inneihna tihtawp a nih hunah te hian dikna chanvo inang an nei thei a ni. 2. Inneihna hi nupui/pasal lo awm turte remtihna zalen leh famkim chauha din a ni. 3. Chhungkua hi khawtlang nunphung (natural and basic unit of society) a nih avangin khawtlang leh state-in a humhim theihna tur dikna a nei a ni. Article 17 1. Mi tin hian mimal leh midangte nen tangkawpin thil neih theihna an nei. 2. Tumah duh duhin a thil neih laksak theih a ni lo. Article 18-na a ni
Mi tin hian ngaihtuahna, chhia leh tha hriatna leh sakhuana zalenna chanvo kan nei a. He dikna hian mahni sakhua emaw, rinna emaw thlak danglam theihna leh mahni chauh emaw, midangte nena inpawlhona emaw, vantlang emaw, mimal emaw, sakhaw thil tih dan, biakna leh serhpui hmanga mahni sakhua emaw rinna lantir theihna zalenna a huam a ni. Article 19 Mi tin hian zalen taka ngaihdan leh thu sawi theihna kan nei a. Heng dikna chanvote hian inrawlhna tel lova ngaihdan neih theihna leh ramri eng pawh ni se, eng media kaltlangin thu leh hla leh ngaihtuahna zawng, hmuh leh insem theihna zalenna te a huam a ni. Article 20 1. Mi tinte hian zalen taka inkhawm leh pawl hrang hrang neih theihna kan nei a. 2. Tumah eng pawlah pawh tel tura nawr luih an ni lovang. Article 21 1. Mi tin hian a ram sorkarah direct emaw, zalen taka thlan bikte kaltlangin emaw tel theihna a nei. 2. Mi tinin mahni ramah vantlang hna chelh turin dikna chanvo inang vek kan nei. 3. Sawrkar thuneihna innghahna chu mipui duhdan a ni. Hei hi universal leh equal suffrage angin thuruk emaw, a tlukpui emaw zalen taka vote thlak dan hmanga inthlanna mumal leh dik tak hmanga tarlan a ni ang. Article 22 Mi tin hian khawtlang mipui angin khawtlang nun himna leh ram pum huap thawhrimna leh ram hrang hrang thawhhona hmangin, leh State tinte inrelbawlna leh sum leh pai ang zelin, a zahawmna leh mize zalen taka tihhmasawn nana sum leh pai, khawtlang leh nunphung lama dikna chanvo pawimawh tak takte tihhlawhtlin theihna tur dikna a nei a ni. Article 23-na a ni

1. Mi tinin hnathawh theihna, zalen taka eizawnna duhthlanna, hnathawhna hmun dik leh tha leh hnathawh tur nei lo laka venhimna kan nei. 2. Hnathawh intluktlang avanga zangnadawmna intluktlanna dawng tur hian mi tinin inthliarna awm lova, zangnadawmna intluktlang kan dawng thei a ni. . 4. Mi tinin mahni duhdan humhalh nan labour union din leh zawm theihna a nei. Article 24 Mi tin hian chawlh hahdam leh chawlh hahdam theihna an nei a, chutah chuan hnathawh hun chhung leh regular paid leave tihkhawtlai theihna a awm. Article 25 1. Mi tinin mahni leh a chhungte hriselna leh hamthatna atana nunphung tling, ei leh in, silhfen, chenna tur, damdawi lam enkawlna leh a tul anga khawtlang nun hamthatna te, hna nei lo, damlohna, rualbanlo, nupui/pasal thihna, tar emaw, a thunun theih loh thil dang emaw avanga eizawnna tlakchhamna a awm chuan himna chanvo a nei a ni. 2. Nu leh fate hian venhimna leh chhawmdawlna bik an nei. Naupang zawng zawng hian an dinhmun thliar lovin khawtlang venhimna inang an nei vek a ni. Article 26 1. Mi tin hian zirna lamah chanvo kan nei a. Zirna hi a thlawna neih a ngai a, a tlem berah chuan primary leh basic level-ah pawh a awm tur a ni. Primary zirna hi tih ngei ngei tur a ni. Technical leh vocational education chu mi zawng zawngin a hmuh theih tur a ni a, zirna sang zawk chu merit a zirin mi zawng zawng tan inang tlang taka zir theih a ni tur a ni. 2. Zirna hian mize tihhmasawn kim leh mihring dikna leh zalenna bulpui zahna tihchak a tum a ni. Zirna hian hriatthiamna, inngaihtlawmna leh
Inṭhianṭhatna kan chawisang tur a ni a, remna vawng reng turin United Nations hmalaknate kan chawisang tur a ni. 3. Nu leh pate hian an fate zirna chi hrang hrang thlan hi an ngai pawimawh ber. Article 27 1. Mi tinin khawtlang nunphung nunah zalen taka tel theihna te, kutchhuak nuam tih te, science hmasawnna leh a hlawkna te chanvo te chanvo kan nei a. 2. Mi tinin a thil siam scientific, literary emaw artistic product atanga lo chhuak thlarau lam leh tisa lam hawi zawng zawng humhalh theihna a nei. Article 28 Mi tin hian he Thupuan-a dikna leh zalenna tarlan te hi a taka tihhlawhtlin theihna tur khawtlang leh khawvel inrelbawlna (social and international order) neih theihna tur dikna kan nei a ni. Article 29 1. Mi tinin mahni mize chauh zalen tak leh famkim taka lo thanglian theihna khawtlang lakah mawhphurhna kan nei a. . 3. Heng dikna leh zalenna te hi eng dinhmunah pawh United Nations thil tum leh thurin kalh zawnga hman tur a ni lo. Article 30 He Thupuan-a engmah hi State, pawl emaw, mimal emaw chu he Thupuan-a dikna leh zalenna tarlan zinga eng emaw zat tihchhiat tumna thil tih emaw, hmalakna emaw tih phalna petu anga ngaih tur a ni lo.
