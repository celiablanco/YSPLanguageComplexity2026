UMUFARANSA No 1. Intangiriro rusange 
Kubinyamakuru byigenga. Ku wa gatandatu, 27 Gitugutu 1787 
HAMILTON 
Kubaturage ba Leta ya New York: 
NYUMA yuburambe budashidikanywaho bwubushobozi buke bwa guverinoma iriho, urahamagarirwa 
kuganira ku Itegeko Nshinga rishya kuri Leta zunze ubumwe za Amerika. Ingingo ivuga akamaro kayo; 
gusobanukirwa ningaruka zayo ntakindi nko kubaho kwa UNION, umutekano n'imibereho myiza ya 
ibice byayo bigizwe, iherezo ryubwami muri byinshi bishimishije kwisi. Ifite 
bakunze kuvugwa ko bisa nkaho byeguriwe abaturage b'iki gihugu, imyitwarire yabo kandi 
urugero, guhitamo ikibazo cyingenzi, niba societe yabagabo ishoboye koko cyangwa idashobora gushingwa 
guverinoma nziza kuva mubitekerezo no guhitamo, cyangwa niba bagenewe iteka gushingira kuri politiki yabo 
itegeko nshinga ku mpanuka n'ingufu. Niba hari ukuri kuri ayo magambo, ikibazo tugezemo kirashobora 
hamwe no gufatwa bifatwa nkigihe icyo cyemezo kigomba gufatwa; n'amatora atari yo igice twe 
izakora irashobora, muriki gitekerezo, ikwiye gufatwa nkibyago rusange byabantu. 
Iki gitekerezo kizongerera imbaraga z'abagiraneza kubakunda igihugu, kugirango bongere ubwigunge bose 
abagabo bitonda kandi beza bagomba kumva ibyabaye. Bizaba byiza niba guhitamo kwacu kugomba kuyoborwa na a 
kugereranya ubushishozi inyungu zacu nyazo, zidatangaye kandi zitabogamye kubitekerezo bitajyanye na 
rusange. Ariko iki nikintu gikomeye cyane kwifuzwa kuruta gukomera kubiteganijwe. Umugambi twahawe 
kuganira bigira ingaruka zinyungu zihariye, guhanga udushya twinshi mubigo byaho, kutabigiramo uruhare 
ikiganiro cyacyo ibintu bitandukanye mumahanga kubikorwa byayo, nibitekerezo, irari nurwikekwe bidakwiye kuri 
kuvumbura ukuri. 
Mu mbogamizi zikomeye mu mbogamizi Itegeko Nshinga rishya rigomba guhura naryo byoroshye 
gutandukanwa ninyungu zigaragara zicyiciro runaka cyabagabo muri buri ntara kugirango barwanye impinduka zose zishobora 
ibyago bigabanya imbaraga, ibimenyetso, ningaruka zibiro bafite munsi yigihugu 
ibigo; n'icyifuzo kigoramye cy'ikindi cyiciro cy'abagabo, bazizera ko bazaguka 
ubwabo kubera urujijo rwigihugu cyabo, cyangwa bazishima bafite ibyiringiro byiza byo kuzamuka bava 
kugabana ubwami mu mashyirahamwe menshi igice kuruta kuva mu bumwe bwayo muri guverinoma imwe.

gutotezwa. 
Kandi ,, nubwo gusa amarangamutima azemererwa kuba, tumaze kwerekana ibimenyetso bihagije byerekana 
bibaho muribi nko mubihe byose byahoze byibiganiro bikomeye byigihugu. Umugezi wibyifuzo bibi kandi bibi 
reka. Gucira urubanza imyitwarire y'ababuranyi, tuzayoborwa gufata umwanzuro ko bazabikora 
twizeranye kwemeza ubutabera bwibitekerezo byabo, no kongera umubare wabahindutse na 
amajwi menshi yo gutangaza no gusharira kwa invectives zabo. Ishyaka rimurikirwa imbaraga kandi 
imikorere ya guverinoma izasuzugurwa nkurubyaro rwumujinya ukunda imbaraga za devotique kandi urwango 
amahame y'ubwisanzure. Ishyari rirenze urugero ryibyago byuburenganzira bwabaturage, nibyinshi 
mubisanzwe amakosa yumutwe kuruta ayumutima, azagaragazwa nkukwiyitirira gusa nubukorikori, ibyambo bishaje 
kubwamamare bitwaje inyungu rusange. Bizibagirana, kuruhande rumwe, ishyari nibisanzwe 
bihuza urukundo, kandi ko ishyaka ryiza ryubwigenge rikwiye kwanduzwa numwuka muto kandi 
kutizerana. Ku rundi ruhande, tuzibagirwa kimwe ko imbaraga za guverinoma ari ngombwa kuri 
umutekano w'ubwisanzure; ko, mugutekereza urubanza rwumvikana kandi rusobanutse neza, inyungu zabo ntizishobora 
gutandukana; kandi ko icyifuzo giteye akaga akenshi cyihishe inyuma ya mask yihariye yo guharanira uburenganzira bwa 
abantu kuruta munsi yabujijwe kwerekana ishyaka ryo gushikama no gukora neza kwa guverinoma. Amateka azabikora 
twigishe ko iyambere yabonetse umuhanda munini cyane wo gutangiza kwiheba kuruta iyanyuma, 
n'iy'abo bagabo bahiritse ubwisanzure bwa repubulika, umubare munini watangiye umwuga wabo 
mu kwishyura urukiko rukurikiraho; gutangira demagogues, no kurangiza abanyagitugu. 
Mugihe cyibitekerezo byabanjirije iki, nagize ijisho, benewacu, kugushyira ku bwawe 
Irinde kugerageza, uhereye igihembwe icyo aricyo cyose, kugirango uhindure icyemezo cyawe mugihe gito cyane kugeza 
imibereho yawe, kubitekerezo byose usibye nibishobora guturuka kubimenyetso byukuri. Uzabikora, oya 
gushidikanya, icyarimwe, bakusanyije kuva murwego rusange rwabo, ko bava mumasoko ntabwo 
mu buryo butemewe n'Itegeko Nshinga rishya. Nibyo, benewacu, ndagutunze ibyo, nyuma yo kubitondera 
kubitekerezaho, ndumva neza ko ari inyungu zawe kubyemera. Nzi neza ko iyi ari yo nzira yizewe 
kubwisanzure bwawe, icyubahiro cyawe, nibyishimo byawe. Ntabwo ngira ingaruka kubigega ntumva. Sinzagusetsa 
hamwe no kugaragara nkungurana ibitekerezo iyo niyemeje. Ndababwiza ukuri ibyo nizera, kandi nanjye 
izashyira imbere yawe impamvu zashingiweho. Imyumvire yintego nziza irasuzugura 
kudasobanuka. Ntabwo, ariko, kugwiza imyuga kuri uyu mutwe. Impamvu zanjye zigomba kuguma mububiko bwa 
amabere yanjye. Impaka zanjye zizakingurwa kuri bose, kandi zishobora gucirwa urubanza na bose. Nibura bazatangwa muri a 
umwuka utazasuzugura impamvu yukuri.

bikenewe kwitabaza amashyirahamwe atandukanye yibice bitandukanye. (1) Iyi nyigisho, muri byose

JAY 
Kubaturage ba Leta ya New York: 
IYO abaturage ba Amerika bagaragaza ko ubu bahamagariwe guhitamo ikibazo, muricyo 
inkurikizi, zigomba kwerekana kimwe mubyingenzi byigeze bikurura ibitekerezo byabo, ubwiza bwabo 
gufata ibintu byuzuye, kimwe bikomeye, kubibona, bizagaragara. 
Ntakintu gifatika kirenze icyifuzo cya leta gikenewe, kandi ntawahakana, ko 
igihe cyose kandi cyaba cyarashyizweho, abaturage bagomba kugiha uburenganzira bumwe na bumwe bwabo kugirango babubone 
n'ububasha bukenewe. Birakwiye rero ko dusuzumwa rero, niba byazana inyungu nyinshi 
by'abaturage ba Amerika ko bagomba, ku ntego rusange, kuba igihugu kimwe, munsi ya guverinoma imwe, 
cyangwa ko bagomba kwigabanyamo amashyirahamwe atandukanye, bagaha umutwe wa buri bwoko bumwe 
ububasha basabwa gushyira muri guverinoma imwe y'igihugu. 
Kugeza vuba aha byakiriwe kandi bitavuguruzanya ko iterambere ryabaturage ba Amerika 
Biterwa no gukomeza ubumwe bwabo, n'ibyifuzo, amasengesho, n'imbaraga z'abaturage bacu beza kandi bafite ubwenge 
bahora berekeza kuri kiriya kintu. Ariko abanyapolitike ubu baragaragara, bashimangira ko iki gitekerezo ari amakosa, 
kandi ko aho gushakisha umutekano n'ibyishimo mubumwe, dukwiye kubishakira mu bice by'ibihugu 
amashyirahamwe atandukanye cyangwa ubusugire. Nubwo bidasanzwe iyi nyigisho nshya irashobora kugaragara, nyamara ifite 
ababunganira; ninyuguti zimwe zarwanyaga cyane mbere, kuri ubu umubare. 
Ibyo aribyo byose bishobora kuba impaka cyangwa induction zakoze iyi mpinduka mumarangamutima kandi 
amatangazo y'aba nyakubahwa, rwose ntibyaba byiza mubantu muri rusange bemera iyo politiki nshya 
amahame utiriwe wemeza neza ko bashingiye mu kuri na politiki yuzuye. 
Byakunze kunshimisha kubona ko Amerika yigenga itari igizwe na kure kandi kure 
ifasi, ariko kiriya gihugu cyahujwe, kirumbuka, cyagutse cyane cyari igice cyabahungu bacu bo muburengerazuba bwubwigenge.
