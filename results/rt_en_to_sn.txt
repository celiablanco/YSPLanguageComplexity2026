Mvura ine inorganic compound ine kemikari formula H2O. Chinhu chiri pachena, chisingaravike, chisinganhuhwi, uye chinenge chisina ruvara. Ndiyo chikamu chikuru cheHova dzePasi, madziva, uye nyanza. Mvura imvura zvakare yezvipenyu zvose zvinozivikanwa, iyo inoshanda sechinhu chinonyungudutsa. Mvura, iri polar molecule, inopinda yakasimba intermolecular hydrogen bonding iyo inobatsira zvikuru kune yayo yemuviri uye makemikari maitiro. Izvo zvakakosha kune ese anozivikanwa marudzi ehupenyu, zvisinei nekusapa chikafu simba kana kuve organic micronutrient. Nekuda kwekuvapo kwayo mune zvese zvipenyu, kugadzikana kwayo kwemakemikari, kuwanda kwayo kwepasirese, uye polarity yayo yakasimba zvichienderana nehukuru hwayo hwemamorekuru, mvura inowanzonzi "universal solvent". Nekuda kwekuti tembiricha yepasi uye kudzvanywa kuri padyo nenzvimbo nhatu dzemvura, mvura iripo paNyika seyakasimba, mvura, uye gasi. Inoumba kunaya kwemvura nenzira yemvura uye maaerosols muchimiro chemhute. Makore anosanganisira madonhwe akarembera emvura neaizi, kusimba kwayo. Kana yakakamurwa zvakanaka, aizi yecrystalline inogona kunaya sechando. Mamiriro egasi emvura imhute kana mhute yemvura. Mvura inovhara chikamu chinosvika makumi manomwe neshanu kubva muzana chePasi penyika, makungwa nemakungwa zvinoumba huwandu hwehuwandu hwemvura (inenge 96.5%). Zvikamu zvidiki zvemvura zvinoitika semvura yepasi (1.7%), mumakomo echando uye mazaya echando eAntarctica neGreenland (1.7%), uye mumhepo semhute, makore (anosanganisira aizi uye mvura yemvura yakaturikwa mumhepo), uye kunaya kwemvura (0.001%). Mvura inofamba nguva dzose kuburikidza nekutenderera kwemvura yekubuda, transpiration (evapotranspiration), condensation, kunaya, uye kumhanya, kazhinji kusvika kugungwa. Mvura ine basa rakakosha muhupfumi hwenyika. Inenge 70% yemvura yakachena inoshandiswa nevanhu inoenda kune zvekurima. Kubata hove mumunyu nemvura yakachena kwave, uye kunoramba kuri, nzvimbo huru yezvokudya kune nzvimbo dzakawanda dzepasi, zvichipa 6.5% yeprotein yepasi rose. Zvizhinji zvekutengesa kureba kwezvinhu (zvakadai semafuta, gasi rechisikigo, uye zvigadzirwa zvinogadzirwa) zvinotakurwa nemagwa nemumakungwa, nzizi, madziva, uye migero. Huwandu hwemvura, aizi, uye chiutsi zvinoshandiswa pakutonhodza nekudziisa mumaindasitiri nemudzimba. Mvura inonyungudutsa yakanakisa yezvakasiyana siyana zvezvinhu, zvese zvicherwa uye organic; sekudaro, inoshandiswa zvakanyanya mumaindasitiri maitiro uye mukubika nekuwacha. Mvura, aizi, uye sinou ndizvowo pakati pemitambo mizhinji nedzimwe nzira dzevaraidzo, dzakadai sokutuhwina, kukwapaidza mafaro, mijaho yezvikepe, kusefa, kuredza mitambo, kudhivha, kutsvedza muchando, kukwira muchando, uye kutsvedza. Etymology

Zvakare cognate, kuburikidza nemudzi weIndo-European, nechiGiriki ύδωρ (ýdor; kubva kuChigiriki Chekare ὕδωρ (hýdōr), panobva Chirungu 'hydro-'), Russian вода́ (vodá), Irish uisce, uye Albanian ujë. Properties
Mvura (H2O) inzvimbo yepolar inorganic compound. Patembiricha yemumba imvura isinganakidze uye isinganhuwidzi, inenge isina ruvara uye inopenya yebhuruu. Iyo yakapfava haidrojeni chalcogenide, ndiyo inonyanya kudzidzwa kemikari musanganiswa uye dzimwe nguva inotsanangurwa se "nyika yose inonyungudutsa" pakukwanisa kwayo kunyungudutsa zvinhu zvakawanda kupfuura chero imwe mvura, kunyangwe isina kunyungudutsa zvinhu zvisiri polar. Izvi zvinobvumira kuti ive "kunyungudika kwehupenyu": chokwadi, mvura sezvainowanikwa mune zvakasikwa inenge nguva dzose inosanganisira zvinhu zvakasiyana-siyana zvakanyungudutswa, uye matanho anokosha anodiwa kuti uwane mvura yakachena yemakemikari. Mvura ndiyo chete chinhu chinowanikwa kuvapo sechinhu chakasimba, mvura, uye gasi mumamiriro ezvinhu epanyika. States
Pamwe chete neoxidane, mvura nderimwe remazita maviri epamutemo emushonga wemakemikari H2O; iri zvakare chikamu chemvura cheH2O. Dzimwe mbiri dzakajairika dzenyaya yemvura idanho rakasimba, inova ice, uye gaseous phase, mvura mhute kana chiutsi. Kuwedzera kana kubviswa kwekupisa kunogona kukonzera shanduko yechikamu: kutonhora (mvura kuenda kuchando), kunyunguduka (chando kumvura), vaporization (mvura kuenda kumhute), condensation (mhute kuenda kumvura), sublimation (aizi kuenda kumhute) uye deposition (mhute kuaizi). Density
Mvura ndechimwe chezvinhu zvishomanana zvakajairika zvinokura uye zvoita kushoma zvakanyanya sezvainotonhora mukati mehumwe tembiricha; ndicho chega chinhu chinozivikanwa chinoitika chega chinoita izvi paine mvura. Pamusoro pezvo, haina kujairika nekuti inozonyanya kushomeka sezvainotonhora, kunyangwe isiri yega mune izvozvo. Pa1 atm pressure, inosvika pakureba kwayo kwe999.972 kg/m3 (62.4262 lb/cu ft) pa3.98 °C (39.16 °F). Pazasi petembiricha iyoyo, asi pamusoro pechando che 0 °C (32 °F), mvura inokura (ichiita kushoma) kusvika yasvika painotonhora, apo kusimba kwayo muchikamu chemvura i999.8 kg/m3 (62.4155 lb/cu ft). Sezvo mvura inotonhora kusvika pa3.98 °C (39.16 °F), huwandu hwemvura hunodzikira. Painotonhora uye ichiita chando, mvura inowedzera ne917%, inosvika pakuwanda kwe917 kg/m3 (57.25 lb/cu ft). Kuwedzera uku kunogona kuita dzvinyiriro huru, kuputika kwepombi uye matombo anotsemuka. Seyakasimba, inoratidza maitiro akajairwa ekubatira uye kuwedzera kuomarara sezvainotonhora. Izvi zvisina kujairika zvinopisa zvine mhedzisiro yakakosha kune hupenyu hwepasi. Mudhamu kana mugungwa, mvura iri pa4 °C (39 °F) inonyura pasi, uye mazaya echando anoita pamusoro, achiyangarara pamusoro pemvura yemvura. Aizi iyi inodzivirira mvura iri pasi, ichidzivirira kuoma kwechando. Pasina iyi dziviriro, zvipenyu zvakawanda zvemumvura zvinogara mumadziva zvaizoparara munguva yechando. Pamusoro pezvo, hunhu uhwu husinganzwisisike chikamu chakakosha chekutenderera kwe thermohaline iyo inoparadzira kupisa kwakatenderedza makungwa epasi. Magnetism
Mvura ndeye diamagnetic material. Kunyangwe kudyidzana kusina kusimba, ine superconducting magineti inogona kuwana kudyidzana kunocherechedzwa. Phase transitions
Pakumanikidzwa kwemhepo imwe chete (atm), aizi inonyunguduka kana kuti mvura inooma (inoomesa) pa 0 °C (32 °F) uye mvura inofashaira kana mhute inodzika pa100 °C (212 °F). Zvisineyi, kunyangwe pazasi penzvimbo yekuvira, mvura inogona kushanduka kuita mhute pamusoro payo nekubuda (vaporization mukati memvura yese inozivikanwa sekuvira). Sublimation uye deposition inoitikawo panzvimbo. Semuenzaniso, chando chinoiswa panzvimbo inotonhora apo mazaya echando achiumbwa nekuiswa pane aerosol particle kana ice nucleus. Mukuita kuomesa nechando, chikafu chinoomeswa nechando chozochengetwa pakadzikama kuitira kuti chando chiri pamusoro payo chidzikame. Kunyungudika nekubikira kunotsamira pakumanikidzwa. Mumazaya echando, kunyunguduka kunogona kuitika pasi pemazaya echando akakora, zvichikonzera madziva ari pasi pechando. Hukama hweClausius-Clapeyron hunoshandawo kunzvimbo yekubikira, asi nekushanduka kwemvura / gasi chikamu chemhute chine density yakaderera kupfuura chikamu chemvura, saka nzvimbo yekubikira inowedzera nekumanikidza. Mvura inogona kuramba iri mumvura patembiricha yakakwira munyanza yakadzika kana pasi pevhu. Semuenzaniso, tembiricha inodarika 205 °C (401 °F) muOld Faithful, geyser muYellowstone National Park. Muma hydrothermal vents, tembiricha inogona kudarika 400 °C (752 °F). Pagungwa, panofashaira mvura i100 °C (212 °F). Sezvo simba remuchadenga richiderera nekukwirira, nzvimbo yekuvira inodzikira ne1 °C pamamita 274 ega ega. Kubika kwepamusoro-soro kunotora nguva yakareba kupfuura kubika kwepamusoro pegungwa. Semuenzaniso, pamamita 1,524 (5,000 ft), nguva yekubika inofanira kuwedzerwa nechechina kuti uwane mhedzisiro yaunoda. Sezvineiwo, yekumanikidza cooker inogona kushandiswa kuderedza nguva yekubika nekusimudza tembiricha yekuvira. Muvacuum, mvura inofashaira patembiricha yekamuri. Zvitatu uye zvakakosha pfungwa
Padhiyagiramu yekumanikidza/tembiricha (ona mufananidzo), pane ma curve anopatsanura gobvu kubva mumhute, mhute kubva pamvura, uye mvura kubva mukuoma. Izvi zvinosangana panzvimbo imwe chete inonzi katatu, apo zvikamu zvitatu zvose zvinogona kuvapo. Nzvimbo yetatu iri pakupisa kwe273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F) uye kumanikidzwa kwe611.657 pascals (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); ndiyo dzvinyiriro yakaderera apo mvura yemvura inogona kuvapo. Kusvika 2019, iyo katatu poindi yakashandiswa kutsanangura iyo Kelvin tembiricha chiyero. Mvura/mhute yechikamu chinotenderera inogumira pa647.096 K (373.946 °C; 705.103 °F) uye 22.064 megapascals (3,200.1 psi; 217.75 atm). Izvi zvinozivikanwa senzvimbo yakakosha. Pamatembiricha epamusoro uye madhindindi emvura nemhute zvinoumba chikamu chinoenderera chinonzi supercritical fluid. Inogona kudzvinyirirwa zvishoma nezvishoma kana kuwedzerwa pakati pegasi-yakafanana uye mvura-yakafanana densities; maitiro ayo (ayo akasiyana zvakanyanya neaya emvura yakadzika) anonzwa kune density. Semuyenzaniso, kumanikidziro akakodzera uye tembiricha inogona kusanganiswa zvakasununguka neasiri polar komisheni, kusanganisira yakawanda organic komisheni. Izvi zvinoita kuti zvive zvakakosha mumhando dzakasiyana-siyana dzekushandisa dzinosanganisira high-temperature electrochemistry uye se ecologically benign solvent kana catalyst mumakemikari maitiro anosanganisira organic compounds. Muchipfeko chePasi, chinoshanda sechinhu chinonyungudutsa panguva yekuumbwa kwemaminerari, kuparara uye kuisa. Zvikamu zvechando nemvura
Mazaya echando ari pamusoro peNyika iaizi Ih, chikamu chinoumba makristasi ane mativi matanhatu akaenzana. Imwe ine cubic crystalline symmetry, ice Ic, inogona kuitika mumhepo yepamusoro. Sezvo kudzvinyirira kunowedzera, chando chinoumba mamwe makristasi maitiro. Kubva muna 2024, makumi maviri akasimbiswa kuyedza uye akati wandei akafanotaurwa. Chimiro chegumi nesere cheaizi, ice XVIII, face-centred-cubic, superionic ice phase, yakawanikwa apo donhwe remvura raive pasi pekuvhunduka kwakasimudza dzvinyiriro yemvura kumamiriyoni emuchadenga uye tembiricha yayo kusvika kuzviuru zvemadhigirii, zvichikonzera chimiro chemaatomu eokisijeni akaomarara umo maatomu ehydrogen aiyerera zvakasununguka. Kana yaiswa pakati pezvikamu zvegraphene, aizi inoita sikweya latisi. Tsanangudzo yemakemikari emvura yemvura haina kunyatsonzwisiswa; dzimwe dzidziso dzinoratidza kuti maitiro ayo asina kujairika anokonzerwa nekuvapo kwenzvimbo mbiri dzemvura. Kuravira uye kunhuwa
Mvura yakachena inowanzotsanangurwa seisina kunaka uye isinganhuhwi, kunyange zvazvo vanhu vane masensor chaiwo anogona kunzwa kuvapo kwemvura mumuromo mavo, uye matatya anozivikanwa kuti anokwanisa kunhuwidza. Nekudaro, mvura kubva kune zvakajairwa zvitubu (kusanganisira mineral water) kazhinji ine zvinhu zvakawanda zvakanyungudutswa zvinogona kuipa zvakasiyana-siyana zvinonaka uye kunhuhwirira. Vanhu nedzimwe mhuka dzakagadzira pfungwa dzinoita kuti vakwanise kuongorora kugona kwemvura kuti vadzivise mvura ine munyu wakawandisa kana kuti yakaora. Ruvara nechitarisiko
Mvura yakachena inooneka yebhuruu nekuda kwekunyudzwa kwechiedza munharaunda c. 600–800 nm. Ruvara runogona kucherechedzwa zviri nyore mugirazi repombi-mvura yakaiswa pakatarisana nechena yakachena kumashure, masikati. Iwo makuru ekunyudza mabhendi ane chekuita neruvara ndeye overtones yeO-H inotambanudza kudengenyeka. Kusimba kuri pachena kweruvara kunowedzera nekudzika kwembiru yemvura, zvichitevera mutemo weBeer. Izvi zvinoshandawo, semuenzaniso, nedziva rekutuhwina kana chitubu chechiedza chiri chiedza chezuva chinoratidzwa kubva mumataira machena edziva. Muchisikigo, ruvara runogonawo kuchinjwa kubva kubhuruu kuenda kugirinhi nekuda kwekuvapo kwezvakarembera zvakamira kana algae. Muindasitiri, pedyo-infrared spectroscopy inoshandiswa neaqueous solutions sezvo kusimba kukuru kwekudzika kwemvura kunoreva kuti magirazi cuvettes ane pfupi nzira-kureba anogona kushandiswa. Kuti ucherechedze kukosha kwekunanzva kwemvura kana kwemushonga wemvura munharaunda yakatenderedza 3,500 cm−1 (2.85 μm) nzira yakareba ingangoita 25 μm inodiwa. Zvakare, iyo cuvette inofanirwa kuve ese akajeka kutenderedza 3500 cm−1 uye isinganyungudika mumvura; calcium fluoride chimwe chinhu chinowanzo shandiswa kune cuvette mahwindo ane aqueous mhinduro. Iyo Raman-inoshanda yakakosha vibrations inogona kucherechedzwa ine, semuenzaniso, 1 cm sampuli sero. Zvidyarwa zvemumvura, algae, uye zvimwe zvipenyu zvephotosynthetic zvinogona kugara mumvura kusvika mazana emamita kudzika, nekuti chiedza chezuva chinogona kusvika pazviri. Kunenge kuti hapana chiedza chezuva chinosvika kumativi emakungwa pasi pe1,000 metres (3,300 ft) pakadzika. Iyo refractive index yemvura yemvura (1.333 pa20 °C (68 °F)) yakakwirira kwazvo kupfuura yemhepo (1.0), yakafanana neye alkanes uye ethanol, asi yakaderera pane yeglycerol (1.473), 1.501), carbon disulfide (1.627), uye inowanikwa kune girazi (1.6 mhando). Iyo refraction index yechando (1.31) yakaderera pane yemvura yemvura. Molecular polarity
Mumorekuru wemvura, maatomu ehydrogen anoumba kona ye104.5° neatomu reokisijeni. Maatomu ehydrogen ari pedyo nemakona maviri etetrahedron ari pakati peokisijeni. Kune mamwe makona maviri kune mapeya ega emagetsi evalence asingabatanidzi mukubatana. Mutetrahedron yakakwana, maatomu aizoumba kona ye109.5°, asi kuremba kuri pakati pemapeya ega kwakakura kudarika kudzorwa kuri pakati pemaatomu ehydrogen. Iyo O-H bond kureba ingangoita 0.096 nm. Zvimwe zvinhu zvine tetrahedral molecular structure, semuenzaniso methane (CH4) uye hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Zvakadaro, okisijeni ine simba remagetsi kupfuura mamwe maelementi akawanda, saka atomu reokisijeni rine chaji isina kunaka ukuwo maatomu ehydrogen achichajiwa zvishoma. Pamwe chete neyakakombama chimiro, izvi zvinopa molecule nguva yemagetsi dipole uye inoiswa seye polar molecule. Mvura yakanaka polar solvent, inonyungudutsa akawanda munyu uye hydrophilic organic molecules seshuga uye nyore doro senge ethanol. Mvura inonyungudutsawo magasi mazhinji, akadai seokisijeni nekabhoni dhaokisaidhi - iyo yekupedzisira ichipa fizz yezvinwiwa zvecarbonated, waini inopenya nedoro. Mukuwedzera, zvinhu zvakawanda muzvisikwa zvipenyu, zvakadai semapuroteni, DNA uye polysaccharides, zvinonyungudutswa mumvura. Kudyidzana pakati pemvura nezvikamu zveaya mabiomacromolecules anoumba protein kupeta, DNA base pairing, uye zvimwe zviitiko zvakakosha kuhupenyu (hydrophobic effect). Zvinhu zvakawanda zveorganic (zvakadai semafuta nemafuta uye alkanes) zvine hydrophobic, kureva, isinganyungudika mumvura. Mazhinji inorganic zvinhu hazvinyungudika zvakare, kusanganisira mazhinji esimbi oxides, sulfides, uye silicates. Hydrogen kubatana
Nekuda kweiyo polarity, morekuru remvura iri mumvura kana yakasimba mamiriro anogona kuumba anosvika mana mabhondi ehydrogen nemamorekuru akavakidzana. Mabhondi eHydrogen akasimba zvakapetwa kagumi sesimba reVan der Waals rinokwezva mamorekuru kune rimwe nerimwe muzvinwiwa zvakawanda. Ichi ndicho chikonzero nei kunyunguduka uye kuvira kwemvura kwemvura kwakakwira zvakanyanya kupfuura yemamwe macompound akafanana sehydrogen sulfide. Vanotsanangurawo simba rayo repamusoro rakasiyana-siyana rekupisa (inenge 4.2 J/(g·K)), kupisa kwemusanganiswa (inenge 333 J/g), kupisa kwemhepo (2257 J/g), uye thermal conductivity (pakati pe0.561 ne0.679 W/(m·K)). Zvinhu izvi zvinoita kuti mvura ishande pakuenzanisa mamiriro ekunze ePasi, nekuchengetedza kupisa nekuitakura pakati pemakungwa nemhepo. Iyo hydrogen zvisungo zvemvura zvakatenderedza 23 kJ/mol (zvichienzaniswa necovalent O-H bond pa492 kJ/mol). Pane izvi, zvinofungidzirwa kuti 90% inokonzerwa ne electrostatics, nepo 10% yasara ine chikamu covalent. Zvisungo izvi ndizvo zvinokonzeresa tension yepamusoro yemvura uye capillary forces. Capillary action (Capillary action) zvinoreva katsika kekukwira kwemvura pachubhu yakamanikana ichipikisana nesimba regiravhiti. Ichi chivakwa chinovimbwa nacho nechero vascular zvirimwa, semiti. Self-ionization
Mvura igadziriso isina simba yehydronium hydroxide - kune yakaenzana 2H2O ⇌ H3O+ + OH-, pamwe nekugadziriswa kweiyo hydronium uye hydroxide ions. Electrical conductivity uye electrolysis
Mvura yakachena ine yakaderera magetsi conductivity, iyo inowedzera nekuparara kwezvinhu zvishoma zveionic zvakadai semunyu wakajairika. Mvura yemvura inogona kupatsanurwa kuita zvinhu zvehaidhirojeni neokisijeni kuburikidza nekupfuura magetsi emagetsi kuburikidza nayo-maitiro anonzi electrolysis. Kuora kunoda simba rakawanda kupfuura kupisa kunoburitswa neinverse process (285.8 kJ/mol, kana 15.9 MJ/kg). Mechanical properties
Mvura yemvura inogona kufungidzirwa kuva isingachinjiki nokuda kwezvinangwa zvakawanda: compressibility yayo inotangira kubva ku4.4 kusvika ku5.1×10−10 Pa−1 mumamiriro ezvinhu akajairwa. Kunyangwe mumakungwa pakadzika makiromita mana, uko kudzvanywa kuri 400 atm, mvura inotambura chete ne1.8% kuderera kwehuwandu. The viscosity yemvura ingangoita 10−3 Pa·s kana 0.01 poise pa20 °C (68 °F), uye kumhanya kweruzha mumvura iri mvura kunosvika pakati pe1,400 ne1,540 metres pasekondi (4,600 uye 5,100 ft/s) zvichienderana nekupisa. Mutsindo unofamba nzendo refu mumvura isina kudzikisira, kunyanya kune yakaderera frequencies (inenge 0.03 dB/km ye 1 kHz), chivakwa chinoshandiswa nemacetaceans uye vanhu kutaurirana uye nharaunda inonzwa (sonar). Reactivity
Metallic zvinhu izvo zvine electropositive kupfuura hydrogen, kunyanya simbi dzealkali uye, kusvika pamwero mudiki, alkaline pasi simbi, inobvisa haidhirojeni kubva mumvura, ichigadzira hydroxides uye kuburitsa haidrojeni. Pakupisa kwakanyanya, coke, imwe kabhoni, inopindirana nechiutsi kuita kabhoni monoxide nehydrogen. Panyika
Hydrology chidzidzo chekufamba, kugovera, uye kunaka kwemvura muPasi rose. Chidzidzo chekugoverwa kwemvura ndeye hydrography. Chidzidzo chekugoverwa uye kufamba kwemvura yepasi ndeye hydrogeology, yeglaciers ndiyo glaciology, yemvura yemukati menyika ndeye limnology uye kugovera kwemakungwa ioceanography. Ecological maitiro ane hydrology ari mukutarisa ecohydrology. Huwandu hwemvura hunowanikwa pamusoro, pasi, nepamusoro penyika inonzi hydrosphere. Huwandu hwemvura yepasi (huwandu hwemvura yepasirese) i1.386 bhiriyoni cubic kilometres (333 miriyoni cubic mamaira). Mvura yemvura inowanikwa mumitumbi yemvura, yakadai segungwa, gungwa, gungwa, rwizi, rukova, mugero, dziva, kana dhamu. Ruzhinji rwemvura paPasi imvura yegungwa. Mvura inowanikwawo mumhepo mumhepo yakasimba, mvura, uye mhute. Inowanikwawo semvura yepasi mumigero yemvura. Mvura yakakosha mune zvakawanda zve geological process. Mvura yepasi iripo mumatombo mazhinji, uye kudzvanywa kwemvura yepasi iyi kunokanganisa maitiro ekukanganisa. Mvura iri mujasi ndiyo inokonzera kunyunguduka kunoburitsa makomo anoputika munzvimbo dzepasi. Pamusoro pePasi, mvura yakakosha mune zvese zvemakemikari uye zvemuviri maitiro emamiriro ekunze. Mvura, uye kune zvishoma asi zvichiri kukosha, aizi, zvakare inokonzera huwandu hukuru hwekutakura sediment kunoitika pamusoro penyika. Deposition of sediment inotakurwa inoumba akawanda marudzi e sedimentary matombo, ayo anoumba geologic rekodhi yePasi nhoroondo. Kutenderera kwemvura
Kutenderera kwemvura (kunozivikanwa nesainzi se hydrologic cycle) ndiko kuenderera kwekuchinjana kwemvura mukati meiyo hydrosphere, pakati pemhepo, mvura yevhu, pamusoro pemvura, pasi pevhu, uye zvidyarwa. Mvura inofamba nekusingaperi kuburikidza neimwe yenzvimbo idzi mukufamba kwemvura kunosanganisira maitiro ekufambisa anotevera:

kufefeterwa kubva mumakungwa nemimwe mitumbi yemvura kupinda mumhepo uye kubuda mumhepo kubva muzvirimwa nemhuka. kunaya, kubva pamhute yemvura inomonereka kubva kumhepo ichiwira pasi kana munyanza. kuyerera kunobva panyika kunowanzosvika kugungwa. Mvura zhinji inowanikwa munyanza inodzokera kwairi, asi mhepo dzinotakura mhute yemvura pamusoro penyika pamwero wakafanana nekuyerera kwemvura mugungwa, ingangoita 47 Tt pagore ukuwo evaporation uye kubuda kwemhepo kunoitika muhuwandu hwepasi kunopawo imwe 72 Tt pagore. Kunaya kwemvura, pamwero we119 Tt pagore pamusoro penyika, kune marudzi akati wandei: kazhinji mvura, sinou, uye chimvuramabwe, pamwe nemupiro wemhute nedova. Dova madonhwe madiki emvura akakonyedzwa kana mhute yakakwira yasangana nenzvimbo inotonhorera. Dova rinowanzovapo mangwanani apo tembiricha inenge yakadzikira, zuva richangobuda uye apo tembiricha yepasi panotanga kuwedzera. Mvura yakakomberedzwa iri mumhepo inogonawo kufuratira chiedza chezuva kuburitsa miraraungu. Mvura inoyerera kazhinji inoungana pamusoro pemvura inoyerera munzizi. Kuburikidza nekukukurwa kwevhu, mvura inoyerera inoumba nharaunda ichigadzira mipata yenzizi nemadelta izvo zvinopa ivhu rakapfuma uye nzvimbo yakaenzana yekutangwa kwenzvimbo dzevagari vemo. Mafashamo anoitika kana nzvimbo yenyika, kazhinji yakadzikira, yakafukidzwa nemvura iyo inoitika kana rwizi rukapfachukira mahombekombe arwo kana dutu remhepo. Ukuwo, kusanaya kwemvura inguva yakawedzerwa yemwedzi kana makore apo nzvimbo inoona kushomeka kwemvura yayo. Izvi zvinoitika kana dunhu richigashira kunaya kweavhareji iri pasi peavhareji nekuda kwenzvimbo yayo kana nekuda kwenzvimbo yayo maererano nelatitude. Zviwanikwa zvemvura
Zviwanikwa zvemvura zviwanikwa zvemvura zvinogona kubatsira vanhu, semuenzaniso senzvimbo yekunwa mvura yekunwa kana yekudiridza. Mvura inowanikwa se "stock" uye "inoyerera". Mvura inogona kuchengetwa semadziva, mhute yemvura, mvura yepasi pevhu kana aquifers, uye ice nesinou. Pahuwandu hwehuwandu hwemvura yakachena yepasi rose, inofungidzirwa kuti 69 muzana yakachengetwa mumatope echando uye muvharo yechando yekusingaperi; 30 muzana iri mumvura yepasi; uye 1 muzana yasara mumadziva, nzizi, mumhepo, uye biota. Urefu hwenguva iyo mvura inoramba ichichengeterwa inosiyana-siyana: mamwe matsime ane mvura yakachengetwa kwezviuru zvemakore, asi uwandu hwemadhamu hunogona kuchinja-chinja maererano nemwaka, huchiderera munguva yakaoma uye kuwedzera munguva dzekunaya. Chikamu chakakura chekupihwa kwemvura kune mamwe matunhu chine mvura inotorwa kubva kumvura yakachengetwa mumasheya, uye kana kubvisa kwapfuura kudzoreredzwa, masheya anodzikira. Nekumwe fungidziro, inosvika zvikamu makumi matatu kubva muzana zvemvura yese inoshandiswa pakudiridza inobva mukubviswa kusingagadzirisike kwemvura yepasi, zvichikonzera kupera kwemvura yepasi. Mvura yegungwa nemafungu
Mvura yegungwa ine inenge 3.5% sodium chloride paavhareji, pamwe netumwe tumwe tunhu. Hunhu hwemvura yegungwa hunosiyana nemvura yakachena mune zvimwe zvinhu zvakakosha. Inotonhora patembiricha yakaderera (inenge −1.9 °C (28.6 °F)) uye density yaro inowedzera nekuderera kwetembiricha kusvika painotonhora, pane kuti isvike pakunyanya kusimba pane tembiricha iri pamusoro pechando. Munyu wemvura mumakungwa makuru unosiyana kubva pa0.7% muGungwa reBaltic kusvika pa4.0% muGungwa Dzvuku. (Gungwa Rakafa, rinozivikanwa nepamusoro-soro mazinga emunyu ari pakati pe30 ne40%, igungwa remunyu chairo.)
Mafungu ndiko kukwira nekudonha kwemazinga emugungwa emunharaunda kunokonzerwa nemhepo ine simba reMwedzi neZuva rinoshanda pamakungwa. Mafungu anokonzera shanduko mukudzika kwemvura yemugungwa neyemumvura uye anoburitsa mafungu anotenderera anozivikanwa sehova dzemvura. Kuchinja kwemafungu anogadzirwa panzvimbo yakapihwa mhedzisiro yekuchinja kwenzvimbo dzeMwedzi neZuva maererano nePasi pamwe nemhedzisiro yekutenderera kwePasi uye yenzvimbo yekugezera. Mutsara wemahombekombe egungwa unonyudzwa pamvura zhinji uye unoratidzwa pamvura yakadzika, iyo intertidal zone, chinhu chakakosha chezvisikwa zvemafungu egungwa. Migumisiro pahupenyu
Kubva pakuona kwebiology, mvura ine zvinhu zvakawanda zvakasiyana izvo zvakakosha pakuwanda kwehupenyu. Inoita basa iri nekubvumira organic komisheni kuita nenzira dzinozobvumira kudzokorora. Mhando dzose dzehupenyu dzinozivikanwa dzinotsamira pamvura. Mvura yakakosha zvese semunyungudusi umo mizhinji yezvinyoro zvemuviri zvinonyungudika uye sechikamu chakakosha chemaitiro mazhinji emetabolism mukati memuviri. Metabolism ndiyo hwerengedzo yeanabolism uye catabolism. Muanabolism, mvura inobviswa kubva kumamorekuru (kuburikidza nesimba rinoda enzymatic chemical reactions) kukura mamorekuru makuru (e.g., starches, triglycerides, and proteins for storage of fuels and information). Mu catabolism, mvura inoshandiswa kutyora zvisungo kuti igadzire mamorekuru madiki (semuenzaniso, glucose, mafuta acids, uye amino acids kuti ashandiswe kuhuturu hwekushandisa simba kana zvimwe zvinangwa). Pasina mvura, aya ma metabolic maitiro haagone kuvapo. Mvura inokosha kune zvose zviri zviviri photosynthesis uye kufema. Masero e<em>photosynthetic anoshandisa simba rezuva kupatsanura haidrojeni yemvura kubva muokisijeni. Mukuvapo kwechiedza chezuva, haidrojeni inosanganiswa neCO2 (inotorwa kubva mumhepo kana mumvura) kugadzira shuga uye kuburitsa okisijeni. Maseru mapenyu ese anoshandisa mafuta akadaro uye oxidize iyo haidrojeni nekabhoni kutora simba rezuva uye kugadzirisa mvura uye CO2 mukuita (cellular respiration). Mvura zvakare iri pakati pe acid-base kusarerekera uye enzyme basa. Asidhi, hydrogen ion (H +, kureva, proton) donor, inogona kuderedzwa nenheyo, proton inogamuchirwa yakadai se hydroxide ion (OH-) kugadzira mvura. Mvura inoonekwa seisina kwayakarerekera, ine pH (yakashata log yehydrogen ion concentration) ye7 munzvimbo yakanaka. Acids ine pH values isingasviki 7 ukuwo mabhesi ane ma values anopfuura 7. Aquatic life forms
Mvura iri pamusoro penyika izere nehupenyu. Zvimiro zvepakutanga zvehupenyu zvakaonekwa mumvura; dzinenge hove dzose dzinogara mumvura chete, uye kune marudzi akawanda emhuka dzomugungwa, dzakadai sema<em>dolphin ne<em>whale. Dzimwe mhando dzemhuka, dzakaita semhuka dzinonzi amphibians, dzinopedza chikamu chehupenyu hwadzo mumvura uye dzimwe dziri panyika. Zvirimwa zvakaita sekelp nealgae zvinokura mumvura uye ndidzo hwaro hwezvimwe zvipenyu zvepasi pemvura. Plankton kazhinji ndiyo nheyo yegungwa rekudya cheni. Mhuka dzemumvura dzinofanirwa kuwana oxygen kuti dzirarame, uye dzinozviita nenzira dzakasiyana. Hove dzine magill pachinzvimbo chemapapu, kunyangwe mamwe marudzi ehove, senge lungfish, ane ese. Mhuka dzemugungwa, dzakadai sema dolphin, whales, otters, uye zvisimbiso zvinoda kuvhurika nguva nenguva kuti zvifeme mweya. Mamwe maamphibians anokwanisa kutora oxygen kuburikidza neganda ravo. Invertebrates inoratidza huwandu hwakawanda hwekugadzirisa kuti urarame mumvura isina oksijeni ine mweya inosanganisira kufema machubhu (ona zvipembenene uye mollusc siphons) uye gills (Carcinus). Nekudaro, sehupenyu hweinvertebrate hwakashanduka munzvimbo inogara mumvura, vazhinji vane zvishoma kana kusaziva nezvekufema mumvura. Migumisiro pakubudirira kwevanhu
Budiriro yakabudirira munhoroondo yakapoteredza nzizi uye nzira huru dzemvura; Mesopotamia, imwe yeinonzi mavambo ebudiriro, yakanga iri pakati penzizi huru Tigirisi naYufratesi; nzanga yekare yavaEgipita yaitsamira chose chose paNairi. Kubudirira kwekutanga kweIndus Valley (c. 3300 BCE - munenge muna 1300 BCE) yakatanga pedyo neRwizi rweIndus uye nzizi dzaiyerera dzichibuda muHimalayas. Roma yakavambwawo pamahombekombe erwizi rweItaly Tiber. Maguta makuru akaita seRotterdam, London, Montreal, Paris, New York City, Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Tokyo, Chicago, neHong Kong ane mukana wekubudirira kwawo muchikamu chekuwanikwa kwawo nyore nemvura uye kuwedzera kwekutengeserana. Zvitsuwa zvine zviteshi zvemvura zvakachengeteka, seSingapore, zvakabudirira nechikonzero chimwe chete. Munzvimbo dzakadai seNorth Africa neMiddle East, uko mvura iri kushomeka zvikuru, kuwana mvura yokunwa yakachena kwaiva uye ndicho chinhu chikuru mukukura kwevanhu. Utano uye kusvibiswa
Mvura yakafanira kunwa nevanhu inonzi mvura yekunwa kana kuti mvura yekunwa. Mvura isinganwike inogona kugadzirwa nekusefa kana kudistillation, kana nedzimwe nzira dzakasiyana. Vanhu vanodarika mamiriyoni mazana matanhatu nemakumi matanhatu havawani mvura yekunwa yakachena. Mvura isingakodzeri kunwa asi isingakuvadzi vanhu kana ichituhwina kana kugeza inodanwa nemazita akasiyana asiri emvura yekunwa kana yekunwa, uye dzimwe nguva inodaidzwa kuti mvura yakachena, kana kuti “safe for kugeza”. Chlorine iganda uye mucous membrane inoshatisa iyo inoshandiswa kuita kuti mvura ive isina njodzi pakugeza kana kunwa. Kushandiswa kwayo kune hunyanzvi hwepamusoro uye kunowanzotariswa nemirairo yehurumende (kazhinji chikamu chimwe pamiriyoni (ppm) yemvura yekunwa, uye 1–2 ppm yeklorini isati yaitwa netsvina yemvura yekugeza). Mvura yekugeza inogona kuchengetedzwa mune inogutsa microbiological mamiriro uchishandisa makemikari ekuuraya utachiona senge chlorine kana ozone kana nekushandisa ultraviolet mwenje. Kudzoreredza kwemvura inzira yekushandura mvura yetsvina (inowanzo tsvina, inodaidzwawo kuti marara ekanzuru) kuita mvura inogona kushandiswazve kune zvimwe zvinangwa. Kune vanhu 2.3 bhiriyoni vanogara munyika dzine dambudziko remvura, zvinoreva kuti munhu wega wega anogamuchira isingasviki 1,700 cubic metres (60,000 cu ft) yemvura pagore. 380 bhiriyoni cubic metres (13 × 10 ^ 12 cu ft) yemvura yetsvina yemanisipala inogadzirwa pasi rose gore rega rega. Mvura yakachena inzvimbo inogona kudzokororwa, yakadzokororwa neyechisikigo hydrologic kutenderera, asi kudzvanywa pamusoro pekuiwana kunokonzerwa nekusaenzana kwechisikigo kugovaniswa munzvimbo nenguva, kukura kwehupfumi kuri kudiwa nekurima neindasitiri, uye kukwira kwevanhu. Pari zvino, vanhu vanoda kusvika bhiriyoni pasi rose havawani mvura yakachena, isingadhuri. Muna 2000, United Nations yakagadzira Zvinangwa zveMillennium Development Goals kuti mvura ipere nepakati panosvika gore ra2015 huwandu hwevanhu pasi rose vasina kuwana mvura yakachena nehutsanana. Kufambira mberi kwechinangwa ichocho kwakanga kusina kuenzana, uye muna 2015 UN yakazvipira kuZvinangwa zveSustainable Development zvekuwana kuwaniswa kwepasi rose kwemvura yakachena uye isingadhuri pamwe neutsanana panosvika gore ra2030. Kushata kwemvura nehutsanana hwakaipa zvinouraya; kufa kwemamiriyoni mashanu pagore kunokonzerwa nezvirwere zvinokonzerwa nemvura. Sangano reWorld Health Organization rinoti mvura yakachena inogona kudzivirira vana vanosvika miriyoni imwe nemazana mana vanofa nemanyoka gore negore. Munyika dzichiri kusimukira, 90% yemvura yetsvina yemanisipala ichiri kuenda isina kucheneswa munzizi nehova dzemuno. Nyika dzinenge 50, dzine chinenge chikamu chimwe muzvitatu chevagari vepasi, dzinotamburawo nekushaikwa kwemvura iri pakati nepakati kana kuti yakawanda uye 17 dzacho dzinotora mvura yakawanda gore negore kupfuura inochajiswa nemvura yadzo chaiyo. Kuomarara uku hakungokanganisa mitumbi yemvura yakachena pamusoro senzizi nemadziva, asi kunoshatisa zviwanikwa zvemvura yepasi. Zvishandiso zvevanhu
Agriculture
Kunyanya kushandiswa kwevanhu kwemvura ndeyekurima, kusanganisira kurima kwekudiridza, uko kunotora chikamu che80 kusvika makumi mapfumbamwe kubva muzana chehuwandu hwemvura inoshandiswa nevanhu. MuUnited States, 42% yemvura yakachena yakabviswa kuti ishandiswe ndeye kudiridza, asi yakawanda yemvura "inodyiwa" (inoshandiswa uye isingadzokeri kune zvakatipoteredza) inoenda kune zvekurima. Kuwanikwa kwemvura yakachena kunowanzotorwa sekureruka, kunyanya munyika dzakabudirira dzakavaka masisitimu emvura akaomesesa ekuunganidza, kuchenesa, uye kuendesa mvura, uye kubvisa tsvina. Asi kukura kwehupfumi, huwandu hwevanhu, uye mamiriro ekunze kuri kuwedzera kushushikana pamusoro penyaya dzemvura, zvichikonzera kuwedzera kukwikwidza kwezviwanikwa zvemvura zvakagadziriswa, zvichipa pfungwa yemvura yepamusoro. Sezvo huwandu hwevanhu nehupfumi huri kuramba huchikura, kudyiwa kwenyama ine nyota yemvura kunowedzera, uye zvinodiwa zvitsva zvinokwira zvebiofuels kana maindasitiri matsva anoshandisa mvura, matambudziko matsva emvura anogona kuitika. Ongororo yekutonga kwemvura mune zvekurima yakaitwa muna 2007 neInternational Water Management Institute muSri Lanka kuona kana nyika ine mvura yakakwana yekupa chikafu chevanhu vari kukura. Yakaongorora kuwanikwa kwemvura yekurima pari zvino pasi rose uye yakaronga nzvimbo dzine dambudziko rekushaikwa kwemvura. Yakawana kuti chikamu chimwe muzvishanu chevanhu venyika, vanopfuura bhiriyoni 1,2, vanogara munzvimbo dzine kushayikwa kwemvura chaiyo, uko kusina mvura yakakwana yokuzadzisa zvose zvinodiwa. Vamwe vanhu bhiriyoni 1,6 vanogara munzvimbo dzine kushomeka kwemvura mune zveupfumi, uko kushomeka kwemari mumvura kana kusakwana kwevanhu kunoita kuti zvitadze kugutsa kudiwa kwemvura. Mushumo wacho wakaona kuti zvaizoita kuti pave nezvokudya zvinodikanwa munguva yemberi, asi kuti kupfuuridzira kugadzirwa kwezvokudya nhasi uye miitiro yemhoteredzo kwaizotungamirira kunhamo mumativi akawanda enyika. Kuti vadzivise dambudziko remvura repasi rose, varimi vachafanira kuedza kuwedzera goho kuti vawane zvokudya zvinokura, nepo maindasitiri nemaguta vachiwana nzira dzokushandisa mvura zvakanaka. Kushaikwa kwemvura kunokonzerwawo nekugadzirwa kwezvinhu zvinoshandisa mvura zvakanyanya. Semuenzaniso, donje: 1 kg yekotoni-yakaenzana nepeya yejini-inoda 10.9 cubic metres (380 cu ft) mvura kugadzira. Kunyange zvazvo donje iine 2.4% yemvura inoshandiswa pasi rose, mvura inopedzwa mumatunhu atova pangozi yekushaya mvura. Kukuvadzwa kukuru kwemhoteredzo kwakakonzerwa: semuenzaniso, kutsauswa kwemvura neyaichimbova Soviet Union kubva munzizi dzeAmu Darya neSyr Darya kuti ibudise donje ndiko kwakakonzera kutsakatika kweGungwa reAral. Semwero wesainzi
Musi wa7 Kubvumbi 1795, giramu rakatsanangurwa muFrance kuti rakaenzana ne "huremu hwakakwana hwehuwandu hwemvura yakachena yakaenzana necube yezana remamita, uye pakupisa kwekunyunguduka kwechando". Nekuda kwezvishandiso zvakadaro, simbi yereferensi yaidiwa, yakapetwa kachuru, iyo kilogram. Saka basa rakapiwa kuti rinyatsoona kuwanda kwerita imwe chete yemvura. Zvisinei nekuti tsananguro yegiremu yakataura mvura iri pa 0 ° C (32 ° F) - tembiricha inogona kuberekana zvakanyanya - masayendisiti akasarudza kutsanangudza muyero uye kuita zviyero zvavo patembiricha yemvura yakanyanya kuwanda, iyo yakayerwa panguva iyoyo se4 ° C (39 ° F). Iyo Kelvin tembiricha yechiyero cheSI system yaive yakavakirwa pane katatu poindi yemvura, inotsanangurwa seyakakwana 273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F), asi kubva muna Chivabvu 2019 yakavakirwa paBoltzmann nguva dzose panzvimbo. Chikero chikero chetembiricha chakazara chine mwero wakafanana nechiyero chetembiricha yeCelsius, iyo yakatanga kutsanangurwa maererano nenzvimbo yekuvira (yakaiswa ku100 °C (212 °F)) nenzvimbo yekunyungudika (yakaiswa ku0 °C (32 °F)) yemvura. Mvura yechisikigo inosanganisira kunyanya isotopes hydrogen-1 uye oxygen-16, asi kunewo huwandu hushoma hwemasotopi anorema okisijeni-18, okisijeni-17, uye hydrogen-2 (deuterium). Iyo muzana yeinorema isotopes idiki kwazvo, asi ichiri kukanganisa maitiro emvura. Mvura inobva munzizi nemadziva inenge iine isotopes shoma shoma kupfuura mvura yegungwa. Naizvozvo, mvura yakajairika inotsanangurwa muVienna Standard Mean Ocean Mvura yakatarwa. Zvekunwa
Muviri wemunhu une, paavhareji, 50-60% yemvura, zvichienderana nezera, murume kana mukadzi uye saizi yemuviri, kunyangwe vanhu vangave chero pakati pe45% ne75%. U.S. National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine inokurudzira kunwa kwezuva nezuva kwemarita 3.7 (0.98 U.S. magaloni) emvura kuvarume vakuru uye 2.7 L (0.71 U.S. gal) kuvakadzi. Iyo chaiyo huwandu hunoenderana nehuwandu hwekuita, tembiricha, humidity, uye zvimwe zvinhu. Zvizhinji zveizvi zvinopinzwa kuburikidza nekudya kana zvinwiwa kunze kwekunwa mvura yakatwasuka. Mabhuku ezvokurapa anofarira kunwa kwakaderera, kazhinji rita remvura kumunhurume wepakati, kusasanganisa zvimwe zvinodiwa nekuda kwekurasikirwa kwemvura kubva mukurovedza muviri kana kudziya kwekunze. Tsvo dzine hutano dzinogona kuburitsa 0.8 kusvika 1 litre yemvura paawa, asi kushushikana kwakadai sekurovedza muviri kunogona kuderedza huwandu uhu. Vanhu vanogona kunwa mvura yakawanda kupfuura inodiwa pavanenge vachisimbisa muviri, zvichivaisa panjodzi yekudhakwa nemvura (hyperhydration), iyo inogona kuuraya. Kutaura kwakakurumbira kwokuti “munhu anofanira kunwa magirazi masere emvura pazuva” kunoratidzika kuva kusina hwaro chaihwo musayenzi. Zvidzidzo zvakaratidza kuti kuwedzera kwemvura kunwa, kunyanya kusvika ku500 milliliters (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) panguva yekudya, yakabatana nekurasikirwa uremu. Kunwa mvura yakakwana kunobatsira mukudzivirira kuvharwa. Zvizhinji zvehuwandu uhwu huri muzvokudya zvakagadzirwa." Chirevo chemazuva ano chekudya chekudya cheUS National Research Council chakakurudzirwa, zvichibva pahuwandu hwemvura inotorwa kubva kuUS data data (kusanganisira chikafu): 3.7 litres (0.81 imp gal; 0.98 US gal) yevarume uye 2.7 litres (0.59 imp gal; 0.71 yemvura inokwana 19% yemvura inopihwa nevakadzi gumi nepfumbamwe). yehuwandu hwemvura inotorwa muongororo. Kunyanya, vakadzi vane pamuviri uye vanoyamwisa vanoda mamwe mvura kuti varambe vaine hydrated. US Institute of Medicine inokurudzira kuti, paavhareji, varume vadye 3 litres (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) uye vakadzi 2.2 litres (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); vakadzi vane pamuviri vanofanira kuwedzera kunwa kusvika ku2,4 litres (0.53 imp gal; 0.63 US gal) uye vakadzi vanoyamwisa vanofanira kuwana marita matatu (12 makapu), sezvo mvura yakawanda inorasika panguva yekuyamwisa. Zvakare zvinocherechedzwa ndezvekuti kazhinji, inenge 20% yemvura yekunwa inobva muchikafu, nepo imwe yese inobva kumvura yekunwa uye zvinwiwa (caffeinated inosanganisirwa). Mvura inobudiswa kubva mumuviri nenzira dzakawanda; nemuweti netsvina, nekudikitira, uye nekufemerwa kwemhute yemvura mukufema. Nekushanda kwemuviri uye kuratidzwa kwekupisa, kurasikirwa kwemvura kuchawedzera uye zvinodikanwa zvemazuva ese zvemvura zvinogona kuwedzera zvakare. Vanhu vanoda mvura ine tsvina shoma. Kusachena kwakajairika kunosanganisira simbi dzakaita semhangura nemutobvu; makemikari emakemikari akadai semishonga yezvipembenene, PFAS, kana bleach; uye mabhakitiriya anokuvadza, akadai seCampylobacter, E. coli O157, uye Vibrio. Mamwe ma solutes anogamuchirwa uye anotodiwa pakuvandudza kuravira uye kupa ma electrolyte anodiwa. Iyo imwe chete yakakura (nehuwandu) yemvura yakachena yakakodzera kunwa iLake Baikal kuSiberia. Kushandiswa kwemakemikari
Mvura inoshandiswa zvakanyanya mukuita kwemakemikari semushonga kana reactant uye isingawanzo kuita solute kana catalyst. Mukuita kwekusagadzikana, mvura inonyungudutsa, inonyungudutsa akawanda eionic komisheni, pamwe nemamwe macompounds epolar akadai seammonia uye makomisheni ane hukama zvakanyanya nemvura. Mune organic reactions, haiwanzo shandiswa seyakagadziriswa solvent, nekuti haina kunyungudutsa reactants zvakanaka uye ndeye amphoteric (acidic uye basic) uye nucleophilic. Zvakadaro, zvinhu izvi dzimwe nguva zvinoshuviwa. Zvakare, kukurumidza kweDiels-Alder maitiro nemvura kwaonekwa. Supercritical mvura ichangobva kuve musoro wekutsvaga. Oxygen-saturated supercritical water inopisa zvinosvibisa organic zvakanaka. Kuchinjana kupisa
Mvura nemhumhu imvura yakajairika inoshandiswa mukutsinhana kupisa, nekuda kwekuvapo kwayo uye nekupisa kwakanyanya, zvese pakutonhodza nekudziisa. Mvura inotonhorera inogona kunge ichiwanikwa kubva mudhamu kana mugungwa. Inonyanya kushanda kutakura kupisa kuburikidza ne vaporization uye condensation yemvura nekuda kwehukuru hwayo hunopisa hwehupfu. Chisina kunaka ndechekuti masimbi anowanzo kuwanikwa mumaindasitiri akadai sesimbi nemhangura anoiswa oxidized nekukurumidza nemvura isina kucheneswa uye chiutsi. Munenge mune zvese zviteshi zvemagetsi zvinopisa, mvura inoshandiswa semvura inoshanda (inoshandiswa pakavharika-loop pakati pebhailer, steam turbine, uye condenser), uye inotonhodza (inoshandiswa kuchinjanisa kupisa kwetsvina kumuviri wemvura kana kuitakura nekubuda mushongwe inotonhorera). MuUnited States, zvidyarwa zvemagetsi ekutonhodza ndiko kushandiswa kukuru kwemvura. Muindasitiri yemagetsi enyukireya, mvura inogona zvakare kushandiswa seneutron moderator. Mumareactor mazhinji enyukireya, mvura inotonhodza uye ine mutarisiri. Izvi zvinopa chimwe chinhu cheyero yekudzivirira, sezvo kubvisa mvura kubva mureactor zvakare kunonotsa kuita kwenyukireya pasi. Zvakadaro dzimwe nzira dzinofarirwa pakumisa kuita uye zvinofarirwa kuchengeta musimboti wenyukireya wakavharwa nemvura kuitira kuti ive nechokwadi chekutonhodza zvakakwana. Mafungiro emoto
Mvura ine kupisa kwakanyanya kwemhupo uye haina kudzika, izvo zvinoita kuti ive mvura yakanaka yekudzima moto. Kubuda kwemvura kunotakura kupisa kure nemoto. Zvine njodzi kushandisa mvura pamoto unosanganisira oiri nezvinonyungudutsa nekuti zvinhu zvakawanda zveorganic zvinoyangarara pamusoro pemvura uye mvura inotaridza kuparadzira mvura inopisa. Kushandiswa kwemvura mukurwisa moto kunofanirawo kufunga nezvenjodzi dzekuputika kwemhepo, iyo inogona kuitika kana mvura inoshandiswa pamoto unopisa zvikuru munzvimbo dzakavharwa, uye yekuputika kwehydrogen, apo zvinhu zvinosangana nemvura, zvakadai semamwe simbi kana kabhoni inopisa semarasha, marasha, kana coke graphite, inoora mvura, ichibudisa gasi remvura. Simba rekuputika kwakadaro rakaonekwa munjodzi yeChernobyl, kunyange zvazvo mvura inobatanidzwa munyaya iyi haina kubva pakupisa moto asi kubva kune reactor pachake mvura inotonhorera. Kuputika kwechitsinga kwakaitika apo kupisa kwakanyanya kwepakati kwakakonzera mvura kupenya muutsi. Kuputika kwehydrogen kunogona kunge kwakaitika semugumisiro wekuita pakati pemhepo inopisa uye zirconium inopisa. Mamwe masimbi oxides, kunyanya ayo ealkali metals uye alkaline earth metals, anogadzira kupisa kwakawanda kunoenderana nemvura zvekuti njodzi yemoto inogona kuitika. Iyo alkaline earth oxide quicklime, inozivikanwawo se calcium oxide, chinhu chinogadzirwa-zhinji chinowanzotakurwa mumabhegi emapepa. Kana izvi zvakanyoroveswa, zvinogona kupfuta sezvo zviri mukati mazvo zvinopinda mumvura. Varaidzo
Vanhu vanoshandisa mvura kuita zvinhu zvakawanda zvekutandara, pamwe nekuita maekisesaizi uye kuita zvemitambo. Zvimwe zveizvi zvinosanganisira kushambira, waterskiing, boating, surfing uye diving. Pamusoro pezvo, mimwe mitambo, senge ice hockey uye ice skating, inoridzwa paaizi. Lakesides, mahombekombe uye mapaki emvura inzvimbo dzakakurumbira dzekuti vanhu vaende kunozorora uye kunakidzwa nekutandara. Vazhinji vanowana ruzha uye kutaridzika kwemvura inoyerera kuve kudzikamisa, uye matsime uye mamwe anoyerera emvura zvimiro zvinoshongedzwa zvakakurumbira. Vamwe vanochengeta hove nezvimwe zvinomera nemhuka mukati memvura yainogara mumvura kana madziva kuitira shoo, mafaro, uye ushamwari. Vanhu vanoshandisawo mvura kumitambo yechando, yakaita seskiing, sledding, snowmobiling kana snowboarding, izvo zvinoda kuti mvura ive pakadzikama tembiricha ingave ice kana kupenya muchando. Indasitiri yemvura
Indasitiri yemvura inopa mvura yekunwa uye masevhisi emvura yetsvina (kusanganisira kuchenesa tsvina) kudzimba nemaindasitiri. Nzvimbo dzekupa mvura dzinosanganisira matsime emvura, matangi ekucheresa mvura inonaya, mambure emvura, nenzvimbo dzekuchenesa mvura, matangi emvura, shongwe dzemvura, pombi dzemvura dzinosanganisira migero yekare. Majenareta emvura emumhepo ari mukugadzirwa. Mvura yekunwa inowanzounganidzwa pazvitubu, inotorwa kubva mumatsime ekugadzira (matsime) ari pasi, kana kuti anopombwa kubva mumadziva nenzizi. Kuvaka matsime akawanda munzvimbo dzakaringana nokudaro inzira inobvira yokubudisa mvura yakawanda, tichifunga kuti matsime anogona kupa mvura inoyerera yakakwana. Mamwe matsime emvura anosanganisira kuunganidzwa kwemvura inonaya. Mvura inogona kuda kucheneswa kuti ishandiswe nevanhu. Izvi zvinogona kusanganisira kubviswa kwezvinhu zvisina kunyungudika, zvinhu zvakanyungudutswa uye utachiona hunokuvadza. Nzira dzine mukurumbira dziri kusefa nejecha rinongobvisa zvinhu zvisina kunyunguduka, ukuwo chlorination uye kufashaira zvichiuraya utachiona hunokuvadza. Distillation inoita mabasa ese ari matatu. Dzimwe nzira dzepamusoro dziripo, dzakadai sereverse osmosis. Kubvisa munyu wemvura yegungwa yakawanda ndiyo mhinduro inodhura inoshandiswa munzvimbo dzakaoma dzegungwa. Kugoverwa kwemvura yekunwa kunoitwa kuburikidza nemasisitimu emvura ekanzuru, kutakura matangi kana semvura yemubhodhoro. Hurumende dziri munyika dzakawanda dzine zvirongwa zvokugovera mvura kune vanotambura pasina muripo. Kuderedza kushandiswa nekushandisa yekunwa (inonwiwa) mvura chete yekunwa nevanhu ndeimwe sarudzo. Mune mamwe maguta akadai seHong Kong, mvura yegungwa inoshandiswa zvakanyanya mukugezesa zvimbuzi muguta rese kuchengetedza zviwanikwa zvemvura yakachena. Mvura inosvibisa ingava kushandiswa kusina kunaka kumwe chete kukuru kwemvura; kusvika pamwero wokuti chinhu chinosvibisa chinoganhurira kumwe kushandiswa kwemvura, inova kutambisa chitubu chacho, pasinei zvapo nezvinobatsira kumusvibisi. Kufanana nedzimwe mhando dzekusvibiswa, izvi hazvipinze yakajairwa accounting yemitengo yemusika, zvichitorwa sekunze izvo musika usingakwanise kuzvidavirira. Saka vamwe vanhu vanobhadhara mutengo wekusvibiswa kwemvura, nepo purofiti yemafemu akazvimirira isingagohwe kuvagari venzvimbo, vakawirwa nekusvibiswa uku. Mishonga inonwiwa nevanhu kazhinji inoguma yave mumvura uye inogona kukanganisa hupenyu hwemumvura kana ikaunganidza bioacumulate uye ikasaora. Mvura yemasupachena neyemaindasitiri inowanzocheneswa pazvicherwa zvekuchenesa mvura. Kuderedza kusvibiswa kwepamusoro pemvura kunogadziriswa kuburikidza nemhando dzakasiyana-siyana dzekudzivirira uye nzira dzokurapa. Industrial applications
Mazhinji maindasitiri maitiro anovimba nemabatiro ekushandisa makemikari akanyungudutswa mumvura, kumiswa kwezvakaoma mumvura slurries kana kushandisa mvura kunyungudutsa uye kubvisa zvinhu, kana kugeza zvigadzirwa kana kugadzira michina. Maitiro akadai semigodhi, makemikari pulping, pulp bleaching, kugadzira mapepa, kugadzira machira, kudhaya, kudhinda, uye kutonhora kwezvidyarwa zvemagetsi zvinoshandisa mvura yakawanda, inoda mvura yakatsaurirwa sosi, uye kazhinji inokonzeresa kusvibiswa kwemvura. Mvura inoshandiswa mukugadzira magetsi. Hydroelectricity imagetsi anowanikwa kubva muhydropower. Simba reHydroelectric rinobva mumvura inotyaira turbine yemvura yakabatana nejenareta. Hydroelectricity inodhura-yakaderera, isingasvibise, inovandudzwa simba sosi. Simba rinopihwa nekufamba kwemvura. Kazhinji dhamu rinovakwa parwizi, richigadzira dziva rekugadzira kumashure kwaro. Mvura inoyerera ichibuda mudhamu inomanikidzwa kuburikidza nematurbines anochinja majenareta. Mvura yakamanikidzwa inoshandiswa mukuputika kwemvura uye kucheka kwejeti yemvura. High pressure water pfuti inoshandiswa pakucheka chaiko. Inoshanda zvakanaka kwazvo, haina njodzi, uye haina kukuvadza kune zvakatipoteredza. Inoshandiswawo mukutonhodza kwemichina kudzivirira kupisa, kana kudzivirira masaha kubva pakupisa. Mvura inoshandiswawo mune dzakawanda maindasitiri maitiro nemichina, senge steam turbine uye heat exchanger, pamwe nekushandiswa kwayo semushonga wemakemikari. Kubuda kwemvura isina kucheneswa kubva mukushandiswa kwemaindasitiri ndiko kusvibiswa. Kusvibiswa kunosanganisira discharged solutes (chemical pollution) uye inobuda mvura inotonhorera (thermal pollution). Indasitiri inoda mvura yakachena kune akawanda maapplication uye inoshandisa akasiyana siyana ekuchenesa matekiniki ese ari mukupa mvura nekubuda. Dhijitari chikamu, kunyanya Artificial intelligence inoshandisa yakawanda yemvura, saka kuwedzera kweAI kunogona "kutyisidzira kuchengetedzwa kwemvura yepasirese neyenyika". Kugadziriswa kwezvokudya
Kufashaidza, kusvira, uye kusimira inzira dzakakurumbira dzekubikira dzinowanzoda kunyudza chikafu mumvura kana mamiriro ayo ane gasi, mupfu. Mvura inoshandiswawo pakusuka ndiro. Mvura zvakare inotamba mabasa mazhinji akakosha mukati memunda wesainzi yechikafu. Solutes yakaita semunyu neshuga inowanikwa mumvura inokanganisa chimiro chemvura. Kufashaira uye kutonhora kwemvura kunokanganiswa ne solutes, pamwe nekumanikidzwa kwemhepo, iyo inozokanganiswa nekukwirira. Mvura inofashaira patembiricha yakadzikira nekudzika kwemhepo kunoitika panzvimbo dzakakwirira. Mole imwe yesucrose (shuga) pakirogiramu yemvura inosimudza panovira mvura ne 0.51 °C (0.918 °F), uye mole imwe yemunyu pakg inosimudza nzvimbo yekuvira ne1.02 °C (1.836 °F); zvakafanana, kuwedzera kuwanda kwezvikamu zvakanyungudutswa kunoderedza kutonhora kwemvura. Solutes mumvura inokanganisawo kuita kwemvura kunokanganisa maitiro mazhinji emakemikari uye kukura kwehutachiona mukudya. Kuita kwemvura kunogona kutsanangurwa sereshiyo yehutsi hwekumanikidza kwemvura mune mhinduro kune mhute yekumanikidza yemvura yakachena. Solutes mumvura inoderedza basa remvura-izvi zvakakosha kuziva nokuti kukura kwebhakitiriya kwakawanda kunopera pamazinga akaderera emabasa emvura. Haisi chete kukura kwehutachiona kunokanganisa kuchengetedzwa kwechikafu, asiwo kuchengetedza uye hupenyu hwesherufu hwekudya. Kuoma kwemvura zvakare chinhu chakakosha mukugadzirisa chikafu uye chinogona kuchinjwa kana kurapwa nekushandisa kemikari ion exchange system. Inogona kukanganisa zvakanyanya kunaka kwechigadzirwa, pamwe nekutamba basa muhutsanana. Kuoma kwemvura kunorongedzerwa zvichienderana nekuwanda kwe calcium carbonate iyo mvura ine. Mvura inorongwa seyakapfava kana iine isingasviki 100 mg/L (UK) kana isingasviki 60 mg/L (US). Muchadenga
Mvura zhinji yomuchadenga inogadzirwa somugumisiro wokuumbwa kwenyeredzi. Kuumbwa kwenyeredzi kunoperekedzwa nemhepo ine simba kunze kwegasi neguruva. Kana uku kubuda kwezvinhu kuchizokanganisa gasi rakatenderedza, masaisai ekuvhundutsa anogadzirwa compress uye kupisa gasi. Mvura inocherechedzwa inokurumidza kugadzirwa mugasi rinodziya rakakora. Musi wa22 Chikunguru 2011, mushumo wakatsanangura kuwanikwa kwegore guru remhute yemvura ine "140 trillion yakapetwa mvura kupfuura makungwa ese ePasi asanganiswa" kutenderedza quasar inowanikwa mabhiriyoni gumi nemaviri echiedza makore kubva paNyika. Mvura uye kugara kwepasi
Kuvapo kwemvura yemvura, uye kusvika pamwero mudiki maitiro ayo ane gasi uye akasimba, paPasi akakosha pakuvapo kwehupenyu paPasi sezvatinozviziva. Nyika iri munzvimbo inogarwa neSolar System; dai yaive padyo zvishoma kana kure kure kubva kuZuva (anenge 5%, kana makiromita angangoita mamirioni masere), mamiriro anotendera kuti mafomu matatu avepo panguva imwe chete angadai asina mukana wekuvepo. Hukuru hwepasi hunoitawo basa: giravhiti rayo rinoibvumira kubata mhepo, kusanganisira mwando wemhepo. Mapuraneti madiki akaita seMars ane mhepo yakatetepa zvakanyanya kana isina. Upfu hwemvura uye mamwe magasi egreenhouse ari mumhepo anopa tembiricha inodzivirira (greenhouse effect) inobatsira kuchengetedza tembiricha yakadzikama kana iripo muhuwandu hwenguva dzose. Mamiriro akaita mvura pasi pano anoenderana nekutsikirira kwenzvimbo, uko kunoonekwa negiravhiti repasi. Kana nyika yakakura zvakakwana, mvura iri pairi inogona kuva yakasimba kunyange pakupisa kwepamusoro, nekuda kwekumanikidzwa kwepamusoro kunokonzerwa negiravhiti, sezvakaonekwa pane exoplanets Gliese 436 b uye GJ 1214 b. Mutemo, zvematongerwo enyika, uye dambudziko
Zvematongerwo enyika zvemvura izvematongerwo enyika zvinokanganiswa nemvura uye zviwanikwa zvemvura. Mvura, kunyanya mvura yakachena, isimba rekushandisa pasi rose uye chinhu chakakosha mukukonana kwakawanda kwezvematongerwo enyika. Zvinokonzera hutano kukanganisa uye kukanganisa kune zvipenyu zvakasiyana-siyana. Kuwanikwa kwemvura yakachena yekunwa kwave nani mukati memakumi emakore apfuura munharaunda dzese dzepasi, asi vanhu vangangosvika bhiriyoni imwe vachiri kushaya mvura yakachena uye vanopfuura bhiriyoni 2.5 havasati vawana hutsanana hwakakwana. Zvakadaro, vamwe vaongorori vanoti panosvika gore ra2025 vanhu vanopfuura hafu pasi rose vachange vakatarisana nedambudziko remvura. Mushumo, wakabudiswa muna Mbudzi 2009, unoratidza kuti panosvika gore ra2030, mune dzimwe nzvimbo dziri kusimukira pasi rose, kudiwa kwemvura kuchapfuura kugoverwa ne50%. Vanhu vanosvika bhiriyoni imwe nemazana matanhatu vakawana mvura yakachena kubva muna 1990. Huwandu hwevanhu vari munyika dzichiri kusimukira vane mvura yakachena hunonzi hwakavandudzika kubva pa30% muna 1970 kusvika pa71% muna 1990, 79% muna 2000, uye 84% muna 2004. Gwaro ra2006 rakati mvura yakaringana kuUnited Nations. nekusatungamirira zvakanaka uye nehuori. Pamusoro pezvo, zvirongwa zvepasi rose zvekuvandudza mashandiro ekupa rubatsiro, zvakaita seParis Declaration on Aid Effectiveness, hazvina kutorwa nevapi vechikamu chemvura nemazvo sezvavakaita mune zvedzidzo uye hutano, zvichisiya vanopa vazhinji vachishanda mumapurojekiti anopindirana uye hurumende dzinogamuchira dzisina simba rekuita. Vanyori ve2007 Comprehensive Assessment of Water Management in Agriculture vakati hutongi hwejekerere sechimwe chikonzero chekushaikwa kwemvura. Utongi hwemvura ndiyo seti yezvirongwa zvakarongeka uye zvisina kurongwa izvo sarudzo dzine chekuita nekutarisirwa kwemvura dzinoitwa. Hutongi hwemvura hwakanaka ndehwekuziva kuti ndeapi maitiro anoshanda zvakanyanya mune imwe mamiriro emuviri uye magariro nehupfumi. Zvikanganiso dzimwe nguva zvakaitwa nekuedza kushandisa 'zvirongwa' zvinoshanda munyika dzakasimukira mukusimudzira nzvimbo nemamiriro epasirese. Rwizi rweMekong mumwe muenzaniso; Ongororo yakaitwa neInternational Water Management Institute yemitemo munyika nhanhatu dzinovimba nerwizi rweMekong kuwana mvura yakawana kuti kuongororwa kwemutengo wakakwana uye kwakajeka uye kuongorora kukanganisa kwezvakatipoteredza zvaisawanzoitwa. Vakaonawo kuti mutemo wokuchera mvura weCambodia wakanga wakaoma zvikuru kupfuura zvawaifanira kuva. Muna 2004, sangano reUK rarity WaterAid rakashuma kuti mwana anofa masekonzi gumi nemashanu ega ega nezvirwere zvinokwanisa kudzivirirwa zvine chekuita nemvura, izvo zvinowanzoenderana nekushaikwa kwehutsanana hwakakwana. Kubva 2003, UN World Water Development Report, yakabudiswa neUNESCO World Water Assessment Programme, yakapa vanoita sarudzo nezvishandiso zvekugadzira mitemo inochengetedza mvura. Chirevo cha2023 chinoti vanhu mabhiriyoni maviri (26% yevagari) havawani mvura yekunwa uye 3.6 bhiriyoni (46%) havakwanise kuwana hutsanana hwakachengetedzeka. Vanhu vari mumaguta (2.4 bhiriyoni) vachasangana nekushomeka kwemvura panosvika gore ra2050. Kushaikwa kwemvura kwave kunonzi kwagara kuchiitika, nekuda kwekushandisa zvakanyanya uye kusvibiswa. Gwaro iri rinoti chikamu gumi kubva muzana chevanhu pasi rose vanogara munyika dzine dambudziko remvura rakawanda kana kuti rakakosha. Zvakadaro mumakore makumi mana apfuura, kushandiswa kwemvura kwakawedzera nekukomberedza 1% pagore, uye inotarisirwa kukura pamwero wakafanana kusvika 2050. Kubva 2000, mafashamo munzvimbo dzinopisa akawedzera kakapetwa kana, nepo mafashamo ari kuchamhembe kwepakati-latitudes akawedzera nechikamu che2.5. Mutengo wemafashama aya pakati pa2000 na2019 waive zviuru zana nemazana matanhatu nemakumi mashanu emamiriyoni ekufa. Masangano ane chekuita nekuchengetedzwa kwemvura anosanganisira International Water Association (IWA), WaterAid, Water 1st, neAmerican Water Resources Association. Sangano reInternational Water Management Institute rinoita mapurojekiti nedonzvo rekushandisa kutonga kwemvura zvine mutsindo kuderedza hurombo. Magungano ane chekuita nemvura anoti United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD), International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships, United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea and Ramsar Convention. Zuva reNyika reMvura rinoitwa musi wa22 Kurume uye World Oceans Day musi wa8 Chikumi. Muna 2026, United Nations yakazivisa kuti vanhu vapinda munguva ye "Mvura kubhuroka" nekuti anopfuura makumi mashanu muzana emadziva makuru akadzikira kubva kuma1990, 35% yematoro akanyangarika kubva 1970, 75% yehuwandu hwepasi rose inogara munyika dzisina kuchengetedzwa kwemvura, 4 bhiriyoni vanhu vanowana kushomeka kwemvura pamwedzi 7 pamwedzi. gore. Chitendero
Mvura inonzi inonatsa mumachechi akawanda. Zvitendero zvinosanganisa kugeza kwetsika (kushambidza) zvinosanganisira chiKristu, chiHindu, chiIslam, chiJudha, sangano reRastafari, Shinto, Taoism, uye Wicca. Kunyudzwa (kana kunyudzwa kana kushushwa) kwemunhu mumvura isakaramende repakati pechiKristu (parinonzi rubhabhatidzo); Ichowo chikamu chetsika yezvimwe zvitendero, zvinosanganisira Islam (Ghusl), Judaism (mikvah) uye Sikhism (Amrit Sanskar). Mukuwedzera, tsika yekugeza mumvura yakachena inoitirwa vakafa muzvitendero zvakawanda zvinosanganisira Islam nechiJudha. MuIslam minamato mishanu yezuva nezuva inogona kuitwa kazhinji mushure mekugeza dzimwe nhengo dzemuviri uchishandisa mvura yakachena (wudhu), kunze kwekunge mvura isipo (ona Tayammum). MuShinto, mvura inoshandiswa munenge miitiro yose yokuchenesa munhu kana kuti nzvimbo (somuenzaniso, mutsika yemisogi). MuchiKristu, mvura tsvene imvura yakacheneswa nemupristi nechinangwa chekubhabhatidzwa, kuropafadza vanhu, nzvimbo, uye zvinhu, kana senzira yekudzinga zvakaipa.
