Amanzi ayinhlanganisela ye-inorganic ne-chemical formula H2O. Kuyinto yamakhemikhali esobala, ayinambitheki, ayinaphunga, futhi cishe ayinambala. Iyingxenye eyinhloko yemifudlana yomhlaba, amachibi, nolwandle. Amanzi futhi awuketshezi lwazo zonke izinto eziphilayo ezaziwayo, lapho asebenza khona njengesincibilikisi. Amanzi, okuyi-molecule emhlophe, ahlangana ne-intermolecular hydrogen bonding eqinile enegalelo elikhulu ezintweni zawo ezibonakalayo namakhemikhali. Ibalulekile kuzo zonke izinhlobo zokuphila ezaziwayo, naphezu kokunganikeli amandla okudla noma i-organic micronutrient. Ngenxa yobukhona bawo kuzo zonke izinto eziphilayo, ukuzinza kwawo kwamakhemikhali, ukuchichima kwawo emhlabeni wonke, kanye ne-polarity yawo eqinile uma kuqhathaniswa nosayizi wawo wamangqamuzana amancane, amanzi ngokuvamile abizwa ngokuthi "i-solvent yendawo yonke". Ngenxa yokuthi izinga lokushisa elingaphezulu komhlaba kanye nokucindezela kusondele endaweni ephindwe kathathu yamanzi, amanzi akhona eMhlabeni njengento eqinile, uketshezi, negesi. Yakha imvula ngendlela yemvula nama-aerosols ngendlela yenkungu. Amafu ahlanganisa amaconsi amanzi neqhwa amisiwe, isimo sawo esiqinile. Lapho ihlukaniswa kahle, iqhwa elicwebezelayo lingase lihle libe yiqhwa. Isimo segesi samanzi isitimu noma umhwamuko wamanzi. Amanzi ahlanganisa cishe u-71% womhlaba, izilwandle nezilwandle okwenza umthamo wamanzi amaningi (cishe u-96.5%). Izingxenye ezincane zamanzi zenzeka njengamanzi angaphansi komhlaba (1.7%), ezindaweni zeqhwa kanye nezinguzunga zeqhwa e-Antarctica naseGreenland (1.7%), futhi emoyeni njengomhwamuko, amafu (ahlanganisa iqhwa namanzi awuketshezi alengiswe emoyeni), kanye nemvula (0.001%). Amanzi ahamba ngokuqhubekayo emjikelezweni wamanzi wokuhwamuka, ukuphefumula (i-evapotranspiration), ukujiya, ukuna kwemvula, nokugeleza, ngokuvamile afinyelela olwandle. Amanzi adlala indima ebalulekile emnothweni womhlaba. Cishe amaphesenti angama-70 amanzi ahlanzekile asetshenziswa abantu aya kwezolimo. Ukudoba emanzini kasawoti kanye namanzi ahlanzekile kuye kwaba, futhi kuyaqhubeka, umthombo omkhulu wokudla ezingxenyeni eziningi zomhlaba, okuhlinzeka ngo-6.5% wamaprotheni omhlaba. Uhwebo oluningi lwempahla (njengowoyela, igesi yemvelo, nemikhiqizo ekhiqizwayo) luthuthwa ngezikebhe ezilwandle, emifuleni, emachibini nasemiseleni. Amanzi amaningi, iqhwa, nesitimu kusetshenziselwa ukupholisa nokufudumeza embonini nasemakhaya. Amanzi ayincibilikisi enhle kakhulu yezinhlobonhlobo zezinto, kokubili amaminerali kanye ne-organic; kanjalo, isetshenziswa kakhulu ezinqubweni zezimboni nasekuphekeni nasekuwasheni. Amanzi, iqhwa, neqhwa nakho kuyisizinda semidlalo eminingi nezinye izinhlobo zokuzijabulisa, ezinjengokubhukuda, ukuhamba ngesikebhe sokuzijabulisa, imijaho yezikebhe, ukuntweza, ukudoba ezemidlalo, ukutshuza, ukushwiba eqhweni, i-snowboarding, nokushushuluza. I-Etymology
Igama elithi amanzi livela ku-Old English wæter, kusukela ku-Proto-Germanic *watar (umthombo futhi we-Old Saxon watar, i-Old Frisian wetir, amanzi e-Dutch, i-Old High German wazzar, i-German Wasser, i-vatn, i-Gothic 𐍅𐌰𐍄𐍉 (wato)), isuka ku-Proto-Indo-European *i-root *wod-or,' izimpande 'ze-suffid;' Futhi ihambisana, ngempande yesi-Indo-European, nesiGreki elithi ύδωρ (ýdor; kusukela ku-Ancient Greek ὕδωρ (hýdōr), lapho isiNgisi 'hydro-'), isiRashiya вода́ (vodá), uisce wase-Irish, nesi-Albanian ujë. Emhlabeni
Izakhiwo
Amanzi (H2O) iyinhlanganisela ye-polar inorganic. Emazingeni okushisa asekamelweni kuwuketshezi olunganambitheki nolungenaphunga, olucishe lungabi nambala noluncane oluluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka. I-hydrogen chalcogenide elula kakhulu, iyinhlanganisela yamakhemikhali ehlolisiswe kakhulu futhi ngezinye izikhathi ichazwa ngokuthi "i-solvent yomhlaba wonke" ngekhono layo lokuncibilikisa izinto eziningi kunanoma yiluphi olunye uketshezi, nakuba ingasebenzi kahle ekuncibilikiseni izinto ezingezona ezomhlaba. Lokhu kuvumela ukuthi kube "i-solvent yokuphila": ngempela, amanzi njengoba etholakala emvelweni cishe ahlanganisa izinto ezihlukahlukene ezincibilikisiwe, futhi izinyathelo ezikhethekile ziyadingeka ukuze kutholwe amanzi ahlanzekile ngamakhemikhali. Amanzi ukuphela kwento evamile ukuba khona njengesiqinile, uketshezi, negesi ezimweni ezivamile zasemhlabeni. Amazwe
Kanye ne-oxidane, amanzi angelinye lamagama amabili asemthethweni enhlanganisela yamakhemikhali i-H2O; futhi yisigaba se-liquid se-H2O. Ezinye izimo ezimbili ezivamile zendaba yamanzi yisigaba esiqinile, okuyiqhwa, nesigaba segesi, umhwamuko wamanzi noma isitimu. Ukwengezwa noma ukususwa kokushisa kungabangela ukuguquguquka kwesigaba: ukuqhwa (amanzi ukuya eqhweni), ukuncibilika (iqhwa emanzini), ukuhwamuka (amanzi abe umhwamuko), ukufingqa (umhwamuko emanzini), ukuncibilika (iqhwa ukuya eqhweni) kanye nokubeka (umhwamuko eqhweni). Ukuminyana
Amanzi angenye yezinto ezimbalwa ezivamile ezinwebekayo futhi zibe zincane kakhulu njengoba ephola phakathi kwamazinga athile okushisa; yiyona kuphela into eyaziwa ngokwemvelo eyenza lokho ngenkathi iwuketshezi. Ngaphezu kwalokho, ayijwayelekile ngoba iyancipha kakhulu njengoba iqina, nakuba ingahlukile kulokho. Ngomfutho we-atm ongu-1, ifinyelela ukuminyana kwayo okuphezulu okungu-999.972 kg/m3 (62.4262 lb/cu ft) ku-3.98 °C (39.16 °F). Ngaphansi kwalelo zinga lokushisa, kodwa ngaphezu kwezinga elingu-0 °C (32 °F), amanzi ayanwebeka (ancinyane kancane) aze afinyelele endaweni yokubanda, lapho ukuminyana kwawo esigabeni soketshezi kungu-999.8 kg/m3 (62.4155 lb/cu ft). Njengoba amanzi ephola aze afike ku-3.98 °C (39.16 °F), umthamo wamanzi uyancipha. Njengoba iqhwa futhi iba yiqhwa, amanzi anda cishe ngo-9%, afinyelele ukuminyana okungu-917 kg/m3 (57.25 lb/cu ft). Lokhu kwandiswa kungafaka ingcindezi enkulu, amapayipi aqhumayo kanye namadwala aqhekekile. Njengokuqinile, kukhombisa ukuziphatha okuvamile kwenkontileka nokuba minyene njengoba iphola. Lezi zakhiwo ezingavamile zokushisa zinemiphumela ebalulekile yokuphila emhlabeni. Echibini noma olwandle, amanzi angu-4 °C (39 °F) ashona phansi, futhi iqhwa lakheka phezulu, lintanta emanzini awuketshezi. Leli qhwa livikela amanzi angezansi, livimbele ukuba abe yiqhwa eliqinile. Ngaphandle kwalesi sivikelo, izinto eziningi eziphilayo zasemanzini ezihlala emachibini zingabhubha ebusika. Ngaphezu kwalokho, lokhu kuziphatha okuxakile kuyingxenye ebalulekile yokujikeleza kwe-thermohaline esabalalisa ukushisa olwandle olwandle. Uzibuthe
Amanzi yi-diamagnetic material. Nakuba ukusebenzisana kubuthakathaka, ngozibuthe abakhulu kungathola ukuxhumana okuphawulekayo. Izinguquko zesigaba
Ngokucindezela komkhathi owodwa (atm), iqhwa liyancibilika noma amanzi abe yiqhwa (aqina) ku-0 °C (32 °F) bese amanzi emathumba noma umhwamuko ujiye ngo-100 °C (212 °F). Nokho, ngisho nangaphansi kwendawo yokubilisa, amanzi angashintsha abe umhwamuko endaweni yawo ngokuhwamuka (ukuhwamuka kulo lonke uketshezi kwaziwa njengokubilisa). I-sublimation kanye nokubekwa phansi kwenzeka ezindaweni ezingaphezulu. Isibonelo, isithwathwa sifakwa endaweni ebandayo kuyilapho amakhekheba eqhwa akheka ngokubeka izinhlayiya ze-aerosol noma i-ice nucleus. Enqubweni yokomisa iqhwa, ukudla kuqandiswe bese kugcinwa ngaphansi kwengcindezi ephansi ukuze iqhwa elingaphezulu kwalo linciphe. Amaphuzu okuncibilika nokubilisa ancike ekucindezelweni. Ukupheka endaweni ephakeme kuthatha isikhathi eside kunokupheka olwandle. Isibonelo, kumamitha angu-1,524 (5,000 ft), isikhathi sokupheka kufanele senyuswe ngesine ukuze kuzuzwe umphumela oyifunayo. Ngokuphambene, umpheki ocindezelayo ungasetshenziswa ukunciphisa izikhathi zokupheka ngokukhuphula izinga lokushisa elibilayo. Ku-vacuum, amanzi azobilisa ekamelweni lokushisa. Amaphuzu amathathu nabalulekile
Emdwebeni wesigaba sokucindezela/izinga lokushisa (bona umfanekiso), kunamajika ahlukanisa okuqinile nomhwamuko, umhwamuko kuketshezi, noketshezi kokuqinile. Lezi zihlangana endaweni eyodwa ebizwa ngokuthi i-triple point, lapho zonke izigaba ezintathu zingahlala ndawonye. Iphuzu eliphindwe kathathu lisezingeni lokushisa elingu-273.16 K (0.01 ° C; 32.02 ° F) kanye nokucindezela kwe-611.657 pascals (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); kuwumfutho ophansi kakhulu amanzi awuketshezi angaba khona. Kuze kube ngu-2019, iphoyinti elithathu lalisetshenziselwa ukuchaza isikali sezinga lokushisa lika-Kelvin. Ijika lesigaba samanzi/umhwamuko linqamula ku-647.096 K (373.946 °C; 705.103 °F) kanye nama-megapascal angu-22.064 (3,200.1 psi; 217.75 atm). Lokhu kwaziwa njengephuzu elibalulekile. Emazingeni okushisa aphezulu kanye nezingcindezi izigaba zoketshezi kanye nomhwamuko zakha isigaba esiqhubekayo esibizwa ngokuthi uketshezi oluphezulu kakhulu. Ingacindezelwa kancane kancane noma inwetshwe phakathi kokuminyana okufana negesi nokufana noketshezi; izakhiwo zayo (ezihluke kakhulu kulezo zamanzi azungezile) zizwela ukuminyana. Isibonelo, ngokucindezela okufanelekile namazinga okushisa ingaxuba ngokukhululekile nezinhlanganisela ze-nonpolar, kuhlanganise nezinhlanganisela eziningi eziphilayo. Lokhu kuyenza isebenziseke ezinhlelweni ezihlukene ezihlanganisa izinga lokushisa eliphezulu likagesi kanye njengesinyibilikisi esinobungozi bemvelo noma i-catalyst ekuphenduleni kwamakhemikhali okubandakanya izinhlanganisela eziphilayo. Emgqeni womhlaba, isebenza njenge-solvent ngesikhathi sokwakheka kwamaminerali, ukuchithwa kanye nokuchithwa. Izigaba zeqhwa namanzi
Uhlobo oluvamile lweqhwa ebusweni boMhlaba yiqhwa Ih, isigaba esakha amakristalu anokulingana kwe-hexagonal. Enye ene-cubic crystalline symmetry, i-ice Ic, ingenzeka emkhathini ongaphezulu. Njengoba ukucindezela kwanda, iqhwa lakha ezinye izakhiwo zekristalu. Kusukela ngo-2024, ezingamashumi amabili ziye zaqinisekiswa ngokuhlolwa futhi ezinye eziningi zibikezelwa ngokomcabango. Uhlobo lweshumi nesishiyagalombili lweqhwa, i-ice XVIII, okuyisigaba seqhwa esimaphakathi nobuso, kwatholakala lapho ithonsi lamanzi lingaphansi kwegagasi lokushaqisa elaphakamisa ukucindezela kwamanzi ezigidini ze-atmosphere kanye nezinga lokushisa elifinyelela ezinkulungwaneni zamadigri, okwaphumela esakhiweni sama-athomu omoya-mpilo aqinile lapho ama-athomu e-hydrogen ayegeleza khona ngokukhululeka. Uma lihlanganiswe phakathi kwezingqimba ze-graphene, iqhwa lakha i-lattice eyisikwele. Imininingwane yemvelo yamakhemikhali amanzi awuketshezi ayiqondi kahle; eminye imibono iphakamisa ukuthi ukuziphatha kwayo okungajwayelekile kungenxa yokuba khona kwezimo ezimbili eziwuketshezi. Ukunambitha kanye nephunga
Amanzi ahlanzekile ngokuvamile achazwa njenganganambitheki futhi awanaphunga, nakuba abantu benezinzwa ezithile ezikwazi ukuzwa ubukhona bamanzi emilonyeni yabo, futhi amaxoxo ayaziwa ukuthi ayakwazi ukuhogela. Kodwa-ke, amanzi avela emithonjeni evamile (okuhlanganisa namanzi amaminerali) ngokuvamile anezinto eziningi ezincibilikisiwe ezingase ziwanikeze ukunambitheka okuhlukahlukene kanye nephunga. Abantu nezinye izilwane baye bahlakulela izinzwa ezibenza bakwazi ukuhlola ukutholakala kwamanzi ukuze bagweme amanzi anosawoti omningi noma abolile. Umbala nokubukeka
Amanzi ahlanzekile aluhlaza ngokusobala ngenxa yokumuncwa kokukhanya endaweni c. 600-800 nm. Umbala ungabonakala kalula engilazini yamanzi kampompi ebekwe ngokumelene nesizinda esimhlophe esimsulwa, emini. Amabhendi okumunca ayinhloko anesibopho sombala angamathoni wokudlidliza okunwebekayo kwe-O–H. Ukuqina okubonakalayo kombala kukhuphuka ngokujula kwekholomu yamanzi, ngokulandela umthetho kabhiya. Lokhu kuphinda kusebenze, ngokwesibonelo, ngedamu lokubhukuda lapho umthombo wokukhanya kuwukukhanya kwelanga okuvela kumathayela amhlophe echibini. Emvelweni, umbala ungabuye uguqulwe usuka koluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka uye koluhlaza ngenxa yokuba khona kwezinto eziqinile ezimisiwe noma ulwelwe. Embonini, i-spectroscopy eseduze ne-infrared isetshenziswa nezixazululo ezinamanzi njengoba ukuqina okukhulu kwamanzi angaphansi kusho ukuthi ama-cuvettes engilazi anobude bendlela obufushane angase asetshenziswe. Ukubona i-spectrum yokumuncwa okuyisisekelo okunwebekayo kwamanzi noma isixazululo esinamanzi endaweni ezungeze u-3,500 cm−1 (2.85 μm) ubude bendlela obungaba ngu-25 μm buyadingeka. Futhi, i-cuvette kufanele ibe kokubili obala cishe ku-3500 cm−1 futhi ingancibiliki emanzini; I-calcium fluoride ingenye yezinto ezisetshenziswa kakhulu kumafasitela e-cuvette anezixazululo ezinamanzi. Ukudlidliza okuyisisekelo kwe-Raman kungase kubonwe, ngokwesibonelo, ngeseli lesampula elingu-1 cm. Izitshalo zasemanzini, ulwelwe, nezinye izinto eziphilayo ze-photosynthetic zingaphila emanzini afinyelele amakhulu amamitha ukujula, ngoba ukukhanya kwelanga kungafinyelela kuzo. Empeleni akukho ukukhanya kwelanga okufinyelela ezingxenyeni zolwandle ngaphansi kwamamitha angu-1,000 (3,300 ft) ukujula. Inkomba ye-refractive yamanzi awuketshezi (1.333 ku-20 °C (68 °F)) iphakeme kakhulu kuneyomoya (1.0), efana naleyo ye-alkanes ne-ethanol, kodwa iphansi kunaleyo ye-glycerol (1.473), i-1.501), i-carbon disulfide (1.627), nezinhlobo ezivamile ze-benzeli1 (1.6). Inkomba ye-refraction yeqhwa (1.31) iphansi kunaleyo yamanzi ayiketshezi. I-polarity yamangqamuzana
Ku-molecule yamanzi, ama-athomu e-hydrogen akha i-engeli engu-104.5° nge-athomu yomoya-mpilo. Ama-athomu e-hydrogen aseduze namakhona amabili e-tetrahedron agxile kumoya-mpilo. Kwamanye amakhona amabili kukhona amapheya awodwa ama-electron e-valence angahlanganyeli ekuhlanganiseni. Ku-tetrahedron efanelekile, ama-athomu angakha i-engeli engu-109.5°, kodwa ukwenyanya phakathi kwamapheya awodwa kukhulu kunokwenyanya phakathi kwama-athomu e-hydrogen. Ubude bebhondi ye-O–H cishe bungu-0.096 nm. Ezinye izinto zinesakhiwo samangqamuzana e-tetrahedral, isibonelo i-methane (CH4) ne-hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Nokho, umoya-mpilo unamandla kagesi kakhulu kunezinye izakhi eziningi, ngakho-ke i-athomu ye-oksijini inengxenye engeyinhle yokushajwa kuyilapho ama-athomu e-hydrogen eshajwe kahle kancane. Kanye nesakhiwo esigobile, lokhu kunikeza i-molecule umzuzu we-dipole kagesi futhi ihlukaniswa njenge-molecule ye-polar. Amanzi yi-solvent enhle ye-polar, ancibilikisa usawoti omningi nama-molecule ephilayo e-hydrophilic njengoshukela notshwala obulula njenge-ethanol. Amanzi aphinde ancibilikise amagesi amaningi, afana ne-oxygen ne-carbon dioxide - lokhu okugcina kunikeza i-fizz yeziphuzo ezine-carbonated, iwayini elikhazimulayo nobhiya. Ngaphezu kwalokho, izinto eziningi eziphilayo eziphilayo, njengamaprotheni, i-DNA nama-polysaccharides, zichithwa emanzini. Ukusebenzisana phakathi kwamanzi nama-subunits alawa ma-biomacromolecules kwakheka ukugoqa kwamaprotheni, ukubhanqa kwesisekelo se-DNA, nezinye izenzakalo ezibalulekile ekuphileni (umphumela we-hydrophobic). Izinto eziningi eziphilayo (njengamafutha namafutha nama-alkanes) zine-hydrophobic, okungukuthi, azincibiliki emanzini. Izinto eziningi ezingaphili nazo azincibiliki, okuhlanganisa ama-oxide ensimbi amaningi, ama-sulfide, nama-silicates. Ukuhlanganiswa kwe-hydrogen
Ngenxa ye-polarity yayo, i-molecule yamanzi oketshezini noma esimweni esiqinile ingakha amabhondi e-hydrogen amane nama-molecule angomakhelwane. Amabhondi e-hydrogen aqine cishe ngokuphindwe kashumi kunamandla ka-Van der Waals adonsela ama-molecule oketshezini oluningi. Lesi yisizathu esenza ukuthi amanzi ancibilike futhi abilayo abe phezulu kakhulu kunawamanye ama-compounds afana ne-hydrogen sulfide. Ziphinde zichaze umthamo wayo wokushisa othize ophakeme kakhulu (cishe u-4.2 J/(g·K)), ukushisa kokuhlanganiswa (cishe u-333 J/g), ukushisa kwe-vaporization (2257 J/g), kanye ne-thermal conductivity (phakathi kuka-0.561 no-0.679 W/(m·K)). Lezi zakhiwo zenza amanzi asebenze kangcono ekulinganiseni isimo sezulu soMhlaba, ngokugcina ukushisa nokukuhambisa phakathi kwezilwandle nomoya. Amabhondi e-hydrogen amanzi acishe abe ngu-23 kJ/mol (uma kuqhathaniswa nebhondi ehlangene ye-O–H ku-492 kJ/mol). Kulokhu, kulinganiselwa ukuthi u-90% ubangelwa ama-electrostatics, kanti u-10% osele uhambisana kancane. Lawa mabhondi ayimbangela yokushuba okuphezulu kwamanzi kanye namandla e-capillary. Isenzo se-capillary sisho ukuthambekela kwamanzi ukunyusa ishubhu elincane ngokumelene namandla adonsela phansi. Lesi sakhiwo sithembele kuzo zonke izitshalo ezinemithambo yegazi, njengezihlahla. I-self-ionization
Amanzi ayisixazululo esibuthakathaka se-hydronium hydroxide - kukhona ukulingana 2H2O ⇌ H3O+ + OH−, kuhlanganiswe nokuxazululwa kwe-ion ye-hydronium ne-hydroxide. I-electrolysis kanye ne-conductivity kagesi
Amanzi ahlanzekile ane-conductivity kagesi ephansi, ekhuphuka ngokuchithwa kwenani elincane le-ionic njengosawoti ovamile. Amanzi aluketshezi angahlukaniswa abe izakhi ze-hydrogen nomoya-mpilo ngokudlulisela amandla kagesi kuwo—inqubo ebizwa ngokuthi i-electrolysis. Ukubola kudinga okokufaka kwamandla amaningi kunokushisa okukhishwe inqubo ephambene (285.8 kJ/mol, noma 15.9 MJ/kg). Izakhiwo zemishini
Amanzi awuketshezi angathathwa njengangenakucindezelwa ngezinjongo eziningi: ukucindezelwa kwawo kuhluka ukusuka ku-4.4 kuya ku-5.1×10−10 Pa−1 ezimweni ezijwayelekile. Ngisho nasezilwandle ekujuleni okungamakhilomitha angu-4, lapho ingcindezi ingu-400 atm, amanzi ehla ngo-1.8% kuphela. I-viscosity yamanzi icishe ibe ngu-10−3 Pa·s noma 0.01 poise ku-20 °C (68 °F), futhi isivinini somsindo emanzini awuketshezi sisukela phakathi kwamamitha angu-1,400 no-1,540 ngomzuzwana (4,600 kanye no-5,100 ft/s) kuye ngezinga lokushisa. Umsindo uhamba amabanga amade emanzini ngokuncishwa kancane, ikakhulukazi kumaza aphansi (cishe u-0.03 dB/km ngo-1 kHz), indawo exhashazwa ama-cetaceans kanye nabantu ekuxhumaneni nasekuzweni kwemvelo (sonar). Ukusebenza kabusha
Izakhi zensimbi ezine-electropositive kakhulu kune-hydrogen, ikakhulukazi izinsimbi ze-alkali futhi, ngokwezinga elincane, izinsimbi zomhlaba ezine-alkali, zisusa i-hydrogen emanzini, zenze ama-hydroxides futhi zikhiphe i-hydrogen. Emazingeni okushisa aphezulu, i-coke, uhlobo lwekhabhoni, ihlangana nesitimu yenze i-carbon monoxide ne-hydrogen. Emhlabeni
IHydrology yisifundo sokuhamba, ukusatshalaliswa, kanye nekhwalithi yamanzi kuwo wonke umhlaba. Ucwaningo lokusatshalaliswa kwamanzi yi-hydrography. Ucwaningo lokusatshalaliswa nokuhamba kwamanzi angaphansi komhlaba i-hydrogeology, yezinguzunga zeqhwa i-glaciology, emanzini aphakathi nezwe i-limnology kanye nokusatshalaliswa kwezilwandle i-oceanography. Izinqubo zemvelo nge-hydrology zigxile ku-ecohydrology. Inqwaba yamanzi atholakala phezu, ngaphansi nangaphezulu kweplanethi ibizwa ngokuthi i-hydrosphere. Umthamo wamanzi olinganiselwe womhlaba (inani eliphelele lamanzi omhlaba) lingama-cubic kilometers angu-1.386 billion (333 million cubic miles). Amanzi aluketshezi atholakala emanzini, njengolwandle, ulwandle, ichibi, umfula, umfudlana, umsele, ichibi, noma i-puddle. Iningi lamanzi eMhlabeni ngamanzi olwandle. Amanzi akhona futhi emkhathini ezimeni eziqinile, eziwuketshezi, neziwumhwamuko. Ikhona futhi njengamanzi angaphansi komhlaba emanzini. Amanzi abalulekile ezinqubweni eziningi ze-geological. Amanzi angaphansi komhlaba akhona emadwaleni amaningi, futhi ukucindezela kwala manzi angaphansi komhlaba kuthinta amaphethini okungasebenzi kahle. Amanzi asesigqokweni anesibopho sokuncibilika okukhiqiza izintaba-mlilo ezindaweni ezincibilikayo. Ebusweni bomhlaba, amanzi abalulekile kuzo zombili izinqubo zesimo sezulu samakhemikhali nezomzimba. Amanzi, futhi ngezinga elincane kodwa elibalulekile, iqhwa, nawo anesibopho sokuthuthwa kwenhlabathi eningi okwenzeka ebusweni bomhlaba. Ukubekwa kwentlenga ehanjiswayo kwakha izinhlobo eziningi zamadwala e-sedimentary, akha irekhodi le-geologic lomlando woMhlaba. Umjikelezo wamanzi
Umjikelezo wamanzi (owaziwa ngokwesayensi ngokuthi umjikelezo we-hydrologic) ukushintshana kwamanzi okuqhubekayo ngaphakathi kwe-hydrosphere, phakathi komkhathi, amanzi enhlabathi, amanzi angaphezulu, amanzi angaphansi komhlaba, nezitshalo. Amanzi ahamba ngokuqhubekayo kusifunda ngasinye salezi kumjikelezo wamanzi ohlanganisa izinqubo zokudlulisa ezilandelayo:

ukuhwamuka olwandle nakweminye imithombo yamanzi emoyeni kanye nokuphuma ezitshalweni zasezweni nasezilwaneni kuya emoyeni. imvula, isuka kumusi wamanzi ojiya usuka emoyeni bese uwela emhlabeni noma olwandle. ukugeleza komhlabathi ngokuvamile okufinyelela olwandle. Umhwamuko omningi otholakala kakhulu olwandle ubuyela kulo, kodwa imimoya ithwala umhwamuko wamanzi phezu komhlaba ngezinga elifanayo nokugeleza olwandle, cishe ama-Tt angama-47 ngonyaka kuyilapho ukuhwamuka nokuguquka kwesimo okwenzeka emhlabeni kuphinde kube nengxenye enye engama-72 Tt ngonyaka. Imvula, ngenani elingu-119 Tt ngonyaka emhlabeni, inezinhlobo ezimbalwa: ngokuvamile imvula, iqhwa, nesichotho, okuhambisana nenkungu namazolo. Amazolo amathonsi amancane amanzi ajiyayo lapho umhwamuko omningi uhlangana nendawo epholile. Ngokuvamile amazolo akheka ekuseni lapho izinga lokushisa liphansi kakhulu, ngaphambi nje kokuphuma kwelanga nalapho izinga lokushisa emhlabeni liqala ukwanda. Amanzi ajiyile asemoyeni angase aphinde akhiphe ukukhanya kwelanga ukuze kukhiqizwe uthingo. Ukugeleza kwamanzi kuvame ukuqoqana phezu kwemifula egelezela emifuleni. Ngokuguguleka komhlabathi, ukugeleza kwamanzi kubumba indawo ezungezile edala izigodi zemifula nama-deltas ahlinzeka umhlabathi ocebile nendawo ekahle yokusungulwa kwezindawo zabantu. Isikhukhula senzeka lapho indawo yezwe, ngokuvamile ephansi, imbozwe ngamanzi okwenzeka lapho umfula ugcwala osebeni lwawo noma ukuhlasela kwesiphepho. Ngakolunye uhlangothi, isomiso isikhathi eside esiyizinyanga noma iminyaka lapho isifunda siphawula ukuntula kwamanzi. Lokhu kwenzeka lapho isifunda sithola imvula engaphansi kwesilinganiso ngokungashintshi ngenxa yesimo sendawo noma ngenxa yendawo yaso ngokuya nge-latitude. Imithombo yamanzi
Imithombo yamanzi iyimithombo yemvelo yamanzi engaba usizo kubantu, isibonelo njengomthombo wamanzi okuphuza noma amanzi okunisela. Amanzi avela njengawo kokubili "izitoko" kanye "nokugeleza". Amanzi angagcinwa njengamachibi, umhwamuko wamanzi, amanzi angaphansi komhlaba noma ama-aquifer, kanye neqhwa neqhwa. Kuwo wonke umthamo wamanzi ahlanzekile embulunga yonke, alinganiselwa kumaphesenti angu-69 agcinwe ezingosini zeqhwa nesembozwe seqhwa unomphela; amaphesenti angu-30 asemanzini angaphansi komhlaba; kanye nephesenti elilodwa elisele emachibini, emifuleni, emkhathini nasezindaweni eziphilayo. Ubude besikhathi samanzi asele endaweni yokugcina buyahlukahluka kakhulu: eminye imithombo yamanzi angaphansi komhlaba iqukethe amanzi agcinwe phakathi nezinkulungwane zeminyaka, kodwa umthamo wamachibi ungase uguquguquke ngokwezinkathi ezithile zonyaka, unciphe ngezikhathi ezomile futhi ande ngesikhathi sezimanzi. Ingxenye enkulu yokunikezwa kwamanzi kwezinye izifunda iqukethe amanzi akhishwe emanzini agcinwe esitokisini, futhi lapho ukuhoxiswa kweqa ukushajwa kabusha, amasheya ayancipha. Ngokwezinye izilinganiso, cishe amaphesenti angu-30 amanzi esewonke asetshenziselwa ukuchelela avela ekukhishweni okungalawuleki kwamanzi angaphansi komhlaba, okubangela ukuncipha kwamanzi angaphansi komhlaba. Amanzi olwandle namagagasi
Amanzi olwandle aqukethe cishe u-3.5% we-sodium chloride ngokwesilinganiso, kanye namanani amancane wezinye izinto. Izinto ezibonakalayo zamanzi olwandle zihlukile emanzini ahlanzekile kwezinye izici ezibalulekile. Iqina ezingeni lokushisa eliphansi (cishe -1.9 °C (28.6 °F)) futhi ukuminyana kwayo kuyanda ngokuncipha kwezinga lokushisa lifinyelele endaweni yokubanda, esikhundleni sokufinyelela ukuminyana okukhulu ezingeni lokushisa ngaphezu kwezingaqhwa. Usawoti wamanzi olwandle olukhulu uyahluka kusuka ku-0.7% oLwandle iBaltic kuya ku-4.0% oLwandle Olubomvu. (ULwandle Olufile, olwaziwa ngamazinga alo anosawoti aphezulu kakhulu aphakathi kuka-30 no-40%, iyichibi likasawoti ngempela.)
Amagagasi angumjikelezo wokukhuphuka nokwehla kwamazinga olwandle asendaweni okubangelwa amandla olwandle eNyanga kanye neLanga asebenza olwandle. Amagagasi adala izinguquko ekujuleni kwamanzi olwandle kanye nemifula futhi akhiqize ama-oscillating currents aziwa ngokuthi ama-tidal streams. Ukushintsha kwamagagasi okukhiqizwa endaweni ethile kuwumphumela wokushintsha ukuma kweNyanga neLanga okuhlobene noMhlaba okuhambisana nemiphumela yokujikeleza komhlaba kanye ne-bathymetry yendawo. Umdweshu wogu lolwandle ocwila emanzini aphezulu futhi uvezwe emanzini aphansi, indawo ephakathi kwamagagasi, uwumkhiqizo obalulekile wemvelo wamagagasi olwandle. Imiphumela empilweni
Ngokombono webhayoloji, amanzi anezici eziningi ezihlukile ezibalulekile ekwandeni kokuphila. Ifeza le ndima ngokuvumela izinto eziphilayo ukuthi zisabele ngezindlela ezivumela ukuphindaphinda. Zonke izinhlobo zokuphila ezaziwayo zincike emanzini. Amanzi abalulekile kokubili njengesincibilikidi lapho izincibilikisi eziningi zomzimba zincibilika futhi njengengxenye ebalulekile yezinqubo eziningi zokugayeka kokudla emzimbeni. I-Metabolism iyisamba esiphelele se-anabolism kanye ne-catabolism. Ku-anabolism, amanzi akhishwa kuma-molecule (ngamandla adinga ukusabela kwamakhemikhali enzymatic) ukuze kukhule ama-molecule amakhulu (isb., isitashi, ama-triglycerides, namaprotheni ukuze kugcinwe amafutha nolwazi). Ku-catabolism, amanzi asetshenziselwa ukunqamula izibopho ukuze kukhiqizwe ama-molecule amancane (isb., ushukela, ama-fatty acids, nama-amino acid azosetshenziselwa izinto zokubasa zokusetshenziswa kwamandla noma ezinye izinjongo). Ngaphandle kwamanzi, lezi zinqubo ze-metabolic bezingeke zibe khona. Amanzi ayisisekelo kukho kokubili i-photosynthesis nokuphefumula. Amangqamuzana e-photosynthetic asebenzisa amandla elanga ukuze ahlukanise i-hydrogen yamanzi nomoya-mpilo. Lapho kukhona ukukhanya kwelanga, i-hydrogen ihlanganiswa ne-CO2 (emuncwa emoyeni noma emanzini) ukuze yakhe i-glucose futhi ikhulule umoya-mpilo. Wonke amangqamuzana aphilayo asebenzisa amafutha anjalo futhi enza i-hydrogen ne-carbon oxidize ukuze abambe amandla elanga futhi aguqule amanzi kanye ne-CO2 kule nqubo (ukuphefumula kwamaselula). Amanzi nawo ayingxenye yokungathathi hlangothi kwe-acid-base kanye nokusebenza kwe-enzyme. I-asidi, i-hydrogen ion (H+, okungukuthi, i-proton) donor, ingasuswa yisisekelo, isamukeli se-proton njenge-hydroxide ion (OH-) ukuze senze amanzi. Amanzi athathwa njengangathathi hlangothi, ane-pH (ilogi engalungile ye-hydrogen ion concentration) engu-7 esesimweni esikahle. Ama-Acids anenani le-pH elingaphansi kuka-7 kuyilapho izisekelo zinamanani angaphezu kuka-7. Amafomu ezinto eziphilayo zasemanzini
Amanzi angaphezulu komhlaba agcwele ukuphila. Izinhlobo zokuphila zakuqala zavela emanzini; cishe zonke izinhlanzi zihlala emanzini kuphela, futhi kunezinhlobo eziningi zezilwane ezincelisayo zasolwandle, njengamahlengethwa nemikhomo. Ezinye izinhlobo zezilwane, njengezilwane ezihlala emanzini nasemanzini, zichitha ingxenye yempilo yazo emanzini kanye nezingxenye ezweni. Izitshalo ezifana ne-kelp kanye ne-algae zikhula emanzini futhi ziyisisekelo sezinto eziphilayo ezingaphansi kwamanzi. I-Plankton ngokuvamile iyisisekelo sochungechunge lokudla kwasolwandle. Izilwane ezihlala emanzini kufanele zithole umoya-mpilo ukuze ziphile, futhi lokhu zikwenza ngezindlela ezihlukahlukene. Izinhlanzi zinamathumba esikhundleni samaphaphu, nakuba ezinye izinhlobo zezinhlanzi, njenge-lungfish, zinakho kokubili. Izilwane ezincelisayo zasolwandle, njengamahlengethwa, imikhomo, ama-otter, nezimvu zamanzi kudingeka zivele phezulu ngezikhathi ezithile ukuze ziphefumule umoya. Ezinye izilwane ezihlala emanzini ziyakwazi ukumunca umoya-mpilo esikhumbeni sazo. Ama-invertebrates abonisa izinhlobonhlobo zokuguqulwa ukuze aphile emanzini angenawo umoya-mpilo kahle kuhlanganise namashubhu okuphefumula (bona izinambuzane nama-mollusc siphons) namagill (Carcinus). Nokho, njengoba ukuphila kwezilwane ezingenamgogodla zazivelela endaweni ehlala emanzini, iningi linolwazi oluncane noma alunawo nhlobo lokuphefumula emanzini. Imithelela empucukweni yabantu
Impucuko iye yachuma ngokomlando ngasemifuleni nasezindleleni ezinkulu zamanzi; IMesophothamiya, enye yezindawo okuthiwa yizindawo zempucuko, yayiphakathi kwemifula emikhulu iTigrisi ne-Ewufrathe; umphakathi wasendulo wabaseGibhithe wawuthembele ngokuphelele emfuleni iNayile. Impucuko yokuqala yase-Indus Valley (c. 3300 BCE - c. 1300 BCE) yakhula eduze nomfula i-Indus kanye nemingenela eyayigeleza iphuma ezintabeni zase-Himalaya. IRoma nayo yasungulwa osebeni lomfula wase-Italy iTiber. Amadolobha amakhulu afana neRotterdam, London, Montreal, Paris, New York City, Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Tokyo, Chicago, neHong Kong ngenxa yempumelelo yawo ngokwengxenye ukufinyeleleka kwawo kalula ngamanzi kanye nokwanda kohwebo. Iziqhingi ezinamachweba amanzi aphephile, njengeSingapore, ziye zachuma ngenxa yesizathu esifanayo. Ezindaweni ezinjengaseNyakatho Afrika naseMpumalanga Ephakathi, lapho amanzi eyivelakancane khona, ukutholakala kwamanzi okuphuza ahlanzekile kwakuyisici esiyinhloko ekuthuthukeni komuntu. Impilo nokungcola
Amanzi alungele ukusetshenziswa abantu abizwa ngokuthi amanzi okuphuza noma amanzi aphuzwayo. Amanzi angenakuphuzwa angenziwa aphuzwe ngokuhluzwa noma ngokuwahluzwa, noma ngohlu lwezinye izindlela. Abantu abangaphezu kwezigidi ezingu-660 abanawo amanzi okuphuza aphephile. Amanzi angafanele ukuphuzwa kodwa angenabungozi kubantu uma ebhukuda noma egeza abizwa ngamagama ahlukene ngaphandle kwalawo aphuzwayo noma okuphuza, kanti kwesinye isikhathi abizwa ngokuthi amanzi aphephile, noma “aphephile ukugeza”. I-Chlorine iyi-irritant yesikhumba kanye nolwelwesi lwamafinyila esetshenziselwa ukwenza amanzi aphephe lapho egeza noma ephuzwa. Ukusetshenziswa kwayo kuwubuchwepheshe obuphezulu futhi kuvame ukugadwa yimithetho kahulumeni (imvamisa ingxenye engu-1 esigidini ngasinye (ppm) yamanzi okuphuza, kanye no-1–2 ppm we-chlorine ongakahlangani nokungcola kwamanzi okugeza). Amanzi okugeza angase agcinwe esesimweni esigculisayo se-microbiological kusetshenziswa izibulala-magciwane zamakhemikhali njenge-chlorine noma i-ozone noma ngokusebenzisa ukukhanya kwe-ultraviolet. Ukulungiswa kabusha kwamanzi kuyinqubo yokuguqula amanzi angcolile (imvamisa indle, abuye abizwe ngokuthi amanzi angcolile kamasipala) abe amanzi angaphinde asetshenziswe ngezinye izinjongo. Kunabantu abayizigidi eziyizinkulungwane ezingu-2.3 abahlala ezizweni ezinokuntuleka kwamanzi, okusho ukuthi umuntu ngamunye uthola ngaphansi kuka-1,700 cubic metres (60,000 cu ft) wamanzi ngonyaka. 380 billion cubic metres (13×10^12 cu ft) wamanzi angcolile kamasipala akhiqizwa emhlabeni jikelele unyaka nonyaka. Amanzi ahlanzekile awumthombo ovuselelekayo, ojikeleziswa kabusha umjikelezo wemvelo we-hydrologic, kodwa izingcindezi zokufinyelela kuwo zibangelwa ukusabalalisa okungalingani ngokwemvelo emkhathini nesikhathi, izimfuno zezomnotho ezikhulayo zezolimo nezimboni, kanye nokwenyuka kwabantu. Njengamanje, cishe abantu abayizigidi eziyinkulungwane emhlabeni wonke abawatholi amanzi aphephile, athengekayo. Ngonyaka wezi-2000, iNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene yasungula iMillennium Development Goals ukuze amanzi anciphise ngesigamu ngo-2015 inani labantu emhlabeni wonke abangenawo amanzi aphephile kanye nokukhucululwa kwendle. Inqubekelaphambili kulowo mgomo yayingalingani, futhi ngo-2015 i-UN yazibophezela Ezinjongweni Zokuthuthukiswa Okusimeme zokufinyelela emhlabeni wonke emanzini aphephile nathengekayo kanye nokukhucululwa kwendle ngo-2030. Izinga lamanzi elingelihle kanye nokukhucululwa kwendle okubi kuyabulala; ukufa okungaba izigidi ezinhlanu ngonyaka kubangelwa izifo ezihlobene namanzi. I-World Health Organization ilinganisela ukuthi amanzi ahlanzekile angavimbela ukufa kwezingane eziyizigidi ezingu-1.4 ngenxa yohudo unyaka ngamunye. Emazweni asathuthuka, u-90% wawo wonke amanzi angcolile kamasipala asangena emifuleni nemifudlana yendawo. Amazwe angaba ngu-50, acishe abe ingxenye eyodwa kwezintathu yenani labantu emhlabeni, nawo anenkinga yokuntuleka kwamanzi aphakathi nendawo noma aphezulu futhi angu-17 kulawa akhipha amanzi amaningi ngonyaka kunalawo agcwaliswa kabusha ngemijikelezo yawo yamanzi emvelo. Lolu hlobo lobunzima akuthinti nje kuphela izindawo ezihlala emanzini ahlanzekile njengemifula namachibi, kodwa futhi lwehlisa isithunzi samanzi angaphansi komhlaba. Ukusetshenziswa komuntu
Ezolimo
Amanzi asetshenziswa kakhulu abantu ngeyokulima, okuhlanganisa nokuniselwa ngenkasa, okubalelwa kumaphesenti angama-80 kuye kwangama-90 amanzi esewonke asetshenziswa abantu. E-United States, u-42% wamanzi ahlanzekile akhishiwe ukuze asetshenziswe awokunisela, kodwa amanzi amaningi "asetshenzisiwe" (asetshenzisiwe futhi angabuyiselwa endaweni ezungezile) aya kwezolimo. Ukufinyelela emanzini ahlanzekile kuvame ukuthathwa njengokubalulekile, ikakhulukazi emazweni athuthukile akhe izinhlelo zamanzi eziyinkimbinkimbi zokuqoqa, ukuhlanza, nokuletha amanzi, nokukhipha amanzi angcolile. Kodwa ukukhula kwengcindezi yezomnotho, yezibalo zabantu, kanye nesimo sezulu kwandisa ukukhathazeka mayelana nezindaba zamanzi, okuholela ekwandeni kokuncintisana ngemithombo yamanzi engashintshi, okubangela umqondo wamanzi aphezulu. Njengoba inani labantu kanye neminotho iqhubeka nokukhula, ukusetshenziswa kwenyama ewomele amanzi kwanda, kanye nezimfuno ezintsha ze-biofuels noma izimboni ezintsha ezidinga amanzi, izinselele ezintsha zamanzi zingenzeka. Ukuhlolwa kokuphathwa kwamanzi kwezolimo kwenziwa ngo-2007 yi-International Water Management Institute eSri Lanka ukuze kubonakale ukuthi umhlaba unawo yini amanzi anele ukuhlinzeka ngokudla kwabantu bawo abakhulayo. Ihlole ubukhona bamanje bamanzi okulima emhlabeni wonke futhi yabala izindawo ezinenkinga yokushoda kwamanzi. Ithole ukuthi ingxenye yesihlanu yabantu bomhlaba, abangaphezu kwezigidi eziyizinkulungwane ezingu-1.2, bahlala ezindaweni lapho amanzi entuleka khona, lapho kungekho khona amanzi anele okuhlangabezana nazo zonke izidingo. Abanye abantu abayizigidi eziyizinkulungwane eziyi-1.6 bahlala ezindaweni ezinenkinga yokushoda kwamanzi kwezomnotho, lapho ukuntuleka kokutshalwa kwezimali emanzini noma umthamo onganele womuntu kwenza kube nzima ngeziphathimandla ukwanelisa isidingo samanzi. Lo mbiko wathola ukuthi kungenzeka ukukhiqiza ukudla okudingekayo esikhathini esizayo, kodwa ukuqhubeka kokukhiqizwa kokudla kwanamuhla nezindlela zemvelo kuzoholela ezinkingeni ezingxenyeni eziningi zomhlaba. Ukuze kugwenywe inkinga yamanzi emhlabeni wonke, abalimi kuyodingeka balwele ukwandisa umkhiqizo ukuze bahlangabezane nezidingo ezikhulayo zokudla, kuyilapho izimboni namadolobha ethola izindlela zokusebenzisa amanzi ngokuphumelelayo. Ukushoda kwamanzi kudalwa futhi ukukhiqizwa kwemikhiqizo edinga amanzi kakhulu. Isibonelo, ukotini: 1 kg kakotini-okulingana ne-jeans-idinga ama-cubic metres angu-10.9 (380 cu ft) amanzi ukuze akhiqize. Nakuba ukotini wenza u-2.4% wokusetshenziswa kwamanzi emhlabeni wonke, amanzi asetshenziswa ezifundeni esezivele zisengozini yokushoda kwamanzi. Kudalwe umonakalo omkhulu emvelweni: isibonelo, ukuphambukiswa kwamanzi yiSoviet Union yangaphambili esuka emfuleni i-Amu Darya kanye ne-Syr Darya ukuze kukhiqizwe ukotini kwaba yimbangela enkulu yokunyamalala koLwandle i-Aral. Njengendinganiso yesayensi
Ngomhla ziyisi-7 kuMbasa 1795, igramu yachazwa eFrance ukuthi ilingane “nesisindo esiphelele somthamo wamanzi ahlanzekile alingana nekhiyubhu lekhulu lemitha, nasezingeni lokushisa leqhwa elincibilikayo”. Nokho, ngezinjongo ezingokoqobo, indinganiso yereferensi yensimbi yayidingeka, ubukhulu obuphindwe izikhathi eziyinkulungwane, ikhilogremu. Ngakho-ke wanikwa umsebenzi wokunquma ngokunembile isisindo selitha elilodwa lamanzi. Naphezu kweqiniso lokuthi incazelo enqunyiwe yegremu icacise amanzi ku-0 °C (32 °F)—izinga lokushisa elikwazi ukukhiqizwa kabusha—ososayensi bakhethe ukulichaza kabusha izinga nokwenza izilinganiso zabo ngezinga lokushisa eliphakeme kakhulu lokuminyana kwamanzi, elalikalwa ngaleso sikhathi njengo-4 °C (39 °F). Izinga lokushisa lika-Kelvin lohlelo lwe-SI lwalusekelwe endaweni ephindwe kathathu yamanzi, echazwa ngokuthi ncamashi 273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F), kodwa kusukela ngoMeyi 2019 sisekelwe ku-Boltzmann njalo. Isikali isikali sokushisa esiphelele esinokwenyuka okufanayo nesikali sezinga lokushisa kweCelsius, esachazwa ekuqaleni ngokuya ngendawo yokubilisa (emiswe ku-100 °C (212 °F)) nendawo yokuncibilika (emiswe ku-0 °C (32 °F)) wamanzi. Amanzi emvelo aqukethe ikakhulukazi ama-isotopes i-hydrogen-1 kanye ne-oxygen-16, kodwa kukhona nenani elincane lama-isotopes oksijini-18, i-oxygen-17, ne-hydrogen-2 (deuterium) esindayo. Iphesenti ye-isotopes esindayo incane kakhulu, kodwa isathinta izakhiwo zamanzi. Amanzi avela emifuleni nasemachibini avamise ukuba nama-isotopes angasinda kakhulu kunamanzi olwandle. Ngakho-ke, amanzi ajwayelekile achazwa ku-Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water. Okokuphuza
Umzimba womuntu uqukethe, ngokwesilinganiso, amanzi angama-50-60%, kuye ngeminyaka, ubulili nobukhulu bomzimba, nakuba abantu bengaba nanoma yikuphi phakathi kuka-45% no-75%. I-U.S. National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine incoma ukuthi kuphuzwe nsuku zonke amanzi angu-3.7 amalitha (0.98 U.S. amalitha) kwabesilisa abadala kanye no-2.7 L (0.71 U.S. gal) kwabesifazane. Inani elinembile lincike ezingeni lomsebenzi, izinga lokushisa, umswakama, nezinye izici. Okuningi kwalokhu kuthathwa ngokudla noma iziphuzo ngaphandle kokuphuza amanzi aqondile. Izincwadi zezokwelapha zithanda ukusetshenziswa okuphansi, ngokuvamile ilitha elingu-1 lamanzi kowesilisa omaphakathi, okungabandakanyi izidingo ezengeziwe ngenxa yokulahlekelwa uketshezi ngenxa yokuzivocavoca noma isimo sezulu esifudumele. Izinso ezinempilo zingakhipha u-0.8 kuya ku-1 ilitha lamanzi ngehora, kodwa ukucindezeleka okufana nokuzivocavoca kunganciphisa lesi samba. Abantu bangaphuza amanzi amaningi kakhulu kunalokho okudingekayo lapho bezivocavoca, okubabeka engcupheni yokudakwa ngamanzi (hyperhydration), okungaba yingozi enkulu. Isimangalo esithandwayo sokuthi “umuntu kufanele aphuze izingilazi zamanzi eziyisishiyagalombili ngosuku” sibonakala singenasisekelo sangempela kwesayensi. Ucwaningo luye lwabonisa ukuthi ukuphuza amanzi engeziwe, ikakhulukazi kufika ku-500 milliliters (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) ngesikhathi sokudla, kwakuhlotshaniswa nokulahlekelwa isisindo. Ukuphuza uketshezi olwanele kuyasiza ekuvimbeleni ukuqunjelwa. Isincomo sangempela sokuphuzwa kwamanzi ngo-1945 seFood and Nutrition Board ye-U.S. National Research Council sasifundeka kanje: “Izinga elivamile labantu abahlukahlukene liyimililitha elilodwa ngekhalori ngalinye lokudla. Iningi laleli nani litholakala ekudleni okuphekiwe.” Umbiko wakamuva wereferensi yezokudla owenziwe yi-U.S. National Research Council ngokuvamile uyatuswa, ngokusekelwe enanini elimaphakathi eliphelele lamanzi elivela kudatha yenhlolovo yase-US (okuhlanganisa nemithombo yokudla): 3.7 amalitha (0.81 imp gal; 0.98 US gal) kwabesilisa kanye namalitha angu-2.7 (0.59 imp gal; 0.71 isamba samanzi anikezwa abesifazane abangaba ngu-19% wabesifazane abangaba yi-gal) inani lamanzi asetshenzisiwe ocwaningweni. Ngokukhethekile, abesifazane abakhulelwe nabancelisayo badinga uketshezi olwengeziwe ukuze bahlale bemanzi. I-US Institute of Medicine incoma ukuthi, ngokwesilinganiso, amadoda adle amalitha angu-3 (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) futhi abesifazane adle amalitha angu-2.2 (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); abesifazane abakhulelwe kufanele banyuse izinga lokudla libe amalitha angu-2.4 (0.53 imp gal; 0.63 US gal) futhi abesifazane abancelisa ibele kufanele bathole amalitha angu-3 (izinkomishi ezingu-12), njengoba kulahleka uketshezi oluningi kakhulu ngesikhathi sokuncelisa. Okunye okuphawulwe ukuthi ngokujwayelekile, cishe amaphesenti angama-20 amanzi athathwayo aqhamuka ekudleni, kanti amanye aphuma emanzini okuphuza neziphuzo (kufakwe ne-caffeinated). Amanzi akhishwa emzimbeni ngezindlela eziningi; ngomchamo nangendle, ngokujuluka, nangokukhipha umhwamuko wamanzi emphefumulweni. Ngokuzikhandla ngokomzimba kanye nokuchayeka ekushiseni, ukulahlekelwa kwamanzi kuzokwanda futhi nezidingo zoketshezi zansuku zonke nazo zingakhula. Abantu badinga amanzi anokungcola okumbalwa. Ukungcola okuvamile kuhlanganisa izinsimbi ezifana nethusi nomthofu; izinhlanganisela zamakhemikhali ezifana nezibulala-zinambuzane, i-PFAS, noma i-bleach; namagciwane ayingozi, njengeCampylobacter, E. coli O157, neVibrio. Amanye ama-solute ayamukeleka futhi ayafiseleka ekuthuthukiseni ukunambitheka kanye nokuhlinzeka ngama-electrolyte adingekayo. Isisetshenziswa esisodwa esikhulu kunazo zonke (ngevolumu) samanzi ahlanzekile esifanele ukuphuzwa yiChibi iBaikal eSiberia. Ukugeza
Ezokuthutha
Ukusetshenziswa kwamakhemikhali
Amanzi asetshenziswa kakhulu ekuphenduleni kwamakhemikhali njengesixazululi noma i-reactant futhi kancane kancane njenge-solute noma i-catalyst. Ekuphenduleni kwe-inorganic, amanzi ayi-solvent evamile, encibilikisa izinhlanganisela eziningi ze-ionic, kanye nezinye izinhlanganisela ze-polar ezifana ne-ammonia nezinhlanganisela ezihlobene eduze namanzi. Ekuphenduleni kwezinto eziphilayo, ngokuvamile ayisetshenziswa njenge-solvent yokusabela, ngoba ayincibiliki kahle ama-reactants futhi i-amphoteric (i-acidic nesisekelo) ne-nucleophilic. Noma kunjalo, lezi zakhiwo ngezinye izikhathi ziyafiseleka. Futhi, ukusheshisa kokusabela kwe-Diels-Alder ngamanzi kuye kwabonwa. Amanzi abaluleke kakhulu muva nje abe yisihloko socwaningo. Amanzi agcwele umoya-mpilo ashisa kakhulu ukungcola kwemvelo. Ukushintshanisa ukushisa
Amanzi nesitimu kuwuketshezi oluvamile olusetshenziselwa ukushintshanisa ukushisa, ngenxa yokutholakala kwalo namandla okushisa aphezulu, kokubili ukupholisa nokufudumeza. Amanzi apholile angase atholakale ngokwemvelo echibini noma olwandle. Kusebenza kahle kakhulu ukuthutha ukushisa ngokusebenzisa ukuhwamuka kanye nokufingqa kwamanzi ngenxa yokushisa kwawo okukhulu okucashile kokuhwamuka. Okubi ukuthi izinsimbi ezivame ukutholakala ezimbonini ezifana nensimbi nethusi zifakwa oksijini ngokushesha ngamanzi angakahlanzwa nesitimu. Cishe kuzo zonke iziteshi zamandla ashisayo, amanzi asetshenziswa njengoketshezi olusebenzayo (asetshenziswa endaweni evaliwe phakathi kukabhayela, i-steam turbine, necondenser), kanye nesipholile (esisetshenziselwa ukushintshanisa ukushisa kwemfucuza kumzimba wamanzi noma ukukuthwala kuhwamuke embhoshongweni wokupholisa). E-United States, izimboni zokupholisa amandla ziwukusetshenziswa kakhulu kwamanzi. Embonini yamandla enuzi, amanzi angasetshenziswa futhi njengomengameli we-neutron. Kumareactor amaningi enuzi, amanzi ayisipholisi kanye nomongameli. Lokhu kunikeza okuthile kwesilinganiso sokuphepha esingenzi lutho, njengoba ukususa amanzi ku-reactor nakho kunciphisa ukusabela kwe-nuclear. Kodwa-ke ezinye izindlela zikhethwa ukumisa ukusabela futhi kuyakhethwa ukugcina inkaba yenuzi imbozwe ngamanzi ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukupholisa okwanele. Ukucatshangelwa komlilo
Amanzi anokushisa okuphezulu kokuhwamuka futhi awasebenzi kangako, okwenza abe uketshezi oluhle lokucima umlilo. Ukuhwamuka kwamanzi kuthwala ukushisa kude nomlilo. Kuyingozi ukusebenzisa amanzi emlilweni ohlanganisa amafutha nezincibilikisi eziphilayo ngoba izinto eziningi eziphilayo zintanta emanzini futhi amanzi avamise ukusabalalisa uketshezi oluvuthayo. Ukusetshenziswa kwamanzi ekucimeni umlilo kufanele futhi kucabangele izingozi zokuqhuma kwesitimu, okungenzeka uma amanzi esetshenziswa emlilweni oshisa kakhulu ezindaweni ezivalekile, kanye nokuqhuma kwe-hydrogen, lapho izinto ezisabela emanzini, njengezinsimbi ezithile noma ikhabhoni eshisayo njengamalahle, amalahle, noma i-coke graphite, zibolisa amanzi, zikhiqize igesi yamanzi. Amandla okuqhuma okunjalo abonakala enhlekeleleni yaseChernobyl, nakuba amanzi ahilelekile kuleli cala engaveli ekucimeni umlilo kodwa ohlelweni lokupholisa amanzi lwe-reactor. Ukuqhuma kwesitimu kwenzeka lapho ukushisa okweqile kwenkaba kubangele ukuthi amanzi akhanye abe isitimu. Ukuqhuma kwe-hydrogen kungenzeka kwenzeke ngenxa yokusabela phakathi kwe-steam ne-zirconium eshisayo. Amanye ama-metallic oxides, ikakhulukazi lawo ensimbi ye-alkali nezinsimbi zomhlaba ezine-alkaline, akhiqiza ukushisa okukhulu lapho kusabelana namanzi kangangokuthi ingozi yomlilo ingase ivele. I-alkaline earth oxide quicklime, eyaziwa nangokuthi i-calcium oxide, iyinto ekhiqizwa ngobuningi evame ukuhanjiswa ngezikhwama zephepha. Uma lezi zicwilisiwe, zingase zivutha njengoba okuqukethwe kuzo kusabela ngamanzi. Ezokungcebeleka
Abantu basebenzisa amanzi ngezinjongo eziningi zokuzijabulisa, kanye nokuzivocavoca kanye nezemidlalo. Ezinye zazo zihlanganisa ukubhukuda, i-waterskiing, ukugibela izikebhe, ukuntweza emanzini kanye nokutshuza emanzini. Ngaphezu kwalokho, eminye imidlalo, njenge-ice hockey ne-ice skating, idlalwa eqhweni. I-Lakesides, amabhishi namapaki amanzi yizindawo ezidumile abantu abangaya kuzo ukuze baphumule futhi bajabulele ukuzijabulisa. Abaningi bathola umsindo nokubukeka kwamanzi agelezayo ukuzola, futhi imithombo nezinye izakhiwo zamanzi agelezayo ziyimihlobiso ethandwayo. Abanye bagcina izinhlanzi nezinye izitshalo nezilwane ngaphakathi kwama-aquariums noma amachibi ngenjongo yombukiso, ukuzijabulisa, nobungane. Abantu baphinde basebenzise amanzi emidlalweni yeqhwa, njengokushushuluza, ukushwiba, ukuhamba eqhweni noma i-snowboarding, edinga ukuthi amanzi abe izinga lokushisa eliphansi njengeqhwa noma acwebezele abe yiqhwa. Imboni yamanzi
Imboni yamanzi ihlinzeka ngamanzi okuphuza kanye nezinsiza zamanzi angcolile (okuhlanganisa nokuhlanza indle) emakhaya nasezimbonini. Izinsiza zokuhlinzeka ngamanzi zihlanganisa imithombo yamanzi, amathangi okuvunwa kuwo amanzi emvula, imigudu yokuphakela amanzi, nezinsiza zokuhlanza amanzi, amathange amanzi, imibhoshongo yamanzi, amapayipi amanzi okuhlanganisa nemisele emidala. Amajeneretha amanzi asemkhathini asathuthuka. Amanzi okuphuza avame ukuqoqwa emithonjeni, akhishwe emigodini yokwenziwa (imithombo) emhlabathini, noma aphonswe emachibini nasemifuleni. Ngakho ukwakha imithombo eyengeziwe ezindaweni ezanele kuyindlela engenzeka yokukhiqiza amanzi engeziwe, kucatshangwa ukuthi i-aquifer inganikeza ukugeleza okwanele. Eminye imithombo yamanzi ihlanganisa ukuqoqwa kwamanzi emvula. Amanzi angase adinge ukuhlanzwa ukuze asetshenziswe abantu. Lokhu kungase kuhlanganise ukususwa kwezinto ezingancibilikisiwe, izinto ezincibilikisiwe kanye namagciwane ayingozi. Izindlela ezidumile ukuhlunga ngesihlabathi esisusa kuphela izinto ezingancibilikisiwe, kuyilapho ukucwiliswa kwe-chlorine nokubilisa kubulala amagciwane ayingozi. I-distillation yenza yonke imisebenzi emithathu. Kukhona amasu athuthuke kakhulu, njenge-reverse osmosis. Ukuchithwa kukasawoti emanzini amaningi olwandle kuyikhambi elibiza kakhulu elisetshenziswa ezindaweni ezisogwini ezomile. Ukusatshalaliswa kwamanzi okuphuza kwenziwa ngezinhlelo zamanzi zikamasipala, ukulethwa kwamathangi noma njengamanzi asemabhodleleni. Ohulumeni emazweni amaningi banezinhlelo zokusabalalisa amanzi kwabaswele mahhala. Ukunciphisa ukusetshenziswa ngokusebenzisa amanzi okuphuza (aphuzwayo) ukuze asetshenziswe abantu kuphela kungenye indlela. Kwamanye amadolobha anjengeHong Kong, amanzi olwandle asetshenziswa kakhulu ekukhucululeni izindlu zangasese edolobheni lonke ukuze onge imithombo yamanzi ahlanzekile. Amanzi angcolisayo angase abe ukusetshenziswa kabi okukodwa okukhulu kwamanzi; ngezinga lapho ukungcola kukhawulela okunye ukusetshenziswa kwamanzi, kuba ukumosha insiza, kungakhathaliseki izinzuzo kumngcolisi. Njengezinye izinhlobo zokungcola, lokhu akufaki ukubalwa kwezimali okujwayelekile kwezindleko zemakethe, okucatshangwa njengezinto zangaphandle imakethe engakwazi ukuziphendulela. Ngakho-ke abanye abantu bakhokha intengo yokungcoliswa kwamanzi, kuyilapho izinzuzo zezinkampani ezizimele zingaphinde zabelwe umphakathi wendawo, izisulu zalokhu kungcola. Imithi esetshenziswa abantu ivamise ukuphelela emanzini futhi ingaba nemiphumela elimazayo empilweni yasemanzini uma inqwabelana ngokwezinto eziphilayo futhi uma ingaboli. Amanzi angcolile kamasipala nawemboni ngokuvamile ahlanzwa ezikhungweni zokuhlanza amanzi angcolile. Ukunciphisa ukugeleza kwamanzi angcolile kulungiswa ngezindlela ezihlukahlukene zokuvimbela nokwelapha. Izicelo zezimboni
Izinqubo eziningi zezimboni zincike ekuphenduleni kusetshenziswa amakhemikhali ancibilike emanzini, ukumiswa kwezinto eziqinile ezidakamizweni zamanzi noma ukusebenzisa amanzi ukuncibilikisa nokukhipha izinto, noma ukugeza imikhiqizo noma ukucubungula amathuluzi. Izinqubo ezinjengokumbiwa kwezimayini, ukuxubha amakhemikhali, ukuxutshwa kwe-pulp, ukukhiqizwa kwephepha, ukukhiqizwa kwezindwangu, ukudaya, ukuphrinta, nokupholisa izimboni zamandla zisebenzisa amanzi amaningi, okudinga umthombo wamanzi ozinikele, futhi ngokuvamile kubangela ukungcoliswa kwamanzi okuphawulekayo. Amanzi asetshenziswa ekuphehleni ugesi. Ugesi wamanzi ugesi otholakala emanzini. Amandla kagesi avela emanzini ashayela i-turbine yamanzi exhunywe ku-generator. Ugesi wamanzi ungumthombo wamandla obiza kancane, ongangcolisi, ovuselelekayo. Amandla anikezwa ukunyakaza kwamanzi. Ngokuvamile idamu lakhiwa emfuleni, okwenza ichibi lokwenziwa ngemuva kwalo. Amanzi agelezayo aphuma echibini aphoqelelwa ngokusebenzisa izinjini zomoya ezishintsha ugesi. Amanzi anomfutho asetshenziswa ekuqhumeni kwamanzi nasezintweni zokusika ijethi yamanzi. Izibhamu zamanzi ezinomfutho ophezulu zisetshenziselwa ukusika okuqondile. Isebenza kahle kakhulu, iphephile uma kuqhathaniswa, futhi ayilimazi imvelo. Ibuye isetshenziswe ekupholiseni kwemishini ukuvimbela ukushisa ngokweqile, noma ukuvimbela ama-saw blades ekushiseni ngokweqile. Amanzi abuye asetshenziswe ezinqubweni eziningi zezimboni nemishini, njenge-steam turbine kanye ne-heater exchanger, ngaphezu kokusetshenziswa kwawo njenge-solvent yamakhemikhali. Ukuchithwa kwamanzi angahlanziwe asetshenziswa ezimbonini kuwukungcola. Ukungcola kuhlanganisa izinto ezincibilikayo ezilahliwe (ukungcola kwamakhemikhali) kanye namanzi apholile akhishiwe (ukungcola okushisayo). Imboni idinga amanzi ahlanzekile ezinhlelweni eziningi futhi isebenzisa amasu anhlobonhlobo okuhlanza kokubili ekuhlinzekeni kwamanzi nasekukhipheni. Umkhakha wedijithali, ikakhulukazi i-Artificial intelligence isebenzisa amanzi amaningi, ngakho-ke ukunwetshwa kwe-AI "kungasongela ukuphepha kwamanzi emhlabeni jikelele nakuzwelonke". Ukucubungula ukudla
Ukubilisa, ukubilisa, nokubilisa izindlela zokupheka ezidumile ezivame ukudinga ukucwiliswa kokudla emanzini noma isimo saso esinegesi, isitimu. Amanzi nawo asetshenziselwa ukugeza izitsha. Amanzi nawo adlala izindima eziningi ezibalulekile emkhakheni wesayensi yokudla. Izincibilikisi ezifana nosawoti kanye noshukela otholakala emanzini zithinta izici ezibonakalayo zamanzi. Amanzi abilayo namakhaza athonywa ama-solute, kanye nomfutho womoya, wona othintekayo ukuphakama. Amanzi abila emazingeni okushisa aphansi anengcindezi yomoya ephansi eyenzeka ezindaweni eziphakeme. Imvukuzane eyodwa ye-sucrose (ushukela) ngekhilogremu yamanzi ikhuphula indawo yokubilisa yamanzi ngo-0.51 °C (0.918 °F), futhi imvukuzane eyodwa kasawoti ngekhilogremu inyusa iphoyinti elibilayo ngo-1.02 °C (1.836 °F); ngokufanayo, ukwandisa inani lezinhlayiya ezincibilikisiwe kwehlisa izinga lokuqandisa lamanzi. Ama-Solutes emanzini aphinde athinte umsebenzi wamanzi othinta ukusabela kwamakhemikhali amaningi kanye nokukhula kwama-microbes ekudleni. Umsebenzi wamanzi ungachazwa njengesilinganiso somfutho womhwamuko wamanzi esixazululweni sokucindezela komhwamuko wamanzi ahlanzekile. I-Solutes emanzini anciphisa umsebenzi wamanzi-lokhu kubalulekile ukwazi ngoba ukukhula kwamagciwane okuningi kuyeka emazingeni aphansi okusebenza kwamanzi. Ukukhula kwe-microbial akuthinti kuphela ukuphepha kokudla, kodwa futhi ukulondolozwa nempilo yeshelufu yokudla. Ukuqina kwamanzi nakho kuyisici esibalulekile ekucutshungulweni kokudla futhi kungase kushintshwe noma kulashwe ngokusebenzisa uhlelo lokushintshisana ngamakhemikhali. Kungathinta kakhulu izinga lomkhiqizo, kanye nokubamba iqhaza ekukhunjweni kwendle. Ukuqina kwamanzi kuhlukaniswa ngokusekelwe ekugxilweni kwe-calcium carbonate amanzi aqukethe. Amanzi ahlukaniswa njengathambile uma equkethe ngaphansi kuka-100 mg/L (UK) noma ngaphansi kuka-60 mg/L (US). Ngokombiko oshicilelwe yinhlangano ye-Water Footprint ngo-2010, ikhilogremu eyodwa yenyama yenkomo idinga amalitha ayizinkulungwane ezingu-15 (3.3×10^3 imp gal; 4.0×10^3 US gal) amanzi; kodwa-ke, abalobi baphinde bacacise ukuthi lokhu kuyisilinganiso somhlaba wonke kanye nezici ezithinta izimo ezinquma inani lamanzi asetshenziswa ekukhiqizeni inyama yenkomo. Emkhathini
Amanzi amaningi asendaweni yonke akhiqizwa njengomphumela wokwakheka kwezinkanyezi. Ukwakheka kwezinkanyezi kuhambisana nomoya onamandla wangaphandle wegesi nothuli. Uma lokhu kuphuma kwezinto kugcina kuba nomthelela kugesi ezungezile, amagagasi athusayo adalekayo acindezela futhi ashise igesi. Amanzi abonwayo akhiqizwa ngokushesha kule gesi eminyene efudumele. Ngomhla zingama-22 kuJulayi 2011, umbiko wachaza ukutholakala kwefu elikhulu lomhwamuko wamanzi eliqukethe "amanzi aphindwe izikhathi eziyizigidi eziyizinkulungwane eziyi-140 kunawo wonke amalwandle oMhlaba ehlangene" azungeze i-quasar etholakala eminyakeni eyizigidi eziyizinkulungwane eziyi-12 ukusuka eMhlabeni. Ngokusho kwabacwaningi, "okutholakele kubonisa ukuthi amanzi abelokhu egcwele endaweni yonke cishe yonke indawo". Amanzi atholwe emafwini aphakathi kwezinkanyezi ngaphakathi kwe-Milky Way. Amanzi cishe akhona ngobuningi nakweminye imithala, ngoba izingxenye zawo, i-hydrogen, nomoya-mpilo, ziphakathi kwezakhi eziyinsada kakhulu endaweni yonke. Ngokusekelwe kumamodeli okwakheka nokuvela kwe-Solar System kanye naleyo yezinye izinhlelo zezinkanyezi, ezinye izinhlelo eziningi zamaplanethi cishe zinezithako ezifanayo. Umhwamuko wamanzi

Amanzi namaplanethi okuhlala kuwo
Ukuba khona kwamanzi awuketshezi, futhi ngezinga elincane izinhlobo zawo zegesi neziqinile, eMhlabeni zibalulekile ebukhoneni bempilo eMhlabeni njengoba siwazi. Umhlaba utholakala endaweni okuhlala kuyo iSolar System; ukube ibiseduze noma iqhelelene neLanga (cishe ama-5%, noma cishe amakhilomitha ayizigidi eziyi-8), izimo ezivumela ukuthi lezi zinhlobo ezintathu zibe khona kanyekanye bekuyoba mancane kakhulu amathuba okuba zibe khona. Ubukhulu bomhlaba nakho kunendima: amandla awo adonsela phansi avumela ukuba ubambe umkhathi, kuhlanganise nomswakama womoya. Amaplanethi amancane njengeMars anomoya omncane kakhulu noma awekho nhlobo. Umhwamuko wamanzi namanye amagesi abamba ukushisa emkhathini kunikeza isigcinalwazi sezinga lokushisa (i-greenhouse effect) esiza ukugcina izinga lokushisa langaphezulu elizinzile uma likhona ngamanani avamile. Isimo samanzi eplanethini sincike ekucindezelekeni kwendawo, okunqunywa amandla adonsela phansi ale planethi. Uma iplanethi inkulu ngokwanele, amanzi akuyo angase aqine ngisho nasemazingeni okushisa aphezulu, ngenxa yokucindezela okukhulu okubangelwa amandla adonsela phansi, njengoba kwabonwa kuma-exoplanets i-Gliese 436 b kanye ne-GJ 1214 b. Umthetho, ipolitiki, kanye nezinkinga
Ipolitiki yezamanzi ipolitiki ethintekayo ngamanzi namanzi. Amanzi, ikakhulukazi amanzi ahlanzekile, ayinsiza yamasu emhlabeni wonke futhi ayisici esibalulekile ezingxabanweni eziningi zezombusazwe. Idala imithelela yezempilo kanye nomonakalo ezinhlobonhlobo zezinto eziphilayo. Ukufinyelela emanzini okuphuza aphephile kuye kwaba ngcono emashumini eminyaka adlule cishe kuzo zonke izingxenye zomhlaba, kodwa cishe abantu abayizigidi eziyinkulungwane basantula amanzi aphephile futhi abangaphezu kwezigidigidi ezingu-2.5 abakwazi ukuthola ukuthuthwa kwendle. Nokho, ezinye izingqapheli ziye zalinganisela ukuthi ngo-2025 ngaphezu kwengxenye yabantu bomhlaba bazobe bebhekene sengozini ngenxa yamanzi. Umbiko, owakhishwa ngoNovemba 2009, uphakamisa ukuthi ngo-2030, kwezinye izifunda ezithuthukayo zomhlaba, isidingo samanzi sizodlula ukuhlinzekwa kwamanzi ngama-50%. Bangu-1.6 billion abantu asebethole umthombo wamanzi aphephile kusukela ngo-1990. Isibalo sabantu emazweni asathuthuka abathola amanzi aphephile sibalwa ukuthi sesithuthukile sisuka ku-30% ngo-1970 saya ku-71% ngo-1990, ngo-79% ngo-2000, no-84% ngo-2004. ngokungaphathi kahle nenkohlakalo. I-Dihydrogen monoxide parody
'I-Dihydrogen monoxide' yigama lekhemikhali lamanzi elilungile kodwa elingavamile ukusetshenziswa. Leli gama lisetshenziswe ochungechungeni lwamanga nemigilingwane ehlekisa ukungakwazi ukufunda nokubhala kwesayensi. Lokhu kwaqala ngo-1983, lapho isihloko se-April Fools' Day sivela ephephandabeni laseDurand, eMichigan. Indaba engamanga yayihlanganisa ukukhathazeka ngokuphepha mayelana nesidakamizwa. Umculo
