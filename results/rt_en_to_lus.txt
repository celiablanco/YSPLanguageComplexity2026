Ruahtui tlak dan, leilung chungah kum khatah Tt 119 vel a tla hian chi hrang hrang a nei a: a tam zawk chu ruah, vur, leh vur tla a ni a, eng emaw zat chu vur leh ruahtui tlak vang a ni. Dew chu tui tla te tak te, tui vapor density sang takin lei lum nena an inhmuh chuan a condense thin a ni. Dew hi a tlangpuiin zing khaw lum ber lai te, ni chhuah hma lawk leh leilung lumna a san chhoh tan lai te a ni. Thli chhunga tui condensed chuan ni êng a tichhe thei bawk a, rainbow a siam thei bawk. Tui luang chhuak hi luia luang chhuak tuikhuah chungah a awm fo thin. Erosion hmangin tui luang chhuak hian boruak a siam a, lui ruam leh delta a siam a, chu chuan mihring awmna hmun dinna atan lei hausa leh lei level a pe a ni. Tuilian hi leilung hmun, a tlangpuiin hmun hniam tak, tuiin a khuh chuan a thleng a, chu chu luiin a kam a luah emaw, thlipui tleh emaw a nih chuan a thleng thin. Kawng lehlamah chuan ruahtui tlak lohna hi thla emaw kum emaw chhunga hun rei tak, bial pakhatin a tui tlakchhamna a hriat chuan a ni. Tui hausakna hi mihringte tana tangkai thei tur, entirnan tui in tur emaw, tui pekna tur emaw atana hmanraw tangkai tak, natural resources a ni. Tui hi "stock" leh "flows" angin a awm vek. Tui hi lui, tui hnim, leihnuai tui emaw aquifer emaw, leh vur leh vur angin dah theih a ni. Khawvel tui thianghlim volume zawng zawng zinga zaa 69 vel chu glacier leh permanent snow cover-ah dah a ni nia chhut a ni a; zaa 30 chu leihnuai tuiah a awm a; tin, a bak zaa 1 chu lui, lui, boruak leh biota-ah te a awm bawk. Tui dahkhawl hun chhung hi a danglam nasa hle: aquifer thenkhat chu kum sang tam tak chhunga tui dahkhawm atanga siam a ni a, mahse lake volume hi season hrang hrangah a inthlak danglam thei a, ruahtui tlak laiin a tlahniam a, hnim laiin a pung thei bawk. Hmun thenkhata tui supply atanga a tam zawk chu stock-a tui dahkhawm atanga tui lakchhuah a ni a, recharge aia tam lakchhuah a nih chuan stock a tlahniam thin. Mi ṭhenkhatin an chhut dan chuan, tui pekna atana tui hman zawng zawng zaa 30 vel chu leihnuai tui lak chhuah chhunzawm zel a\anga lo chhuak a ni a, chu chuan leihnuai tui a tichhe thei a ni. Tuipui tui leh tides
Tuipui tuiah hian a vaiin sodium chloride 3.5% vel a awm a, chubakah thil dang tlem zawk a awm bawk. Tuipui tui taksa pianphung hi tui thianghlim nen hian thil pawimawh thenkhatah a danglam a. Temperature hniam zawk (−1.9 °C (28.6 °F) vel)-ah a freeze a, a density chu a lum chhoh zel chuan freezing point thlengin a pung a, freezing aia temperature-ah chuan maximum density a thleng lo. Tuipui lianah tui chi (salinity) hi Baltic Sea-ah 0.7% vel atanga Red Sea-ah 4.0% vel a ni. (Tuipui Thi, ultra-high salinity level 30 leh 40% inkar neia hriat lar hi salt lake a ni tak zet a ni.)
Tides chu Tuipui chunga Thla leh Ni tidal force te avanga local sea level cyclic rising leh falling a ni. Tides hian tuipui leh tuipui kam tui thuk zawng a tidanglam a, tidal stream tia hriat oscillating current a siam bawk. Hmun pakhata tide danglam lo chhuak hi Lei nena khaikhin chuan Thla leh Ni awmna hmun inthlak danglam vang a ni a, chu chu Lei inher leh local bathymetry-in a nghawng dan nen a inzawm a ni. Tuipui kam strip, tuipui lian laiin tuiah a pil a, tuipui hniam laiin a lang chhuak a, intertidal zone hi tuifinriat tuifinriat atanga ecological product pawimawh tak a ni. Nun chunga nghawng a neih dan
Biological lam atanga thlir chuan tui hian thil chi hrang hrang tam tak a nei a, chu chu nunna pung zelna atana pawimawh tak a ni. He hna hi a thawk a, organic compound te chu a tawpah replication theihna tur kawng hrang hranga an inrem tir a ni. Nunna chi hriat tawh zawng zawng hi tuiah a innghat vek a ni. Tui hi taksa chhunga thil awm tam tak a inthiar chhuahna solvent angin leh taksa chhunga metabolic process tam takah a pawimawh hle. Metabolism chu anabolism leh catabolism zawng zawng inzawmkhawm a ni. Anabolism-ah chuan tui chu molecule (enzymatic chemical reaction mamawh energy hmangin) lakchhuah a ni a, chu chuan molecule lian zawk (e.g., starch, triglycerides, leh fuel leh information dahkhawmna atana protein te) a tipung a ni. Catabolism-ah chuan tui hi bonds tihchhiat nan molecule tenau zawk (e.g., glucose, fatty acids, leh amino acids te chu energy hmanna atana fuel atana hman tur emaw thil dang atana hman tur) siam nan hman a ni. Tui tel lo chuan heng metabolic process bikte hi a awm thei lo. Tui hi photosynthesis leh thawkna kawngah pawh a pawimawh hle. Photosynthetic cells te hian ni chakna hmangin tui hydrogen chu oxygen atanga an then darh thin. Ni êng awmnaah chuan hydrogen chu CO2 (thli emaw tui emaw atanga lak chhuah) nen inzawmkhawmin glucose siamin oxygen a chhuak thin. Cell nung zawng zawng hian chutiang fuel chu an hmang a, hydrogen leh carbon te chu oxidize in ni chakna chu an man a, chutiang karah chuan tui leh CO2 chu an siam tha leh thin (cellular respiration). Tui hi acid-base neutrality leh enzyme function-ah pawh a pawimawh ber a ni. Acid, hydrogen ion (H+, chu chu proton) donor chu base, hydroxide ion (OH−) ang chi proton acceptor hmangin neutralizein tui a siam thei a ni. Tui hi neutral anga ngaih a ni a, pH (hydrogen ion concentration negative log) 7 chu ideal state-ah a awm. Acids te hian pH value 7 aia hniam an nei a, base te erawh chuan value 7 aia sang an nei thung.Tui chhunga nungcha chi hrang hrang
Lei chung tui hi nunnain a khat a. Nunna hmasa berte chu tuiah an lo lang a; sangha zawng zawng deuhthaw chu tuiah chauh an cheng a, tuifinriata rannung chi hrang hrang, dolphin leh whale te pawh an awm bawk. Ramsa chi thenkhat, tui leh leia nungchate ang chi te hian an dam chhung hmun thenkhat chu tuiah an hmang a, a then chu leiah an hmang bawk. Tuiah hian kelp leh algae ang chi thlai te hi an thang lian a, tui hnuaia ecosystem thenkhat dinna bulpui an ni. Plankton hi a tlangpuiin tuifinriat ei leh in (ocean food chain) dinna bulpui ber a ni. Tuia awm vertebrates te hian an dam khawchhuah theih nan oxygen an neih a ngai a, chu chu kawng hrang hrangin an ti thin. Nga hian lung aiah gills an nei a, mahse sangha chi thenkhat, lungfish ang chi te hian an nei ve ve tho. Tuipuia rannung, dolphin, whale, otter, leh seal te chu boruak thawk turin a hun hunah lei chung an chhuah a ngai a ni. Amphibian thenkhat chuan an vun kaltlangin oxygen an la lut thei a. Invertebrates te hian tui oxygen tha lo takah an dam khawchhuah theih nan siamthatna hrang hrang an lantir a, chung zingah chuan thawkna tube (insect leh mollusc siphons en rawh) leh gills (Carcinus) te pawh a tel. Mahse, tui chhunga invertebrate nunna lo piang chhuak a nih avangin a tam zawk chuan tuiah thawkna kawngah specialization an nei tlem hle emaw, an nei lo emaw a ni. Mihring civilization-a nghawng a neih dan
Civilization hi tunhma atang tawhin lui leh tuipui lian tak takah a lo thang lian tawh a; Civilization awmna hmun (cradles of civilization) tia sawi zinga mi Mesopotamia chu Tigris leh Euphrates lui lian tak takte inkarah a awm a; hmân lai Aigupta mite khawtlâng chu Nile luiah a innghat vek a ni. Indus Valley civilization hmasa ber (c. 3300 BCE – c. 1300 BCE) chu Indus lui leh Himalaya tlang atanga luang chhuak lui kamah a lo thang lian a. Rome ram pawh hi Italian lui Tiber kamah din a ni bawk. Rotterdam, London, Montreal, Paris, New York City, Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Tokyo, Chicago, leh Hong Kong ang khawpui lian tak takte hlawhtlinna chhan chu tui hmanga awlsam taka thlen theihna leh a rah chhuah sumdawnna tihpun vang a ni. Singapore ang bawka tui him lawng chawlhna hmun nei thliarkarte pawh chutiang bawk chuan an lo thanglian ve bawk. North Africa leh Middle East ang hmun, tui tlakchhamna hmunah chuan tui thianghlim in tur hmuh theihna chu mihring hmasawnna atana thil pawimawh tak a ni a, a ni reng bawk. Hriselna leh boruak bawlhhlawh
Mihring ei tur tui tling chu tui in tur emaw tui in tur emaw an ti a. Tui in theih loh chu filtration emaw distillation emaw hmangin emaw, hmanraw dang hrang hrang hmangin in theihah siam theih a ni. Mi maktaduai 660 chuangin tui thianghlim in tur an nei lo. Tui in tur tling lo mahse tui chawi emaw inbualna atana hman a nih chuan mihring tana hlauhawm lo chu in tur emaw tui in tur emaw tih loh hming hrang hrangin an ko a, a then chuan tui thianghlim, emaw "inbualna atana him" an ti bawk. Chlorine hi vun leh mucous membrane irritant a ni a, tui inbual emaw in tur emaw atan a him a ni. A hman dan hi technical tak a ni a, sorkar danin a enkawl tlangpui (a tlangpuiin tui in tur atan part per million (ppm) 1, leh tui inbualna atana bawlhhlawh nena inrem lo chlorine 1–2 ppm). Inbualna tur tui chu chemical disinfectant chlorine emaw ozone emaw hmangin emaw, ultraviolet light hmangin emaw microbiological condition lungawithlak takah dah theih a ni. Water reclamation chu tui bawlhhlawh (a tam zawk chu bawlhhlawh paih, municipal wastewater an ti bawk) chu thil dang atana hman leh theih tur tuiah siam a ni. Tui tlakchhamna hnam zinga cheng mi tluklehdingawn 2.3 an awm a, chu chu mimal tin hian kum khatah tui cubic meter 1,700 (cu ft 60,000) aia tlem an dawng tihna a ni. Khawvel pumah kum tin municipal bawlhhlawh cubic metre tluklehdingawn 380 (13×10^12 cu ft) siam chhuah a ni. Tui thianghlim hi renewable resource a ni a, natural hydrologic cycle-in a recirculate a, mahse a hmuh theihna tura pressure a awm chhan chu naturally uneven distribution in space and time, agriculture leh industry-in economic demand a pun zel vang leh mihring pun zel vang a ni. Tunah hian khawvel hmun hrang hrangah mi maktaduai khat velin tui thianghlim, man tlawm tak an nei lo. Kum 2000 khan United Nations chuan kum 2015-ah chuan khawvel puma tui thianghlim leh bawlhhlawh paihna nei lo mipui zat chu a chanvea tihtlem a nih theih nan Millennium Development Goals a siam a. Chu thil tum thlenna tura hmasawnna chu a inang lo hle a, kum 2015 khan UN chuan kum 2030-ah chuan mi zawng zawngin tui thianghlim leh man tlawm zawk leh bawlhhlawh paihna an neih theihna tur Sustainable Development Goals chu a tiam a, tui quality \ha lo leh bawlhhlawh paihna \ha lo chu thihna thlentu a ni a kum khata thi maktaduai nga vêl chu tui kaihhnawih natna avânga thi an ni. World Health Organization chuan tui thianghlim hian kum tin naupang riltam vanga thi maktaduai 1.4 vel chu a veng thei niin a chhut. Ram changkang zawkah chuan municipal bawlhhlawh tui zawng zawng zinga 90% chu tualchhung lui leh luipuiah tihthianghlim lohvin a la kal zel a ni. Khawvel mihring hmun thuma ṭhena hmun khat vel awmna ram 50 vel pawhin tui tlakchhamna hniam tak emaw, sang emaw an tuar a, heng zinga 17 chuan kum tin tui an la chhuak tam zawk a, an natural water cycle hmanga recharge aiin an tam zawk a ni. Agriculture lam a ni
Mihringin tui a hman tam ber chu agriculture atan a ni a, irrigated agriculture pawh a tel a, hei hi mihring tui hman zawng zawng zaa 80 atanga 90 vel a ni. United States-ah chuan tui thianghlim hman tur lakchhuah zinga 42% chu tui pekna atan a ni a, mahse tui "an hman" (hman tawh leh boruak lama kir leh loh) tam zawk chu agriculture-ah a kal thung. Tui thianghlim hmuh theihna chu thil awlsam takah ngaih a ni fo va, a bik takin ram changkang zawk, tui khawlkhawmna, tihthianghlimna, leh thawn chhuahna tur leh tui bawlhhlawh paih chhuahna tur tui hmanraw changkang tak tak siamtuteah chuan. Mahse economic, demographic leh climatic pressure sang zel hian tui chungchangah ngaihtuahna a tipung a, hei hian fixed water resources lama inelna a tipung a, hei hian peak water concept a siam ta a ni. Mipui leh economy a pun zel avang te, tui tuihal sa ei a pun zel avang te, biofuel emaw tui hmang nasa industry thar emaw mamawhna thar a pun zel avangin tui lama harsatna thar a awm a rinawm. Agriculture lama tui hman dan tehna chu kum 2007 khan Sri Lanka-a International Water Management Institute chuan khawvelin a mihring pung zelte ei tur pe thei tur tui a nei em tih enfiah a ni. Khawvel pum huapa agriculture atana tui awm mek chu a zirchiang a, tui tlakchhamna tuar hmunte pawh map-ah a siam bawk. Khawvel mihring hmun ngaa ṭhena hmun khat, tluklehdingawn 1.2 chuangte chu taksa lama tui tlakchhamna hmunah an cheng tih a hmuchhuak a, chutah chuan mamawh zawng zawng phuhruk thei tur tui a awm lo. Mi tluklehdingawn 1.6 dang chu sum leh pai lama tui tlakchhamna hmunah an cheng a, chutah chuan tui lama investment a awm loh vang emaw, mihring chakna a tlem vang emaw chuan thuneitute tan tui mamawhna chu a phuhruk thei lo a ni. Report chuan nakin lawkah ei tur mamawh siam chhuah theih a nih tur thu a hmuchhuak a, mahse tunlai ei tur siam chhuah leh boruak humhalh chhunzawm zel chuan khawvel hmun tam takah harsatna a thlen dawn tih a hmuchhuak a ni. Khawvel pum huapa tui harsatna awm lo tur chuan loneitute chuan ei tur mamawhna sang zel phuhruk nan productivity tihpun an tum a ngai dawn a, chutih rualin industry leh khawpui te chuan tui hman tangkai dan tur an zawng bawk ang. Tui tlakchhamna hi tui ngaina thil siam chhuah vang a ni bawk. Entîr nân, cotton: Cotton kg 1—jeans pahnih tlukpui—chu siam chhuah nân tui cubic metre 10.9 (380 cu ft) a ngai a ni. Khawvel tui hman zinga 2.4% chu cotton a nih laiin, tui tlakchhamna hmuna awm tawhah tui hi hman a ni. Environment chhiatna nasa tak a awm a: entirnan, Soviet Union hlui-in Amu Darya leh Syr Darya lui atanga tui a diversion a, cotton siam chhuahna chu Aral tuipui bo chhan lian tak a ni. Science tehfung angin
7 April 1795 khan France ramah chuan gram chu "tui thianghlim volume khat, meter zaa khata zau cube khat tlukpui, leh ice hmin temperature-a a rit zawng zawng" nen a inang tih an sawi. Mahse, a hmantlakna atan chuan metallic reference standard a ngai a, chu chu kilogram aiin a let sangkhatin a lian zawk a ni. Chuvangin tui litre khata a rit zawng chiang taka hriat theih nan hna thawh a ni ta a ni. Gram sawifiahna thupek chuan tui chu 0 °C (32 °F)-ah a tarlang a—chu chu temperature siam chhuah theih sang tak a ni—chuti chung pawhin scientist-te chuan standard chu siam thar leh an thlang a, an tehna chu tui density sang ber temperature-ah tih an thlang a, chu chu chutih laia 4 °C (39 °F) anga teh a ni. SI system-a Kelvin temperature scale hi tui triple point-a siam a ni a, chu chu 273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F) dik tak anga sawi a ni a, mahse May 2019 thleng khan Boltzmann constant-ah a innghat zawk a ni. Scale hi Celsius temperature scale nena increment inang, absolute temperature scale a ni a, a tir chuan tui boiling point (100 °C (212 °F)-a dah) leh melting point (0 °C (32 °F)-a dah) atanga siam a ni. Natural water hi isotope hydrogen-1 leh oxygen-16 atanga siam a ni ber a, mahse isotope rit zawk oxygen-18, oxygen-17, leh hydrogen-2 (deuterium) te pawh a tlem hle bawk. Isotope rit zawk percentage hi a tlem hle a, mahse tui property a la nghawng tho tho. Lui leh lui atanga tui lo chhuak hian tuifinriat tui aiin isotope rit zawk a nei tlem zawk thin. Chuvangin, standard water hi Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water specification-ah hian tarlan a ni. Zu in tur atan

Mite chuan exercise an tih laiin a tul aia tam tui an in thei a, chu chuan tui ruih (hyperhydration) hlauhawmna a siam a, chu chuan thihna a thlen thei a ni. Chemical hman dan tur
Tui hi chemical reaction-ah solvent emaw reactant emaw atan hman a ni nasa hle a, solute emaw catalyst emaw atan hman a ni lo. Inorganic reaction-ah chuan tui hi solvent hman tlanglawn tak a ni a, ionic compound tam tak a tithianghlim a, chubakah polar compound dang ammonia leh tui nena inzawm tlat compound te pawh a tithianghlim bawk. Organic reaction-ah chuan reaction solvent atan hman a ni tlangpui lo, a chhan chu reactants te chu a dissolve tha lo va, amphoteric (acidic leh basic) leh nucleophilic a nih vang a ni. Chuti chung pawh chuan heng property te hi a chang chuan duhthusam a ni. Tin, tuiin Diels-Alder reaction a ti chak zawk tih hmuhchhuah a ni bawk. Tun hnaiah supercritical water hi zirchianna a ni nasa hle. Oxygen-saturated supercritical water hian organic pollutants te chu a kang tha hle. Heat inthlak danglamna
Tui leh steam hi heat exchange atana hman tlanglawn tak a ni a, a awm theihna leh heat capacity sang tak avang hian, cooling leh heating atan pawh a ni. Tui lum chu lui emaw, tuifinriat emaw aṭangin pianphungah pawh hmuh theih a ni thei. A bik takin tui vaporization leh condensation hmanga lumna phurh chhuah hi a hlawk hle a, a chhan chu a vaporization latent heat lian tak a nih vang a ni. A chhiatna chu steel leh copper ang chi industry-a hmuh tlanglawn tak tak metal te hi tui leh steam tihfai loh hmangin an oxidized rang zawk a ni. Thermal power station zawng zawngah te chuan tui hi working fluid (boiler, steam turbine leh condenser inkara closed-loop-a hman), leh coolant (waste heat chu tui body-a thlak emaw, cooling tower-a evaporation hmanga phurh emaw) atan hman a ni. United States-ah chuan cooling power plant hi tui hman tam ber a ni. Nuclear power industry-ah pawh tui hi neutron moderator atan hman theih a ni. Nuclear reactor tam zawkah chuan tui hi coolant leh moderator a ni ve ve. Hei hian passive safety measure ang deuh a pe a, reactor atanga tui lakchhuah hian nuclear reaction pawh a ti hniam bawk. Tui hian vaporization heat sang tak a nei a, a inert deuh bawk a, hei hian kangmei tihmitna tui tha tak a ni. Tui a vawt hian mei atanga lumna a phur bo thin. Oil leh organic solvent hmanga kangmeiah tui hman hi a hlauhawm hle a, a chhan chu organic material tam tak tui chungah a inphah a, tui chuan tui kang chu a thehdarh duh vang a ni. Kangmei venna atana tui hman hian steam puak hlauhawmna te pawh ngaihtuah tel tur a ni a, chu chu hmun zau takah kangmei lum tak takah tui hman a nih chuan a thleng thei a, hydrogen puak thei thil, tui nena inrem thei thil, metal thenkhat emaw, carbon lum emaw, coal, charcoal, emaw coke graphite ang chi te chuan tui chu a chhe vek a, tui gas a siam chhuak thei tih te pawh ngaihtuah tel tur a ni. Chutiang puak chakna chu Chernobyl chhiatnaah khan hmuh a ni a, mahse he thubuaia tui inrawlh hi kangmei venna atanga lo chhuak ni lovin reactor-a tui lumna system atanga lo chhuak a ni. Steam puak chu a core a lum lutuk avangin tui chu steam-ah a rawn inher chhuak a ni. Hydrogen puak hi steam leh zirconium lum inkara thil thleng vang a ni thei. Metallic oxide thenkhat, a langsar ber chu alkali metal leh alkaline earth metal te hian tui nena an inrem chuan lumna nasa tak an siam a, kangmei hlauhawmna a lo awm thei a ni. Alkaline earth oxide quicklime, calcium oxide tia hriat bawk hi thil tam tak siam chhuah a ni a, paper bag-ah phurh luh a ni fo. Chûngte chu tuiah an inhnim pil chuan a chhunga thil awmte chu tui nena an inrem angin an kang thei a ni. Infiamna lam hawi
Mihringte hian tui hi intihhlimna tam tak atan kan hmang a, chubakah exercise leh infiamna atan pawh kan hmang bawk. Mi tam tak chuan tui luang ri leh a lan dan chu a thlamuang hle niin an ngai a, tuikhuah leh tui luang dangte chu cheimawina lar tak a ni. Ṭhenkhat chuan sangha leh thing leh mau dangte chu aquarium emaw, tuikhuah emaw chhungah an dah a, chu chu inlanna, hlimhlawp leh ṭhian nihna atan an hmang a ni. Mihringte hian vur infiamna atan pawh tui hi kan hmang a, chu chu skiing, sledding, snowmobiling emaw snowboarding emaw a ni a, chu chuan tui chu ice ang emaw, snow-a crystallized emaw a nih chuan temperature hniam takah a awm a ngai a ni. Tui lama industry
Tui industry hian chhungkaw leh industry te tan tui in tur leh tui bawlhhlawh service (sewage treatment te pawh tel) a pe thin a ni. Tui pekna hmunte chu tuikhuah, ruahtui lakna tur cistern, tui supply network, leh tui thianghlimna hmun, tuikhuah, tui tower, tui pipe te bakah aqueduct hlui te a ni. Atmospheric water generator siam chhuah mek a ni. Tui in tur hi tuikhuah-ah an khawn khawm fo va, leia artificial boring (tuikhuah) atanga lak chhuah emaw, lui leh lui atanga pump chhuah emaw a ni fo thin. Chuvangin hmun remchang takah tuikhuah tam zawk siam chu tui tam zawk siam theihna kawng awm thei a ni a, aquifer-te hian tui luang tling tak a pe thei ang tih ngaihtuah chuan. Tui lakna dangte chu ruahtui khawlkhawm a ni. Tui hi mihring ei tur atan tihthianghlim a ngai mai thei. Hei hian thil hmin lo, thil hmin lo leh microbes hlauhawm tak takte paih chhuah a huam thei a ni. Thil tih dan lar tak chu sand hmanga filter a ni a, chu chuan undissolved material chauh a paih chhuak a, chlorination leh boiling erawh chuan microbes tha lo tak tak a that thung. Distillation hian hna pathumte hi a thawk vek a ni. Technique hmasawn zawk a awm a, chu chu reverse osmosis te hi a ni. Tuipui tui tam tak desalination hi tuipui kama khaw lum leh vawt takah hman thin solution man to zawk a ni. Tui in tur sem hi municipal water system hmangin, tanker delivery emaw, tui bottle angin emaw tih a ni. Ram tam takah sawrkârte chuan mi harsate hnênah pawisa chawi lova tui sem tûrin program an nei a. Mihring ei tur chauha tui in tur (in tur) hmanga hman tihtlem hi duhthlan tur dang a ni. Hong Kong ang khawpui thenkhatah chuan tui thianghlim humhalh nan khawpui pum huapa toilet silfai nan tuifinriat tui hi nasa takin an hmang a ni. Tui bawlhhlawh hi tui hman dik lohna pakhatah chuan a lian ber mai thei; bawlhhlawh pakhatin tui hmanna dang a tihkhawtlai phawt chuan, bawlhhlawh siamtu tana hlawkna eng pawh ni se, chu chu a hmanralna a lo ni ta a ni. Pollution chi dang ang bawkin hei hi market costs standard accounting-ah a lut lo va, market-in a account theih loh externalities anga ngaih a ni. Chutiang chuan mi dangte chuan tui bawlhhlawh man chu an pe a, chutih laiin private firm-te hlawkna chu tualchhung mipui, he boruak bawlhhlawh tuarte hnenah sem leh a ni lo. Mihringin damdawi ei hi tui kawngah a tawp fo a, bioaccumulate a nih chuan leh biodegradable a nih loh chuan tui chhunga nungchate tan nghawng tha lo tak a nei thei a ni. Municipal leh industrial bawlhhlawh hi bawlhhlawh sawngbawlna hmunah tihfai a ni tlangpui. Polluted surface runoff tihziaawmna hi invenna leh enkawl dan hrang hrang hmangin hmalak a ni. Industrial hmanna tur a ni
Industrial process tam tak chu tuiah chemical hmin hmanga reaction, tui slurry-a solids suspension emaw, tui hmanga thil tihthianghlim leh lakchhuah emaw, thil siam emaw, process hmanrua emaw silfai emaw hmanga reaction-ah an innghat a ni. Mining, chemical pulping, pulp bleaching, paper siam, textile siam, dyeing, printing, leh power plant tihlum ang chi process-ah hian tui tam tak hman a ni a, tui lakna hmun bik a ngai a, tui bawlhhlawh nasa tak a thlen fo bawk. Tui hi power siamchhuahnaah hman a ni. Hydroelectricity chu tui hmanga electric hmuh chhuah a ni. Hydroelectric power hi generator nena inzawm tui turbine khalhtu tui atanga lo chhuak a ni. Hydroelectricity hi man tlawm, boruak bawlhhlawh lo, renewable energy source a ni. Energy chu tui chetna atanga pek chhuah a ni. A tlangpuiin lui chungah dam siam a ni a, a hnungah hian artificial lake siam a ni. Lake atanga tui luang chhuak chu turbine hmanga nawr luih a ni a, chu chuan generator a tidanglam a ni. Pressurized water hi water blasting leh water jet cutter-ah te hman a ni. High pressure water gun hi precise cutting atan hman a ni. A thawk tha hle a, a him tawk a, boruak tichhe lo a ni. Machinery lum lutuk lo turin emaw, saw blade lum lutuk lo turin emaw, a lum lutuk loh nan emaw hman a ni bawk. Tui hi industrial process leh machine tam takah pawh hman a ni a, steam turbine leh heat exchanger-ah te pawh chemical solvent atan hman a ni bawk. Industrial hmanna atanga tui thianghlim lo paih chhuah hi boruak bawlhhlawh a ni. Pollution-ah hian discharged solutes (chemical pollution) leh discharged coolant water (thermal pollution) te a tel a ni. Industry hian hmanna tam takah tui thianghlim a mamawh a, tui supply leh discharge-ah pawh purification technique hrang hrang a hmang thin. Digital sector, a bik takin Artificial intelligence hian tui tam tak a hmang a, chuvangin AI tihpun hian "khawvel leh ram pum tui himna a tichhe thei" a ni. Chaw siam dan tur
Boiling, steaming, leh simmering te hi chaw siam dan lar tak a ni a, ei tur chu tui emaw a gaseous state, steam emaw-ah thun a ngai fo thin. Tui hi dishwashing atan pawh hman a ni. Tui hian food science field chhungah pawh hmun pawimawh tak tak a chang bawk. Tui a awm, salt leh sugar ang chi solutes te hian tui physical property a nghawng a ni. Tui boiling leh freezing point hi solutes te hian a nghawng a, chubakah air pressure pawh a nghawng a, chu chu altitude in a nghawng bawk. Tui hi temperature hniam zawkah a boil a, chu chu hmun sang zawka awm air pressure hniam zawk nen a ni. Tui kilogram khatah sucrose (sugar) mole khat chuan tui lumna chu 0.51 °C (0.918 °F)-in a tisang a, kg khatah chi mole khat chuan tui lumna chu 1.02 °C (1.836 °F)-in a tisang a chutiang bawkin, a hmin (dissolved particles) tam chuan tui freezing point a tihhniam bawk. Tui chhunga solutes te hian tui hnathawh a nghawng bawk a, chu chuan chemical reaction tam tak a nghawng a, ei tur a microbes te a lo thang lian zel bawk. Tui hnathawh hi solution pakhata tui vapor pressure leh tui thianghlim vapor pressure ratio anga sawi theih a ni. Tui chhunga solutes hian tui activity a tihhniam a—hei hi hriat a pawimawh a, a chhan chu bacteria tam zawk hi tui activity level hniam takah chuan a tawp thin a ni. Microbial growth hian ei tur himna a nghawng mai bakah ei tur humhalh leh shelf life a nghawng bawk. Tui khauh pawh hi ei tur siamna atana thil pawimawh tak a ni a, chemical ion exchange system hmangin tihdanglam emaw tihfai emaw theih a ni. Thil siam quality nasa takin a nghawng thei a, chubakah bawlhhlawh paihna kawngah pawh hmun a chang thei bawk. Tui khauhzia hi tui chhunga calcium carbonate awm zat atanga thliar hran a ni. Tui hi 100 mg/L (UK) aia tlem emaw, 60 mg/L (US) aia tlem emaw a awm chuan a nem anga classified a ni. Tui leh planetary habitability
Tui tui (liquid water) awmna, leh a tlem berah chuan a gaseous leh solid form te hi khawvela kan hriat angin khawvela nunna awm theihna atan a pawimawh hle. Leilung hi Solar System-a habitable zone-ah a awm a; Ni hnaih deuh emaw, Ni atanga hla deuh emaw (5% vel, a nih loh leh kilometer maktaduai 8 vel) ni ta se, chutiang chi pathumte chu a ruala awm theihna tur dinhmun chu a awm tlem hle ang. Lei lian leh te pawh hian chanvo a nei a: a gravity hian boruak a vawng thei a, chutah chuan boruak hnim pawh a chelh thei a ni. Mars ang planet tenau zawkte hian boruak an nei lo hle emaw, boruak an nei lo emaw a ni. Boruak chhunga tui vapor leh greenhouse gas dangte hian temperature buffer (greenhouse effect) a pe a, hei hian a tam tawk a awm chuan lei chung boruak lum leh vawt (surface temperature) a awm reng theih nan a pui a ni. Planet pakhata tui awm dan hi ambient pressure-ah a innghat a, chu chu planet gravity atanga teh a ni. Planet pakhat chu a lian tawk a nih chuan a chunga tui chu temperature sang takah pawh a solid thei a, hei hi gravity avanga pressure sang tak avang a ni a, hei hi exoplanet Gliese 436 b leh GJ 1214 b-a hmuh a nih angin a ni. Dan, politics leh buaina te
Tui politics hi tui leh tui hausaknain a nghawng politics a ni. Tui, a bik takin tui thianghlim hi khawvel puma strategic resource a ni a, politics buaina tam takah pawh thil pawimawh tak a ni. Hriselna a tichhe a, nungcha chi hrang hrang a tichhe bawk. Kum sawmhnih kalta chhung khan khawvel hmun tinah te pawh tui thianghlim in tur hmuh theihna a tha chho hle a, mahse mi maktaduai khat velin tui thianghlim an la hmu lo va, tluklehdingawn 2.5 chuangin bawlhhlawh paihna tha an la nei lo bawk. Mahse, entu ṭhenkhat chuan kum 2025-ah chuan khawvel mihring zinga a chanve aia tam chuan tui hmanga harsatna an tawk dawn niin an chhut. Report pakhat, November 2009-a tihchhuahah chuan kum 2030-ah chuan khawvel ram changkang thenkhatah chuan tui mamawhna hian supply aiin 50% zetin a pel dawn niin a sawi. Kum 1990 atang khan mi tluklehdingawn 1.6 chuan tui thianghlim an hmu tawh a, ram changkang zawka tui thianghlim hmu thei zat chu kum 1970-a 30% atanga 1990-a 71%, kum 2000-a 79%, leh 2004-a 84%-ah a lo tha chho tawh niin an chhut a, kum 2006-a United Nations report chuan "mi zawng zawng tan tui a tling" tih a tarlang. mahse, chu chu a thlen theihna chu enkawlna dik lo leh eiruknain a tikhawtlai a ni. Chu bakah, khawvel pum huapa tanpuina pek chhuah dan tur tihchangtlunna tura hmalakna, Paris Declaration on Aid Effectiveness ang chite chu tui sector donor-te chuan zirna leh hriselna lama an tih angin an la hlawhtling lo a, hei hian project inzawmkhawma thawktu tam tak leh sorkar dawngtu sorkarte chu hmalakna tur thuneihna nei lovin a awm thei a ni. Kum 2007-a Comprehensive Assessment of Water Management in Agriculture ziaktute chuan tui tlakchhamna chi hrang hrang awm chhan pakhatah rorelna \ha lo an sawi a. Water governance chu tui hman dan tur chungchanga thutlukna siamna atana formal leh informal process set a ni. Tui enkawlna tha chu a bul berah chuan taksa leh khawtlang nunphung bik pakhatah eng kalphung nge thawk tha ber tih hriat hi a ni. Khawvel changkang zawka hnathawk ‘blueprint’ te chu khawvel hmun leh context hmasawn mekte hman tumna hmangin tihsual a awm fo thin. Mekong lui hi entirnan pakhat a ni a; International Water Management Institute-in tui atana Mekong lui rinchhan ram paruk-a policy hrang hrangte thlirletnaah chuan cost-benefit analysis leh environment impact assessment kimchang leh langtlang tak neih a tlem hle tih a tarlang. Cambodia-in tui dan a siam chu a tul aia buaithlak zawk a ni tih an hmuchhuak bawk. Kum 2004 khan UK charity WaterAid chuan second 15 danah naupang pakhat chu tui nena inzawm natna awlsam taka ven theih vangin a thi tih a tarlang a, hei hi bawlhhlawh paihna tha tawk lohna nen a inzawm fo thin. Kum 2003 atang khan UNESCO World Water Assessment Programme siam, UN World Water Development Report chuan thutlukna siamtute hnenah tui chungchanga policy nghet tak siamna hmanrua a pe a ni. Kum 2023 report-ah chuan mi maktaduai hnih (mipui 26%) chuan tui in tur an nei lo a, tluklehdingawn 3.6 (46%) chuan safely managed sanitation an nei lo tih a tarlang. Khawpui chhunga mite (tld 2.4) chuan kum 2050-ah tui tlakchhamna an tawk dawn a, tui tlakchhamna hi endemic anga sawi a ni a, hei hi hman tam lutuk leh boruak bawlhhlawh vang a ni. Report-ah hian khawvel mihring 10% chu tui lama harsatna sang tak emaw, tui harsatna nasa tak awmna ramah an cheng tih a tarlang. Mahse, kum 40 kalta chhung khan kum khatah tui hman hi 1% velin a pung a, kum 2050 thleng pawhin chutiang bawk chuan a pung zel tura ngaih a ni a, kum 2000 atang khan tropics-a tuilian hi a let liin a pung a, hmar lam mid-latitude-ah chuan tuilian hi factor 2.5-in a pung thung. Kum 2000 leh 2019 inkar chhunga heng tuilian avanga sum senso hi mi 100,000 thihna leh dollar maktaduai 650 vel a ni. Tui humhalh chungchanga pawl hrang hrangte chu International Water Association (IWA), WaterAid, Water 1st, leh American Water Resources Association te an ni. International Water Management Institute hian retheihna tihziaawmna atana tui hman dan tha tak hman tumna nen project hrang hrang a kalpui thin. Tui kaihhnawih convention-te chu United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD), International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships, United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea leh Ramsar Convention te an ni. Hmanlai Greek philosopher Empedocles-a chuan tui hi classical element pali (mei, lei leh boruak te nen) zinga pakhat niin a ngai a, ylem, a nih loh leh universe-a thil bulpui angin a ngai a ni. Aristotle-a’n astronomer leh engineer anga a tarlan Thales-a chuan tui aiin leilung hi a denser zawk chu tui atanga lo chhuak niin a theorized a. Thales, monist chuan thil zawng zawng hi tui atanga siam a nih thu a ring belh leh a. Plato-a chuan tui pianzia chu icosahedron – cube ang maia leilung nena khaikhin chuan a luang awlsam hle niin a ngai a. Taksa hlimthla palite theory chuan tui chu phlegm nen a inzawm tir a, chu chu a lum leh hnim a ni. Tui chungchanga classical element pawh hi Chinese philosophy hlui (lei, mei, thing leh thir te nen) element panga zinga pakhat a ni bawk. Asian philosophical system hlui leh lar tak tak thenkhat chuan tui hi entawn tur atan an la thin. Kum 1891-a James Legge-a lehlin Dao De Jing-ah chuan, "Thil ṭhatna sang ber chu tui (that of) ang a ni. Tui ṭhatna chu thil engkim a hlawkpuinaah a lang a, mi zawng zawngin an duh loh hmun hniam tak chu beih ngai lovin (a lehlamah) a luahnaah a lang bawk a ni," a ti. Chuvangin (a kawng) chu (a kawng) Tao" leh "Khawvelah hian tui aia nêm leh chak lo zawk engmah a awm lo va, chuti chung pawh chuan thil nghet leh chak zawk beih nan chuan a hmasa ber tur a awm lo—chutiang taka tihdanglam theih tur engmah (chutiang taka hlawhtling) a awm lo si a." Grimm fairy-tale ("Nunna Tui") leh Russian tui nung leh tui inthliarna (dichotomy) te hi chhinchhiah teh. Dihydrogen monoxide hmanga siam a ni
‘Dihydrogen monoxide’ tih hi technically dik tak, mahse chemical hming hman tlem tak a ni. He hming hi scientific ziak leh chhiar thiam lohna nuihzatna thu dik lo leh prank hrang hrangah hman a ni tawh a ni. Hei hi kum 1983 atang khan a intan a, chutih lai chuan April Fools’ Day article pakhat chu Durand, Michigan-a chanchinbu pakhatah a chhuak a.
