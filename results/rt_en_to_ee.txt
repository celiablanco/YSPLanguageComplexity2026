Tsi nye nusi me agbe mele o si ƒe atike ƒe nɔnɔme nye H2O. Enye atike si me kɔ, mevivina o, meʋẽna o, eye amadede aɖeke mele eŋu kloe o. Eyae nye Anyigba ƒe tɔsisiwo, tawo, kple atsiaƒuwo ƒe akpa vevitɔ. Tsi hã nye nu gbagbe siwo katã wonya ƒe tsi, si me wòwɔa dɔ le abe atike ene. Tsi, esi wònye polar molecule ta la, etoa molecule dome hydrogen kadodo sesẽ si nye akpa gã aɖe le eƒe dzɔdzɔme kple atike ƒe nɔnɔmewo me. Ele vevie na nu gbagbe ƒomevi siwo katã wonya, togbɔ be menaa nuɖuɖu ŋusẽ o alo menye nunyiame sue si me agbe mele o hã. Le eƒe anyinɔnɔ le nugbagbewo katã me, eƒe atikewo ƒe liƒo, eƒe agbɔsɔsɔ le xexeame katã, kple eƒe polarity sesẽ ne wotsɔe sɔ kple eƒe molecule sue ta la, woyɔa tsi zi geɖe be "xexeame katã ƒe ʋeʋẽ". Esi wònye be Anyigba ƒe yame ƒe dzoxɔxɔ kple eƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu te ɖe tsi ƒe teƒe etɔ̃ ŋu vie ta la, tsi nɔa Anyigba dzi abe nu sesẽ, tsi, kple ya ene. Ewɔa tsidzadza le tsidzadza ƒe nɔnɔme me kple aerosols le afu ƒe nɔnɔme me. Tsi kple tsikpe siwo le tsia dzi, si nye eƒe nɔnɔme sesẽ, ye le alilikpowo me. Ne womae nyuie la, tsikpe si le abe kristalo ene ate ŋu adza ɖe sno ƒe nɔnɔme me. Tsi ƒe ya ƒe nɔnɔme nye dzoxɔxɔ alo tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ. Tsi xɔa Anyigba ƒe akpa si ade 71%, eye atsiaƒuwo kple atsiaƒuwoe nye tsi ƒe akpa gãtɔ (anɔ abe 96.5%). Tsi ƒe akpa suewo dzɔna abe anyigba te tsi (1.7%), le tsikpetowo kple tsikpe siwo le Antarctica kple Greenland (1.7%) me, eye le ya me abe yaƒoƒo, alilikpo (si me tsikpe kple tsi tsi si le tsia me le), kple tsidzadza (0.001%) ene. Tsi zɔna ɣesiaɣi to tsi ƒe tsatsam si nye yaƒoƒo, yaƒoƒo (evapotranspiration), tsidzadza, tsidzadza, kple tsi si sina toa eme, zi geɖe la, eɖoa atsiaƒu me. Tsi wɔa akpa vevi aɖe le xexeame ƒe ganyawo me. Tsi nyui si amegbetɔwo zãna ƒe 70% lɔƒo yia agbledede me. Tɔƒodede le dze kple tsi nyuiwo me nye, eye wòyi edzi nye nuɖuɖutsoƒe vevi aɖe na xexeame ƒe akpa geɖe, si naa xexeame katã ƒe protein ƒe 6.5%. Tɔdziʋuwo tsɔa adzɔnuwo (abe ami, dzɔdzɔmeŋusẽ ƒe gas, kple nusiwo wowɔ) ƒe asitsatsa si yia didiƒe ƒe akpa gãtɔ to atsiaƒuwo, tɔsisiwo, tawo, kple tɔdzisasrãwo dzi. Wozãa tsi, tsikpe, kple dzoxɔxɔ gbogbo aɖewo tsɔ naa fafa kple dzoxɔxɔ le dɔwɔƒewo kple aƒewo me. Tsi nye atike nyui aɖe si wotsɔna tsia nu vovovowo, siwo nye mineral kple organic siaa; esi wòle alea ta la, wozãnɛ geɖe le dɔwɔƒewo ƒe dɔwɔwɔwo kple nuɖaɖa kple nudzadzra me. Tsi, tsikpe, kple sno hã nye nu vevi aɖe le kamedefefe kple modzakaɖeɖe ƒomevi bubuwo me, abe tsiƒuƒu, modzakaɖetɔdziʋu, tɔdziʋukuku, ƒutsotsoewo ƒe duɖimekeke, kamedefefe me tɔƒodede, tsi me nɔnɔ, tsikpedzifefe, snodzizɔzɔ, kple snodzizɔzɔ ene. Le Anyigba dzi
Nusiwo le ame si
Tsi (H2O) nye polar inorganic compound. Le xɔme ƒe dzoxɔxɔ nu la, enyea tsi si mevivina o eye meʋẽna o, si me amadede aɖeke mele o kloe eye blɔ sue aɖe le eŋu. Haidrodzin chalcogenide bɔbɔetɔ kekeake, enye atike si ŋu wosrɔ̃ nu tsoe wu sã eye wogblɔna ɣeaɖewoɣi be enye "xexeame katã ƒe atike" le eƒe ŋutete be wòatsɔ nu geɖe alɔ nu geɖe wu tsi bubu ɖesiaɖe ta, togbɔ be mewɔa nu nyuie le nusiwo menye polar o ƒe ʋeʋẽ me o hã. Esia na wòte ŋu nye "agbe ƒe ʋeʋẽ": le nyateƒe me la, tsi abe alesi wokpɔnɛ le dzɔdzɔme me ene la lɔa nu vovovo siwo me wodea tsii ɖe eme ɣesiaɣi kloe, eye afɔɖeɖe tɔxɛwo hiã be woakpɔ tsi si ŋu atike le. Tsi koe nye nusi bɔ si nɔa anyi abe nu sesẽ, tsi, kple gas ene le anyigba ƒe nɔnɔme siwo sɔ me. Dukɔwo
Tsɔ kpe ɖe oxidane ŋu la, tsi nye ŋkɔ eve siwo dziɖuɖua tsɔ na atike si nye H2O la dometɔ ɖeka; enye H2O ƒe tsi ƒe akpa hã. Tsi ƒe nɔnɔme ƒe nɔnɔme eve bubu siwo bɔ enye nu sesẽ, si nye tsikpe, kple ya ƒe akpa, tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ alo dzoxɔxɔ. Dzoxɔxɔ ƒe tsɔtsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu alo eɖeɖeɖa ateŋu ahe phase ƒe tɔtrɔ vɛ: tsikpe (tsi yi tsikpe me), ʋeʋẽ (tsikpe yi tsi me), yaƒoƒo (tsi yi dzoxɔxɔ me), yaƒoƒo (yaƒoƒo yi tsi me), sublimation (tsikpe yi tsikpe me) kple tsikpe (yaƒoƒo yi tsikpe me). Density ƒe kpekpeme
Tsi nye nu ʋɛ siwo bɔ siwo kekena eye womegasẽna o ne wole fafam le dzoxɔxɔ aɖewo me la dometɔ ɖeka; eya koe nye nusi wonya be ele dzɔdzɔme nu si wɔa esia esime wònye tsi. Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, mebɔ o elabena eƒe ʋeʋẽ dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna kura ne ele tsikpe xɔm, togbɔ be menye etɔxɛ le go ma me o hã. Le atm 1 ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu me la, eɖoa eƒe kpekpeme si sɔ gbɔ wu si nye 999.972 kg/m3 (62.4262 lb/cu ft) le 3.98 °C (39.16 °F). Le dzoxɔxɔ ma te, gake wòwu tsikpe ƒe dzoxɔxɔ si nye 0 °C (32 °F) la, tsi kekena (megava nɔa ʋeʋẽm boo o) vaseɖe esime wòɖoa tsikpeƒe, afisi eƒe ʋeʋẽ le tsi ƒe akpa si nye 999.8 kg/m3 (62.4155 lb/cu ft) le. Ne tsi fa va ɖo 3.98 °C (39.16 °F) la, tsi ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna. Ne tsi xɔdzo eye wòzu tsikpe la, tsi kekena abe 9% ene, eye wòɖoa 917 kg/m3 (57.25 lb/cu ft). Kekeɖenudɔ sia ate ŋu ahe nyaƒoɖeamenu gã aɖe vɛ, pɔmpiwo agbã eye kpewo agbã. Abe nu sesẽ ene la, eɖea nuwɔna si bɔ si nye be eƒona ɖe enu eye wòva nɔa ʋeʋẽm ɖe edzi ne ele fafam la fiana. Dzoxɔxɔ ƒe nɔnɔme siawo siwo mebɔ o la gblẽa nu le agbenɔnɔ le anyigba dzi ŋu vevie. Le ta alo atsiaƒu me la, tsi si ƒe dzoxɔxɔ nye 4 °C (39 °F) nyrɔna yia anyime, eye tsikpe dzɔna ɖe edzi, eye wònɔa tsia dzi. Tsikpe sia xea mɔ na tsi si le ete, si wɔnɛ be metsia tsikpe sesẽ o. Ne womekpɔ ametakpɔkpɔ sia ta o la, tsi me nugbagbe akpa gãtɔ siwo nɔa tawo me atsrɔ̃ le dzomeŋɔli. Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, nuwɔna sia si mesɔ o nye dzoxɔxɔ ƒe tsatsam si maa dzoxɔxɔ ɖe ɣletinyigba la ƒe atsiaƒuwo ŋu ƒe akpa vevi aɖe. Magnet-ŋusẽ ƒe ŋusẽ
Tsi nye nusi wotsɔ wɔa diamagnetic. Togbɔ be kadodo mesẽ o hã la, ne magnet siwo kplɔa nu wu la ate ŋu aɖo kadodo ɖedzesi aɖe gbɔ. Akpa ƒe tɔtrɔwo
Le yame ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu ɖeka (atm) me la, tsikpe ƒuna alo tsi xɔa tsikpe (wòsẽna) le dzoxɔxɔ 0 °C (32 °F) me eye tsi ƒoa alo yaƒoƒo ƒoa ƒu le dzoxɔxɔ 100 °C (212 °F) me. Le nyaƒoɖeamenu/dzoxɔxɔ ƒe akpa ƒe nɔnɔmetata me (kpɔ nɔnɔmetata) la, fli siwo ma nu sesẽ kple yaƒoƒo, yaƒoƒo kple tsi, kple tsi kple nu sesẽ dome. Esiawo doa go le teƒe ɖeka si woyɔna be teƒe etɔ̃, afisi akpa etɔ̃awo katã ate ŋu anɔ anyi ɖekae le. Teƒe etɔ̃lia le dzoxɔxɔ si nye 273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F) kple nyaƒoɖeamenu si nye 611.657 pascals (0.00604 atm; 0.0887 psi); enye nyaƒoɖeamenu si bɔbɔ wu si me tsi si nye tsi ate ŋu anɔ. Vaseɖe ƒe 2019 me la, wozãa teƒe etɔ̃lia tsɔ ɖea Kelvin ƒe dzoxɔxɔdzidzenu gɔme. Le dzoxɔxɔ kple nyaƒoɖeamenu siwo lolo wu me la, tsi kple yaƒoƒo ƒe akpawo wɔa akpa si yia edzi si woyɔna be tsi si le vevie ŋutɔ. Woate ŋu aƒoe ɖe enu vivivi alo akekee ɖe enu le nusiwo le abe gas ene kple esiwo le abe tsi ene dome; eƒe nɔnɔmewo (siwo to vovo kura tso tsi si ƒo xlãe tɔ gbɔ) sea veve ɖe eƒe kpekpeme nu. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, le nyaƒoɖeamenu kple dzoxɔxɔ siwo sɔ gome la, ate ŋu atsaka faa kple nusiwo menye polar o, siwo dome lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe akpa gãtɔ hã le. Esia na wòɖea vi le dɔ vovovowo wɔwɔ me siwo dometɔ aɖewoe nye dzoxɔxɔ gã ƒe elektrikŋusẽŋutinunya kple abe atike si mewɔa nuvevi nu gbagbewo ƒe agbenɔnɔ o alo nusi naa nu ʋãna le atikewo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ siwo me lãmenugbagbeviwo le me ene. Le Anyigba ƒe mantle me la, ewɔa dɔ abe solvent ene le mineral ƒe wɔwɔ, eƒe ʋuʋu kple eƒe ƒuƒu me. Tsikpe kple tsi ƒe akpa vovovowo
Tsikpe ƒe nɔnɔme si sɔ le Anyigba ƒe anyime enye tsikpe Ih, si nye akpa si wɔa kristalo siwo ƒe dzogoe ade sɔ. Azɔ hã, ele be cuvette la nadze ƒã le 3500 cm−1 lɔƒo eye wòate ŋu alɔ tsi me o; calcium fluoride nye nusiwo wozãna zi geɖe na cuvette fesre siwo me tsi le la dometɔ ɖeka. Woate ŋu akpɔ Raman-active fundamental vibrations kple, le kpɔɖeŋu me, 1 cm sample cell. Tsi me numiemiewo, ƒumegbewo, kple nugbagbevi bubu siwo wɔa keklẽŋusẽ ate ŋu anɔ tsi si goglo meta alafa geɖe me, elabena ɣe ƒe keklẽ ate ŋu aɖo wo gbɔ. Ɣe ƒe keklẽ aɖeke kloe meɖoa atsiaƒuwo ƒe akpa siwo ƒe goglome mede meta 1,000 o. Tsi si nye tsi (1.333 le dzoxɔxɔ 20 °C (68 °F)) ƒe keklẽŋusẽ sɔ gbɔ sã wu ya tɔ (1.0), si sɔ kple alkanes kple ethanol tɔ, gake ebɔbɔ wu glycerol (1.473), benzene (1.501), carbon disulfide (1.627), kple glass ƒomevi siwo bɔ (1.4 va ɖo 1.6) tɔ. Tsikpe ƒe keklẽŋusẽ ƒe dzesi (1.31) le ʋɛ wu tsi si nye tsi tɔ. Molekyulãwo ƒe polarity
Le tsi ƒe molecule me la, haidrodzin atɔmwo wɔa dzogoe 104.5° kple ɔksidzin atɔm. Haidrodzin atɔmawo te ɖe tetrahedron si ƒe titina le oxygen la ƒe dzogoe eve ŋu. Valence elektrɔnik eve ɖekaɖeka siwo mekpɔa gome le kadodoa me o la le dzogoe eve bubuawo dzi. Le tetrahedron deblibo me la, atɔmawo awɔ dzogoe si ƒe didime nye 109.5°, gake alesi wogbea eve ɖekaɖekaawo dome la lolo wu alesi wogbea woe le haidrodzin atɔmawo dome. O–H ƒe kadodo ƒe didime anɔ abe 0.096 nm ene. Nu bubuwo ƒe molecule ƒe wɔwɔme nye tetrahedral, le kpɔɖeŋu me, methane (CH4) kple hydrogen sulfide (H2S). Gake oxygen nye elektrɔnik-negative wu nu bubu akpa gãtɔ, eyata oxygen ƒe atɔm ƒe akpa aɖe ƒe dzoxɔxɔ nye manyomanyo gake haidrodzin atɔm ya ƒe akpa aɖe nye nyuitɔ. Tsɔ kpe ɖe ƒoƒo ƒe wɔwɔme ŋu la, esia naa molecule la elektrik dipole moment eye wodae ɖe hatsotso me be enye polar molecule. Tsi nye polar solvent nyui aɖe, si lɔ̃a dze geɖe kple hydrophilic organic molecules abe sukliwo kple aha bɔbɔewo abe ethanol ene. Tsi hã ƒoa ya geɖe, abe oxygen kple carbon dioxide ene – mamlɛtɔa naa fizz si nye aha siwo me carbon le, wein siwo le dzo dam kple biyawo. Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, nu geɖe siwo le nugbagbewo me, abe protein, DNA kple polysaccharides ene, ƒoa tsi ɖe tsi me. Tsi kple biomacromolecules siawo ƒe akpa suewo dome kadodoa trɔa asi le protein ƒe babla, DNA ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi ƒe ƒoƒo ɖekae, kple nudzɔdzɔ bubu siwo le vevie na agbe (hydrophobic effect) ŋu. Nu gbagbe geɖe (abe ami kple ami kple alkane ene) melɔ̃a tsi o, si fia be womelɔ̃na le tsi me o. Nu geɖe siwo me agbe mele o hã melɔ̃na o, siwo dometɔ aɖewoe nye ga oxide, sulfide, kple silicate akpa gãtɔ. Haidrodzin ƒe kadodo
Le eƒe polarity ta la, tsi ƒe molecule si le tsi alo nu sesẽ me ate ŋu awɔ haidrodzin kadodo ene kple molecule siwo te ɖe eŋu. Haidrodzin ƒe kadodowo sesẽna abe Van der Waals ƒe ŋusẽ si hea moleculewo ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu le tsi akpa gãtɔ me ƒe teƒe ewo ene. Esia tae tsi ƒe ʋeʋẽ kple ƒoƒo ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme sɔ gbɔ sã wu atike bubu siwo sɔ kplii abe hydrogen sulfide ene tɔ. Woɖe eƒe dzoxɔxɔ tɔxɛ ƒe ŋutete si lolo etɔxɛe (anɔ abe 4.2 J/(g·K)), dzoxɔxɔ si wotsɔ ƒoa ƒui (anɔ abe 333 J/g ene), dzoxɔxɔ si wotsɔna ƒoa yae (2257 J/g), kple dzoxɔxɔ ƒe tsɔtsɔme (si le 0.561 kple 0.679 W/(m·K) dome) hã me. Nɔnɔme siawo nana tsi wɔa dɔ nyuie wu le Anyigba ƒe yame ƒe nɔnɔme dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ me, to dzoxɔxɔ dzadzraɖo kple etsɔtsɔ yi atsiaƒuwo kple yamenutome dome me. Tsi ƒe haidrodzin ƒe kadodowo anɔ abe 23 kJ/mol ene (ne wotsɔe sɔ kple covalent O–H kadodo si le 492 kJ/mol). Le esia dome la, wobu akɔnta be 90% tso elektrikŋusẽ ƒe dɔwɔwɔ gbɔ, gake 10% susɔea ya nye covalent ƒe akpa aɖe. Kadodo siawo gbɔe tsi ƒe yame ƒe tete kple ʋukawo ƒe ŋusẽ sesẽ tsona. Ʋukawo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ fia alesi tsi dina be yeaʋu ayi dzi le mɔ̃ gbadza aɖe me atsi tre ɖe nuheŋusẽ ƒe ŋusẽ ŋu. Nɔnɔme sia dzie ʋuka me numiemiewo katã, abe atiwo ene, ɖoa ŋu ɖo. Ameɖokui ƒe ionization
Tsi nye hydronium hydroxide ƒe ʋeʋẽ si gbɔdzɔ –dasɔ 2H2O ⇌ H3O+ + OH− li, si wotsɔ kpe ɖe hydronium kple hydroxide ions siwo do tso eme ƒe ʋeʋẽ ŋu. Elektrikŋusẽ ƒe tsɔtsɔme kple elektrikŋusẽ ƒe ʋuʋu
Elektrikŋusẽ ƒe tsɔtsɔme medea tsi dzadzɛ o, si dzina ɖe edzi ne ion-nu sue aɖe abe dze si bɔ ene ƒe ʋeʋẽ lilili. Woate ŋu ama tsi si le tsi me ɖe nu vevi siwo nye haidrodzin kple ɔksidzin me to elektrikŋusẽ to eme me—si nye nusi woyɔna be elektrikŋusẽ ƒe tsɔtsɔ. Gbegblẽa bia ŋusẽ geɖe si woatsɔ ade eme wu dzoxɔxɔ si dona to mɔnu si wogbugbɔ trɔna me (285.8 kJ/mol, alo 15.9 MJ/kg). Mɔ̃ɖaŋununya ƒe nɔnɔmewo
Woateŋu asusui be tsi si le tsi me mateŋu aƒoe o le taɖodzinu akpa gãtɔ ta: eƒe nutete tso 4.4 va ɖo 5.1×10−10 Pa−1 le nɔnɔme dzrowo me. Le atsiaƒu siwo ƒe goglome nye kilometa 4 gɔ̃ hã me, afisi tsi ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu nye 400 atm la, tsi ƒe lolome dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna 1.8% ko. Tsi ƒe ʋeʋẽme anɔ abe 10−3 Pa·s alo 0.01 poise le dzoxɔxɔ 20 °C (68 °F), eye gbeɖiɖi ƒe duƒuƒu le tsi si me tsi le me nɔa meta 1,400 va ɖo 1,540 le sɛkɛnd ɖeka me (4,600 kple 5,100 ft/s) le dzoxɔxɔ nu. Gbeɖiɖi zɔa mɔ didiwo le tsi me evɔ meɖea ŋusẽ boo aɖeke o, vevietɔ le gbeɖiɖi siwo bɔbɔ (anɔ abe 0.03 dB/km ene na 1 kHz), si nye nɔnɔme si lãklewo kple amegbetɔwo zãna hena kadodo kple nutome ƒe dzesi (sonar). Nuwɔwɔ ɖe nu ŋu ƒe nuwɔna
Ganuwo siwo nye elektrikŋusẽ wu haidrodzin, vevietɔ alkali gawo eye le mɔ sue aɖe nu la, alkaline anyigba gawo, ɖea haidrodzin ɖa le tsi me, wowɔa haidroksida eye woɖea haidrodzin ɖe go. Ne dzoxɔxɔ sesẽ la, koklozi, si nye carbon ƒomevi aɖe, wɔa dɔ kple dzoxɔxɔ tsɔ wɔa carbon monoxide kple hydrogen. Le Anyigba dzi
Tsiŋutinunya nye tsi ƒe ʋuʋu, eƒe mama, kple eƒe nyonyome ŋuti nusɔsrɔ̃ le Anyigba bliboa dzi. Tsi ƒe mama ŋuti nusɔsrɔ̃e nye tsiŋutinunya. Anyigba te tsiwo ƒe mama kple woƒe ʋuʋu ŋuti nusɔsrɔ̃ nye tsi ƒe anyigbaŋutinunya, tsikpewo ŋuti nusɔsrɔ̃ nye tsikpeŋutinunya, anyigba me tsiwo ŋuti nusɔsrɔ̃ nye limnology eye atsiaƒuwo ƒe mama nye atsiaƒuŋutinunya. Nu gbagbewo ƒe lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ kple tsiŋutinunya le nu gbagbewo ƒe tsiŋutinunya ƒe susu me. Woyɔa tsi gbogbo siwo wokpɔna le ɣletinyigba aɖe dzi, ete, kple etame be tsi ƒe ƒuƒoƒo. Anyigba ƒe tsi ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme (tsi si le xexeame katã) nye kubik kilometa biliɔn 1.386 (kubik kilometa miliɔn 333). Tsi si nye tsi le tsidzɔƒewo, abe atsiaƒu, atsiaƒu, ta, tɔsisi, tɔsisi, tɔdzisasrã, tsita, alo tsiʋe ene me. Tsi siwo le Anyigba dzi ƒe akpa gãtɔ nye atsiaƒu me tsiwo. Tsi hã le yamenutome le nɔnɔme sesẽ, tsi, kple yaƒoƒo me. Enɔa anyi hã abe anyigba te tsi ene le tsidzɔƒewo. Tsi le vevie le anyigbaŋutinunya ƒe dɔwɔwɔ geɖe me. Anyigba te tsiwo le kpe akpa gãtɔ me, eye anyigba te tsi sia ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu kpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe alesi wogblẽa nu le wo ŋui dzi. Tsi si le mantle la me gbɔe dzoxɔxɔ si naa dzotowo dona le nuto siwo me dzotowo le la tsona. Le Anyigba ƒe anyime la, tsi le vevie le yame ƒe nɔnɔme ƒe tɔtrɔ le atikewo kple dzɔdzɔmenuwo siaa me. Tsi, kple le mɔ sue aɖe nu gake wògaɖe dzesi kokoko la, tsikpe hã gbɔe tsikpe gbogbo aɖewo tsɔtsɔ yi anyigba dzi tsona. Tsiƒuƒu siwo wotsɔ yi teƒe bubuwo ƒe ƒuƒu wɔa kpe ƒomevi geɖe siwo me tsikpewo le, siwo nye anyigbaŋutinunya ƒe nuŋlɔɖi le Anyigba ƒe ŋutinya me. Tsi ƒe tsatsam
Tsi ƒe tsatsam (si woyɔna le dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya me be tsi ƒe tsatsam) nye tsi ƒe asitɔtrɔ atraɖii le tsi ƒe tsatsam me, le yame, anyigba me tsi, anyigbadzitsi, anyigba te tsi, kple numiemiewo dome. Tsi zɔna to nuto siawo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe me ɖaa le tsi ƒe tsatsam si me tsi ƒe asitɔtrɔ ƒe ɖoɖo siwo gbɔna le:

ya si dona tso atsiaƒuwo kple tsi bubuwo me yia yame kple ya si dona tso anyigba dzi numiemiewo kple lãwo me yia yame. tsidzadza, tso tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ si ƒo ƒu tso ya me eye wòge ɖe anyigba alo atsiaƒu me gbɔ. tsi si sina tso anyigba dzi va ɖoa atsiaƒu dzi zi geɖe. Tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ akpa gãtɔ siwo wokpɔna akpa gãtɔ le atsiaƒu me la trɔna yia eme, gake yawo tsɔa tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ toa anyigba dzi le agbɔsɔsɔ ma ke me abe alesi tsi si sina yia atsiaƒu me ene, abe Tt 47 ene ƒe sia ƒe esime yaƒoƒo kple yaƒoƒo si dzɔna le anyigba ƒe lolome me hã kpena ɖe Tt 72 bubu ŋu ƒe sia ƒe. Tsidzadza, si ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme nye Tt 119 ƒe sia ƒe le anyigba dzi, ƒe ƒomevi vovovowo le esi: zi geɖe la, tsidzadza, sno, kple tsikpe, eye afu kple zãmu hã kpena ɖe eŋu vie. Zãmu nye tsi suesuesue siwo ƒoa ƒu ne tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ si lolo ŋutɔ do go anyigba fafɛ aɖe. Zi geɖe la, zãmu dzɔna le ŋdi me ne yame ƒe dzoxɔxɔ le sue wu, do ŋgɔ teti na ɣedzeƒe kple ne anyigba ƒe yame ƒe dzoxɔxɔ te dzidziɖedzi. Tsi si ƒo ƒu ɖe yame hã ate ŋu agblẽ ɣe ƒe keklẽ nu wòazu dziŋgɔli. Zi geɖe la, tsi siwo sina toa tsidzɔƒe siwo sina yia tɔsisiwo me dzi. To tsiɖɔɖɔ me la, tsi si sina toa eme la trɔa asi le nutome ŋu si wɔnɛ be tɔsisiwo ƒe baliwo kple tɔʋu siwo naa anyigba si me kesinɔnuwo le kple anyigba si le gbadzaa hena amewo ƒe nɔƒewo ɖoɖo. Tsiɖɔɖɔ dzɔna ne tsi xɔ anyigba ƒe akpa aɖe, si zi geɖe la, eƒe anyigba bɔbɔ, si dzɔna ne tɔsisi aɖe yɔ eƒe gowo dzi alo ahom ƒe ƒutsotsoe aɖe dzɔ. Le go bubu me la, kuɖiɖi nye ɣleti alo ƒe geɖe ƒe ɣeyiɣi didi aɖe si me nuto aɖe de dzesii be tsi si le ye si la mesɔ gbɔ o. Esia dzɔna ne nuto aɖe xɔ tsidzadza si mede mamã dedie nu ɣesiaɣi o le eƒe anyigba ƒe nɔnɔme ta alo le afisi wòle le eƒe didime nu ta. Tsi ƒe nunɔamesiwo
Tsidzɔƒewo nye tsi ƒe dzɔdzɔmenunɔamesi siwo ate ŋu aɖe vi na amegbetɔwo, le kpɔɖeŋu me, abe tsinono alo tsidede tsidzɔƒe ene. Tsi dzɔna abe "stocks" kple "flows" siaa ene. Woate ŋu adzra tsi ɖo abe ta, tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ, anyigba te tsi alo tsidzɔƒe, kple tsikpe kple sno ene. Le xexeame katã ƒe tsi nyuiwo ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ bliboa me la, wobu akɔnta be wodzraa wo dometɔ 69 le alafa me ɖo ɖe tsikpewo kple sno si nɔa anyi ɖaa me; 30 le alafa me le anyigba te tsiwo me; eye 1 le alafa me susɔea le tawo, tɔsisiwo, yamenutome, kple nugbagbewo me. Ɣeyiɣi didi si tsi nɔa nudzraɖoƒe la trɔna ŋutɔ: tsidzɔƒe aɖewo nyea tsi siwo wodzrana ɖo le ƒe akpe geɖe me, gake taawo ƒe lolome ate ŋu atrɔ le ɣeyiɣi aɖewo nu, aɖiɖi le kuɖiɖiɣiwo eye wòadzi ɖe edzi le tsidzaɣiwo. Tsi si wokpɔna le nuto aɖewo me ƒe akpa gã aɖe nyea tsi si woɖe tso tsi siwo wodzra ɖo ɖe tsidzraɖoƒewo me, eye ne tsi siwo woɖena le tsia me wu tsi si wogbugbɔ dea tsi me la, tsi siwo le tsidzraɖoƒewo dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna. Le akɔntabubu aɖewo nu la, tsi siwo katã wozãna tsɔ dea tsii ƒe alafa memama 30 tsoa anyigba te tsi siwo woɖena ɖa le mɔ si mate ŋu anɔ anyi ɖaa o nu, si wɔnɛ be anyigba te tsiwo vɔna. Atsiaƒu me tsi kple ƒutsotsoewo
Sodium chloride si ade 3.5% le atsiaƒu me tsi me le mama dedie nu, tsɔ kpe ɖe nu bubu siwo le sue wu ŋu. Atsiaƒumetsi ƒe nɔnɔme ŋutɔŋutɔ to vovo na tsi nyui le go vevi aɖewo me. Exɔa tsikpe le dzoxɔxɔ si bɔbɔ wu (anɔ abe −1.9 °C (28.6 °F)) me eye eƒe kpekpeme dzina ɖe edzi ne dzoxɔxɔa le ɖiɖim va ɖoa tsikpeƒe, le esi teƒe be wòaɖo ʋeʋẽ si sɔ gbɔ wu gbɔ le dzoxɔxɔ si wu tsikpe me. Tsi ƒe dze le atsiaƒu gãwo me toa vovo tso abe 0.7% le Baltik Ƒua me va ɖo 4.0% le Ƒudzĩa me. (Ƒukukua, si wonya be eƒe dze sɔ gbɔ ŋutɔ si ade 30 va ɖo 40% la nye dzeta ŋutɔŋutɔ.)
Ƒutsotsoewo nye nutoa me atsiaƒu ƒe kɔkɔme ƒe dziyiyi kple eƒe ɖiɖi le tsatsam si Ɣleti kple Ɣe ƒe ƒutsotsoewo ƒe ŋusẽ siwo wɔa dɔ ɖe atsiaƒuwo dzi hena vɛ. Ƒutsotsoewo naa tɔtrɔwo vaa atsiaƒu kple tɔʋuwo ƒe tsidzɔƒewo ƒe goglome eye wòhea tsi siwo le ʋuʋum siwo woyɔna be ƒutsotsoewo ƒe tɔsisiwo vɛ. Ƒutsotsoe si le tɔtrɔm si dona le teƒe aɖe la nye Ɣleti kple Ɣe ƒe nɔƒe siwo le tɔtrɔm ne wotsɔe sɔ kple Anyigba tsɔ kpe ɖe Anyigba ƒe tɔtrɔ kple teƒea ƒe tsiƒuƒu ƒe ŋusẽkpɔɖeamedziwo ŋu. Ƒuta ƒe akpa si nyrɔna ne ƒutsotsoewo le sesẽm eye wòdzena le ƒutsotsoewo ƒe sesẽɣi, si nye ƒutsotsoewo dome nuto, nye nu vevi aɖe si atsiaƒu ƒe ƒutsotsoewo dona le lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ me. Ŋusẽ si wòkpɔna ɖe agbe dzi
Le nugbagbeŋutinunya ƒe nukpɔsusu nu la, nɔnɔme vovovo geɖe le tsi ŋu siwo le vevie ŋutɔ na agbe ƒe dzidziɖedzi. Ewɔa akpa sia to mɔɖeɖe na lãmenugbagbeviwo be woawɔ nu le mɔ siwo ana woate ŋu agbugbɔ wo awɔ wo nɔewo mlɔeba nu. Agbe ƒomevi siwo katã wonya la nɔ te ɖe tsi dzi. Tsi le vevie ŋutɔ abe atike si me ŋutilãa me tsi geɖe ƒoa ƒu ɖo ene eye wònyea nu vevi aɖe le lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ geɖe siwo le ŋutilãa me me siaa. Metabolism nye anabolism kple catabolism ƒe ƒuƒoƒo. Le anabolism me la, woɖea tsi ɖa le moleculewo me (to ŋusẽ si hiã enzymatic chemical reactions me) be woatsi molecule gãwo (e.g., starches, triglycerides, kple proteins hena dzotsinuwo kple nyatakakawo dzadzraɖo). Le catabolism me la, wozãa tsi tsɔ gbãa kadodowo tsɔ wɔa molecule suewo (e.g., sukli, ami, kple amino acid siwo woazã na dzotsinuwo hena ŋusẽzazã alo taɖodzinu bubuwo). Tsi manɔmee la, lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ tɔxɛ siawo mate ŋu anɔ anyi o. Tsi nye nu vevi aɖe le keklẽŋusẽ ƒe dɔwɔwɔ kple gbɔgbɔ siaa me. Lãmenugbagbevi siwo wɔa keklẽŋusẽ zãa ɣe ƒe ŋusẽ tsɔ mãa tsi ƒe haidrodzin tso ɔksidzin me. Ne ɣe ƒe keklẽ le afima la, wotsɔa haidrodzin tsakana kple CO2 (si woxɔna tso ya alo tsi me) tsɔ wɔa sukli eye woɖea ɔksidzin ɖa. Lãmenugbagbevi gbagbewo katã zãa dzotsinu mawo eye wowɔa oxidation na haidrodzin kple carbon tsɔ léa ɣea ƒe ŋusẽ eye wòtrɔa asi le tsi kple CO2 ŋu le esia wɔwɔ me (lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe gbɔgbɔ). Tsi hã nye nu vevi aɖe le acid-base ƒe akpaɖekedzimademade kple enzyme ƒe dɔwɔwɔ me. Woate ŋu atsɔ gɔmeɖoanyi, proton xɔla abe hydroxide ion (OH−) awɔ tsi, si nye haidrodzin ion (H+, si nye proton) nunala, wòazu nusi mewɔa dɔ o. Wobua tsi be medea akpa aɖeke dzi o, eye eƒe pH (haidrodzin ion ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme ƒe nuŋlɔɖi manyomanyo) nye 7 le nɔnɔme nyuitɔ me. Acid ƒe pH ƒe xexlẽme mede 7 o esime basewo ƒe asixɔxɔ lolo wu 7. Tsi me nugbagbe ƒomeviwo
Agbe yɔ anyigba ƒe gota tsiwo me fũ. Agbe gbãtɔwo dze le tsi me; tɔmelãwo katã kloe nɔa tsi me ko, eye ƒumelã ƒomevi geɖe siwo naa no wo viwo, abe tɔmelã siwo woyɔna be dolphins kple whale ene, li. Lã ƒomevi aɖewo, abe tsi kple tsi me lãwo ene, zãa woƒe agbe ƒe akpa aɖewo ɖe tsi me eye woƒe akpa aɖewo nɔa anyigba dzi. Numiemiewo abe kelp kple algae ene tsina le tsia me eye woawo dzie wotu tsi te lãwo ƒe agbenɔnɔ aɖewo ɖo. Zi geɖe la, plankton ye nyea atsiaƒu me nuɖuɖu ƒe kɔsɔkɔsɔ ƒe gɔmeɖoanyi. Ele be tsi me lã siwo ƒe akɔta naxɔ ɔksidzin hafi woate ŋu anɔ agbe, eye wowɔa esia le mɔ vovovowo nu. Tɔmelãwo ƒe lãkusiwo le esi teƒe be woƒe lãkusiwo nanɔ wo ŋu, togbɔ be evea siaa le tɔmelã ƒomevi aɖewo abe lãkusi me tɔmelãwo ene ŋu hã. Ele be ƒumelã siwo naa no wo viwo, abe tɔmelã siwo woyɔna be dolphins, whale, otters, kple seals ene nanɔ tsia dzi ɣeaɖewoɣi hafi woate ŋu agbɔ ya. Tsi kple tsi kple anyigba me lã aɖewo te ŋu xɔa ɔksidzin to woƒe ŋutigbalẽ me. Lã siwo me akɔta mele o ɖea tɔtrɔ vovovowo fiana be woate ŋu anɔ agbe le tsi siwo me oxygen mele o me siwo dometɔ aɖewoe nye gbɔgbɔtsixe ƒe mɔ̃wo (kpɔ nudzodzoewo kple lãklewo ƒe siphons) kple gills (Carcinus). Gake esi lã siwo me akɔta mele o ƒe agbenɔnɔ va dzɔ le tsi me ta la, wo dometɔ akpa gãtɔ mekpɔa yaƒoƒo le tsi me etɔxɛ kura o alo womewɔa dɔ aɖeke kura o. Ŋusẽ si wòkpɔna ɖe amegbetɔ ƒe dekɔnuwo dzi
Le ŋutinya me la, dekɔnuwo dzi ɖe edzi ƒo xlã tɔsisiwo kple tsimɔ gãwo; Mesopotamia, si nye nuto siwo woyɔna be dekɔnuwo ƒe vidzidɔwo dometɔ ɖeka, nɔ Tigris kple Frat tɔsisi gãwo dome; Egiptetɔwo ƒe blema habɔbɔa nɔ te ɖe Nil-tɔsisia dzi bliboe. Indus Bali me dekɔnu gbãtɔ (ƒe 3300 D.M.Ŋ. lɔƒo – ƒe 1300 D.M.Ŋ. lɔƒo) va do le Indus Tɔsisi kple alɔdze siwo sina doa go tso Himalaya-towo dzi la to. Woɖo Roma hã ɖe Italytɔwo ƒe Tiber-tɔsisia to. Dugã gãwo abe Rotterdam, London, Montreal, Paris, New York City, Buenos Aires, Shanghai, Tokyo, Chicago, kple Hong Kong ƒe dzidzedzekpɔkpɔ ƒe akpa aɖe tso alesi woate ŋu aɖo wo bɔbɔe to tsi dzi kple asitsatsa ƒe kekeɖenudɔ si do tso eme gbɔ. Susu ma ke tae ƒukpo siwo dzi tsi melidzeƒewo le dedie le, abe Singapore ene, dzi ɖe edzi. Le teƒewo abe Dziehe Afrika kple Titina Ɣedzeƒe ene, afisi tsi mebɔ ɖo wu o la, tsi nyui nono ƒe amesinɔnɔ nye nu vevi aɖe si na amegbetɔwo tsi eye wògale nenema hã. Lãmesẽ kple ɖiƒoƒo
Woyɔa tsi si sɔ na amegbetɔ ƒe nuɖuɖu be tsinono alo tsinono. Woate ŋu ana tsi si womate ŋu ano o la nano to tsi si woɖe tso eme alo tsi si woɖe tso eme me, alo to mɔnu bubu vovovowo dzi. Ame miliɔn 660 kple edzivɔ mekpɔa tsi nyui nono o. Tsi si mesɔ na nono o gake megblẽa nu le amegbetɔwo ŋu ne wozãe hena tsiƒuƒu alo tsilele o la, woyɔa ŋkɔ vovovowo tsɔ wu tsinono alo tsinono, eye woyɔnɛ ɣeaɖewoɣi be tsi nyui, alo "si le dedie na tsilele". Chlorine nye nusi doa dziku na ŋutigbalẽ kple lãkusi si wozãna tsɔ naa tsi nɔa dedie na tsilele alo nono. Eƒe zazã nye mɔ̃ɖaŋununya ŋutɔ eye zi geɖe la, dziɖuɖu ƒe sededewo léa ŋku ɖe eŋu (zi geɖe la, akpa 1 le miliɔn ɖeka me (ppm) na tsinono, kple chlorine 1–2 ppm si mewɔ dɔ kple ɖiƒoƒo haɖe o hena tsilele tsi). Woate ŋu azã atike siwo wua dɔlékuiwo abe chlorine alo ozone ene alo alé tsi si woatsɔ aklɔ tsi la ɖe nugbagbevi suewo ƒe nɔnɔme si me dzidzeme le me alo to kekeli si me dzo le zazã me. Tsi gbugbɔgaɖɔɖo nye alesi wotrɔa tsi gbegblẽwo (zi geɖe la, tsi gbegblẽwo, si woyɔna hã be dugã me tsi gbegblẽwo) wòzua tsi si woate ŋu azã ake hena nu bubuwo wɔwɔ. Ame biliɔn 2.3 ye le dukɔ siwo me tsi mesɔ gbɔ ɖo o me, si fia be ame ɖesiaɖe xɔa tsi si mede kubik meta 1,700 o ƒe sia ƒe. Wowɔa dugã me tsi gbegblẽ siwo ƒe lolome nye kubik meta biliɔn 380 (13×10^12 cu ft) le xexeame katã ƒe sia ƒe. Tsi nyui nye nusi woate ŋu awɔ yeyee, si dzɔdzɔme tsi ƒe tsatsam gbugbɔna trɔna, gake nyaƒoɖeamenu siwo le edziyiyi ŋu tsoa dzɔdzɔme ƒe mama si mesɔ o le yamenutome kple ɣeyiɣi me, ganyawo ƒe didi siwo le dzidzim ɖe edzi le agbledede kple dɔwɔƒewo gbɔ, kple amewo ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ si le dzidzim ɖe edzi gbɔ. Fifia la, ame siwo ade biliɔn ɖeka le xexeame godoo mekpɔa tsi nyui si ƒe asi bɔbɔ o. Le ƒe 2000 me la, Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ɖo Ƒe Akpe Ðeka ƒe Ŋgɔyiyi ƒe Taɖodzinuwo be tsi naɖe amesiwo si tsi nyui kple dzadzɛnyenye mele o le xexeame katã ƒe afã dzi akpɔtɔ va ɖo ƒe 2015 me. Ŋgɔyiyi si wowɔ ɖe taɖodzinu ma gbɔ mesɔ o, eye le ƒe 2015 me la, Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo tsɔ eɖokui na Ŋgɔyiyi si Li Eteƒe Didi ƒe Taɖodzinu siwo nye be yeaɖo tsi kple dzadzɛnyenye si le dedie eye wòxɔ asi na amewo katã gbɔ le ƒe 2030. Tsi ƒe nyonyome gbegblẽ kple dzadzɛnyenye gbegblẽ wua ame; dɔléle siwo tso tsi gbɔe nana ame siwo ade miliɔn atɔ̃ kuna ƒe sia ƒe. Xexeame ƒe Lãmesẽ Habɔbɔ bu akɔnta be tsi nyui ate ŋu axe mɔ na ɖevi miliɔn 1.4 ƒe ku le aɖuɖɔtoe ta ƒe sia ƒe. Le dukɔ madeŋgɔwo me la, dugã me tsi gbegblẽwo katã ƒe 90% gakpɔtɔ yia nutoa me tɔsisiwo kple tɔsisiwo me. Dukɔ siwo ade 50, siwo me xexeamenɔlawo ƒe akpa etɔ̃lia lɔƒo le hã kpea fu le tsi ƒe veve le titina alo esi lolo me eye esiawo dometɔ 17 ɖea tsi geɖe ƒe sia ƒe wu esi wogbugbɔna toa woƒe dzɔdzɔme tsi ƒe tsatsam me. Menye ɖeko ƒomevi sia gblẽa nu le tsi nyui siwo le anyigba dzi abe tɔsisiwo kple tawo ene ŋu ko o, ke egblẽa nu le anyigba te tsiwo hã ŋu. Amegbetɔ ƒe nuzazãwo
Agbledede
Tsi si amegbetɔwo zãna wue nye agbledede, si me agbledede si wode tsii hã le, si nye tsi si amegbetɔwo zãna katã ƒe alafa memama 80 va ɖo 90. Le United States la, tsi nyui siwo woɖena ɖa be woazã la ƒe 42% nyea tsidede agble, gake tsi si "zã" (si wozãna eye wometrɔna yia nutome o) ƒe akpa gãtɔ yia agbledede me. Zi geɖe la, wobua tsi nyui ƒe amesinɔnɔ nu tsɛe, vevietɔ le dukɔ deŋgɔ siwo tu tsi ƒe ɖoɖo deŋgɔwo hena tsi nuƒoƒoƒu, kɔklɔ, kple etsɔtsɔ yi teƒe bubuwo, kple tsi gbegblẽwo ɖeɖeɖa me. Gake ganyawo, amewo ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ, kple yame ƒe nɔnɔme ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu siwo le dzidzim ɖe edzi na amewo le dzi tsim ɖe tsinyawo ŋu geɖe wu, si na be hoʋiʋli le tsidzɔƒe siwo li ɖaa ŋu le dzidzim ɖe edzi, si na wova le tsi ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ si sɔ gbɔ wu ƒe nukpɔsusu vɛ. Esi amewo ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ kple ganyawo yi edzi le dzidzim ɖe edzi, lã siwo tsikɔ ​​le wuwum la ɖuɖu le ta kekem, eye mɔ̃memi yeyewo alo dɔwɔƒe yeye siwo zãa tsi geɖe ƒe didi le dzidzim ɖe edzi la, anɔ eme be tsi ƒe kuxi yeyewo ate ŋu ado mo ɖa. Dukɔwo Dome Tsidzikpɔkpɔ Dɔwɔƒe si le Sri Lanka wɔ tsizazã nyuie le agbledede me ŋuti numekuku aɖe le ƒe 2007 me be woakpɔe ɖa be tsi sɔ gbɔ le xexeame si be wòana nuɖuɖu eƒe ameha siwo le dzidzim ɖe edzi hã. Eda tsi si li fifia na agbledede kpɔ le xexeame katã eye wòwɔ anyigbatata na teƒe siwo tsi ƒe veve le fu ɖem na. Ekpɔe be xexeamenɔlawo ƒe akpa atɔ̃lia, siwo wu biliɔn 1.2, le nuto siwo me tsi ŋutɔŋutɔ mebɔ ɖo o, afisi tsi mesɔ gbɔ le be woatsɔ akpɔ nudidi ɖesiaɖe gbɔ o. Ame biliɔn 1.6 bubuwo le nuto siwo me tsi ƒe veve le ganyawo me le, afisi gadede tsi me ƒe anyimanɔmanɔ alo amegbetɔ ƒe ŋutete si mesɔ gbɔ o na be dziɖuɖumegãwo mate ŋu akpɔ tsi ƒe didi gbɔ o. Nyatakakaa ɖee fia be anya wɔ be woawɔ nuɖuɖu si hiã le etsɔme, gake ne egbe nuɖuɖu wɔwɔ kple nutome ƒe nɔnɔmewo yi edzi ahe xaxa vɛ le xexeame ƒe akpa geɖe. Be agbledelawo naƒo asa na xexeame katã ƒe tsikuxi la, ahiã be woadze agbagba adzi woƒe kutsetsewo ɖe edzi be woakpɔ nuɖuɖu ƒe didi si le dzidzim ɖe edzi gbɔ, evɔ dɔwɔƒewo kple dugãwo ya le mɔ siwo dzi woato azã tsi nyuie wu la dim. Tsi ƒe veve hã tsoa nusiwo me tsi mesɔ gbɔ ɖo o wɔwɔ gbɔ. Le kpɔɖeŋu me, kotoklobo: Kotoklobo kilogram 1—si sɔ kple jeans ɖeka—hiã tsi kubik meta 10.9 (cu ft 380) hafi woate ŋu awɔe. Togbɔ be kotoklobo nye xexeame ƒe tsizazã ƒe 2.4% hã la, wozãa tsia le nuto siwo me tsi ƒe veve le afɔku me xoxo. Nu gblẽ le nutome ŋu vevie: le kpɔɖeŋu me, tsi si Soviet Union tsãtɔ trɔ tso Amu Darya kple Syr Darya tɔsisiwo me be woatsɔ awɔ kotokloboe la gbɔe Aral-ƒua bu vevie. Abe dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya ƒe dzidzenu ene
Le 7 April 1795 dzi la, woɖe gram gɔme le France be esɔ kple "tsi dzadzɛ ƒe lolome si sɔ kple kubi si ƒe lolome nye meta alafa ɖeka, kple le tsikpe si le ƒoƒom ƒe dzoxɔxɔ nu". Gake le taɖodzinu ŋutɔŋutɔwo gome la, wobia be woatsɔ ga si woatsɔ akpɔ nu dzii, si lolo wu kilogram la zi gbɔ zi akpe ɖeka. Eyata wode dɔ asi na dɔwɔwɔ be woanya tsi lita ɖeka ƒe lolome pɛpɛpɛ. Togbɔ be gram la ƒe gɔmesese si wode se na la gblɔ tsi si le 0 °C (32 °F)—si nye dzoxɔxɔ si woate ŋu agbugbɔ awɔ ŋutɔ—la hã la, dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunyalawo tiae be yewoagbugbɔ aɖɔ dzidzenua eye yewoawɔ yewoƒe dzidzenuwo le dzoxɔxɔ si me tsi sɔ gbɔ ɖo wu me, si wodzidze ɣemaɣi be enye 4 °C (39 °F). Wotu Kelvin ƒe dzoxɔxɔdzidzenu si le SI ɖoɖoa me ɖe tsi ƒe teƒe etɔ̃ dzi, si woɖe fia be enye 273.16 K (0.01 °C; 32.02 °F) pɛpɛpɛ, gake tso May 2019 me la, wotu ɖe Boltzmann ƒe nɔnɔme madzudzɔmadzudzɔe dzi boŋ. Nudzidzenu nye dzoxɔxɔdzidzenu blibo si ƒe dzidziɖedzi sɔ kple Celsius dzoxɔxɔdzidzenu, si gɔme woɖe le gɔmedzedzea me le tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ (si woɖo ɖe 100 °C (212 °F)) kple eƒe ʋeʋẽ (si woɖo ɖe 0 °C (32 °F)) nu. Dzɔdzɔmetsi koŋue nye isotope siwo nye hydrogen-1 kple oxygen-16, gake isotope siwo kpekpe wu oxygen-18, oxygen-17, kple hydrogen-2 (deuterium) hã ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ sue aɖe li. Isotope siwo kpekpe wu ƒe alafa memamã le sue ŋutɔ, gake egakpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe tsi ƒe nɔnɔmewo dzi kokoko. Tsi siwo tso tɔsisiwo kple tawo me dina be isotope siwo me kpekpe boo mele o wu atsiaƒu me tsiwo. Eyata woɖe tsi si wozãna ɖaa gɔme le Vienna Standard Mean Ocean Water ƒe nɔnɔmetata me. Le ahanono ta
Tsi le amegbetɔ ƒe ŋutilã me, le mama dedie nu, 50–60%, le ƒexɔxɔ, ŋutsu alo nyɔnu nyenye kple ŋutilã ƒe lolome nu, togbɔ be ame ɖekaɖekawo ateŋu akpɔ 45% va ɖo 75% le afi sia afi hã. U.S. Dukɔa ƒe Dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya, Mɔ̃ɖaŋuŋutinunya, Kple Atikewɔwɔ Suku kafui be woano tsi lita 3.7 (U.S. galɔn 0.98) gbesiagbe na ŋutsu tsitsiwo eye woano tsi L 2.7 (U.S. gal 0.71) na nyɔnuwo. Agbɔsɔsɔ si sɔ pɛpɛpɛ nɔ te ɖe alesi gbegbe wòwɔa dɔe, dzoxɔxɔ, fafa, kple nu bubuwo dzi. Woɖua esia ƒe akpa gãtɔ to nuɖuɖu alo nunono bubuwo me tsɔ wu tsi dzɔdzɔe nono. Atikewɔwɔ ŋuti gbalẽwo doa dzidzɔ na tsinono si bɔbɔ wu, zi geɖe la, tsi lita 1 na ŋutsu mamã dedie, eye wometsɔa nudidi bubu siwo hiã le tsi si bu le kamedede alo yame ƒe dzoxɔxɔ ta o. Ayiku siwo le lãmesẽ me ate ŋu aɖe tsi lita 0.8 va ɖo 1 le gaƒoƒo ɖeka me, gake nuteɖeamedzi abe kamedede ene ate ŋu aɖe agbɔsɔsɔ sia dzi akpɔtɔ. Amewo ate ŋu ano tsi geɖe wu alesi wòhiã ne wole kame dem, si ana woano tsi ƒe ahamumu (tsi ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ fũ), si ate ŋu awu ame. Edze abe nya si ame geɖe gblɔna be "ele be ame nano tsi kplu enyi gbesiagbe" la mekpɔ gɔmeɖoanyi ŋutɔŋutɔ aɖeke le dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya me o ene. Numekukuwo ɖee fia be tsi geɖe nono, vevietɔ vaseɖe mililita 500 (18 imp fl oz; 17 US fl oz) le nuɖuɣi, do ƒome kple lolo dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ. Lãmetsi si sɔ nono kpena ɖe ame ŋu le mɔxexe ɖe dɔgbo nu me. Kafukafu gbãtɔ aɖe si U.S. Dukɔa ƒe Numekuku Aɖaŋudeha ƒe Nuɖuɖu Kple Nunyiame Ŋuti Dɔwɔha ɖo le ƒe 1945 me ku ɖe tsinono ŋu xlẽ be: "Dzidzenu si bɔ na ame vovovowoe nye mililita 1 ɖe nuɖuɖu ƒe calorie ɖesiaɖe ta. Agbɔsɔsɔ sia ƒe akpa gãtɔ le nuɖuɖu siwo woɖa me." Nuɖuɖu ŋuti nyatakaka yeyetɔ si U.S. Dukɔa ƒe Numekuku Aɖaŋudeha wɔ le goawo katã me kafui, si wotu ɖe tsi si woxɔna le mama dedie nu tso United States ƒe numekuku ŋuti nyatakakawo me (si me nuɖuɖutsoƒewo hã le): lita 3.7 (0.81 imp gal; 0.98 US gal) na ŋutsuwo kple lita 2.7 (0.59 imp gal; 0.71 US gal) le tsi bliboa me na nyɔnuwo, eye wode dzesii be tsi si le nuɖuɖu si wona me tsi siwo katã wozã le numekukua me ƒe 19% lɔƒo. Etɔxɛe la, funɔwo kple nyɔnu siwo le no nam viwo hiã tsi bubuwo be tsi nanɔ wo me. United States ƒe Atikewɔwɔ Ŋuti Nusrɔ̃ƒe kafui be, le mamã dedie nu la, ŋutsuwo naɖu lita 3 (0.66 imp gal; 0.79 US gal) eye nyɔnuwo naɖu lita 2.2 (0.48 imp gal; 0.58 US gal); ele be funɔwo nadzi tsi si wono ɖe edzi va ɖo lita 2.4 (0.53 imp gal; 0.63 US gal) eye ele be nyɔnu siwo le no nam wo viwo naxɔ lita 3 (kplu 12), elabena tsi gbogbo aɖe koŋ buna le no nana wo me. Wode dzesii hã be zi geɖe la, tsi si woxɔna ƒe 20% lɔƒo tsoa nuɖuɖu me, gake susɔea tsoa tsinono kple nunono (caffeinated hã le eme) me. Woɖea tsi doa goe le ŋutilãa me le mɔ vovovowo nu; to aɖuɖɔ kple aɖuɖɔ me, to fifia me, kple to tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ si dona le gbɔgbɔ me me. Ne wodze agbagba vevie eye dzoxɔxɔ le ame ŋu la, tsi si bu la adzi ɖe edzi eye tsi si hiã gbesiagbe hã ate ŋu adzi ɖe edzi. Amegbetɔwo hiã tsi si me ɖiƒoƒo ʋɛ aɖewo koe le. Ðiƒoƒo siwo bɔ dometɔ aɖewoe nye ga siwo le abe akɔbli kple lifi ene; atikewo abe nudzodzoeviwutikewo, PFAS, alo bleach ene; kple dɔlékui siwo gblẽa nu le ame ŋu, abe Campylobacter, E. coli O157, kple Vibrio ene. Nusiwo me wodea tsii aɖewo sɔ eye wodina gɔ̃ hã hena vividodoɖeameŋu kple be woana elektrikŋusẽ si hiã. Tsi nyui ɖeka kolia si lolo wu (le eƒe lolome nu) si sɔ na nonoe nye Baikal Ta si le Siberia. Klɔklɔ
Ʋuɖoɖo
Atikewo zazã
Wozãa tsi geɖe le atikewo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ me abe atike alo nusi wotsɔna wɔa dɔe ene eye womezãnɛ boo abe atike alo nusi naa nu ʋãna ene o. Le nusiwo me nugbagbeviwo mele o ƒe nuwɔna me la, tsi nyea atike si bɔ, si lɔ̃a ion-nu geɖe, kpakple polar-nu bubuwo abe amonia kple nusiwo do ƒome kplikplikpli kple tsi. Le lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ me la, zi geɖe la, womezãnɛ abe nuwɔna ƒe ʋeʋẽ ene o, elabena melɔ̃a nusiwo wɔa dɔ nyuie o eye wònye amphoteric (acidic kple basic) kple nucleophilic. Ke hã, nɔnɔme siawo dzroa ame ɣeaɖewoɣi. Azɔ hã, wokpɔ Diels-Alder ƒe dɔwɔwɔ kabakaba to tsi dzi. Tsi si le vevie wu la nye numekuku ƒe nyati nyitsɔ laa. Tsi si me oxygen sɔ gbɔ ɖo si le vevie ŋutɔ la tɔa dzo ɖiƒoƒonu siwo me agbe le nyuie. Dzoxɔxɔ ƒe asitɔtrɔ
Tsi kple dzoxɔxɔ nye tsi si wozãna zi geɖe hena dzoxɔxɔ ɖɔliɖɔli, le eƒe anyinɔnɔ kple dzoxɔxɔ ƒe ŋutete gã ta, hena fafa kple dzoxɔxɔ siaa. Tsi fafɛ ate ŋu anɔ dzɔdzɔme nu tso ta alo atsiaƒu me gɔ̃ hã. Ewɔa dɔ nyuie ŋutɔ be woatsɔ dzoxɔxɔ to tsi ƒe yaƒoƒo kple eƒe ƒoƒo me le eƒe dzoxɔxɔ ɣaɣla gã si nye yaƒoƒo ta. Nusi gblẽ le eŋue nye be ga siwo wokpɔna zi geɖe le dɔwɔƒewo abe gakpo kple akɔbli ene me la, tsi kple dzoxɔxɔ si womewɔ atike na o la xɔa oxidation kabakaba. Le dzoxɔxɔŋusẽdɔwɔƒewo katã kloe la, wozãa tsi abe tsi si wɔa dɔ (si wozãna le mɔ si wotu ɖe dzodoƒe, dzoxɔxɔnamɔ̃, kple tsifamɔ̃ dome), kple fafanu (si wozãna tsɔ ɖɔlia dzoxɔxɔ gbegblẽa yia tsi aɖe me alo tsɔnɛ toa yaƒoƒo me le fafamɔ̃ me). Esia naa nane si nye dedienɔnɔ ƒe afɔɖeɖe si mewɔa naneke o, elabena tsia ɖeɖe le nusi me wowɔa nu le me hã nana nuklia ƒe dɔwɔwɔ dzi ɖena kpɔtɔna. Ke hã wolɔ̃a mɔnu bubuwo hena nuwɔna aɖe nutsitsi eye wònyo wu be woatsɔ tsi atsyɔ nuklia ƒe nuto la dzi ale be woakpɔ egbɔ be fafa si sɔ. Dzo ŋuti nukpɔsusuwo
Dzoxɔxɔ gã aɖe le tsi ŋu eye mewɔa dɔ vie o, si wɔe be wònye dzotsimɔ̃ nyui aɖe. Tsi ƒe yaƒoƒo tsɔa dzoxɔxɔ dzona le dzoa gbɔ. Afɔku le eme be woazã tsi ɖe dzo siwo me ami kple lãmenugbagbeviwo ƒe ʋeʋẽ lilili le ŋu elabena nu gbagbe geɖe nɔa tsi dzi eye tsia dina be yeakaka tsi si le bibim la. Ele be tsi zazã le dzotsitsi me nabu afɔku siwo le dzoxɔxɔ ƒe wowó me hã ŋu, si ate ŋu adzɔ ne wozã tsi ɖe dzo si le dzo dam ŋutɔ dzi le teƒe siwo woxe, kple haidrodzin ƒe wowó, ne nusiwo wɔa dɔ kple tsi, abe ga aɖewo alo carbon dzodzoe abe dzoka xɔxɔ, dzoka xɔxɔ, alo koklozi graphite ene, gblẽ tsia, si na tsi ƒe gas dona. Wokpɔ ŋusẽ si le wowó mawo ŋu le Chernobyl afɔkua me, togbɔ be tsi si le nya sia me la metso dzotsitsi me o ke boŋ etso reactor la ŋutɔ ƒe tsifamɔ̃ me hã. Dzoxɔxɔ aɖe wó esime dzoxɔxɔ si gbɔ eme si le nuto si le eme la na tsi klẽ wòzu dzoxɔxɔ. Ðewohĩ haidrodzin ƒe wowó le dzoxɔxɔ kple zirconium dzodzoe ƒe nuwɔna ta. Ga oxide aɖewo, vevietɔ esiwo nye alkali metals kple alkaline earth metals, naa dzoxɔxɔ geɖe ne wowɔ nu kple tsi ale gbegbe be dzo ƒe afɔku ate ŋu ado mo ɖa. Alkaline earth oxide quicklime, si wogayɔna be calcium oxide, nye nusi wowɔna le agbɔsɔsɔ gã aɖe me si wotsɔna yia pepakotokuwo me zi geɖe. Ne wotsɔ nusiawo ƒo ɖe eme la, woate ŋu atɔ dzo esime wo me nuwo le nu wɔm kple tsi. Modzakaɖeɖe
Amegbetɔwo zãa tsi hena modzakaɖeɖe geɖe, kpakple kamedede kple kamedefefewo. Esiawo dometɔ aɖewoe nye tsiƒuƒu, tsidzizɔzɔ, tɔdziʋukuku, ƒutsotsoewo dzi ƒuƒu kple tsi me nɔnɔ. Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, wowɔa kamedefefe aɖewo, abe ice hockey kple ice skating ene, le tsikpe dzi. Tawo, ƒutawo kple tsitsaƒewo nye teƒe siwo amewo lɔ̃a yiyi be yewoaɖi ɖe eme ahase vivi na modzakaɖeɖe. Ame geɖe kpɔe be tsi si le sisim ƒe ɖiɖi kple dzedzeme nana ame ƒe dzi dzea eme, eye tsidzɔƒewo kple tsi si le sisim ƒe xɔ bubuwo nye atsyɔ̃ɖonu siwo ame geɖe lɔ̃na. Ame aɖewo dzraa tɔmelãwo kple numiemie kple lã bubuwo ɖo ɖe lãwo ƒe lãkpɔwo alo tsitawo me hena wo ɖokui ɖeɖefia, modzakaɖeɖe, kple hadede. Amegbetɔwo zãa tsi hã hena snodzifefewo, abe snodzizɔzɔ, sledding, snodzizɔzɔ alo snodzizɔzɔ ene, si bia be tsia nanɔ dzoxɔxɔ si bɔbɔ me abe tsikpe ene alo wòazu kristalo wòazu sno. Tsidɔwɔƒewo
Tsidɔwɔƒewo naa tsinono kple tsi gbegblẽwo (siwo dome tsi gbegblẽwo ɖeɖeɖa hã le) aƒewo kple dɔwɔƒewo. Tsidzɔƒewo dometɔ aɖewoe nye tsivudowo, tsidzadza ƒe tsidzɔƒewo, tsidzɔƒewo, kple tsikɔƒewo, tsidzɔƒewo, tsixɔwo, tsipɔmpi siwo dome tsimɔ xoxowo hã le. Wole tsidzɔƒe siwo le yame la wɔm. Zi geɖe la, woƒoa tsinono nu ƒu le tsidzɔƒewo, woɖenɛ tso do (vudo) siwo wowɔ kple asi siwo le anyigba me, alo woɖenɛ tsoa ta kple tɔsisiwo me. Eyata vudo geɖe tutu le teƒe siwo sɔ nye mɔ si dzi woate ŋu ato akpɔ tsi geɖe, ne wotsɔe be tsidzɔƒewo ate ŋu ana tsi si sɔ. Tsidzɔƒe bubuwoe nye tsidzadza ƒe tsiwo nuƒoƒoƒu. Ðewohĩ ahiã be woakɔ tsi ŋu hafi amegbetɔwo naɖu. Esia ate ŋu abia be woaɖe nusiwo me mekɔ o, nusiwo me mekɔ o kple nugbagbevi suesue siwo gblẽa nu le ame ŋu la ɖa. Mɔnu siwo ame geɖe zãnae nye be woatsɔ ke si ɖea nusiwo me mekɔ o ɖa ko, evɔ chlorination kple eƒoƒo wua nugbagbevi suesue siwo gblẽa nu le ame ŋu. Distillation wɔa dɔ etɔ̃awo katã. Mɔnu siwo de ŋgɔ wu li, abe reverse osmosis ene. Atsiaƒu me tsi gbogbowo ƒe dze ɖeɖeɖa nye egbɔkpɔnu si xɔ asi wu si wozãna le ƒuta ƒe yame ƒe nɔnɔme si me tsi medzana le o. Wowɔa tsinono mama to dudzikpɔƒe ƒe tsiɖoɖowo, tsigoewo tsɔtsɔ yi na amewo alo abe tsigoe me tsi ene. Ðoɖowo le dziɖuɖuwo si le dukɔ geɖe me be woama tsi na hiãtɔwo femaxee. Zãza dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ to tsinono (nono) zazã na amegbetɔwo ƒe nuɖuɖu ɖeɖeko me nye mɔnu bubu. Le du aɖewo abe Hong Kong ene me la, wozãa atsiaƒumetsi geɖe tsɔ klɔa nugododeƒewo le dua me godoo be woatsɔ akpɔ tsi nyuiwo ta. Ðiƒoƒo tsi ate ŋu anye tsi zazã le mɔ gbegblẽ nu zi ɖeka kolia; vaseɖe afisi ɖiƒoƒonu aɖe ɖo seɖoƒe na tsia zazã bubuwo la, eva zua nunɔamesi la gbegblẽ, eɖanye viɖe ka kee wòaɖe na ɖiƒoƒoa o. Abe ɖiƒoƒo ƒomevi bubuwo ene la, esia megena ɖe asitsatsa ƒe gazazãwo ƒe akɔntabubu si sɔ me o, wosusunɛ be enye gotagomenuwo siwo ŋu asitsalawo mate ŋu abu akɔnta le o. Eyata ame bubuwo xea tsi ƒe ɖiƒoƒo ƒe fe, evɔ womegamaa viɖe siwo dɔwɔƒe siwo menye dziɖuɖu tɔ o kpɔna la na nutoa me tɔwo, amesiwo ŋu ɖiƒoƒo sia gblẽ nu le o. Zi geɖe la, atike siwo amegbetɔwo ɖuna la va wu enu ɖe ​​tsimɔwo me eye woate ŋu agblẽ nu le tsi me nugbagbewo ŋu ne woƒo ƒu ɖe nugbagbewo me eye ne womate ŋu agblẽ nu le nugbagbewo ŋu o. Zi geɖe la, wowɔa tsi gbegblẽwo le dugãwo kple dɔwɔƒewo ƒe tsi gbegblẽwo ŋu le tsi gbegblẽwo ɖeɖeɖa dɔwɔƒewo. Wokpɔa tsi si sina toa anyigba dzi si ƒo ɖi la dzi ɖeɖe kpɔtɔ gbɔ to mɔxexeɖedɔléle nu kple edadamɔnu vovovowo dzi. Dɔwɔƒewo ƒe dɔwɔwɔ
Dɔwɔƒe geɖe ƒe dɔwɔwɔ nɔa te ɖe nuwɔna siwo zãa atike siwo wotsɔ ƒo tsi me, nu sesẽwo tsɔtsɔ de tsi ƒe ʋeʋẽ me alo tsi zazã atsɔ alɔ nu ahaɖe wo ɖa, alo atsɔ aklɔ nusiwo wowɔ alo dɔwɔnu siwo wotsɔ wɔa dɔe dzi. Dɔwɔwɔwo abe tomenukuƒe, atikewo tsɔtsɔ ƒu gbe, aŋɔwo ɖeɖeɖa, pepa wɔwɔ, avɔwɔwɔ, amadede, agbalẽtata, kple elektrikdɔwɔƒewo ƒe fafa zãa tsi gbogbo aɖe, si bia be woakpɔ tsidzɔƒe tɔxɛ aɖe, eye zi geɖe la, ehea ɖiƒoƒo tsi geɖe vɛ. Wozãa tsi le elektrikŋusẽ wɔwɔ me. Tsiŋusẽ nye elektrikŋusẽ si wokpɔna tso tsiŋusẽ me. Tsiŋusẽ tsoa tsi si ʋua tsimɔ̃ si wotsɔ do ka kple elektrikŋusẽnamɔ̃ me. Tsiŋusẽ nye ŋusẽ yeye si mexɔ asi o, si meƒoa ɖi o, si woate ŋu azã yeyee. Tsi ƒe ʋuʋu ye naa ŋusẽa. Zi geɖe la, wotua tɔdzisasrã ɖe tɔsisi aɖe dzi, si wɔnɛ be ta aɖe si wowɔ kple asi le megbe nɛ. Wozia tsi si sina dona le ta la me dzi to turbine siwo trɔa elektrikŋusẽnamɔ̃wo me. Wozãa tsi si wotsɔ nyaƒoɖeamenu wɔe le tsi ƒe dzodzo kple tsi ƒe ʋuʋu ƒe mɔ̃wo me. Wozãa tu siwo me tsi ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu sesẽ le tsɔ lãa nu pɛpɛpɛ. Ewɔa dɔ nyuie ŋutɔ, mele dedie vie, eye megblẽa nu le nutome ŋu o. Nuɖaɖa, dzotsitsi, kple nuɖaɖa nye nuɖaɖamɔnu xɔŋkɔ siwo bia zi geɖe be woanyrɔ nuɖuɖu ɖe tsi alo eƒe ya si nye dzoxɔxɔ me. Wozãa tsi hã tsɔ klɔa nuɖazewo. Nusiwo me tsi le, kpakple ya ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu kpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe tsi ƒe ƒoƒo kple tsikpe ƒe teƒewo dzi, eye eƒe kɔkɔme hã kpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe edzi. Tsi ƒoa dzo le dzoxɔxɔ si bɔbɔ wu me kple ya ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu si bɔbɔ ɖe anyi si dzɔna le teƒe siwo kɔkɔ wu. Le xexeame katã
Wowɔa xexeame ƒe tsi akpa gãtɔ abe ɣletiviwo ƒe wɔwɔme ƒe akpa aɖe ene. Ya sesẽ aɖe si ƒona le gota si nye gas kple ke kpena ɖe ɣletiviwo ƒe wɔwɔme ŋu. Le July 22, 2011 dzi la, nyatakaka aɖe ƒo nu tso tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ ƒe alilikpo gã aɖe si me "tsi si sɔ gbɔ wu Anyigba ƒe atsiaƒuwo katã ƒo ƒu zi gbɔ zi triliɔn 140" si ƒo xlã quasar si le kekeli ƒe ƒe biliɔn 12 tso Anyigba gbɔ ŋu. Tsi kple ɣletinyigbawo dzi nɔnɔ
Tsi si nye tsi ƒe anyinɔnɔ, kple le mɔ sue aɖe nu la, eƒe ya kple nu sesẽ ƒe nɔnɔmewo, le Anyigba dzi le vevie ŋutɔ na agbe ƒe anyinɔnɔ le Anyigba dzi abe alesi míenyae ene. Anyigba le Ɣe ƒe Ƒuƒoƒoa ƒe nuto si me woate ŋu anɔ la me; nenye be ete ɖe Ɣea ŋu vie alo didi tso egbɔ (anɔ abe 5%, alo abe kilometa miliɔn 8 ene) la, nɔnɔme siwo ɖea mɔ be nɔnɔme etɔ̃awo nanɔ anyi le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me la manɔ anyi kura o. Anyigba ƒe lolome hã wɔa akpa aɖe: eƒe nuheŋusẽ na wòte ŋu léa yamenutome aɖe, si me ya ƒe tsidzadza hã le. Ɣletinyigba suewo abe Mars ene ƒe yamenutome le tsɛ ŋutɔ alo womele nenema kura o. Tsi ƒe dzoxɔxɔ kple ya bubu siwo gblẽa nu le yame ŋu le yamenutome naa dzoxɔxɔ ƒe mɔxenu (yame ƒe dzoxɔxɔ ƒe ŋusẽkpɔɖeamedzi) si kpena ɖe eŋu be yame ƒe dzoxɔxɔ si nɔa anyi ɖaa vie ne ele agbɔsɔsɔ si sɔ me. Tsi ƒe nɔnɔme le ɣletinyigba aɖe dzi nɔ te ɖe yame ƒe nyaƒoɖeamenu dzi, si ɣletinyigba la ƒe nuheŋusẽ dea dzesii. Ne ɣletinyigba aɖe lolo ale gbegbe la, tsi si le edzi ate ŋu asẽ le dzoxɔxɔ gã gɔ̃ hã me, le nyaƒoɖeamenu gã si nuheŋusẽ hena vɛ ta, abe alesi wokpɔe le ɣletinyigba siwo le gota Gliese 436 b kple GJ 1214 b dzi ene. Se, dunyahehe, kple xaxa
Tsi ŋuti dunyahehe nye dunyahehe si dzi tsi kple tsidzɔƒewo kpɔa ŋusẽ ɖo. Tsi, vevietɔ tsi nyui, nye mɔnu si wozãna le xexeame godoo eye wònye nu vevi aɖe le dunyahehe me dzrehehe geɖe me. Egblẽa nu le lãmesẽ ŋu eye wògblẽa nu le nugbagbe ƒomevi vovovowo ŋu. Tsi nyui nono ƒe amesinɔnɔ nyo ɖe edzi le ƒe bla nane siwo va yi me le xexeame ƒe akpa sia akpa kloe, gake ame siwo ade biliɔn ɖeka gakpɔtɔ mekpɔa tsi nyui o eye ame biliɔn 2.5 kple edzivɔwo mekpɔa dzadzɛnyenye nyui o. Gake eteƒekpɔla aɖewo bu akɔnta be kaka ƒe 2025 naɖo la, xexeamenɔlawo ƒe afã kple edzivɔ adze ŋgɔ tsi ƒe afɔku. Nyatakaka aɖe, si woɖe ɖe go le November 2009 me, ɖee fia be kaka ƒe 2030 naɖo la, le xexeame ƒe nuto madeŋgɔ aɖewo me la, tsi ƒe hiahiã agbɔ tsi si woakpɔ ŋu 50%. Ame biliɔn 1.6 kpɔ tsi nyui tso ƒe 1990. Wobu akɔnta be ame siwo le dukɔ madeŋgɔwo me si si tsi nyui le ƒe agbɔsɔsɔme nyo ɖe edzi tso 30% le ƒe 1970 me va ɖo 71% le ƒe 1990 me, 79% le ƒe 2000 me, kple 84% le ƒe 2004. Dukɔ Ƒoƒuawo ƒe nyatakaka aɖe gblɔ le ƒe 2006 me be "tsi sɔ gbɔ na amesiame", gake be dɔdzikpɔkpɔ gbegblẽ kple nufitifitiwɔwɔ xea mɔ na eƒe yiyi. Gakpe ɖe eŋu la, xexeame katã ƒe ɖoɖo siwo wowɔ be woana kpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe dɔwɔwɔ nanyo ɖe edzi, abe Paris ƒe Nyametsotso si ku ɖe Kpekpeɖeŋunana ƒe Dɔwɔwɔ Ŋu ene, mewɔ dɔ nyuie abe alesi tsidzɔlawo wɔe le sukudede kple lãmesẽnyawo me ene o, si ate ŋu ana kpekpeɖeŋunala geɖe siwo le dɔ wɔm le dɔ siwo tsia tre ɖe wo nɔewo ŋu me kple dziɖuɖu siwo xɔa kpekpeɖeŋu manɔmee ŋusẽ manɔmee be woawɔ nu. Ƒe 2007 ƒe Tsizazã Ŋuti Numekuku Blibo si wowɔ le Agbledede me ŋlɔlawo yɔ dziɖuɖu gbegblẽ be enye nusiwo gbɔ tsi ƒe veve ƒomevi aɖewo tso la dometɔ ɖeka. Tsidzikpɔkpɔ nye ɖoɖo siwo wowɔ le se nu kple esiwo womewɔ le ɖoɖo nu o siwo dzi woato awɔ nyametsotso siwo ku ɖe tsizazã ŋu. Tsidzikpɔkpɔ nyuie ku ɖe ɖoɖo siwo wɔa dɔ nyuie wu le ŋutilã kple hadome ganyawo ƒe nɔnɔme aɖe koŋ me nyanya ŋu koŋ. Wowɔ vodadawo ɣeaɖewoɣi to agbagbadzedze be woawɔ ‘ɖoɖowɔɖi’ siwo wɔa dɔ le dukɔ deŋgɔwo me ŋudɔ le xexeame ƒe teƒe siwo le ŋgɔ yim kple nɔnɔme siwo me wole me. Mekong-tɔsisia nye esia ƒe kpɔɖeŋu ɖeka; dukɔ ade siwo ɖoa ŋu ɖe Mekong-tɔsisia ŋu hena tsi ƒe ɖoɖowo me toto ɖee fia be ƒã hafi wowɔa gazazã kple viɖe ŋuti numekuku tsitotsito si me kɔ kple nusiwo gblẽa nu le nutome ŋu ƒe dodokpɔwo. Ƒe 2023 ƒe nyatakakaa gblɔ be ame biliɔn eve (amewo ƒe 26%) mekpɔa tsinono o eye ame biliɔn 3.6 (46%) mekpɔa dzadzɛnyenye si dzi wokpɔna dedie o. Ke hã le ƒe 40 siwo va yi me la, tsizazã dzi ɖe edzi abe 1% ene ƒe sia ƒe, eye wole mɔ kpɔm be adzi ɖe edzi nenema ke vaseɖe ƒe 2050. Subɔsubɔhawo
Wobua tsi be enye nusi kɔa ame ŋu le subɔsubɔha akpa gãtɔ me. Xɔse siwo me kɔnuwɔwɔ ƒe tsilele (tsilele) le dometɔ aɖewoe nye Kristotɔnyenye, Hindu-subɔsubɔ, Islam-subɔsubɔ, Yuda-subɔsubɔ, Rastafari-ha, Shinto-subɔsubɔ, Tao-subɔsubɔ, kple Wicca-subɔsubɔ. Ame nyrɔ ɖe tsi me (alo eƒe tsɔtsɔ ƒu gbe alo eƒe tsɔtsɔ ƒu gbe) nye Kristotɔnyenye ƒe Sakramento vevi aɖe (afisi woyɔnɛ be nyɔnyrɔxɔxɔ le); enye subɔsubɔha bubuwo hã ƒe nuwɔna ƒe akpa aɖe, siwo dometɔ aɖewoe nye Islam (Ghusl), Yuda-subɔsubɔ (mikvah) kple Sikh-subɔsubɔ (Amrit Sanskar). Tsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu la, wowɔa kɔnuwɔwɔ ƒe tsilele le tsi dzadzɛ me na ame kukuwo le subɔsubɔha geɖe siwo dome Islam kple Yuda-subɔsubɔ hã le me. Le Islam me la, woateŋu awɔ gbesiagbe gbedodoɖa atɔ̃awo le go geɖe me ne woklɔ ŋutilã ƒe akpa aɖewo to tsi dzadzɛ (wudu) zazã me vɔ megbe, negbe ɖe tsi meli o (kpɔ Tayammum). Le Shinto me la, wozãa tsi le kɔnuwɔwɔwo katã kloe me tsɔ kɔa ame alo nuto aɖe ŋu (e.g., le misogi ƒe kɔnu me). Le Kristotɔnyenye me la, tsi kɔkɔe nye tsi si nunɔla aɖe kɔe kple susu be yeaxɔ nyɔnyrɔ, yeyra amewo, teƒewo, kple nuwo, alo be wòanye mɔ si dzi woato anya vɔ̃ɖinyenye ɖa.
