Article I  
Vanhu vose vanozvarwa vakasununguka uye vakaenzana pachiremera nekodzero. Ndivo 
vane njere nehana uye vanofanira kuita mumwe kune mumwe mu 
mweya wehama.  
Chisungo chechipiri  
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekodzero nerusununguko rwese zvakataurwa muChiziviso chino, 
pasina mutsauko werudzi rwupi zvarwo, serudzi, ruvara, murume kana mukadzi, mutauro, chinamato, 
zvematongerwo enyika kana mamwe maonero, nyika kana magariro, pfuma, kuzvarwa kana humwe hunhu.  
Pamusoro pezvo, hapana mutsauko uchaitwa maererano nezvematongerwo enyika, 
hutongi kana mamiriro epasi rose enyika kana dunhu kwaanogara munhu 
ndeyayo, ingave yakazvimiririra, yakavimbwa, isingazvitongi kana iri pasi pechimwewo zvacho 
kuganhurirwa kwouchangamire.  
Chisungo chetatu  
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yehupenyu, yerusununguko nekuchengetedzeka.  
Chisungo chechina  
Hapana munhu achabatwa muuranda kana kushandiswa senhapwa; uranda uye kutengeswa kwenhapwa kuchave
zvinorambidzwa mumhando dzavo dzose.  
Chisungo chechishanu  
Hapana munhu achatambudzwa kana kuitirwa hutsinye, hutsinye kana kubatwa zvinoshoreka 
kana kurangwa.  
 

 
Chisungo chechitanhatu  
Munhu wese anekodzero yekucherechedzwa kwese kwese semunhu pamberi pemutemo.  
Chisungo chechinomwe  
Vese vakaenzana pamberi pemutemo uye vane kodzero yekuenzana pasina rusarura 
kuchengetedzwa kwemutemo. Vese vane kodzero yekudzivirirwa zvakaenzana kune chero 
rusarura rwuri kutyora Chirevo ichi uye nekukurudzira kupi zvako kune zvakadaro 
rusarura.  
Chisungo chechisere  
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokuwana rubatsiro runoshanda kubva kumatare edzimhosva ane kodzero 
nokuda kwezviito zvinotyora kodzero dzakakosha dzaanopiwa nebumbiro remutemo kana nomutemo.  
Chisungo chechipfumbamwe  
Hapana anofanirwa kusungwa, kuvharirwa kana kudzingwa munyika zvisiri pamutemo.  
Chisungo chegumi  
Munhu wese anekodzero yekuenzana kuzere nekunzwikwa kweruzhinji nemunhu akazvimiririra 
uye dare redzimhosva risina rusaruro, mukugadzirisa kwekodzero dzake nezvisungo uye chero ipi zvayo 
mhosva yaari kupomerwa.  
Chisungo 11  
1. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anenge apomerwa mhosva ine chirango anekodzero yekutongwa 
asina mhosva kusvikira awanikwa aine mhosva maererano nemutemo mudare redzimhosva raaizotongwa 
ave nevimbiso dzose dzinodiwa pakudzivirira kwake.  
2. Hapana munhu achanzi ane mhosva nokuda kwechiito chipi zvacho kana 
kusiya iyo isingaite mhosva yechirango, pasi penyika kana 
mutemo wenyika dzakawanda, panguva yawakaitwa. Kana rinorema
chirango chipihwe kupfuura chaishandiswa panguva yechirango 
mhosva yakaitwa.  
Chisungo 12  
Hapana munhu achavhiringwa zvisina tsarukano pakuvanzika kwake, mhuri, imba yake 
kana tsamba, kana kurwisa rukudzo nemukurumbira wake. Munhu wose ane 
kodzero yekuchengetedzwa kwemutemo pakukanganiswa kana kurwiswa kwakadaro.  
Chisungo 13  
1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva norusununguko rwokufamba nekugara mukati meguta 
miganhu yenyika imwe neimwe.  
2. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokubuda munyika ipi zvayo, kusanganisira yekwake, uye 
kudzokera kunyika yake.  
Chisungo 14  
1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekutsvaga nekunakidzwa nekupotera kune dzimwe nyika 
kutambudzwa.  
2. Kodzero iyi haigone kushandiswa kana munhu achipomerwa zvemazvirokwazvo 
kubva pamhosva dzisiri dzezvematongerwo enyika kana kuita zvinopesana nezvinangwa uye 
nheyo dzeUnited Nations.  
Chisungo 15  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yerudzi rwenyika.  
2. Hapana munhu achatorerwa chizvarwa chake kana kunyimwa kodzero 
kuchinja rudzi rwake.  
Chisungo 16  
 

 
1. Varume nevakadzi vezera rakazara, pasina muganhu nekuda kwerudzi, nyika 
kana kuti chitendero, vane kodzero yokuroora nokuva nemhuri. Vane kodzero 
kukodzero dzakaenzana pawanano, panguva yewanano nepakuparara kwayo.  
2. Muchato uchapindwa chete nemvumo yemahara uye yakazara 
vakaroorana.  
3. Mhuri inhengo yechisikigo uye inokosha yeboka renzanga uye ndiyo
vane kodzero yekuchengetedzwa nenharaunda neHurumende.  
Chisungo 17  
1. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva nemidziyo ari ega kana akabatana nayo 
vamwe.  
2. Hapana munhu achatorerwa pfuma yake.  
Chisungo 18  
Munhu wese anekodzero yerusununguko rwekufunga, rwehana uye rwechitendero; kodzero iyi 
kunosanganisira rusununguko rwekuchinja chitendero chake kana kutenda, uye rusununguko, ari ega kana mukati 
munharaunda nevamwe uye pachena kana pachivande, kuti aratidze chitendero chake kana zvaanotenda mazviri 
kudzidzisa, kuita, kunamata nekucherechedza.  
Chisungo 19  
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva nerusununguko rwokufunga zvaanoda uye zvaanotaura; kodzero iyi inosanganisira 
rusununguko rwekubata pfungwa pasina kukanganisa uye kutsvaga, kugamuchira nekupa 
ruzivo uye mazano kuburikidza neipi midhiya uye zvisinei nemiganhu.  
Chisungo 20  
1. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuva nerusununguko rwekuungana pamwe nekushamwaridzana murunyararo.  
2. Hapana anogona kumanikidzwa kuva nhengo yesangano.  
Chisungo 21  
 

 
1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekutora chikamu muhurumende yenyika yake. 
zvakananga kana kuburikidza nevamiriri vakasarudzwa vakasununguka.  
2. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yakaenzana yekuwana rubatsiro rwehurumende munyika yake.  
3. Kuda kwevanhu kuchava hwaro hwesimba rehurumende; 
chido ichi chicharatidzwa nguva nenguva uye nesarudzo dzechokwadi dzinoita 
ichave nemubvumo wepasi rose uye wakaenzana uye ichaitwa nevhoti yakavanzika kana na 
nzira dzakaenzana dzekuvhota dzakasununguka.  
Chisungo 22
Munhu wese, senhengo yenharaunda, anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa kwevanhu uye anekodzero 
kuzadzisa, kuburikidza nekuedza kwenyika uye kushandira pamwe kwenyika uye mu 
zvinoenderana nesangano uye zviwanikwa zveNyika imwe neimwe, yehupfumi, 
kodzero dzemagariro netsika dzinokosha pakuremekedzwa kwake uye budiriro yakasununguka 
zvehunhu hwake.  
Chisungo 23  
1. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokushanda, kusarudza basa rakasununguka, kuita zvakarurama uye 
mamiriro akanaka ebasa uye kudzivirirwa pakurovha.  
2. Munhu wese, pasina kusarura kupi zvako, anekodzero yokuwana muhoro wakaenzana 
shanda.  
3. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anoshanda anekodzero yokuwana mubairo wakakodzera 
achizviitira iye nemhuri yake upenyu hunokodzera chiremera chemunhu, 
uye inowedzerwa, kana zvichidikanwa, nedzimwe nzira dzekuchengetedzwa kwevanhu.  
4. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuumba nekupinda mumasangano evashandi kuti adzivirirwe 
zvaanoda.  
Chisungo 24  
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuzorora nekutandara, kusanganisira kuganhurirwa 
maawa ekushanda uye nguva nenguva mazororo ane mubhadharo.  
 

 
Chisungo 25  
1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekurarama zvinoenderana neutano uye 
kugara zvakanaka kwake uye kwemhuri yake, kusanganisira zvokudya, zvokupfeka, pokugara 
uye kurapwa uye mabasa anodiwa munharaunda, uye kodzero yekuchengetedzwa 
pakaitika kushaya basa, kurwara, kuremara, kufirwa, kuchembera kana 
kumwe kushayiwa chokurarama nacho mumamiriro ezvinhu aasingagoni kudzora.
2. Humai nehudiki zvinekodzero yekuchengetwa nerubatsiro rwakakosha. Zvose 
vana, vangave vakaberekerwa muwanano kana kuti kunze kwewanano, vachanakidzwa nekushamwaridzana kwakafanana 
kudzivirira.  
Chisungo 26  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yedzidzo. Dzidzo ichave yemahara, kunyanya mu 
nhanho dzekutanga uye dzakakosha. Dzidzo yepuraimari ichave 
zvinomanikidzirwa. Dzidzo yehunyanzvi neyehunyanzvi ichaitwa 
inowanika uye dzidzo yepamusorosoro ichawaniswa vanhu vose zvakaenzana 
pahwaro hwekukodzera.  
2. Dzidzo ichanangana kukukura kuzere kwemunhu 
hunhu nekusimbaradza kuremekedzwa kwekodzero dzevanhu uye 
rusununguko runokosha. Ichakurudzira kunzwisisa, kushivirira uye 
ushamwari pakati pemarudzi ose, marudzi kana mapoka ezvitendero, uye achaenderera mberi 
mabasa eUnited Nations ekuchengetedza runyararo.  
3. Vabereki vane kodzero yekutanga yekusarudza rudzi rwedzidzo ichave 
kupiwa vana vavo.  
Chisungo 27  
1. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekupinda akasununguka mutsika nemagariro evanhu 
munharaunda, kunakidzwa nehunyanzvi uye kugoverana mukufambira mberi kwesainzi uye 
zvayakanakira.  
 

 
2. (Chekutanga) - (Chekutanga) 
zvichibva kune chero sainzi, zvinyorwa kana hunyanzvi kugadzirwa kwaari iye 
munyori.  
Chisungo 28  
Munhu wese anekodzero yekugarisana nepasi rose mune kodzero uye 
rusununguko rwakanyorwa muChiziviso chino runogona kuzadzikiswa zvizere.  
Chisungo 29
1. Munhu wese ane mabasa munharaunda umo chete vakasununguka uye vakazara 
Kukura kwounhu hwake kunobvira.  
2. Mukushandisa kodzero nerusununguko rwake, munhu wese achatongwa chete 
kune zvipimo zvakatemwa nemutemo chete nechinangwa che 
kuwana kuremekedzwa kwakakodzera uye kuremekedzwa kwekodzero nerusununguko rwevamwe 
uye yekuzadzisa zvinodikanwa zvehunhu, kurongeka kwevanhu uye 
magariro akanaka munharaunda yedemocracy.  
3. Kodzero nerusununguko urwu hazvigone kushandiswa zvinopesana ne 
zvinangwa nemisimboti yeUnited Nations.

Hapana chiri muChiziviso chino chingadudzirwa sechinoreva chero nyika, boka kana

kuparadzwa kwekodzero dzipi nedzipi nerusununguko zvakataurwa pano.
