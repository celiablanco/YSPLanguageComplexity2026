momwe anthu adzakhala ndi ufulu wolankhula ndi kukhulupirira ndi ufulu 
kuchokera ku mantha ndi kusowa kwalengezedwa kuti ndicho chikhumbo chachikulu cha anthu wamba 
anthu,  
Pamene kuli kofunikira, ngati munthu sangakakamizidwe kupeza njira, monga wotsiriza

mayiko,

Imalengeza Chikalata Chapadziko Lonse cha Ufulu Wachibadwidwe ichi ngati mulingo wofanana wa 
kupindula kwa anthu onse ndi mayiko onse, kuti munthu aliyense payekha ndi

kuphunzitsa ndi maphunziro kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu ndi kumasuka ndi mwa 
njira zopita patsogolo, zapadziko lonse lapansi komanso zapadziko lonse lapansi, kuti ateteze chilengedwe chawo chonse 
kuzindikira kogwira mtima ndi kutsata, pakati pa anthu a Mayiko Amembala 
iwo okha ndi pakati pa anthu a m’madera amene anali kuwayang’anira.  
Article I  
Anthu onse amabadwa aufulu ndi ofanana mu ulemu ndi ufulu. Iwo ali 
opatsidwa nzeru ndi chikumbumtima ndipo ayenera kuchitirana wina ndi mnzake mu a 
mzimu wa ubale.  
Ndime 2  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu ku ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zafotokozedwa m'chikalatachi, 
popanda kusiyana kulikonse, monga mtundu, khungu, kugonana, chinenero, chipembedzo, 
ndale kapena maganizo ena, dziko kapena chikhalidwe, katundu, kubadwa kapena udindo wina.  
Kuphatikiza apo, palibe kusiyanitsa komwe kudzachitike potengera ndale, 
aulamuliro kapena mayiko adziko kapena gawo lomwe munthu ali 
ndi zake, kaya zikhale zodziyimira pawokha, zodalirika, zosadzilamulira kapena zili pansi pa china chilichonse 
kuchepetsa ulamuliro.  
Ndime 3  
Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo, ufulu ndi chitetezo.  
Ndime 4  
Palibe amene adzakhale kapolo kapena ukapolo; ukapolo ndi malonda a akapolo adzakhala
zoletsedwa m'njira zonse.  
Ndime 5  
Palibe amene adzazunzidwe kapena kuchitidwa nkhanza, mopanda umunthu kapena monyozetsa 
kapena chilango.  
 

 
Ndime 6  
Aliyense ali ndi danga lozindikirika paliponse ngati munthu pamaso pa lamulo.  
Ndime 7  
Onse ndi ofanana pamaso pa lamulo ndipo ali ndi ufulu wofanana popanda tsankho 
chitetezo cha lamulo. Onse ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana kwa aliyense 
tsankho lophwanya Chidziwitsochi komanso zolimbikitsa zilizonse 
tsankho.  
Ndime 8  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lolandira thandizo kuchokera ku bwalo lamilandu ladziko 
chifukwa chophwanya ufulu wachibadwidwe woperekedwa ndi malamulo oyendetsera dziko lino.  
Ndime 9  
Palibe amene adzamangidwe, kutsekeredwa m'ndende kapena kuthamangitsidwa popanda chifukwa.  
Ndime 10  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wolandila ufulu wodziyimira pawokha 
ndi bwalo lamilandu lopanda tsankho, pakutsimikiza za ufulu ndi udindo wake ndi chilichonse 
mlandu womutsutsa.  
Ndime 11  
1. Aliyense wopalamula ali ndi ufulu woganiziridwa 
osalakwa mpaka atapezeka kuti ndi wolakwa malinga ndi lamulo pamlandu wapagulu pomwe iye 
wakhala ndi zitsimikizo zonse zofunika pachitetezo chake.  
2. Palibe amene adzaimbidwe mlandu wolakwa chifukwa cha mchitidwe uliwonse kapena 
kulephera komwe sikunali kulakwa, pansi pa dziko kapena 
malamulo apadziko lonse lapansi, pa nthawi yomwe idaperekedwa. Kapena cholemera kwambiri
chilango chidzaperekedwa kuposa chomwe chinkaperekedwa pa nthawi ya chilangocho 
cholakwa chinachitidwa.  
Ndime 12  
Palibe amene adzasokonezedwe ndi zinsinsi zake, banja lake, nyumba yake 
kapena kulemberana makalata, kapena kutsutsa ulemu ndi mbiri yake. Aliyense watero 
ufulu wotetezedwa ndi lamulo kuti asasokonezedwe kapena kuwukira.  
Ndime 13  
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga loyenda ndikukhala m'dziko 
malire a Boma lililonse.  
2. Aliyense ali ndi danga lochoka m'dziko lina lililonse, kuphatikizira dziko lake, ndi kupita 
kubwerera ku dziko lake.  
Ndime 14  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lothawirako ndi kusangalala kumaiko ena 
kuzunzidwa.  
2. Ufuluwu sungagwiritsidwe ntchito poyimbidwa mlandu moona mtima 
zobwera chifukwa cha milandu yosagwirizana ndi ndale kapena zochita zosemphana ndi zolinga ndi 
mfundo za United Nations.  
Ndime 15  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi dziko.  
2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe dziko lake kapena kuletsedwa mwachisawawa 
kusintha mtundu wake.  
Ndime 16  
 

 
1. Amuna ndi akazi a msinkhu, popanda malire chifukwa cha mtundu, dziko 
kapena chipembedzo, ali ndi ufulu wokwatira ndi kukhala ndi banja. Iwo ali ndi ufulu 
kukhala ndi ufulu wofanana m’banja, m’nthawi yaukwati ndi pamene banja lithe.  
2. Ukwati udzalowetsedwa mwaufulu ndi chilolezo chathunthu cha 
ofuna okwatirana.  
3. Banja ndi gulu lachilengedwe komanso lofunika kwambiri la anthu ndipo ndilo
ali oyenera kutetezedwa ndi anthu komanso boma.  
Ndime 17  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi chuma pa yekha kapena mogwilizana nazo 
ena.  
2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe chuma chake mwachisawawa.  
Ndime 18  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wa kuganiza, chikumbumtima ndi chipembedzo; ufulu uwu 
kumaphatikizapo ufulu wosintha chipembedzo kapena chikhulupiriro chake, ndi ufulu, kaya payekha kapena mkati 
kusonkhana ndi ena komanso poyera kapena mwamseri, kusonyeza chipembedzo kapena chikhulupiriro chake 
kuphunzitsa, kuchita, kulambira ndi kusunga.  
Ndime 19  
Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi maganizo ake; ufulu uwu umaphatikizapo 
ufulu wokhala ndi malingaliro popanda kusokonezedwa ndi kufunafuna, kulandira ndi kupereka 
zambiri ndi malingaliro kudzera muzofalitsa zilizonse komanso mosasamala kanthu za malire.  
Ndime 20  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga losonkhana pamodzi mwamtendere.  
2. Palibe amene angakakamizidwe kukhala membala wa bungwe.  
Ndime 21  
 

 
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lotenga mbali mu boma la dziko lake; 
mwachindunji kapena kudzera mwa nthumwi zosankhidwa mwaufulu.  
2. Aliyense ali ndi danga lofanana ndi nchito za boma m'dziko lake.  
3. Zofuna za anthu zidzakhala maziko a ulamuliro wa boma; 
chifunirochi chidzawonetsedwa mu nthawi ndi nthawi ndi chisankho chenicheni chomwe chidzachitike 
kukhale ndi ufulu wapadziko lonse ndi wofanana ndipo udzachitidwa mwachinsinsi kapena mwachinsinsi 
njira zofanana zovota mwaulere.  
Ndime 22
Aliyense, monga membala wa gulu, ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa ndipo ali ndi ufulu 
kukwaniritsa, kupyolera mu khama la dziko ndi mgwirizano wa mayiko ndi mu 
molingana ndi bungwe ndi chuma cha Boma lililonse, zachuma, 
ufulu wa chikhalidwe ndi chikhalidwe ndizofunikira kwambiri pa ulemu wake ndi chitukuko chaulere 
za umunthu wake.  
Ndime 23  
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kugwira ntchito, kusankha ntchito mwaufulu, kuchita chilungamo ndi kuchitapo kanthu 
mikhalidwe yabwino pantchito komanso chitetezo ku ulova.  
2. Aliyense, mopanda tsankho, ali ndi danga lolandira malipiro ofanana 
ntchito.  
3. Aliyense wogwira ntchito ali ndi danga lolandira malipiro oyenera 
kudzipezera yekha ndi banja lake kukhala ndi moyo koyenera ulemu waumunthu, 
ndi kuwonjezeredwa, ngati kuli kofunikira, ndi njira zina zotetezera chikhalidwe cha anthu.  
4. Aliyense ali ndi danga lopanga ndi kulowa mipingo ya anthu ogwira nchito kuti atetezedwe 
zokonda zake.  
Ndime 24  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kupumula ndi kusanguluka, kuphatikizirapo malire oyenerera 
maola ogwira ntchito ndi tchuthi chanthawi ndi nthawi ndi malipiro.  
 

 
Ndime 25  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo wokwanira kuti akhale ndi thanzi labwino 
ubwino wake ndi wa banja lake, kuphatikizapo chakudya, zovala, nyumba 
ndi chithandizo chamankhwala ndi ntchito zofunikira za anthu, komanso ufulu wotetezedwa 
pakagwa ulova, matenda, kulumala, umasiye, ukalamba kapena 
kusowa zina zofunika pamoyo m'mikhalidwe yomwe iye sangathe kulamulira.
2. Umayi ndi ubwana ali oyenera kusamalidwa ndi chithandizo chapadera. Zonse 
ana, kaya obadwa m’banja kapena kunja kwa banja, azisangalala ndi mayanjano ofananawo 
chitetezo.  
Ndime 26  
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wophunzira. Maphunziro adzakhala aulere, osachepera mu 
magawo oyambira komanso oyambira. Maphunziro a pulayimale adzakhala 
mokakamizidwa. Maphunziro aukadaulo ndi ukatswiri adzapangidwa 
zopezeka kawirikawiri komanso maphunziro apamwamba azipezeka kwa onse mofanana 
pamaziko a kuyenerera.  
2. Maphunziro adzalunjikitsidwa ku chitukuko chokwanira cha munthu 
umunthu ndi kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu wa anthu ndi 
ufulu wachibadwidwe. Zidzalimbikitsa kumvetsetsa, kulolerana ndi 
ubwenzi pakati pa mafuko onse, mafuko kapena magulu achipembedzo, ndipo adzapitiriza 
ntchito za United Nations zolimbikitsa mtendere.  
3. Makolo ali ndi ufulu woyamba kusankha mtundu wa maphunziro omwe adzakhale 
kupatsidwa kwa ana awo.  
Ndime 27  
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa chikhalidwe cha anthu 
anthu, kusangalala ndi zaluso komanso kugawana nawo zachitukuko cha sayansi ndi 
ubwino wake.  
 

 
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lotetezedwa pa makhalidwe ake ndi zofuna zakuthupi 
chifukwa cha sayansi, zolemba kapena zaluso zilizonse zomwe iye ali 
wolemba.  
Ndime 28  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kukhala ndi chikhalidwe cha anthu ndi mayiko momwe ufulu ndi ufulu 
Ufulu wofotokozedwa m'Chikalatachi ukhoza kukwaniritsidwa.  
Ndime 29
1. Aliyense ali ndi ntchito mdera limene muli mfulu ndi wodzaza 
kukula kwa umunthu wake ndi kotheka.  
2. Pogwiritsa ntchito ufulu ndi kumasulika kwake, aliyense azimvera yekha 
ku malire omwe akhazikitsidwa ndi lamulo ndi cholinga cha 
kupeza kuzindikirika koyenera ndi kulemekeza ufulu ndi kumasuka kwa ena 
ndi kukwaniritsa zofunikira za makhalidwe abwino, dongosolo la anthu ndi 
ubwino wamba mu gulu lademokalase.  
3. Ufulu ndi kumasuka izi sizingagwiritsidwe ntchito motsutsana ndi 
zolinga ndi mfundo za United Nations.  
Ndime 30  
Palibe chilichonse mu Chidziwitsochi chomwe chingatanthauzidwe kuti chikuimira boma lililonse, gulu kapena 
munthu ali ndi ufulu wochita chilichonse kapena kuchita chilichonse chokhudza 
kuonongedwa kwa ufulu uliwonse ndi kumasuka zomwe zafotokozedwa apa.
