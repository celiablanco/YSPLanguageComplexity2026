Umntu unelungelo lokuthathwa njengomsulwa de kuchotshelwe ityala lakhe kuze kungqinwe ityala lakhe ngokomthetho. 
2. (ii) Nasiphi na isenzo okanye ukungenziwa kwakhe nawuphi na umntu, okuthe, ngexesha senzeka, kungathintelwanga ngumthetho wesizwe okanye wezizwe ngezizwe.

Amalungiselelo omthetho asebenzayo xa usenza ulwaphulo-mthetho.

Inqaku lama-21
