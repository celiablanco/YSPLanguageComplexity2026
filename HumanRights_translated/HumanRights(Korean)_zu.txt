Isimemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu

Ukuqaphela isithunzi esizalwa nakho namalungelo alinganayo nangenakugwenywa awo wonke amalungu ohlanga lwesintu kuyisisekelo senkululeko, ubulungisa nokuthula emhlabeni; ukungahloniphi nokudelelwa kwamalungelo abantu kuye kwaholela ezenzweni ezimbi ezithukuthelise unembeza wesintu; kanye nokufika kwezwe lapho umuntu engajabulela khona inkululeko yokukhuluma nenkolelo kanye nokukhululeka ekwesabeni nasekusweleni kuye kwamenyezelwa njengesifiso esikhulu kunazo zonke sabantu bonke, funa umuntu aphoqwe ukuba ahlubuke njengesinyathelo sokugcina sokulwa nobudlova nengcindezelo; Kubalulekile ukuthi amalungelo abantu avikelwe umthetho, futhi kubalulekile ukuthuthukisa ubudlelwano bobungani phakathi kwezizwe. KuSomqulu wawo, wonke amalungu eNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene aphinde aqinisekisa inkolelo yawo emalungelweni abantu ayisisekelo, isithunzi nokufaneleka komuntu, namalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabesifazane, futhi azibophezele ekukhuthazeni inqubekelaphambili yomphakathi kanye namazinga angcono okuphila ngenkululeko ebanzi. Amazwe Angamalungu azibophezele ukubambisana neNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene ukukhuthaza ukuhlonishwa komhlaba wonke kanye nokugcinwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo. Njengoba ukuqonda okufanayo ngamalungelo nenkululeko kubaluleke kakhulu ekufezekisweni ngokugcwele kwalesi Sivumelwano, uMkhandlu-jikelele weNhlangano Yezizwe, ngakho-ke, uzama ukukhuthaza ukuhlonishwa kwala malungelo nenkululeko ngokufunda nokufundisa phakathi kwabo bonke abantu nezikhungo zomphakathi, njalo ukhumbula lesi Simemezelo, futhi ulwela, ngokusebenzisa izinyathelo eziqhubekayo zezwe nezamazwe ngamazwe, ukuqinisekisa ukuqashelwa emhlabeni wonke nangempumelelo kwamalungelo kanye nokugcinwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko yamazwe ngokwawo. abantu bezindawo ezingaphansi kombuso wabo; Simemezela i-Universal Declaration of Human Rights. I-athikili 1

Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi belingana ngesithunzi nangamalungelo. Abantu ngokwemvelo baphiwe ukucabanga nonembeza futhi kufanele benze ngomoya wobuzalwane komunye nomunye. Isigaba 2 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lawo wonke amalungelo nenkululeko ebalulwe kulesi Simemezelo, ngaphandle kokubandlululwa kwanoma yiluphi uhlobo, njengobuhlanga, ibala, ubulili, ulimi, inkolo, ipolitiki noma omunye umbono, ubuzwe noma inhlalakahle, impahla, ukuzalwa noma esinye isikhundla. Ngaphezu kwalokho, angeke kube khona ukucwasa ngenxa yesimo sezombangazwe, somthetho noma samazwe ngamazwe sezwe noma indawo yomuntu ngamunye, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi lelo zwe noma indawo iyizwe elizimele, i-trusteeship, indawo engabuseki, noma ingaphansi kweminye imikhawulo embusweni wayo. Isigaba 3 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kanye nokuvikeleka kwakhe. Isigaba 4 Akekho oyogcinwa ebugqilini noma ebugqilini. Zonke izinhlobo zobugqila nokuhweba ngezigqila azivunyelwe. Isigaba 5 Akukho muntu oyohlukunyezwa noma aphathwe ngonya, aphathwe ngendlela engenabuntu noma elulazayo noma ajeziswe. Isigaba 6 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokwamukelwa njengomuntu phambi komthetho yonke indawo. I-athikili yesi-7
Bonke abantu bayalingana phambi komthetho futhi banelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo emthethweni ngaphandle kokubandlululwa. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo kunoma yikuphi ukucwaswa okwephula lesi Simemezelo kanye nanoma yikuphi ukugqugquzela lokho kubandlululwa. Isigaba 8 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuthola impumuzo esebenzayo ezinkantolo zasekhaya ezifanele ngezenzo ezephula amalungelo ayisisekelo anikezwa uMthethosisekelo noma umthetho. Isigaba 9 Akekho oyoboshwa ngokungafanele, avalelwe noma adingiswe. Isigaba 10 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuquliswa icala ngendlela efanele futhi esobala ngokulingana okuphelele phambi kwenkantolo ezimele futhi engachemi ukuze kunqunywe amalungelo akhe, izibopho kanye namacala akhe obugebengu. Isigaba 11 1. Wonke umuntu osolwa ngobugebengu unelungelo lokubhekwa njengomsulwa kuze kutholakale ukuthi unecala ngokomthetho ekuqulweni kwamacala omphakathi lapho kuqinisekiswa khona konke okudingekayo ukuze azivikele. 2. Akekho oyotholwa enecala ngenxa yanoma yisiphi isenzo noma ukweqiwa, ngesikhathi sesenzo, okwakungelona icala ngaphansi komthetho kazwelonke noma wamazwe ngamazwe. Ukwengeza, asikho isijeziso esinzima esizonikezwa ukwedlula isijeziso ebesingasetshenziswa ngesikhathi sobugebengu. Isigaba 12 Akukho muntu oyophazanyiswa ngokungafanele empilweni yakhe yangasese, ikhaya, indawo ahlala kuyo noma izincwadi zakhe, noma ukugxekwa ngesithunzi nesithunzi sakhe. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngumthetho kulokho kugxambukela noma ukugxekwa. Isigaba 13

1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhamba nokuhlala ezweni lakhe. 2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokushiya noma yiliphi izwe, kuhlanganise nelakhe, nelungelo lokubuyela ezweni lakubo. Isigaba 14 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokufuna nokuthola indawo yokukhosela kwamanye amazwe ekushushisweni. 2. Leli lungelo alinakugonyelwa emacaleni okushushiswa ngamacala angelona ezepolitiki ngempela noma izenzo ezingqubuzana nezinjongo nezimiso zeNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene. Isigaba 15 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lobuzwe. 2. Akekho oyophucwa ubuzwe bakhe noma ancishwe ilungelo lokushintsha ubuzwe bakhe. Isigaba 16 1. Abesilisa nabesifazane abadala banelungelo lokushada futhi bakhe umndeni ngaphandle kwemingcele esekelwe ngokobuhlanga, ubuzwe noma inkolo. Banelungelo lokuthola amalungelo alinganayo mayelana nomshado, ngesikhathi somshado kanye nalapho kuchithwa umshado. 2. Umshado usungulwa kuphela ngemvume ekhululekile nephelele yabaganene bakusasa. 3. Umndeni uyingxenye yemvelo neyisisekelo yomphakathi futhi unelungelo lokuvikelwa umphakathi kanye nombuso. Isigaba 17 Isigaba 17 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba nobunikazi bempahla kokubili ngabanye nangokuhlanganyela nabanye. 2. Akekho oyophucwa impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu. Isigaba 18
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga, kanembeza neyenkolo. Leli lungelo lihlanganisa inkululeko yokuguqula inkolo noma inkolo yomuntu kanye nokubonakalisa inkolo noma izinkolelo zakhe, eyedwa noma emphakathini nabanye futhi esidlangalaleni noma ngasese, ngemikhuba yezenkolo, ukukhulekela nokugcinwa. Isigaba 19 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokubeka imibono yakhe nokuveza imizwa yakhe. La malungelo ahlanganisa inkululeko yokuba nemibono ngaphandle kokuphazanyiswa kanye nenkululeko yokufuna, ukuthola nokudlulisa ulwazi nemibono nganoma iyiphi imidiya, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi imingcele ikuphi. Isigaba 20 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana nokuhlangana ngokuthula. 2. Akekho oyophoqwa ukuthi abambe iqhaza kunoma iyiphi inhlangano. Isigaba 21 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza kuhulumeni wezwe lakhe, ngokuqondile noma ngabamele abakhethwe ngokukhululeka. 2. Wonke umuntu unamalungelo alinganayo okuba nesikhundla emphakathini ezweni lakhe. 3. Intando yabantu iyisisekelo samandla kahulumeni. Lokhu kuzovezwa ngokhetho olujwayelekile nolwangempela ngezinqubo zokuvota eziyimfihlo noma ngokulinganayo ngokuvumelana nelungelo lokuzikhethela elitholakala emhlabeni wonke nelilinganayo. Isigaba 22 Wonke umuntu, njengelungu lomphakathi, unelungelo lokuvikeleka komphakathi kanye nelungelo lokubona, ngemizamo yesizwe nokubambisana kwamazwe ngamazwe, futhi ngokuhambisana nenhlangano nezinsiza zoMbuso ngamunye, amalungelo ezomnotho, ezenhlalakahle namasiko abalulekile ukuze kuthuthukiswe ngokukhululeka isithunzi nobuntu bakhe. Isigaba 23

1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukukhetha ngokukhululekile umsebenzi wakhe, izimo zokusebenza ezilungile nezivumayo kanye nokuvikelwa ekuphelelweni umsebenzi. 2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuthola isinxephezelo esilinganayo ngomsebenzi ofanayo ngaphandle kokubandlululwa. 3. Wonke umuntu osebenzayo unelungelo lokuthola inkokhelo efanele nenenzuzo, eqinisekisa yena nomndeni wakhe ukusinda okuhambisana nesithunzi sobuntu futhi kulekelelwa, uma kunesidingo, ngezinye izindlela zokuvikeleka komphakathi. 4. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusungula nokujoyina inyunyana yabasebenzi ukuze avikele izithakazelo zakhe. Isigaba 24 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula nelokungcebeleka, okuhlanganisa nemikhawulo efanele yamahora okusebenza nekhefu elikhokhelwayo elivamile. Isigaba 25 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lezinga lempilo nenhlalakahle yakhe nomndeni wakhe, okuhlanganisa ukudla, okokwembatha, indawo yokuhlala, ukunakekelwa kwezokwelapha kanye nenhlalakahle yenhlalakahle edingekayo, nelungelo lokuvikeleka uma entula indlela yokuziphilisa ngenxa yokungasebenzi, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, ukushona komngane wakhe womshado, ukuguga noma ezinye izimo ezingaphezu kwamandla akhe. 2. Omama nezingane banelungelo lokuvikelwa nokusekelwa okukhethekile. Zonke izingane zijabulela ukuvikelwa komphakathi okufanayo kungakhathaliseki isimo sazo. Isigaba 26 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo kumele ibe mahhala, okungenani emazingeni aphansi naphansi. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi kufanele ibe yimpoqo. Imfundo yezobuchwepheshe neyemisebenzi yezandla kufanele ifinyeleleke emhlabeni wonke, futhi imfundo ephakeme kufanele ifinyeleleke ngokulinganayo kubo bonke ngokusekelwe ekuzuzeni impumelelo. 2. Imfundo ihlose ukuthuthukisa ubuntu ngokugcwele kanye nokuqinisa ukuhlonishwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo. Imfundo ikhuthaza ukuqonda, ukubekezelelana kanye
Kufanele sikhuthaze ubungane futhi sikhuthaze imisebenzi yeNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene ukuze silondoloze ukuthula. 3. Abazali banokubaluleka kokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo enikezwa izingane zabo. Isigaba 27 1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ngokukhululekile empilweni yamasiko omphakathi, athokozele ezobuciko, futhi ahlanganyele ekuthuthukisweni kwesayensi kanye nezinzuzo zayo. 2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ezintweni ezingokomoya nezingokwenyama ezivela emikhiqizweni yesayensi, yemibhalo noma yobuciko ayidalayo. Isigaba 28 Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhleleka kwezenhlalo kanye nezamazwe ngamazwe lapho amalungelo nenkululeko ekulesi simemezelo kungafezeka khona ngokugcwele. Isigaba 29 1. Wonke umuntu unezibopho emphakathini lapho ubuntu bakhe kuphela bungathuthuka ngokukhululeka nangokugcwele. 2. Wonke umuntu, lapho esebenzisa amalungelo nenkululeko yakhe, ungaphansi kwemikhawulo ebekwe umthetho kuphela ngenjongo yokuqinisekisa ukuthi amalungelo nenkululeko yabanye ayaqashelwa futhi ayahlonishwa, futhi ngenjongo yokuhlangabezana nezidingo ezingokomthetho zokuziphatha, ukuhleleka komphakathi kanye nenhlalakahle jikelele emphakathini wentando yeningi. 3. Lawa malungelo kanye nenkululeko ngeke kusetshenziswe ngaphansi kwezimo eziphambene nezinjongo nezimiso zeNhlangano yeziZwe. Isigaba 30 Akukho lutho kulesi siMemezelo oluyohunyushwa njengokugunyaza noma yimuphi uMbuso, iqembu noma umuntu ukuba enze noma yimuphi umsebenzi noma ukuthatha isinyathelo esihloselwe ukucekela phansi noma yimaphi amalungelo nezinkululeko ezibalulwe kulesi Simemezelo.
