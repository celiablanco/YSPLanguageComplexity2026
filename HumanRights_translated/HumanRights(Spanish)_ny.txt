General Assembly  
Ikulengeza kuti Universal Declaration of Human Rights yomwe ilipo panopa ndi yabwino 
cholinga chimodzi chimene anthu onse ndi mayiko ayenera kuyesetsa, kotero kuti 
anthu ndi mabungwe, nthawi zonse kukopa chidwi kuchokera kwa izo, 
kulimbikitsa, kupyolera mu kuphunzitsa ndi maphunziro, kulemekeza ufulu umenewu 
ndi kumasulika, ndikuwonetsetsa, kupyolera mu njira zopita patsogolo za dziko ndi 
malamulo apadziko lonse lapansi, kuzindikira kwake kwapadziko lonse komanso kogwira ntchito, pakati pawo 
anthu a Mayiko Amembala komanso omwe ali m'madera omwe aikidwa 
pansi pa ulamuliro wake.  
Ndime 1  
Anthu onse amabadwa aufulu ndi ofanana mu ulemu ndi ufulu, 
monga momwe alili ndi kulingalira ndi chikumbumtima, ayenera kuchita zinthu mwaubale 
ndi wina ndi mzake.  
Ndime 2  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zalengezedwa m'chikalatachi, 
popanda kusiyanitsa mtundu, mtundu, kugonana, chinenero, chipembedzo, maganizo a ndale kapena 
chikhalidwe china chilichonse, dziko kapena chikhalidwe, udindo chuma, kubadwa kapena 
chikhalidwe china chilichonse.
Kuphatikiza apo, palibe kusiyanitsa komwe kudzachitike potengera zandale, zamalamulo kapena ndale. 
yapadziko lonse lapansi kapena gawo lomwe munthu amadalira, 
kaya ndi dziko lodziyimira pawokha kapena gawo lake 
fiduciary administration, osati kudziyimira pawokha kapena kutengera malire ena aliwonse 
ulamuliro.  
Ndime 3  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi moyo, ufulu ndi chitetezo cha moyo wake. 
munthu.  
 

 
Ndime 4  
Palibe amene adzakhala kapolo kapena ukapolo; ukapolo ndi malonda 
Akapolo amaletsedwa m'njira zosiyanasiyana.  
Ndime 5  
Palibe amene adzazunzidwe, kuzunzidwa, kuchitidwa nkhanza, kuzunzidwa kapena kulangidwa. 
kutsitsa ulemu  
Ndime 6  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu, kulikonse, kuti azindikire zawo 
umunthu walamulo.  
Ndime 7  
Onse ndi ofanana pamaso pa lamulo ndipo ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana popanda kusiyanitsa 
wa lamulo. Aliyense ali ndi danga lotetezedwa kukusalidwa kulikonse 
ikuphwanya Chigamulochi komanso motsutsana ndi zoyambitsa tsankho zotere.  
Ndime 8  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lolandira chithandizo choyenera ku bwalo lamilandu. 
maulamuliro oyenerera, kuwateteza ku zinthu zophwanya ufulu wawo 
ufulu wachibadwidwe wovomerezedwa ndi lamulo ladziko kapena lamulo.  
Ndime 9  
Palibe amene angatsekedwe popanda zifukwa zomveka, kumangidwa kapena kuthamangitsidwa.  
Ndime 10  
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu, mogwirizana, kuti azimveka
poyera ndi mwachilungamo ndi bwalo loyima palokha komanso lopanda tsankho, chifukwa cha 
 

 
kudziwa za ufulu ndi udindo wawo kapena kuunika chilichonse 
mlandu womuneneza pankhani zaupandu.  
Ndime 11  
1. Munthu aliyense woimbidwa mlandu ali ndi ufulu wongoganiziridwa kuti ndi ndani 
kukhala wosalakwa kufikira atatsimikizidwa kuti ndi wolakwa, monga mwa lamulo ndi mwa 
mlandu wapagulu momwe zitsimikizo zonse zatsimikiziridwa 
zofunika kwa chitetezo chake.  
2. Palibe amene adzatsutsidwe chifukwa cha zochita kapena zolephera zomwe panthawiyo 
olakwa sanali mlandu malinga ndi malamulo a dziko kapena mayiko. 
Komanso sipadzaperekedwa chilango choopsa kuposa chimene chinkaperekedwa pa nthawiyo 
kuchitidwa kwa mlanduwo.  
Ndime 12  
Palibe amene angasokonezedwe mosasamala pa moyo wake wamseri, banja lawo, lawo 
nyumba kapena makalata ake, kapena kuukira ulemu wake kapena mbiri yake. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutetezedwa ndi lamulo kuti asasokonezedwe kapena 
kuwukira.  
Ndime 13  
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kuyenda mwaufulu ndikusankha malo okhala 
m'dera la State.  
2. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kuchoka m'dziko lililonse, kuphatikizira dziko lake, ndi 
kuti abwerere ku dziko lake.  
Ndime 14  
1. Ngati akuzunzidwa, aliyense ali ndi ufulu wofunafuna chitetezo, ndi 
Sangalalani nazo, m'dziko lililonse.  
 

 
2. Ufuluwu sungagwiritsidwe ntchito poweruza mlandu wowona 
zoyambitsidwa ndi milandu wamba kapena zochita zosemphana ndi zolinga ndi
mfundo za United Nations.  
Ndime 15  
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi dziko.  
2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe mtundu wake kapena ufulu wake 
kusintha dziko.  
Ndime 16  
1. Amuna ndi akazi, kuyambira msinkhu wokwatira, ali ndi ufulu, opanda 
chiletso chilichose pachifukwa cha fuko, dziko kapena chipembedzo, kukwatira 
ndipo anapeza banja; ndipo adzakhala ndi ufulu wofanana pa 
ukwati, pa nthawi ya ukwati komanso ngati ukwati uthe.  
2. Pokhapokha mwa ufulu ndi chilolezo chokwanira cha okwatirana amtsogolo angathe 
kukwatiwa.  
3. Banja ndi chibadwidwe ndi chikhazikitso cha chikhalidwe cha anthu ndipo chatero 
ufulu wotetezedwa kwa anthu ndi boma.  
Ndime 17  
1. Munthu ali yense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi katundu, payekha ndi gulu lonse.  
2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe chuma chake mwachisawawa.  
Ndime 18  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu woganiza, chikumbumtima ndi 
chipembedzo; Ufulu umenewu umaphatikizapo ufulu wosintha chipembedzo kapena chikhulupiriro, komanso 
monga ufulu wosonyeza chipembedzo kapena zikhulupiriro zake, payekha komanso 
pamodzi, ponse pagulu ndi mwachinsinsi, mwa kuphunzitsa, kuchita, 
kupembedza ndi kusunga.  
 

 
Ndime 19  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi maganizo ake; izi 
ufulu umaphatikizapo kusasokonezedwa chifukwa cha maganizo ake, ufulu wofufuza ndi
kulandira zidziwitso ndi malingaliro, ndikufalitsa, popanda malire, 
mwa njira iliyonse yofotokozera.  
Ndime 20  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga losonkhana pamodzi ndi kusonkhana pamodzi 
wamtendere.  
2. Palibe amene angakakamizidwe kukhala membala wa bungwe.  
Ndime 21  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lotengapo mbali mu boma la dziko lake; 
mwachindunji kapena kudzera mwa nthumwi zosankhidwa mwaufulu.  
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi mwayi wopeza mipata, malinga ndi mikhalidwe yofanana 
ntchito za boma za dziko lawo.  
3. Chifuniro cha anthu ndicho maziko a ulamuliro wa boma; izi 
zidzawonetsedwa kudzera muzosankha zenizeni zomwe ziyenera kukhala 
zizichitika nthawi ndi nthawi, ndi ufulu wapadziko lonse lapansi komanso wofanana ndi mavoti 
chinsinsi kapena njira ina yofananira yomwe imatsimikizira ufulu wa 
voti.  
Ndime 22  
Munthu aliyense, monga membala wa gulu, ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa 
chikhalidwe, ndi kupeza, kupyolera mu khama la dziko ndi mgwirizano wa mayiko, 
poganizira za dongosolo ndi chuma cha Boma lililonse, kukhutitsidwa 
za ufulu wa zachuma, chikhalidwe ndi chikhalidwe, zofunika pa ulemu wawo 
ndi chitukuko chaulere cha umunthu wawo.  
 

 
Ndime 23  
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kugwira ntchito, kusankha ntchito mwaufulu, ku 
mikhalidwe yolingana ndi yokhutiritsa ya ntchito ndi chitetezo 
ulova. 
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu, popanda tsankho, malipiro ofanana
Ndimagwira ntchito chimodzimodzi.  
3. Munthu aliyense wogwira ntchito ali ndi ufulu kulandira malipiro oyenera 
zokhutiritsa, zomwe zimatsimikizira kuti iye, komanso banja lake, kukhalapo 
molingana ndi ulemu wa munthu ndipo izi zidzakwaniritsidwa, ngati zingatheke 
zofunikira, ndi njira zina zilizonse zotetezera anthu.  
4. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhazikitsa migwirizano ndi kulowa mipingo 
kuteteza zofuna zawo.  
Ndime 24  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wopuma, kusangalala ndi nthawi yopuma, a 
kuchepetsa koyenera kwa nthawi ya ntchito ndi tchuthi chanthawi ndi nthawi 
kulipira.  
Ndime 25  
1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo wabwino umene umaonetsetsa, 
komanso banja lanu, thanzi ndi moyo wabwino, makamaka 
chakudya, zovala, nyumba, chithandizo chamankhwala ndi ntchito 
zofunika social Mulinso ndi ufulu inshuwalansi ngati 
ulova, matenda, kulumala, umasiye, ukalamba ndi zina zotayika 
za njira zawo zopezera zofunika pa moyo chifukwa cha zinthu zosagwirizana ndi iwo 
adzatero.  
2. Umayi ndi ubwana zili ndi ufulu wosamalidwa ndi kuthandizidwa 
zapadera. Ana onse, kaya anabadwira m’banja kapena kunja kwa banja 
m'banja, ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana.  
 

 
Ndime 26  
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wophunzira. Maphunziro ayenera kukhala 
kwaulere, makamaka pankhani ya maphunziro a pulaimale ndi 
zofunika. Maphunziro a pulayimale adzakhala ovomerezeka. Malangizo
ukadaulo ndi ukatswiri uyenera kukhala wokhazikika; mwayi wopeza maphunziro 
akuluakulu adzakhala ofanana kwa onse, malingana ndi ubwino wawo.  
2. Maphunziro adzayang'ana pa chitukuko chonse cha umunthu 
ufulu wa anthu ndi kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu wa anthu ndi 
maufulu ofunika; zidzalimbikitsa kumvetsetsa, kulolerana ndi 
ubwenzi pakati pa mitundu yonse ndi mafuko onse kapena magulu achipembedzo; ndi 
kulimbikitsa chitukuko cha ntchito za United Nations 
kusunga mtendere.  
3. Makolo adzakhala ndi ufulu wosankha mtundu wa maphunziro 
amene adzapatsidwa kwa ana awo.  
Ndime 27  
1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa chikhalidwe chawo 
za anthu ammudzi, kusangalala ndi zaluso komanso kutenga nawo mbali pantchitoyi 
zasayansi ndi mapindu ake.  
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lotetezedwa ku zokomera mtima ndi 
zinthu zomwe zimagwirizana nazo chifukwa cha zopanga zasayansi, 
zolemba kapena zaluso zomwe iye ndiye mlembi wake.  
Ndime 28  
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhazikitsa dongosolo la chikhalidwe ndi dziko 
momwe ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zalengezedwa mu Chidziwitso ichi zikupangidwa 
mokwanira.  
Ndime 29  
 

 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ntchito yokhudzana ndi anthu ammudzi, kuyambira mu 
amatha kukulitsa umunthu wake momasuka komanso mokwanira.  
2. Pogwiritsa ntchito ufulu wawo ndi kusangalala ndi ufulu wawo, onse
munthu adzakhala ndi malire okhazikitsidwa ndi lamulo 
ndi cholinga chokhacho chowonetsetsa kuzindikirika ndi ulemu wa 
ufulu ndi kumasuka kwa ena, ndi kukwaniritsa zofuna zachilungamo 
za makhalidwe abwino, dongosolo la anthu ndi umoyo wabwino wa anthu onse 
wademokalase.  
3. Ufulu ndi kumasuka izi sizingagwiritsidwe ntchito mwanjira iliyonse 
kutsutsa zolinga ndi mfundo za United Nations. 
Ndime 30  
Palibe mu Chilengezo ichi chomwe chingatanthauzidwe kuti chikutanthauza zimenezo 
limapereka ufulu uliwonse ku Boma, gulu kapena munthu, kuchita 
ndikukhazikitsa ntchito kapena kuchita zinthu zomwe zikufuna kupondereza chilichonse 
za ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zalengezedwa m'chikalatachi.
