IsiBhengezo seHlabathi samaLungelo oLuntu 
Yamkelwa yaza yabhengezwa ngokwesigqibo seNdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo 217 A (III) yomhla we-10 kuDisemba 
1948. 
INTSHAYELELO 
Ngelixa kuthathelwa ingqalelo isidima sendalo sabo bonke 
amalungu oluntu, kunye namalungelo awo alinganayo nangenakohluthwa 
isiseko senkululeko, ubulungisa noxolo lwehlabathi; kwaye  
Ngexa ukungahoywa nokujongelwa phantsi kwamalungelo oluntu 
kukhokelele kwizenzo zoburhalarhume ezicaphukisa isazela soluntu, kwaye 
ukuba ukudalwa kwehlabathi apho abantu baya kuba nenkululeko yokuthetha kunye 
iinkolelo kwaye uya kukhululeka kuloyiko kunye nentswelo, ibhengezwe njenge 
ubushushu bomntu ophezulu; kwaye 
Nangona kuyimfuneko ukuba amalungelo oluntu akhuselwe 
igunya lomthetho ukuqinisekisa ukuba umntu akanyanzeliswa 
ikhefu, njengento yokugqibela, ukuvukela ubuzwilakhe kunye 
ingcinezelo; kwaye  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba kuyimfuneko ukukhuthaza uphuhliso 
ubudlelwane phakathi kwabantu; kwaye  
Ngoxa abantu beZizwe eziManyeneyo baye baqinisekisa 
Itshatha inkolelo yayo kumalungelo oluntu asisiseko, isidima kunye nokuxabiseka 
ubuntu bomntu kunye nokulingana kwamadoda nabasetyhini kunye nesigqibo 
ukukhuthaza inkqubela phambili yentlalo nokuphucula iimeko zokuphila phantsi 
inkululeko enkulu; kwaye 
Nangona amaZwe angamaLungu ezibophelele ekuncedeni, kwi 
intsebenziswano neZizwe eziManyeneyo, jikelele 
intlonipho nokuhoywa kwamalungelo oluntu neziseko zenkululeko; Kwaye
Nangona kukho ukuqonda ngokubanzi uhlobo lwala malungelo kunye 
iinkululeko zibaluleke kakhulu ekuphunyezweni ngokupheleleyo koku 
izibophelelo, 
 

 
INdibano Jikelele,  
ibhengeza le Universal Declaration of Human Rights njenge 
umsebenzi apho zonke izizwe kunye 
chaza ukuba wonke umntu kunye nalo lonke iqumrhu loluntu, 
Nisikhumbula esi siBhengezo ngamaxesha onke, zabalazelani ukudlula 
imfundo noqeqesho lokukhuthaza ukuhlonitshwa kwala malungelo nenkululeko kunye 
ukuqinisekisa, ngenkqubela phambili yesizwe neyehlabathi 
imisebenzi, ukuqondwa kunye nokuphunyezwa kwehlabathi jikelele 
kokubini phakathi kwabantu bamazwe angamalungu oMbutho naphakathi kwabantu 
imimandla ephantsi kolawulo lwabo.  
Inqaku loku-1  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile kwaye belingana ngesidima nangamalungelo. 
Banikwe ingqiqo nesazela kwaye bamele benze ngokunxulumene omnye nomnye. 
umhlobo ngomoya wobuzalwana.  
Inqaku lesi-2 
Wonke umntu kufuneka abe nawo onke amalungelo kunye nazo zonke iinkululeko, 
ibhengezwe ngesi siBhengezo, ngaphandle kwayo 
iiyantlukwano ezifana nobuhlanga, umbala, isini, ulwimi, inkolo, 
iinkolelo zezopolitiko okanye ezinye, ezelizwe okanye ezentlalo 
imvelaphi, ipropati, udidi okanye enye iwonga.  
Ngaphezu koko, akukho mahluko kufuneka enziwe ngesiseko 
ubume bezopolitiko, bomthetho okanye behlabathi belizwe okanye 
ummandla akuwo umntu, nokuba ukuwo
nokuba loo mmandla uzimele, uthenjwe, awuzilawulanga okanye 
ngenye indlela isikelwe umda kulongamo lwawo. 
Inqaku lesi-3 
 

 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kunye nokuba neemfihlo 
ukunganyaniseki.  
Inqaku lesi-4 
Akukho mntu ufanele abanjwe ebukhobokeni okanye ebukhobokeni; 
Ubukhoboka nokurhweba ngamakhoboka akuvumelekanga ngandlela zonke.  
Inqaku lesi-5 
Akukho namnye ofanele angcungcuthekiswe okanye aphathwe ngenkohlakalo, engenabuntu okanye 
impatho ethoba isidima nesohlwayo. 
Inqaku lesi-6 
Wonke umntu, nokuba uphi na, unelungelo lokwamkelwa 
ubuntu obusemthethweni. 
Inqaku lesi-7 
Bonke abantu bayalingana phambi komthetho kwaye banelungelo, kungekho mahluko, ukuba 
ukhuseleko olulinganayo lomthetho. Bonke abantu banelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo nakuwuphi na 
naluphi na ucalucalulo olwaphula esi siBhengezo, kwaye ukusuka 
nakuphi na ukuphembelela kucalucalulo olunjalo.  
Inqaku lesi-8 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokubuyiselwa ngempumelelo kwamalungelo 
iinkundla zesizwe ezifanelekileyo kumatyala okwaphulwa kwesiseko sayo 
amalungelo awanikwe ngumgaqo-siseko okanye ngumthetho.  
Inqaku lesi-9 
 

 
Akukho mntu unokubanjwa, avalelwe okanye avalelwe 
elubhacweni.  
Inqaku le-10 
Umntu ngamnye, ukugqiba amalungelo akhe kunye nemisebenzi kunye noku 
eqinisekisa ubunyani bezityholo zolwaphulo-mthetho ezibekwa ngakuye; 
unelungelo, ngesiseko sokulingana okupheleleyo, ukuba abe netyala lakhe 
iphononongwe esidlangalaleni kwaye ithobela zonke iimfuno zobulungisa 
inkundla ezimeleyo nengakhethi cala.  
Inqaku le-11
1. Wonke umntu otyholwa ngokwenza ulwaphulo-mthetho uye 
ilungelo lokubonwa umsulwa de ufunyanwe unetyala 
iya kusekwa ngokusemthethweni ngengxoxo yoluntu 
iinkqubo apho abonelelwa ngawo onke amathuba 
ukhuseleko. 
2. Akukho mntu unokugwetyelwa ulwaphulo-mthetho ngokwesiseko 
ukumiselwa kwaso nasiphi na isenzo okanye ukungasebenzi, okuthe 
ixesha lekomishini yabo ayizange ibe bulwaphulo-mthetho ngokwelizwe 
imithetho okanye umthetho wamazwe ngamazwe. Ayinakunyanzeliswa kwakhona 
Isohlwayo esiqatha kuneso ebesinokufakwa kuso 
ixesha lokwenziwa kolwaphulo-mthetho. 
Inqaku le-12 
Akukho mntu unokuphazanyiswa ngokungenamkhethe kwizinto zakhe zobuqu kunye 
ubomi bentsapho, ukuhlaselwa ngokungenasizathu kwingqibelelo ye 
ikhaya lakhe, imfihlo yembalelwano yakhe okanye imbeko nodumo lwakhe. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokhuseleko lomthetho ngokuchasene nolo ngenelelo 
okanye uhlaselo olunjalo.  
 

 
Inqaku le-13 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululekileyo nokukhetha 
indawo yakhe yokuhlala ngaphakathi kwiphondo ngalinye. 
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokushiya nakweliphi na ilizwe, kuquka nelakhe 
bagoduke, babuyele ezweni labo. 
Inqaku le-14 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufuna indawo yokhuseleko kwintshutshiso 
kwamanye amazwe kwaye basebenzise eli thuba lokusabela. 
2. Eli lungelo alinakusetyenziswa kwimeko yentshutshiso, kwi 
inyaniso esekwe kwikhomishini engeyiyo eyezopolitiko
ubugebenga okanye isenzo esichasene neenjongo nemigaqo 
IZizwe eziManyeneyo. 
Inqaku le-15 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lobumi. 
2. Akukho mntu unokuhluthwa ubuzwe bakhe okanye 
ilungelo lokutshintsha ubumi bakho. 
Inqaku le-16 
1. Amadoda nabafazi abafikelele kwiminyaka yobudala banelungelo ngaphandle 
naziphi na izithintelo ezisekelwe kubuhlanga, ubuzwe okanye inkolo 
tshata kwaye uqale usapho. Bayayonwabela 
amalungelo alinganayo ngokubhekiselele emtshatweni, ngexesha 
iimeko ngexesha lomtshato nangexesha lokuchithwa kwawo. 
2. Umtshato unokuqukunjelwa kuphela ngokukhululekileyo nangemvume epheleleyo 
omabini amaqabane emtshatweni. 
3. Usapho yinxalenye yendalo kunye nesiseko soluntu kwaye inalo 
ilungelo lokhuseleko eluntwini nakurhulumente. 
 

 
Inqaku le-17 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuba nezinto ezizezakhe njengomntu, 
kunye nabanye. 
2. Akukho mntu ufanele ahluthwe impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu. 
Inqaku le-18  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucinga, isazela nenkolo; oku 
Ilungelo liquka inkululeko yokutshintsha unqulo okanye ukholo nenkululeko 
ukuxela inkolo okanye iinkolelo zikabani, umntu ngamnye okanye ekuhlaleni kunye 
abanye, esidlangalaleni okanye bucala, ekufundiseni, ekunquleni kunye 
ukwenza amatheko enkolo kunye nezithethe.  
Inqaku le-19 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yoluvo lwakhe kunye nenkululeko 
ezibonakalisa; eli lungelo liquka inkululeko ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka 
ukubambelela kwiinkolelo kunye nenkululeko yokufuna, ukufumana kunye
ukusasaza ulwazi kunye neengcinga ngayo nayiphi na indlela kwaye kungakhathaliseki 
imida karhulumente. 
Inqaku lama-20 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yeendibano zoxolo kunye 
imibutho. 
2. Akukho mntu uya kunyanzelwa ukuba azibandakanye nawo nawuphi na umbutho. 
Inqaku lama-21  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthatha inxaxheba kulawulo lwakhe 
ilizwe ngokuthe ngqo okanye ngokunyulwa ngokukhululekileyo 
abameli. 
 

 
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo elilinganayo lokufikelela kuluntu 
inkonzo kwilizwe lakho. 
3. Intando yabantu kufuneka ibe sisiseko segunya likarhulumente; le ntando 
kufuneka ifumane inkcazo kwi periodic kunye 
unyulo lobuqhophololo ekufuneka lubanjwe phantsi kwalo 
inkululeko yehlabathi jikelele nelinganayo ngevoti eyimfihlo 
okanye ngezinye iifom ezifanayo ezibonelela 
inkululeko yokuvota. 
Inqaku lama-22 
Wonke umntu, njengelungu loluntu, unelungelo kwintlalontle 
ulungiselelo nokuphunyezwa okuyimfuneko ukuyigcina 
isidima kunye nophuhliso olukhululekileyo lwamalungelo akhe obuntu kwezoqoqosho, 
ezentlalo nenkcubeko ngeenzame zesizwe 
kunye nentsebenziswano yamazwe ngamazwe kwaye ngokuhambelana nesakhiwo kunye 
izixhobo zombuso ngamnye.  
Inqaku lama-23 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukukhetha umsebenzi ngokukhululekileyo, 
kwiimeko ezifanelekileyo nezilungileyo zomsebenzi kunye nokukhuselwa 
ukungaqeshwa. 
2. Wonke umntu, ngaphandle kwalo naluphi na ucalucalulo, unelungelo loku 
umvuzo olinganayo ngomsebenzi olinganayo.
3. Wonke umsebenzi unelungelo lobulungisa kunye 
Umvuzo owanelisayo obonelela ngokundilisekileyo 
ubukho bomntu yena nosapho lwakhe, kwaye 
kongezwa, ukuba kuyimfuneko, ngezinye iindlela zentlalo 
ubonelelo. 
4. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuyila iimanyano zabasebenzi kunye 
bajoyine iimanyano zabasebenzi ukukhusela umdla wabo. 
 

 
Inqaku lama-24 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphumla kunye nokuzonwabisa, kuquka nelungelo lokufumana ingqiqo 
ukulinganiselwa kweeyure zokusebenza kunye nekhefu elihlawulelwayo ngamaxesha athile.  
Inqaku lama-25 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lomgangatho wokuphila owaneleyo, kubandakanywa 
ukutya, impahla, indlu, unyango kunye nentlalo eyimfuneko 
iinkonzo eziyimfuneko ukugcina impilo kunye 
intlalontle yakhe nosapho lwakhe, kunye nelungelo lokhuseleko 
imeko yokungaphangeli, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, ubuhlolokazi, 
ubudala okanye enye imeko yokuphulukana nobomi ngenxa 
iimeko zixhomekeke kuye. 
2. Ukubeleka kunye nosana lunika ilungelo lononophelo olulodwa kunye 
uncedo. Bonke abantwana abazalelwe emtshatweni okanye ngaphandle komtshato kufuneka 
ukonwabela ukhuseleko loluntu olufanayo. 
Inqaku lama-26 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufumana imfundo. Imfundo kufuneka 
ukhululeke ubuncinci ukuya kuthi ga ekuqaleni kwaye 
imfundo jikelele. Imfundo yaseprayimari ifanele ukuba 
isinyanzelo. Imfundo yobuGcisa neyoMsebenzi 
ifanele ifikeleleke kumntu wonke, yaye imfundo ephakamileyo ifanele ifikeleleke
ifikeleleka ngokulinganayo kubo bonke ngokusekelwe kubuchule bomntu ngamnye. 
2. Imfundo kufuneka ijolise kuphuhliso olupheleleyo 
ubuntu boluntu kunye nokwandisa intlonipho kumalungelo oluntu 
kunye nenkululeko esisiseko. Imfundo kufuneka ikhuthaze 
ukuqondana, ukunyamezelana nobuhlobo phakathi kwabantu bonke, 
amaqela eentlanga neenkolo, kwaye kufuneka akhuthaze 
 

 
imisebenzi yeZizwe eziManyeneyo ekugcineni 
uxolo. 
3. Abazali banelungelo lokubeka phambili umbandela ekukhetheni uhlobo lwemfundo 
abantwana babo abancinci. 
Inqaku lama-27  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba ngokukhululekileyo kwinkcubeko 
ubomi boluntu, yonwabela ubugcisa, uthathe inxaxheba kwezenzululwazi 
inkqubela kwaye wonwabele iingenelo zayo. 
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa kwisimilo sakhe kunye 
imidla yezinto eziphathekayo evela kwinzululwazi, 
imisebenzi yoncwadi okanye yobugcisa engumbhali wayo 
yi. 
Inqaku lama-28 
Wonke umntu unelungelo locwangco lwentlalontle nolwamazwe ngamazwe, 
apho amalungelo kunye nenkululeko ecaciswe kwesi siBhengezo 
ziphunyezwe ngokupheleleyo.  
Inqaku lama-29 
1. Wonke umntu unoxanduva kuluntu akulo 
Kuphela uphuhliso lwamahhala nolupheleleyo lobuntu bakhe lunokwenzeka. 
2. Xa besebenzisa amalungelo abo nenkululeko, umntu ngamnye kufuneka 
zixhomekeke kuphela kwizithintelo ezinjalo njengoko zisekiwe 
umthetho kuphela ngenjongo yokuqinisekisa ulwamkelo olufanelekileyo kunye 
ukuhlonelwa kwamalungelo kunye nenkululeko zabanye kunye nokwaneliseka kokulungileyo 
iimfuno zokuziphatha, ucwangco loluntu jikelele
intlalontle kuluntu lwedemokhrasi. 
 

 
3. Ukusetyenziswa kwala malungelo kunye nenkululeko makungaze kwenzeke 
ziphikisana neenjongo kunye nemithetho-siseko yeZizwe eziManyeneyo 
Iintlanga. 
Inqaku lama-30  
Akukho nanye into kwesi siBhengezo eya kuthatyathwa njenge 
ukunika nawuphina urhulumente, iqela labantu okanye abantu 
ilungelo lokubandakanyeka kuwo nawuphi na umsebenzi okanye ukwenza izenzo, 
ezijolise ekutshabalaliseni amalungelo kunye nenkululeko echazwe koku 
Izibhengezo.
