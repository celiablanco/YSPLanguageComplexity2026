"Isibhengezo seHlabathi samaLungelo oLuntu"
  
(Yamkelwa yiNdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo yesi-3 ngoDisemba 10, 1948)
  
<Intshayelelo>
  
Ukuqonda isidima esizalwa naso kunye namalungelo alinganayo nangenakohluthwa kuwo onke amalungu oluntu;
ukuqondwa sisiseko senkululeko, ubulungisa noxolo ehlabathini,
 
Ukungawahoyi nokungawahloniphi amalungelo oluntu kukhokelele kwizenzo ezikhohlakeleyo ezinyhashe isazela somntu,
Ukufika kwehlabathi elingenalo uloyiko kunye nokuswela, kunye nenkululeko yenkolo kunye nenkolo, yeyona nto iphambili kubantu abaqhelekileyo.
Yabhengezwa njengomnqweno we
 
Hleze abantu babhenele kwimvukelo njengokugqibela ekuchaseni ubuzwilakhe nengcinezelo.
Ukuze kukhuselwe amalungelo abantu, kubalulekile ukukhusela amalungelo abantu ngolawulo lomthetho.
Kuba kubalulekile ukukhuthaza uphuhliso lobudlelwane bobuhlobo phakathi kwamazwe,
Abantu beZizwe eziManyeneyo bazibophelele kumalungelo oluntu asisiseko, isidima somntu nokuxabiseka, kunye noku
ukuqinisekisa kwakhona ukukholelwa kwethu kumalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabasetyhini, kunye nokonwabela inkululeko enkulu yentlalontle.
Sizimisele ukukhuthaza inkqubela kunye nokuphucula umgangatho wokuphila,
 
AMazwe angamaLungu, ngokusebenzisana neZizwe eziManyeneyo, aya kukhuthaza intlonipho yehlabathi jikelele kunye nokuthotyelwa kwamalungelo oluntu kunye neziseko zenkululeko.
Ndizibophelele ngokuphumelela ukunyuselwa,
  
Ukuqonda okufanayo kwala malungelo kunye neenkululeko kubalulekile ukugqibezela lo Mnqophiso.

Ke ngoko, iNdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo,
