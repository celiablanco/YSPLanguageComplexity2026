Isimemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu 
Yamukelwa futhi yamenyezelwa yi-General Assembly isinqumo 217 A (III) sangomhla ziyi-10 kuZibandlela 
1948. 
ISANDULELA 
Nakuba siqaphela isithunzi esingokwemvelo sabo bonke 
amalungu omndeni wesintu, kanye namalungelo awo alinganayo nangenakwephucwa 
isisekelo senkululeko, ubulungisa kanye nokuthula kwezwe; futhi  
Nakuba ukungahloniphi nokubukela phansi amalungelo abantu 
kuholele ezenzweni zobuqaba ezicasula unembeza wesintu, futhi 
ukuthi ukudalwa komhlaba lapho abantu beyoba nenkululeko yokukhuluma futhi 
izinkolelo futhi izokhululeka ekwesabeni nasekusweleni, kumenyezelwe njenge 
ukulangazelela okukhulu kwabantu; futhi 
Nakuba kubalulekile ukuthi amalungelo abantu avikelwe 
igunya lomthetho ukuqinisekisa ukuthi umuntu akaphoqwa 
resort, njengendlela yokugcina, ukuvukela ubushiqela kanye 
ukucindezelwa; futhi  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi kuyadingeka ukukhuthaza ukuthuthukiswa 
ubungane phakathi kwabantu; futhi  
Nakuba abantu beNhlangano Yezizwe beqinisekisile ku 
Shatha inkolelo yayo emalungelweni abantu ayisisekelo, isithunzi nokubaluleka 
ubuntu kanye nokulingana kwabesilisa nabesifazane futhi wanquma 
ukukhuthaza inqubekelaphambili yomphakathi kanye nokwenza ngcono izimo zokuphila ngaphansi 
inkululeko enkulu; futhi 
Nakuba Amazwe Angamalungu azibophezele ukuthi azosiza, ngo 
ukusebenzisana neNhlangano Yezizwe, emhlabeni jikelele 
ukuhlonishwa nokugcinwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo; Futhi
Nakuba kukhona ukuqonda okuvamile ngohlobo lwala malungelo kanye 
inkululeko ibaluleke kakhulu ukuze lokhu kusetshenziswe ngokugcwele 
izibopho, 
 

 
General Assembly,  
umemezela lesi Simemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu njenge 
umsebenzi lapho bonke abantu kanye 
sisho ukuthi wonke umuntu nazo zonke izinhlaka zomphakathi, 
Ukukhumbula lesi simemezelo ngaso sonke isikhathi, lwela ukuba 
imfundo nokuqeqeshwa ukukhuthaza ukuhlonishwa kwala malungelo nenkululeko kanye 
ukuqinisekisa, ngenqubekelaphambili kazwelonke neyamazwe ngamazwe 
imisebenzi, ukuqashelwa kanye nokusebenza ngempumelelo kwendawo yonke 
kokubili phakathi kwabantu bamazwe angamalungu eNhlangano kanye naphakathi kwabantu 
izindawo ezingaphansi kombuso wabo.  
I-athikili 1  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi belingana ngesithunzi nangamalungelo. 
Banikezwe ukucabanga nonembeza futhi kufanele benze ngokuphathelene nomunye nomunye. 
umngane ngomoya wobuzalwane.  
I-athikili 2 
Wonke umuntu kufanele abe nawo wonke amalungelo kanye nayo yonke inkululeko, 
emenyezelwe yilesi siMemezelo, ngaphandle kwanoma yisiphi 
umehluko onjengobuhlanga, umbala, ubulili, ulimi, inkolo, 
ezombusazwe noma ezinye izinkolelo, zezwe noma zezenhlalo 
imvelaphi, indawo, isigaba noma esinye isimo.  
Ngaphezu kwalokho, akukho mehluko okufanele wenziwe ngesisekelo 
isimo sezepolitiki, somthetho noma samazwe ngamazwe sezwe noma 
indawo umuntu ahlala kuyo, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi ukuyo
noma ngabe indawo izimele, ithembekile, ayizibuse noma 
ngenye indlela ilinganiselwe ebukhosini bayo. 
I-athikili yesi-3 
 

 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kanye nokuba yimfihlo 
ukungabi namthetho.  
I-athikili yesi-4 
Akekho okufanele agqilazwe noma agqilazwe; 
Ubugqila nokuhweba ngezigqila kwenqatshelwe ngazo zonke izinhlobo zako.  
I-athikili yesi-5 
Akekho okufanele ahlukunyezwe noma ahlukunyezwe, angabi nobuntu noma 
impatho eyehlisa isithunzi nesijeziso. 
I-athikili yesi-6 
Wonke umuntu, noma ngabe ukuphi, unelungelo lokuhlonishwa 
ubuntu bomthetho. 
I-athikili yesi-7 
Bonke abantu bayalingana phambi komthetho futhi banelungelo, ngaphandle kokuhlukaniswa, loku 
ukuvikelwa okulinganayo komthetho. Bonke abantu banelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo kunoma yikuphi 
noma yikuphi ukucwasa okwephula lesi Simemezelo, futhi kusukela 
noma yikuphi ukugqugquzela ukucwasa okunjalo.  
Isigaba 8 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubuyiselwa ngempumelelo kwamalungelo 
izinkantolo zikazwelonke ezinekhono emacaleni okwephulwa kwesisekelo sawo 
amalungelo anikezwe wona ngumthethosisekelo noma umthetho.  
I-athikili yesi-9 
 

 
Akekho ongaboshwa ngokungafanele, avalelwe noma 
ukudingiswa.  
Isigaba 10 
Umuntu ngamunye, ukunquma amalungelo nemisebenzi yakhe kanye noku 
eqinisekisa ubuqiniso bamacala obugebengu abekwe wona, 
unelungelo, ngesisekelo sokulingana okugcwele, lokuba necala lakhe 
kubuyekezwe esidlangalaleni nangokuhambisana nazo zonke izidingo zobulungisa 
inkantolo ezimele futhi engachemile.  
Isigaba 11
1. Wonke umuntu osolwa ngokwenza ubugebengu une 
ilungelo lokuthathwa njengomsulwa kuze kutholakale ukuthi unecala 
izosungulwa ngokusemthethweni ngokulalelwa komphakathi 
izinqubo lapho enikezwa wonke amathuba 
ukuze kuvikelwe. 
2. Akekho ongatholwa enecala ngesisekelo 
ukwenziwa kwanoma yisiphi isenzo noma ukungenzi lutho, okuthi 
isikhathi sokuthunywa kwabo asibanga ubugebengu ngokwezwe lonke 
imithetho noma umthetho wamazwe ngamazwe. Awukwazi futhi ukuphoqelelwa 
isijeziso esinzima ukwedlula leso ebesingasetshenziswa kuso 
isikhathi lapho kwenziwa ubugebengu. 
Isigaba 12 
Akekho ongaphazanyiswa ngokungemthetho ezindabeni zakhe 
impilo yomndeni, ukuhlaselwa ngokungenamthetho ubuqotho be 
ikhaya lakhe, imfihlo yezincwadi zakhe noma udumo nesithunzi sakhe. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngumthetho kulokho kugxambukela 
noma ukuhlaselwa okunjalo.  
 

 
Isigaba 13 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululeka nokukhetha 
indawo yakhe yokuhlala ngaphakathi kwesifunda ngasinye. 
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokushiya noma yiliphi izwe, kuhlanganise nelakhe 
badle, babuyele ezweni lakubo. 
Isigaba 14 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokucela ukukhoseliswa ekushushisweni 
kwamanye amazwe futhi basebenzise lesi siphephelo. 
2. Leli lungelo alinakusetshenziselwa ukushushiswa, ku 
iqiniso elisekelwe kukhomishini engezona ezombangazwe
ubugebengu noma ukwenza okuphambene nemigomo nezimiso 
IZizwe Ezihlangene. 
Isigaba 15 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba yisakhamuzi. 
2. Akekho ovunyelwe ukuphucwa ubuzwe bakhe noma 
ilungelo lokushintsha ubuzwe bakho. 
Isigaba 16 
1. Abesilisa nabesifazane asebefinyelele iminyaka eminingi banelungelo ngaphandle 
noma yimiphi imingcele esekelwe kuhlanga, ubuzwe noma inkolo 
shada uqale umndeni. Bayakujabulela 
amalungelo alinganayo mayelana nomshado, ngesikhathi 
izimo ngesikhathi somshado nangesikhathi sokuchithwa kwawo. 
2. Umshado ungaphothulwa kuphela ngokukhululeka nangemvume egcwele 
bobabili abasemshadweni. 
3. Umndeni uyingxenye yemvelo neyisisekelo yomphakathi futhi unayo 
ilungelo lokuvikelwa emphakathini kanye nombuso. 
 

 
Isigaba 17 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba nobunikazi bempahla njengomuntu ngamunye, 
kanye nabanye. 
2. Akekho okufanele aphucwe impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu. 
Isigaba 18  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga, unembeza nenkolo; lokhu 
ilungelo lihlanganisa inkululeko yokushintsha inkolo noma izinkolelo nenkululeko yomuntu 
ukusho inkolo noma izinkolelo zakho, kungaba ngabanye noma emphakathini 
abanye, esidlangalaleni noma ngasese, ekufundiseni, ekukhonzeni kanye 
ukwenza imikhosi yenkolo nemikhuba.  
Isigaba 19 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga kanjalo nelenkululeko 
ukuziveza; leli lungelo lihlanganisa inkululeko ngaphandle kokuphazanyiswa 
bambelela ekukholelweni komuntu kanye nenkululeko yokufuna, ukwamukela kanye
isabalalisa ulwazi nemibono nganoma iyiphi indlela futhi ngaphandle kokunaka 
imingcele yezwe. 
Isigaba 20 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana ngokuthula kanye 
izinhlangano. 
2. Akekho oyophoqwa ukuthi ajoyine noma iyiphi inhlangano. 
Isigaba 21  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ekuphathweni komsebenzi wakhe 
izwe ngokuqondile noma ngokukhethwa ngokukhululeka 
abameleli. 
 

 
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo elilinganayo lokufinyelela emphakathini 
isevisi ezweni lakini. 
3. Intando yabantu kumele ibe yisisekelo segunya likahulumeni; lokhu kuthanda 
kumele ithole ukuvezwa ngezikhathi ezithile kanye 
ukhetho lomgunyathi okufanele lubanjwe ngaphansi kwalo 
ukuvota okuyimfihlo nokulinganayo 
noma ngamanye amafomu alinganayo ahlinzekayo 
inkululeko yokuvota. 
Isigaba 22 
Wonke umuntu, njengelungu lomphakathi, unelungelo lokuhlalisana nabantu 
ukuhlinzekwa kanye nokuqaliswa okudingekayo ukuze kugcinwe 
isithunzi kanye nokuthuthukiswa kwamahhala kwamalungelo akhe obuntu kwezomnotho, 
izinkundla zokuxhumana namasiko ngemizamo kazwelonke 
kanye nokubambisana kwamazwe ngamazwe nangokuhambisana nesakhiwo kanye 
izinsiza zombuso ngamunye.  
Isigaba 23 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusebenza, lokuzikhethela ngenkululeko umsebenzi, 
ezimeni ezifanele nezivumayo zokusebenza kanye nokuvikelwa kuzo 
ukungasebenzi. 
2. Wonke umuntu, ngaphandle kokubandlulula, unelungelo loku 
iholo elilinganayo ngomsebenzi olinganayo.
3. Wonke umsebenzi unelungelo lokungenzeleli kanye 
iholo eligculisayo elihlinzeka ngenhlonipho 
ubukhona bomuntu yena nomndeni wakhe, kanye 
kulekelelwa, uma kunesidingo, ngezinye izindlela zokuxhumana 
ukuhlinzekwa. 
4. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusungula izinyunyana zabasebenzi kanye 
bajoyine izinyunyana ukuze bavikele izimfuno zabo. 
 

 
Isigaba 24 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula nokungcebeleka, okuhlanganisa nelungelo lokufaneleka 
umkhawulo wamahora okusebenza kanye nekhefu elikhokhelwayo lezikhathi ezithile.  
Isigaba 25 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lezinga lempilo elanele, okuhlanganisa 
ukudla, okokugqoka, indawo yokuhlala, ukunakekelwa kwezempilo kanye nenhlalo edingekayo 
izinsiza ezidingekayo ukuze kugcinwe impilo kanye 
inhlalakahle yakhe nomndeni wakhe, kanye nelungelo lokuvikeleka 
icala lokungasebenzi, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, ubufelokazi, 
ukuguga noma olunye udaba lokulahlekelwa yimpilo ngenxa 
izimo zincike kuye. 
2. Ukubeletha kanye nobungane kunikeza ilungelo lokunakekelwa okukhethekile kanye 
usizo. Zonke izingane ezizalwe ngaphakathi noma ngaphandle komshado kufanele 
ujabulele ukuvikelwa okufanayo komphakathi. 
Isigaba 26 
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo kufanele 
khululeka okungenani kuze kufike ekuqaleni futhi 
imfundo jikelele. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi kufanele ibe 
impoqo. Imfundo Yezobuchwepheshe Nemisebenzi Yezandla 
kufanele ifinyeleleke kubo bonke, futhi imfundo ephakeme kufanele ibe njalo
ifinyeleleka ngokulinganayo kubo bonke ngokusekelwe emandleni omuntu ngamunye. 
2. Imfundo kufanele iqondiswe ekuthuthukisweni okuphelele 
ubuntu kanye nokwenyusa inhlonipho yamalungelo abantu 
kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo. Imfundo kufanele ikhuthaze 
ukuqondana, ukubekezelelana nobungane phakathi kwabo bonke abantu, 
amaqembu ezinhlanga nezenkolo, futhi kufanele akhuthaze 
 

 
imisebenzi yeNhlangano yeziZwe ekugcineni 
ukuthula. 
3. Abazali banelungelo lokubeka phambili ekukhetheni uhlobo lwemfundo 
izingane zabo ezincane. 
Isigaba 27  
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ngokukhululeka kwezamasiko 
impilo yomphakathi, jabulela ubuciko, iqhaza kwezesayensi 
thuthuka futhi ujabulele izinzuzo zayo. 
2. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa kokuziphatha kwakhe kanye 
izithakazelo ezibonakalayo ezivela kusayensi, 
imisebenzi yemibhalo noma yobuciko engumbhali wayo 
kuyinto. 
Isigaba 28 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhleleka kwezenhlalo namazwe ngamazwe, 
lapho amalungelo nezinkululeko ezibalulwe kulesi siMemezelo zingaba khona 
zisetshenziswe ngokugcwele.  
Isigaba 29 
1. Wonke umuntu unezibopho emphakathini lapho 
Kuphela ukuthuthukiswa kwamahhala nokuphelele kobuntu bakhe kungenzeka. 
2. Lapho besebenzisa amalungelo nenkululeko yabo, umuntu ngamunye kufanele 
kube ngaphansi kwemikhawulo enjalo kuphela emisiwe 
umthetho ngenjongo yokuqinisekisa ukuqashelwa okufanele kanye 
ukuhlonishwa kwamalungelo nenkululeko yabanye kanye nokwaneliseka kokungenzeleli 
izidingo zokuziphatha, ukuhleleka komphakathi kanye nokujwayelekile
inhlalakahle emphakathini wentando yeningi. 
 

 
3. Ukusetshenziswa kwala malungelo kanye nenkululeko akumele neze kube njalo 
ziphikisana nezinjongo nemigomo yeNhlangano Yezizwe 
Izizwe. 
Isigaba 30  
Akukho lutho kulesi siMemezelo oluzohunyushwa ngokuthi 
ukuhlinzeka kunoma yisiphi isimo, iqembu labantu noma abantu ngabanye 
ilungelo lokuzibandakanya kunoma yimuphi umsebenzi noma ukwenza izenzo, 
okuhloswe ngayo ukucekela phansi amalungelo nenkululeko ebekwe kulokhu 
Izimemezelo.
