Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt 
Fɔs di wɔd dɛn 
We wi tink bɔt dat di rɛkɔgnishɔn fɔ di inhɛrɛnt dignity fɔ ɔl di pipul dɛn na di mɔtalman famili ɛn dɛn rayt dɛn 
ikwal ɛn nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ tek am na in na di fawndeshɔn fɔ fridɔm, jɔstis ɛn pis na di wɔl;  
We wi tink bɔt se di we aw pipul dɛn nɔ no natin ɛn we dɛn nɔ de tink bɔt mɔtalman rayt dɔn mek pipul dɛn de du bad bad tin dɛn we de mek pipul dɛn vɛks 
di kɔnshɛns fɔ Mɔtalman ɛn se di kam fɔ wan wɔl we mɔtalman fri fɔ tɔk ɛn biliv, . 
fri frɔm fred ɛn sɔfa, dɛn bin prɛd am as di ay inspɛkshɔn fɔ Mɔtalman;  
We wi tink bɔt se fɔ protɛkt mɔtalman rayt tru wan rijim fɔ lɔ impɔtant, so dat man 
nɔ fɔ fos, insay di suprɛm risɔt, fɔ tɔn agens di bad we aw pipul dɛn de rul ɛn we dɛn de mek dɛn sɔfa;  
We i rili impɔtant fɔ ɛnkɔrej di divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ padi biznɛs bitwin neshɔn dɛn;  
We wi tink bɔt dat, insay di Chata, di pipul dɛn na di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn de prɛd, wan tɛm bak, dɛn fet pan di men rayt dɛn we di 
Man, insay di rɛspɛkt ɛn valyu we mɔtalman pɔsin gɛt, insay di ikwal rayt we man ɛn uman gɛt ɛn if 
bin deklare dɛn disayd fɔ mek sɔshal prɔgrɛs ɛn fɔ mek bɛtɛ layf kɔndishɔn insay fridɔm 
we wayd pasmak;  
We wi tink bɔt se di mɛmba stet dɛn dɔn kɔmit fɔ protɛkt, insay di wok we dɛn de du wit di Ɔganayzeshɔn fɔ 
Yunaytɛd Neshɔn, fɔ rɛspɛkt mɔtalman rayt ɛn di men fridɔm dɛn we ɔlman gɛt ɛn we go wok fayn;
We wi tink bɔt se wan kɔmɔn kɔnsɛpshɔn bɔt dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya rili impɔtant fɔ ful-ɔp 
satisfay wit da kayn kɔmitmɛnt de:  
Di Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli de prɛd dis Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn  
fɔ Yuman Rayt  
as wan kɔmɔn ideal we ɔl pipul ɛn ɔl di neshɔn dɛn fɔ ajɔst, so dat ɔl di wan dɛn ɛn ɔl di ɔgan dɛn na di 
sosayti, we gɛt am na dɛn maynd ɔltɛm, de tray, tru tichin ɛn ɛdyukeshɔn, fɔ divɛlɔp rɛspɛkt 
fɔ dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya ɛn fɔ protɛkt, tru prɔgrɛsiv nashɔnal ɛn intanashɔnal mɛsej dɛn, dɛn 
yunivasal ɛn ifektiv rɛkɔgnishɔn ɛn aplikeshɔn ɔl tu bitwin di pipul dɛn na di mɛmba stet dɛnsɛf ɛn 
bitwin di wan dɛn we de na di teritɔri dɛn we dɛn put ɔnda in jɔrisdikshɔn.  
Atikul 1  
Dɛn bɔn ɔl mɔtalman fri ɛn ikwal pan rɛspɛkt ɛn rayt. We dɛn gi dɛn rizin ɛn kɔnshɛns, dɛn fɔ du dat 
akt to dɛnsɛf wit spirit fɔ bi brɔda ɛn sista.  
Atikul 2  

Ɔl mɔtalman kin yuz di rayt ɛn fridɔm we dɛn dɔn prɛd insay dis Diklɛreshɔn, we nɔ gɛt difrɛns 
ɛni, dat na di rays, kɔlɔ, sɛks, langwej, rilijɔn, pɔlitiks ɔ ɔda opinion, nashɔnal ɔrijin 
ɔ sɔshal, fɔchɔ, bɔn ɔ ɛni ɔda sityueshɔn. 
Dɔn bak, dɛn nɔ go mek ɛni difrɛns bay di pɔlitikal, ligal ɔ intanashɔnal stetmɛnt we di kɔntri gɛt ɔ 
teritɔri usay dɛn bɔn di pɔsin, ilɛksɛf na da kɔntri de ɔ indipɛndɛnt teritɔri, ɔnda gad, ɔtonamɛnt ɔ ɔnda sɔm
limiteshɔn fɔ di sovereignty.  
Atikul 3 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv, fridɔm ɛn in yon sikyɔriti.  
Atikul 4  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go bi slev ɔ slev; slev wok ɛn di we aw dɛn de trit slev dɛn, pan ɔl kayn we, na 
dɛn nɔ gri fɔ du dat.  
Atikul 5  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ mek dɛn sɔfa ɔ trit am bad, we nɔ gɛt wanwɔd ɔ we de mek i nɔ gɛt wan rɛspɛkt.  
Atikul 6 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ no, ɔlsay, fɔ dɛn ligal pɔsin.  
Atikul 7  
Ɔlman ikwal bifo di lɔ ɛn, if dɛn nɔ gɛt difrɛns, dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn frɔm di lɔ. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn 
agens ɛni diskrimineshɔn we nɔ gri wit dis Diklɛreshɔn ɛn agens ɛni diskrimineshɔn we de mek dɛn du da kayn diskrimineshɔn de.  
Atikul 8 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ yuz di kɔmpiten nashɔnal jɔrisdikshɔn dɛn fayn fayn wan agens akt dɛn we de agens di 
fawndeshɔnal rayt dɛn we di Kɔnstityushɔn ɔ lɔ gri wit.  
Atikul 9 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ arɛst, detɛn ɔ kɛr pɔsin go na ɔda kɔntri bay wilful.  
Atikul 10  

Ɔlman gɛt rayt, insay ful ikwal, fɔ mek kɔt jɔj in kes fayn ɛn na pɔblik 
indipɛndɛnt ɛn nɔ tek wan pɔsin bɛtɛ pas ɔda pɔsin fɔ disayd bɔt yu rayt ɛn ɔbligayshɔn ɔ di rizin dɛn fɔ ɛni akɔdin pan sɔntin 
penalty we dɛn chaj am.  
Atikul 11 
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi we dɛn aks fɔ kriminal akt, dɛn kin tek am se i nɔ du ɛnitin te dɛn pruv se i gilti.
we dɛn pruv bay lɔ we dɛn de du pɔblik prɔses we dɛn de gi ɔl di difens garanti dɛn we nid 
dɛn de mek shɔ se dɛn de du am.  
2. 2. . 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go kɔndɛm fɔ di tin dɛn we i du ɔ we i nɔ du we, di tɛm we dɛn du am, i nɔ bin bi akt 
kriminal ɔnda domestik ɔ intanashɔnal lɔ. Semweso, dɛn nɔ go pɔnish pɔsin we rili bad pas 
dat we bin de di tɛm we dɛn du di kriminal akt.  
Atikul 12  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go sɔfa fɔ ambɔg dɛn prayvet layf, in famili, in os ɔ dɛn 
kɔrɛspɔndɛns, ɔ atak in ɔnɔ ɛn gudnem. Agens dɛn kayn intafɛreshɔn ɔ atak ya ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ du am 
protɛkshɔn fɔ di lɔ.  
Atikul 13  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ muv fri wan ɛn pik usay i fɔ de insay wan Stet.  
2. 2. . 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ kɔmɔt na di kɔntri usay i de, ivin in yon, ɛn i gɛt rayt fɔ kam bak 
to yu kɔntri.  
Atikul 14  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi we dɛn de mek sɔfa gɛt rayt fɔ go fɛn ɛn bɛnifit frɔm asaylans na ɔda kɔntri.  
2. 2. . 
Bɔt dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ yuz dis rayt if dɛn rili gɛt kes fɔ kraym ɔnda di lɔ. 
ɔ fɔ tin dɛn we nɔ gri wit wetin di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn want ɛn prinsipul dɛn.  
Atikul 15  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt in kɔntri.  
2. 2. . 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ pul in kɔntri ɔ in rayt fɔ chenj in kɔntri bay wilful.  
Atikul 16  
1. 1. 1. .
Frɔm di ej we dɛn mared, man ɛn uman gɛt rayt fɔ mared ɛn fɛn famili, ɛn dɛn nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ stɔp dɛn. 
we kɔmɔt na difrɛn trayb, kɔntri ɔ rilijɔn. We di mared de ɛn di tɛm we dɛn de skata, dɛn ɔl tu gɛt rayt 
ikwal.  
2. 2. . 
Dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ sɛlibret mared if di wan dɛn we go mared nɔ gri fɔ du wetin dɛn want.  
3. 3. . 
Di famili na di natura ɛn di men tin we de na di sosayti ɛn i gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt am frɔm di sosayti ɛn di Stet.  

Atikul 17  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi, wan wan ɔ ɔlman, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt prɔpati.  
2. 2. . 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ go ebul fɔ tek in prɔpati bay wilful.  
Atikul 18 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tink, kɔnshɛns ɛn rilijɔn; Dis rayt de sho se pɔsin gɛt fridɔm fɔ 
chenj yu rilijɔn ɔ biliv, ɛn bak di fridɔm fɔ sho yu rilijɔn ɔ biliv, yu wan ɔ insay 
kɔmɔn, na pɔblik ɛn prayvet, tru tichin, praktis, wɔship ɛn rites.  
Atikul 19 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tink ɛn tɔk wetin i want, we min se i gɛt rayt fɔ mek i nɔ ambɔg am 
dɛn opinion ɛn fɔ fɛn, gɛt ɛn sheb, we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit bɔda, infɔmeshɔn ɛn aydia we ɛnibɔdi gɛt 
we fɔ tɔk bɔt sɔntin.  
Atikul 20° 
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ gɛda ɛn jɔyn an wit pis.  
2. 2. . 
Dɛn nɔ go fos ɛnibɔdi fɔ bi pat pan wan ɔganayzeshɔn.  
Atikul 21  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan di we aw i de du pɔblik biznɛs na in kɔntri, ilɛksɛf na dairekt, .
ɔ tru pipul dɛn we dɛn kin pik fɔ ripresent dɛn fri wan.  
2. 2. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt, ɔnda ikwal kɔndishɔn, di pɔblik wok dɛn we in kɔntri de du.  
3. 3. . 
Di pipul dɛn want na di fawndeshɔn fɔ di pawa we pɔblik pawa gɛt: ɛn dɛn fɔ sho am tru ilɛkshɔn 
ɔnɛs ilɛkshɔn we dɛn fɔ du wan wan tɛm bay we ɔlman gɛt ɛn ikwal rayt fɔ vot, wit sikrit vot ɔ sɛkɔn prɔses 
ikwal we de protɛkt di fridɔm fɔ vot.  
Atikul 22  
Ɛnibɔdi, as mɛmba na sosayti, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt soshal sikyɔriti; ɛn i kin aks fɔ di satisfayshɔn fɔ 
indispensable ikɔnomi, soshal ɛn kɔlchɔral rayt dɛm, tank to di nashɔnal ɛfɔt ɛn intanashɔnal kɔpɔreshɔn, ɔf 
wanwɔd wit di ɔganayzeshɔn ɛn di tin dɛn we ɛni kɔntri gɛt.  
Atikul 23 
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ wok, fɔ fri fɔ pik wok, fɔ wok we ikwal ɛn satisfay 
wok ɛn protɛkshɔn frɔm pipul dɛn we nɔ gɛt wok.  
2. 2. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt, witout ɛni diskrimineshɔn, fɔ gɛt ikwal pe fɔ ikwal wok.  

3. 3. . 
Ɛnibɔdi we de wok gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal ɛn satisfay pe, we de alaw dɛn ɛn dɛn famili a 
ɛgzistens in akɔdin to mɔtalman rɛspɛkt, ɛn sɔpɔt am, if i pɔsibul, wit ɔl ɔda we dɛn fɔ 
soshal protɛkshɔn.  
4. 4. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ mek yunion wit ɔda pipul dɛn ɛn fɔ jɔyn yunion fɔ difens 
fɔ di tin dɛn we yu lɛk.  
Atikul 24
Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ rɛst ɛn ɛnjɔy insɛf, mɔ fɔ mek i nɔ ebul fɔ du di wok we i fɔ du ɛn 
vaykeshun we dɛn kin pe fɔ ɔltɛm.  
Atikul 25 
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ liv in layf we go mek shɔ se insɛf ɛn in famili gɛt wɛlbɔdi ɛn wɛl-at
bi, mɔ pan tin dɛn we gɛt fɔ du wit it, klos, ples fɔ slip, ɛp fɔ mɛn pipul dɛn ɛn bak pan tin dɛn we gɛt fɔ du wit 
soshal savis dɛn we nid, ɛn i gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt sikyɔriti if i nɔ gɛt wok, i sik, i gɛt disabled, . 
uman we in man dɔn day, ol ej ɔ ɔda kes dɛn we pɔsin nɔ gɛt mɔni fɔ liv bikɔs ɔf tin dɛn we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit di 
yu wil.  
2. 2. . 
Materniti ɛn pikin dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt spɛshal ɛp ɛn ɛp. Ɔl pikin dɛn, we dɛn bɔn insay ɔ 
Apat frɔm mared, dɛn kin gɛt di sem soshal protɛkshɔn.  
Atikul 26  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ lan buk. Edukeshon fɔ fri, at least di wan we kɔrɛkt to tichin 
fondamental ɛlimɛntri. Ɛlimɛntri ɛdyukeshɔn na sɔntin we pɔsin fɔ du. Teknik ɛn pɔshɔnal ɛdyukeshɔn fɔ bi 
we de ɔlsay; Akses to ay stɔdi fɔ opin fɔ ɔlman insay ful ikwal, dipen pan di 
di gud tin we i gɛt.  
2. 2. . 
Edukeshon fɔ aim fɔ ful-ɔp mɔtalman pɔsin ɛn fɔ mek mɔtalman rayt strɔng ɛn 
fondamental fridɔm ɛn i fɔ mek ɔl di neshɔn dɛn ɔndastand, tolɛreshɔn ɛn padi biznɛs ɛn 
ɔl di rays ɔ rilijɔn grup dɛm, ɛn bak di divɛlɔpmɛnt fɔ di wok dɛm we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn de du fɔ di 
fɔ kip pis.  
3. 3. .
Mama ɛn papa dɛn gɛt di rayt fɔ pik di kayn edyukeshɔn we dɛn go gi dɛn pikin dɛn.  
Atikul 27  
1. 1. 1. . 
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat fri wan pan di kɔlchɔ layf na di kɔmyuniti, fɔ ɛnjɔy di art ɛn 
tek pat pan sayɛns prɔgrɛs ɛn di bɛnifit dɛn we de kɔmɔt frɔm am.  
2. 2. . 
Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt in gud abit ɛn tin dɛn we gɛt fɔ du wit ɛni sayɛns, buk we dɛn mek 
ɔ yu yon atis wok.  
Atikul 28  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ɔda we de rul, na di soshal ɛn intanashɔnal plen, we ebul fɔ du am ful wan 
di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de na dis Diklɛreshɔn de wok.  

Atikul 29  
1. 1. 1. . 
Di pɔsin gɛt wok fɔ du to di kɔmyuniti, we ausayd fri ɛn ful divɛlɔpmɛnt nɔ pɔsibul. 
fɔ yu pɔsin we yu bi.  
2. 2. . 
We dɛn de yuz dɛn rayt ya ɛn ɛnjɔy dɛn fridɔm ya, nɔbɔdi nɔ de ɔnda di lɔ pas di lɔ we se dɛn fɔ du am 
we lɔ dɔn mek wit di wangren aim fɔ mek pipul dɛn no ɛn rɛspɛkt di rayt dɛn ɛn 
fridɔm fɔ ɔda pipul dɛn ɛn fɔ mek dɛn ebul fɔ satisfay di jɔs dimand dɛn fɔ gud abit, pɔblik ɔda ɛn wɛlbɔdi 
insay wan sosayti we gɛt dimokrasi.  
3. 3. . 
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ yuz dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya we nɔ gri wit wetin di 
Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn.  
Atikul 30°  
Nɔtin nɔ de na dis Diklɛreshɔn we dɛn nɔ go tek as sɔntin we min fɔ ɛni Stet, .
grup ɔ pɔsin di rayt fɔ du sɔm tin ɔ fɔ du sɔm tin we dɛn want fɔ pwɛl di 
rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de ya.
