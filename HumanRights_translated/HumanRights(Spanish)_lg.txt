Olukiiko Olukulu  
Elangirira ekiwandiiko ky’ensi yonna ekikwata ku ddembe ly’obuntu ekiriwo kati ng’ekigendererwa 
ekigendererwa eky’awamu abantu bonna n’amawanga gonna kye balina okufuba, olwo 
abantu ssekinnoomu n’ebitongole, buli kiseera nga bafuna okubudaabudibwa okuva mu kyo, . 
okutumbula, nga bayita mu kusomesa n’okusomesa, okussa ekitiibwa mu ddembe lino 
n’eddembe, n’okukakasa, okuyita mu bipimo ebigenda mu maaso eby’eggwanga n’ 
amateeka g’ensi yonna, okumanyibwa n’okukozesebwa kwago mu bantu bonna era mu ngeri ennungi, byombi mu 
abantu b’amawanga agali mu mukago awamu n’abo ab’ebitundu ebiteekeddwawo 
wansi w’obuyinza bwayo.

buli omu ne munne.

embeera endala yonna.

Akawaayiro 5

Akawaayiro 6  
Buli muntu alina eddembe, buli wamu, okusiimibwa ku...

Akawaayiro 7  
Bonna benkanankana mu maaso g’amateeka era balina, awatali njawulo, eddembe ly’obukuumi obwenkanankana 
wa mateeka. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuumibwa kyenkanyi ku kusosola kwonna 
emenya Ekirangiriro kino era n’ewakanya okunyiiza kwonna okusosola ng’okwo.  
Akawaayiro 8  
Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna eddagala erikola obulungi mu kkooti z’eggwanga. 
ab’obuyinza, okubakuuma okuva ku bikolwa ebityoboola eddembe lyabwe 
eddembe ery’omusingi erikkirizibwa ssemateeka oba mu mateeka.  
Akawaayiro 9  
Tewali muntu yenna ayinza kusibibwa mu ngeri ya kimpowooze, okusibwa oba okuwang’angusibwa.  
Akawaayiro 10  
Buli muntu alina eddembe, mu mbeera ey’okwenkanankana mu bujjuvu, okuwulirwa
mu lujjudde era mu bwenkanya kkooti eyetongodde era etaliimu kyekubiira, kubanga 
 

 
okusalawo ku ddembe lyabwe n’obuvunaanyizibwa bwabwe oba okwekenneenya ekimu kyonna 
okumulumiriza mu nsonga z’obumenyi bw’amateeka.  
Akawaayiro 11  
1. Buli muntu avunaanibwa omusango alina eddembe okuteeberezebwa nti omuntu ye 
obutaliiko musango okutuusa ng’akakasiddwa omusango, okusinziira ku mateeka ne mu 
okuwozesebwa mu lujjudde nga mu kino emisingo gyonna gikakasiddwa 
ekyetaagisa okusobola okugikuuma.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuvunaanibwa olw’ebikolwa oba okulekayo nti mu kiseera kino 
abaakoze tebaali bamenyi b’amateeka mu mateeka g’eggwanga oba ag’ensi yonna. 
Era ekibonerezo eky’amaanyi tekijja kuweebwa okusinga ekyo ekyaliwo mu kiseera kya... 
okukola omusango.  
Akawaayiro 12  
Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuyingizibwa mu bulamu bwe obw’ekyama, mu maka ge, mu... 
awaka oba ebbaluwa ze, oba okulumba ekitiibwa kye oba erinnya lye. 
Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuumibwa amateeka okuva ku kuyingirira okwo oba 
okulumba.  
Akawaayiro 13  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutambula mu ddembe n’okulonda ekifo w’abeera 
mu kitundu ky’Eggwanga.  
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okuva mu nsi yonna, omuli n’eyaye, era 
okuddayo mu nsi ye.  
Akawaayiro 14  
1. Mu mbeera y’okuyigganyizibwa, buli muntu alina eddembe okunoonya obubudamu, n’oku... 
Nyumirwa, mu nsi yonna.  
 

 
2. Eddembe lino teriyinza kukozesebwa ku musango ogw’ekiramuzi ogwa nnamaddala 
esibuka ku bikolobero ebya bulijjo oba ebikolwa ebikontana n’ebigendererwa era
emisingi gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte.  
Akawaayiro 15  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna eggwanga.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako ggwanga lye oba eddembe lye 
okukyusa eggwanga.  
Akawaayiro 16  
1. Abasajja n’abakazi, okuva ku myaka egy’okufumbirwa, balina eddembe, awatali 
okuziyiza kwonna olw’eggwanga, eggwanga oba eddiini, okufumbirwa 
n’afuna amaka; era bajja kufuna eddembe eryenkanankana ku bikwata ku... 
obufumbo, mu kiseera ky’obufumbo ne mu mbeera y’okusasika kw’obufumbo.  
2. Okuyita mu kukkiriza okw’eddembe era okujjuvu okw’abafumbo ab’omu maaso bokka basobola 
okufumbirwa.  
3. Amaka kye kintu eky’obutonde era ekikulu mu bantu era galina 
eddembe ly’okukuuma abantu n’Eggwanga.  
Akawaayiro 17  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera n’ebintu, ssekinnoomu ne mu kibiina.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako bintu bye mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira.

Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okulowooza, omuntu ow’omunda n’...

okugatta awamu, mu lujjudde ne mu bwannannyini, nga basomesa, nga bakola, .

1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwetaba mu gavumenti y’eggwanga lye, .

akalulu.  
Akawaayiro 22  
Buli muntu, ng’omuntu mu kibiina, alina eddembe okufuna obukuumi 
mu mbeera z’abantu, n’okufuna, okuyita mu kaweefube w’eggwanga n’enkolagana y’ensi yonna, . 
nga otunuulidde enteekateeka n’eby’obugagga bya buli Ggwanga, okumatizibwa 
wa ddembe ly’ebyenfuna, ery’embeera z’abantu n’ery’obuwangwa, eryetaagisa eri ekitiibwa kyabwe 
n’okukulaakulanya omuntu waabwe mu ddembe.

1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukola, okulonda emirimu mu ddembe, oku...

kyetaagisa, mu ngeri endala yonna ey’obukuumi bw’abantu.  
4. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutandikawo ebibiina n’okwegatta ku bibiina olw’...

1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna omutindo gw’obulamu ogumala ogukakasa, .

2. Obumaama n’obuto bulina eddembe okulabirira n’okuyambibwa

Akawaayiro 26  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okusomesebwa. Okusoma kulina okuba... 
ya bwereere, waakiri ku bikwata ku by’enjigiriza ebya pulayimale ne 
omusingi. Okusoma kwa pulayimale kujja kuba kwa kiragiro. Ekiragiro
eby’ekikugu n’eby’ekikugu bijja kuba birina okuba nga bya bulijjo; okufuna emisomo 
abakulu bajja kuba be bamu eri bonna, okusinziira ku bulungibwansi bwabwe.  
2. Okusomesa kujja kugenderera okukulaakulanya omuntu mu bujjuvu 
eddembe ly’obuntu n’okunyweza okussa ekitiibwa mu ddembe ly’obuntu n’... 
eddembe ery’omusingi; kijja kutumbula okutegeeragana, okugumiikiriza n’... 
omukwano wakati w’amawanga gonna n’amawanga gonna oba eddiini; era 
okutumbula enkulaakulana y’emirimu gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte olw’... 
okukuuma emirembe.  
3. Abazadde bajja kuba n’eddembe ery’enkizo okulonda ekika ky’obuyigirize 
ekyo ekigenda okuweebwa abaana baabwe.  
Akawaayiro 27  
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwetaba mu bulamu bw’obuwangwa mu ddembe 
wa kitundu, okunyumirwa eby’emikono n’okwetaba mu nkulaakulana 
bya ssaayansi n’emigaso egivaamu.  
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuumibwa empisa n’... 
ebintu ebikwatagana nayo olw’ebintu ebyakolebwa mu bya ssaayansi, . 
ebiwandiiko oba eby’ekikugu nga ye muwandiisi waabyo.  
Akawaayiro 28  
Buli muntu alina eddembe okuteekawo enkola y’embeera z’abantu n’ensi yonna 
mu kino eddembe n’eddembe ebirangiriddwa mu Kirangiriro kino bikolebwa 
ekola mu bujjuvu.  
Akawaayiro 29  
 

 
1. Buli muntu alina emirimu gy’assa ekitiibwa mu kitundu, okuva bwe kiri nti mu...

2. Mu kukozesa eddembe lyabwe ne mu kunyumirwa eddembe lyabwe, bonna

n’ekigendererwa kyokka eky’okulaba ng’aba...

3. Eddembe n’eddembe lino mu mbeera yonna tebiyinza kukozesebwa mu 
okuwakanya ebigendererwa n’emisingi gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte. 
Akawaayiro 30  
Tewali kintu kyonna mu Kirangiriro kino kiyinza kutaputibwa nga kitegeeza ekyo 
egaba eddembe lyonna eri Gavumenti, ekibiina oba omuntu, okweyama 
n’okukulaakulanya emirimu oba okukola ebikolwa ebigendereddwamu okunyigiriza ekimu kyonna 
wa ddembe n’eddembe ebirangiriddwa mu Kirangiriro kino.
