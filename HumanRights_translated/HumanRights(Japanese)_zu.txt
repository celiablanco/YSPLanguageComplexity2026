"Isimemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu"
  
(Yamukelwe yi-3rd United Nations General Assembly ngoDisemba 10, 1948)
  
<Isethulo>
  
Ukuqaphela isithunzi esizalwa naso kanye namalungelo alinganayo nangenakuphikwa awo wonke amalungu omphakathi wesintu;
ukuqashelwa kuyisisekelo senkululeko, ubulungisa nokuthula emhlabeni,
 
Ukunganaki nokungawahloniphi amalungelo abantu sekuholele ezenzweni ezinobulwane ezinyathele unembeza wesintu,
Ukufika kwezwe elingenalo ukwesaba nokuswela, elinenkululeko yenkolo nenkolo, kuyinto ebaluleke kakhulu kubantu abavamile.
Kwamenyezelwa njengesifiso se
 
funa abantu baphendukele ekuhlubukeni njengesinyathelo sokugcina sokulwa nobudlova nengcindezelo.
Ukuze kuvikelwe amalungelo abantu, kubalulekile ukuvikela amalungelo abantu ngokusebenzisa umthetho.
Njengoba kubalulekile ukukhuthaza ukuthuthukiswa kobudlelwano bobungani phakathi kwamazwe,
Abantu beZizwe Ezihlangene bazibophezele kumalungelo abantu ayisisekelo, isithunzi nokufaneleka komuntu, kanye noku
ukuqinisa kabusha inkolelo yethu emalungelweni alinganayo amadoda nabesifazane, kanye nokujabulela inkululeko eyengeziwe yomphakathi.
Sizimisele ukukhuthaza inqubekelaphambili nezinga lokuphila elithuthukisiwe,
 
Amazwe angamalungu, ngokubambisana neNhlangano yeziZwe, azothuthukisa ukuhlonishwa komhlaba wonke kanye nokugcinwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo.
Njengoba ngizibophezele ekuzuzeni ukukhushulwa,
  
Ukuqonda okufanayo kwala malungelo kanye nenkululeko kubalulekile ukuze kuqedwe lesi Sivumelwano.
Zombili zibalulekile, ngakho
NGAKHO-KE, i-United Nations General Assembly,
 
Umuntu ngamunye kanye nesikhungo ngasinye emphakathini kumele sikhumbule lesi Simemezelo Sendawo Yonke Samalungelo Abantu, futhi
 

 
Kokubili phakathi kwabantu bakubo kanye naphakathi kwabantu bezindawo ezingaphansi kombuso Wamazwe Angamalungu.
Ukukhuthaza ukuhlonishwa kwamalungelo nenkululeko ngokusebenzisa isiqondiso nemfundo, kanye nokuqinisekisa ukuthi zitholakala emhlabeni wonke.
ukuthi bonke abantu kanye nezizwe zonke kufanele balwele ukuqinisekisa ngo
Ngalokhu simemezela lesi Simemezelo Samalungelo Abantu njengezinga elifanayo okufanele lifinyelelwe.   
 
  
I-athikili 1
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi banelungelo lesithunzi namalungelo.
ukulingana ngokoMthetho Abantu banikezwe ukucabanga nonembeza, futhi bayafana.
Kufanele senze ngomoya wokuhlonipha.  
 
I-athikili 2
Bonke abantu bakhululekile kunoma yiluphi uhlanga, ibala, ubulili, ulimi, inkolo, ipolitiki noma omunye umbono,
 
imvelaphi yobuzwe noma yenhlalo, impahla, uzalo lomndeni, noma esinye isimo noma izici ezifanayo;
 
wonke amalungelo aqukethwe kulesi Simemezelo, ngaphandle kokubandlululwa kwanoma yiluphi uhlobo;
 
bangajabulela izinzuzo nenkululeko. 
Ngaphezu kwalokho, uma izwe noma indawo umuntu ahlala kuyo iyizwe elizimele, indawo ye-trust.
 
Uma ikhona, iyindawo engabuseki yona noma ingaphansi komunye umkhawulo wobukhosi.

Akufanele kube nokucwasa kwanoma yiluphi uhlobo.

Ngeke ujeziswe.

1. Wonke umuntu kufanele akwazi ukusebenza, azikhethele ngokukhululekile umsebenzi wakhe, futhi ajabulele izimo zokusebenza ezinobulungiswa nezivumayo.
banelungelo lokuthola umsebenzi nokuvikelwa ekusweleni umsebenzi.

banelungelo lokujoyina.

Isigaba 26
1. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo okungenani ingeyaphansi
Izigaba eziyisisekelo neziyisisekelo kumele zibe mahhala. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi
Kumele kube yimpoqo. Imfundo yezobuchwepheshe neyemisebenzi yezandla ngokuvamile iyatholakala
Imfundo ephakeme kufanele ifinyelelwe yibo bonke ngokwamakhono.
Kumele ivuleke futhi ivuleke. 
2. Imfundo kufanele ifundise isihloko sokukhula kobuntu ngokugcwele nokuhlonishwa kwamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo.
Kumele kube inhloso. Imfundo itholakala kubo bonke ubuzwe noma uhlanga noma inkolo
Ukuthuthukisa ukuqondana, ukubekezelelana nobungane phakathi kwamaqembu, nokugcina ukuthula.
Ngakho-ke, kufanele ikhuthaze imisebenzi yeNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene. 
     
3. Abazali banelungelo elikhethekile lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo enikezwa izingane zabo.   
Isigaba 27
1. Bonke abantu bakhululekile ukubamba iqhaza empilweni yamasiko omphakathi, bathokozele ezobuciko, kanye
banelungelo lokuhlanganyela ekuthuthukisweni kwesayensi nasezinzuzweni zayo.      
2. Bonke abantu bavela emisebenzini yesayensi, yemibhalo noma yobuciko abayidalayo.

kanye nelungelo lokuhleleka kwamanye amazwe.   
Isigaba 29
1. Wonke umuntu angaba khona kuphela ekukhuleni okukhululekile nokugcwele kobuntu bakhe.
Sinomsebenzi ongenzeka emphakathini.
2. Ekusebenziseni amalungelo nenkululeko yakhe, wonke umuntu kufanele ahloniphe amalungelo abanye.
ukuqinisekisa ukuqashelwa okufanele nokuhlonipha izintshisekelo nezizathu, kanye nasemphakathini wentando yeningi.
ngenhloso eyodwa yokuhlangabezana nezidingo ezisemthethweni zokuziphatha, ukuhleleka komphakathi kanye nenhlalakahle jikelele.
ngaphansi kwemikhawulo esungulwe ngumthetho kuphela.  
3. Lawa malungelo kanye nenkululeko ngeke nakancane kungqubuzane nezinjongo nezimiso zeNhlangano yeziZwe.
Akumele isetshenziswe ngokwephula imithetho.   
Isigaba 30
Akukho okukulesi Simemezelo okusebenza kunoma yiliphi izwe, iqembu noma umuntu.
ukuzibandakanya noma ukuzibandakanya emisebenzini ehloselwe ukucekela phansi amalungelo nenkululeko ebhalwe kulesi Simemezelo;
ngeke kuthathwe njengokunikeza noma yiliphi ilungelo lokwenza noma yisiphi isenzo ngalezo zinhloso.
