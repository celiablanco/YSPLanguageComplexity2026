Ma Dekemaba 10, 1948, ka United Nations 
ua ʻākoakoa lākou a ʻae a hōʻike i nā kānaka 
kuleana, ʻo ia wale nō ka mea i ʻōlelo ʻia ma kēia puke.  
Ua kūkākūkā ka ʻaha kūkā i nā pono o nā kānaka a pau 
e hoʻolauna ʻia, aʻo ʻia, a heluhelu ʻia ma kēlā me kēia ʻāina, 
pono e hoʻolaha ʻia ma nā ʻano kula hoʻonaʻauao āpau 
a me nā ʻokoʻa ma waena o kēia mau lāhui.  
 
HOOLAHA HUI  
KUMU O NA KANAKA A PAU 
Ka hoomaka ana o ka  
NĀ MEA: Ke kūʻokoʻa a me ka maluhia ma ka honua 
ua paʻa ke ola ma kēia mau mea: ʻO kēlā me kēia kanaka 
maikaʻi ʻaʻole e hoʻonele i kekahi mai ka hānau ʻana o ko mākou makuahine iā mākou 
pono mākou e mālama ʻia a mālama like ʻia.  
LOOK: Ua loaʻa i nā kānaka nā ʻano mea ʻino a pau, 
ʻoiai inā ʻaʻole lākou i ʻike piha a ʻike paha i nā pono kanaka 
me ka nānā ʻole iā lākou, makemake mākou a pau i ka wā e hiki mai ana 
nā lā o ka maikaʻi; i na makau a me na ehaeha 
ke hoʻopau ʻia ka ʻilihune, loaʻa i nā kānaka a pau ke kūʻokoʻa e ʻōlelo 
ka mea i loko o ko mākou mau poʻo, a e loaʻa iā mākou ka ʻike me ka wiwo ʻole 
a me kā mākou makemake.  
KA MANAʻO: Pono e loaʻa kahi mea hoʻomalu pono kanaka 
rula kaulike ole 
kōkua i ka poʻe i hoʻoluhi ʻia a hoʻohana ʻia e makemake e hoʻopono i ko lākou pono.
KA MANAʻO: No nā lāhui o ka honua ke kūkulu i nā hoaaloha 
pono mākou e makemake i loko o ko mākou naʻau a me kēlā makemake 
pono kakou e hoopuehu.  
MANAʻO: Ua ʻōlelo ʻia nā lāhui o ka United Nations ma kēia puke 
ua hōʻoia i ko lākou manaʻoʻiʻo i ka pono o ka Iliw a 
ua ʻae like nā kānaka me ka nānā ʻole i ke kāne 
e hoʻomaopopo he kuleana like kona. Maikaʻi kēia mau mea a pau 
ʻike ʻoi aku ka nani o ka hoʻoikaika ʻana o ko mākou kūʻokoʻa 
e hana kākou e hoʻonui i ko kākou ʻāina.  
KA MANAʻO: Ua ʻae nā lāhui o ka United Nations 
pono kanaka a me ke kuokoa o ke ao holookoa 
e loaa mai i ka  
NOTE NYA: Ua ʻike maikaʻi ʻia nā pono kanaka a me nā kuokoa 
ina he oiaio.  
IKEIA KA PONO KANAKA MA KA AHAHUI LAHUI 
OIA IA  
Aia i loko o kēia puke nā lāhui i komo i ke kuʻikahi nui 
e ʻike i nā pono kanaka āpau, no laila nā kānaka 
ma na wahi a pau a lakou e noho ai, hiki ia lakou ke ike i keia qeliqa me ka mahalo a me ke kuokoa 
e aʻo e ola; i nā mea ʻē aʻe e like me kā lākou hiki 
e hoike mai lakou he oiaio keia mau mea a pau; e hana ʻia kēia mau mea 
e noho ana ma nā ʻāina o ka hui a i ʻole kēia 
ka malu o na aupuni.  
MUA (1) 
Ua hānau kūʻokoʻa nā kānaka a pau a loaʻa iā lākou nā pono āpau 
ua like mākou. No ka naʻauao, ua like mākou me ka ʻohana maluhia 
aie ke ola i kela mea keia mea.  
KELUA (2)
No kākou a pau nā loina kuleana i loko o kēia palapala, 
ʻaʻole pili i ka lāhui a me ke kāne 
ʻAʻole pili i kāna ʻōlelo a i ke akua paha āna i manaʻoʻiʻo ai 
ʻaʻole hoʻi mai ke ʻano o ke aupuni āna i manaʻo ai 
he ʻilihune paha, he waiwai paha, a he ʻano mea paha.  
He ʻokoʻa nā lāhui a pau, manuahi a ʻaʻole paha 
He aupuni ko kakou, a ke hilinai nei kekahi ma na aupuni e. 
akā, ʻaʻole pono mākou e hana ʻokoʻa.  
ʻĀpana Ekolu (3) 
Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke ola a me ke kuokoa 
a e hoomaluia.  
ʻEhā (4) 
ʻAʻole hiki i kekahi ke lilo i kauā hao wale, ua pāpā ʻia ma nā ʻano a pau 
ka hookauwa a me ke kalepa kanaka.  
ELIMA (5) 
ʻAʻole hiki ke loaʻa i kekahi ʻano ʻeha 
ʻAʻole hiki iā mākou ke hoʻopaʻi ʻia no ka hōʻino ʻana i ko mākou ʻano kanaka.  
EONO (6) 
Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka ho'okolokolo 'ana ma nā wahi a pau 
e hooiaioia.  
HIKU (7) 
He kuleana like ko na kanaka a pau malalo o ke kanawai 
ʻo ia, pono ke kānāwai e hoʻomalu iā mākou me ka ʻole. 
He kuleana ko mākou e kūʻē iā mākou no ka mālama ʻole ʻana i kēia mau aʻo 
 

 
o ka poe makemake e ku iluna. Loaʻa iā mākou kēia mau aʻo ʻana 
a e hoomaluia e ke kanawai ke hana hewa koke.  
ʻEhā (8)
Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka ho'olohe pono 
pale aku i ko kakou mau pono kanawai 
pono mākou e pale ʻia mai ka poʻe hana.  
MOKUNA EWA (9) 
ʻAʻohe mea i paʻa me ka ʻike ʻole i kona hewa, 
paahao a pio paha.  
UMI (10) 
Ua like na kanaka a pau me ka hooponopono kaulike ole 
he kuleana ko mākou e hoʻolohe ʻia a he kuleana hoʻi i ka hoʻokolokolo ʻana 
ko makou kuleana a kuleana paha e ike. ʻO kāna mea i hoʻopiʻi ʻia ai 
pono e hoike maopopo ia mai ia kakou, i hiki ia kakou ke ao ina ka mamao.  
ʻUmikūmākahi (11) 
1. ʻAʻole hewa nā kānaka liʻiliʻi i nā hewa i hoʻopiʻi ʻia iā lākou 
he kuleana ko mākou e hōʻoiaʻiʻo iā mākou iho, e like me ke kānāwai 
a hiki i ka wa e hookolokoloia ai makou. Aia nō ka hoʻokolokolo 
ua paʻa a pale ʻia ko mākou mau pono 
e lilo i poe.  
2. ʻAʻole hoʻopaʻi i ke kanaka no kekahi mea āna i hana ai 
ka hoʻopaʻi inā hana i nā lā ma mua o ke kānāwai 
i kona hele ana iwaho a hoike mai he hewa ia. ʻO ka hoʻopaʻi ka 
ma na hoopai wale no o ke kanawai i na la o kona hewa.  
 

 
UMIKUMAMALUA (12) 
ʻAʻole hiki i kekahi ke hoʻopilikia i ko mākou ola me ke kumu ʻole 
a o ko kakou mau ohana, aole hiki ia ia ke komo makehewa 
i ko makou mau home, aole e lawe wale ia ka makou mau palapala. 
Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka palekana o ke kānāwai mai ia 'ano keakea.  
ʻumikūmākolu (13)
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke kū'oko'a o ka ne'e 'ana. 
he kuleana ko mākou i ka noho ʻana ma ko mākou ʻāina.  
2. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka lāhui a i 'ole 
e haʻalele i kekahi ʻāina a like 
e hoʻi i ko mākou ʻāina.  
ʻumikūmāhā (14) 
1. Ina e hoomaauia ke kanaka e like me ka hana ole ana o ke aupuni o kona aina 
no ka mea, manaʻo ʻo ia, a laila pono ʻo ia i ka neʻe ʻana 
e hee a e puuhonua.  
2. Hoʻopiʻi ʻia no ka hewa maoli ma kona ʻāina 
ʻAʻole hiki i ke kanaka ke hilinaʻi i nā pono kanaka ʻē aʻe 
e holo i kahi ʻāina a noho i laila me ke kūʻokoʻa.  
UMUMAMALIMA (15) 
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka lāhui 
no kakou.  
2. ʻAʻole hiki ke hoʻonele wale ʻia kēia kuleana. 
He kuleana ko mākou e hōʻole i ka hoʻololi ʻana i ko mākou lāhui 
kekahi e hooki iā mākou.  
ʻumikūmāono (16) 
 

 
1. I ka hiki ana o ke kane a me ka wahine i na makahiki o ka mare ana, aia laua malaila 
ko makou pono e mare a loaa ka ohana, aole 
ʻaʻole pili i ka lāhui a i ʻole ka lāhui, inā he kāne a he wahine paha 
He pono like ko ka wahine ma ko maua mare ana 
e ola a e kaʻana like.  
2. ʻAʻole hiki i ke kāne a me ka wahine ke mare me ko lāua ʻae ʻole.  
3. ʻO ka makua ka kuleana o ka ʻohana holoʻokoʻa i ka palekana o ka mokuʻāina 
he kuleana ko mākou, no ka mea, he ʻohana nui mākou e hoʻomohala i ka lāhui 
ʻo mākou ka mea.
ʻumikūmāhiku (17) 
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka noho ho'okahi a i 'ole 'elua 
i waiwai me kanaka.  
2. ʻAʻole e hoʻonele wale ʻia ka waiwai o kekahi.  
UMUMAMAWALU (18) 
Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke kū'oko'a o ka mana'o, 
ka lunamanao a me ke akua hea 
a i mea e manaoio ai kakou. Ma muli o kēia kuleana, ua kūʻokoʻa mākou 
e ae i ka hoomana a e manaoio ma na ano a pau 
e hana i ka hana. Hiki iā mākou ke hana hoʻokahi a ma kahi hui 
kēlā manaʻoʻiʻo a hiki ke aʻo ʻia ma ka home a i ʻole 
ma na wahi a pau.  
UMIKUMAMAIWA (19) 
He kuleana ko kēlā me kēia kanaka e hōʻike i ko lākou manaʻo 
he kuleana ko makou i keia mau mea me ke kaohi ole 
hiki iā mākou ke kaʻana like i ko mākou mau manaʻo a ʻae i ka manaʻo o nā poʻe ʻē aʻe.  
IWAKALUA (20) 
 

 
1. He kuleana ko mākou i ke kūʻokoʻa o ka ʻaha kanaka a me ka hui 
a e kukulu i ka maluhia.  
2. ʻAʻole e koi ʻia kekahi e hele i kekahi hālāwai 
e komo me ka makemake.  
IWAKALUA KUMAMAKAHI (21) 
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke aupuni o kona ʻāina 
e lawelawe iā mākou iho, a pilikino paha 
ʻoiai inā koho mākou i kā mākou makemake.  
2. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke komo like i ka lawelawe aupuni ma kona ʻāina 
e komo i nā hale a mālama.  
3. ʻO ka mana o ke aupuni ʻo ia ka makemake o nā kānaka a pau
ke hāʻawi koke mākou i ka mana, ʻae mākou i kēlā makemake 
ma ke koho wale ana; pono mākou e hana i kēlā koho i kekahi 
ʻike i hiki iā mākou ke koho i kā mākou makemake.  
IWAKALUA-KUMAMALUA (22) 
Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka ho'ona'auao, ka waiwai a me ka 
e hoomaluia ma ko kakou mau aoao a pau i maikai na mea a pau 
e kiʻi iā mākou i ʻaneʻi. Loaʻa iā mākou kēlā hōʻoia e: 
Pono ke aupuni e hoʻonohonoho a hana i nā mea e hiki ai iā mākou 
me na kanaka o ko kakou aina, pono ia e hana no ko kakou pono e like me kona ikaika a 
a me na kokua haole.  
IWAKALUA KUMUMAKALU (23) 
1. Ua noa i na kanaka ke kuokoa o ka hoomana. 
loaʻa iā mākou ke kuleana e hana inā ʻaʻole mākou hana 
e imi a loaa, ke makemake ole kekahi 
hiki iā mākou ke hoʻokuʻu ʻia mai kā mākou mau hana.  
 

 
2. Ua noa i ke kanaka ke kuokoa o ka olelo 
e uku ʻia e like me kā mākou hana.  
3. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke kū'oko'a o ka ne'e 'ana a me ka noho 'ana ma loko o nā palena o kēlā me kēia Moku'āina 
i loaa ia makou ka uku e like me na hana maikai 
ka maikaʻi o ka ʻohana holoʻokoʻa; i ole ia, e loaa ke kokua 
o nā kākoʻo i hāpai ʻia no ia kumu.  
4. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke kū'oko'a o ka hui 'ana me nā kānaka 'ē a'e 
ko mākou kuleana e hoʻokumu a komo paha i ia mau hui 
e vechiku ia makou iho.  
IWAKALUA-KUMAMAHA (24) 
He kuleana ko kēlā me kēia kanaka e hoʻomaha a hoʻomaha
he kuleana ko mākou e ʻakaʻaka, a he kuleana ko mākou e hoʻomau e like me ka hiki i kēlā me kēia 
e hana i ka lā a hoʻomaha. 
IWAKALUA-KUMAMALIMA (25) 
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke kūlana ola kūpono no kona iho a me kona 'ohana 
hānai, ʻaʻahu maikaʻi a olakino. Aia nō iā ia ke kuleana 
ua kuleana mākou i ka palekana, ʻoiai inā he kino ko mākou, he wahine kāne make, a he kāne make paha 
ina he elemakule a he opiopio paha; ia makou ke kuleana 
ʻoiai ʻaʻole hiki iā mākou ke loaʻa kahi hana a i ʻole loaʻa koke kahi mea e kākoʻo ai iā mākou iho 
e hoomaluia ke poho.  
2. He kuleana ko nā wāhine hāpai a me nā keiki 
e malama pono ia. Mare a ʻaʻole paha 
Loaʻa i nā keiki a pau nā kuleana like me ka ʻole o ka hānau ʻana ma ka male 
e hoomaluia.  
IWAKALUA-KUMAMAONO (26) 
 

 
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ka ho'ona'auao. ʻAʻole loa 
He manuahi ke kula haʻahaʻa, no laila he mea koi ia no kēlā me kēia me ke kula kumu 
e hiki ai. Wahi a kā mākou leka maikaʻi, ʻoi aku ka nui 
hiki iā mākou ke hoʻonaʻauao iā mākou iho i hiki iā mākou ke hoʻokō i kā mākou mau pahuhopu.  
2. ʻO ka hopena o ka hoʻonaʻauao ka pono o nā kānaka a me nā mea a pau 
ʻike ʻia kona mau pono i mahalo nui ʻia kona kūʻokoʻa 
e loaa. Pono kākou e noho mālie a hoʻomaopopo kekahi i kekahi 
me nā lāhui kanaka, nā lāhui ʻē aʻe, a me kekahi akua 
a me ka poe manaoio. Pono kākou e kākoʻo i ka United Nations 
maluhia ma ka honua.  
3. He kuleana mua ko nā mākua i ka ho'ona'auao
a e hookipa i ko makou ohana.  
IWAKALUA-KUMAMAHIKU (27) 
1. Ua noa i nā kānaka apau ke kū'oko'a o ka ne'e 'ana a me ka noho 'ana ma loko o nā palena o kēlā me kēia Moku'āina 
e kōkua iā mākou i kēlā me kēia kūlana. Hiki iā mākou ke kōkua ma nā ʻano āpau 
ko kakou aina e hooulu nani a ia kakou ke kuleana 
e ʻae i ka mea a ke kūlanakauhale e hāʻawi ai.  
2. Inā mākou i hoʻoneʻe noʻonoʻo i kekahi mea waiwai a i ʻole 
hiki iā mākou ke hana i kahi mea ʻaʻole i hana ʻia e kekahi ma mua 
kuleana mākou i ka waiwai o ia mau hana 
e hoomalu i ka mea e noho nei.  
IWAKALUA-KUMAMAWALU (28) 
He kuleana ko mākou ma ko mākou ʻāina a ma nā ʻāina āpau 
e lilo i hui pono kanaka, i mea e pau ai keia 
e hoʻokō i ko mākou mau pono a me nā kūʻokoʻa.  
IWAKALUA-KUMAMAIWA (29) 
 

 
1. Ua noa na kanaka a pau ma ko kakou aina, nolaila no ko kakou aina 
he kuleana ko makou.  
2. Ma ke kānāwai wale nō ke ala e hoʻokō ai i ko mākou mau pono 
hiki ke pale iā mākou, e like me nā kuleana o nā mea ʻē aʻe a i ʻole nā ​​kūʻokoʻa 
ʻAʻole i kāʻili ʻia ko lākou ola ʻana, i ʻole e hakakā nā hakakā 
e loaa. No laila, aia kēlā me kēia kanaka i kona ʻāina ponoʻī 
e noho maluhia.  
3. ʻAʻole hiki ke hoʻohana ʻia kēia mau pono a me nā kuokoa 
hiki ke, ina ma kekahi ano o na aupuni hui 
ina he kue i kona makemake.  
kanakolu (30) 
ʻAʻole hiki i kekahi a aupuni paha ke hilinaʻi i kēia ʻōlelo
e hana i nā mea a pau, ko mākou kuleana a me ke kūʻokoʻa 
e kaili i ka aina.
