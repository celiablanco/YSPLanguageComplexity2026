IsiBhengezo seHlabathi samaLungelo oLuntu 
Intshayelelo 
Nangona ukuqondwa kwesidima sendalo kunye nokulingana nokungenakohluthwa 
amalungelo awo onke amalungu entsapho yoluntu asisiseko senkululeko, ubulungisa 
noxolo ehlabathini,  
Ngelixa ukungahoywa nokujongelwa phantsi kwamalungelo oluntu kubangele uburhalarhume 
izenzo eziye zacaphukisa isazela soluntu, kunye nokufika kwehlabathi 
apho abantu baya konwabela inkululeko yokuthetha nenkolelo nenkululeko 
ukusuka kuloyiko kunye nokuswela kuye kwabhengezwa njengowona mnqweno uphezulu woluntu 
abantu,  
Nangona kuyimfuneko, ukuba umntu akayi kunyanzelwa ukuba afune uncedo, njengowokugqibela 
ukubhenela, ukuvukela ubuzwilakhe kunye nengcinezelo, ukuba amalungelo oluntu kufuneka abe 
ukhuselwe ngumthetho,  
Nangona kubalulekile ukukhuthaza uphuhliso lobudlelwane bobuhlobo phakathi 
izizwe,  
Ngelixa abantu boMbutho weZizwe eziManyeneyo bathe baqinisekisa kwakhona uMgaqo-siseko wabo 
ukukholelwa kumalungelo oluntu asisiseko, kwisidima nakwixabiso lomntu 
nakumalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabafazi kwaye bazimisele ukukhuthaza 
inkqubela phambili yentlalo kunye nemigangatho engcono yobomi kwinkululeko enkulu,  
Nangona amaZwe angamaLungu ezinikele ekuphumezeni, ngentsebenziswano 
kunye neZizwe eziManyeneyo, ukukhuthazwa kwentlonipho yehlabathi jikelele kunye nokuthotyelwa 
amalungelo oluntu kunye nenkululeko esisiseko,  
Ngelixa ukuqonda okuqhelekileyo kwala malungelo kunye neenkululeko kuyekwe 
okubaluleke kakhulu ekufezekisweni ngokupheleleyo kwesi sibhambathiso,  
Ke ngoku,
INdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo,  
Ibhengeza le Universal Declaration of Human Rights njengomgangatho ofanayo we 
impumelelo yabo bonke abantu nezizwe zonke, de wonke umntu kunye 
Lonke iqumrhu loluntu, lisigcina esi siBhengezo rhoqo, liya kulwela 
 

 
ukufundisa kunye nemfundo ukukhuthaza ukuhlonitshwa kwala malungelo neenkululeko kunye 
amanyathelo aqhubekayo, esizwe nakwamanye amazwe, ukukhusela jikelele kunye 
ukuqondwa okusebenzayo kunye nokugcinwa, kokubini phakathi kwabantu baMazwe angamaLungu 
bona naphakathi kwezizwe eziphantsi kolawulo lwazo.  
Inqaku I  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile kwaye belingana ngesidima nangamalungelo. Banjalo 
banikwe ingqiqo nesazela kwaye kufuneka benze omnye komnye nge 
umoya wobuzalwana.  
Inqaku lesi-2  
Wonke umntu unelungelo kuwo onke amalungelo kunye neenkululeko ezichazwe kwesi siBhengezo, 
ngaphandle kocalucalulo naluphi na uhlobo, njengobuhlanga, umbala, isini, ulwimi, inkolo, 
uluvo lwezopolitiko okanye olunye uluvo, imvelaphi yesizwe okanye yezentlalo, impahla, ukuzalwa okanye obunye ubume.  
Ngaphaya koko, akukho mahluko uya kwenziwa ngokwesiseko sezopolitiko, 
igunya okanye iwonga lamazwe ngamazwe lelizwe okanye ummandla apho umntu 
yeyabo, nokuba izimele, ithembekile, ayizilawulanga okanye iphantsi kwayo nayiphi na enye into 
ukusikelwa umda kolongamo.  
Inqaku lesi-3  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kunye nokhuseleko lomntu.  
Inqaku lesi-4  
Akukho mntu uya kugcinwa ebukhobokeni okanye ebukhobokeni; ubukhoboka kunye norhwebo ngamakhoboka kuya kuba
zithintelwe kuzo zonke iintlobo zazo.  
Inqaku lesi-5  
Akukho mntu uya kungcungcuthekiswa okanye aphathwe ngenkohlakalo, ngendlela engenabuntu okanye ethoba isidima 
okanye isohlwayo.  
 

 
Inqaku lesi-6  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokwamkelwa yonke indawo njengomntu phambi komthetho.  
Inqaku lesi-7  
Bonke bayalingana phambi komthetho kwaye banelungelo lokulingana ngaphandle kocalucalulo 
ukhuseleko lomthetho. Bonke banelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo kuyo nayiphi na 
ucalucalulo olwaphula esi siBhengezo nangakuphi na ukuphembelela oko 
ucalucalulo.  
Inqaku lesi-8  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lolungiso olusebenzayo yinkundla yamatyala esizwe 
ngezenzo ezinyhasha amalungelo asisiseko awanikwa ngumgaqo-siseko okanye ngumthetho.  
Inqaku lesi-9  
Akukho mntu uya kubanjwa, avalelwe okanye agxothwe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
Inqaku le-10  
Wonke umntu unelungelo elilinganayo nelipheleleyo lokuxoxwa kwetyala esidlangalaleni ngokuzimeleyo 
nenkundla engakhethi buso, ekumiselweni kwamalungelo nezibophelelo zakhe kunye nayo nayiphi na 
ityala lolwaphulo-mthetho ngakuye.  
Inqaku le-11  
1. Wonke umntu omangalelwe kuba enetyala unelungelo lokucingelwa 
umsulwa de wafunyaniswa enetyala ngokomthetho kwinkundla yamatyala apho athe wafunyanwa enetyala 
ubenazo zonke iziqinisekiso eziyimfuneko kukhuseleko lwakhe.  
2. Akukho mntu uya kufunyanwa enetyala ngenxa yaso nasiphi na isenzo okanye 
ukutyeshela okungazange kube netyala lesohlwayo, phantsi kwesizwe okanye 
umthetho wamazwe ngamazwe, ngexesha owawusenziwa ngawo. Akuyi kuba nzima ngakumbi
kuwiswe isohlwayo kuneso besisebenza ngexesha lesohlwayo 
kwenziwa ityala.  
Inqaku le-12  
Akukho mntu uya kuphazanyiswa kubomi bakhe babucala, kusapho lwakhe, nakwikhaya lakhe 
okanye imbalelwano, okanye ukuhlasela imbeko nodumo lwakhe. Wonke umntu unalo 
ilungelo lokukhuselwa ngumthetho ngokuchasene nokuphazanyiswa okanye uhlaselo.  
Inqaku le-13  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhamba nokuhlala ngaphakathi 
imida yeLizwe ngalinye.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokushiya nakweliphi na ilizwe, kuquka nelakhe, kunye nokuhamba 
abuyele ezweni lakhe.  
Inqaku le-14  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufuna nokonwabela indawo yokhuseleko kwamanye amazwe 
intshutshiso.  
2. Eli lungelo alinakusetyenziswa ngokunyanisekileyo kwityala lokutshutshisa 
ezivela kulwaphulo-mthetho olungelulo olwapolitiki okanye kwizenzo ezichasene neenjongo kunye 
imigaqo yeZizwe eziManyeneyo.  
Inqaku le-15  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lobuzwe.  
2. Akukho mntu uya kuhluthwa ubuzwe bakhe okanye avinjwe ilungelo loko 
atshintshe ubuzwe bakhe.  
Inqaku le-16  
 

 
1. Amadoda nabasetyhini abasele bekhulile, ngaphandle kokuthintelwa ngenxa yobuhlanga, ubuzwe 
okanye unqulo, banelungelo lokutshata baze babe nosapho. Banelungelo 
kumalungelo alinganayo emtshatweni, ngexesha lomtshato naxa uqhawulwa.  
2. Kuya kutshatwa kuphela ngokukhululekileyo nangemvume epheleleyo 
abaza kutshata nabo.  
3. Usapho yinxalenye yendalo kunye nesisiseko seqela loluntu kwaye lu
unelungelo lokukhuselwa luluntu kunye noRhulumente.  
Inqaku le-17  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuba nezinto ezizezakhe yedwa okanye azidibanise ne 
abanye.  
2 Akukho bani uya kuhluthwa impahla yakhe.  
Inqaku le-18  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucinga, isazela nenkolo; eli lungelo 
ibandakanya inkululeko yokutshintsha inkolo okanye inkolelo yakhe, nenkululeko, nokuba uwedwa okanye ungaphakathi 
ekuhlaleni nabanye nasesidlangalaleni okanye ngasese, ukubonisa inkolo okanye inkolelo yakhe kuyo 
ukufundisa, ukwenza, unqulo nokugcina.  
Inqaku le-19  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuvakalisa izimvo zakhe; eli lungelo libandakanya 
inkululeko yokubamba izimvo ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka kunye nokufuna, ukufumana kunye nokwabelana 
ulwazi kunye neengcinga ngokusebenzisa nayiphi na imidiya kunye nokuba yeyiphi imida.  
Inqaku lama-20  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yeendibano zoxolo kunye neentlanganiso zoxolo.  
2. Akukho mntu unokunyanzelwa ukuba abe lilungu lombutho.  
Inqaku lama-21  
 

 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba kurhulumente welizwe lakhe. 
ngqo okanye ngokusebenzisa abameli abakhethwe ngokukhululekileyo.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo elilinganayo lokufikelela kwinkonzo karhulumente kwilizwe lakhe.  
3. Intando yabantu iya kuba sisiseko segunya likarhulumente; 
oku kuya kuchazwa kunyulo lwamaxesha ngamaxesha nolwenyani oluya kuthi 
Ilungelo lomntu wonke nelilinganayo kwaye liya kubanjwa ngevoti eyimfihlo okanye ngo 
iinkqubo zokuvota simahla ezilinganayo.  
Inqaku lama-22
Wonke umntu, njengelungu loluntu, unelungelo lokhuseleko loluntu kwaye unelungelo 
ukufezekisa, ngeenzame zesizwe kunye nentsebenziswano yamazwe ngamazwe kunye 
ngokuhambelana nombutho kunye nemithombo yeLizwe ngalinye, yezoqoqosho, 
amalungelo ezentlalo nenkcubeko ayimfuneko kwisidima sakhe kunye nophuhliso olukhululekileyo 
kobuntu bakhe.  
Inqaku lama-23  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukuzikhethela umsebenzi ngokukhululekileyo, ubulungisa kunye 
iimeko ezifanelekileyo zomsebenzi kunye nokukhuselwa ngokuchasene nentswela-ngqesho.  
2. Wonke umntu, ngaphandle kokucalula, unelungelo lomvuzo olinganayo ngokulinganayo 
umsebenzi.  
3. Wonke umntu osebenzayo unelungelo lomvuzo ofanelekileyo nolungileyo 
ukuqinisekisa yena kunye nosapho lwakhe ubomi obufanele isidima sobuntu, 
kwaye yongezwe, ukuba kuyimfuneko, ngezinye iindlela zokukhusela uluntu.  
4. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuseka kunye nokuba lilungu lemibutho yabasebenzi ukuze akhuseleke 
imidla yakhe.  
Inqaku lama-24  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphumla kunye nokuzonwabisa, kuquka nemida efanelekileyo 
iiyure zokusebenza kunye neeholide ngamaxesha kunye umvuzo.  
 

 
Inqaku lama-25  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lomgangatho wokuphila ofanelekileyo ngokwempilo kunye 
intlalontle yakhe neyentsapho yakhe, kuquka ukutya, impahla, indawo yokuhlala 
kunye nokhathalelo lwezonyango kunye neenkonzo zentlalo eziyimfuneko, kunye nelungelo lokhuseleko 
xa ungasebenzi, ugula, unesiphene, ungumhlolokazi, ukwaluphala okanye 
okunye ukungabikho kokuziphilisa kwiimeko ezingaphaya kwamandla akhe.
2. Ubunina nobuntwana bunelungelo lokhathalelo noncedo olulodwa. Konke 
abantwana, nokuba bazalwe emtshatweni okanye ngaphandle komtshato, baya konwabela intlalo efanayo 
ukhuselo.  
Inqaku lama-26  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo iya kuba simahla, ubuncinci kwi 
amanqanaba asisiseko kunye nesiseko. Imfundo yamabanga aphantsi iya kuba 
kunyanzelekile. Kuya kwenziwa imfundo yobugcisa nobungcali 
ifumaneka ngokubanzi kwaye imfundo ephakamileyo iya kufikeleleka ngokulinganayo kubo bonke 
ngokwesiseko sokufaneleka.  
2. Imfundo iya kujoliswa kuphuhliso olupheleleyo lomntu 
ubuntu nokomelezwa kwentlonipho yamalungelo oluntu kunye 
iinkululeko ezisisiseko. Iya kukhuthaza ukuqonda, ukunyamezelana kunye 
ubuhlobo phakathi kwezizwe zonke, uhlanga okanye amaqela enkolo, kwaye uya kuqhubeka 
imisebenzi yeZizwe eziManyeneyo yokugcina uxolo.  
3. Abazali banelungelo eliphambili lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo abaya kuba yiyo 
banikwe abantwana babo.  
Inqaku lama-27  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba ngokukhululekileyo kubomi benkcubeko yelizwe 
uluntu, ukonwabela ubugcisa kunye nesabelo kwinkqubela phambili yenzululwazi kunye 
iingenelo zayo.  
 

 
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa kwiminqweno yakhe yokuziphatha kunye nezinto eziphathekayo 
ngenxa yayo nayiphi na imveliso yenzululwazi, yoncwadi okanye yobugcisa anguye 
umbhali.  
Inqaku lama-28  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lentlalontle kunye nelizwe jikelele apho amalungelo kunye 
Iinkululeko ezichazwe kwesi siBhengezo zinokufezekiswa ngokupheleleyo.  
Inqaku lama-29
1. Wonke umntu unemisebenzi kuluntu apho eyedwa abantu abakhululekileyo nabazeleyo 
ukuphuhliswa kobuntu bakhe kunokwenzeka.  
2. Ekusebenziseni amalungelo kunye nenkululeko, wonke umntu uya kuthotyelwa kuphela 
kwimida emiselwe ngumthetho kuphela ngenjongo 
ukufumana ulwamkelo olufanelekileyo kunye nentlonipho yamalungelo nenkululeko yabanye 
kunye nokuhlangabezana neemfuno zobulungisa zokuziphatha, ucwangco loluntu kunye ne 
intlalontle jikelele kuluntu lwedemokhrasi.  
3. La malungelo kunye neenkululeko mazingaze nakanye zisetyenziswe ngokuchaseneyo 
iinjongo kunye nemithetho-siseko yeZizwe eziManyeneyo.  
Inqaku lama-30  
Akukho nto kwesi siBhengezo inokutolikwa njengebhekisa kulo naliphi na iLizwe, iqela okanye 
umntu naliphi na ilungelo lokubandakanyeka kuwo nawuphi na umsebenzi okanye ukwenza nasiphi na isenzo esijolise kuko 
ukutshatyalaliswa kwawo nawaphi na amalungelo kunye neenkululeko ezichazwe apha.
