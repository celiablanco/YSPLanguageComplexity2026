Ekirangiriro ky’ensi yonna ekikwata ku ddembe ly’obuntu

Okukkiriza ekitiibwa ekizaaliranwa n’eddembe eryenkanankana era eritaggyibwako eri abantu bonna ab’olulyo lw’omuntu gwe musingi gw’eddembe, obwenkanya n’emirembe mu nsi; obutafaayo n’okunyooma eddembe ly’obuntu kivuddeko ebikolwa eby’obukambwe ebinyiiza omuntu ow’omunda ow’omunda; era okujja kw’ensi omuntu mw’asobola okunyumirwa eddembe ly’okwogera n’okukkiriza n’eddembe okuva mu kutya n’okwagala kulangiriddwa ng’ekiruubirirwa eky’oku ntikko eky’abantu bonna, sikulwa ng’omuntu awalirizibwa okujeema ng’eky’okusembayo okulwanyisa obutyobooli n’okunyigirizibwa; Kikulu nnyo eddembe ly’obuntu okukuumibwa enfuga y’amateeka, era kyetaagisa nnyo okutumbula enkolagana ey’omukwano wakati w’amawanga. Mu ndagaano yaayo, amawanga gonna agali mu kibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte gazzeemu okukakasa enzikiriza yaabwe mu ddembe ly’obuntu ery’omusingi, ekitiibwa n’omuwendo gw’omuntu, n’eddembe eryenkanankana ery’abasajja n’abakazi, era ne beeyama okutumbula enkulaakulana mu mbeera z’abantu n’omutindo omulungi ogw’obulamu mu ddembe erigazi. Amawanga agali mu mukago geeyamye okukolagana n’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte okutumbula okussa ekitiibwa mu bantu bonna n’okukuuma eddembe ly’obuntu n’eddembe ery’omusingi. Okuva okutegeera okw’awamu ku ddembe n’eddembe bwe kuli okukulu ennyo mu kussa mu nkola mu bujjuvu Endagaano eno, Olukiiko Olukulu olw’Ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte, n’olwekyo, lufuba okutumbula okussa ekitiibwa mu ddembe n’eddembe lino nga luyita mu kuyiga n’okusomesa mu bantu bonna ssekinnoomu n’ebitongole by’abantu, bulijjo nga bifaayo ku Kirangiriro kino, era nga lufuba, nga luyita mu nkola z’eggwanga n’ensi yonna ezigenda mu maaso, okulaba ng’okumanyibwa n’okugoberera abantu bonna era nga bakola bulungi ku ddembe n’eddembe lino mu bantu b’amawanga agali mu mukago gennyini n’abantu b’ebitundu ebiri wansi w’obuyinza bwago; Tulangirira ekirangiriro ky’ensi yonna ekikwata ku ddembe ly’obuntu. Akawaayiro 1

Abantu bonna bazaalibwa ba ddembe era benkanankana mu kitiibwa n’eddembe. Abantu mu butonde baweereddwa ensonga n’omuntu ow’omunda era balina okukola mu mwoyo gw’obwasseruganda eri bannaabwe. Akawaayiro 2 Buli muntu alina eddembe n’eddembe byonna ebirambikiddwa mu Kirangiriro kino, awatali kusosola mu ngeri yonna, gamba ng’amawanga, langi, ekikula, olulimi, eddiini, endowooza y’ebyobufuzi oba endala, ensibuko y’eggwanga oba mu bantu, ebintu, obuzaale oba embeera endala. Ekirala, tewajja kubaawo kusosola ku musingi gw’embeera y’ebyobufuzi, ey’amateeka oba ey’ensi yonna ey’ensi oba ekitundu ky’omuntu ssekinnoomu, awatali kulowooza oba ensi oba ekitundu ekyo ggwanga eryetongodde, ery’obwesige, ekitundu ekitali kya kwefuga, oba kirina obukwakkulizo obulala ku bwetwaze bwayo. Akawaayiro 3 Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’obulamu, eddembe n’obukuumi bw’omuntu. Akawaayiro 4 Tewali muntu yenna ajja kukwatibwa mu mbeera ya buddu oba buddu. Engeri zonna ez’obuddu n’okusuubula abaddu bikugirwa. Akawaayiro 5 Tewali muntu yenna ajja kutulugunyizibwa oba okuyisibwa oba okubonerezebwa mu ngeri ey’obukambwe, etali ya buntu oba ey’okutyoboola. Akawaayiro 6 Buli muntu alina eddembe okumanyibwa ng’omuntu mu maaso g’amateeka buli wamu. Akawaayiro 7
Abantu bonna benkanankana mu maaso g’amateeka era balina eddembe okukuuma amateeka kyenkanyi awatali kusosola kwonna. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuumibwa kyenkanyi ku kusosola kwonna okumenya Ekirangiriro kino n’okukuma omuliro mu bantu okusosola okwo. Akawaayiro 8 Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna obuweerero obulungi okuva mu kkooti z’omunda ezirina obuyinza ku bikolwa ebityoboola eddembe ery’omusingi eriweebwa Ssemateeka oba mu mateeka. Akawaayiro 9 Tewali muntu yenna ajja kukwatibwa mu ngeri ya kimpowooze, okusibirwa oba okuwang’angusibwa. Akawaayiro 10 Buli muntu alina eddembe okuwozesebwa mu bwenkanya era mu lujjudde nga yenkanankana mu bujjuvu mu kkooti eyetongodde era etaliimu kyekubiira okusalawo ku ddembe lye, obuvunaanyizibwa bwe n’emisango gy’avunaanibwa. Akawaayiro 11 1. Buli muntu avunaanibwa omusango alina eddembe okutwalibwa ng’atalina musango okutuusa ng’akakasiddwa omusango okusinziira ku mateeka mu musango gw’olukale nga buli kimu ekyetaagisa okwewozaako kikakasiddwa. 2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuvunaanibwa olw’ekikolwa kyonna oba okulemererwa, mu kiseera ekikolwa ekyo, tekyali musango mu mateeka g’eggwanga oba ag’ensi yonna. Okugatta ku ekyo, tewali kibonerezo kizito kijja kuweebwa okusinga ekibonerezo ekyandibadde kiweebwa mu kiseera omusango we gwakolebwa. Akawaayiro 12 Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuyingizibwa mu bulamu bwe obw’ekyama, amaka ge, gy’abeera oba ebbaluwa ze, oba okunenya ekitiibwa kye n’erinnya lye. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuumibwa amateeka okuva ku kuyingirira oba okunenya ng’okwo. Akawaayiro 13

1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutambula n’okubeera mu nsi ye. 2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okuva mu nsi yonna, omuli n’eye, n’eddembe okudda mu nsi ye. Akawaayiro 14 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okunoonya n’okufuna obubudamu mu nsi endala okuva mu kuyigganyizibwa. 2. Eddembe lino teriyinza kukakasibwa mu misango gy’okuvunaanibwa emisango egy’amazima egitali gya byabufuzi oba ebikolwa ebikontana n’ebigendererwa n’emisingi gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte. Akawaayiro 15 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera eggwanga. 2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako ggwanga lye mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira oba okugaanibwa eddembe ly’okukyusa eggwanga lye. Akawaayiro 16 1. Abasajja n’abakazi abakulu balina eddembe okufumbirwa n’okukola amaka awatali kuziyizibwa kwonna okusinziira ku ggwanga, eggwanga oba eddiini. Balina eddembe eryenkanankana ku bikwata ku bufumbo, mu kiseera ky’obufumbo ne nga obufumbo bwasasika. 2. Obufumbo butandikibwawo nga abafumbo abagenda okufumbiriganwa bakkirizza ku bwereere era mu bujjuvu. 3. Amaka kitundu kya butonde era kya musingi mu bantu era galina eddembe okukuumibwa abantu n’eggwanga. Akawaayiro 17 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera n’ebintu kinnoomu ne wamu n’abalala. 2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako bintu bye mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira. Akawaayiro 18
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okulowooza, omuntu ow’omunda n’eddiini. Eddembe lino lirimu eddembe ly’okukyusa eddiini oba enzikiriza ye n’okwolesa eddiini oba enzikiriza ye, yekka oba mu kitundu n’abalala ne mu lujjudde oba mu kyama, ng’akozesa enkola z’eddiini, okusinza n’okukuuma. Akawaayiro 19 Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okulowooza n’okwogera. Eddembe lino mulimu eddembe ly’okukwata endowooza awatali kuyingirira n’eddembe ly’okunoonya, okufuna n’okubunyisa amawulire n’ebirowoozo nga bayita mu mikutu gyonna, awatali kufaayo ku nsalo. Akawaayiro 20 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okukuŋŋaana n’okwegatta mu mirembe. 2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kukakibwa kwetaba mu kibiina kyonna. Akawaayiro 21 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwetaba mu gavumenti y’eggwanga lye, oba butereevu oba ng’ayita mu bantu abalondeddwa mu ddembe. 2. Buli muntu alina eddembe eryenkanankana okubeera mu bifo bya gavumenti mu nsi ye. 3. Okwagala kw’abantu gwe musingi gw’obuyinza bwa gavumenti. Kino kijja kulagibwa nga tuyita mu kulonda okwa bulijjo era okwa nnamaddala nga tuyita mu nkola y’okulonda ey’ekyama oba ey’obwereere eyenkanankana okusinziira ku ddembe ly’okulonda erya bonna era eryenkanankana. Akawaayiro 22 Buli muntu, ng’omuntu mu kibiina, alina eddembe okufuna obukuumi mu mbeera z’abantu n’eddembe okutegeera, okuyita mu kaweefube w’eggwanga n’enkolagana y’ensi yonna, era okusinziira ku nteekateeka n’eby’obugagga bya buli Ggwanga, eddembe ly’ebyenfuna, ery’embeera z’abantu n’ebyobuwangwa eryetaagisa okukulaakulanya eddembe lye ery’ekitiibwa n’obuntu bwe. Akawaayiro 23

1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukola, eddembe ly’okwesalirawo emirimu, embeera y’emirimu ey’obwenkanya era ennungi n’okukuuma ebbula ly’emirimu. 2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okuliyirirwa kyenkanyi olw’omulimu ogw’enkanankana awatali kusosola kwonna. 3. Buli muntu akola alina eddembe okufuna omusaala ogw’obwenkanya era ogw’enkizo, ogukakasa ye n’ab’omu maka ge okuwangaala okukwatagana n’ekitiibwa ky’omuntu era nga kyongerwako, bwe kiba kyetaagisa, n’enkola endala ez’obukuumi bw’abantu. 4. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutandikawo n’okwegatta ku kibiina ky’abakozi okukuuma ebirungi bye. Akawaayiro 24 Buli muntu alina eddembe okuwummulamu n’okuwummulamu, omuli okussa ekkomo ensaamusaamu ku ssaawa z’okukola n’oluwummula olusasulwa buli kiseera. Akawaayiro 25 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna omutindo gw’obulamu ogumala ku bulamu bwe n’obulungi bw’ab’omu maka ge, omuli emmere, engoye, aw’okusula, obujjanjabi n’obulungi bw’abantu obwetaagisa, n’eddembe ly’okufuna obukuumi singa wabaawo obutaba na kye yeeyimirizaawo olw’ebbula ly’emirimu, obulwadde, obulemu, okufa kw’omufumbo, obukadde oba embeera endala ezitasobola kufuga. 2. Bamaama n’abaana balina eddembe okukuumibwa n’okuyambibwa mu ngeri ey’enjawulo. Abaana bonna bafuna obukuumi bwe bumu mu mbeera z‟abantu awatali kufaayo ku mbeera yaabwe. Akawaayiro 26 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okusomesebwa. Okusoma kulina okuba okw’obwereere, waakiri ku mutendera gwa pulayimale n’ogw’omusingi. Okusoma pulayimale kulina okuba nga kukakatako. Obuyigirize obw’ekikugu n’obw’emikono bulina okutuukirirwa abantu bonna, ate amatendekero aga waggulu bulina okutuukirirwa kyenkanyi eri bonna okusinziira ku bumanyirivu. 2. Ebyenjigiriza bigenderera okukulaakulanya omuntu mu bujjuvu n’okunyweza okussa ekitiibwa mu ddembe ly’obuntu n’eddembe ery’omusingi. Okusomesa kutumbula okutegeeragana, okugumiikiriza n’...
Tulina okutumbula omukwano n’okutumbula emirimu gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte okukuuma emirembe. 3. Abazadde be bakulembeza okulonda ekika ky’obuyigirize obuweebwa abaana baabwe. Akawaayiro 27 1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwenyigira mu ddembe mu bulamu bw’obuwangwa bw’ekitundu, okunyumirwa eby’emikono, n’okwenyigira mu kutumbula ssaayansi n’emigaso gyayo. 2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuuma eby’omwoyo n’eby’omubiri ebiva mu bintu bya ssaayansi, ebiwandiiko oba eby’ekikugu by’ayiiya. Akawaayiro 28 Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera n’enteekateeka y’embeera z’abantu n’ensi yonna eddembe n’eddembe ebiragiddwa mu Kirangiriro kino mwe bisobola okutuukirira mu bujjuvu. Akawaayiro 29 1. Buli muntu alina obuvunaanyizibwa eri ekitundu omuli omuntu we yekka y’asobola okukula mu ddembe era mu bujjuvu. . 3. Eddembe n’eddembe lino mu mbeera yonna tebijja kukozesebwa nga bikontana n’ebigendererwa n’emisingi gy’ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte. Akawaayiro 30 Tewali kintu kyonna mu Kirangiriro kino kijja kutambulizibwa ng’ekikkiriza Eggwanga lyonna, ekibiina oba omuntu ssekinnoomu okwenyigira mu mulimu gwonna oba okukola ekikolwa kyonna ekigendereddwamu okusaanyaawo eddembe lyonna n’eddembe eriragiddwa mu Kirangiriro kino.
