Universal Declaration of Human Rights 
Mawu Oyamba 
Poganizira kuti kuzindikira ulemu wobadwa nawo wa mamembala onse a banja laumunthu ndi ufulu wawo 
zofanana ndi zosasinthika zimapanga maziko a ufulu, chilungamo ndi mtendere padziko lapansi;  
Poganizira kuti umbuli ndi kunyalanyaza ufulu wa anthu kwachititsa kuti kuchita zinthu zankhanza kwambiri 
chikumbumtima cha Anthu ndi kuti kubwera kwa dziko limene anthu ali omasuka kulankhula ndi kukhulupirira, 
atamasulidwa ku zoopsa ndi zowawa, adalengezedwa ngati kudzoza kwapamwamba kwa Munthu;  
Poganizira kuti kutetezedwa kwa ufulu wa anthu kudzera muulamuliro wa malamulo ndikofunikira, kotero kuti munthu 
musakakamizidwe, mu njira yapamwamba, kupandukira nkhanza ndi kuponderezana;  
Pamene kuli kofunikira kulimbikitsa kukulitsa ubale waubwenzi pakati pa mayiko;  
Poganizira kuti, mu Charter, anthu a United Nations akulengeza, kachiwiri, chikhulupiriro chawo mu ufulu wachibadwidwe wa 
Munthu, mu ulemu ndi mtengo wa munthu, mu ufulu wofanana wa amuna ndi akazi ndi ngati 
adalengeza kutsimikiza mtima kwawo kulimbikitsa kupita patsogolo kwa anthu ndikukhazikitsa mikhalidwe yabwinoko mkati mwaufulu 
chokulirapo;  
Poganizira kuti mayiko omwe ali mamembala adzipereka kulimbikitsa, mogwirizana ndi bungwe la 
United Nations, kulemekeza kwapadziko lonse ndi koyenera kwa ufulu wa anthu ndi ufulu wachibadwidwe;
Poganizira kuti lingaliro lofanana la ufulu ndi ufuluwu ndilofunika kwambiri mokwanira 
kukhutitsidwa ndi kudzipereka kotere:  
General Assembly ikulengeza za Universal Declaration  
wa Ufulu Wachibadwidwe  
monga lingaliro loyenera kukwaniritsidwa ndi anthu onse ndi mayiko onse, kotero kuti anthu onse ndi ziwalo zonse za 
anthu, pokhala nazo nthawi zonse m'maganizo, amayesetsa, kupyolera mu kuphunzitsa ndi maphunziro, kukulitsa ulemu 
za maufulu awa ndi kumasuka ndi kulimbikitsa, kupyolera mu njira zopita patsogolo za dziko ndi mayiko, zawo 
kuzindikira kwapadziko lonse ndi kogwira mtima ndikugwiritsa ntchito pakati pa anthu a mayiko omwe ali membala komanso 
pakati pa madera omwe ali pansi pa ulamuliro wake.  
Ndime 1  
Anthu onse amabadwa aufulu ndi ofanana mu ulemu ndi ufulu. Atapatsidwa nzeru ndi chikumbumtima, ayenera 
kuchitirana zinthu mwa mzimu waubale.  
Ndime 2  

Anthu onse atha kugwiritsa ntchito ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zalengezedwa m'chikalatachi, popanda kusiyanitsa 
chilichonse, chomwe ndi mtundu, mtundu, kugonana, chinenero, chipembedzo, ndale kapena maganizo ena, fuko 
kapena chikhalidwe, mwayi, kubadwa kapena vuto lina lililonse. 
Kuphatikiza apo, palibe kusiyanitsa komwe kudzachitike potengera momwe dzikolo lilili, zamalamulo kapena zapadziko lonse lapansi 
gawo la kubadwa kwa munthuyo, kaya dzikolo kapena gawo lodziyimira palokha, pansi pa ulonda, wodzilamulira kapena wolamulidwa ndi
kuchepetsa ulamuliro.  
Ndime 3 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi moyo, ufulu ndi chitetezo.  
Ndime 4  
Palibe amene adzakhala muukapolo kapena ukapolo; ukapolo ndi kuchitira akapolo, mwa mitundu yonse, ndi 
zoletsedwa.  
Ndime 5  
Palibe amene adzazunzidwe kapena kuzunzidwa, mwankhanza kapena monyozetsa.  
Ndime 6 
Anthu onse ali ndi ufulu wozindikira, kulikonse, umunthu wawo walamulo.  
Ndime 7  
Onse ndi ofanana pamaso pa lamulo ndipo, popanda kusiyanitsa, ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana ndi lamulo. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana 
motsutsana ndi tsankho lililonse lomwe likuphwanya Chikalatachi komanso zolimbikitsa tsankho.  
Ndime 8 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi mphamvu zogwilira nchito mutsogoleli wadziko lonse kuphwanya malamulo 
Ufulu wachibadidwe wovomerezedwa ndi Constitution kapena lamulo.  
Ndime 9 
Palibe amene angamangidwe, kumangidwa kapena kuthamangitsidwa popanda chilolezo.  
Ndime 10  

Aliyense ali ndi ufulu, mofanana, kuti mlandu wake uweruzidwe mwachilungamo ndi pagulu 
chigamulo chodziyimira pawokha komanso chopanda tsankho paufulu wanu ndi zomwe mukufuna kapena zifukwa zomwe mukunamizira pankhaniyi 
chilango choperekedwa pa iye.  
Ndime 11 
1. 
Munthu aliyense woimbidwa mlandu wopalamula amaganiziridwa kuti ndi wosalakwa mpaka atatsimikiziridwa kuti ndi wolakwa.
kutsimikiziridwa mwalamulo panthawi ya ndondomeko ya anthu momwe zitsimikizo zonse zofunikira zotetezera zimaperekedwa 
zimatsimikiziridwa.  
2. 
Palibe amene adzatsutsidwe chifukwa cha zochita kapena zosiya zomwe, panthawi ya ntchito yawo, sizinapange kanthu. 
chigawenga pansi pa malamulo apakhomo kapena apadziko lonse lapansi. Momwemonso, palibe chilango chokhwima chomwe chidzaperekedwa kuposa 
zomwe zinali zoyenera pa nthawi yomwe mchitidwe waupandu unkachitika.  
Ndime 12  
Palibe amene adzavutike ndi kusokonezedwa mwachisawawa pa moyo wake wamseri, banja lawo, nyumba yawo kapena yawo 
makalata, kapena kutsutsa ulemu ndi mbiri yake. Polimbana ndi kusokonezedwa koteroko kapena kuwukira aliyense ali ndi ufulu 
chitetezo cha lamulo.  
Ndime 13  
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi danga losamuka mwaufulu ndi kusankha kokhala m'dziko lina.  
2. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kuchoka m'dziko limene ali, kuphatikizapo dziko lake, ndi ufulu wobwerera 
ku dziko lako.  
Ndime 14  
1. 
Aliyense amene akuzunzidwa ali ndi ufulu wofunafuna ndikupindula ndi chitetezo kumayiko ena.  
2. 
Ufulu umenewu, komabe, sungagwiritsidwe ntchito pa milandu yomwe ilipo pa mlandu wotsatira lamulo. 
kapena zochita zosemphana ndi zolinga ndi mfundo za United Nations.  
Ndime 15  
1. 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi dziko.  
2. 
Palibe amene angalandidwe mtundu wake kapena ufulu wosintha mtundu wake mwachisawawa.  
Ndime 16  
1.
Kuyambira m’zaka za m’banja, amuna ndi akazi ali ndi ufulu wokwatirana ndi kupeza banja, popanda zoletsa zilizonse. 
mtundu, dziko kapena chipembedzo. Panthawi yaukwati komanso pa nthawi ya kutha, onse awiri ali ndi ufulu 
ofanana.  
2. 
Ukwati sungakhoze kukondweretsedwa popanda chilolezo chaufulu ndi chokwanira cha okwatirana amtsogolo.  
3. 
Banja ndi gawo lachilengedwe komanso lofunika kwambiri pagulu la anthu ndipo lili ndi ufulu wotetezedwa kwa anthu ndi boma.  

Ndime 17  
1. 
Munthu aliyense payekha kapena gulu ali ndi ufulu kukhala ndi katundu.  
2. 
Palibe amene angalandidwe katundu wake mwachisawawa.  
Ndime 18 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wa kuganiza, chikumbumtima ndi chipembedzo; Ufuluwu ukutanthauza ufulu wa 
sinthani chipembedzo kapena chikhulupiriro chanu, komanso ufulu wowonetsa chipembedzo chanu kapena chikhulupiriro chanu, nokha kapena mkati 
zofala, ponse pagulu ndi mseri, kudzera mu kuphunzitsa, kuchita, kupembedza ndi miyambo.  
Ndime 19 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi maganizo omasuka, zomwe zikutanthauza kuti ali ndi ufulu wosasokonezedwa 
malingaliro awo ndi kufunafuna, kulandira ndi kufalitsa, mosaganizira malire, chidziwitso ndi malingaliro ndi aliyense 
njira zofotokozera.  
Ndime 20 ° 
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi danga losonkhana pamodzi mwamtendere.  
2. 
Palibe amene angakakamizidwe kukhala mbali ya gulu.  
Ndime 21  
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa kayendetsedwe ka kayendetsedwe ka kayendetsedwe ka boma m'dziko lake, kaya mwachindunji,
kapena kudzera mwa nthumwi zosankhidwa mwaufulu.  
2. 
Aliyense ali ndi danga lopeza, molingana ndi nchito za dziko lace.  
3. 
Chifuniro cha anthu ndicho maziko a ulamuliro wa mphamvu za boma: ndipo ziyenera kuwonetsedwa kupyolera mu chisankho 
zisankho zowona ziyenera kuchitidwa nthawi ndi nthawi ndi ufulu wapadziko lonse lapansi komanso wofanana, ndi voti yachinsinsi kapena njira yachiwiri. 
zofanana zomwe zimateteza ufulu wovota.  
Ndime 22  
Munthu aliyense, ngati membala wa gulu, ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa; ndipo mwina movomerezeka amafuna kukhutitsidwa kwa 
Ufulu wofunikira pazachuma, chikhalidwe ndi chikhalidwe, chifukwa cha zoyesayesa za dziko ndi mgwirizano wapadziko lonse, wa 
mogwirizana ndi dongosolo ndi chuma cha dziko lililonse.  
Ndime 23 
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi danga logwila nchito, kusankha nchito mwaufulu, kukhala ndi nchito yolingana ndi yokwanilitsa 
ntchito ndi chitetezo ku ulova.  
2. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu, popanda tsankho, malipiro ofanana pa ntchito yofanana.  

3. 
Aliyense wogwira ntchito ali ndi ufulu wolandira malipiro oyenera, omwe amalola kuti iye ndi banja lake alandire malipiro oyenera. 
kukhalako mogwirizana ndi ulemu wa munthu, ndi kuwonjezeredwa, ngati nkotheka, ndi njira zina zonse 
chitetezo cha anthu.  
4. 
Aliyense ali ndi danga lopeza migwirizano ndi anthu ena ndikukhala nawo mu mabungwe achitetezo 
za zokonda zanu.  
Ndime 24
Aliyense ali ndi danga lopuma ndi kupumula, makamaka, kupyola malire pa nthawi ya nchito ndi 
tchuti cholipira nthawi ndi nthawi.  
Ndime 25 
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi moyo wokwanira kuti iye ndi banja lake akhale ndi thanzi labwino
kukhala, makamaka pankhani ya chakudya, zovala, malo ogona, thandizo lachipatala komanso mwa mawu a 
zofunikira zothandizira anthu, ndipo ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa pakagwa ntchito, matenda, olumala, 
umasiye, ukalamba kapena milandu ina yakutayikiridwa zinthu zofunika pa moyo chifukwa cha zinthu zosadalira pa 
chifuniro chanu.  
2. 
Amayi ndi ubwana ali ndi ufulu wothandizidwa ndi chithandizo chapadera. Ana onse, obadwa mkati kapena 
Kunja kwa ukwati, amasangalala ndi chitetezo chofanana ndi cha anthu.  
Ndime 26  
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wophunzira. Maphunziro ayenera kukhala aulere, osachepera ofanana ndi kuphunzitsa 
zoyambira zoyambira. Maphunziro a pulayimale ndi ovomerezeka. Maphunziro aukadaulo ndi akatswiri ayenera kukhala 
kufalikira; Kupeza maphunziro apamwamba kuyenera kukhala kotseguka kwa onse mofanana, kutengera 
kuyenera kwake.  
2. 
Maphunziro ayenera kuyesetsa kukulitsa umunthu waumunthu ndi kulimbikitsa ufulu wa anthu ndi 
maufulu ofunikira ndipo ayenera kulimbikitsa kumvetsetsana, kulolerana ndi ubwenzi pakati pa mayiko onse ndi 
magulu onse amitundu kapena azipembedzo, komanso chitukuko cha ntchito za United Nations za 
kusunga mtendere.  
3.
Makolo ali ndi ufulu woyamba kusankha mtundu wa maphunziro operekedwa kwa ana awo.  
Ndime 27  
1. 
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa chikhalidwe cha anthu, kusangalala ndi zaluso ndi 
kukhala ndi phande m’kupita patsogolo kwa sayansi ndi mapindu amene amatulukapo.  
2. 
Aliyense ali ndi danga lotetezedwa ku zokonda zake zakuthupi ndi zakuthupi zolumikizidwa ndi zolemba zilizonse zasayansi 
kapena ntchito zaluso zanu.  
Ndime 28  
Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi dongosolo lolamulila, pa nkhani za chikhalidwe ndi zapadziko lonse lapansi, kuchita mokwanira 
maufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zafotokozedwa m'chikalatachi ndi zogwira mtima.  

Ndime 29  
1. 
Munthuyo ali ndi ntchito kwa anthu ammudzi, zomwe kunja kwake sikutheka chitukuko chaulere ndi chokwanira. 
za umunthu wanu.  
2. 
Pogwiritsa ntchito maufulu amenewa ndi kusangalala ndi ufulu umenewu, palibe amene ali pansi pa malire 
zokhazikitsidwa ndi lamulo ndi cholinga chokhacho cholimbikitsa kuzindikirika ndi kulemekeza ufulu ndi 
ufulu wa ena ndi cholinga chokwaniritsa zofuna za makhalidwe abwino, bata ndi mtendere wa anthu 
m’gulu la demokalase.  
3. 
Mulimonsemo, ufulu ndi ufuluwu ungagwiritsidwe ntchito motsutsana ndi zolinga ndi mfundo za 
United Nations.  
Ndime 30 °  
Palibe chilichonse mu Chidziwitso ichi chomwe chingatanthauze kuti chikutanthauza dziko lililonse,
gulu kapena munthu ali ndi ufulu wochita zinazake kapena kuchita zinazake zofuna kuwononga 
ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zafotokozedwa apa.
