Article 1-na a ni  
Mihring zawng zawng hi zalen taka piang, zahawmna leh dikna chanvo inang vek kan ni. Thiltihtheihna leh chhia leh tha hriatna neia pek an nih avangin an ni ngei tur a ni 
unau nihna rilru pu chungin inhmangaih tawn rawh.  
Article 2-na a ni  

Mihring zawng zawng hian he Thupuan-a puan chhuah dikna leh zalenna te hi kan hmang thei a, thliar hran lovin 
eng pawh, chu chu hnam, rawng, sex, tawng, sakhaw, politics emaw ngaihdan dang emaw, hnam chhuahna 
emaw, khawtlang, vanneihna, pianpui emaw, dinhmun dang emaw pawh ni se. 
Chubakah, ram politics, legal emaw international status emaw atanga thliar hran a ni lovang 
chu mi pianna ram, chu ram emaw, independent territory emaw pawh ni se, enkawltu hnuaia awm, mahni inrintawkna nei emaw, mi thenkhat hnuaia awm emaw
thuneihna tihkhawtlai (limitation of sovereignty) a ni.  
Article 3-na a ni 
Mimal tin hian nunna, zalenna leh mimal himna chanvo kan nei a.  
Article 4-na a ni  
Tumah salah leh bawihah an awm lo vang; saltanna leh salte chunga thil tih dan, chi hrang hrangah chuan 
khap a ni.  
Article 5-na a ni  
Mi tumah tihduhdah emaw, nunrawng, mihring nih lohna emaw, zahmawh taka tihduhdah emaw an tawk tur a ni lo.  
Article 6-na a ni 
Mimal zawng zawng hian, khawi hmunah pawh, an dan anga mi nihna hriat theihna an nei vek a ni.  
Article 7-na a ni  
Dan hmaah mi zawng zawng hi intluktlang vek an ni a, thliar hran lohvin danin humhalhna inang tlang an dawng vek a ni. Mi tin hian intluktlang taka venhimna chanvo kan nei a 
he Thupuan bawhchhia inthliarna eng pawh dodalna leh chutiang inthliarna thlentu eng pawh dodalna a ni.  
Article 8. A rilru a hah lutuk chuan a rilru a buai em em a

Constitution emaw dan emaw-in a pawm fundamental rights.  
Article 9. A rilru a hah lutuk chuan a rilru a buai em em a 
Tumah duh duhin man, hren leh ram danga thawn chhuah theih an ni lo.

ram chhung emaw ram pawn emaw dan hnuaia dan bawhchhia. Chutiang bawkin hremna na zawk pek a ni lovang
