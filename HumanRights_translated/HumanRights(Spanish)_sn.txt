Musangano Mukuru  
Inozivisa Universal Declaration of Human Rights iripo seyakanaka 
chinangwa chimwe chete icho vanhu vose nemarudzi vanofanira kuvavarira, kuitira kuti 
vanhu uye masangano, vachigara vachikwevera kurudziro kubva mazviri, 
kukurudzira, kuburikidza nekudzidzisa nedzidzo, kuremekedzwa kwekodzero idzi 
uye rusununguko, uye kuona, kuburikidza nenhanho dzebudiriro yenyika uye 
mutemo wepasi rose, kucherechedzwa kwayo kwepasirese uye kunoshanda uye kushandiswa, zvese pakati 
vanhu veNhengo dzeMatunhu pamwe chete neavo venzvimbo dzakaiswa 
pasi pesimba rayo.

Chisungo 11  
1. Munhu wese anenge apomerwa mhosva ane kodzero yekuti aonekwe kuti ndiani 
kusava nemhosva kusvikira anzi ane mhosva, nemurairo nemurairo 
kutongwa kweruzhinji uko vimbiso dzese dzakavimbiswa 
zvinodiwa pakudzivirira kwayo.  
2. Hapana achapiwa mhosva nokuda kwezviito kana kusiiwa panguva iyoyo 
kutongwa kwaisava mhosva pasi pemutemo wenyika kana wepasi rose. 
Uyewo hazvizopiwi chirango chakakomba kupfuura icho chinoshanda panguva ye 
kuparwa kwemhosva.  
Chisungo 12  
Hapana anozopindirwa zvisina tsarukano muhupenyu hwavo hwepachivande, mhuri yavo, yavo 
musha kana tsamba dzake, kana kurwisa rukudzo kana mukurumbira wake. 
(Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa nemutemo kubva mukukanganiswa kwakadai kana 
kurwisa.  
Chisungo 13  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yekufamba akasununguka uye kusarudza pekugara 
munharaunda yeHurumende.  
2. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokubuda munyika ipi zvayo, kusanganisira neyake, uye 
kuti adzokere kunyika yake.  
Chisungo 14  
1. Pakutambudzwa, munhu wese anekodzero yekutsvaga hupoteri, uye 
Nakidzwa nazvo, munyika ipi zvayo.  
 

 
2. Kodzero iyi inogona kusashandiswa mukupikisa kutongwa kwechokwadi 
yakatanga nemhosva dzinozivikanwa kana nezviito zvinopesana nezvinangwa uye
nheyo dzeUnited Nations.  
Chisungo 15  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yerudzi rwenyika.  
2. Hapana munhu achatorerwa nyika yake kana kodzero 
kuchinja rudzi.  
Chisungo 16  
1. Varume nevakadzi, kubva pazera rekuroora, vane kodzero, vasina 
chero ganhuriro pazvikonzero zvedzinza, nyika kana chitendero, kuroora 
ndokuwana mhuri; uye vachawana kodzero dzakaenzana maererano ne 
kuroorana, panguva yewanano uye kana muchato waparara.  
2. Kuburikidza chete nemvumo yemahara uye yakazara yevakaroorana vachauya vanogona 
kuroora.  
3. Mhuri chinhu chakasikwa uye chinokosha chenzanga uye ine 
kodzero yekuchengetedzwa kwenharaunda neHurumende.  
Chisungo 17  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yekuva nemidziyo, ega uye pamwe chete.  
2. Hapana achatorerwa pfuma yake nemadiro.  
Chisungo 18  
Munhu wese anekodzero yerusununguko rwekufunga, rwehana uye 
chitendero; Kodzero iyi inosanganisira rusununguko rwokuchinja chitendero kana kutenda, uyewo 
sorusununguko rwokuratidza chitendero chomunhu kana zvaanotenda, somunhu mumwe nomumwe uye 
pamwe chete, paruzhinji nepachivande, nekudzidzisa, kuita, 
kunamata nokuchengeta.  
 

 
Chisungo 19  
Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva nerusununguko rwokufunga zvaanoda uye zvaanotaura; izvi 
kodzero inosanganisira kusakanganiswa nekuda kwemaonero emunhu, kodzero yekuferefeta uye
kugamuchira ruzivo nemafungiro, uye kuaparadzira, pasina miganhu yemiganhu, 
nenzira ipi neipi yekutaura.  
Chisungo 20  
1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yokuva norusununguko rwokusangana nokusangana 
rugare.  
2. Hapana anogona kumanikidzwa kuva nhengo yesangano.

1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekutora chikamu muhurumende yenyika yake.

3. Kuda kwevanhu ndihwo hwaro hwesimba resimba reruzhinji; izvi

Chisungo 22  
Munhu wese, senhengo yenharaunda, anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa

yekodzero dzehupfumi, dzemagariro netsika, dzinokosha pakuremekedzwa kwavo 
uye kusununguka kusununguka kwehunhu hwavo.  
 

 
Chisungo 23  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yekushanda, kusarudza basa akasununguka, kuita 
akaenzana uye anogutsa mamiriro ebasa uye kudzivirirwa kubva 
urovha. 
2. Munhu wese anekodzero, pasina rusaruro, yekuenzana nemuhoro
Ndinoshanda zvakafanana.  
3. Munhu wese anoshanda anekodzero yekuwana muhoro wakaenzana uye

4. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekuumba mibatanidzwa uye kuva nhengo yemubatanidzwa 
kudzivirira zvido zvavo.

Chisungo 26  
1. Munhu wese anekodzero yedzidzo. Dzidzo inofanira kuva 
zvemahara, zvirinani maererano nedzidzo yepuraimari uye 
fundamental. Dzidzo yekutanga ichave inosungirwa. Murairo
tekinoroji uye nyanzvi ichafanirwa kuve yakajairika; kuwana zvidzidzo 
vakuru vachave vakafanana kune vese, zvichienderana nekukodzera kwavo.  
2. Dzidzo ichavavarira kukura kuzere kwehunhu 
kodzero dzevanhu nekusimbaradzwa kwekuremekedzwa kwekodzero dzevanhu uye 
rusununguko runokosha; ichasimudzira kunzwisisa, kushivirira uye 
ushamwari pakati pemarudzi ose nemadzinza ose kana mapoka ezvitendero; uye 
kukurudzira kuvandudzwa kweUnited Nations zviitiko zve 
kuchengetedza runyararo.  
3. Vabereki vachava nekodzero yekusarudza rudzi rwedzidzo 
zvichapiwa vana vavo.  
Chisungo 27  
1. (Chekutanga) - Munhu wese anekodzero yekutora chikamu chetsika nemagariro akasununguka 
yenharaunda, kunakidzwa nehunyanzvi uye kutora chikamu mukufambira mberi 
zvesainzi uye zvikomborero zvinobva mazviri.  
2. Munhu wese anekodzero yekuchengetedzwa kwehunhu hwakanaka uye 
zvinhu zvinoenderana nazvo nekuda kwezvigadzirwa zvesainzi, 
zvinyorwa kana mabasa eunyanzvi zvaari munyori wawo.  
Chisungo 28  
Munhu wese anekodzero yekuumba hurongwa hwemagariro nepasi rose

Chisungo 29  
 

 
1. Munhu wese ane mabasa ane chekuita nenharaunda, kubva mu 
anogona kusununguka uye zvizere kukudziridza unhu hwake.  
2. Mukushandisa kodzero dzavo uye mukunakidzwa norusununguko rwavo, vose
munhu anongove pasi pezvirambidzo zvakatemwa nemutemo 
nechinangwa chega chekuona kucherechedzwa nekuremekedzwa kwe 
kodzero uye rusununguko rwevamwe, uye kugutsa zvinodiwa zvakarurama 
zvehunhu, kurongeka kwevanhu uye kugara zvakanaka munharaunda 
zvegutsaruzhinji.  
3. Kodzero nerusununguko urwu hazvigone kushandiswa mune chero mamiriro ezvinhu 
kupokana nezvinangwa nemisimboti yeUnited Nations.
