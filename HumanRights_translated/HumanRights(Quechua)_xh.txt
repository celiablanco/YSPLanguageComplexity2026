NgoDisemba 10, 1948, iZizwe Ezimanyeneyo 
bahlanganisana, bakuvuma oko, bakuxela ebantwini 
amalungelo, okuyiyo yonke le nto ithethwayo kule ncwadana.  
Kule nkomfa kwaxutyushwa ngamalungelo abo bonke abantu 
ukwaziswa, ukufundiswa, nokufunda kuwo onke amazwe, 
kufuneka kwaziswe kuzo zonke iindidi zamaziko emfundo 
kunye neeyantlukwano phakathi kwezi zizwe.  
 
UKUNIKEZWA KWEZIZWE EZIMANYENEYO  
AMALUNGELO ABANTU BONKE 
Ukuqala kwe  
QAPHELA: Inkululeko noxolo ehlabathini 
ubomi bumiliselwe koku kulandelayo: Wonke umntu 
kuhle ukuba kungabikho mntu uchithayo oko umama wasizalayo 
kufuneka siphathwe kwaye siphathwe ngokulinganayo.  
KHANGELA: Abantu baye bafikelela kuzo zonke iintlobo zezinto ezimbi, 
nokuba abawazi ngokupheleleyo okanye abawazi ngokupheleleyo amalungelo oluntu 
singabahoyi, sonke sifuna ikamva 
iintsuku zokuphila kakuhle; kulo lonke uloyiko neembandezelo 
xa ubuhlwempu bupheliswe, wonke umntu uya kuba nenkululeko yokuthetha 
oko kusezintloko zethu, kwaye siya kwamkela ngenkalipho ulwazi olunjani 
kunye nento esiyifunayo.  
QAPHELA: Kufuneka kubekho uMkhuseli wamaLungelo oLuntu 
imithetho engakhethi buso 
nceda abacinezelweyo nabaxhatshaziweyo abafuna ukuthethelela amalungelo abo.
QAPHELA: Yenzelwe izizwe zehlabathi ukuba zakhe ubuhlobo 
kufuneka sinqwenele ezintliziyweni zethu naloo mnqweno 
kufuneka sichithachithe.  
PHAWULA: Amazwe angamalungu eZizwe Ezimanyeneyo akhankanyiwe kule mpapasho 
yaqinisekisa ukukholelwa kwabo kumalungelo e-Iliw kunye 
bavumile ukuba abantu bayalingana nokuba sisiphi na isini 
qonda ukuba unamalungelo afanayo. Zonke ezi zinto kakuhle 
sisazi ukuba inkuthazo yenkululeko yethu intle ngakumbi 
masisebenzele ukwenza ilizwe lethu libe likhulu.  
PHAWULA: Amazwe angamalungu eZizwe Ezimanyeneyo avumile 
amalungelo oluntu kunye nenkululeko zizonke 
ukuba yamkelwe yi  
QAPHELA NYA: Amalungelo oluntu nenkululeko amkelwa kakuhle 
ukuba kunjalo, yoba yinyaniso.  
AMALUNGELO OLUNTU IYAQHUBEKA KWINDLELA YOKUBANZI 
UKUBA KWAKU  
Iintlanga ezawufikela umnqophiso omkhulu zikule ncwadi 
ukuqaphela amalungelo oluntu jikelele, ukuze abantu 
naphina apho bahlala khona, bayayibona le qeliqa ngentlonipho nenkululeko 
ukufunda ukuphila; kwabanye kangangoko banako 
baya kwenza kwazeke ukuba zonke ezi zinto ziyinyani; ezi ziya kwenziwa 
ukuhlala kumazwe akumanyano okanye oku 
ikhusi leentlanga.  
OKUQALA (1) 
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile kwaye banamalungelo onke 
siyalingana. Ekubeni silumkile, sifana nentsapho eseluxolweni 
sityala ubomi kumntu wonke.  
ISIBINI (2)
Imigaqo yamalungelo equlethwe kolu xwebhu yeyethu sonke, 
akukhathaliseki nokuba uluphi uhlanga okanye isini somntu 
yaye akukhathaliseki nokuba uthetha njani okanye ukholelwa kuye nawuphi na uthixo 
okanye kuhlobo lorhulumente awayecinga ukuba uya kuba nalo 
nokuba ulihlwempu, usisityebi, nokuba unjani na.  
Zonke iintlanga, zikhululekile okanye azikho, zahlukene 
sinorhulumente, kwaye abanye baxhomekeke kwezinye izizwe, 
kodwa kuyo nayiphi na imeko akufuneki siphathwe ngokwahlukileyo.  
ISIQENDU SESITHATHU (3) 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lobomi nenkululeko 
kunye nokukhuselwa.  
ESINE (4) 
Akukho mntu unokuba likhoboka elihluthiweyo, akuvumelekanga ngandlela zonke 
ebukhobokeni kunye nokurhweba ngabantu.  
ESESIHLANU (5) 
Akukho namnye unokuva nakuphi na ukubandezeleka 
asinakohlwaywa ngokuhlazisa ubuntu bethu.  
LWESITHANDATHU (6) 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuchotshelwa ityala naphina apho akhoyo 
ukuqinisekiswa.  
LWESIXHENXE (7) 
Bonke abantu banamalungelo alinganayo phantsi komthetho 
ukuze kube njalo, umthetho kufuneka usikhusele ngokungenamkhethe. 
Sikwanelungelo lokuchasa thina ngokutyeshela ezi mfundiso 
 

 
kwabo bafuna ukuphakama. Sikwanelungelo lokufumana ezi mfundiso 
kunye nokukhuselwa ngumthetho xa kukho impazamo ngequbuliso.  
ESINE (8)
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuchotshelwa kwetyala ngendlela enobulungisa 
ukhuseleko kumalungelo ethu asemthethweni 
kufuneka sikhuseleke kwabo basenzayo.  
ISAHLUKO SEsithoba (9) 
Akukho mntu uvalelwayo engalazi ityala lakhe; 
ukuvalelwa okanye ukugxothwa.  
ISHUMI (10) 
Bonke abantu bayalingana ngobulungisa obungenamkhethe 
sinelungelo lokumanyelwa nelungelo lokuchotshelwa kwetyala 
umsebenzi wethu okanye ilungelo lethu lokwazi. Yintoni atyholwa ngayo 
ifanele ibonakaliswe kuthi, ukuze sifunde nokuba sikude.  
ISHUMI elinanye (11) 
1. Abantu abangenatyala bamsulwa kulo naluphi na ulwaphulo-mthetho ababekwa lona 
sinelungelo lokuzithethelela ngokomthetho 
side sifunyanwe sinetyala ematyaleni. Kukwakho netyala 
amalungelo ethu aqinisekisiwe kwaye akhuselwe ngokwenene 
ukuba babe ngabo.  
2. Ukungagwetyelwa umntu ngento ayenzileyo 
isohlwayo ukuba isenzo ngeentsuku eziphambi komthetho 
xa ephuma aze abonise ukuba lityala. Isohlwayo siya kuba seso 
kuphela kungohlwaywa ngumthetho ngemihla yesono sakhe.  
 

 
SHUMI LINESIBINI (12) 
Akukho mntu unokonakalisa ubomi bethu ngaphandle kwesizathu 
kunye neentsapho zethu, akanako ukungena ngelize 
emakhayeni ethu, kwaye iincwadi zethu azifanele ukuba zifumaneke. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa ngumthetho ngokuphazanyiswa.  
ISHUMI elinesithathu (13)
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululekileyo, 
sikwanelungelo lokufumana izindlu kwilizwe lethu.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lobuzwe okanye 
ukushiya naliphi na ilizwe kwaye ngokufanayo 
ukubuyela kwilizwe lethu.  
Ishumi elinesine (14) 
1. Ukuba umntu uyatshutshiswa njengoko urhulumente welizwe lakhe engatshutshisi 
kuba ecinga, ke unelungelo lokufudukela kwelinye ilizwe 
ukuba asabe, azimela.  
2. Utshutshiselwe ubugebenga bokwenyani kwilizwe lakhe 
umntu akanako ukuxhomekeka kumalungelo oluntu ngaphandle 
ukuba asabele kwilizwe elithile, ahlale khona ngokukhululekileyo.  
ISHUMI elinesihlanu (15) 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lobuzwe 
ukuba ngowethu.  
2. Eli lungelo alinakuhluthwa amalungelo alo ngaphandle kwesizathu. 
Sikwanelungelo lokwala ukutshintsha ubuzwe bethu 
umntu ozosinqanda.  
ISHUMI elinesithandathu (16) 
 

 
1. Xa indoda nomfazi befikelela kwiminyaka yokutshata, sele bekho 
ilungelo lethu lokutshata nokuba nosapho, hayi 
akukhathaliseki nokuba singoluphi na uhlanga okanye isizwe, enoba singamadoda okanye amabhinqa 
umfazi unamalungelo alinganayo emtshatweni wethu okulungileyo 
ukuphila nokwabelana.  
2. Indoda nomfazi abanakutshata ngaphandle kwemvume yabo.  
3. Ubuzali lilungelo losapho lonke kukhuseleko lukarhulumente 
sinelungelo, kuba silusapho olukhulu oluphuhlisa isizwe 
singabo.
Ishumi elinesixhenxe (17) 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuba yedwa okanye ngababini 
ukuba nepropathi nabantu.  
2 Akukho bani uya kuhluthwa impahla yakhe.  
ISHUMI elinesibhozo (18) 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucinga, 
isazela nakuwuphi na uthixo 
nokusenza sikholwe. Ngokwelo lungelo, sikhululekile 
ukwamkela inkolo nokukholelwa kuyo nayiphi na indlela 
ukwenza umsebenzi. Sinokukwenza sodwa okanye siliqela 
loo nkolelo kwaye inokufundiswa ekhaya okanye 
naphi na.  
ISHUMI elinesithoba (19) 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuvakalisa uluvo lwakhe 
sinelungelo kwezi zinto ngaphandle kwemida 
sinako ukwabelana ngeengcinga zethu kwaye samkele izimvo zabanye.  
AMASHUMI amabini (20) 
 

 
1. Sinelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlanganisana nokuhlanganisana 
nokwakha uxolo.  
2. Akukho mntu uya kunyanzelwa ukuba aye kuyo nayiphi na intlanganiso 
ukuba ngowokuzithandela.  
AMASHUMI amabini ananye (21) 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo kurhulumente welizwe lakhe 
ukuze sikhonze ngokwethu, mhlawumbi ngokobuqu okanye 
nokuba sikhetha into esiyifunayo.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo elilinganayo lokufikelela kwinkonzo karhulumente kwilizwe lakhe 
ukungena emakhayeni kwaye ufumane ukhathalelo.  
3. Amandla karhulumente kukuba bonke abantu bayawafuna
kamsinya nje sakuba sinike igunya, siyawuvuma loo mnqweno 
ngokuvota ngokukhululekileyo; kufuneka siyenze loo voti hayi omnye umntu 
siyazi ukuze sivotele into esiyifunayo.  
AMASHUMI amabini anesibini (22) 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lemfundo, impahla kunye 
ukukhuselwa kuzo zonke iindlela zethu ukuze yonke into ilunge 
ukuze sifike apha. Sifumana eso siqinisekiso ngokuthi: 
Urhulumente kufuneka alungelelane kwaye asenzele oko anokukwenza 
nabantu belizwe lethu, kufuneka asebenzele inzuzo yethu ngokwamandla akhe okanye 
kunye noncedo lwangaphandle.  
AMASHUMI amabini anesithathu (23) 
1. Abantu abanganyanisekanga banelungelo lenkululeko yonqulo, 
sinelungelo lokusebenza nokuba asiphangeli 
ukuba afune, afumane, ukuba akukho bani uthandayo 
singagxothwa na kwimisebenzi yethu.  
 

 
2. Abantu banelungelo lokuthetha ngokukhululekileyo 
ukuba sihlawulwe ngokomsebenzi wethu.  
3. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuhamba nokuhlala phakathi kwemida yeLizwe ngalinye 
ukuze siwufumane ngokwemisebenzi yethu elungileyo 
intlalo-ntle yentsapho yonke; kungenjalo kufuneka afumane uncedo 
yabaxhasi abaphakanyiselwe le njongo.  
4. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokunxulumana nabanye 
ilungelo lethu lokuseka okanye ukuba ngamaqela anjalo 
ukuba siziku.  
AMASHUMI amabini anesine (24) 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphumla nokuphumla
sinelungelo lokuncuma, kwaye sinelungelo lokuqhubeka kangangoko sinako 
ukusebenza emini nokuthatha iholide yethu. 
AMASHUMI amabini anesihlanu (25) 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lomgangatho owaneleyo wokuphila yena nosapho lwakhe 
wondliwe, unxibe kakuhle kwaye usempilweni. Unelungelo, naye 
sinelungelo lokukhuselwa, kwanokuba sineziphene, sifelwe ngabahlolokazi, okanye singabafelwa 
nokuba umdala okanye uselula; sikwanelungelo 
nokuba asinakufumana umsebenzi okanye ngequbuliso sinento yokuzixhasa 
lokukhuselwa kwimeko yelahleko.  
2. Abafazi abakhulelweyo nabantwana banamalungelo 
kufuneka zigcinwe ngokufanelekileyo. Utshatile okanye akunjalo 
bonke abantwana banamalungelo alinganayo nokuba bazalelwe emtshatweni 
ukukhuselwa.  
AMASHUMI amabini anesithandathu (26) 
 

 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lemfundo. Akunjalo kwaphela 
Isikolo samabanga aphantsi sisimahla, ngoko ke kunyanzelekile kuye wonke umntu onesikolo samabanga aphantsi 
ukukwazi. Ngokwenqaku lethu elihle, konke kungaphezulu 
singakwazi ukuzifundisa ukuze sifezekise iinjongo zethu.  
2. Isiphumo semfundo siya kuba yintlalontle yabantu nabo bonke 
amalungelo akhe aqondwa kakuhle ukuze inkululeko yakhe ihlonelwe 
ukuba ifumaneke. Kufuneka sizole kwaye sivane 
nezizwe, nezizwe, nakuwuphi na uthixo 
kunye namakholwa. Kufuneka kwakhona sixhase iZizwe eziManyeneyo 
uxolo emhlabeni.  
3. Abazali banelungelo eliphambili kwimfundo
nokwamkela usapho lwethu.  
AMASHUMI AMASHUMI ANESIXHENXE (27) 
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuhamba nokuhlala phakathi kwemida yeLizwe ngalinye 
ukusinceda kuyo nayiphi na imeko. Sinokunceda ngandlela zonke 
ilizwe lethu liphuhle kakuhle kwaye sinelungelo 
ukwamkela into esinayo isixeko.  
2. Ukuba siye sasusa ngengqondo into yexabiso okanye 
sinokwenza into engazange yenziwe mntu ngaphambili 
sinelungelo kwixabiso lezo ndalo 
ukukhusela ekhoyo.  
AMASHUMI amabini anesibhozo (28) 
Sinamalungelo kwilizwe lethu nakuwo onke amazwe 
ukuba ngumbutho wamalungelo oluntu, ukuze konke oku 
ukufezekisa amalungelo ethu kunye nenkululeko.  
AMASHUMI amabini anesithoba (29) 
 

 
1. Wonke umntu ukhululekile kwilizwe lethu, ke ngoko kwilizwe lethu 
sinomsebenzi.  
2. Ekuphela kwendlela yokunyanzelisa amalungelo okanye inkululeko yethu ngumthetho 
zinokusithintela, njengamalungelo abanye okanye inkululeko 
ubukho babo abuxhatshazwa, ukuze ungquzulwano lungabikho 
ukuba ifumaneke. Ukuze wonke umntu abe kwilizwe lakhe 
ukuhlala ngoxolo.  
3. La malungelo kunye neenkululeko mazingaze nakanye zisetyenziswe 
inokuba, ukuba nangaluphi na uhlobo lwezizwe ezimanyeneyo 
ukuba kuchasene nentando yakhe.  
AMASHUMI AMATHATHU (30) 
Akukho mntu okanye urhulumente onokuthembela kwesi sicatshulwa
singenza nantoni na, amalungelo neenkululeko zethu 
ukuhlutha ilizwe.
