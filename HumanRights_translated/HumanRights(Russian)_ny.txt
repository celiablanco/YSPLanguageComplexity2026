Universal Declaration of Human Rights 
Adavomerezedwa ndikulengezedwa ndi chigamulo cha General Assembly 217 A (III) cha Disembala 10 
1948. 
ZOYAMBA 
Pomwe kuzindikira ulemu wobadwa nawo wa onse 
mamembala a banja laumunthu, ndi ufulu wawo wofanana ndi wosakanizidwa 
maziko a ufulu, chilungamo ndi mtendere wapadziko lonse; ndi  
Pomwe kunyozera ndi kunyoza ufulu wa anthu 
zatsogolera ku zinthu zankhanza zomwe zimakwiyitsa chikumbumtima cha anthu, ndi 
kuti kulengedwa kwa dziko limene anthu adzakhala ndi ufulu wolankhula ndi 
zikhulupiriro ndipo adzakhala opanda mantha ndi kusowa, kulengeza ngati 
chikhumbo chachikulu cha anthu; ndi 
Pamene kuli kofunikira kuti ufulu wa anthu utetezedwe 
mphamvu zamalamulo kuwonetsetsa kuti munthu sakakamizidwa 
njira, ngati njira yomaliza, kupandukira nkhanza ndi 
kuponderezana; ndi  
Poganizira kuti ndikofunikira kulimbikitsa chitukuko 
mgwirizano waubwenzi pakati pa anthu; ndi  
Pamene anthu a United Nations atsimikizira mu 
Kupereka chikhulupiliro chake mu ufulu wachibadwidwe, ulemu ndi ulemu 
umunthu waumunthu ndi kufanana kwa amuna ndi akazi ndipo anaganiza 
kulimbikitsa kupita patsogolo kwa anthu ndikuwongolera mikhalidwe ya moyo pansi 
ufulu wokulirapo; ndi 
Pomwe Mayiko Amembala adzipereka kuthandiza, mu 
mgwirizano ndi United Nations, padziko lonse lapansi 
kulemekeza ndi kusunga ufulu wa anthu ndi kumasulika kofunikira; Ndipo
Pomwe pali kumvetsetsa kwamtundu wa maufuluwa ndi 
ufulu ndiwofunikira kwambiri kuti izi zitheke 
udindo, 
 

 
General Assembly,  
imalengeza Chikalata Chapadziko Lonse cha Ufulu Wachibadwidwe ngati 
ntchito imene anthu onse ndi 
kuti munthu aliyense ndi gulu lililonse la anthu, 
Pokumbukira Chilengezo ichi nthawi zonse, yesetsani 
maphunziro ndi maphunziro kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu ndi kumasuka ndi 
kuonetsetsa, kudzera m'mayiko ndi padziko lonse lapansi 
ntchito, kuzindikira konsekonse komanso kothandiza komanso kukhazikitsa 
pakati pa anthu a mayiko omwe ali m'bungweli komanso pakati pa anthu 
madera omwe ali m'manja mwawo.  
Ndime 1  
Anthu onse amabadwa aufulu ndi ofanana mu ulemu ndi ufulu. 
Amapatsidwa nzeru ndi chikumbumtima ndipo ayenera kuchita zinthu mogwirizana. 
bwenzi mu mzimu wa ubale.  
Ndime 2 
Munthu aliyense akhale ndi ufulu ndi kumasuka, 
zolengezedwa ndi Chidziwitso ichi, popanda aliyense 
kusiyana monga mtundu, mtundu, kugonana, chinenero, chipembedzo, 
zikhulupiriro zandale kapena zina, zadziko kapena zachikhalidwe 
chiyambi, katundu, kalasi kapena zina.  
Komanso, palibe kusiyana koyenera kuchitidwa pamaziko 
ndale, zamalamulo kapena zapadziko lonse lapansi za dziko kapena 
gawo limene munthu ali, mosasamala kanthu kuti ali
kaya gawolo ndi lodziimira palokha, lodalirika, losadzilamulira kapena 
mwanjira ina malire mu ulamuliro wake. 
Ndime 3 
 

 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi moyo, ufulu ndi chinsinsi 
kusalakwa.  
Ndime 4 
Palibe amene ayenera kukhala kapolo kapena ukapolo; 
Ukapolo ndi malonda a akapolo ndi zoletsedwa m’njira zosiyanasiyana.  
Ndime 5 
Palibe amene ayenera kuzunzidwa kapena kuchitidwa nkhanza, mopanda umunthu kapena 
kunyozetsa ndi chilango. 
Ndime 6 
Munthu aliyense, kulikonse komwe ali, ali ndi ufulu wozindikiridwa 
umunthu walamulo. 
Ndime 7 
Anthu onse ndi ofanana pamaso pa lamulo ndipo ali ndi ufulu, popanda kusiyana kulikonse 
chitetezo chofanana cha lamulo. Anthu onse ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana kwa aliyense 
tsankho lililonse lophwanya Chidziwitso ichi, komanso kuchokera 
chosonkhezera kusankhana koteroko.  
Ndime 8 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kubwezeredwa moyenera 
makhothi oyenerera pamilandu yophwanya mfundo zake 
ufulu woperekedwa kwa iye ndi malamulo oyendetsera dziko lino.  
Ndime 9 
 

 
Palibe amene angamangidwe, kutsekeredwa m'ndende popanda chifukwa kapena 
kuthamangitsidwa.  
Ndime 10 
Munthu aliyense, kuti adziwe za ufulu wake ndi ntchito zake komanso kuti 
kutsimikizira kutsimikizika kwa milandu yomwe adayimbidwa, 
ali ndi ufulu, pamaziko a kufanana kwathunthu, kukhala ndi mlandu wake 
kuwunikiridwa poyera komanso motsatira zofunikira zonse zachilungamo 
khoti loyima palokha komanso lopanda tsankho.  
Ndime 11
1. Aliyense woimbidwa mlandu wachita 
ufulu woganiziridwa kuti ndi wosalakwa mpaka atapezeka kuti ndi wolakwa 
idzakhazikitsidwa mwalamulo kupyolera mu msonkhano wa anthu 
zochitika zomwe amapatsidwa mwayi uliwonse 
kwa chitetezo. 
2. Palibe amene angaimbidwe mlandu pazifukwa zake 
kuchita chilichonse kapena kusachitapo kanthu, zomwe mwa 
nthawi ya ntchito yawo sinakhale milandu malinga ndi dziko 
malamulo kapena malamulo apadziko lonse lapansi. Sizingathekenso kukakamizidwa 
chilango chokhwima kwambiri kuposa chimene chikadaperekedwa 
nthawi yomwe mlanduwo udachitika. 
Ndime 12 
Palibe amene angasokonezedwe mwachisawawa pazake komanso 
moyo wabanja, kuukira mosasamala pa kukhulupirika kwa 
nyumba yake, chinsinsi cha makalata ake kapena pa ulemu ndi mbiri yake. 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutetezedwa ndi lamulo kuti asasokonezedwe ndi izi 
kapena kuukira koteroko.  
 

 
Ndime 13 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kuyenda momasuka ndi kusankha 
malo okhala m'chigawo chilichonse. 
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga locoka m'dziko lina lililonse, kuphatikizapo dziko lake 
iwo, nabwerera ku dziko lawo. 
Ndime 14 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wopeza chitetezo ku mazunzo 
mayiko ena ndikupezerapo mwayi pothawirako. 
2. Ufuluwu sungagwiritsidwe ntchito ngati tikuzunzidwa, mu 
zenizeni zochokera ku ntchito yosagwirizana ndi ndale
umbanda kapena kuchita zosemphana ndi zolinga ndi mfundo zake 
United Nations. 
Ndime 15 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala nzika. 
2. Palibe amene angalandidwe mtundu wake mwachisawawa kapena 
ufulu wosintha kukhala nzika. 
Ndime 16 
1. Amuna ndi akazi omwe afika zaka zambiri ali ndi ufulu popanda 
zoletsa zilizonse zochokera mtundu, dziko kapena chipembedzo 
kukwatiwa ndi kuyamba banja. Amasangalala 
maufulu ofanana pa nkhani ya ukwati, nthawi 
mikhalidwe pa nthawi yaukwati ndi m’nthawi ya kutha kwake. 
2. Ukwati ukhoza kuthetsedwa mwaufulu ndi chilolezo chonse 
onse okwatirana. 
3. Banja ndi gawo lachilengedwe komanso loyambira la anthu ndipo latero 
ufulu wotetezedwa kwa anthu ndi boma. 
 

 
Ndime 17 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi katundu ngati payekha; 
ndi pamodzi ndi ena. 
2. Palibe amene akuyenera kulandidwa katundu wake mwachisawawa. 
Ndime 18  
Munthu ali yense ali ndi ufulu wa kuganiza, chikumbumtima ndi chipembedzo; izi ndi 
ufulu umaphatikizapo ufulu wosintha chipembedzo kapena chikhulupiriro ndi ufulu 
kudzinenera chipembedzo kapena zikhulupiriro, kaya payekha kapena pagulu 
ena, pagulu kapena payekha, pakuphunzitsa, kupembedza ndi 
kuchita miyambo yachipembedzo ndi miyambo.  
Ndime 19 
Munthu ali yense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi maganizo omasuka 
kuwafotokozera; ufulu umenewu umaphatikizapo ufulu popanda kusokonezedwa 
kumamatira ku kukhudzika kwa munthu ndi ufulu wofunafuna, kulandira ndi
kufalitsa zidziwitso ndi malingaliro mwanjira iliyonse posatengera 
malire a boma. 
Ndime 20 
1. Munthu ali yense ali ndi ufulu kusonkhana mwamtendere ndi 
mayanjano. 
2. Palibe amene adzakakamizidwe kulowa mgulu lililonse. 
Ndime 21  
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa kayendetsedwe ka kayendetsedwe kake 
dziko mwachindunji kapena kudzera mwa osankhidwa mwaufulu 
oimira. 
 

 
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wofanana ndi anthu 
utumiki m'dziko lanu. 
3. Zofuna za anthu ziyenera kukhala maziko a ulamuliro wa boma; chifuniro ichi 
ayenera kupeza mawu mu periodic ndi 
zisankho zachinyengo zomwe zikuyenera kuchitika 
ufulu wapadziko lonse ndi wofanana mwa voti yachinsinsi 
kapena kudzera mu mafomu ena ofanana omwe amapereka 
ufulu wovota. 
Ndime 22 
Munthu aliyense, monga membala wa gulu, ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi chikhalidwe cha anthu 
kupereka ndi kukhazikitsa zofunikira kuti zisungidwe 
ulemu ndi chitukuko chaulere cha ufulu wa umunthu wake pazachuma, 
madera a chikhalidwe ndi chikhalidwe kupyolera mu zoyesayesa za dziko 
ndi mgwirizano wapadziko lonse lapansi komanso molingana ndi kapangidwe kake ndi 
chuma cha dziko lililonse.  
Ndime 23 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kugwira ntchito, kusankha ntchito mwaufulu, 
ku malo abwino ndi abwino a ntchito ndi chitetezo ku 
ulova. 
2. Munthu aliyense, popanda tsankho, ali ndi ufulu 
malipiro ofanana pa ntchito yofanana.
3. Wogwira ntchito aliyense ali ndi ufulu kuchita chilungamo 
malipiro okhutiritsa opereka zabwino 
kukhalapo kwa munthu kwa iye yekha ndi banja lake, ndi 
kuwonjezeredwa, ngati kuli kofunikira, ndi njira zina zochezera 
kupereka. 
4. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kukhazikitsa mabungwe ogwira ntchito ndi 
alowe m'mabungwe kuti ateteze zofuna zawo. 
 

 
Ndime 24 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kupumula ndi kusanguluka, kuphatikizapo ufulu wopeza bwino 
kuchepa kwa maola ogwirira ntchito komanso tchuthi cholipidwa nthawi ndi nthawi.  
Ndime 25 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo wabwino, kuphatikizapo 
chakudya, zovala, nyumba, chithandizo chamankhwala ndi chikhalidwe chofunikira 
ntchito zofunika kusunga thanzi ndi 
ubwino wake ndi banja lake, ndi ufulu wotetezedwa 
vuto la ulova, matenda, kulumala, umasiye, 
ukalamba kapena vuto lina lakutaya moyo chifukwa cha 
mikhalidwe yodalira pa iye. 
2. Uchembere ndi ukhanda umapereka ufulu wachisamaliro chapadera ndi 
thandizo. Ana onse obadwa m'banja kapena kunja kwa banja ayenera 
sangalalani ndi chitetezo chofanana cha anthu. 
Ndime 26 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu wophunzira. Maphunziro ayenera 
khalani omasuka mpaka momwe mungayambire komanso 
maphunziro onse. Maphunziro a pulaimale ayenera kukhala 
mokakamizidwa. Maphunziro aukadaulo ndi maphunziro aukadaulo 
ziyenera kupezeka kwa onse, ndipo maphunziro apamwamba ayenera kukhala
kupezeka kwa onse molingana ndi luso la munthu aliyense. 
2. Maphunziro ayenera kukhala ndi cholinga cha chitukuko chokwanira 
umunthu waumunthu ndi kuonjezera kulemekeza ufulu wa anthu 
ndi ufulu wofunikira. Maphunziro ayenera kulimbikitsa 
kumvetsetsana, kulolerana ndi ubwenzi pakati pa anthu onse, 
magulu amitundu ndi azipembedzo, ndipo ayenera kulimbikitsa 
 

 
ntchito za United Nations posamalira 
mtendere. 
3. Makolo ali ndi ufulu woika patsogolo posankha mtundu wa maphunziro 
ana awo aang’ono. 
Ndime 27  
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa chikhalidwe 
moyo wa anthu, kusangalala ndi luso, kutenga nawo mbali mu sayansi 
kupita patsogolo ndi kusangalala ndi mapindu ake. 
2. Munthu aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutetezedwa ku chikhalidwe chake 
zokonda zakuthupi zochokera ku sayansi, 
zolemba kapena zaluso zomwe iye ndiye mlembi wake 
ndi. 
Ndime 28 
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi mtendere pa umoyo wa munthu, 
momwe ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zafotokozedwa m'Chikalatachi zikhonza 
kukhazikitsidwa kwathunthu.  
Ndime 29 
1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi udindo pagulu 
Kukula kwaufulu ndi kokwanira kwa umunthu wake ndiko kotheka. 
2. Pogwiritsa ntchito ufulu ndi kumasuka kwawo, munthu aliyense ayenera 
azingotsatira zoletsa zomwe zakhazikitsidwa 
lamulo ndi cholinga chokhacho chowonetsetsa kuzindikirika koyenera ndi 
kulemekeza ufulu ndi kumasuka kwa ena ndi kukhutitsidwa kwa chilungamo 
zofunikira zamakhalidwe, dongosolo la anthu ndi zonse
ubwino m'gulu la demokalase. 
 

 
3. Kugwiritsiridwa ntchito kwa ufulu ndi kumasuka kumeneku sikuyenera kuchitika konse 
zikutsutsana ndi zolinga ndi mfundo za United Nations 
Mayiko. 
Ndime 30  
Palibe chilichonse mu Chidziwitso ichi chomwe chidzamasuliridwe ngati 
kupereka ku boma lililonse, gulu la anthu kapena anthu 
ufulu wochita chilichonse kapena kuchita chilichonse, 
cholinga chake ndikuwononga ufulu ndi ufulu zomwe zafotokozedwa m'nkhaniyi 
Zolengeza.
