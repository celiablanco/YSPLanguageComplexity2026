IsiBhengezo seHlabathi samaLungelo oLuntu

Ukuqonda isidima esizalwa naso kunye namalungelo alinganayo nangenakohluthwa kuwo onke amalungu oluntu kusisiseko senkululeko, ubulungisa noxolo ehlabathini; ukungakhathali nokujongelwa phantsi kwamalungelo abantu kuye kwakhokelela kwizenzo zenkohlakalo eziye zacaphukisa isazela soluntu; nokufika kwehlabathi apho umntu anokonwabela inkululeko yokuthetha nenkolelo nenkululeko kuloyiko nasekusweleni kuye kwabhengezwa njengowona mnqweno uphakamileyo wabo bonke abantu, hleze umntu anyanzelwe ukuba avukele njengelinge lokugqibela lokuchasa ubuzwilakhe nengcinezelo; Kubalulekile ukuba amalungelo oluntu akhuselwe ngumthetho, kwaye kubalulekile ukukhuthaza ukuphuhliswa kobudlelwane bobuhlobo phakathi kwezizwe. KuMqulu wawo, onke amalungu eZizwe eziManyeneyo aqinisekisa kwakhona ukukholelwa kwawo kumalungelo oluntu asisiseko, isidima kunye nokuxabiseka komntu, kunye namalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabasetyhini, kwaye azibophelele ekukhuthazeni inkqubela phambili yentlalo kunye nemigangatho engcono yokuphila kwinkululeko ebanzi. AMazwe angamaLungu azibophelele ekusebenzisaneni neZizwe eziManyeneyo ukukhuthaza intlonipho yehlabathi jikelele kunye nokuthotyelwa kwamalungelo oluntu kunye neenkululeko ezisisiseko. Ekubeni ukuqonda okuqhelekileyo kwamalungelo kunye nenkululeko kubaluleke kakhulu ekuphunyezweni okupheleleyo kwalo Mnqophiso, iNdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo, ngoko ke, izama ukukhuthaza ukuhlonelwa kwala malungelo kunye nenkululeko ngokufunda kunye nemfundo phakathi kwabo bonke abantu kunye namaziko oluntu, ehlala ekhumbula esi sibhengezo, kwaye uzama, ngokusebenzisa amanyathelo aqhubela phambili esizwe kunye namazwe ngamazwe, ukuqinisekisa ukuqondwa kwehlabathi kunye nokusebenza ngokufanelekileyo kwamalungelo kunye nenkululeko yabantu kunye nenkululeko yamazwe kunye nenkululeko. abantu bemimandla ephantsi kolawulo lwabo; Sibhengeza i-Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Inqaku loku-1

Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile kwaye belingana ngesidima nangamalungelo. Abantu ngokwendalo banikwe ingqiqo nesazela kwaye kufuneka basebenze ngomoya wobuzalwana omnye komnye. Inqaku lesi-2 Wonke umntu unelungelo kuwo onke amalungelo neenkululeko ezicaciswe kwesi siBhengezo, ngaphandle kocalu-calulo naluphi na uhlobo, olunjengobuhlanga, ibala, isini, ulwimi, inkolo, ipolitiki okanye olunye uluvo, ubuzwe okanye imvelaphi, umhlaba, ukuzalwa okanye olunye uhlobo. Ngaphezu koko, akusayi kubakho lucalucalulo olusekelwe kwisiseko sezopolitiko, somthetho okanye isimo samazwe ngamazwe selizwe okanye ummandla womntu, kungakhathaliseki ukuba ilizwe okanye ummandla lowo ngumbuso ozimeleyo, i-trusteeship, ummandla ongazilawulanga, okanye uxhomekeke kwezinye izithintelo kulawulo lwalo. Inqaku lesi-3 Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko nokhuseleko. Inqaku lesi-4 Akukho mntu uya kugcinwa ebukhoboka okanye ebukhobokeni. Zonke iintlobo zobukhoboka kunye norhwebo ngamakhoboka azivumelekanga. Inqaku lesi-5 Akukho mntu uya kungcungcuthekiswa okanye ngenkohlakalo, ngempatho engenabuntu okanye ethotywa isidima okanye isohlwayo. Inqaku lesi-6 Wonke umntu unelungelo lokwamkelwa njengomntu phambi komthetho kuyo yonke indawo. Inqaku lesi-7
Bonke abantu bayalingana phambi komthetho kwaye banelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo lomthetho ngaphandle kocalucalulo. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa ngokulinganayo kulo naluphi na ucalucalulo olwaphulwa kwesi siBhengezo kunye nakuko nakuphi na ukuphembelela olo calucalulo. Inqaku lesi-8 Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufumana isiqabu esisebenzayo kwiinkundla zasekhaya ezifanelekileyo ngenxa yezenzo ezinyhasha amalungelo angundoqo anikezelwa ngumGaqo-siseko okanye ngumthetho. Inqaku lesi-9 Akukho mntu uya kubanjwa ngokungekho mthethweni, avalelwe okanye agxothwe. Inqaku le‐10 Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuchotshelwa enkundleni ngokungenamkhethe nangokungakhethi cala phambi kwenkundla yamatyala ezimeleyo okanye inkundla yamatyala engakhethi cala ukuze kumiswe amalungelo akhe, izinyanzeliso kunye nezityholo zolwaphulo-mthetho abekwa zona. ISiqendu 11 1. Wonke umntu otyholwa ngolwaphulo-mthetho unelungelo lokuthathwa njengomntu ongenatyala ade abe ufunyenwe enetyala ngokwasemthethweni kwinkundla yamatyala apho kuqinisekiswa yonke into efunekayo ukuze aziphendulele. 2. Akukho mntu uya kufunyanwa enetyala ngenxa yaso nasiphi na isenzo okanye ukushiyiwa kwesenzo, okungazange kube bulwaphulo-mthetho phantsi komthetho wesizwe okanye wamazwe ngamazwe, ngexesha lokwenziwa kwesenzo. Ukongeza, akukho sohlwayo sinzima siya kuwiswa kunesohlwayo ebesinokuthi sisetyenziswe ngexesha lolwaphulo-mthetho. ISiqendu 12 Akukho namnye oya kuphazanyiswa kubomi bakhe babucala, kwikhaya, kwindawo ahlala kuyo okanye kwimbalelwano yakhe, okanye kugxekwe ngesidima nesidima sakhe. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa ngumthetho ngokuchasene nokuphazanyiswa okanye ukugxekwa. Inqaku le-13

1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhamba nokuhlala kwilizwe lakhe. 2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokushiya nakweliphi na ilizwe, kuquka nelakhe, nelungelo lokubuyela kwilizwe lakhe. Inqaku le-14 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufuna nokufumana indawo yokhuseleko kwamanye amazwe kwintshutshiso. 2. Eli lungelo alinakuqinisekiswa kumatyala okutshutshisa amatyala angengawo apolitiki ngokwenene okanye achasene neenjongo nemigaqo yoMbutho weZizwe eziManyeneyo. Inqaku le-15 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lobuzwe. 2. Akukho mntu uya kuhluthwa ubuzwe bakhe okanye avinjwe ilungelo lokutshintsha ubuzwe bakhe. Inqaku le-16 1. Amadoda nabafazi abadala banelungelo lokutshata babe nosapho ngaphandle kwezithintelo ezisekelwe kubuhlanga, ubuzwe okanye inkolo. Banelungelo lokonwabela amalungelo alinganayo mayela nomtshato, ngexesha lomtshato nasemva kokuqhawulwa komtshato. 2. Umtshato usekwe kuphela ngemvume ekhululekileyo nepheleleyo yabatshati bexesha elizayo. 3. Usapho yinxalenye yendalo kunye nesiseko soluntu kwaye inelungelo lokukhuselwa luluntu kunye norhulumente. Inqaku le-17 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuba nezinto ezizezakhe eyedwa nangokudibeneyo. 2 Akukho bani uya kuhluthwa impahla yakhe. Inqaku le-18
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucinga, isazela nenkolo. Eli lungelo liquka inkululeko yokutshintsha inkolo okanye inkolelo kabani nokubonakalisa inkolo okanye inkolo kabani, eyedwa okanye ekunye nabanye, esidlangalaleni okanye emfihlekweni, ngezenzo zonqulo, unqulo nokuthobela. Inqaku le‐19 Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yoluvo nokuthetha. La malungelo aquka inkululeko yokubamba izimvo ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka kunye nenkululeko yokufuna, ukufumana kunye nokuhambisa ulwazi kunye neengcamango ngawo nawaphi na amajelo eendaba, nokuba yeyiphi na imida. Inqaku la‐20 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yeendibano zoxolo nokunxulumana. 2. Akukho mntu uya kunyanzelwa ukuba athathe inxaxheba kuwo nawuphi na umbutho. Inqaku la‐21 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba kurhulumente welizwe lakhe, ngokuthe ngqo okanye ngabameli abanyulwe ngokukhululekileyo. 2. Wonke umntu unamalungelo alinganayo okuba nesikhundla kurhulumente kwilizwe lakhe. 3. Intando yabantu sisiseko samandla karhulumente. Oku kuya kubonakaliswa ngonyulo lwarhoqo nolwenyani ngenkqubo yokuvota eyimfihlo okanye ngokulinganayo ngokukhululekileyo nangokulinganayo. Inqaku lama-22 Wonke umntu, njengelungu loluntu, unelungelo kukhuseleko lwezentlalo nelungelo lokuqonda, ngeenzame zesizwe nangentsebenziswano yamazwe ngamazwe, nangokungqinelana nentlangano nezibonelelo zeLizwe ngalinye, amalungelo ezoqoqosho, ezentlalo nenkcubeko ayimfuneko ukuze kuphuhliswe ngokukhululekileyo isidima sakhe nobuntu bakhe. Inqaku lama-23

1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukukhetha ngokukhululekileyo umsebenzi wakhe, ubulungisa neemeko ezifanelekileyo zokusebenza kunye nokukhuseleka ekungaqeshwani. 2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufumana imbuyekezo elinganayo ngomsebenzi olinganayo ngaphandle kocalucalulo. 3. Wonke umntu osebenzayo unelungelo lomvuzo ofanelekileyo nonengenelo, oqinisekisa kuye yena nosapho lwakhe ukuphila okuhambelana nesidima sobuntu kwaye wongezwa, ukuba kuyimfuneko, ngezinye iindlela zokhuseleko loluntu. 4. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuseka kunye nokujoyina umbutho wabasebenzi ukukhusela iimfuno zakhe. Inqaku la‐24 Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphumla nokuba nexesha lokuphumla, kubandakanywa nemiqathango emiselweyo yeeyure zokusebenza nekhefu elihlawulelwayo rhoqo. Inqaku lama-25 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lomgangatho wokuphila ofanelekileyo ngokwempilo yakhe nentlalo-ntle yakhe nosapho lwakhe, kubandakanywa ukutya, impahla, indawo yokuhlala, unyango kunye nentlalo-ntle eyimfuneko, kunye nelungelo lokhuseleko xa engakwazi ukuphila ngenxa yokungasebenzi, ukugula, isiphene, ukusweleka kweqabane, ukwaluphala okanye ezinye iimeko ezingaphaya kwamandla akhe. 2. Oomama nabantwana banelungelo lokhuseleko olukhethekileyo kunye nenkxaso. Bonke abantwana banandipha ukhuseleko olufanayo loluntu kungakhathaliseki isimo sabo. Inqaku lama-26 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo mayibe simahla, ubuncinane kumabanga aphantsi nasezantsi. Imfundo yaseprayimari ifanele ibe sisinyanzelo. Imfundo yobugcisa neyobugcisa kufuneka ifikeleleke kumntu wonke, kwaye imfundo ephakamileyo ifanele ukufikeleleka ngokulinganayo kuye wonke umntu ngokusekelwe kukufaneleka kwayo. 2. Imfundo ijolise ekuphuhlisweni ngokupheleleyo kobuntu nokomeleza intlonipho yamalungelo oluntu kunye neziseko zenkululeko. Imfundo ikhuthaza ukuqonda, ukunyamezelana kunye
Kufuneka sikhuthaze ubuhlobo kwaye sikhuthaze imisebenzi yeZizwe eziManyeneyo ukugcina uxolo. 3. Abazali banokubaluleka kokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo efundiswa abantwana babo. Inqaku lama-27 1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba ngokukhululekileyo kubomi benkcubeko yoluntu, onwabele ubugcisa, kwaye abe nesabelo kwinkqubela phambili yenzululwazi kunye neenzuzo zayo. 2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa kwizinto zomoya nezezinto eziphathekayo ezivela kwiimveliso zenzululwazi, zoncwadi okanye zobugcisa aziyileyo. Inqaku la‐28 Wonke umntu unelungelo lentlalontle kunye neyezizwe ngezizwe apho amalungelo nenkululeko ecaciswe kwesi siBhengezo anokuphunyezwa ngokupheleleyo. Inqaku la‐29 1. Wonke umntu unoxanduva eluntwini apho ubuntu bakhe bunokukhula ngokukhululekileyo nangokupheleleyo. 2. Wonke umntu, ekusebenziseni amalungelo nenkululeko yakhe, uxhomekeke kwizithintelo ezisekwe ngumthetho kuphela ngenjongo yokuqinisekisa ukuba amalungelo nenkululeko yabanye ayamkelwa yaye ayahlonelwa, nangeenjongo zokuhlangabezana neemfuno ezisemthethweni zokuziphatha okuhle, ucwangco loluntu nentlalo-ntle jikelele kuluntu olulawulwa ngokwentando yesininzi. 3. La malungelo neenkululeko azisayi kusetyenziswa naphantsi kwazo naziphi na iimeko ngokuchasene neenjongo nemithetho-siseko yoMbutho weZizwe eziManyeneyo. ISiqendu 30. Akukho nto kwesi siBhengezo eya kuthathwa njengokugunyazisa naliphi na iLizwe, iqela okanye umntu ukuba enze nawuphi na umsebenzi okanye athabathe naliphi na inyathelo elinenjongo yokutshabalalisa nawaphi na amalungelo neenkululeko ekuthethwa ngazo kwesi siBhengezo.
