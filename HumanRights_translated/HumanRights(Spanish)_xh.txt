IsiBhengezo seHlabathi samaLungelo oLuntu 
Yamkelwa yaza yabhengezwa yiNdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo kwisigqibo sayo sama-217 A (III), so 
NgoDisemba 10, 1948  
Intshayelelo  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba inkululeko, ubulungisa noxolo ehlabathini zisekelwe 
ukuqondwa kwesidima sangaphakathi kunye namalungelo alinganayo nangenakohluthwa 
kuwo onke amalungu osapho loluntu,  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba ukungazi kunye nokudelela amalungelo 
abantu baye benza izenzo zoburhelembhe ezimasikizi kwisazela somntu 
ubuntu; kwaye oko kuye kwavakaliswa, njengowona mnqweno uphakamileyo welizwe 
umntu, ukuvela kwehlabathi apho abantu, bakhululwe kulo 
uloyiko nenkxwaleko, yonwabele inkululeko yokuthetha nenkululeko yokuthetha 
iinkolelo,  
Xa kucingwa ukuba kubalulekile ukuba amalungelo oluntu akhuselwe a 
ulawulo loMthetho, ukuze umntu anganyanzelwa koyena mkhulu 
umthombo wemvukelo ngokuchasene nengcinezelo nengcinezelo,  
Ukuqwalaselwa kwakhona kubalulekile ukukhuthaza uphuhliso lobudlelwane bobuhlobo 
phakathi kwezizwe,  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba abantu beZizwe eziManyeneyo baye baqinisekisa kwakhona kwi 
Vumela ukholo lwakho kumalungelo angundoqo omntu, kwisidima kunye nexabiso 
oluntu kunye namalungelo alinganayo amadoda nabasetyhini; kwaye yona 
baye babhengeza isigqibo sabo sokukhuthaza inkqubela phambili yentlalo kunye nokuphakamisa umgangatho wokuphila 
kwingqikelelo ebanzi yenkululeko,
Ngokuthathela ingqalelo ukuba amaZwe angamaLungu azimisele ukuqinisekisa, ngo 
intsebenziswano noMbutho weZizwe eziManyeneyo, intlonipho yehlabathi kunye 
esebenzayo kumalungelo angundoqo kunye neenkululeko zomntu, kunye  
Ukuqwalasela ukuba ingcamango efanayo yala malungelo kunye neenkululeko 
eyona nto ibaluleke kakhulu ekuzalisekeni ngokupheleleyo kwesi sibophelelo esixeliweyo,  
 

 
INdibano yeZizwe eziManyeneyo  
Ibhengeza isiBhengezo seHlabathi jikelele samaLungelo oLuntu njengento efanelekileyo 
injongo efanayo bonke abantu nezizwe kufuneka bazame, ukuze 
bobabini abantu kunye namaziko, rhoqo befumana impembelelo kuyo, 
ukukhuthaza, ngokufundisa nangemfundo, ukuhlonelwa kwala malungelo 
kunye nenkululeko, kunye nokuqinisekisa, ngemilinganiselo yenkqubela phambili yesizwe kunye 
Umthetho wamazwe ngamazwe, ukuqondwa kwawo jikelele kunye nokusebenza okusebenzayo, zombini phakathi 
abantu baMazwe angamaLungu kunye naphakathi kwaleyo yemimandla ebekiweyo 
phantsi kolawulo lwayo.  
Inqaku loku-1  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile kwaye belingana ngesidima namalungelo kwaye, 
njengoko benikwe ingqondo nesazela, kufuneka baziphathe ngobudlelwane 
omnye komnye.  
Inqaku lesi-2  
Wonke umntu unamalungelo kunye nenkululeko ebhengezwe kwesi siBhengezo, 
ngaphandle kocalucalulo ngokobuhlanga, ngebala, ngokwesini, ngolwimi, ngokwenkolo, ngoluvo lwezopolitiko okanye 
naluphi na olunye uhlobo, imvelaphi yesizwe okanye yezentlalo, isikhundla sezoqoqosho, ukuzalwa okanye 
nayiphi na enye imeko.
Ngaphaya koko, akukho mahluko uya kwenziwa ngokwezopolitiko, ngokwasemthethweni okanye kwezopolitiko. 
ngamazwe ngamazwe okanye ummandla apho umntu axhomekeke phantsi kolawulo lwakhe, 
nokuba lilizwe elizimeleyo okanye ngummandla ophantsi kwayo 
ulawulo oluthembekileyo, aluzimelanga okanye luxhomekeke kulo naluphi na olunye unyino lwe 
ulongamo.  
Inqaku lesi-3  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kunye nokhuseleko lobomi bakhe. 
umntu.  
 

 
Inqaku lesi-4  
Akukho mntu uya kufakwa ebukhobokeni okanye ebukhobokeni; ubukhoboka nokurhweba 
Amakhoboka awavumelekanga ngandlela zonke.  
Inqaku lesi-5  
Akukho mntu uya kungcungcuthekiswa okanye ngenkohlakalo, ngokungenabuntu okanye ngempatho ekhohlakeleyo okanye isohlwayo. 
ukuthoba isidima  
Inqaku lesi-6  
Wonke umntu unelungelo, kuyo yonke indawo, lokuqondwa kwabo 
ubuntu obusemthethweni.  
Inqaku lesi-7  
Bonke bayalingana phambi komthetho kwaye, ngaphandle kokwahlulo, banelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo 
yomthetho. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokhuseleko olulinganayo kulo lonke ucalucalulo 
waphula esi siBhengezo kunye nakukuphi na ukuxhokonxa olocalucalulo.  
Inqaku lesi-8  
Wonke umntu unelungelo loncedo olusebenzayo phambi kweenkundla zesizwe. 
amagunya afanelekileyo, ukubakhusela kwizenzo ezinyhasha amalungelo abo 
amalungelo asisiseko amkelwa ngumgaqo-siseko okanye ngumthetho.  
Inqaku lesi-9  
Akukho mntu unokuvalelwa ngaphandle kwesizathu, avalelwe okanye agxothwe elubhacweni.  
Inqaku le-10  
Wonke umntu unelungelo, phantsi kweemeko zolingano olupheleleyo, lokuviwa
esidlangalaleni nangobulungisa yinkundla ezimeleyo nengakhethi cala, ukulungiselela 
 

 
ukumiselwa kwamalungelo kunye nezibophelelo zabo okanye ukuphonononga nayiphi na 
isityholo ngakuye kwimiba yolwaphulo-mthetho.  
Inqaku le-11  
1. Wonke umntu otyholwa ngolwaphulo-mthetho unelungelo lokuba isazisi sakhe sicingelwe 
ade abe msulwa ade abe unetyala, ngokomthetho nangokwezomthetho 
kulingo lukawonke-wonke apho zonke iziqinisekiso ziqinisekisiwe 
iyimfuneko ekukhuseleni kwayo.  
2. Akukho mntu uya kugwetywa ngezenzo okanye ukushiyelela oko ngexesha le 
amatyala ayengelolwaphulo-mthetho phantsi komthetho wesizwe okanye wezizwe ngezizwe. 
Kananjalo akusayi kuwiswa isohlwayo esiqatha kuneso sisebenzayo ngexesha lomhla womhla womhla womhla wokuphela kwetyala 
ukwenziwa kolwaphulo-mthetho.  
Inqaku le-12  
Akukho mntu uya kuphazanyiswa kubomi bakhe babucala, kusapho lwakhe, kwabo 
ikhaya okanye imbalelwano yakhe, okanye ukuhlaselwa kwembeko okanye isidima sakhe. 
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokukhuselwa ngumthetho ngokuchasene nokuphazanyiswa okanye 
uhlaselo.  
Inqaku le-13  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululekileyo kwaye azikhethele indawo yokuhlala 
kummandla woMbuso.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokushiya nakweliphi na ilizwe, kuquka nelakhe, kunye 
ukuba abuyele kwilizwe lakhe.  
Inqaku le-14  
1. Kwimeko yentshutshiso, wonke umntu unelungelo lokufuna indawo yokukhusela, kunye noku 
Yonwabele, nakweliphi na ilizwe.  
 

 
2. Eli lungelo alinakusetyenziswa ngokuchasene nesenzo sobulungisa ngokwenene 
zivela kulwaphulo-mthetho oluqhelekileyo okanye ngezenzo ezichasene neenjongo kunye
imigaqo yeZizwe eziManyeneyo.  
Inqaku le-15  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lobuzwe.  
2. Akukho namnye oya kuthi ahluthwe ubuzwe bakhe okanye ilungelo lokuba 
ukutshintsha ubuzwe.  
Inqaku le-16  
1. Amadoda nabafazi, ukususela kwiminyaka yokutshata, banelungelo, ngaphandle 
Nasiphi na isithintelo ngokobuhlanga, ubuzwe okanye inkolo, ukutshata 
waza wafumana usapho; kwaye baya kuxhamla amalungelo alinganayo malunga ne 
emtshatweni, ngexesha lomtshato naxa umtshato uyachithwa.  
2. Kuphela ngokukhululeka kunye nemvume epheleleyo yabatshati bexesha elizayo banakho 
tshata.  
3. Usapho yinxalenye yendalo nesisiseko soluntu kwaye lunayo 
ilungelo lokhuseleko loluntu kunye noRhulumente.  
Inqaku le-17  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo kwipropati, eyedwa nangokudibeneyo.  
2. Akukho mntu uya kuhluthwa impahla yakhe.  
Inqaku le-18  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucinga, isazela kunye 
inkolo; Eli lungelo liquka inkululeko yokutshintsha unqulo okanye inkolelo, ngokunjalo 
njengenkululeko yokubonakalisa inkolo okanye inkolelo kabani, eyedwa kunye 
ngokudibeneyo, esidlangalaleni nangasese, ngokufundisa, ngokwenza, 
unqulo nokugcinwa.  
 

 
Inqaku le-19  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuvakalisa izimvo zakhe; oku 
ilungelo libandakanya ukungaphazanyiswa ngenxa yezimvo zomntu, ilungelo lokuphanda kunye
ukufumana ulwazi kunye nezimvo, kunye nokuzisasaza, ngaphandle kokuthintelwa kwemida, 
ngayo nayiphi na indlela yokubonakalisa.  
Inqaku lama-20  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlanganisana nokunxulumana 
enoxolo.  
2. Akukho mntu unokunyanzelwa ukuba abe lilungu lombutho.  
Inqaku lama-21  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba kurhulumente welizwe lakhe; 
ngqo okanye ngokusebenzisa abameli abakhethwe ngokukhululekileyo.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokufikelela, phantsi kweemeko zolingano, ku 
imisebenzi yoluntu lwelizwe labo.  
3. Intando yabantu sisiseko segunya lamandla oluntu; oku 
iyakubonakaliswa ngokhetho oluyinyani oluya kufuneka lube 
zibanjwa ngamaxesha athile, ngokuvunyelwa jikelele nangokulinganayo nangevoti 
imfihlo okanye enye inkqubo elinganayo eqinisekisa inkululeko ye 
ivoti.  
Inqaku lama-22  
Wonke umntu, njengelungu loluntu, unelungelo lokhuseleko 
ezentlalo, kunye nokufumana, ngeenzame zesizwe kunye nentsebenziswano yamazwe ngamazwe, 
kuthathelwa ingqalelo umbutho kunye nezibonelelo zoMbuso ngamnye, ukwaneliseka 
amalungelo ezoqoqosho, ezentlalo nenkcubeko, ayimfuneko kwisidima sabo 
kunye nophuhliso olukhululekileyo lobuntu babo.  
 

 
Inqaku lama-23  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokusebenza, ukukhetha umsebenzi ngokukhululekileyo, uku 
iimeko ezilinganayo kunye nezanelisayo zomsebenzi kunye nokhuseleko 
ukungaqeshwa. 
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo, ngaphandle kwalo naluphi na ucalucalulo, lokufumana umvuzo olinganayo
Ndisebenza ngokufanayo.  
3. Wonke umntu osebenzayo unelungelo lomvuzo olinganayo kunye 
eyanelisayo, eqinisekisa ukuba yena, kunye nentsapho yakhe, ubukho 
ngokuhambelana nesidima somntu kwaye oko kuya kugqitywa, ukuba kunokwenzeka 
kuyimfuneko, ngayo nayiphi na enye indlela yokhuseleko loluntu.  
4. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuseka imibutho yabasebenzi kunye nokuba lilungu lemibutho yabasebenzi 
ukukhusela iimfuno zabo.  
Inqaku lama-24  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuphumla, ukonwabela ixesha lokukhululeka, a 
ukulinganiselwa okufanelekileyo kobude bexesha lomsebenzi kunye neeholide zamaxesha athile 
ihlawulwe.  
Inqaku lama-25  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lomgangatho wokuphila owaneleyo oqinisekisayo, 
kunye nosapho lwakho, impilo kunye nentlalontle, kwaye ngakumbi 
ukutya, impahla, indlu, unyango kunye neenkonzo 
zentlalo eziyimfuneko Ukwanalo nelungelo kwi-inshorensi kwimeko 
ukungaqeshwa, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, ubuhlolokazi, ukwaluphala nezinye iimeko zokulahlekelwa 
indlela yabo yokuphila ngenxa yeemeko ezingaxhomekekanga kubo 
intando.  
2. Ubunina nobuntwana banelungelo lokukhathalelwa noncedo 
ezizodwa. Bonke abantwana, nokuba bazalelwe emtshatweni okanye ngaphandle komtshato 
umtshato, banelungelo lokhuseleko loluntu olulinganayo.  
 

 
Inqaku lama-26  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo kufuneka ibe 
free, ubuncinane ngokubhekiselele kwimfundo yamabanga aphantsi kunye 
esisiseko. Imfundo yamabanga aphantsi iya kunyanzeliswa. Umyalelo
ubuchwephesha kunye nobuchwephesha kuya kufuneka wenziwe ngokubanzi; ukufikelela kwizifundo 
abaphezulu baya kufana kubo bonke, kuxhomekeke kwizinto zabo.  
2. Imfundo iya kujolisa ekuphuhliseni ngokupheleleyo ubuntu 
amalungelo oluntu kunye nokomelezwa kwentlonipho yamalungelo oluntu kunye 
iinkululeko ezingundoqo; iya kukhuthaza ukuqonda, ukunyamezelana kunye 
ubuhlobo phakathi kwezizwe zonke nazo zonke iintlanga okanye amaqela enkolo; kwaye 
ukukhuthaza uphuhliso lwemisebenzi yeZizwe eziManyeneyo ukwenzela 
ukugcina uxolo.  
3. Abazali baya kuba nelungelo elikhethekileyo lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo 
baya kunikwa abantwana babo.  
Inqaku lama-27  
1. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuthabatha inxaxheba ngokukhululekileyo kubomi benkcubeko 
yoluntu, ukonwabela ubugcisa kunye nokuthatha inxaxheba kwinkqubela phambili 
ezenzululwazi kunye neengenelo ezibangelwa kuko.  
2. Wonke umntu unelungelo lokhuseleko lweminqweno yokuziphatha kunye 
izinto ezihambelana nayo ngenxa yeemveliso zesayensi, 
imisebenzi yoncwadi okanye yobugcisa engumbhali wayo.  
Inqaku lama-28  
Wonke umntu unelungelo lokuseka inkqubo yentlalontle neyezizwe ngezizwe 
apho enziwa khona amalungelo neenkululeko ezibhengezwe kwesi siBhengezo 
ngempumelelo ngokupheleleyo.  
Inqaku lama-29  
 

 
1. Wonke umntu unemisebenzi ngokubhekiselele kuluntu, ekubeni kuphela 
unakho ngokukhululekileyo kwaye ngokupheleleyo aphuhlise ubuntu bakhe.  
2. Ekusebenziseni amalungelo abo nasekuxhamleni inkululeko yabo, bonke
umntu uya kuxhomekeka kuphela kwimida esekwe ngumthetho 
ngenjongo eyodwa yokuqinisekisa ukuqatshelwa kunye nentlonipho 
amalungelo nenkululeko yabanye, kunye nokwanelisa amabango anobulungisa 
yokuziphatha, ucwangco loluntu kunye nokuphila kakuhle kuluntu 
yedemokhrasi.  
3. La malungelo kunye neenkululeko mazingaze naphantsi kwazo naziphi na iimeko zisetyenziswe 
ukuchasa iinjongo kunye nemigaqo yeZizwe eziManyeneyo. 
Inqaku lama-30  
Akukho nto kwesi siBhengezo inokutolikwa njengethetha oko 
inika naliphi na ilungelo kuRhulumente, kwiqela okanye emntwini, lokuthabatha 
kunye nokuphuhlisa imisebenzi okanye ukwenza izenzo ezijolise ekuthinteleni nayiphi na 
yamalungelo nenkululeko ebhengezwe kwesi siBhengezo.
