Nga Ddesemba 10, 1948, ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte 
baakuŋŋaana ne bakikkiriza ne bakimanyisa abantu 
eddembe, nga bino byonna byogerwa mu katabo kano.  
Olukungaana luno lwateesezza ku ddembe ly’abantu bonna 
okuyingizibwa, okusomesebwa, n’okusomebwa mu buli nsi, . 
kisaana okumanyisibwa mu matendekero g’ebyenjigiriza aga buli ngeri 
n’enjawulo wakati w’amawanga gano.  
 
OKUSIIMA AMAWANGA AMAGATTE  
EDDEMBE LY'ABANTU BONNA 
Entandikwa y’...  
NOTE: Eddembe n'emirembe mu nsi 
obulamu busimbiddwa bulungi mu bino wammanga: Buli muntu 
kirungi nti tewali muntu yenna ayonoona okuva nnyaffe lwe yatuzaala 
tulina okuyisibwa n’okuyisibwa kyenkanyi.  
LABA: Abantu batuuse ku bintu ebibi ebya buli ngeri, . 
ne bwe baba nga tebamanyi bulungi ddembe ly’obuntu oba nga tebamanyi 
nga tuzibuusa amaaso, ffenna twagala ebiseera eby’omu maaso 
ennaku z’okubeera obulungi; mu kutya kwonna n’okubonaabona 
obwavu bwe bunaggwaawo, buli muntu ajja kuba n’eddembe okwogera 
ebiri mu mitwe gyaffe, era tujja kufuna n’obuvumu okumanya 
ne bye twagala.  
NOTE: Wateekwa okubaawo Omulwanirizi w’Eddembe ly’Obuntu 
amateeka agataliimu kyekubiira 
muyambe abanyigirizibwa n’abakozesebwa abaagala okulaga obutuufu bw’eddembe lyabwe.
NOTE: Kiba kya mawanga g’ensi okuzimba emikwano 
tulina okwegomba mu mitima gyaffe n’okwegomba okwo 
tulina okusaasaana.  
WEETEGEREZE: Amawanga agali mu kibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte googerwako mu kitabo kino 
yakakasa enzikiriza yaabwe mu ddembe ly’Abailiw era 
bakkiriziganyizza nti abantu benkanankana awatali kufaayo ku kikula kyabwe 
kimanye nti naye alina eddembe lye limu. Ebintu bino byonna birungi 
okumanya nti ekigendererwa ky’eddembe lyaffe kisingako awo okulabika obulungi 
tukole okufuula eggwanga lyaffe eddene.  
NOTE: Amawanga agali mu kibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte gakkaanyizza 
eddembe n’eddembe ly’obuntu bikwata ku bantu bonna 
okufunibwa aba  
NOTE NYA: Eddembe n’eddembe ly’obuntu bimanyiddwa bulungi 
bwe kiba bwe kityo, kijja kuba kituufu.  
EDDEMBE LY’OBUNTU ERIKKIRIZIDDWA MU LUKUNG’AANA LW’ABANTU 
NTI KYALI  
Amawanga agaajja ku ndagaano ennene gali mu kitabo kino 
okukkiriza eddembe ly’obuntu erya bonna, abantu 
wonna we babeera, basobola okulaba qeliqa eno n’ekitiibwa n’eddembe 
okuyiga okubeera omulamu; eri abalala nga bwe basobola 
balitegeeza nti ebintu bino byonna bituufu; bino bijja kukolebwa 
okubeera mu mawanga agali mu mukago oba kino 
obuddukiro bw’amawanga.  
EKISOOKA (1) . 
Abantu bonna bazaalibwa ba ddembe era balina eddembe lyonna 
twenkanankana. Olw’okuba tuli ba magezi, tulinga amaka ag’emirembe 
tubanja obulamu eri buli muntu.  
EKYOKUBIRI (2) .
Emisingi gy’eddembe egiri mu kiwandiiko kino gyaffe ffenna, . 
si nsonga ggwanga ly’omuntu oba ekikula kye 
era si kikulu ngeri gy’ayogeramu oba katonda ki gw’akkiririzaamu 
wadde okuva mu ngeri ya gavumenti gye yalowooza nti ejja kuba 
oba mwavu oba mugagga oba kyonna ky’aba.  
Amawanga gonna, ga ddembe oba nedda, ga njawulo 
tulina gavumenti, era abamu beesigamye ku mawanga amalala, . 
naye mu ngeri yonna tetulina kuyisibwa mu ngeri ya njawulo.  
EKITUNDU EKYOKUSATU (3) . 
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’obulamu n’eddembe 
era n’okukuumibwa.  
EKYOKUNA (4) . 
Tewali ayinza kuba muddu awambibwa, kikugirwa mu ngeri zonna 
okufuula abantu abaddu n’okukukusa abantu.  
EKYOKUTAANO (5) . 
Tewali muntu yenna ayinza kubonaabona mu ngeri yonna, era 
tetusobola kubonerezebwa olw’okunyooma obuntu bwaffe.  
EKYOKUKAGA (6) . 
Buli muntu alina eddembe okuwozesebwa wonna w’ali 
okukakasibwa.  
OMUSANVU (7) . 
Abantu bonna balina eddembe eryenkanankana mu mateeka 
okubeera, amateeka galina okutukuuma awatali kusosola. 
Era tulina eddembe okutuwakanya olw’okubuusa amaaso enjigiriza zino 
 

 
w’abo abaagala okuyimirira. Era tulina eddembe okufuna enjigiriza zino 
n’okukuumibwa amateeka singa wabaawo ensobi ey’amangu.  
EKYOKUNA (8) .
Buli muntu alina eddembe okuwulirwa mu bwenkanya 
obukuumi okuva ku ddembe lyaffe ery’amateeka 
tulina okukuumibwa okuva ku abo abakikola.  
ESSUULA Y'OMWENDA (9) . 
Tewali muntu yenna asibibwa nga tamanyi musango gwe, . 
okusibwa oba okuwangangusibwa.  
EKKUMI (10) . 
Abantu bonna benkanankana n’obwenkanya obutaliimu kyekubiira 
tulina eddembe okuwulirwa n’eddembe okuwozesebwa 
omulimu gwaffe oba eddembe lyaffe okumanya. Bye bamulumiriza 
kitulagibwa bulungi tusobole okuyiga ne bwe tuba nga tuli wala.  
EKKUMI N'emu (11) . 
1. LIew abantu tebalina musango gwonna ogubavunaanibwa 
tulina eddembe okwekakasa, okusinziira ku mateeka 
okutuusa nga tusingiddwa omusango mu musango. Waliwo n’okuwozesebwa 
eddembe lyaffe likakasiddwa era liwolereza mu mazima 
okubeera abo abalina okubeera.  
2. Obutasalira muntu kibonerezo lwa kintu ky’akoze 
ekibonerezo singa ekikolwa mu nnaku nga amateeka tegannabaawo 
bw’afuluma n’akakasa nti musango. Ekibonerezo kijja kuba kya... 
ku bibonerezo by’amateeka byokka mu nnaku z’ekibi kye.  
 

 
KKUMI NA BBIRI (12) . 
Era tewali ayinza kutabula bulamu bwaffe awatali nsonga 
ne mu maka gaffe, tayinza kuyingira bwereere 
mu maka gaffe, era ebbaluwa zaffe tezirina ku ieeed bwereere. 
Buli muntu alina eddembe okukuumibwa amateeka okuva ku kuyingirira okwo.  
KKUMI MU bisatu (13) .
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutambula, . 
era tulina eddembe ly’amayumba mu nsi yaffe.  
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna eggwanga oba 
okuva mu nsi yonna era kye kimu 
okuddayo mu nsi yaffe.  
KKUMI NNA (14) . 
1. Omuntu bwayigganyizibwa nga gavumenti y’ensi ye bwetayigganyizibwa 
kubanga alowooza, olwo aba alina eddembe okuyingira mu nsi 
okudduka n’okuddukira.  
2. Avunaanibwa omusango ogwa nnamaddala mu nsi ye 
omuntu tayinza kwesigama ku ddembe lya buntu okuggyako 
okuddukira mu nsi n’okubeera eyo mu ddembe.  
KKUMI NA TANO (15) . 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna eggwanga 
okubeera ebyaffe.  
2. Eddembe lino teriyinza kuggyibwako ddembe lyalyo mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira. 
Era tulina eddembe okugaana okukyusa eggwanga lyaffe 
omuntu okutulemesa.  
KKUMI MUKAaga (16) . 
 

 
1. Omusajja n’omukazi bwe batuuka mu myaka gy’obufumbo, baba babeerawo dda 
eddembe lyaffe ery’okufumbirwa n’okuba n’amaka, nedda 
si nsonga ggwanga ki oba ggwanga ki, oba tuli basajja oba bakazi 
omukazi tulina eddembe eryenkanankana mu bufumbo bwaffe obulungi 
okubeera omulamu n’okugabana.  
2. Omusajja n’omukazi tebasobola kufumbiriganwa nga tebakkirizza.  
3. Obuzadde ddembe ly’amaka gonna okukuumibwa gavumenti 
tulina eddembe, kubanga tuli maka manene agakulaakulanya eggwanga 
ffe ffe.
KKUMI MUSANVU (17) . 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okubeera yekka oba mu babiri 
okubeera n’ebintu n’abantu.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuggyibwako bintu bye mu ngeri ey’ekimpatiira.  
KKUMI NOMWANA (18) . 
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okulowooza, . 
omuntu ow’omunda era mu katonda ki 
n’okutukkiriza. Okusinziira ku ddembe lino, tuli ba ddembe 
okukkiriza eddiini n’okukkiriza mu ngeri yonna 
okukola omulimu. Tusobola okukikola ffekka oba mu kibinja 
nti enzikiriza era esobola okusomesebwa mu maka oba 
wonna wonna.  
EKKUMI MU MWENDA (19) . 
Buli muntu alina eddembe okwogera endowooza ye 
tulina eddembe ku bintu bino awatali kuziyizibwa kwonna 
era tusobola okugabana ebirowoozo byaffe n’okukkiriza endowooza z’abalala.  
AMAKUMI ABIRI (20) . 
 

 
1. Tulina eddembe ly’okukuŋŋaana n’okwegatta 
n’okuzimba emirembe.  
2. Tewali muntu yenna ajja kuwalirizibwa kwetaba mu lukiiko lwonna 
okubeera nga kyeyagalire.  
ABIRI MU MU (21) . 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuga gavumenti y’eggwanga lye 
okweweereza, oba ffekka oba 
ne bwe tuba nga tulonda ekyo kye twagala.  
2. Buli muntu alina eddembe okwenkanankana mu mirimu gya gavumenti mu nsi ye 
okuyingira mu maka n’okufuna okulabirira.  
3. Amaanyi ga gavumenti gali nti abantu bonna bagala
amangu ddala nga tumaze okuwa obuyinza, tukkiriza obwagazi obwo 
nga bakuba akalulu mu ddembe; tulina okufuula akalulu ako obutaba muntu mulala 
amanyi tusobole okulonda kye twagala.  
ABIRI MU BBIRI (22) . 
Buli muntu alina eddembe ly’okusoma, eby’obugagga n’... 
okukuumibwa mu makubo gaffe gonna buli kimu kibeere kirungi 
okututuusa wano. Obukakafu obwo tubufuna nga: 
Gavumenti erina okutegekebwa era etukole ky’esobola 
n’abantu b’eggwanga lyaffe, alina okukola ku lw’okutuganyula okusinziira ku maanyi ge oba 
n’obuyambi okuva ebweru w’eggwanga.  
AMAKUMI ABIRI MU bisatu (23) . 
1. LIew abantu balina eddembe ly’eddiini, . 
tulina eddembe okukola ne bwe tuba nga tetulina mirimu 
okunoonya n’okuzuula, bwe kiba nga tewali ayagala 
tusobola okugobwa ku mirimu gyaffe.  
 

 
2. LIew abantu balina eddembe ly’okwogera 
okusasulwa okusinziira ku mulimu gwaffe.  
3. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutambula n’okubeera mu nsalo za buli Ggwanga 
tulyoke tufune empeera yaffe ng'ebikolwa ebirungi bwe biri 
obulungi bw’amaka gonna; bwe kitaba ekyo alina okufuna obuyambi 
wa bawagizi abakuziddwa olw’ekigendererwa kino.  
4. Buli muntu alina eddembe okukolagana n’abalala 
eddembe lyaffe okukola oba okubeera mu bibinja ng’ebyo 
oku vechiku ffekka.  
AKUMI ABIRI MU BANA (24) . 
Buli muntu alina eddembe okuwummula n’okuwummulamu
tulina eddembe okumwenya, era tulina eddembe okugenda mu maaso nga bwe tusobola buli 
okukola emisana n’okutwala oluwummula lwaffe. 
AMAKUMI ABIRI MU TANO (25) . 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okufuna omutindo gw’obulamu ogumala ye n’ab’omu maka ge 
ekiriisa, ayambadde obulungi era nga mulamu bulungi. Naye alina eddembe 
tulina obukuumi, ne bwe tuba nga tuli balema, bannamwandu, oba bannamwandu 
oba oli mukadde oba muto; era tulina eddembe 
ne bwe tuba nga tetusobola kufuna mulimu oba mu bwangu nga tulina kye tuyinza okweyimirizaawo 
okukuumibwa singa wabaawo okufiirwa.  
2. Abakyala ab’embuto n’abaana balina eddembe 
zirina okulabirira obulungi. Mufumbo oba nedda 
abaana bonna balina eddembe eryenkanankana si nsonga oba bazaalibwa mu bufumbo 
okukuumibwa.  
AMAKUMI ABIRI mu MUKAaga (26) . 
 

 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okusomesebwa. Si bwe kiri n’akatono 
pulayimale ya bwereere, kale kikakatako eri buli muntu alina pulayimale 
okusobola. Okusinziira ku note yaffe ennungi, byonna bisingako 
tusobola okwesomesa tusobole okutuukiriza ebiruubirirwa byaffe.  
2. Ekinaava mu kusoma kijja kuba bulungi bwa bantu na bonna 
eddembe lye limanyiddwa bulungi olwo eddembe lye ne lissibwamu ekitiibwa 
okusobola okubeerawo. Tulina okuba abakkakkamu n’okutegeeragana 
n’amawanga, n’amawanga amalala, ne mu katonda yenna 
era n’abakkiriza. Tulina n’okuwagira ekibiina ky’Amawanga Amagatte 
emirembe mu nsi.  
3. Abazadde balina eddembe ly’okusoma nga tebannaba
n’okusembeza amaka gaffe.  
AMAKUMI ABIRI MU MUSANVU (27) . 
1. Buli muntu alina eddembe okutambula n’okubeera mu nsalo za buli Ggwanga 
okutuyamba mu mbeera yonna. Tusobola okuyamba mu buli ngeri 
ensi yaffe okukulaakulana obulungi era tulina eddembe 
okukkiriza ekibuga kye kirimu.  
2. Singa tuba tuggyewo mu birowoozo ekintu eky’omuwendo oba 
tusobola okukola ekintu omuntu omulala yenna ky’atakoze 
tulina eddembe okufuna omuwendo gw’ebitonde ebyo 
okukuuma ebiriwo.  
AMAKUMI ABIRI MU MWANA (28) . 
Tulina eddembe mu nsi yaffe ne mu nsi zonna 
okubeera ekibiina ekirwanirira eddembe ly’obuntu, olwo bino byonna 
okutuuka ku ddembe n’eddembe lye tulowooza nti tulina.  
AMAKUMI ABIRI mu MWENDA (29) . 
 

 
1. Buli muntu wa ddembe mu nsi yaffe, n’olwekyo ku lw’eggwanga lyaffe 
tulina omulimu.  
2. Engeri yokka ey’okussa mu nkola eddembe oba eddembe lyaffe lye mu mateeka 
kiyinza okutulemesa, gamba ng’eddembe ly’abalala oba eddembe 
okubeerawo kwabwe tekuwambibwa, enkaayana ne zitakwatibwa 
okusobola okubeerawo. Olwo buli muntu abeere mu nsi ye 
okubeera mu mirembe.  
3. Eddembe n’eddembe lino mu ngeri yonna tebiyinza kukozesebwa 
asobola okuba, bwe kiba mu ngeri yonna ey’amawanga ag’omukago 
bwe kiba nga kimenya okwagala kwe.  
AMAKUMI ASATU (30) . 
Tewali muntu yenna oba gavumenti eyinza kwesigama ku quote eno
yandikoze ekintu kyonna, eddembe lyaffe n’eddembe lyaffe 
okuwamba eggwanga.
