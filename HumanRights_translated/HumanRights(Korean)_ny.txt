Universal Declaration of Human Rights

Kuzindikira ulemu wachibadwa ndi ufulu wofanana ndi wosalandirika wa anthu onse amtundu wa anthu ndiye maziko a ufulu, chilungamo ndi mtendere padziko lapansi; kunyalanyaza ndi kunyoza ufulu wa anthu kwachititsa nkhanza zomwe zakwiyitsa chikumbumtima cha anthu; ndi kubwera kwa dziko limene munthu akhoza kusangalala ndi ufulu wolankhula ndi zikhulupiriro ndi kumasuka ku mantha ndi kusoweka kwalengezedwa kukhala chikhumbo chachikulu cha anthu onse, kuopera kuti munthu angakakamizidwe kupanduka monga njira yomalizira yolimbana ndi nkhanza ndi kuponderezana; Ndikofunikira kuti ufulu wachibadwidwe utetezedwe ndi malamulo, ndipo ndikofunikira kulimbikitsa kukulitsa ubale wabwino pakati pa mayiko. Mu Tchata chake, mamembala onse a bungwe la United Nations adatsimikiziranso chikhulupiriro chawo pa ufulu wachibadwidwe, ulemu ndi kufunikira kwa umunthu waumunthu, ndi ufulu wofanana wa amuna ndi akazi, ndipo adadzipereka kulimbikitsa kupita patsogolo kwa chikhalidwe cha anthu ndi miyezo yabwino yakukhala ndi ufulu wochuluka. Mayiko omwe ali mamembala adalonjeza kuti adzagwirizana ndi bungwe la United Nations kuti alimbikitse kulemekeza ndi kusunga ufulu wachibadwidwe ndi ufulu wachibadwidwe. Popeza kumvetsetsa kwaufulu ndi ufulu ndikofunikira kwambiri kuti Panganoli likwaniritsidwe kwathunthu, Msonkhano Waukulu wa United Nations, motero, umayesetsa kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu ndi ufuluwu kudzera mu maphunziro ndi maphunziro pakati pa anthu onse ndi mabungwe a anthu, nthawi zonse kukumbukira Chidziwitso ichi, ndipo amayesetsa, kupyolera mu njira zopita patsogolo zapadziko lonse ndi zapadziko lonse, kuonetsetsa kuti mayiko ndi mayiko onse akuzindikiridwa ndi kuvomerezedwa kwa ufulu ndi ufulu wa anthu ndi ufulu wawo. anthu a m'madera omwe ali pansi pa ulamuliro wawo; Timalengeza za Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Ndime 1

Anthu onse amabadwa aufulu ndi ofanana mu ulemu ndi ufulu. Anthu mwachibadwa anapatsidwa nzeru ndi chikumbumtima ndipo ayenera kuchita zinthu mu mzimu wa ubale kwa wina ndi mnzake. Ndime 2 Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wokhala ndi ufulu ndi kumasuka zonse zomwe zafotokozedwa m'chikalatachi, popanda tsankho lamtundu uliwonse, monga mtundu, khungu, kugonana, chinenero, chipembedzo, ndale kapena maganizo ena, dziko kapena chikhalidwe, katundu, kubadwa kapena udindo wina uliwonse. Komanso, sipadzakhala tsankho pazandale, zalamulo kapena zapadziko lonse lapansi za dziko kapena gawo la munthu, mosasamala kanthu kuti dziko kapena gawolo ndi dziko lodziyimira pawokha, trusteeship, gawo lomwe silikudzilamulira, kapena lili ndi ziletso zina paulamuliro wake. Ndime 3 Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo, ufulu ndi chitetezo. Ndime 4 Palibe amene adzakhale muukapolo kapena ngati kapolo. Ukapolo wamtundu uliwonse ndi malonda a akapolo ndizoletsedwa. Ndime 5 Palibe amene adzazunzidwe, kuzunzidwa, kuchitiridwa nkhanza, monyozetsa kapena kulangidwa. Ndime 6 Aliyense ali ndi danga lozindikirika ngati munthu pamaso pa lamulo paliponse. Ndime 7
Anthu onse ndi ofanana pamaso pa lamulo ndipo ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa mofanana ndi lamulo popanda tsankho. Aliyense ali ndi danga lotetezedwa ku tsankho lina lililonse limene lingaphwanyidwe ndi chigamulochi komanso kuti asayambitse tsankho. Ndime 8 Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kulandira mpumulo kuchokera ku mabwalo amilandu oyenerera ngati atachita zinthu zophwanya ufulu wachibadwidwe woperekedwa ndi Constitution kapena lamulo. Ndime 9 Palibe amene adzamangidwe, kutsekeredwa m'ndende kapena kuthamangitsidwa popanda chilolezo. Ndime 10 Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wozengedwa mlandu mwachilungamo ndi pagulu komanso mofanana mokwanira ku bwalo lamilandu loyima palokha komanso lopanda tsankho kuti likhazikitse ufulu wake, udindo wake komanso milandu yomwe ali nayo. Ndime 11 1. Munthu aliyense woimbidwa mlandu ali ndi ufulu wotengedwa kuti ndi wosalakwa mpaka atapezeka kuti ndi wolakwa malinga ndi lamulo pamlandu wapagulu pomwe pali chilichonse chofunikira pachitetezo chake. 2. Palibe amene adzaimbidwe mlandu chifukwa cha zomwe anachita kapena kulephera kuchitapo kanthu, zomwe, panthawiyi, sizinali zolakwa pansi pa malamulo a dziko kapena mayiko. Kuonjezera apo, palibe chilango chokhwima chomwe chidzaperekedwa kuposa chilango chomwe chikanagwiritsidwa ntchito pa nthawi ya mlanduwo. Ndime 12 Palibe amene adzasokonezedwe ndi moyo wake wachinsinsi, nyumba yake, nyumba yake kapena makalata ake, kapena kudzudzulidwa pa ulemu ndi mbiri yake. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutetezedwa ndi lamulo kuti asasokonezedwe kapena kutsutsidwa. Ndime 13

1. Aliyense ali ndi danga loyenda ndikukhala m'dziko lake. 2. Aliyense ali ndi danga locoka m’dziko lina lililonse, kuphatikizira dziko lake, ndi ufulu wobwerera ku dziko lake. Ndime 14 1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lofunafuna chitetezo ku maiko ena kuzunzidwa. 2. Ufulu umenewu sungatsimikizidwe pa milandu yosagwirizana ndi ndale kapena zochita zosemphana ndi zolinga ndi mfundo za United Nations. Ndime 15 1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala nzika za dziko. 2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe dziko lake mwachisawawa kapena kuletsedwa kusintha mtundu wake. Ndime 16 1. Amuna ndi akazi akulu akulu ali ndi ufulu kukwatirana ndi kupanga banja popanda chiletso chotengera mtundu, dziko kapena chipembedzo. Ayenera kukhala ndi ufulu wofanana pabanja, m'banja komanso banja litatha. 2. Ukwati umakhazikitsidwa pokhapokha ndi chilolezo chaulere ndi chokwanira cha okwatirana amtsogolo. 3. Banja ndi gawo lachilengedwe komanso lofunikira la anthu ndipo lili ndi ufulu wotetezedwa ndi anthu komanso boma. Ndime 17 1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi katundu payekha payekha komanso mothandizana ndi ena. 2. Palibe amene adzalandidwe chuma chake mwachisawawa. Ndime 18
Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wa kuganiza, chikumbumtima ndi chipembedzo. Ufulu umenewu umaphatikizapo ufulu wosintha chipembedzo kapena chikhulupiriro chako ndi kusonyeza chipembedzo kapena zikhulupiriro zako, payekha kapena pagulu ndi ena, poyera kapena payekha, mwa miyambo yachipembedzo, kupembedza ndi kutsatira. Ndime 19 Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi maganizo omasuka. Ufulu umenewu umaphatikizapo ufulu wokhala ndi maganizo popanda kusokonezedwa ndi ufulu wofuna, kupeza ndi kupereka mauthenga ndi malingaliro kudzera muzofalitsa zilizonse, mosasamala kanthu za malire. Ndime 20 1. Aliyense ali ndi danga losonkhana pamodzi mwamtendere. 2. Palibe amene adzakakamizidwe kutenga nawo mbali mu bungwe lililonse. Ndime 21 1. Aliyense ali ndi danga lotengapo mbali mu boma la dziko lake, molunjika kapena kupyolera mwa woyimilira mwaufulu. 2. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wofanana kukhala ndi udindo wa boma m'dziko lake. 3. Chifuniro cha anthu ndicho maziko a mphamvu za boma. Izi zidzawonetsedwa kudzera mu zisankho zanthawi zonse ndi zenizeni mwachinsinsi kapena njira zofanana zovota mwaufulu malinga ndi ufulu wapadziko lonse komanso wofanana. Ndime 22 Aliyense, monga membala wa gulu la anthu, ali ndi ufulu wopeza chitetezo cha chikhalidwe cha anthu ndi ufulu wozindikira, kupyolera mu khama la dziko lonse ndi mgwirizano wa mayiko, molingana ndi bungwe ndi chuma cha boma lililonse, ufulu wa zachuma, chikhalidwe ndi chikhalidwe ndizofunikira kuti munthu atukule ulemu ndi umunthu wake mwaulele. Ndime 23

1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kugwira ntchito, kusankha ntchito mwaufulu, ntchito yabwino ndi yotetezedwa ku ulova. 2. Aliyense ali ndi danga lolandila malipilo ofanana pa nchito yofanana naye popanda tsankho. 3. Aliyense wogwila nchito ali ndi danga lolandila malipilo oyenera, amene amamupatsa iye yekha ndi banja lake kukhala ndi moyo mogwilizana ndi ulemu wa umunthu ndi kuwonjezeredwa, ngati kuli koyenera, ndi njira zina zotetezera anthu. 4. Aliyense ali ndi danga loyambitsa ndi kulowa bungwe la ogwira ntchito pofuna kuteteza zofuna zake. Ndime 24 Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kupumula ndi kusanguluka, kuphatikizirapo malire pa nthawi yogwira ntchito komanso tchuthi cholipidwa. Ndime 25 1. Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi moyo wokwanira pa umoyo wake ndi banja lake, kuphatikizapo chakudya, zovala, pogona, chithandizo chamankhwala ndi umoyo wabwino wa anthu, ndi ufulu wopeza chitetezo pakakhala kusowa ntchito chifukwa cha ulova, matenda, kulumala, imfa ya mnzace, ukalamba kapena zinthu zina zimene sangazikwanitse. 2. Amayi ndi ana ali ndi ufulu wotetezedwa ndi chithandizo chapadera. Ana onse amasangalala ndi chitetezo chofanana cha anthu mosasamala kanthu za udindo wawo. Ndime 26 1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu wamaphunziro. Maphunziro ayenera kukhala aulere, kusukulu ya pulaimale ndi yoyambira. Maphunziro a pulaimale ayenera kukhala okakamiza. Maphunziro aukadaulo ndi ntchito zaumisiri azipezeka kwa onse, ndipo maphunziro apamwamba azipezeka kwa onse molingana ndi kuyenerera. 2. Maphunziro amayang'ana pa chitukuko chokwanira cha umunthu ndi kulimbikitsa kulemekeza ufulu wa anthu ndi ufulu wofunikira. Maphunziro amalimbikitsa kumvetsetsa, kulolerana ndi
Tiyenera kulimbikitsa ubwenzi ndi kulimbikitsa ntchito za United Nations kuti tisunge mtendere. 3. Makolo ali ndi udindo wosankha mtundu wa maphunziro operekedwa kwa ana awo. Ndime 27 1. Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa chikhalidwe cha anthu, kusangalala ndi zaluso, ndi kugawana nawo pa chitukuko cha sayansi ndi ubwino wake. 2. Aliyense ali ndi danga lotetezedwa ku zinthu zauzimu ndi zakuthupi zochokera kuzinthu za sayansi, zolembalemba kapena zaluso zomwe amapanga. Ndime 28 Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala mu chikhalidwe cha dziko ndi mayiko momwe ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zafotokozedwa mu chikalata ichi zingatheke kukwaniritsidwa. Ndime 29 1. Aliyense ali ndi udindo kudera limene umunthu wake wokha ungakule momasuka ndi mokwanira. 2. Aliyense, pogwilitsila nchito maufulu ndi kumasuka kwace, ali ndi ziletso zokhazikitsidwa ndi lamulo ndi cholinga chofuna kuonetsetsa kuti ufulu ndi kumasuka kwa anthu ena zikuzindikirika ndi kulemekezedwa, ndi cholinga chokwaniritsa zofuna zovomerezeka za makhalidwe abwino, bata ndi mtendere wa anthu onse m’dziko la demokalase. 3. Ufulu ndi kumasulika kumeneku sikudzachitidwa mwanjira iliyonse mosemphana ndi zolinga ndi mfundo za United Nations. Ndime 30 Palibe chilichonse m'Chikalatachi chomwe chidzatengedwe ngati kuloleza boma, gulu kapena munthu aliyense kuchitapo kanthu kapena kuchitapo kanthu pofuna kusokoneza ufulu ndi kumasuka kulikonse komwe kwafotokozedwa m'chikalatachi.
