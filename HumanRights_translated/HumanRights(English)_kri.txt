Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt 
Fɔs di wɔd dɛn 
We dɛn de rɛkɔgnayz di rɛspɛkt we pɔsin gɛt ɛn di ikwal ɛn we nɔ go ebul fɔ tek am 
rayt fɔ ɔl di pipul dɛn na di mɔtalman famili na di fawndeshɔn fɔ fridɔm, jɔstis 
ɛn pis de na di wɔl, .  
We di we aw pipul dɛn nɔ de tek tɛm wit pipul dɛn rayt ɛn disgres dɔn mek dɛn de du bad bad tin dɛn 
akt dɛm we dɔn mek mɔtalman kɔnshɛns vɛks, ɛn di kam fɔ wan wɔl 
we mɔtalman go ɛnjɔy fridɔm fɔ tɔk ɛn biliv ɛn fridɔm 
frɔm fred ɛn want dɔn bi di ay aspireshɔn fɔ di kɔmɔn 
pipul dɛn, .  
We i impɔtant, if dɛn nɔ fɔ fos mɔtalman fɔ gɛt rikɔs, as las wan 
resort, to rebellion against tyranny ɛn oppression, se mɔtalman rayt fɔ bi 
we di lɔ de protɛkt, .  
We i impɔtant fɔ mek padi biznɛs bitwin 
neshɔn dɛn, .  
We di pipul dɛm na di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɔn insay di Chata riaffirm dɛn 
fet pan di men rayt dɛn we mɔtalman gɛt, pan di rɛspɛkt ɛn valyu we di mɔtalman pɔsin gɛt 
ɛn insay di ikwal rayt dɛn we man ɛn uman gɛt ɛn dɛn dɔn disayd fɔ mek pipul dɛn go bifo 
soshal prɔgrɛs ɛn bɛtɛ standad fɔ layf na big fridɔm, .  
We di Mɛmba Stet dɛn dɔn prɔmis fɔ ajɔst, insay wok togɛda 
wit di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn, fɔ mek ɔlman gɛt rɛspɛkt fɔ ɛn fɔ obe 
mɔtalman rayt ɛn di men fridɔm dɛn, .  
We wan kɔmɔn ɔndastandin fɔ dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya na fɔ di 
di impɔtant tin we pas ɔl fɔ mek dɛn du dis prɔmis ful wan, .  
So naw, .
Di Jɛnɛral Asɛmbli, .  
Prɔklaym dis Yunivasal Diklɛreshɔn fɔ Yuman Rayt as kɔmɔn standad fɔ 
ajɔstmɛnt fɔ ɔl pipul ɛn ɔl neshɔn, to di ɛnd we ɔlman ɛn 
ɛvri ɔgan na sosayti, we de kip dis Diklɛreshɔn na dɛn maynd ɔltɛm, fɔ tray fɔ du am 
 

 
tichin ɛn ɛdyukeshɔn fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya ɛn bay 
prɔgrɛsiv mɛsej dɛn, nashɔnal ɛn intanashɔnal, fɔ mek dɛn sikyuɔr dɛn yunivasal ɛn 
fɔ no ɛn fɔ obe am fayn fayn wan, ɔl tu bitwin di pipul dɛn na di Mɛmba Stet dɛn 
dɛnsɛf ɛn bitwin di pipul dɛn we de na di say dɛn we dɛn de ɔnda dɛn.  
Atikul I  
Dɛn bɔn ɔl mɔtalman fri ɛn ikwal pan rɛspɛkt ɛn rayt. Na dɛn de 
endowed wit rizin ɛn kɔnshɛns ɛn dɛn fɔ du sɔntin to dɛnsɛf insay a 
spirit fɔ bi brɔda ɛn sista.  
Atikul 2  
Ɔlman gɛt ɔl di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de insay dis Diklɛreshɔn, . 
we nɔ gɛt ɛni kayn difrɛns, lɛk rays, kɔlɔ, man ɔ uman, langwej, rilijɔn, . 
pɔlitikal ɔ ɔda opinion, nashɔnal ɔ soshal ɔrijin, prɔpati, bɔn ɔ ɔda stetmɛnt.  
Dɔn bak, dɛn nɔ fɔ mek ɛni difrɛns pan di pɔlitiks, . 
jɔrisdikshɔnal ɔ intanashɔnal stetmɛnt fɔ di kɔntri ɔ teritɔri we pɔsin de go 
na in yon, ilɛksɛf na indipɛndɛnt, trɔst, nɔ de rul insɛf ɔ ɔnda ɛni ɔda pɔsin 
limiteshɔn fɔ di sovereignty.  
Atikul 3  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ liv, fridɔm ɛn sef.  
Atikul 4  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ bi slev ɔ slev; slev wok ɛn di slev biznɛs go bi
we dɛn nɔ gri fɔ du ɔl kayn we.  
Atikul 5  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ mek dɛn sɔfa ɔ trit am bad, we nɔ gɛt wanwɔd ɔ we de mek i nɔ gɛt wan rɛspɛkt 
ɔ pɔnishmɛnt.  
 

 
Atikul 6  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ no am ɔlsay as pɔsin bifo di lɔ.  
Atikul 7  
Ɔlman ikwal bifo di lɔ ɛn dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ ikwal witout ɛni diskrimineshɔn 
protɛkshɔn fɔ di lɔ. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal protɛkshɔn agens ɛni wan 
diskrimineshɔn we nɔ gri wit dis Diklɛreshɔn ɛn agens ɛnitin we go mek dɛn du dat 
diskrimineshɔn.  
Atikul 8  
Ɛvribɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ mek di kɔmpiten nashɔnal traybɔn dɛn gɛt di rayt fɔ sɔlv di prɔblɛm 
fɔ akt dɛn we de agens di fawndeshɔnal rayt dɛn we di kɔnstityushɔn ɔ lɔ gi am.  
Atikul 9  
Dɛn nɔ go arɛst ɛnibɔdi, detɛn am ɔ kɛr am go na ɔda kɔntri fɔ insɛf.  
Atikul 10  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt rayt fɔ mek indipɛndɛnt yɛri fayn ɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn yɛri am 
ɛn traybɔn we nɔ de tek wan pɔsin bɛtɛ pas ɔda pɔsin, we i de disayd in rayt ɛn ɔbligayshɔn ɛn ɛni wan 
kriminal chaj agens am.  
Atikul 11  
1. Ɛnibɔdi we dɛn chaj fɔ penal ɔfens gɛt rayt fɔ mek dɛn tek am 
inosɛnt te dɛn pruv se i gilti akɔdin to lɔ na pɔblik trial we i 
dɔn gɛt ɔl di garanti dɛn we i nid fɔ mek i ebul fɔ protɛkt insɛf.  
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go gilti fɔ ɛni penal ɔfens bikɔs ɔf ɛnitin we i du ɔ 
omishɔn we nɔ bin bi penal ɔfens, ɔnda nashɔnal ɔ 
intanashɔnal lɔ, di tɛm we dɛn bin de du am. Nɔto wan we ebi pas am nɔ go ebul fɔ du dat
penalty fɔ bi pas di wan we bin de di tɛm we dɛn bin de penal 
dɛn bin dɔn du bad tin.  
Atikul 12  
Nɔbɔdi nɔ fɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit in prayvet pat, in famili, in os 
ɔ kɔrɛspɔndɛns, ɔ fɔ atak in ɔnɔ ɛn gudnem. Ɔlman gɛt 
di rayt fɔ gɛt di protɛkshɔn we di lɔ de gi fɔ mek dɛn nɔ ambɔg ɔ atak dɛn kayn tin dɛn de.  
Atikul 13  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ muv ɛn fɔ de insay di 
bɔda dɛn fɔ ɛni Stet.  
2. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ kɔmɔt na ɛni kɔntri, ivin in yon, ɛn fɔ 
go bak na in kɔntri.  
Atikul 14  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ go fɛn ɛn ɛnjɔy na ɔda kɔntri fɔ asaylans frɔm 
we dɛn de mek pipul dɛn sɔfa.  
2. Dɛn nɔ go yuz dis rayt if dɛn prosɛkute am fɔ tru 
we kɔmɔt frɔm kraym dɛn we nɔto pɔlitikal ɔ frɔm akt dɛn we nɔ gri wit di tin dɛn we dɛn want fɔ du ɛn 
prinsipul dɛn we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn gɛt.  
Atikul 15  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt in kɔntri.  
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go tek in kɔntri bay wilful ɔ dɛn nɔ go gi am di rayt fɔ 
chenj di kɔntri we i kɔmɔt.  
Atikul 16  
 

 
1. Man ɛn uman dɛn we dɔn ol, we nɔ gɛt ɛni limit bikɔs ɔf dɛn kɔlɔ, dɛn kɔntri 
ɔ rilijɔn, gɛt rayt fɔ mared ɛn fɔ mek famili. Dɛn gɛt rayt fɔ du dat 
fɔ ikwal rayt as fɔ mared, di tɛm we dɛn de mared ɛn we dɛn de disɔl.  
2. Dɛn fɔ mek mared nɔmɔ if di 
we dɛn want fɔ mared.  
3. Di famili na di natura ɛn fondamental grup yunit na sosayti ɛn na
we gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt protɛkshɔn frɔm di sosayti ɛn di Stet.  
Atikul 17  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt prɔpati in wan ɛn bak wit 
ɔda wan dɛn.  
2. Nɔbɔdi nɔ go tek in prɔpati bay wilful.  
Atikul 18  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tink, kɔnshɛns ɛn rilijɔn; dis rayt 
inklud fridɔm fɔ chenj in rilijɔn ɔ biliv, ɛn fridɔm, ilɛksɛf na in wan ɔ insay 
kɔmyuniti wit ɔda pipul dɛn ɛn na pɔblik ɔ prayvet, fɔ sho in rilijɔn ɔ biliv pan 
tichin, praktis, wɔship ɛn ɔbshɔbishɔn.  
Atikul 19  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tink ɛn tɔk wetin i want; dis rayt inklud 
fridɔm fɔ ol opinion we nɔ gɛt ɛnitin fɔ du wit am ɛn fɔ fɛn, gɛt ɛn gi 
infɔmeshɔn ɛn aydia dɛn tru ɛni midia ɛn ilɛksɛf na di frɔnt.  
Atikul 20  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛda ɛn jɔyn an wit pis.  
2. Dɛn nɔ go fos ɛnibɔdi fɔ de na wan ɔganayzeshɔn.  
Atikul 21  
 

 
1. Ɛnibɔdi gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat pan in kɔntri gɔvmɛnt, . 
dairekt ɔ tru pipul dɛn we dɛn pik fri wan.  
2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal chans fɔ gɛt pɔblik savis na in kɔntri.  
3. Di pipul dɛn want fɔ bi di men tin we gɔvmɛnt gɛt fɔ du; 
dis wil go bi insay ilɛkshɔn we dɛn kin gɛt wan wan tɛm ɛn we dɛn kin du tru tru ilɛkshɔn we go 
bi bay ɔlman ɛn ikwal rayt fɔ vot ɛn dɛn go gɛt am bay sikrit vot ɔ bay 
ikwal fri vot prosidur.  
Atikul 22
Ɔlman, as mɛmba na sosayti, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt soshal sikyɔriti ɛn i gɛt rayt 
fɔ riliys, tru nashɔnal ɛfɔt ɛn intanashɔnal kɔpɔreshɔn ɛn in 
akɔdin to di ɔganayzeshɔn ɛn di tin dɛn we ɛni Stet gɛt, fɔ di ikɔnomi, . 
soshal ɛn kɔlchɔral rayt dɛn we impɔtant fɔ in rɛspɛkt ɛn fɔ di fri divɛlɔpmɛnt 
fɔ di kayn pɔsin we i bi.  
Atikul 23  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ wok, fɔ fri fɔ pik wok, fɔ jɔs ɛn 
fayn fayn kɔndishɔn fɔ wok ɛn fɔ protɛkt pipul dɛn frɔm pipul dɛn we nɔ gɛt wok.  
2. Ɔlman, we nɔ gɛt ɛni diskrimineshɔn, gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt ikwal pe fɔ ikwal 
wok.  
3. Ɛnibɔdi we de wok gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt di rayt ɛn fayn mɔni 
fɔ mek shɔ se fɔ insɛf ɛn in famili gɛt layf we fit fɔ gɛt mɔtalman rɛspɛkt, . 
ɛn ad pan am, if nid de, wit ɔda we dɛn fɔ protɛkt pipul dɛn na soshal layf.  
4. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ mek ɛn jɔyn tred yunion fɔ protɛkshɔn fɔ 
di tin dɛn we i lɛk.  
Atikul 24  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ rɛst ɛn ɛnjɔy insɛf, ivin di rayt fɔ rɛst 
wok awa ɛn ɔlide dɛn we dɛn kin gɛt wan wan tɛm wit pe.  
 

 
Atikul 25  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ liv in layf we fit in wɛlbɔdi ɛn 
wɛlbɔdi fɔ insɛf ɛn fɔ in famili, inklud it, klos, os 
ɛn mɛdikal kia ɛn soshal savis dɛn we nid, ɛn di rayt fɔ gɛt sikyɔriti 
if i nɔ gɛt wok, sik, disabled, uman we in man dɔn day, ol ɔ 
ɔda tin dɛn we nɔ gɛt bɛtɛ tin fɔ liv pan tin dɛn we i nɔ ebul fɔ kɔntrol.
2. Mama ɛn pikin gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt spɛshal kia ɛn ɛp. Ɔl dis 
pikin dɛn, ilɛksɛf dɛn bɔn dɛn we dɛn mared ɔ we dɛn nɔ mared, fɔ ɛnjɔy di sem soshal 
protɛkshɔn.  
Atikul 26  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ lan buk. Edukeshon go bi fri, at least in di 
ɛlimɛntri ɛn fawndeshɔnal stej dɛn. Ɛlimɛntri ɛdyukeshɔn go bi 
na tin we pɔsin fɔ du. Dɛn fɔ mek tɛknikal ɛn pɔshɔnal ɛdyukeshɔn 
jɛnɛral wan ɛn ay edyukeshɔn fɔ bi ikwal akses to ɔlman 
pan di bies fɔ di mɛrit.  
2. Edukeshon go de fo di ful divelopment of di human 
pɔsin in pasɔnaliti ɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn rɛspɛkt mɔtalman rayt ɛn 
di men fridɔm dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt. I go mek pipul dɛn ɔndastand, fɔ lɛ dɛn nɔ gri wit wetin dɛn de tɔk ɛn 
padi biznɛs bitwin ɔl neshɔn, rays ɔ rilijɔn grup, ɛn i go go bifo 
di wok we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn de du fɔ mek pis kɔntinyu fɔ de.  
3. Mama ɛn papa dɛn gɛt rayt bifo tɛm fɔ pik di kayn edyukeshɔn we dɛn go gɛt 
we dɛn kin gi to dɛn pikin dɛn.  
Atikul 27  
1. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ tek pat fri wan pan di kɔlchɔ layf na di 
kɔmyuniti, fɔ ɛnjɔy di art ɛn fɔ sheb pan sayɛns advansmɛnt ɛn 
di bɛnifit dɛn we i kin gɛt.  
 

 
2. Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ protɛkt in gud abit ɛn prɔpati 
we kɔmɔt frɔm ɛni sayɛns, litrɛri ɔ atis prodakshɔn we i bi di 
di pɔsin we rayt di buk.  
Atikul 28  
Ɔlman gɛt rayt fɔ gɛt sɔshal ɛn intanashɔnal ɔda we di rayt ɛn 
fridɔm dɛn we de insay dis Diklɛreshɔn kin rili apin.  
Atikul 29
1. Ɔlman gɛt wok fɔ du to di kɔmyuniti we na in nɔmɔ di fri ɛn ful 
divεlכpmεnt fכ in pasכnaliti pכsibul.  
2. We i de yuz in rayt ɛn fridɔm, ɔlman fɔ de ɔnda am nɔmɔ 
to dɛn kayn limit dɛn de we di lɔ dɔn disayd fɔ di rizin nɔmɔ 
fɔ mek shɔ se dɛn no di rayt we ɛn rɛspɛkt ɔda pipul dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm 
ɛn fɔ mit di jɔs rikwaymɛnt dɛn fɔ gud abit, pɔblik ɔda ɛn di 
jenɛral wɛlbɔdi na dimokrasi sosayti.  
3. Dɛn rayt ɛn fridɔm ya nɔ go ɛva yuz dɛn we de agens di 
di tin dɛn we di Yunaytɛd Neshɔn dɛn want ɛn di prinsipul dɛn.  
Atikul 30  
Nɔtin nɔ de na dis Diklɛreshɔn we dɛn nɔ go ebul fɔ ɛksplen as sɔntin we min fɔ ɛni Stet, grup ɔ 
pɔsin ɛni rayt fɔ du ɛnitin ɔ fɔ du ɛnitin we dɛn aim fɔ di 
fɔ pwɛl ɛni wan pan di rayt ɛn fridɔm dɛn we de na dis say.
