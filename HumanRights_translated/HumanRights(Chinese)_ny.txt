Ndime 11

2. (ii) Mchitidwe uliwonse kapena kulephera kwa munthu aliyense, komwe, panthawiyo, sikuletsedwa ndi lamulo ladziko kapena mayiko.

Ndime 20
1. (1) Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi danga losonkhana mwamtendere. 
2. (ii) Palibe amene angakakamizidwe kukhala m’gulu. 
Ndime 21
1. (1) Aliyense ali ndi danga lotengapo mbali pa kayendetsedwe ka dziko lake, mwachindunji kapena kupyolera mwa nthumwi zosankhidwa mwaufulu. 
2. (2) Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhala ndi mwayi wofanana wogwila nchito za boma m’dziko lake.

Kuvota kwachinsinsi kapena njira yofananira nayo yovotera mwaulele idzachitidwa.

Zothandizira.  
Ndime 23
1. (1) Aliyense ali ndi danga logwira ntchito, kusankha ntchito mwaufulu, ndi malo ogwirira ntchito mwachilungamo
mafayilo ndikusangalala ndi chitetezo ku ulova. 
 

 
2. (2) Aliyense ali ndi danga lolandira malipiro ofanana pa ntchito yofanana popanda tsankho. 
3. (3) Aliyense wogwira ntchito ali ndi ufulu kulandira malipiro oyenera, omwe ndi otsimikizika kuti athe kugwira ntchito yake.
Anthu ndi mabanja awo amakhala ndi moyo womwe umakwaniritsa zosowa zawo, wowonjezeredwa ndi njira zina pakafunika kutero
chitetezo cha anthu. 
4. (4) Aliyense ali ndi danga lokhazikitsa ndi kulowa mipingo kuti ateteze zofuna zake.

Ufulu wotenga tchuthi cholipidwa.

Ndime 27
 

 
1. (1) Aliyense ali ndi ufulu kutenga nawo mbali pa chikhalidwe cha anthu, kusangalala ndi zaluso, ndi kugawana nawo sayansi
Kupita patsogolo ndi phindu lomwe limapanga.

Zokonda zawo zauzimu ndi zakuthupi zili ndi ufulu wotetezedwa. 
Ndime 28
Munthu aliyense ali ndi danga lofuna kuti pakhale bata pa chikhalidwe cha anthu ndi m'mayiko ena momwe mfundo zili mu chilengezochi
Ufulu ndi kumasuka zomwe zili mmenemo zikhoza kukwaniritsidwa.  
Ndime 29
1. (1) Aliyense ali ndi udindo kwa anthu, chifukwa m'dera lokha m'mene umunthu wake ungatheke
Landirani ufulu ndi chitukuko chokwanira. 
2. (2) Aliyense, pakugwiritsa ntchito ufulu ndi kumasuka kwake, ali ndi zoletsa zokhazikitsidwa ndi lamulo.
Cholinga chokhacho chokhazikitsa ziletso zotere ndicho kuonetsetsa kuti ufulu ndi ufulu wa ena ulemekezedwa
Kuzindikirika kwina ndi ulemu, ndikusintha kumakhalidwe abwino ndi dongosolo la anthu mu demokalase
ndi zofunika zovomerezeka za umoyo wabwino. 
3. (iii) Ufulu ndi kumasuka izi sizingagwiritsidwe ntchito motsutsana ndi mgwirizano muzochitika zilizonse.
Zolinga ndi Mfundo za United Nations. 
Ndime 30
Palibe chilichonse m'chikalatachi chomwe chidzamasuliridwe ngati kuvomereza mwachichewa ufulu wa dziko, gulu kapena munthu aliyense.
Zochita kapena khalidwe lililonse lofuna kunyozetsa ufulu ndi kumasuka komwe kwafotokozedwa mu chikalatachi.
