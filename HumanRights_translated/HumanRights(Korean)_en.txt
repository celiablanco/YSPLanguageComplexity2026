Universal Declaration of Human Rights

Recognizing the inherent dignity and equal and inalienable rights of all members of the human race is the foundation of freedom, justice and peace in the world; disregard and contempt for human rights have led to atrocities that have outraged the conscience of humanity; and the advent of a world in which man can enjoy freedom of speech and belief and freedom from fear and want has been declared to be the highest aspiration of all people, lest man be forced to rebel as a last resort against tyranny and oppression; It is essential that human rights be protected by the rule of law, and it is essential to promote the development of friendly relations among nations. In its Charter, all members of the United Nations reaffirmed their belief in fundamental human rights, the dignity and worth of the human person, and the equal rights of men and women, and committed themselves to promoting social progress and better standards of living in wider freedoms. The Member States pledged themselves to cooperate with the United Nations to promote universal respect for and observance of human rights and fundamental freedoms. Since a common understanding of rights and freedoms is of paramount importance for the full implementation of this Covenant, the General Assembly of the United Nations, therefore, endeavors to promote respect for these rights and freedoms through learning and education among all individuals and institutions of society, always mindful of this Declaration, and strives, through progressive national and international measures, to ensure universal and effective recognition and observance of these rights and freedoms among the peoples of the Member States themselves and the peoples of the territories under their jurisdiction; We proclaim the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Article 1

All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. Human beings are naturally endowed with reason and conscience and must act in a spirit of brotherhood toward one another. Article 2 Everyone is entitled to all the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration, without discrimination of any kind, such as race, colour, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, property, birth or other status. Furthermore, there shall be no discrimination on the basis of the political, legal or international status of an individual's country or territory, regardless of whether that country or territory is an independent state, a trusteeship, a non-self-governing territory, or is subject to other restrictions on its sovereignty. Article 3 Everyone has the right to life, liberty and security of person. Article 4 No one shall be held in a state of slavery or servitude. All forms of slavery and the slave trade are prohibited. Article 5 No one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment. Article 6 Everyone has the right to be recognized as a human being before the law everywhere. Article 7
All people are equal before the law and have the right to equal protection of the law without any discrimination. Everyone has the right to equal protection against any discrimination in violation of this Declaration and against any incitement to such discrimination. Article 8 Everyone has the right to obtain effective relief from competent domestic courts for acts that violate the fundamental rights granted by the Constitution or law. Article 9 No one shall be arbitrarily arrested, detained or exiled. Article 10 Everyone has the right to a fair and public trial with full equality before an independent and impartial tribunal for the determination of his or her rights, obligations and criminal charges against him or her. Article 11 1. Every person accused of a crime has the right to be presumed innocent until proven guilty according to the law in a public trial where everything necessary for his or her defense is guaranteed. 2. No one shall be held guilty by reason of any act or omission which, at the time of the act, did not constitute a crime under national or international law. Additionally, no heavier punishment shall be imposed than the punishment that could have been applied at the time of the crime. Article 12 No one shall be subjected to arbitrary interference with his private life, home, residence or correspondence, or to criticism of his honor and reputation. Everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference or criticism. Article 13

1. Everyone has the right to freedom of movement and residence within his country. 2. Everyone has the right to leave any country, including his own, and the right to return to his country. Article 14 1. Everyone has the right to seek and receive asylum in other countries from persecution. 2. This right cannot be asserted in cases of prosecution for truly non-political crimes or acts contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations. Article 15 1. Everyone has the right to nationality. 2. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his nationality or denied the right to change his nationality. Article 16 1. Adult men and women have the right to marry and form a family without any restrictions based on race, nationality or religion. They are entitled to enjoy equal rights regarding marriage, during marriage and upon dissolution of marriage. 2. Marriage is established only with the free and complete consent of the future spouses. 3. The family is a natural and basic unit of society and has the right to be protected by society and the state. Article 17 1. Everyone has the right to own property both individually and jointly with others. 2. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his property. Article 18
Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion. This right includes freedom to change one's religion or belief and to manifest one's religion or belief, alone or in community with others and in public or private, by religious practices, worship and observance. Article 19 Everyone has the right to freedom of opinion and expression. These rights include freedom to hold opinions without interference and freedom to seek, obtain and impart information and ideas through any media, regardless of frontiers. Article 20 1. Everyone has the right to freedom of peaceful assembly and association. 2. No one shall be forced to participate in any association. Article 21 1. Everyone has the right to participate in the government of his country, either directly or through freely elected representatives. 2. Everyone has equal rights to hold public office in their country. 3. The will of the people is the basis of government power. This will be expressed through regular and genuine elections by secret or equivalently free voting procedures according to universal and equal suffrage. Article 22 Everyone, as a member of society, has the right to social security and the right to realize, through national efforts and international cooperation, and in accordance with the organization and resources of each State, the economic, social and cultural rights indispensable for the free development of his dignity and personality. Article 23

1. Everyone has the right to work, free choice of occupation, just and favorable working conditions and protection against unemployment. 2. Everyone has the right to receive equal compensation for equal work without any discrimination. 3. Everyone who works has the right to just and advantageous remuneration, which guarantees to himself and his family a survival consistent with human dignity and is supplemented, if necessary, by other social security measures. 4. Everyone has the right to form and join a labor union to protect his or her interests. Article 24 Everyone has the right to rest and leisure, including reasonable limitations on working hours and regular paid leave. Article 25 1. Everyone has the right to a standard of living adequate for the health and well-being of himself and his family, including food, clothing, shelter, medical care and necessary social welfare, and the right to security in case of lack of livelihood due to unemployment, illness, disability, death of a spouse, old age or other circumstances beyond his control. 2. Mothers and children have the right to special protection and support. All children enjoy the same social protection regardless of their status. Article 26 1. Everyone has the right to education. Education must be free, at least at the primary and basic levels. Primary education should be compulsory. Technical and vocational education should be universally accessible, and higher education should be equally accessible to all based on merit. 2. Education aims at the full development of personality and strengthening respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms. Education promotes understanding, tolerance and
We must promote friendship and promote the activities of the United Nations to maintain peace. 3. Parents have the priority to choose the type of education provided to their children. Article 27 1. Everyone has the right to freely participate in the cultural life of the community, enjoy the arts, and share in the advancement of science and its benefits. 2. Everyone has the right to protection of spiritual and material interests arising from scientific, literary or artistic products he or she creates. Article 28 Everyone has the right to a social and international order in which the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration can be fully realized. Article 29 1. Everyone has obligations to the community in which only his personality can develop freely and fully. 2. Everyone, in exercising his or her rights and freedoms, is subject to restrictions established by law solely for the purpose of ensuring that the rights and freedoms of others are recognized and respected, and for the purpose of meeting the legitimate needs for morality, public order and general welfare in a democratic society. 3. These rights and freedoms shall under no circumstances be exercised contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations. Article 30 Nothing in this Declaration shall be construed as authorizing any State, group or individual to engage in any activity or to take any action aimed at destroying any of the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration.
