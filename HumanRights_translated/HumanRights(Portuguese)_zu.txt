Isimemezelo Somhlaba Wonke Samalungelo Abantu 
Isendlalelo 
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi ukuhlonishwa kwesithunzi sawo wonke amalungu omndeni wesintu kanye namalungelo awo 
ukulingana nokungenakunakwa kwakha isisekelo senkululeko, ubulungisa kanye nokuthula emhlabeni;  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi ukungazi nokungahloniphi amalungelo abantu sekuholele ezenzweni zobulwane ngaleyo ndlela 
unembeza woBuntu nokuthi ukufika komhlaba lapho abantu bekhululekile ukukhuluma futhi bakholwe, 
ekhululiwe ekwesabeni nasekuhluphekeni, wamenyezelwa njengogqozi oluphakeme kakhulu loMuntu;  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi ukuvikelwa kwamalungelo abantu ngombuso womthetho kubalulekile, ukuze umuntu 
ungaphoqwa, endaweni ephakeme kakhulu, ukuba uvukele ubushiqela nengcindezelo;  
Nakuba kubalulekile ukukhuthaza ukuthuthukiswa kobudlelwano bobungani phakathi kwezizwe;  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi, kuSomqulu, abantu beNhlangano Yezizwe bamemezela, futhi, ukholo lwabo emalungelweni ayisisekelo eNhlangano Yezizwe. 
Umuntu, ngesithunzi nangokubaluleka komuntu, emalungelweni alinganayo amadoda nabesifazane futhi uma 
bamemezele ukuzimisela kwabo ukukhuthaza inqubekelaphambili yomphakathi kanye nokusungula izimo zokuphila ezingcono ngaphakathi kwenkululeko 
banzi;  
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi amazwe angamalungu azibophezele ekukhuthazeni, ngokubambisana neNhlangano ka 
IZizwe Ezihlangene, inhlonipho emhlabeni wonke nangempumelelo yamalungelo abantu kanye nenkululeko eyisisekelo;
Uma kubhekwa ukuthi umbono ofanayo walawa malungelo nenkululeko ubaluleke kakhulu ngokugcwele 
ukwaneliseka kwalokhu kuzibophezela:  
I-General Assembly imemezela lesi Simemezelo Sendawo Yonke  
Yamalungelo Abantu  
njengento evamile okufanele izuzwe yibo bonke abantu nezizwe zonke, ukuze bonke abantu kanye nazo zonke izitho zomzimba 
umphakathi, ukuhlale ucabanga ngakho, ulwela, ngokufundisa nangemfundo, ukuthuthukisa inhlonipho 
la malungelo nenkululeko kanye nokukhuthaza, ngezinyathelo eziqhubekayo zikazwelonke nezamazwe ngamazwe, zabo 
ukuqashelwa kanye nokusetshenziswa kwendawo yonke nangempumelelo kokubili phakathi kwemiphakathi yamazwe angamalungu ngokwawo kanye 
phakathi kwalezo zindawo ezibekwe ngaphansi kombuso wayo.  
I-athikili 1  
Bonke abantu bazalwa bekhululekile futhi belingana ngesithunzi nangamalungelo. Njengoba benikezwe ingqondo nonembeza, kumelwe 
sebenzelana omunye nomunye ngomoya wobuzalwane.  
I-athikili 2  

Bonke abantu bangasebenzisa amalungelo nenkululeko emenyezelwe kulesi Simemezelo, ngaphandle kokuhlukaniswa 
noma yimuphi, okungukuthi uhlanga, ibala, ubulili, ulimi, inkolo, umbono wezombangazwe noma omunye, imvelaphi yobuzwe 
noma kwezenhlalo, inhlanhla, ukuzalwa nanoma yisiphi esinye isimo. 
Ngaphezu kwalokho, akukho mehluko oyokwenziwa ngokusekelwe esimweni sezombusazwe, somthetho noma samazwe ngamazwe sezwe noma 
indawo umuntu azalelwa kuyo, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi lelo zwe noma indawo ezimele, ngaphansi kokugadwa, ukuzimela noma ngaphansi kokunye
ukulinganiselwa kobukhosi.  
I-athikili yesi-3 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphila, inkululeko kanye nokuphepha komuntu siqu.  
I-athikili yesi-4  
Akekho oyogqilazwa noma agqilazwe; ubugqila kanye nokuphathwa kwezigqila, ngazo zonke izinhlobo 
kwenqatshelwe.  
I-athikili yesi-5  
Akekho oyohlukunyezwa noma aphathwe ngonya, aphathwe ngendlela engenabuntu noma elulazayo.  
I-athikili yesi-6 
Bonke abantu banelungelo lokuqashelwa, yonke indawo, ubuntu babo obusemthethweni.  
I-athikili yesi-7  
Bonke bayalingana phambi komthetho futhi, ngaphandle kokuhlukaniswa, banelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo komthetho. Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa ngokulinganayo 
ngokumelene nanoma yikuphi ukucwasa okwephula lesi Simemezelo kanye nanoma yikuphi ukugqugquzela lokho kucwasa.  
Isigaba 8 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuthola usizo olusebenzayo emagunyeni kazwelonke afanelekile ngokumelene nezenzo eziphula umthetho 
amalungelo ayisisekelo ahlonishwa nguMthethosisekelo noma umthetho.  
I-athikili yesi-9 
Akekho ongaboshwa ngokungafanele, avalelwe noma adingiswe.  
Isigaba 10  

Wonke umuntu unelungelo, ngokulingana okuphelele, lokuthi udaba lwakhe lugwetshwe ngendlela efanele futhi esidlangalaleni yinkantolo 
isinqumo esizimele nesingathathi hlangothi samalungelo nezibopho zakho noma izizathu zanoma yikuphi ukusolwa odabeni 
inhlawulo abekwe yona.  
Isigaba 11 
1. 
Wonke umuntu osolwa ngesenzo sobugebengu uthathwa njengomsulwa kuze kutholakale ukuthi unecala.
kufakazelwe ngokomthetho ngesikhathi senqubo yomphakathi lapho kunikezwa khona zonke iziqinisekiso zokuvikela ezidingekayo 
ziyaqinisekiswa.  
2. 
Akekho oyolahlwa ngezenzo noma ukweqiwa lokho, ngesikhathi sokuthunywa kwakhe, kwakungesona isenzo. 
ubugebengu ngaphansi komthetho wasekhaya noma wamazwe ngamazwe. Ngokufanayo, akukho sijeziso esinzima esiyonikezwa ukwedlula 
lokho okwakusebenza ngesikhathi kwenziwa isenzo sobugebengu.  
Isigaba 12  
Akekho ozobhekana nokugxambukela empilweni yakhe yangasese, emndenini wakhe, emzini wakhe noma kubo 
izincwadi, noma ukuhlasela isithunzi nesithunzi sakhe. Ngokumelene nokuphazamiseka noma ukuhlaselwa okunjalo wonke umuntu unelungelo loku 
ukuvikelwa komthetho.  
Isigaba 13  
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuhamba ngokukhululeka futhi azikhethele indawo yokuhlala ngaphansi koMbuso.  
2. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokushiya izwe ahlala kulo, okuhlanganisa nelakhe, kanye nelungelo lokubuyela 
ezweni lakini.  
Isigaba 14  
1. 
Wonke umuntu ongaphansi kokushushiswa unelungelo lokufuna nokuzuza endaweni yokukhosela kwamanye amazwe.  
2. 
Leli lungelo ngeke, nokho, lisetshenziswe endabeni yezinqubo ezikhona zobugebengu ngaphansi komthetho. 
noma ngemisebenzi ephambene nezinjongo nemigomo yeNhlangano yeziZwe.  
Isigaba 15  
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba nobuzwe.  
2. 
Akekho ongase aphucwe ubuzwe noma ilungelo lokushintsha ubuzwe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
Isigaba 16  
1.
Kusukela eminyakeni yokushada, amadoda nabesifazane banelungelo lokushada futhi bathole umndeni, ngaphandle kwemingcele. 
ngobuhlanga, ubuzwe noma inkolo. Ngesikhathi somshado nangesikhathi sokuchithwa kwawo, izinhlangothi zombili zinamalungelo 
kulingana.  
2. 
Umshado awukwazi ukugujwa ngaphandle kwemvume ekhululekile negcwele yabashade nabo bakusasa.  
3. 
Umndeni uyingxenye yemvelo neyisisekelo yomphakathi futhi unelungelo lokuvikelwa emphakathini nakuHulumeni.  

Isigaba 17  
1. 
Wonke umuntu, ngabanye noma ngokuhlanganyela, unelungelo lempahla.  
2. 
Akekho ongaphucwa impahla yakhe ngaphandle kwesizathu.  
Isigaba 18 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokucabanga, unembeza nenkolo; Leli lungelo lisho inkululeko ye 
shintsha inkolo noma inkolelo yakho, kanye nenkululeko yokubonisa inkolo noma inkolelo yakho, uwedwa noma ngaphakathi 
evamile, kokubili emphakathini nangasese, ngokufundisa, ukwenza, ukukhulekela kanye nemikhuba.  
Isigaba 19 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokubeka imibono neyokuveza imizwa yakhe, okusho ukuthi unelungelo lokungaphazanyiswa 
imibono yabo kanye nokufuna, ukwamukela nokusabalalisa, ngaphandle kokubheka imingcele, ulwazi kanye nemibono yanoma yimuphi 
izindlela zokukhuluma.  
I-athikili 20° 
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lenkululeko yokuhlangana nokuhlangana ngokuthula.  
2. 
Akekho ongaphoqwa ukuthi abe yingxenye yenhlangano.  
Isigaba 21  
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ekuphathweni kwezindaba zomphakathi wezwe lakhe, noma ngabe ngokuqondile,
noma ngabamele abakhethwe ngokukhululeka.  
2. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokufinyelela, ngaphansi kwezimo ezilinganayo, emisebenzini yomphakathi yezwe lakhe.  
3. 
Intando yabantu iyisisekelo segunya lamandla omphakathi: futhi kufanele ivezwe ngokhetho 
ukhetho oluqotho okufanele lwenziwe ngezikhathi ezithile ngokugunyazwa okulinganayo nokulinganayo, ngevoti eliyimfihlo noma inqubo yesibili 
okulinganayo okuvikela inkululeko yokuvota.  
Isigaba 22  
Wonke umuntu, njengelungu lomphakathi, unelungelo lokuvikeleka komphakathi; futhi angase afune ngokusemthethweni ukwaneliseka kwe 
Amalungelo abalulekile ezomnotho, ezenhlalakahle namasiko, ngenxa yemizamo kazwelonke kanye nokubambisana kwamazwe ngamazwe, we 
ukuvumelana nenhlangano nezinsiza zezwe ngalinye.  
Isigaba 23 
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusebenza, lokuzikhethela ngokukhululekile umsebenzi, nezimo zokusebenza ezilinganayo nezigculisayo 
umsebenzi kanye nokuvikela ekusweleni umsebenzi.  
2. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo, ngaphandle kokubandlululwa, lokuthola inkokhelo elinganayo ngomsebenzi ofanayo.  

3. 
Noma ngubani osebenzayo unelungelo lokuthola iholo elilinganayo neligculisayo, elivumela yena nomndeni wakhe 
ukuba khona ngokuhambisana nesithunzi somuntu, futhi kwenezelwe, uma kungenzeka, ngazo zonke ezinye izindlela 
ukuvikelwa komphakathi.  
4. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokusungula izinyunyana nabanye kanye nokujoyina izinyunyana ukuze azivikele 
ngezintshisekelo zakho.  
Isigaba 24
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuphumula nelokungcebeleka, ikakhulukazi, umkhawulo ofanele ngesikhathi sokusebenza kanye 
iholide elikhokhelwayo ngezikhathi ezithile.  
Isigaba 25 
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lezinga lempilo elanele ukuqinisekisa impilo nenhlalakahle yakhe nomndeni wakhe
kube, ikakhulukazi mayelana nokudla, okokugqoka, indawo yokuhlala, usizo lwezokwelashwa kanye nangokwemibandela 
izidingo zomphakathi ezidingekayo, futhi unelungelo lokuphepha uma kwenzeka ungasebenzi, ukugula, ukukhubazeka, 
ubufelokazi, ukuguga noma amanye amacala okulahlekelwa yizinto zokuziphilisa ngenxa yezimo ezizimele 
intando yakho.  
2. 
Ukubeletha kanye nobuntwana banelungelo losizo olukhethekile nosizo. Zonke izingane, ezalelwe noma 
Ngaphandle komshado, bajabulela ukuvikeleka komphakathi okufanayo.  
Isigaba 26  
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lemfundo. Imfundo kumele ibe mahhala, okungenani ehambisana nokufundisa 
okuyisisekelo. Imfundo yamabanga aphansi iphoqelekile. Imfundo yobuchwepheshe neyomsebenzi kufanele ibe 
sandile; Ukufinyelela ezifundweni eziphakeme kumele kuvuleke kubo bonke ngokulingana okuphelele, kuye ngokuthi 
ukufaneleka kwayo.  
2. 
Imfundo kumele ihlose ukwandisa ngokugcwele ubuntu bomuntu kanye nokuqiniswa kwamalungelo abantu kanye 
inkululeko eyisisekelo futhi kumele ikhuthaze ukuqonda, ukubekezelelana kanye nobungane phakathi kwazo zonke izizwe kanye 
wonke amaqembu ezinhlanga noma ezenkolo, kanye nokuthuthukiswa kwemisebenzi yeNhlangano Yezizwe Ezihlangene 
ukugcina ukuthula.  
3.
Abazali banelungelo elibalulekile lokukhetha uhlobo lwemfundo okufanele inikezwe izingane zabo.  
Isigaba 27  
1. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokubamba iqhaza ngokukhululeka empilweni yamasiko omphakathi, ajabulele ezobuciko kanye 
iqhaza ekuthuthukisweni kwesayensi nasezinzuzweni eziwumphumela.  
2. 
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuvikelwa kwezithakazelo zokuziphatha kanye nezinto ezibonakalayo ezixhumene nanoma iyiphi isayensi, ukukhiqizwa kwemibhalo 
noma umsebenzi wobuciko owakho.  
Isigaba 28  
Wonke umuntu unelungelo lokuba nomyalo olawulayo, endizeni yezenhlalo neyomhlaba wonke, ekwazi ngokugcwele ukwenza lokho 
amalungelo kanye nenkululeko ebekwe kulesi Simemezelo ayasebenza.  

Isigaba 29  
1. 
Umuntu unemisebenzi emphakathini, ngaphandle kwawo intuthuko yamahhala negcwele ingenzeki. 
yobuntu bakho.  
2. 
Ekusetshenzisweni kwala malungelo nasekuthokozeleni lezi nkululeko, akekho ongaphansi kwemikhawulo 
esungulwe ngokomthetho ngenhloso eyodwa yokuthuthukisa ukuqashelwa nokuhlonipha amalungelo kanye 
inkululeko yabanye kanye nokwanelisa izimfuno ezinobulungiswa zokuziphatha, ukuhleleka komphakathi kanye nenhlalakahle 
emphakathini wentando yeningi.  
3. 
Akunakwenzeka ukuthi la malungelo kanye nenkululeko kusetshenziswe ngokuphambene nezinjongo nezimiso zoMthethosisekelo 
IZizwe Ezihlangene.  
I-athikili 30°  
Akukho kulokhu kuMemezelo okungahunyushwa njengokusho noma yimuphi uMbuso,
iqembu noma umuntu ilungelo lokuhlanganyela emsebenzini othile noma ukwenza isenzo esihloselwe ukucekela phansi 
amalungelo nenkululeko ebekwe lapha.
